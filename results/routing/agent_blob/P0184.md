# Scene prompt

Crea un mapa 3D de escape obby ambientado en un laberinto de neón, no basado en un teclado. Organiza 5 etapas pequeñas conectadas en un recorrido de avance, con saltos y obstáculos de neón a través de pasillos y secciones del laberinto. La salida final es un punto brillante y claramente visible al final del recorrido.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Obby & Platformer, with Puzzle secondary because the escape route is explicitly a labyrinth whose exit must remain reachable.

## Shape and preset

Use `puzzle-maze` for one solvable neon labyrinth divided into five sequential obstacle stages. This shape comes from outside Obby & Platformer’s usual set and replaces the flat course in the Classic Obby preset; the preset remains recognizable through its sequential jumps and checkpoints.

## Config requirements

Draw `path-track` as one ordered route through all five stages, with every required jump kept within normal avatar physics limits. Add `obstacle-jump` as neon gaps, stepping platforms, and wrap-around jumps within the corridors, and `obstacle-maze` as branching luminous passages that still preserve the authored solution route. Show the physical pads for `checkpoint-respawn` at stage entries. Draw `winner-zone` as the bright final exit and payoff area visible at the route’s end.

## Layout requirements

Place five `checkpoint-respawn` respawn markers, one on the safe pad at the entrance to each stage. Place one `winner-zone` completion volume at the luminous exit after stage five. Nothing else invisible needs placement.

## Layout components

Build five small neon stages in order inside a bounded 3D labyrinth. Each stage occupies a compact corridor section with jump platforms and obstacles, linked to the next by a clear passage; branches may loop locally but cannot break the route to the exit. Use dark floors and walls with bright neon trim, strong color changes between stages, and safe flat checkpoint pads at all five entrances. Enclose the maze with solid outer walls and frame the whole course so its progressive route and brilliant final exit read clearly.

## Render order

Use `authored_plan`: generate the solvable maze and its reachable exit first, then dress its top-down and isometric views without changing the validated route.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small because there are exactly five compact stages. The theme is dark futuristic neon. Use `P6` because both the maze topology and every jump chain must be valid by construction.
