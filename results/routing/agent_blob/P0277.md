# Scene prompt
Create a bright, stylized 3D survival map built as a large, difficult obby arranged as an open warren of interconnected obstacle sections. Provide multiple looping routes, crossings, and double-back paths through the course so players can chase and evade each other; keep the hard obstacles visually clear and easy to read.

# Agent decision
### Genre
The dominant genre is Survival, as the timer-based tagger-versus-hiders chase is the game’s purpose. Obby & Platformer is secondary because difficult movement obstacles are the terrain through which that chase happens.

### Shape and preset
Use `warren-looping` from Survival: obstacle sections form interconnected circles with no dead ends, allowing hiders to double back and preventing the tagger from winning through artificial corners. The preset is 1 vs All, retaining its looping pursuit space, sightline breaks, and separated role starts while dropping the escape gate because hiders survive in place until the timer ends.

### Config requirements
Use `cover-los` for obstacle walls, raised blocks, and localized recesses that interrupt direct tagger sightlines without hiding the course’s readable edges. Use `obstacle-jump` for demanding but clearly legible jump chains distributed across the warren. Use `path-shortcut` for tighter alternate obstacle lines that reconnect farther around each loop. Crossings and double-back links must remain visually distinct so every hard route can be read at speed.

### Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` starts for the tagger and hiders on separated, screened safe pads so the chase cannot begin with immediate contact. No `winner-zone` or `gate-escape` is required because the round ends by timer rather than at a destination.

### Layout components
Build a large open obstacle warren divided into several recognizable sections, each containing hard jump sequences and at least two connections onward. Looping paths should cross at readable junctions, then reconnect through risky shortcuts and longer evasive routes. Use broad safe junction pads between obstacle clusters, with no terminal corridors or forced finish line. The boundary should be bright, stylized, and clearly impassable, while the frame shows the overall web of routes and its major crossings.

### Render order
Use `topdown`: both the zero-dead-end chase network and physics-legal obstacle connections must be valid before the isometric image dresses the course.

### Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large, driven by a giant obby with enough loops for sustained pursuit. The theme is bright, stylized, and high-contrast. Use `P6` because loop connectivity and traversable obstacle spacing must be valid by construction.
