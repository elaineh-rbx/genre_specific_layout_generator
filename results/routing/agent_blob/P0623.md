# Scene prompt

Create a large open-road 3D map spanning a few kilometres for free motorcycle riding. Lay out connected open roads across a spacious landscape, with long clear stretches that support maneuvering and wheelies; keep the world feeling expansive but manageable for a first version.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Simulation, specifically free motorcycle operation. The author explicitly excludes races and stunt challenges, so neither Racing nor an objective-course genre applies; realistic riding and wheelies are vehicle behavior within a shared space.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open`, one Shared Persistent World whose connected roads can be explored in any order. Match the Vehicle Sim preset, retaining the open world and vehicle-route option while dropping the unrelated shared resource field.

## Config requirements

Use `path-road-vehicle` for a connected network of open motorcycle roads spanning a few kilometres. Adapt it away from hauling: roads should have long, wide, clear stretches, gentle junctions, and enough lateral room for maneuvering and wheelies. Draw one rideable motorcycle as the focal vehicle. Do not add race markings, stunt ramps, gates, shops, buildings, NPCs, water, islands, or pronounced terrain relief because none was requested. Realistic motorcycle handling and wheelie physics are runtime behavior, not additional map geometry.

## Layout requirements

Place one motorcycle spawn marker at a broad roadside pull-off near the map entrance, with the bike aligned toward the network and clear of traffic space. Place the player entry point beside it. No race starts, finish triggers, lap volumes, stunt scoring zones, checkpoints, pickups, task stations, or enemy emitters are needed because riding is entirely free-form.

## Layout components

Build one spacious continuous landscape crossed by connected open roads. Include several long straight or gently curving segments suitable for stable wheelies, linked by broad bends and uncomplicated intersections that support free exploration. Keep shoulders clear and preserve generous sight distance. The world boundary should sit far enough beyond the roads to maintain an expansive feeling without implying a city-sized build. Use simple neutral ground and sparse low-detail surroundings so the motorcycle and usable road surface dominate the composition. Frame the network so its connectivity and longest riding stretches are immediately visible.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, then dress the isometric image, because every road segment must connect into a usable free-riding network.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is large, driven by the requested few kilometres of road, but remains manageable for a first version. The theme is a clean, neutral open-road landscape. Route `P0`: one continuous surface needs no overhang, interior transition, separate map, volumetric check, or validity-by-construction pass.
