# Scene prompt

Create a 3D game map of a bright, approachable cartoon suburban town with pre-built houses already standing on claimable residential lots.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, specifically an open-ended town-life roleplay rather than a task-based job simulation.

## Shape and preset

Use `settlement-claimable`: a finished suburban town whose houses already stand on distinct lots and can be claimed rather than constructed. The matching preset is Life.

## Config requirements

Draw `path-road-vehicle` as broad, flat, grid-aligned two-way neighborhood roads, at least 30 studs wide, with enough turning room around the residential lots. Use `district-zoned` to distinguish the quiet residential area from a smaller civic or commercial town center. Include `building-interior` for the claimable homes, which players enter from the street.

## Layout requirements

Place one house-claim marker on each residential lot, aligned to its front approach and associated only with that lot. No exact house count was supplied, so the lot count should follow the rendered town rather than inventing a fixed quantity.

## Layout components

Build a medium suburban town with a compact town center and surrounding residential blocks. A connected two-way road network loops from the center through rows of separated house lots, with sidewalks or short front paths linking each road to each home. Each lot contains one pre-built, enterable detached house and a small yard. Keep civic or commercial façades near the center and homes on quieter outer streets. Use mostly level grassy terrain and pavement, an enclosing suburban edge, and a readable three-quarter composition that keeps the road hierarchy and individual lots visible.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down plan; the bright cartoon look and approachable neighborhood composition lead, while the road network does not require computed topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a medium scale, driven by several residential blocks around one center. The theme is bright, friendly cartoon suburbia. Apply `P3` because play crosses between outdoor streets and enterable house interiors.
