# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game layout for “Silenced,” beginning in a lobby with roads bordered by invisible barriers and four separate walk-in queue areas for one-player, two-player, three-player, and four-player sessions. Each queue leads into the same compact, realistic three-story school map with a few corridors per floor, connected by stairways. Include classrooms, large halls, and bathrooms throughout; some classrooms have chairs and tables packed away, while a few sleeping students are scattered around the building. Place cleaning-task areas in toilets, classrooms, and halls. Include several toilets marked out of order and a bathroom with shallow flooding spilling from the stalls. Populate parts of the school with students, including groups of wealthy-looking students acting possessively around the corridors.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Simulation is dominant, with Adventure secondary. Completing a janitor task loop ends each shift, while the missing-people story unfolds through the same school.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-staged`, taken from outside Simulation’s usual shapes, because the queue lobby is fully separate from the shared school experience. Start from the Role Sim preset, substituting this shape and retaining task stations and walkable circulation while dropping vehicle routes.

## Config requirements
Use `trigger-task` to draw recognizable cleaning stations in toilets, classrooms, and halls. Use `path-circulation` for compact corridors and stairways connecting every task area. Use `npc-population` for interactive students, sleeping students, and wealthy-looking corridor groups. Use `water-body` for shallow bathroom flooding from the stalls. Draw packed-away classroom furniture, out-of-order toilet signs, four lobby queue bays, roads, and visible edges aligned to invisible barriers.

## Layout requirements
Place the layout half of `trigger-task` at every cleaning area, aligned to the relevant floor, fixture, or rubbish site. Place four uncatalogued queue trigger volumes in the lobby with exact capacities of one, two, three, and four players, all routing to the same school start. Place the layout half of `npc-population` at conversational corridor positions, seated or sleeping classroom positions, and possessive groupings that do not block circulation.

## Layout components
Build two separate spaces: one bounded queue lobby and one compact three-story school. In the lobby, arrange exactly four walk-in queue areas beside roads with invisible edge barriers. In the school, build a few corridors per floor, connected by clearly aligned stairways, with classrooms, large halls, and bathrooms distributed across all three levels. Include several out-of-order toilets, one shallow-flooded bathroom, cleaning areas in toilets, classrooms, and halls, packed furniture in some classrooms, and scattered student groupings. Keep the school fully interior and omit campus grounds.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first, with authoritative plans for the lobby and each school floor, then dress the isometric views; vertical circulation and task coverage must remain clear.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use large scale because two maps include a three-story task environment. Theme: realistic after-hours school with restrained mystery and neglect. Route with `P4` for the isolated lobby and school, `P2` for stacked playable floors, and `CHECK` for the shallow flood volume.
