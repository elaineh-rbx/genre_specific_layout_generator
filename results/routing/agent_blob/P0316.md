# Scene prompt

Create a bright tropical cartoon pirate sea region with the same island-for-island layout as the referenced First Sea: a broad ocean dotted with distinct inhabited islands, each with its own shoreline, settlement, docks, terrain, and landmark structures, connected by open sailing routes.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is RPG, with Adventure secondary. The referenced game is a combat-and-progression RPG whose spatial appeal also depends on exploring distinct islands.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open`: one contiguous sea map whose islands can be visited in flexible order. No preset is an honest fit, because the requested island-for-island replica is more specific than the available generic RPG and exploration bundles.

## Config requirements

Apply `island-cluster` for the distinct landmasses separated by navigable sea; `water-body` for the broad ocean and open sailing channels; `settlement-density` for the inhabited built-up areas on each island; `terrain-relief` for individually recognizable island profiles; and `landmark-focal` for each island’s signature structure. Add visible docks and unobstructed sailing routes as scene-specific geometry.

## Layout requirements

The layout stage needs no invisible gameplay placements stated by this brief. Preserve clear water approaches to every dock and keep landmarks aligned with the corresponding island rather than inventing quest, spawn, or combat markers.

## Layout components

Reproduce the referenced First Sea island-for-island across one broad ocean. Every island is a distinct zone with its matching shoreline silhouette, settlement footprint, dock position, terrain profile, and landmark structures. Open-water paths connect the docks as readable sailing routes while leaving generous navigable channels. Use beaches, tropical rock, varied elevation, and bright vegetation. The outer boundary is open sea. Frame the region from high above so the full island arrangement and route network are legible.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, because fidelity to the island-for-island arrangement and clear sailing topology matters more than a look-first composition.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is huge, driven by an entire multi-island sea region. The theme is bright tropical cartoon piracy. Use `tiered` for raised island terrain and `CHECK` for sailing across a volumetric water play space; no self-occluding underwater layers are requested.
