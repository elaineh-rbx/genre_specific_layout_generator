# Scene prompt
Create a compact, high-quality 3D prehistoric roleplay map roughly 250–300 studs across, designed for several player-controlled T-Rex dinosaurs to roam together. Use simple natural terrain with grassy ground, scattered rocks and trees, and a shallow water area, arranged with open space for walking and running while keeping the environment optimized. Frame the map for a third-person behind-the-dinosaur camera, with clear sightlines and enough open ground to keep a full T-Rex visible.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim: several players inhabit T-Rex avatars and freely roleplay together, with no combat, quests, survival loop, or progression.

## Shape and preset
Use the single `wilderness-open` shape, meaning a compact prehistoric natural world with no settlement and broad ground for dinosaur movement. It comes from the Animal Sim preset, adjusted to omit its unrequested den, landmark, and formal gathering-hub features.

## Config requirements
The image configuration includes `water-body` as one shallow, visibly bounded watering area that fits into the traversable terrain. Draw grassy ground, scattered rocks, sparse prehistoric trees, and generous clearings as scene-specific geometry; keep tree spacing and silhouettes compatible with a full T-Rex viewed from behind.

## Layout requirements
After segmentation, place several player spawn markers on separate patches of open grass around the shared roaming area, far enough apart that multiple T-Rex bodies do not overlap, while preserving immediate sightlines between players. No NPC, pickup, checkpoint, combat, scoring, or trigger placements are needed.

## Layout components
Build one open natural zone 250–300 studs across. A broad central grass clearing supports walking, running, and social clustering; looser grassy routes circulate around scattered rock groups and tree clusters. Put the shallow water area off-center so it creates a destination without splitting the map. Keep terrain gently varied but predominantly open and single-surface. Use a simple natural perimeter of denser trees, rocks, and low terrain shaping. Compose the scene around the central clearing, with the water and foliage framing rather than obscuring the T-Rex-scale play space.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down view, because visual scale, camera clearance, and prehistoric atmosphere lead while topology remains simple.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, fixed by the explicit 250–300-stud width. The theme is realistic but Roblox-friendly prehistoric grassland. Use `CHECK` for the shallow `water-body`; its simple, non-overlapping volume should pass. No other modifier is forced.
