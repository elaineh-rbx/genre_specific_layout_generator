# Scene prompt

Create a small 3D stone colosseum arena sized for four fighters, with an open central combat floor enclosed by tiered stone seating and surrounding arena walls.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Action because four players fight with powers in one shared arena.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-bounded` from the Battlegrounds preset. For this scene it means one clean, open colosseum combat floor enclosed by walls. Keep the preset’s protected-start concept, but omit ring-out edges and line-of-sight cover because the prompt explicitly calls for an enclosed, open central floor.

## Config requirements

Draw the visible screening geometry for `spawn-protected` as four shallow stone starting alcoves spaced around the outer edge of the combat floor. Use `spectator-zone` as tiered stone seating rising outside the playable floor, fully contained by tall colosseum walls. Keep the center free of columns, pits, pickups, and minor tripping geometry. The powers are player abilities available from the start, so no powerup props or pickup stations should appear.

## Layout requirements

For `spawn-protected`, place exactly four player start markers, one inside each perimeter alcove, evenly distributed around the floor and angled toward the center. Keep every start outside direct cross-arena sightlines until its player steps forward. No `powerup-buffs`, NPC spawners, scoring zones, or additional triggers are required.

## Layout components

Build one small circular or oval stone colosseum with a broad, flat central combat zone sized for four fighters. Place four evenly spaced entry alcoves at the combat-floor perimeter. Beyond that floor, raise several tiers of stone spectator seating toward a continuous high outer wall. Separate the seating from play with a low stone barrier that does not obstruct the view into the arena. Keep the arena entirely open to the sky or overhead view, with no roofs, tunnels, balconies, or overhanging playable surfaces. Compose the combat floor as the dominant centered shape and keep all four starts visible in the plan.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative, then derive the top-down image; the colosseum’s stone character and concentric presentation lead, and its topology is simple.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, driven by exactly four fighters. The theme is a simple ancient-stone colosseum for supernatural combat. Use `tiered` for the rising spectator seating; nothing overhangs another playable surface.
