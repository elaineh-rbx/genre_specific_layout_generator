# Scene prompt

Crea un mapa 3D de simulador de viaje en el tiempo centrado en un laboratorio/base destruido del presente, con una máquina del tiempo averiada y grandes portales desbloqueables que conducen a cuatro zonas separadas. Cada era es una zona temática compacta y claramente explorable, con espacio de recolección, su portal y Cápsulas del Tiempo: un valle prehistórico con dinosaurios gigantes, volcanes y energía de ámbar; un Antiguo Egipto con pirámides, oasis y energía mística; una era medieval con castillos imponentes, caballeros y magia antigua; y un futuro ciberpunk con ciudad de neón, autos voladores y energía de plasma.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Simulation because collection, selling, tool upgrades, capacity upgrades, and unlocking new gathering zones form the game’s progression loop.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-chaptered`, outside Simulation’s usual shape set, for a present-day laboratory hub and four genuinely separate era maps reached through portals. The preset is Incremental Simulator, retaining its central hub, progression gates, and circulation while substituting the chaptered time-travel structure for one open world.

## Config requirements

Apply `social-hub` as the ruined present-day laboratory and broken time machine, `gate-progression` as four giant unlockable portals, `path-circulation` as clear movement from each arrival point through its gathering ground, and `resource-shared` as each era’s communal energy field. Apply `terrain-relief` to the prehistoric volcanoes, `water-body` to the Egyptian oasis, and `npc-population` to the giant dinosaurs and medieval knights. The four zones use amber, mystical energy, ancient magic, and plasma as distinct visible resource languages.

## Layout requirements

Place `collectible-nodes` throughout each compact era: amber energy in Prehistory, mystical energy in Egypt, ancient magic in the medieval zone, and plasma in the cyberpunk future. Place four portal pairs, each linking one laboratory gate to exactly one era’s return portal. Place one Time Capsule interaction marker in each of the four eras. Site non-hostile population markers only against the visible dinosaur and knight areas.

## Layout components

Build five separate compact maps total. The present hub is a ruined laboratory containing the damaged time machine and four monumental portal gates. Prehistory contains a walkable valley, giant dinosaurs, volcanoes, and amber gathering ground. Egypt contains pyramids, an oasis, and mystical collection space. The medieval map contains a dominant castle, knights, and ancient-magic ground. The future is a neon cyberpunk city with flying-car scenery and plasma collection space. Each era needs one arrival portal, one Time Capsule location, clear circulation, and enough open ground for gathering. Keep every era visually self-contained and easily readable rather than region-scale.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first as authoritative, then dress the isometric images, because the hub-to-era portal pairing and compact gathering circulation must remain unambiguous.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a large total scale: five maps, each intentionally compact. The theme contrasts ruined present-day science with vivid prehistoric, Egyptian, medieval, and cyberpunk eras. Apply `P4` for separate maps, `tiered` for volcano relief without overhangs, and `CHECK` for the oasis and flying open-air elements.
