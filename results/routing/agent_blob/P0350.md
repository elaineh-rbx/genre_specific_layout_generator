## Enriched image prompt
Create a compact, fully 3D first-person combat district made from a few explorable streets converging on one fixed central defense position. Make every approach wide and clear enough for detailed car- and tank-sized enemy vehicles to drive toward the center, with several surrounding street entrances, readable junction chokepoints, and no disconnected roads. Use a creepy undead urban visual register for the street dressing while keeping all routes legible. The scene is a real walkable 3D environment, not a flat board, top-down game, screen, or UI set.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Shooter, specifically a first-person PvE wave shooter. Survival is secondary because the player holds one position against repeated waves.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-network`, named Lane Network: several vehicle-width streets channel waves from the perimeter toward the central defense point. The preset is PvE Shooter, retaining hostile origins and contested chokepoints while omitting the unrequested enterable-building feature.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle`, taken from Simulation and routed as ordinary streets rather than a race course, for the connected vehicle-width approaches. Use `choke-bottleneck` for selected junctions where incoming vehicle waves concentrate. Use the visible side of `tracker-core`, taken from Strategy, for the fixed central position being defended. The undead cars and tanks are dynamic enemy actors rather than static map geometry.

## Layout requirements
Use `spawner-npc` for hostile vehicle origins at the outer end of every approach street, with each origin aligned to its roadway so cars and tanks enter facing the center. Use `tracker-core` to place the defended objective at the exact street-network center. Place one scene-specific player SpawnZone beside that objective, screened from direct first-frame contact with the perimeter spawners. Enemy models must be unique 3D undead vehicles, never humanoids, sprites, or GUI images.

## Layout components
One small exterior combat map contains a central defense zone, a handful of connected surrounding streets, several perimeter vehicle entrances, and clear junction chokepoints. Roads are broad enough for cars and tanks to turn without clipping, and every road reaches the center. Solid outer blocks bound the few-street district without adding enterable interiors.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: make the connected vehicle approach network, central objective, and aligned spawner directions authoritative before dressing the isometric scene.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is medium because a few streets must fit while vehicle-sized routes and one central defense position stay legible. The theme is creepy undead urban wave combat. The route is `P0`; all traversal occupies one connected exterior surface with no separate maps, interiors, or overhangs.
