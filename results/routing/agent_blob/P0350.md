# Scene prompt
Create a true 3D first-person wave-survival combat map set in a compact, explorable town section spanning a few streets. Place a fixed defended position at the central intersection, with surrounding streets radiating toward it as clear vehicle approach routes; arrange buildings and street-side structures around the perimeter to frame the combat area while leaving sightlines down each road. The player occupies the central ground-level scene, facing creepy undead-themed enemy vehicles such as cars and tanks that drive inward from the surrounding streets; each vehicle is a distinct detailed 3D model rather than a humanoid character or flat sprite.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Shooter is dominant, with Survival secondary because this is first-person wave combat built around holding a position.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-network`: the few surrounding streets act as readable vehicle lanes converging on the central defended intersection. The preset is PvE Shooter, retaining hostile origins and the central street bottleneck while dropping its enterable structure because perimeter buildings are only framing façades.

## Config requirements
Use `choke-bottleneck` for the central intersection where the vehicle lanes meet and concentrate the defense. Use `settlement-density` for a compact town section of a few streets, with perimeter buildings and street-side structures preserving long sightlines down every road. Draw a varied set of distinct detailed undead cars and tanks, each visibly creepy and vehicle-shaped rather than humanoid or sprite-based.

## Layout requirements
Use `spawner-npc` as hostile vehicle wave origins: place one origin at the outer end of each approach street, aligned to drive inward toward the intersection. Place one unkeyed player position at the fixed central defense point with clear visibility into all incoming lanes.

## Layout components
The central zone is one broad ground-level intersection containing the defended position. A few streets radiate outward as direct vehicle approaches, ending at the map boundary and connecting only through the center. Low street furniture and perimeter façades frame each lane without blocking vehicle width or sightlines. The terrain is flat paved roadway with sidewalks; buildings form the outer boundary. Compose the view from near the defended center, showing several inbound roads and representative undead vehicles.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: render the street plan first so every vehicle lane connects cleanly from an outer origin to the defended intersection, then dress that authoritative topology isometrically.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, spanning only a few streets. The theme is a compact modern town overrun by grotesque undead vehicles. The route is `P0`; all roads share one visible ground surface, with no enterable interiors, overhang-dependent play, or separate maps.
