## Enriched image prompt
Create three small, separately loaded story maps. Chapter one is an ordinary hometown with a short street route to the player’s house and a simple family interior. Chapter two is a new town centered on an enterable school containing a classroom, lunchroom, principal’s office area, interconnected chase corridors, one hidden key-like item, locked doors, and a clear escape exit leading back toward home; glitch corruption begins around the teacher and affected student. Chapter three begins as an office but transforms into an authoritative corrupted-school encounter space with room for the teacher, assembled students and staff, and one glitchy ally, ending at a visible “Part 2 soon” sign. Keep each map compact and the chapter sequence unmistakable.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure because this is an explicitly ordered multiplayer story. Survival is secondary because an AI teacher pursues the players through the school.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-chaptered` for exactly three separate maps. Start from the Story preset, retaining forward chapter thresholds and one-way progression while dropping its structural-reveal option because the office-to-school change is a state transformation rather than a passage opening onto a vista.

## Config requirements
Use `gate-chapter` and `gate-oneway` at the two forward transitions between three maps, `building-interior` for the house and school entered from their towns, and the visible half of `gate-escape` for the locked school exit. Use the visible half of `npc-population` for one glitchy ally in the finale. The classroom, lunchroom, principal area, corridors, office, corrupted finale, and ending sign are direct requirements.

## Layout requirements
Place one `collectible-nodes` key-like item along the chapter-two school route and the functional half of `gate-escape` at its exit. Place `spawner-npc` for the teacher in chapter two and for the teacher, students, and staff in the finale. Place the layout half of `npc-population` once for the glitchy ally in the finale. Place three `checkpoint-respawn` markers, one at each chapter start. Dialogue, impossible-question logic, police calls, and transformation timing add no geometry.

## Layout components
Exactly three map segments connect only forward. Hometown uses `settlement-static` for a short ordinary street and family house. New Town and School uses `warren-looping` for the dominant interconnected chase corridors, classroom, lunchroom, principal area, one unlock item, and one escape door, with no dead-end trap. Office-to-School Finale uses `space-bounded` for one compact corrupted encounter footprint beneath an initial office dressing. Each map has a clear boundary.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: chapter-two escape circulation and exit reachability must be coherent before glitch-horror dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small per map, driven by rooms, corridors, and story props rather than a large town extent. The theme shifts from ordinary contemporary town life to glitch horror. Use `P4` for three maps, `P3` for town-to-building transitions, and `P6` so the school’s `warren-looping` chase network has no dead ends.
