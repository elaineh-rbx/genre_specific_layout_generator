# Scene prompt
Create a multiplayer story-game layout across separate loading places: a small hometown with the player's house, then a new town centered on a school. The school includes a lunch area, classrooms, principal's office, connecting corridors, and locked exit doors, with spaces for players to flee through as the normal school becomes unsettling and glitchy. After escape, return to the hometown and house, then load into an office that transforms into a distorted version of the school for the finale, populated by students and staff and joined by a glitchy helper character.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure, with Survival secondary: the authored story progression leads, while an AI teacher chase and escape structure materially shape the school.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-chaptered` because the hometown, new town and school, and office-to-school finale are separate loading places. The preset is Story, with its chapter structure retained and its shape matching the brief.

## Config requirements
Use `gate-chapter` for each load transition, `settlement-density` for the compact hometown and new-town school surroundings, `building-interior` for the house and school spaces, `gate-escape` for the locked school exits, and `npc-population` for students, staff, the principal, and the glitchy helper. Give the school readable lunch, classroom, corridor, and office spaces, then reuse and distort its visual language in the finale.

## Layout requirements
Place group spawn markers at the hometown house, school arrival, and finale office. Place `spawner-npc` once for the teacher's school chase origin and additional hostile origins around the transformed finale school. Place the `gate-escape` trigger at the locked exterior doors and one unlock-object pickup on a reachable corridor branch. Place character markers for students and staff in the school and finale, with the single glitchy helper near the players' finale arrival.

## Layout components
Build three loaded places: a small hometown focused on one house; a compact new town centered on a school with lunch area, classrooms, principal's office, looping connecting corridors, and locked exits; and an office that transforms into a distorted school arena. Return routing revisits the hometown rather than creating another copy. Keep chase corridors broad and interconnected, with furniture and corners breaking sightlines.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, then dress the isometric views, because escape circulation and chase-route connectivity must remain playable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large across several compact places. The theme shifts from ordinary small-town school to glitch horror. Use `P4` for separate loaded maps and `P3` for exterior-to-interior travel through the house and school.
