# Scene prompt

Create a 3D indoor birthday party scene in a small party hall sized for a handful of players. Decorate the room with colorful balloons and streamers, with presents and a birthday cake arranged as the central party setup.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Entertainment, specifically a small social hangout intended to be occupied and enjoyed rather than won.

## Shape and preset

Use `interior-single`: the whole map is one enclosed party hall, with no exterior or outside-to-inside transition. The preset is Indoor Venue, pared back to the requested gathering space without adding graphics set pieces or separate photo installations.

## Config requirements

Use `social-hub` as the open central floor where a handful of players can gather around the birthday setup without blocking the room entrance. Draw colorful balloons and streamers around the walls and ceiling line, a central cake table, and a nearby grouping of wrapped presents. Keep the decorative density cheerful but appropriate to a small hall.

## Layout requirements

No specialized triggers, scoring volumes, pickups, checkpoints, or teleporters are needed because the scene is purely a hangout. A normal player spawn may be placed just inside the hall entrance, oriented toward the cake table, but it does not require a genre-specific option.

## Layout components

Build one small rectangular party room with a flat floor, enclosed walls, a ceiling, and one visible entrance defining the boundary. Leave a compact open circulation area through the center. Make one birthday table with one cake the main focal setup, with presents grouped beside or beneath it. Arrange balloon clusters near corners and the focal table, and run streamers along the walls and overhead edges. Add only enough simple party-hall furniture to support a handful of guests, keeping the central gathering area clear. Compose the room so the entrance view immediately frames the cake, presents, and colorful decorations.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative, then derive the top-down plan; the decorative focal composition and friendly room atmosphere lead, while the one-room topology is trivial.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a small scale, explicitly sized for a handful of players. The theme is a simple, bright indoor birthday party with colorful balloons, streamers, gifts, and cake. Use `P0` because the entire build is one flat interior and no transition or overlapping level is required.
