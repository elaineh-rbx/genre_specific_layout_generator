# Scene prompt
Create a 3D present-day city map for free driving, with an open street grid of asphalt roads between concrete buildings. Place a drivable car on the streets, and use simple cube-based props throughout the city.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The genre is Simulation, specifically free vehicle operation without laps, checkpoints, deliveries, or a race finish.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open` with the Vehicle Sim preset. Keep its shared persistent city and vehicle-width road network, but drop `resource-shared` because there is no extraction or production activity. The city is one contiguous free-roam map.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for a connected asphalt street grid wide enough for comfortable driving and turning. Use `settlement-density` for a present-day city of concrete building blocks arranged around that grid; the buildings are exterior street scenery, not enterable interiors. Render one drivable car on a street and distribute simple cube-based props such as barriers, streetlights, planters, signs, and utility boxes without obstructing the carriageways.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly one vehicle spawn and interaction position on a clear street segment, aligned with traffic direction and surrounded by enough room to enter and begin driving. Nothing else needs placement after segmentation: there are no checkpoints, timed gates, deliveries, resource nodes, or race triggers.

## Layout components
Build one large city zone with a connected grid of broad asphalt roads, regular intersections, sidewalks, and concrete building blocks. Include a few wider avenues and smaller cross streets so drivers can choose routes and loop back without leaving the map. Put the single car on a clear curbside or lane-start position, not inside an intersection. Arrange cube-built buildings as readable perimeter blocks with varied heights but no overhanging roads or interiors. Place simple cube props mainly on sidewalks and corners, preserving sightlines and turn clearance. Use outer building walls and blocked street ends as the map boundary. Compose the frame to show the car in the foreground and several connected streets extending through the city.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: draw the road plan first and treat it as authoritative so intersections connect and free-driving loops remain usable, then dress that plan as the present-day city.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a large scale to support meaningful free driving across several blocks. The theme is present-day asphalt and concrete with deliberately simple cube-built props. Route through `P0`; one exterior surface with non-enterable buildings requires no additional modifier.
