# Scene prompt
Create a fully 3D, third-person explorable aura-collector map as a small single place. A central Spawn Hub contains a roll-station platform, NPC vendors, and a crafting building or room with a workbench NPC. Five connected physical biome regions ring the hub—Normal, Windy, Rainy, Snowy, and Starfall—with short walkable paths radiating outward to each region. Give each biome distinct terrain, atmospheric weather particles, sky and lighting character, and scatter collectible material nodes along the paths and throughout the regions. Keep the hub as the clear central focal point, with the five biome entrances visibly arranged around it for easy navigation.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation, matching the self-declared incremental RNG collector; Adventure is secondary because physical exploration and material discovery across the biome ring are meaningful.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open` as one contiguous place with a central hub and five freely visited regions. The closest preset is Incremental Simulator, retaining its central progression storefront and circulation, dropping unrequested physical purchase gates, and adding the collector’s roll, crafting, and exploration features.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for the central Spawn Hub with vendors and roll platform. Use `path-circulation` for five short radial paths. Use `trigger-task` in drawn form for exactly one roll-station platform and one crafting workbench. Use `npc-population` for the hub vendors and workbench NPC. Use `building-interior` for an enterable crafting room linked to the hub exterior. Each of the five named biomes needs visibly distinct terrain and atmospheric dressing.

## Layout requirements
Place two `trigger-task` interactions: one on the roll platform and one on the crafting workbench. Place `npc-population` anchors behind vendor counters and at the workbench. Scatter `collectible-nodes` along all five radial paths and within every biome region, keeping them reachable from walkable ground. Place one spawn marker at the hub center facing the biome entrances.

## Layout components
Build a compact circular hub as the focal zone, with vendor positions, roll platform, and a small crafting room. Arrange exactly five outer regions in a ring—Normal, Windy, Rainy, Snowy, and Starfall—and connect each directly to the hub with a short walkable path. Give every region a distinct ground treatment, weather-particle character, lighting accent, and clear entrance while preserving one continuous map. Bound the place beyond the biome ring with non-walkable scenery. Frame all five spokes legibly around the hub.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, then dress the isometric image, because all five regions must connect cleanly to the central hub and remain distinct.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale for one compact hub plus five small biomes. The theme is magical 3D aura-collector fantasy with strongly differentiated weather regions. Route through `P3` because players cross from the outdoor hub into the crafting interior.
