# Scene prompt

Create a 3D open-world simulation map set in a contemporary city spanning a few interconnected blocks. Arrange streets, sidewalks, and accessible building exteriors with paintable walls and doors, plus interior or storefront spaces containing books and other everyday paintable surfaces.

# Agent decision

## Genre

Simulation is the sole genre. The player performs a repeatable painting activity across a persistent open-world city slice rather than racing, fighting, or roleplaying without a task loop.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open`, one contiguous cluster of city blocks that players traverse in any order. Use the Role Sim preset, retaining its shared-world task stations and circulation while dropping its unrequested vehicle-width hauling roads; ordinary city streets remain part of the scene rather than a simulation option.

## Config requirements

Draw `settlement-density` as a compact contemporary city of a few interconnected blocks. Draw `path-circulation` as connected sidewalks, crossings, and streets linking all paintable locations. Draw `building-interior` as accessible storefront rooms entered from the sidewalk, containing books and other everyday objects. Draw the visible half of `trigger-task` as clearly recognizable paintable surfaces: broad wall panels, doors, books, signs, shelving, and small household objects. Preserve real-world paint-brand context through believable retail paint displays without relying on flat UI.

## Layout requirements

Place functional `trigger-task` paint targets on the designated wall, door, book, and object surfaces throughout each block. Match placement counts to the generated surfaces because the prompt gives no exact total. Every target must be reachable from a sidewalk or within an accessible storefront, and no target may be hidden behind permanent geometry. Exterior and interior targets should be distributed across all blocks rather than concentrated at one shop.

## Layout components

Build a few contiguous city blocks with a connected street grid, sidewalks, crosswalks, alleys, storefront frontages, and accessible ground-floor shops. Exterior paint zones include side walls, shutters, and doors; interior zones contain shelving, books, display tables, and paintable everyday props. Buildings form the boundary around streets while the outer edge of the city slice is closed by continued facade frontage. Keep terrain level and urban. Compose from an elevated three-quarter angle that shows the block connectivity while cutting into at least one representative storefront.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: the visual character and distribution of paintable surfaces lead, and the top-down plan can be derived from that authoritative city view.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is large, driven by several walkable blocks. Theme is grounded contemporary city and paint retail. Use `P3` because play crosses between streets and storefront interiors.
