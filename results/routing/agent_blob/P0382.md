# Scene prompt

Create a 3D outer-space flight course viewed from the side, with gameplay confined to a single vertical plane. A small flying bird character passes through a forward sequence of space-themed obstacles against an open cosmic backdrop.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant and sole genre is Infinite Runner. Forward progress is endless, score derives from distance, and the player reacts to a scrolling obstacle sequence.

## Shape and preset

Use `lane-free` as the single shape, interpreted as continuous vertical steering within one narrow side-view plane rather than snapping among fixed lanes. Match the Free Runner preset: modular course chunks, clearable obstacle gaps, and collision hazards all describe this 3D Flappy-style run.

## Config requirements

Use `chunk-modular` for side-scrolling space segments whose forward entry and exit pivots align exactly. Use `obstacle-jump` for repeated paired space obstacles with one traversable vertical gap between them. Use `hazard-kill` for the solid obstacle bodies that end a run on contact. Dress the corridor with three-dimensional cosmic rocks, structures, or energy formations against an open starfield while keeping gameplay on a single plane.

## Layout requirements

Place one `trigger-despawn` cleanup volume a fixed distance behind the side-view camera and active segment queue. For every obstacle pair, place the clear gap at a reachable vertical offset and preserve speed-dependent reaction spacing between successive pairs. No `collectible-nodes`, power-ups, checkpoints, lap regions, or terminal finish trigger are needed.

## Layout components

Build a repeating horizontal queue of compact course chunks in one narrow vertical gameplay plane. Each chunk contains open flight space and a readable pair of upper and lower space-themed obstacles framing one gap. Vary gap height without introducing depth-layer routes, intersections, or dead ends. Keep the bird’s silhouette and upcoming opening clear against stars and distant cosmic scenery. The frame should read from the side, with forward travel moving consistently across it.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: establish the deterministic chunk and obstacle sequence first, then dress it as the authoritative side-presented 3D space course. Structural validity leads because every generated gap and interval must remain clearable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale band is small per chunk, with an endless assembled course. The theme is bright 3D outer-space arcade flight. Use `P6` for exact chunk alignment and valid speed-aware obstacle spacing. No `CHECK` is required because movement is confined to a single plane rather than a self-occluding volume.
