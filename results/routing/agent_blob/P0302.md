# Scene prompt
Crea un mapa 3D de una ciudad moderna, realista y sencilla, grande pero manejable, extendida a lo largo de varios bloques urbanos. Organiza calles entre edificios altos y rascacielos, con una composición densa que ofrezca fachadas, azoteas y alturas para recorrer la ciudad balanceándose libremente. El borde exterior del mapa queda delimitado por límites invisibles.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Action is the dominant and only genre. Free physical traversal through the city is the complete activity; the absence of missions does not erase the traversal game.

## Shape and preset
Use `traversal-city`: rooftops, façades, ledges, and gaps are the playable movement network, while connected streets provide a continuous fallback route below. Use the Parkour Traversal preset because its defining city-crossing shape fits, but omit its combat cover, chokepoint, and lethal-edge options because the prompt asks only for free swinging.

## Config requirements
Use `settlement-density` for several dense modern city blocks containing tall buildings and skyscrapers rather than isolated towers. Keep architecture simple and realistic, with varied façade planes, rooflines, setbacks, and heights that supply readable swing anchors. No combat, shop, interior, collectible, or objective option is requested.

## Layout requirements
Place one player arrival marker on a broad central rooftop or plaza with immediate sight of several viable swing directions and a safe route down to street level. Place an invisible boundary volume around the outer map edge, aligned beyond the last playable façades and rooftops so players cannot leave while the city still appears to continue. No checkpoints, enemies, pickups, scoring zones, or task triggers are needed.

## Layout components
Build one contiguous city spanning several blocks. Lay out a connected street grid with intersections and sidewalks beneath dense clusters of tall buildings and skyscrapers. Vary building spacing and height so swing arcs can chain across façades, around corners, and between rooftops without relying on one prescribed route. Preserve open street corridors and occasional plazas as low recovery spaces. The visible boundary should be extended by distant skyline massing, while the actual outer edge is the invisible limit. Compose the initial frame from a central elevated point across layered roof heights, with multiple nearby anchors and taller towers drawing the eye deeper into the map.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the architectural silhouettes, believable density, and layered swing-height composition lead, and there is no objective topology or fixed course that requires a plan-first render.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large because the brief requests a manageable but expansive multi-block city with skyscrapers. The theme is simple, grounded modern realism that keeps free swinging visually dominant. Use `P2` because rooftop and façade traversal surfaces overhang streets and lower building levels.
