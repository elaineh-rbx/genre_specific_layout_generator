# Scene prompt

Create a 3D army no-man’s-land battlefield map with a 1000x1000-stud playable area. Lay out exposed, war-torn open ground with hostile soldier NPC positions distributed across the battlefield, providing opposing firing approaches within the playable bounds. Keep any border or invisible limits at or beyond the map’s edge.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Shooter: the player is an army soldier firing on hostile Robloxian NPCs that return fire across a battlefield.

## Shape and preset

Use `open-battlefield`, replacing the lane-based shape associated with the PvE Shooter preset because the prompt explicitly describes exposed open ground with distributed hostile positions rather than defined lanes. The preset is none: after changing its shape and omitting its interior and chokepoint features, only its hostile-emitter concept remains.

## Config requirements

Draw a 1000-by-1000-stud no-man’s-land: scarred earth, shallow shell craters, broken ground, and sparse ruined battlefield debris. Keep the land broadly open so opposing firing approaches remain legible, without adding buildings, trenches, shops, or other unrequested geometry. The border should be visually minimal and sit only at the playable edge or beyond it.

## Layout requirements

Use `spawner-npc` for hostile soldier origins distributed across the battlefield. Place several emitters in separated groups across the far and side regions, with each group facing inward toward readable firing approaches and none immediately adjacent to the player’s entry side. The prompt gives no NPC count, so do not invent one. Place one continuous invisible boundary around the 1000-by-1000-stud playable square at its edge.

## Layout components

Build one huge, contiguous square battlefield exactly 1000 studs wide and 1000 studs long. The central zone is exposed war-torn ground with widely spaced craters and low debris that texture the scene without turning it into lanes or dense cover. Leave broad approaches from the near side toward hostile positions across the center, far side, and flanks. Keep all playable terrain outdoors on one surface and frame the complete square from a high angle so its openness and distributed enemy fronts read clearly.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative, then derive the top-down image; atmosphere and battlefield dressing lead, while exact route topology is not structurally constrained.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is huge because the playable area is explicitly 1000 by 1000 studs. The theme is bleak, exposed, war-torn no-man’s-land. Use `P0` because the whole battlefield is one exterior surface with no overhangs or interior transitions.
