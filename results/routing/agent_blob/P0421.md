# Scene prompt
Create a compact 3D flooded-temple treasure-hunt map built around one clear, directed route with simple forward progression and no backtracking. The entrance and first crossing are plainly visible at the start, leading through water-filled temple chambers and a dangerous traversal section toward three relic locations arranged sequentially along the path. Place the exit beyond the final relic area, visibly sealed until all three relics are retrieved. Keep the full route short enough for a couple-minute run, with water or lower recovery areas beneath hazardous crossings.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure: this is a compact directed treasure-hunt journey with sequential objectives and an exit.

## Shape and preset
Use `route-guided`, one continuous temple route that communicates forward progression and does not require backtracking. The matched preset is Guided Trail, retaining its directed shape and clear `path-trail`, then adding the relic, gate, hazard, and recovery requirements stated by the brief.

## Config requirements
Use `path-trail` for the plainly readable route through connected flooded chambers, `gate-progression` for the visibly sealed exit beyond the final relic, `hazard-kill` for dangerous crossings above lower recovery areas, and `water-body` for the flooded chambers and water beneath crossings. The entrance and first crossing must be obvious from the starting composition.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly three `collectible-nodes`, one at each sequential relic location along the route. Place `checkpoint-respawn` recovery points immediately after major hazardous crossings so a fall causes only a short replay; the lower recovery areas must feed back to the latest safe point. The exit gate’s unlock volume sits after relic three and requires all three relic placements.

## Layout components
Build a short sequence of flooded temple chambers joined by one unmistakable forward path. Start with an entrance zone that exposes the first crossing, progress through dangerous elevated or broken-stone traversals over water or lower recovery floors, then pass three distinct relic sites in order. End at a sealed architectural exit beyond the third site. Enclose the route in temple walls and frame it from entrance toward the first objective.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, then dress the isometric image from that plan, because a disconnected crossing or bypass around the three sequential relics would make the run unplayable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by a couple-minute end-to-end run. The theme is ancient flooded temple adventure. Use `CHECK` for the water volume; no outside-to-inside transition or separate map is required.
