# Scene prompt

Create a 3D endless-runner map with a forward-running path that procedurally passes through original adventure environments: ancient jungle ruins, mountain temples, lava and underground caverns, ice canyons, desert ruins, floating islands, bamboo forests, waterfalls, and forgotten cities. Build each biome as a short, readable 60–90-second stretch, joined by brief blended transition segments. Arrange a clear central route with collectible trails and varied traversable hazards including gaps, broken or collapsing bridges, fallen trees, rolling boulders, rotating blades, swinging axes, spike and fire traps, crumbling floors, moving platforms, narrow ledges, climbing sections, rope swings, and zip lines. About every 20–30 seconds, split the route into branching paths that rejoin after short alternate segments. Use dramatic terrain, animated foliage, water, weather, fog, dynamic lighting, and day-to-night atmosphere, with a pursuing original creature visible behind the runner during chase sections.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant and sole genre is Infinite Runner. Adventure is the visual register, but automatic endless forward movement and reaction-timed procedural hazards define the game.

## Shape and preset

Use `lane-free` for continuous steering along a route that turns, forks, and rejoins. Match the Free Runner preset and expand it with moving hazards, collectible trails, power-ups, and chase pressure explicitly requested by the author.

## Config requirements

Use `chunk-modular` for axis-aligned procedural segments and `obstacle-jump` for gaps, broken bridges, trees, spikes, fire traps, and crumbling floors. Use `obstacle-moving` for boulders, blades, axes, platforms, and collapsing bridges. Use `hazard-kill` for lethal obstacles and the pursuing creature. Draw `terrain-relief`, `water-body`, and `island-cluster` as dramatic mountains, canyons, waterfalls, caverns, and floating islands. Blend original jungle ruins, mountain temples, lava caverns, ice canyons, desert ruins, bamboo forests, waterfalls, forgotten cities, and underground spaces.

## Layout requirements

Place `collectible-nodes` as coins, gems, crystals, chests, relics, keys, and event trails along readable safe and risk-reward lines. Place `powerup-buffs` in visible approach lanes. Place one `trigger-despawn` behind the active queue. Fork the route about every 20–30 seconds and rejoin branches after short alternates. Keep obstacle spacing speed-relative and every generated pattern clearable.

## Layout components

Generate 60–90-second biome stretches linked by brief blended transitions. Each stretch needs one central traversable route, occasional short rejoining branches, terrain and architecture unique to its biome, and selected hazards from the full requested set, including narrow ledges, climbs, rope swings, and zip lines. Keep water, foliage, fog, weather, lighting, and day-to-night atmosphere compositionally readable, with the pursuer visible behind during chase segments.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: validate chunk connections, branches, rejoins, and hazard clearance first, then dress the plan isometrically. The plan is authoritative because an impossible generated sequence ends the run unfairly.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale band is huge because the endless route rotates through nine substantial environments. The theme is premium original adventure spectacle. Use `P6` for valid procedural structure, `P2` for overhanging caverns and traversal structures, `P3` for outdoor-to-cavern transitions, and `CHECK` for layered open-air and water play around floating islands.
