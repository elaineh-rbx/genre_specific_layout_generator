## Genre
The dominant genre is Infinite Runner because forward movement is automatic through an endless generated path.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-free`: tilt and swipe input steer continuously across the corridor rather than snapping among fixed lanes. The matched preset is Free Runner, extended with chase pressure, moving hazards, branching chunks, and many biome sets.

## Config requirements
Use `chunk-modular` for deterministic chunks with matching pivots and fair branch-and-rejoin variants. Use `obstacle-jump`, `obstacle-moving`, and `hazard-kill` for all stated static, moving, and run-ending obstacles. Use `barrier-horizon` to hide the live chunk frontier. Use universal `terrain-relief` for mountain, canyon, and ruin stretches without overhang. Use `reveal-exit`, taken from Adventure's catalogue row, for blended outdoor-to-cavern transitions. Use `marker-distance` for subtle environmental progress increments.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` Collectibles for all seven stated collectible families along safe and risky lines. Place `powerup-buffs` PowerUps for all nine stated boost families. Place one `spawner-npc` Spawner, taken from Survival's catalogue row, behind the runner for the original pursuing creature. Place one `trigger-despawn` TriggerZone two full modular chunks behind the camera. Input, scoring, missions, progression, UI, audio, saves, and device controls are mechanics.

## Layout components
The build is an endless queue of compatible path chunks, including straight, turn, fork, rejoin, obstacle, and blended-biome transition pieces. Each 60–90-second biome stretch draws from one of the ten named environments. Branches appear about every 20–30 seconds and always rejoin after a short alternate segment. The corridor boundary is formed by each biome's terrain, ruins, cliff edges, or cavern walls, with the active horizon occluded.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the endless chunk pivots, speed-legal obstacle sequence, forks, and rejoins must be valid before each biome is dressed. `authored_plan` is unavailable because this is neither a supported maze nor a racing route.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is medium per active chunk, while endless extent is created by streaming the queue; individual obstacles and pickups must remain legible at speed. The theme is original cinematic adventure spanning ten natural and ruin biomes. The pipeline requires `P6` for structurally valid endless generation, `tiered` for mountain and canyon relief, and `P3` for transitions between outdoor stretches and underground cavern interiors.
