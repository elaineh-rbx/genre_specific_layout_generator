# Scene prompt
Create a bright, cartoony pet-collection map made of 12 connected, small yard-sized zones, each with its own simple visual look. Arrange the areas as a clear progression path, with eggs and breakable lucky blocks placed throughout each zone, and a visible gateway or boundary leading to the next area. The gateways require increasing pet totals to open: 1 pet for area 2, rising by 1 per area until area 12 requires 11 pets.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation: collecting pets and breaking reward blocks unlock an incremental chain of areas.

## Shape and preset
Use `tier-ladder` as exactly 12 small yard-sized zones in one ordered progression. The preset is Incremental Simulator, replacing its open-world shape with the prompt’s explicit tier ladder while retaining progression gates and circulation.

## Config requirements
Use `path-circulation` as one readable route through all 12 zones. Draw `gate-progression` as 11 full-width gateways, each visibly labeled with its required pet total. Make `trigger-task` the egg-hatching stones or stands in every zone. Use `physics-rig` for visibly breakable lucky blocks distributed throughout each yard. Give every zone its own simple visual identity while preserving one bright cartoon art style.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `trigger-task` at every rendered egg stand. Place `collectible-nodes` at the lucky-block positions so all blocks can issue rewards and respawn without obstructing the route. Add one unlock trigger to each gateway: area 2 requires 1 pet, area 3 requires 2, continuing by one per gate until area 12 requires 11 pets. There are exactly 11 gate triggers.

## Layout components
Build exactly 12 compact yard-sized zones in a continuous chain, numbered from the starting yard to area 12. Each contains eggs, several lucky blocks, open movement space, and a direct view of the next gate. Eleven visible boundaries separate consecutive areas. Keep the ground mostly flat with short paths, low themed decorations, and distinct color or prop motifs per yard. Side fences enclose the chain and prevent bypassing gates. Compose the map as a clear forward sequence rather than a branching world.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: draw the ordered 12-zone plan first so every yard connects exactly once and no gate can be bypassed, then dress the authoritative plan isometrically.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a large scale band because 12 small zones collectively create a long progression map. The theme is bright, cartoony, and varied by simple zone motifs. Apply `P0`; everything remains on one flat continuous surface.
