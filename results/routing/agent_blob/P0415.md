# Scene prompt

Create a small, clean modern hospital on a single playable floor. Arrange a central reception and monitoring area with security camera screens, connected by corridors to a handful of patient examination or treatment rooms containing patients, including subtly unsettling anomalous-looking patients. Include a medicine storage room or wall of shelves stocked with many medicines, with a clear treatment area nearby.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Simulation because players perform a defined hospital job loop: monitor patients, identify anomalies, and treat normal and anomalous patients differently.

## Shape and preset

Use `interior-single`, taken from outside Simulation’s usual shape set, because the entire map is one enclosed, single-floor hospital and players never cross to an exterior. The preset is Role Sim, retaining repeatable task stations and walkable circulation while replacing its open-world shape and dropping vehicle roads.

## Config requirements

Apply `trigger-task` to visible examination and treatment stations tailored to patient diagnosis and care. Apply `path-circulation` as short, legible hospital corridors connecting reception, monitoring, patient rooms, medicine storage, and treatment. Apply `npc-population` to the normal and subtly anomalous patients occupying the rooms. Include a bank of security-camera screens facing the monitoring position and shelves visibly stocked with many medicines.

## Layout requirements

Place `trigger-task` interaction areas at each examination or treatment position, aligned to beds, chairs, or diagnostic equipment with standing clearance for the player. Place `npc-population` markers in the handful of patient rooms, including both normal and anomalous-looking patients, and ensure every monitored room corresponds clearly to a security-camera feed. No enemy spawners or combat volumes are needed.

## Layout components

Build one compact single-floor hospital interior. Put reception and the security monitoring desk at the center, with a small number of short corridors branching to a handful of examination or treatment rooms. Add one medicine storage room, or a clearly bounded medicine wall, packed with readable shelves and located beside a dedicated treatment area. Furnish patient rooms with beds or examination chairs, clinical counters, and camera coverage. Keep doors and corridors wide, sightlines clear, and travel distances short. The outer hospital walls form the boundary; no exterior is rendered. Compose the frame to reveal the central monitoring area and the surrounding room relationships.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first as authoritative, then derive top-down. The clean clinical appearance and subtle contrast between ordinary and anomalous patients lead, while the one-floor room network is simple.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a small scale because the brief specifies one floor and only a handful of rooms. The theme is a clean modern clinical hospital made unsettling by anomalous patients and surveillance. Route through `P0`: one non-stacked interior needs no outside transition, overhang pass, or computed topology.
