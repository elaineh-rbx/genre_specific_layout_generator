# Scene prompt
Create a compact 5v5 tactical-shooter bomb-defusal map set in a fictional war-damaged, dense urban district. Arrange tight streets and alleys between visibly damaged buildings, with clear lanes converging on two distinct objective-site courtyards or building interiors; include rubble, broken walls, and partially collapsed structures as physical cover and route connections.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Shooter, with no secondary genre. Bomb defusal and 5v5 tactical lane control are the decisive signals.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-network`, with several tight urban approaches linking opposing bases to two objective sites. The preset is Bomb Defusal. Make one site a courtyard and the other an enterable damaged-building interior so the two sites play and read distinctly.

## Config requirements
Use `capture-zone` for exactly two visible bomb sites, `choke-bottleneck` where alleys and breaches compress each approach, `cover-los` for rubble piles, broken masonry, low walls, and full-height ruined corners, and `spawn-teambase` for two shielded bases sized for five players each. Add `settlement-density` for the compact block of dense urban streets and `building-interior` for the one breachable objective building entered from outside.

## Layout requirements
Place two functional `capture-zone` volumes, one in the courtyard and one inside the objective building. Place two `spawn-teambase` markers at opposite ends, each supporting a five-player team and fully screened from direct enemy sightlines. Site the lanes so both teams have readable primary and alternate approaches to each objective without a direct spawn-to-site firing line.

## Layout components
Build two compact team-base zones, a dense middle district of tight streets and alleys, one rubble-filled objective courtyard, and one damaged interior objective room. Connect them with clear main lanes plus short broken-wall breaches that function as alternate route links. Fill corners and firing corridors with collapsed masonry, wrecked facades, barricades, and partial wall remnants as calibrated cover. Keep inaccessible upper ruins as silhouettes rather than extra play floors. Enclose the small map with continuous damaged urban blocks and compose it overhead-diagonally so both sites, lane convergence, and spawn separation remain legible.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: lane connectivity, balanced access to both sites, and protected spawn sightlines must be settled before the authoritative isometric dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by compact 5v5 rounds and exactly two sites. The theme is a fictional, war-damaged dense urban district. Use `P3` because play crosses from streets into the objective-building interior; no `P2` is needed because collapsed upper structures are not traversable.
