# Scene prompt
Create a bright, stylised 3D obstacle-course map layout with 3 selectable parkour courses. Each course is a compact tower rising 8–10 parkour sections high, with a clear finish area at the top and a lava-filled lower level beneath the climbing route.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Obby & Platformer, `obby-platformer`, with Survival, `survival`, secondary because each climb is a race away from rising lava.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-tower` for each of the three separately selectable vertical courses. Tower Obby is the matching preset, extended with course-selection teleporters and the survival hazard.

## Config requirements
Draw three valid `path-track` climbs, each exactly 8–10 short sections tall. Use `obstacle-jump`, `obstacle-moving`, and `obstacle-climb` to make each route distinct and readable. Draw `hazard-kill` as a lava-filled lower level beneath every tower, visible around the climbing route. Draw three summit `winner-zone` platforms and three visible `teleporter-link` gateways from a compact selection area.

## Layout requirements
Place three `teleporter-link` destinations, one at each tower base, aligned to the three selection gateways. Place three `winner-zone` triggers, one on each summit. Place one rising lava damage volume in each course, totaling three, beginning at the lower lava level and moving upward through that tower’s climb.

## Layout components
Build one bright course-selection platform with three clearly differentiated gateways, leading to three isolated compact towers. Each tower has 8–10 ascending obstacle sections, safe intermediate landings, open sides, a lava basin below, and a broad finish platform at the top. Give the courses distinct color families and obstacle silhouettes while retaining one clean stylized Roblox language. Keep the critical paths singular and unobscured; lava surrounds lower structures without covering the initial spawn pads. Frame each tower from base to summit, with the selection platform presented as a separate overview space.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: each legal obstacle chain and its separation from the rising hazard must be authoritative before isometric dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large because there are three separate 8–10-section towers. The theme is bright, approachable stylized parkour over vivid lava. `P6` guarantees valid climbs, `P2` handles stacked tower surfaces, and `P4` handles the three isolated selectable course maps.
