# Scene prompt

Create a true 3D third-person obstacle-course parkour map in a clean, colorful cartoon style. A compact course of roughly 10–15 distinct elevated obstacle sections progresses from a clear start platform through platforms separated by real gaps, rising and shifting through space toward a visible finish line; include a few checkpoint platforms spaced along the route. Use bright, readable 3D platform geometry and clearly visible hazards such as spinning bars, sliding blocks, and disappearing-tile sections, with open fall space beneath the course rather than a flat baseplate.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Obby & Platformer, centered on deliberate third-person jumping through a finite obstacle sequence.

## Shape and preset

Use `course-terraced`: the course climbs through open space with strong vertical gain but no stacked overhangs. The Classic Obby preset applies, with its usual flat shape replaced by this ascending form and `obstacle-timing` added for the disappearing tiles.

## Config requirements

Draw `path-track` as a single ordered chain of roughly 10–15 obstacle sections. Use `obstacle-jump` for distinct platforms separated by real, physics-legal gaps, `obstacle-moving` for spinning bars and sliding blocks, and `obstacle-timing` for disappearing-tile runs. Draw a clear start platform, the visible safe pads of `checkpoint-respawn`, and the finish platform of `winner-zone`. Keep the geometry bright, chunky, cartoon-clean, and visually legible against the open fall space.

## Layout requirements

Place `checkpoint-respawn` markers on a few safe checkpoint platforms spaced across the 10–15-section route, with fall resets targeting the latest passed marker. Place one `winner-zone` on the visible final platform. The first spawn sits on the start platform, before any gap.

## Layout components

Build one compact ascending course containing roughly 10–15 distinct obstacle sections. A broad start platform leads through ordinary jump chains, spinning-bar platforms, laterally sliding blocks, and disappearing-tile sequences, with a few larger enclosed checkpoint pads breaking the run into recoverable segments. Ramps or stepped platform chains carry the route upward and sideways without placing one playable section directly beneath another. Open air and a non-playable fall void surround and underlie the entire course; there is no baseplate. The boundary is the finite obstacle chain itself. Compose the view diagonally from above so the start, rising progression, hazard silhouettes, checkpoints, and distant finish all read clearly.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: the authoritative plan comes first because every gap, landing, and connection must form a completable sequence, then the isometric image dresses that plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is medium: the course is compact but contains roughly 10–15 sections. The theme is a clean, colorful cartoon obstacle course with bright platforms and high-contrast hazards. Use `P6` for validity by construction and `tiered` for the rising relief without overhang.
