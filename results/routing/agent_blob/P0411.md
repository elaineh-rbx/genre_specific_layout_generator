# Scene prompt

Create a 3D free-driving vehicle playground set in a rural dirt yard, with broad open dirt areas and dedicated drift tracks. Place multiple drift cars and off-road rally cars around the yard, with the tracks and open ground forming the main driving space.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Simulation because players freely operate drift and rally cars in a persistent driving playground. It is not Racing: the author explicitly requested no race, finish condition, or scoring.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-bounded`, taken from outside Simulation’s usual shape set, as one clearly bounded rural vehicle yard containing all activity. The preset is Vehicle Sim with its usual open-world shape replaced by the bounded yard and its unrequested shared resource field omitted.

## Config requirements

Apply `path-road-vehicle` as broad dirt driving routes: dedicated sweeping drift tracks linked directly to open off-road practice ground. The visible vehicle props must include multiple drift cars and multiple off-road rally cars, parked with enough clearance to read as usable vehicles rather than dense scenery. No shops, progression gates, extraction areas, or task stations are requested.

## Layout requirements

Nothing invisible needs post-segmentation placement. The map has no checkpoints, race starts, scoring volumes, collectibles, or triggers. Cars are named visible props and therefore belong in the rendered build rather than the layout-placement pass.

## Layout components

Build one rural dirt yard with a broad central free-driving zone and dedicated drift tracks curling around its edges. Connect every track back into the open ground so drivers can change activities without a forced route. Shape the drift lanes with wide turns, broad skid pads, and generous runoff; reserve rougher patches and mild dirt undulations for rally driving without introducing overhangs. Scatter multiple drift cars and off-road rally cars in distinct loose parking groups near the yard’s edge. Use packed dirt, tire marks, dust, sparse grass, low timber fencing, and distant rural fields. Enclose the playground with a simple fence and leave wide gates or visual openings. Compose the view to show both the track curves and the central dirt area.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down image. The atmosphere, vehicle grouping, and distinction between drift and rally surfaces lead; exact track closure is not a gameplay requirement.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a medium scale, large enough for several cars to maneuver but still one rural yard. The theme is a dusty, informal countryside motorsport playground. Route through `P0`: one contiguous ground surface has no interiors, stacked crossings, separate maps, or required computed topology.
