# Scene prompt

Create a small stylized battle royale lobby space designed to accommodate up to 10 players; do not build a match map.

# Agent decision

## Genre

This is No Genre: the requested deliverable is only a themed lobby, while the battle royale match and its gameplay are explicitly out of scope.

## Shape and preset

No catalogue shape applies to No Genre. Keep all five routing axes at their defaults: exterior enclosure, single-surface verticality, one zone, dressed rather than structure-critical construction, and grounded play-space. Use the Social Space preset.

## Config requirements

Use `social-hub` for one open gathering pad sized for up to 10 players without crowding. Use `spawn-area` to draw a clear arrival platform facing into the lobby, and `landmark-focal` as a compact battle-royale-style centerpiece or branded backdrop that gives the otherwise small space a visual anchor. Do not depict a match arena.

## Layout requirements

Place one `spawn-area` arrival region on the entry platform, with capacity for up to 10 simultaneous player arrivals and its facing direction aimed toward the centerpiece. Nothing else needs post-segmentation placement. The R15 warning and visual-mode menu are interface behavior, not layout geometry, and therefore do not enter the map handoff.

## Layout components

Build a single compact lobby pad with a central gathering area, a distinct arrival edge, and one focal battle-royale display opposite the spawn. Leave broad, uncluttered standing room for 10 avatars and short circulation around the centerpiece. Use a firm outer boundary dressed as stylized barriers or scenery, with no exits suggesting a generated match map. Compose the lobby symmetrically and keep the full footprint readable in one frame.

## Render order

Render in `isometric` order because visual styling leads and the simple lobby plan can be projected from the authoritative isometric image.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, explicitly capped at 10 players. The theme is polished, colorful battle-royale lobby presentation. The route is `P0`: a single flat exterior lobby requires no extra geometry pass.
