# Scene prompt
Create a 3D football World Cup map set in a modern 2026 stadium, built as one connected environment rather than on a baseplate. The central stadium contains a full football pitch, surrounding spectator stands, and a large 3D screen overlooking the field. Attach a lobby area to the stadium where players gather and vote for countries before entering the match, with a direct route from the lobby into the stadium.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The genre is Sports. This is a football match venue whose attached voting lobby supports, rather than changes, the field-sport identity.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded` for the central rigid football pitch and its connected stadium complex. The preset is Full Stadium, expanded with the requested attached lobby and football play markings.

## Config requirements
Draw `spectator-bleachers` as tiered stands surrounding the pitch, `barrier-perimeter` as the modern stadium shell, and `spectator-zone` as team-side areas beyond the touchlines. Add `trigger-bounds` as the marked field perimeter, `startpoint-play` as the center spot and team start positions, and `trigger-scoring` as two normal goals. Use `building-interior` for the enclosed voting lobby connected to the open stadium bowl. Also draw a large 3D screen overlooking the field, a country-voting bank in the lobby, and the direct concourse route between lobby and pitch.

## Layout requirements
Place one `trigger-bounds` perimeter on the touchlines. Place one ball `startpoint-play` at center plus mirrored player starts on the two halves. Place two `trigger-scoring` planes inside the opposing goal mouths. Place country-vote interaction volumes at the lobby voting fixtures, then place one match-entry threshold on the direct route into the stadium.

## Layout components
Build one full marked football pitch at the center of a modern 2026 stadium bowl, with two goals, team-side aprons, surrounding tiered stands, and one large screen with an unobstructed view over the field. Attach an enclosed gathering lobby to one side through a broad, obvious concourse entrance. The lobby contains a readable voting bank and enough open floor for a crowd. Keep lobby and match in one continuous environment rather than separate maps.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the modern venue’s appearance leads, and its simple lobby-to-bowl connection can be projected afterward without procedural topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge because it combines a full pitch, stadium bowl, and lobby. The theme is sleek international 2026 tournament architecture. Use `tiered` for stands and `P3` for movement between the enclosed lobby and open stadium.
