# Scene prompt
Create a huge stylised, cartoonish wildlife survival map shared by land animals, birds, and fish. The expansive world combines broad land areas, large connected water habitats, and open airspace, with food and water sources spread widely across the map so crossing it takes far longer than a few minutes. Populate the landscape and waters with varied animal, bird, fish, and dinosaur inhabitants, with space for players to move through each habitat.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Survival: players remain one chosen animal until death while managing food and water and avoiding hostile creatures or players.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, taken from outside Survival’s usual shapes, for one huge contiguous world traversable across land, connected water, and air. No preset remains recognisable: Resource Survival contributes gathering but incorrectly assumes graded separate biomes and player base building.

## Config requirements
Use `water-body` for large, genuinely connected aquatic habitat rather than decorative ponds. Use `npc-population` for the visible habitat ground and water occupied by non-hostile animals, birds, fish, and dinosaurs. Broad land, open flight space, and clear cartoon habitat silhouettes are required free scene geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` for food and drinkable water widely across every appropriate habitat, spaced so long journeys require route choices without leaving any animal class unsupported. Place `spawner-npc` origins for hostile AI animals, predatory fish, birds, and dinosaurs within their matching habitats and away from immediate player arrival areas. Place the functional half of `npc-population` across land, water, and aerial habitat zones, keeping inhabitants ecologically grouped and navigable.

## Layout components
Build one huge contiguous map with broad land regions, large connected waterways deep and wide enough for fish traversal, and unobstructed airspace for birds. Habitat edges should interlock rather than become separate maps, with shorelines joining land and water movement. Distribute feeding grounds, watering places, aquatic feeding areas, nesting or roosting terrain, and varied creature habitat throughout. Use distant scenic boundaries and a wide composition that makes the world’s long crossing distance clear.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: connected water routes and continuous habitat access must be established first so land animals, fish, and birds all receive playable movement space; the isometric is dressed from that authoritative plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is huge because traversal must take far longer than a few minutes. The theme is stylised, cartoonish, varied wildlife. Use `CHECK` because fish and birds move through water and open-air volumes; confirm those volumes do not self-occlude.
