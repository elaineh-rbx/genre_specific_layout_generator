## Enriched image prompt
Create one huge shared wildlife-survival world that takes much longer than a couple of minutes to cross and supports land animals, birds, and fish at the same time. Compose broad connected land habitats around substantial lakes and waterways, with open air above the land and water for bird flight and navigable underwater space for fish. Distribute clearly readable feeding grounds and watering areas across the entire map so resources are not concentrated in one location. Reserve navigable habitats and approaches for hostile AI land animals, fish, dinosaurs, and birds as well as player-controlled creatures. Use a stylised, cartoonish wildlife look with clear silhouettes that keeps the mixed creature types approachable.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Survival because hunger, thirst, predation, and persistent life until death define the loop. Roleplay & Avatar Sim is secondary because players inhabit a chosen land animal, bird, or fish.

## Shape and preset
Use `wilderness-open` from outside Survival’s usual shape set: one contiguous natural world with no settlement, shared by all creature types. No preset fits without adding building, progression, or social features the author did not request.

## Config requirements
Use `water-body` for the connected lakes and waterways that are both drinking resources and fish habitat. Use `volume-open` from the shared catalogue for the bird flight space and underwater fish volume, adapting it to free wildlife movement rather than a race. Do not add dens, towns, or build plots because none were requested.

## Layout requirements
Use `collectible-nodes` for food and drink interactions distributed among multiple feeding grounds and shoreline watering areas across the map. Use `spawner-npc` for hostile AI origins, with separate emitters sited in land, aquatic, dinosaur, and aerial habitat zones so each actor enters appropriate navigable space. Exact resource and emitter counts remain unspecified.

## Layout components
Build one continuous region containing broad land habitat, multiple connected water bodies, fish-accessible underwater areas, and unobstructed airspace. Spread feeding grounds and watering areas across distant parts of the region and connect land areas without forcing fish or birds onto terrestrial routes. Keep shorelines, major habitats, and resource areas readable in the full-map frame while leaving individual food items to placement. The map boundary is the outer natural-world extent.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the broad habitat composition and creature-readable visual theme lead, while the map has no maze or race topology requiring a generated blueprint.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use huge scale, driven by the explicitly long traversal time and region-wide habitat distribution; resource zones, not individual food props, are the smallest details expected to read in the overview.
