## Enriched image prompt
Create a roughly 2,000-by-2,000-stud contiguous stylized low-poly heroic-fantasy MMORPG landmass, with three realm homelands surrounding a shared central frontier. To one side, Caerwyn March uses green fields, pale limestone, aged oak, blue and crimson cloth, Caerwyn's round-towered town, Crown Square, Hall of Oaths, Rowan Road, White Hart Wood, Barrowfield, the Empty Grail chapel, Redcap Camp, Old Imperial Quarry, Mere of Glass, and an abandoned watchtower. Another side forms Hrafnvik Reach as a cold fjord beneath giant-haunted mountains, with dark timber and turf roofs, a long hall, shield-yard, forge, raven cliffs, pine hunting ground, troll caves, burial mounds, frozen tarn, and ruined dwarf road. The third side is Rowanfast, a luminous mossed forest settlement with living wood, bronze, moon pools, a Moonwell, bardic circle, forge, orchard paths, whispering barrows, Fomorian landing camp, glassy marsh, and frontier standing-stone ring.

All three road systems pass defended realm gates into the Crownless March: three outer keeps, capturable towers, supply routes, three relic-shrine sites, open fields, forests, ridges, rivers, alternate army and scout paths, and one central approach to ruined Camelot. Keep mid-level and high-level regions attached behind their corresponding homelands. Use generated-looking modular forms, readable silhouettes, weathered natural materials, no photorealism, no modern objects, no embedded writing, and no copied heraldry.

## Genre
RPG is the sole genre, specifically a realm-war MMORPG with progression, quests, combat, settlements, and a shared frontier.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, named Single Contiguous Map, because the author explicitly requires one landmass with homelands feeding directly into the frontier. This uses the MMO Town Hub preset but drops its teleporter option because realm gates are defended border entrances, not map transitions.

## Config requirements
Use `safezone-town` for Caerwyn, Hrafnvik Reach, and Rowanfast as three realm sanctuary settlements. Use `social-hub` for each settlement's clustered quest, market, forge, and class-service ground. Use `hazard-aggro` for enemy clearings and nests set away from safe roads. Use `landmark-focal` for the three realm gates, outer keeps, standing stones, watchtowers, and ruined Camelot. Use universal `terrain-relief`, `water-body`, `npc-population`, and `building-interior` for the named mountains, cliffs, rivers, fjord, tarns, marshes, inhabitants, halls, keeps, caves, shops, and shrines. Use shared `control-zone` for capturable keeps and towers and shared `capture-zone` for the three relic-shrine objectives.

## Layout requirements
Place `npc-population` inhabitants in each sanctuary, including one starter quest-giver position per realm. Place `spawner-npc` emitters in the named hostile grounds for wolves, boars, redcaps, oathbreaker bandits, barrow dead, draugr, raiders, cave trolls, ice wights, black hounds, thornlings, Fomorian scouts, and restless spirits, with one separate elite origin for the Antlered Exile, Old Ironclaw, and Bristle of the First Grove. Place `control-zone` ControlZones at exactly three outer keeps and at every capturable tower. Place three `capture-zone` CaptureZones on the relic shrines. Keep all homeland spawns outside cross-realm conflict and orient frontier placements toward the central Camelot approach.

## Layout components
The top-level world contains the three detailed starter homelands and central Crownless March described above. Behind Caerwyn attach Avalon Weald and Wyrmwall; behind Hrafnvik attach Jötunspine Foothills and Niflscar; behind Rowanfast attach Glassmere Wilds and Fomor Coast, all as zones of the same landmass rather than separate maps. Main roads connect each sanctuary to its defended realm gate; supply routes continue to three outer keeps and converge on the one ruined-Camelot approach. Smaller alternate paths cross forests, ridges, and rivers for solo scouts and small groups. Linked interior captures serve every enterable hall, keep, cave, shop, and shrine without splitting the exterior world.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish the three-realm ring, roads, gates, frontier routes, keeps, and central approach before dressing the isometric view, because the contiguous conflict geography must remain connected and balanced.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge, fixed by the roughly 2,000-by-2,000-stud vertical slice and its three homelands plus frontier. The theme is original stylized heroic fantasy with distinct Arthurian limestone, northern timber-and-basalt, and luminous western grove registers. Use `tiered` for mountains, cliffs, and ridges; `P2` for caves and stacked fortifications; `P3` for exterior-to-interior links; and `CHECK` for traversable rivers, fjord water, tarns, marshes, and moon pools. Do not use `P4`: the author explicitly requires one contiguous landmass.
