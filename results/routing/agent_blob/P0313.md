# Scene prompt
Create a 3D heroic-fantasy MMORPG landmass, roughly 2,000 by 2,000 studs, with three distinct realm homelands arranged around a shared central frontier for immediate travel and conflict. Logres occupies a green borderland centered on fortified Caerwyn: Crown Square, Hall of Oaths, Rowan Road, White Hart Wood, Barrowfield, a chapel, quarry, glassy mere, and abandoned watchtower, built in pale limestone, oak, blue wool, and crimson accents. Skeldmark lies along a cold fjord beneath mountain ridges, with a long hall, shield-yard, forge, raven cliffs, pine grounds, troll caves, burial mounds, frozen tarn, and ruined dwarf road in timber, turf, basalt, and iron. Tír Annwn forms a luminous western forest around a sacred grove, moonwell, bardic circle, bronze forge, orchard paths, barrows, marsh, shoreline camp, and standing-stone ring amid living wood, mossed stone, bronze, green, and violet.

At the center, build the Crownless March: open fields, forests, ridges, and rivers with three defended realm gates leading inward, three outer keeps, capturable towers, supply roads, relic shrines, and alternate paths converging on the ruins of Camelot. Extend each homeland outward into a mid-level wilderness and a high-level border region, preserving clear routes from starter settlements toward the central frontier.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG, with Action secondary. Levels, classes, quests, equipment, realm progression, and distinct PvE regions define the MMORPG; contested keeps and frontier combat add open-world action.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open` as one contiguous 2,000-by-2,000-stud landmass with three homelands surrounding the Crownless March. The preset is MMO Town Hub, expanded from one hub to three realm equivalents and with its teleporter option dropped because realm gates are defended roads, not transitions.

## Config requirements
Use `safezone-town` and `social-hub` for Caerwyn and the Skeldmark and Tír Annwn starter centers. Use `hazard-aggro` for starter, mid-level, and high-level hostile ground outside safe settlements. Add `landmark-focal` for three outer keeps, relic shrines, and ruined Camelot; `path-circulation` for realm roads, supply roads, and alternate frontier routes; `settlement-density` for the three homelands; `building-interior` for halls, chapel, caves, keeps, and civic buildings; `terrain-relief` for mountains, cliffs, and ridges; and `water-body` for fjord, rivers, mere, tarn, and marsh.

## Layout requirements
Place three protected realm starts inside their homelands. Place `npc-population` markers for quest givers, guards, merchants, and services at each starter center, and `spawner-npc` emitters across realm-appropriate hostile regions. Place capture volumes on three outer keeps and all capturable towers, ownership markers at three realm gates, and relic-objective markers at each shrine. Keep PvE emitters away from safe routes.

## Layout components
Build all named Logres, Skeldmark, and Tír Annwn landmarks and materials exactly as stated. Grade each homeland from starter settlement through mid-level wilderness to high-level border. Center the Crownless March on ruined Camelot, with three gates, three keeps, towers, shrines, fields, forests, ridges, rivers, supply roads, and distinct alternate paths for scouts, groups, and armies. Preserve three balanced travel spokes into the frontier.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: realm balance, continuous routes, gate approaches, and converging frontier paths must be authoritative before isometric dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is huge, fixed at roughly 2,000 by 2,000 studs. The theme is original low-poly heroic Arthurian, Norse, and Otherworld fantasy. Use `P3` for enterable structures, `P2` for troll caves and overhanging terrain, `tiered` for ridges, and `CHECK` for varied waters.
