# Scene prompt
Create a peaceful, nostalgic 3D exploration map as one seamless, compact but dense walkable valley, only a few minutes across by bicycle yet expanded by layered vertical routes, hidden areas, and real walkable interiors. Rolling green hills, colorful wildflower meadows, forests, rivers, and waterfalls fill the central valley beneath endless blue skies; paths and waterways lead toward mountain slopes, cliffs, beaches, and mysterious landmarks. Place abandoned ruins, hidden temples, caves, secret underground tunnels, and ancient mechanisms throughout the terrain, with secret passages and exploration spaces containing scattered journals, relics, treasure chests, fossils, glowing crystals, rare plants, and mysterious artifacts. Include floating islands overhead and climbable mountain routes above the valley. Populate the landscape with friendly NPC locations, birds, butterflies, deer, rabbits, foxes, and other wildlife. Add a customizable player home as a real interior space, with room for furniture, collected decorations, and trophies.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure, with Puzzle secondary because environmental mechanisms and secret passages create spatial solves within the exploration world.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, one seamless valley explored freely. The Adventure preset is Exploration, and all three of its spatial ideas fit the brief.

## Config requirements
Use `landmark-focal` for temples, ruins, and mysterious skyline landmarks; `alcove-secret` for concealed caves and passages; and `path-trail` for bicycle paths, climbing routes, and foot trails. Use `building-interior` for the temples, ruins, caves, tunnels, and customizable home; `water-body` for rivers, waterfalls, and beach water; `terrain-relief` for hills, cliffs, mountains, and the valley; and `island-cluster` for floating islands overhead. Use `npc-population` for friendly NPC gathering places and wildlife habitat. Add `gate-solve` for secret areas opened by observation and `button-solve` for visible ancient levers, plates, or mechanisms.

## Layout requirements
Place hundreds of `collectible-nodes` across landmarks, hidden rooms, caves, tunnels, high routes, underwater edges, and ordinary trail detours, covering journals, relics, chests, fossils, crystals, plants, and artifacts. Place `npc-population` in friendly quest locations and wildlife habitat zones. Place the functional half of `button-solve` beside each associated ancient gate or secret passage.

## Layout components
Build a compact central valley of meadows, forests, rivers, and waterfalls, ringed by mountain slopes, cliffs, and beaches. Paths and waterways connect ruins, temples, caves, a player home, and landmark sites. Vertical branches climb mountains and reach floating islands, while tunnels form an underground layer. Use natural rock, grass, flowers, forest floor, sand, and water. Mountains and cliffs enclose the map. Frame the green valley centrally, with high routes and islands layered above.

## Render order
Render in `topdown` order because every surface, interior entrance, tunnel, and vertical route must remain connected and reachable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use large scale: geographically compact but densely layered. The theme is peaceful, magical, nostalgic countryside. Apply `P2` for tunnels and stacked routes, `P3` for outdoor-to-interior transitions, `CHECK` for swimming and layered floating-island play, and `tiered` for major relief.
