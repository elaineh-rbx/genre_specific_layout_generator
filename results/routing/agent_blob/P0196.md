## Enriched image prompt
Create one seamless compact but dense 3D countryside valley for peaceful exploration, with no loading divisions. Arrange rolling green hills and colorful wildflower meadows beneath a broad blue sky, threaded by walkable and bicycle-friendly trails. Layer forests, mountains, rivers, waterfalls, beaches, cliffs, and several floating islands around the valley so routes continue across ground, climbs, water, and gliding gaps. Embed real walkable caves and secret underground tunnels beneath the terrain, and place enterable abandoned ruins, hidden temples, and one customizable player home throughout the valley. Distribute hundreds of journals, relics, treasure chests, fossils, glowing crystals, rare plants, and mysterious artifacts across landmarks, concealed passages, interiors, and vertical routes. Add environmental puzzles and ancient mechanisms around hidden areas. Populate paths and landmarks with friendly story-giving characters, and place birds, butterflies, deer, rabbits, foxes, and other wildlife in suitable natural ground. Use peaceful nostalgic lighting and a soft magical atmosphere.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure (`adventure`), specifically open-ended exploration without combat.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`: every biome, landmark, interior entrance, and route belongs to one contiguous seamless valley. The matching preset is Exploration.

## Config requirements
Use `path-trail` for walking and bicycle routes, `landmark-focal` for mysterious ruins and natural anchors, and `alcove-secret` for concealed passages and hidden cutouts. Use `building-interior` for temples, ruins, caves, tunnels, and the player home; `water-body` for rivers, waterfalls, and beach water; `terrain-relief` for hills, mountains, cliffs, and underground cuts; `island-cluster` for several floating islands; and `npc-population` for friendly characters and wildlife. Use `button-solve`, taken from the shared catalogue, for visible ancient environmental-puzzle mechanisms.

## Layout requirements
Use `collectible-nodes` for hundreds of journals, relics, treasure chests, fossils, glowing crystals, rare plants, and mysterious artifacts, concentrated near landmarks, in secret passages, inside structures, and along vertical routes. For `npc-population`, place friendly quest characters near paths and landmark clearings and wildlife in matching natural areas. For `button-solve`, align each puzzle input with its ancient mechanism and hidden area. Place free-text TriggerZone discovery markers at concealed landmarks and secret locations. The journal, rewards, weather, and day/night cycle add no fixed placements.

## Layout components
The `world-open` container is one finite valley. Its subordinate zones are the meadow-and-forest surface, mountain and cliff routes, rivers and beaches, several floating islands, the linked cave-and-tunnel layer, enterable ruins and temples, and one player-home interior. Ground trails connect all surface regions; climbs, water crossings, and glide gaps reach vertical areas; entrances link exterior terrain to interiors. Natural ridges bound the composition.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish seamless connectivity among trails, water, vertical routes, entrances, and hidden destinations before applying the nostalgic isometric dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large, compressing the author’s few-minutes-by-bicycle valley enough that trails, structures, plants, and collectibles remain legible. The theme is peaceful, nostalgic magical countryside. Use `tiered` for strong relief, `P2` for caves, tunnels, and layered floating land, `P3` for exterior-to-interior travel, and `CHECK` for swimming and gliding play volumes; do not use `P4` because the world is explicitly seamless.
