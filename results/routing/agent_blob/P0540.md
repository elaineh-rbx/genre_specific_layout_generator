# Scene prompt
Create a bright, colourful 3D pizza-making simulator layout. Begin in a Small Town world with a single tiny pizza stand that visibly expands through dough-mixing, topping, baking, delivery, restaurant, and factory-scale work areas; include room around it for this staged growth. Connect separate unlockable worlds through a central world hub with teleport portals arranged in progression order: Small Town, Busy City, Beach Resort, Snow Village, a neon Japanese-inspired food district, Space Colony, Underwater City, Candy Kingdom, Volcano Island, and Future Mega City. Each destination is a distinct themed cooking environment with its own pizza stand or production area, local ingredients and equipment, customer spaces, and a few hidden areas.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The genre is Simulation. The factory expansion, upgrades, and prestige economy make the pizza-making loop an incremental simulator rather than an adventure.

## Shape and preset
Use `hub-portals`, taken from outside Simulation’s usual shapes, because a central hub sends players to ten genuinely separate worlds. The preset is Incremental Simulator, with its shape replaced while retaining the progression gates, central hub, and circulation.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for the central world-selection plaza, `gate-progression` for ten portals unlocked in listed order, `path-circulation` for routes through each production environment, and `trigger-task` for visible dough, topping, baking, delivery, restaurant, and factory workstations. Add a few hidden recesses in each destination as free scene geometry because the brief explicitly requests them.

## Layout requirements
The functional half of `trigger-task` places six staged work triggers in the Small Town expansion and equivalent production triggers in each later world. Place ten teleport destinations in progression order around the hub, each paired with its destination arrival marker. Keep arrivals clear of equipment and customer areas.

## Layout components
Build one central portal hub and ten separate maps: Small Town, Busy City, Beach Resort, Snow Village, neon Japanese-inspired food district, Space Colony, Underwater City, Candy Kingdom, Volcano Island, and Future Mega City. Each map contains a pizza stand or production zone, local equipment and ingredient props, customer space, circulation, and a few concealed recesses. Small Town starts with one tiny stand and reserves adjacent footprints for six visible stages through factory scale. Compose the hub with all ten portal identities readable.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: portal order, expansion footprints, and clear production circulation should be established before visual dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge because there are ten destination maps plus a hub. The theme is bright, colourful, polished pizza-simulator fantasy. Route `P4` because the worlds are explicitly separate teleport-accessed maps.
