# Scene prompt

Create a 3D Roblox obby map in a classic bright block style: a small, plainly playable flat course progressing forward along the ground through 10 sequential stages. Each stage contains one short colorful platform obstacle sequence and a checkpoint, with modest platform elevation where needed. Arrange clearly visible lava hazard parts beneath or between obstacles, while keeping a clear forward route from the starting area through the final stage.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Obby & Platformer. The Robux shop, inventory persistence, and purchasable coils are UI and gameplay systems and do not change the spatial classification.

## Shape and preset

Use `course-flat`, a finite forward-running course with only modest local elevation. The Classic Obby preset applies, augmented by the explicitly requested lava hazards.

## Config requirements

Draw `path-track` as exactly 10 sequential stages, each containing one short obstacle sequence. Use `obstacle-jump` for simple colorful gap and block challenges, and `obstacle-moving` only where a short classic moving-platform challenge adds variety. Draw `hazard-kill` as clearly visible lava beneath or between obstacles. Include the visible safe platforms for `checkpoint-respawn` and the final payoff pad for `winner-zone`. Keep the critical route obvious through strong block colors and high contrast against the lava.

## Layout requirements

Place exactly 10 `checkpoint-respawn` markers, one on a safe platform at the end of each numbered stage, with contact with any `hazard-kill` surface returning the player to the latest saved marker. Place the initial spawn before stage 1 and one `winner-zone` immediately beyond the stage 10 checkpoint. Do not place Speed Coil, Jump Coil, or Rainbow Fly pickups in the map; those are granted through the requested purchase system rather than found spatially.

## Layout components

Build one small linear ground-level course divided into exactly 10 clearly numbered stages. A safe start area feeds short sequences of bright block platforms, modest steps, simple gaps, and occasional moving parts. Lava beds fill fall spaces beneath and between challenges without covering the safe checkpoint platforms. Each stage terminates at its own broad checkpoint before the next begins, and stage 10 leads directly to the finish pad. Keep all stages on one continuous surface footprint, with modest height changes and no overhangs. Low side edging and the lava field define the boundary. Compose the course as a readable forward strip or gentle zigzag so all 10 checkpoints and the final destination are visible.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: establish the authoritative 10-stage route first because every jump, checkpoint, and lava separation must be playable, then dress it isometrically.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is medium, driven by exactly 10 short stages. The theme is classic bright Roblox block geometry with vivid lava. Use `P6` because obstacle spacing and route continuity must be valid by construction.
