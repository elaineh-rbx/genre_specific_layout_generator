# Scene prompt
Create a 3D horror escape-room map inside a small, dark, spooky locked house at night. The front lobby contains the player spawn point and a locked exit door at the front of the house. Four or five rooms include the lobby, a kitchen, and a back bedroom area, linked by mostly linear, narrow hallways that can trap players; a wooden plank blocks the kitchen doorway. Hide a keycard in one of the back bedrooms. Place a spooky bear-like monster within the house.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Survival, with Puzzle secondary: a pursuing monster supplies the pressure while an item-and-barrier chain enables escape.

## Shape and preset
Use `rooms-sequence`, outside Survival’s usual shortlist, for four or five rooms connected by mostly linear narrow halls with intentional trapping points. The preset is Mascot Horror, retaining its monster origin and escape gate but replacing its single-interior shape.

## Config requirements
Use `gate-escape` for the locked front exit door and draw the wooden plank as a destructible barrier across the kitchen doorway. The house is a dark, spooky nighttime interior with no looped chase warren. Include the bear-like monster visibly, but do not add unrequested hiding cutouts.

## Layout requirements
Place one player spawn in the front lobby. Place one `spawner-npc` origin for the bear-like monster deeper in the house, clear of the initial spawn. Place one `collectible-nodes` keycard at a randomized eligible point among the back bedrooms. Place the `gate-escape` completion trigger on the front door, conditional on the keycard. Put one three-click interaction on the kitchen plank and preserve a navigable route after it breaks.

## Layout components
Build a small house of four or five rooms: front lobby, kitchen, back bedroom area, and supporting rooms, joined by mostly linear narrow hallways. The front door borders the lobby and map edge. The kitchen branch begins behind the plank. Keep the back bedrooms distant from the exit and allow dead-end trapping spaces. Enclose the map completely and compose it for claustrophobic readability.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first as authoritative, then dress the isometric, because the monster’s pathfinding route, keycard access, and breakable kitchen path must stay connected.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small. The theme is a dark nighttime haunted house with a mascot-horror bear. Use `P0`: the map is one interior surface without exterior traversal or stacked floors.
