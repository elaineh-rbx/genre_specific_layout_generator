Keep the hallways deliberately constricted and capable of trapping the player, while preserving a connected patrol path for one pursuing spooky-bear monster. Use `collectible-nodes` for exactly one keycard, randomly assigned to a valid hiding point in the back-bedroom area. The front exit sits on the lobby boundary. One plank blocks the kitchen doorway. The player and monster starts occupy opposite ends. The outer house walls enclose the entire play space.
