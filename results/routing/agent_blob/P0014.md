# Scene prompt
Create a 3D high-fantasy medieval adventure map set in a small, walkable valley for a knight avatar. The valley contains explorable terrain and distinct areas for undead encounters, with boss locations distributed across the world.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure, with Action secondary. Exploration across a world is the central activity, while zombie and boss combat gives the destinations their purpose.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`: the entire small valley is one contiguous map that can be explored in any order. The matched preset is Exploration, retaining its open-world shape, directional landmarks, and walkable exploration trail while omitting the unrequested secret alcoves.

## Config requirements
Use `landmark-focal` for visible medieval ruins, towers, or boss structures that pull the knight toward encounter areas. Use `path-trail` for a worn route linking the valley’s distinct undead grounds without turning the map into a linear corridor. Use `terrain-relief` for the enclosing valley slopes and gentle high-fantasy elevation. No settlement, interiors, water, or environmental kill hazards were requested.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` emitters for zombies within each undead encounter area and separate boss emitters at the distributed boss locations. Keep all emitters set back from the main walking trail so ordinary exploration does not trigger every fight at once. The prompt gives no exact number of encounter areas or bosses, so their counts remain unspecified.

## Layout components
Build one small walkable valley enclosed by rising medieval-fantasy hills. A primary dirt trail loops through several visually distinct undead encounter clearings and reconnects near the starting side, with short branches leading to boss landmarks around the valley. Use grass, weathered stone, dead patches of earth, ruined walls, grave markers, and sparse trees to distinguish combat grounds while keeping navigation readable. Preserve open sightlines to at least one landmark from most of the valley. The hill rim forms the boundary, and the full valley should compose as a coherent explorable basin.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the isometric image is rendered first and is authoritative because the high-fantasy landscape and readable landmarks lead, while the top-down can be projected from that look.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, set by a fully walkable valley requiring no fast travel. The theme is high-fantasy medieval with approachable undead corruption. Use `tiered` for valley relief with no overhangs; no other modifier is forced.
