Keep it as one contiguous landscape with natural elevation, readable paths through the valley, scattered undead encounter ground, and distinct open areas large enough for boss fights. Arrange the boss areas as discoverable destinations across the valley so the whole world can be explored without fast travel. Treat the zombies as medieval undead enemies rather than horror or post-apocalyptic decoration, and compose the valley so its paths and major encounter areas remain visible in one isometric frame.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure, with Action as the secondary genre because exploration structures the world while zombie and boss combat supplies the encounters.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, one contiguous Open World that can be explored in any order. The matching preset is Exploration, with its secret-alcove option omitted because the author did not ask for hidden spaces.

## Config requirements
Use `path-trail` for walkable routes linking the valley's encounter areas, `landmark-focal` for visually distinct boss destinations that pull exploration across the map, and the universal `terrain-relief` for the valley sides and rolling ground. No unrelated settlement or interior is added.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` origins for undead in the scattered combat grounds and for each boss inside its own encounter area, set back from the travel paths so exploration does not begin inside active combat. The number of bosses is unspecified, so use the smallest useful plural arrangement: two distinct boss encounter areas.

## Layout components
The build is one bounded but visually open valley, with a connected footpath network, rolling valley relief, scattered undead encounter clearings, and two separated boss arenas positioned as major destinations. The valley floor carries the main circulation, while the slopes form the natural perimeter. Keep every destination reachable on foot and frame the complete valley as one coherent landscape.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the high-fantasy valley look first and derive the top-down from it, because the open exploration space does not require computed maze or track topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is medium, preserving individual paths and combat grounds within the author's small walkable valley. The theme is high-fantasy medieval, with zombies presented as undead enemies. The only modifier is `tiered`, forced by the valley's natural elevation; otherwise the map remains a single-surface baseline build.
