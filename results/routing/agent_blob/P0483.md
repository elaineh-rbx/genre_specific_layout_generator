# Scene prompt
Create a 3D horror-adventure map for Floor 3: The Forgotten Underground, a huge subterranean world beneath an abandoned building. Build 100+ connected rooms as separately generated and stitched underground sections using a controlled modular layout: dark tunnels link forgotten laboratories, storage areas, rooms with broken machines, underground caves, and concealed secret rooms. Preserve key progression spaces including locked-door routes, puzzle and power-restoration rooms, checkpoints, hiding places, and trap-filled passages, while allowing the surrounding room arrangement to change each session. The map culminates in a large underground escape route leading into a separate final boss arena, followed by a cinematic exit area.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Survival is dominant, with Adventure secondary. Pursuing monsters, hiding, traps, and escape pressure define play; the long underground journey supplies the story progression.

## Shape and preset
Use `rooms-sequence` for 100+ enclosed rooms and corridors worked through toward a finale. The closest preset is Liminal Horror, substituting this terminating sequence for its endless interior while retaining hiding spaces, hostile origins, and collectibles.

## Config requirements
Use `cover-los` for lockers, crawlspaces, and machine recesses; `gate-progression` for locked doors between required rooms; `gate-solve` for puzzle and power-restoration barriers; and `gate-escape` for the final underground exit. Draw laboratories, storage rooms, broken-machine chambers, caves, secret rooms, trap passages, the escape route, a separate boss arena, and a cinematic exit.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` origins and patrol ground for three enemy patterns: sound hunter, darkness apparition, and chase initiator. Distribute `collectible-nodes` for keys and coins through optional and required rooms. Place `checkpoint-respawn` markers at stitched-section boundaries and before the finale. Place the layout half of `gate-escape` at the transition from escape route to boss arena. Preserve required puzzle, power, checkpoint, and finale modules in every procedural arrangement.

## Layout components
Generate and stitch 100+ connected rooms across several underground sections. Mix dark tunnels, forgotten labs, storage areas, broken-machine rooms, caves, concealed rooms, hiding pockets, and trap corridors around a guaranteed main progression route. Branches may change per session, but every section must reconnect without sealing required content. Terminate the underground in one large escape corridor, then transition to one separate boss arena and one cinematic exit area. Bound the entire run with rock, concrete, and locked industrial bulkheads.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, then dress the isometric views. `authored_plan` is not available because this is a modular room network rather than a maze; controlled generation must still validate connectivity.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use huge scale, driven by 100+ rooms plus finale spaces. Theme: abandoned industrial underground horror with laboratories and natural caves. Route with `P6` for valid modular connectivity and `P4` for the separate boss arena.
