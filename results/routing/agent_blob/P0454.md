# Scene prompt
Create a 3D arcade soccer map centered on one compact, enclosed 3v3 street court roughly 150 studs across. The rectangular pitch is bounded on all sides by solid rebound walls, with goals centered at the two short ends and no out-of-bounds space; keep the court open, highly readable, and framed for fast continuous play. Build one consistent collision layout that can be redressed as four colorful, stylized urban-themed variants: a rooftop pitch, narrow alley court, neon night court, and beach court. Use high-contrast surfacing and clear sightlines so the ball and goals remain visible from a slightly elevated, dynamic gameplay camera.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Sports because two 3v3 teams compete to score goals on a fixed soccer pitch.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded` for the rigid rectangular street court enclosing all play. Match the Physics Sport preset: rebound walls replace out-of-bounds behavior while goals and reset positions retain a sports-field structure.

## Config requirements
Use `barrier-perimeter` for solid rebound walls on all four sides. Use `trigger-scoring` for the two visible goal mouths centered at opposite short ends. Use `startpoint-play` for the kickoff and mirrored team positions. Draw one clean, high-contrast collision layout that supports four cosmetic redresses—rooftop, narrow alley, neon night, and beach—without changing wall, goal, or play-surface geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place two `trigger-scoring` planes, one inside each opposing goal mouth. Place one central ball reset and six player `startpoint-play` markers, three per team, mirrored across the centerline. Keep every start clear of the walls and goals, and keep all triggers identical across the four visual variants. No foul-boundary trigger is needed because the physical wall continuously contains the ball.

## Layout components
Build one open rectangular pitch roughly 150 studs across, with a solid rebound wall enclosing every side and one goal centered in each short wall. Preserve wide, unobstructed sightlines from a slightly elevated camera and use high-contrast floor and wall materials so the ball and goals remain legible. Produce four dressed versions of this exact geometry: rooftop skyline, tight urban alley, glowing neon night court, and bright beach court. Decorative surroundings may change, but the pitch footprint, collision, starts, walls, and two goals must not.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the symmetric goal alignment, mirrored starts, wall enclosure, and shared collision footprint must be fixed before any theme is applied.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by the stated roughly 150-stud compact court. The theme is colorful, stylized, high-contrast arcade street soccer across four visual skins. Use `P0`; all variants reuse one single-level collision layout, with no separate maps, interiors, overhangs, or structural generator requirement.
