# Scene prompt
Create a 3D escape-game layout set in an old mansion: a small chain of 3 compact, single-room escape areas connected sequentially. Each room has distinct mansion furnishings and objects arranged as places to conceal keys, with an exit leading onward to the next room.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Puzzle: each room is searched and unlocked before the player can enter the next.

## Shape and preset
Use `rooms-sequence`, exactly three sealed mansion chambers worked through in order. Use the Escape Room preset, retaining its collectible key items and sequenced unlock gates while omitting unrequested keypad and clue-wall features.

## Config requirements
Draw `gate-progression` as three locked exits: the first connects room 1 to room 2, the second connects room 2 to room 3, and the third leaves the final room. Give every room distinct old-mansion furnishings with believable concealment spaces. Draw `winner-zone` as a small clear landing beyond the third exit.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly three `collectible-nodes` keys, one in each room. Site the first in an easy-to-read furnishing cluster, the second in a less obvious but visible search area, and the third in the hardest concealment among the final room’s objects. Each key must remain reachable before its room’s corresponding exit unlocks. Place one `winner-zone` trigger immediately beyond the third door.

## Layout components
Build exactly three compact enclosed rooms in a linear chain with no bypass around a locked exit. Make room 1 a dusty sitting room with sofas, side tables, and a fireplace; room 2 a study with bookcases, desk, cabinets, and display objects; and room 3 a bedroom or private gallery with wardrobes, chests, screens, and denser decorative clutter. Keep each room easy to traverse while arranging furniture around several plausible key-hiding places. Use short thresholds between rooms, a final outward-facing door, level wooden floors, aged paneling, and hard enclosing walls. Compose the plan so all three chambers and their ordered doors are immediately legible without revealing the exact key positions.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the three-room sequence, key reachability, and locked-door order must be correct before mansion dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by three compact rooms. The theme is dusty old-mansion mystery with distinct period furnishings. Use `P0`; the complete sequence occupies one level with no overhang or separate map.
