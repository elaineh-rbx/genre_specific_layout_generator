# Scene prompt
Create a 3D bright cartoon petting-zoo map centered on a dog shop and individual player pens. The shop displays dogs ranging from common breeds to rare breeds, including a greyhound as the rarest, while each player has one nearby fenced pen sized for six dogs; newly placed dogs appear in small cages inside the pen. Keep the build compact, clean, and simple for a dog-collecting zoo.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation: the spatial loop is a shared dog shop feeding each player’s personal collection pen.

## Shape and preset
Use `plot-isolated`, interpreted as one independent fenced pen per player with enough separation to prevent dogs or cages overlapping neighboring collections. The closest preset is Sandbox, retaining the personal-plot collection structure while replacing extractable materials with living dogs.

## Config requirements
Draw `social-hub` as the central dog shop where common-to-rare breeds are displayed, with the greyhound presented as the rarest focal display. Use `path-circulation` for short, clean walks between the shop and every pen. Show `npc-population` as the shop’s dog displays and the dogs occupying player pens. Each pen must visibly include six small cage positions and enough open area for six dogs.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `npc-population` at breed display positions in the shop and at exactly six dog slots inside each player pen. Place one player ownership/spawn marker per pen. Each newly acquired dog should resolve to one of the six small cage positions; no money-producing stations or reward machinery should be placed.

## Layout components
The shared central zone is a compact cartoon dog shop with a readable breed display progressing from common dogs to the rare greyhound. Individual fenced pens sit nearby in a tidy ring or short rows, one per player, each sized for six dogs and containing exactly six small cages. Broad simple paths connect the shop to every pen. Use flat grass, bright paving, rounded fences, colorful awnings, and clean signage. A low decorative zoo fence encloses the whole map. Frame the shop as the central landmark while keeping the repeating pen layout easy to read.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the bright petting-zoo presentation first, then derive the uncomplicated top-down arrangement from it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a small scale band because the build is explicitly compact and simple. The theme is bright cartoon petting zoo. Apply `P0`; all activity occupies one flat, walkable outdoor surface without interiors or overhangs.
