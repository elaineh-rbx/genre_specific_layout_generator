## Enriched image prompt
Build an original compact open modern city for a persistent police-versus-criminal role simulation. Lay out a few connected drivable blocks around close-set landmarks: one combined police and SWAT compound, one criminal hideout, one bank, one jewelry store, two gas stations, one open-front weapon shop, and one open vehicle dealership lot. Give the two faction bases and all four robbery targets linked enterable interiors; keep houses and apartment blocks as exterior scenery only. Connect every landmark with a coherent two-way street network, intersections, sidewalks, civilian traffic lanes, and short chase loops. Include protected arrival areas at opposite faction bases and practical vehicle bays at the police compound, dealership, and street edges. Preserve an original contemporary crime-city identity rather than copying any referenced map, asset, or branding.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation because police and criminal roles perform repeatable missions in a persistent city. Shooter is secondary for armed team conflict.

## Shape and preset
Use Simulation's `world-open` Shared Persistent World: one contiguous city supports both roles without rounds or separate match maps. The matched preset is Role Sim, with pedestrian circulation, drivable roads, and task stations.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` as ordinary two-way city streets rather than a racing course, so it routes without `P6`. Use `path-circulation` for sidewalks and short routes between all landmarks. Use universal `settlement-density` for the compact few-block city fabric. Use `building-interior` for exactly six linked interiors: the police/SWAT compound, criminal hideout, bank, jewelry store, and two gas stations. Draw two visible `spawn-teambase` structures, taken from Shooter's catalogue row, as protected opposing faction bases. Draw the visible half of `trigger-task` at robbery counters and faction mission points, and `npc-population` as civilian pedestrians and traffic occupying the streets.

## Layout requirements
Place two `spawn-teambase` SpawnZones, one inside each faction base. Place six `trigger-task` TriggerZones: one in each of the four robbery-target interiors and one mission point in each faction base. Place `npc-population` occupancy along sidewalks and traffic lanes. Place free-text Spawners in the police vehicle bays, dealership lot, and selected street-edge parking bays for service and civilian vehicles.

## Layout components
The city exterior contains a few connected blocks, two faction compounds, four robbery targets, an open weapon storefront, a vehicle dealership lot, facade-only housing, roads, sidewalks, intersections, and parking or vehicle bays. Six subordinate interior zones use `interior-single` and link directly to their corresponding exteriors. Keep the police compound and criminal hideout on different sides of the city, with robbery landmarks between them to sustain short chases. Frame the entire compact road network and all major landmarks together.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the drivable street loops, faction separation, landmark access, and six linked interiors must be connected before urban dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is large because several city blocks and main roads must fit, while individual landmarks, sidewalks, and vehicle bays remain readable. The theme is an original contemporary urban police-and-criminal city. The pipeline requires `P3` for the six exterior-to-interior transitions; no `P6` applies because the streets are a city network, not a fixed race course.
