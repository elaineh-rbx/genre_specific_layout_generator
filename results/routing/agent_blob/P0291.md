# Scene prompt
Create a compact open 3D city spanning a few connected, drivable blocks, laid out for active police chases. Place key landmarks close together: a central police station and SWAT base, robbery-target bank, jewelry store, and gas station, plus a weapon shop, vehicle dealership, criminal hideout, houses, and apartment buildings. Use streets with working traffic links between the landmarks; houses and apartments remain exterior scenery, while the robbery targets and the two faction bases have enterable interiors.

# Agent decision

## Genre
Simulation is dominant because police and criminal roles perform repeatable robberies, arrests, missions, and vehicle work in a persistent shared city. Shooter is secondary because armed faction conflict and protected team starts remain part of the game.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, one compact persistent city that all players operate in together. The preset is Role Sim, retaining its task stations, circulation, and vehicle-road network for police and criminal work.

## Config requirements
Use `trigger-task` for visible robbery and faction-mission interaction sites; `path-circulation` for city movement; and `path-road-vehicle` for chase-ready streets and traffic links. Use `spawn-teambase` for the police-side facilities and criminal hideout, with starts screened from direct opposition. Use `building-interior` only for the bank, jewelry store, gas station, police/SWAT facilities, and criminal hideout; houses and apartments stay facade-only scenery. Use `settlement-density` for the compact urban block fabric.

## Layout requirements
Place the two `spawn-teambase` functions at the police facility cluster and criminal hideout, one faction start on each side of the role split and outside direct sightlines. Place one `trigger-task` interaction at each of the three robbery targets—the bank, jewelry store, and gas station—and task access at the relevant faction bases. The visible portions of both options belong in the image configuration, while their functional markers belong here.

## Layout components
Build a few connected drivable blocks around a central police station and adjacent SWAT base. Place one bank, one jewelry store, and one gas station as nearby but distinct robbery targets, plus one weapon shop, one vehicle dealership, and one criminal hideout. Houses and apartment facades fill the remaining block edges without enterable interiors. Streets form short loops with intersections, turning room, and uninterrupted traffic links so chases stay active without long travel. Sidewalks and alleys support on-foot movement. Conceal the compact boundary behind dense city continuation. Frame the map to show both faction anchors and the close landmark network.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, because chase loops and uninterrupted links among all key landmarks must remain valid for play.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by a few vehicle-scaled blocks rather than the source’s initially huge city. The theme is an original contemporary urban police-versus-criminal sandbox with day/night-ready lighting. Use `P3` because play crosses seamlessly between streets and selected interiors.
