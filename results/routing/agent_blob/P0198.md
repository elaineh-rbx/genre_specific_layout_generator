# Scene prompt
Create a peaceful, stylized storybook forest map for exploration: a short, dense wooded walk with abundant trees and flowers, arranged to feel calm and easy to wander through.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Entertainment (Showcase & Hub), specifically an environment showcase: wandering and looking are the whole activity, with no objective, discovery reward, combat, or finish.

## Shape and preset
Use `route-guided` for one short, clear wooded walk that sequences visitors through the forest’s strongest compositions. The closest preset is Showcase, keeping its guided route and curated presentation while dropping the unrequested hero landmark.

## Config requirements
Use `spawn-first-reveal` so the entrance opens directly onto an inviting view of layered storybook trees and abundant flowers rather than a boundary or backstage edge. Use `vignette-photo` for several distinct flower-rich clearings and framed bends along the walk. The dense trees, flowers, shrubs, and storybook forest dressing are explicit scene geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `spawn-first-reveal` once at the beginning of the wooded walk, oriented down the first attractive path composition. No collectibles, objectives, portals, NPCs, or other functional placements are needed.

## Layout components
Build one short walking path that curves gently through a dense but readable forest. Arrange abundant stylized trees in layered clusters on both sides, opening periodically into small flower clearings. Use colorful wildflower beds, soft grass, rounded shrubs, exposed roots, stones, and occasional fallen logs as visual accents without obstructing the route. The path should remain easy to follow and wide enough for relaxed wandering, with no confusing branches or dead ends. Dense woodland and softened foliage walls enclose the play space. Compose the map as a sequence of calm foreground flower patches, midground tree trunks, and deeper painted-looking canopy layers, with each bend revealing another small vignette.

## Render order
Render in `isometric` order because the storybook style, foliage layering, and sequence of composed views lead; the simple walking plan can be derived afterward.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a small scale, driven by a short wooded walk rather than a forest to get lost in. The theme is peaceful, colorful, soft-edged storybook woodland. Route through `P0`; the walk occupies one flat, continuous outdoor surface with no required interiors, overhangs, or separate maps.
