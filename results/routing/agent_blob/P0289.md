# Scene prompt
Create a 3D cosmic hub map centered on the Time Nest, an ultra-realistic monumental hyper-structure suspended in a dark cosmic void where time appears still. Its interior and surrounding platforms frame floating holographic galaxies, with numerous portal openings arranged around the central structure to lead to separate planet maps and alternate realities. Include jagged glowing purple-and-black dimensional rifts as space tears, revealing the void beyond; portals and rifts form the visual connection to the wider endless Xenoverse.

# Agent decision

## Genre
Action is dominant because the original game is built around hyper-kinetic martial combat, energy clashes, and destructible battle environments. Adventure is secondary because the Time Nest connects an endless set of alternate worlds for exploration.

## Shape and preset
Use `hub-portals`, outside Action’s usual shapes: the Time Nest is a central destination whose spatial purpose is dispatching players to separate planet maps and realities. Preset is none. Quest Hub is the closest family, but its quest-board and progression-gate bundle is absent, and the prompt instead requires a dedicated portal-hub shape.

## Config requirements
Use `landmark-focal` for the monumental Time Nest as the unmistakable orientation anchor. Use `teleporter-link` for the visible portal openings and jagged dimensional rifts leading outward to separate destinations. Use `building-interior` because play crosses between the Time Nest interior and its surrounding exterior platforms. Floating holographic galaxies, cosmic void views, and purple-black space tears are explicit uncatalogued image geometry.

## Layout requirements
For `teleporter-link`, place one functional destination marker within every authored portal opening and dimensional rift, distributed around the hub’s central circulation ring. Their exact number is deliberately “numerous,” not a numeric count. Each marker must face a safe approach platform and remain visually distinct from decorative holographic galaxies. No combat spawners or objective trackers belong in this hub scene.

## Layout components
The Time Nest occupies the center as a monumental enclosed hyper-structure, with its interior opening onto a ring of suspended exterior platforms. Walkable bridges and circulation paths connect those platforms around the structure. Numerous portals face inward from the perimeter, while jagged purple-and-black rifts tear through selected void-facing edges. Floating holographic galaxies fill major interior and platform focal bays without obstructing travel. The dark cosmic void is the map boundary, with platforms safely defining its walkable edge. Compose the view around a strong radial silhouette so the Nest, circulation ring, portal array, and surrounding void read at once.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, because the monumental cosmic presentation and portal framing lead the straightforward radial circulation plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large, driven by the monumental hub and numerous portal bays. The theme is ultra-realistic cosmic architecture with luminous dimensional fractures. Use `P4` because portals lead to genuinely separate planet maps, plus `P3` for linked interior and exterior hub spaces.
