# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] goal — Q: Is finding the items the whole game, or is something in the forest hunting you? A: Finding and exploring for items and houses is the whole game; nothing is hunting the player.
- [author] scale — Q: How far should the forest run — a few minutes of walking, or a large open area you can get lost in? A: Use a forest that takes a few minutes to walk across, with enough paths and points of interest to explore.
- [agent_inferred] enterability — Q: Must the discovered houses have playable interiors? A: Keep them as small exterior woodland houses and place finds around them; the prompt does not explicitly request interiors.

## Enriched image prompt
Build a realistic nighttime forest that takes a few minutes to walk across, with darkness deep enough to motivate flashlight exploration. Thread several branching footpaths through dense trees and natural undergrowth, leading to scattered clearings and small woodland houses that serve as discoverable points of interest. Add concealed side pockets behind foliage and off the main trails where items can be found, but include no hunter, enemy lair, combat arena, or escape route. Keep the forest contiguous, with enough path and landmark variation for exploration without making the houses enterable.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure because freely exploring and finding items is the entire activity.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, Open World: one contiguous forest can be explored in any order. Match the Scavenger Hunt preset, retaining discoverable items and hidden pockets while omitting an unrequested quest board.

## Config requirements
Use `path-trail` for branching forest footpaths, `alcove-secret` for concealed clearings and foliage-hidden side pockets, and `landmark-focal` for scattered woodland houses that orient exploration. The realistic nighttime forest, dense trees, undergrowth, and exterior houses are required visible geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` Collectible items across off-trail pockets, near houses, and at the ends of minor branches, distributed broadly enough to reward exploring the whole forest. The flashlight itself is player equipment, not a map placement.

## Layout components
One large forest zone contains a branching trail network, dense wooded ground, several small clearings, scattered exterior houses, hidden side pockets, and item sites. Natural tree density forms the outer boundary and prevents the map edge from reading as an artificial wall.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: nighttime atmosphere, forest density, and house landmarks lead, while the branching but non-critical trail plan can be projected afterward.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use large scale because the forest must take a few minutes to cross while houses and routes remain recognisable. The theme is realistic dark nighttime woodland. Only `P0` is required: the build is one exterior surface with no enterable houses, overhangs, or separate maps.
