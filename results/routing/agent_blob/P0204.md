# Scene prompt
Create a realistic 3D forest exploration map set at night in deep darkness, with a flashlight-lit view. The forest spans a few minutes of walking across, with winding paths through dense trees leading to scattered houses, searchable items, and distinct points of interest.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure: exploration and finding items are the whole activity, with no pursuing threat or combat loop.

## Shape and preset
Use the single `world-open` shape as one contiguous forest that can be explored in any order. The closest preset is Scavenger Hunt, retaining its searchable discoveries and item objectives but dropping the unrequested physical quest board.

## Config requirements
Use `path-trail` for winding, flashlight-readable forest tracks; `alcove-secret` for concealed searchable pockets among dense trees; and `landmark-focal` for distinct houses and other points of interest that pull exploration through the darkness. Use `building-interior` because the scattered houses are places to explore inside, linked from the surrounding forest.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` as searchable items distributed across the house interiors and distinct outdoor points of interest, with several discoveries along different branches rather than one linear chain. No checkpoint, enemy emitter, score volume, or spawn arrangement beyond the ordinary player entry is requested.

## Layout components
Build a medium forest a few minutes across, enclosed by dense impassable tree growth. A loose web of winding dirt paths should branch from the entry into deep woods, reconnect selectively, and reach scattered realistic houses, hidden clearings, and recognizable natural landmarks. Dense trees and undergrowth form dark visual corridors while small clearings give each discovery room to read. Compose the forest from a slightly elevated isometric view with the path network, separated houses, and major points of interest all legible despite the night setting.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down plan, because atmosphere, realistic forest density, and flashlight-led composition matter more than exact route topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a medium scale, driven by the stated few-minute walk. The theme is realistic, deep-night woodland with sparse flashlight illumination. Route through `P3` because play crosses between the outdoor forest and enterable house interiors; no other modifier is forced.
