# Scene prompt
Create a 3D roleplay map set on a small downtown high street with a modern city feel. A walkable main street is lined with street-level buildings; the main streetwear shops are enterable for outfit browsing, while the remaining buildings form a street-facing backdrop.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim: players hang out, roleplay, and dress their avatars without a job loop or scored objective. There is no secondary genre.

## Shape and preset
Use `settlement-static`, interpreted as a fixed small downtown high street with no claimable property or player construction. The preset is none: the streetwear shopping focus keeps the shape but does not retain enough of Life, Morph Roleplay, or Dress Up to name one honestly.

## Config requirements
Use `path-street` for a broad pedestrian main street and sidewalks linking every storefront. Use `social-hub` for a widened central stretch where avatars can gather without obstructing shop entrances. Use `building-interior` only for the main streetwear shops that players actually enter; all remaining street-facing buildings are facade geometry. Use `settlement-density` for the compact, continuous downtown frontage rather than scattered buildings. Add visible streetwear racks, mannequins, fitting displays, signs, street furniture, and modern shop windows inside and along the main stores.

## Layout requirements
Place one free-text avatar spawn area at an open end or widened entry plaza of the high street, oriented toward the main shops. Site outfit interaction positions beside the racks and mannequins inside each enterable streetwear store. No job stations, scoring volumes, checkpoints, hostile spawners, or vehicle markers are needed.

## Layout components
Build one straight or gently bending walkable high street with sidewalks on both sides, a compact central gathering area, and a continuous line of modern street-level facades. Make a small number of focal streetwear shops enterable, each with a linked interior, clear doorway, browsing aisles, outfit displays, and fitting space; keep all other buildings shallow street-facing backdrops. Use level paved terrain, urban planters, benches, lamps, signs, and storefront glazing. Bound the map behind the facade rows and frame it down the street axis.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the modern retail atmosphere and storefront composition lead, and the uncomplicated street plan can be derived afterward.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, based on one downtown block-length high street. The theme is clean modern city streetwear. Use `P3` because play crosses from the outdoor street into the focal shop interiors.
