## Enriched image prompt
Build a third-person 3D action-roguelike dungeon as five separate generated floor maps descending through an abandoned underground temple. Every floor contains 6–10 compact stone rooms joined by short corridors and selected from combat, treasure-chest, shop, elite-enemy, and rest-room types. Each room is a small space crossed in seconds; combat rooms hold only one enemy wave with enough clear ground for jumping, rolling, ranged attacks, and close melee. End Floors 1–4 at a prominent downward stone stair or lift passage that loads the next generated floor. Floor 5 terminates in a larger boss chamber for the Echo King and its summoned minions. Keep one coherent torchlit stone-temple language across all five maps, becoming progressively darker, more ruined, and more strongly blue-purple with depth.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Action because room combat, dodging, and weapon switching define the run. Adventure is secondary for the five-floor descent and exploration.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-chaptered`, taken from outside Action's usual set, because each of the five floors is a separately loaded generated map. Within every floor, use `rooms-sequence` for the 6–10 compact chambers and corridors. The matched preset is Hack & Slash, extended across five generated chapters.

## Config requirements
Use `arena-lockin` for doors sealing each combat or elite room until its wave is cleared. Use `gate-chapter` for the visible downward stair or lift threshold at the end of Floors 1–4. Draw the visible halves of four `teleporter-link` pairs as those descent passages and the corresponding arrival points on the next floors. Use free-text image content for treasure rooms, shop counters, rest rooms, and the larger Floor 5 boss chamber. No exterior temple map is generated.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawner-npc` Spawner in every generated combat and elite room, with one boss origin and supporting minion origins in the terminal Floor 5 chamber. Place `collectible-nodes` Collectibles at generated treasure-chest positions. Place four `teleporter-link` Teleporter pairs between consecutive floor exits and arrivals. Place free-text TriggerZones on generated shop counters and rest-room stations. Rune choices, currency, upgrades, weapon switching, health, death persistence, and the ending cinematic are mechanics rather than additional layout.

## Layout components
There are exactly five map segments, Floors 1 through 5. Each uses a compact `rooms-sequence` of 6–10 rooms drawn from the five stated room types. Floors 1–4 end at one downward stair or lift; Floor 5 replaces that terminal threshold with the Echo King boss chamber. Corridors keep every selected room reachable, while stone walls fully enclose each map. Compose each floor as a readable compact network and intensify ruin, darkness, and blue-purple lighting gradually with depth.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: each generated 6–10-room floor graph, room connections, lock-in doors, and reachable descent must be established before the temple dressing. `authored_plan` is not used because this is neither a supported maze nor a racing route.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is medium per floor because 6–10 compact rooms must fit while doors, chests, shops, rest stations, and combat ground remain legible. The theme is a dim mysterious abandoned underground stone temple with torchlight and increasingly strong blue-purple tones. The pipeline requires `P4` for five separately loaded floor maps and `P6` so every generated room graph and descent remains valid by construction.
