# Scene prompt

Create a 3D low-poly retro arena for a platform brawler, framed for PC and console play. A tight duel/practice stage centers on a 60-stud-wide main platform, with two side platforms 24 studs apart and 10 studs above the main stage; a smaller center platform sits directly between them another 8 studs higher. Leave open space around and below the platforms to the stage borders, with a clear sky-facing spawn area above the arena. Use an upscaled N64/PS1-to-GameCube-era visual style, and include a stationary practice dummy on the main platform.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Action, specifically a platform brawler.

## Shape and preset

Use `arena-stacked` instead of the Platform Fighter preset’s `arena-tiered` shape because three upper platforms directly overhang the main stage. Keep the Platform Fighter preset and its requested ring-out structure.

## Config requirements

Use `hazard-kill` for open stage borders and the bottomless space surrounding and beneath the platforms. Use `cover-los` in the preset’s platform-fighter sense: the three upper platforms create movement and attack interruption without adding unrelated pillars. Draw one stationary practice dummy on the main platform.

## Layout requirements

Place one player sky-spawn above the arena, centered to produce a safe drop onto the main platform. Place the single practice dummy on the main platform with clear combo space around it. Add kill volumes beyond every stage border and below the lowest recoverable height. Temporary spawn invincibility and the three-life rule are gameplay logic, not extra geometry.

## Layout components

Build one 60-stud-wide main platform. Place exactly two side platforms 24 studs apart and 10 studs above it, then one smaller center platform directly between them and another 8 studs higher. Preserve open air around and below all platforms, with no scenery obstructing recoveries. Keep the stage tight, symmetrical, sky-facing, and centered in frame; use chunky low-poly forms and clean retro materials.

## Render order

Render in `topdown` order so the exact platform spacing and safe recovery relationships are authoritative before the isometric dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, driven by a tight duel/practice footprint. The theme is upscaled N64/PS1-to-GameCube low-poly retro. Use `P2` because upper platforms occlude the stage below, and `P6` because platform reachability and ring-out geometry must be valid by construction.
