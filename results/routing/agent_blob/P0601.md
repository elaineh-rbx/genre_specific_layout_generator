# Scene prompt
Create a small free-cooking sandbox set in a clean modern restaurant kitchen, composed for a fixed, zoomed-out top-down third-person camera. Arrange an accessible central chopping table with loose food ingredients such as mushrooms, meat, and onions, alongside a cooktop or stove with a frying pan; place a trash can and additional kitchen props around the work area.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The genre is Simulation, specifically an open-ended cooking sandbox where physical kitchen stations and ingredients support free interaction.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single`, taken from outside Simulation’s usual shape set, because the entire playable map is one enclosed restaurant kitchen and the player never transitions outdoors. The preset is none: Simulation’s Sandbox preset assumes isolated building plots and a resource/social loop that this compact cooking room does not have.

## Config requirements
Use `trigger-task` in its visible form for two accessible workstations: one central chopping table and one adjacent cooktop or stove with a frying pan. Also render loose food props including mushrooms, meat, and onions, plus one trash can and additional clean modern kitchen props around the work area. Keep counters and appliances low and clearly separated for the fixed zoomed-out camera.

## Layout requirements
Use `trigger-task` in its placed form for two interaction zones, one aligned to the reachable edge of the chopping table and one aligned to the stove and frying pan. Use `collectible-nodes` for loose interactive food pickups, distributed on the chopping table and nearby prep surfaces without blocking circulation. No ordered progression, gates, or scoring volumes are needed.

## Layout components
Build one small rectangular restaurant-kitchen interior. Put the chopping table at the center as the primary station, with broad circulation on every side. Place the stove and frying pan against one perimeter wall but within a short unobstructed walk from the table. Set one trash can near the prep area without narrowing the route. Arrange mushrooms, meat, onions, and other food items as readable clusters on work surfaces. Use clean counters, pale flooring, stainless appliances, and uncluttered walls. The room walls form the boundary, and every important prop must remain visible and selectable from the fixed top-down camera.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: establish the clean modern materials and readable station silhouettes first, then derive the top-down plan; the layout is simple and does not require topology guaranteed by construction.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a small scale for one focused kitchen room. The theme is clean modern restaurant kitchen with readable stylised props. Route through `P0`; one interior-only room on a single surface needs no outside-to-inside transition.
