# Scene prompt
Create a 3D obby map built as a small vertical tower of bright, colourful floating blocks and platforms against a clear sky. The climb rises roughly 12–15 platform jumps from a base at the bottom to a flag at the top, with a few checkpoint platforms spaced along the ascent; frame the full tower in one view.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Obby & Platformer. The entire game space is a short, player-paced vertical jumping challenge.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-tower`: bright floating platforms stack and wrap upward above the starting base, with upper jumps directly over lower parts of the course. The preset is Tower Obby, tuned away from timed and ladder obstacles toward the explicitly requested block-and-platform jumps.

## Config requirements
Use `path-track` for one ordered chain of roughly 12–15 physics-legal platform jumps. Use `obstacle-jump` for the bright floating block gaps and stepped vertical transfers. Draw the visible halves of `checkpoint-respawn` as a few larger, flat, safely enclosed checkpoint platforms distributed along the ascent. Use the visible half of `winner-zone` as the top platform with a prominent finish flag. Do not introduce moving, timed, guessing, or lethal-contact hazards.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `checkpoint-respawn` on each of the few enlarged checkpoint platforms, spaced through the climb so a fall does not always reset the whole tower. Place the functional half of `winner-zone` on the final top platform directly around the flag. Put the initial spawn and fallback respawn on the broad base platform below the first jump. Every checkpoint must sit on the ordered route rather than on optional scenery.

## Layout components
Build one broad bottom base and one vertical chain of roughly 12–15 brightly colored floating block and platform jumps, rising in a compact spiral or alternating stack. Insert a few visibly larger checkpoint platforms at sensible intervals and finish with one secure top platform carrying a single flag. Keep each jump distinct against a clear sky, with no decorative pieces that could be mistaken for playable footing. Use open air as the boundary around the tower and a simple cloud layer far below. Compose the full structure tightly enough that base, checkpoints, route, and flag all fit in one view.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish the authoritative sequential jump chain and legal gap spacing first, then render the colorful floating-tower appearance.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, set by the roughly 12–15-jump climb and one-pass framing. The theme is bright, simple cartoon blocks against a clear sky. Use `P6` for a valid jump sequence and `P2` because stacked platforms overhang and hide lower surfaces in a single top-down capture.
