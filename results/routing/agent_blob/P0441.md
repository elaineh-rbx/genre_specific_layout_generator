# Scene prompt
Create a realistic 3D layout for a small single-room arcade. The player-facing view is positioned directly in front of one playable pinball machine, with its full cabinet, slanted glass playfield, controls, and upright backbox clearly visible. Place a few additional arcade cabinets around the room as non-playable set dressing, arranged along the walls or behind the pinball machine without obstructing the central view.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Entertainment because this build presents and operates a realistic arcade display rather than supporting avatar traversal. No secondary genre is needed; the pinball rules and menu are gameplay and interface concerns.

## Shape and preset
Use `set-display` with the Display Piece preset. The pinball machine is the deliverable, and the small arcade room frames it for operation from a fixed position without anyone walking through the set.

## Config requirements
Use `landmark-focal` to make the playable pinball cabinet the room’s hero object, fully showing its cabinet, slanted glass playfield, controls, and upright backbox. Use `vignette-photo` for the deliberately composed straight-on player view. Apply `zone-graphics` to concentrate realistic high-detail materials, lights, playfield parts, reflections, and signage on the pinball machine. Draw the visible staging for `spawn-first-reveal` as an unobstructed viewing pocket directly in front of the controls.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawn-first-reveal` player-view marker directly in front of the playable machine, centered on its controls and oriented toward the full cabinet. The view must leave the backbox and playfield visible while excluding obstructing cabinets. No traversal spawns, checkpoints, pickups, or other placement passes are needed.

## Layout components
Build one small enclosed arcade room centered on a single realistic playable pinball machine. Place a few non-playable upright arcade cabinets as wall and rear set dressing, maintaining a clear central viewing cone and access-free buffer around the hero machine. Use dark commercial flooring, painted walls, ceiling light panels, restrained neon accents, power trim, and realistic cabinet materials. The room boundary is fully enclosed, but no exterior or doorway transition is required. Compose the frame from the fixed player position, with the pinball machine dominant and the surrounding cabinets providing depth without visual competition.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the realistic display composition and machine appearance lead, and the top-down is derived from that authoritative look.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by one room and one playable machine. The theme is realistic contemporary arcade with controlled neon detail. Use `P0` with `SET`: ordinary single-surface rendering is sufficient, and traversal and jump-gap checks are skipped because nobody walks through the geometry.
