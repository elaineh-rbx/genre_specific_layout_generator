# Scene prompt
Create a 3D escape-room map set in a hidden underground temple, with four connected chambers forming a focused, logical route toward a final locked exit. Arrange the chambers in sequence through carved stone corridors, each containing distinct puzzle fixtures such as riddle inscriptions, interactive mechanisms, tile-pattern surfaces, item sockets, and stone plinths; include a water container and a plinth that reveals a pattern used in a later chamber. Place the sealed temple exit at the end of the chain, and incorporate lava channels or a rising-lava presence through the temple as a visible threat.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Puzzle: a linked chain of riddles, mechanisms, tiles, and item interactions unlocks the escape.

## Shape and preset
Use `rooms-sequence` for exactly four underground temple chambers solved in order. The preset is Escape Room.

## Config requirements
Use `facade-clue` for riddle inscriptions and the revealed pattern, `button-solve` for interactive mechanisms and tile-pattern inputs, `trigger-solve` for item sockets and the water-receiving plinth, and `gate-progression` for barriers between chambers and the sealed final exit. Draw visible lava channels and rising-lava apertures as a scene-specific threat.

## Layout requirements
Place `trigger-solve` interactions at the water container, the revealing plinth, and the later pattern-use fixture. Place `button-solve` interactions on each mechanical or tile input. Place `collectible-nodes` for the water container and other required key items before their matching sockets. Site one rising-lava hazard volume in each of the four chambers, plus one final exit trigger beyond the unlocked temple door; the 30-minute hard fail resets the run.

## Layout components
Build four carved-stone chambers connected by short corridors in a strict sequence. Give each chamber distinct puzzle surfaces and enough clear floor for interaction. Put the water container before its plinth, frame the revealed pattern clearly, and place the matching input in a later chamber. Thread lava channels visibly through the temple without blocking the initial route. The sealed exit terminates chamber four. Rock walls enclose the entire complex.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first as authoritative, then dress the isometric, because the four-stage dependency chain and corridor connectivity must remain playable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, fixed by four focused chambers. The theme is an ancient hidden stone temple under lava threat. Use `P0`: it is one interior surface without stacked crossings or exterior transitions.
