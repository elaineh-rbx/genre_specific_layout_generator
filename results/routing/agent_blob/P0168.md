## Layout components

Chamber one holds the bucket and water source; chamber two holds the plinth and reveal surface; chamber three holds the pattern-driven tile or interactive input; chamber four holds the final unlock and escape door. Riddle and clue walls face the player's arrival direction. Lava channels connect beneath or along all chambers with visible inlets into each floor. Solid temple walls bound the run. Compose the chain left-to-right in cutaway so all four chambers and their dependencies remain legible.
