# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map set inside a cosy modern family home with a playful cartoon style, viewed at cat scale. Build the house at roughly three times normal scale so the player appears very small beside huge furniture. Arrange explorable domestic rooms connected by doorways and hallways, with prominent scratchable furniture placed throughout each room.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation because scratching furniture produces currency for an endless upgrade loop; the player’s cat form is thematic rather than open-ended roleplay.

## Shape and preset
Use the single `interior-single` shape from outside Simulation’s usual set: the entire map is one oversized family-home interior, with no exterior transition. Preset is none because no published Simulation preset preserves both this house-scale space and the furniture-scratching loop without adding unrequested shops, gates, plots, or roads.

## Config requirements
Use `path-circulation` for cat-scale movement through connected doorways and hallways between every domestic room. Draw `trigger-task` as prominent scratchable furniture distributed through the home: oversized sofas, armchairs, rugs, bed frames, and similar household pieces with accessible scratching faces. Keep all architecture and furniture at roughly three times normal scale relative to the tiny cat.

## Layout requirements
Place a `trigger-task` interaction volume against each scratchable furniture item, aligned to a reachable lower face rather than the top or hidden rear. Every room must contain at least one scratch target, with denser furniture rooms allowed more. Place the player spawn on clear floor near the central circulation route. No winner zone is needed because the scratch-and-upgrade loop is endless.

## Layout components
Build one cohesive home interior comprising a living room, kitchen and dining area, bedrooms, bathroom, and connecting halls. Oversized door openings and uncluttered floor routes connect all rooms while huge furniture creates cat-scale landmarks and sheltered pockets. Use warm cartoon wood, fabric, tile, and painted-wall materials. Walls enclose the whole play space; there is no outside area. Compose the image from a low cat-height angle so furniture towers overhead while the connected route remains legible.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, then dress the isometric, because every explorable room must remain connected by valid doorways and halls.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium in footprint but explicitly three-times-normal in object scale. Theme is cozy modern family home in playful cartoon styling. Use `P0`: one connected, single-level interior needs no overhang, exterior transition, or separate-map pass.
