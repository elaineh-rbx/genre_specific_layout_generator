## Enriched image prompt
Create a compact isolated player farm in a soft pastel, rounded, child-friendly style using buttery yellow, mint green, blush pink, and sky blue. Place one enterable cozy barn beside a flower garden laid out as a 4x5 grid: six central plots active at the start and 14 surrounding plots visibly reserved for expansion, for roughly 20 total. Add a few initial animal-care spaces that can expand with the barn to hold about 12 mini animals, including mini horses, pygmy goats, teacup pigs, fluffy chicks, round bunnies, and baby cows. Connect the garden, barn, paddocks, owner arrival, approved-visitor arrival, shared-goal board, and friendly gift mailbox with short clear paths. Keep all edges soft and safe, with no sharp, scary, or competitive-looking elements.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation because planting, harvesting, animal care, and farm expansion form a repeatable task loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `plot-isolated`, because every player owns a compact independent farm that visitors can enter without sharing ownership. Preset is none because no Simulation preset combines a personal flower farm, expandable barn, and cooperative visits without importing unrelated extraction or tycoon structures.

## Config requirements
`path-circulation` draws short paths among garden, barn, paddocks, arrivals, goal board, and gift mailbox. `gate-progression` draws gentle expansion boundaries around the 14 locked garden plots and future animal spaces. `trigger-task` draws flower and animal-care stations. Universal `building-interior` draws the enterable barn, and `npc-population` draws the active mini animals while respecting an expanded capacity of about 12. Reach outside Simulation's shortlist to Adventure's `tracker-quest` for a plain shared-goal board; the friendly gift mailbox remains direct scene geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place `trigger-task` on 20 flower plots in a 4x5 grid, with six active and 14 expansion-locked initially; each supports sunflowers, tulips, daisies, and roses. Place further `trigger-task` care stations in barn and paddock spaces, and site `npc-population` across those spaces for up to about 12 active animals. Place `collectible-nodes` for harvested flowers, eggs, wool, and milk at their producing zones without inventing per-harvest counts. Place one `tracker-quest` at the shared-goal board, one free-text TriggerZone at the gift mailbox, and free-text SpawnZones for one owner arrival and one approved-visitor arrival per farm.

## Layout components
Each repeated farm module contains one compact isolated plot, one enterable expandable barn, one 20-cell garden, six initially active flower cells, 14 expansion cells, animal spaces for about 12 mini animals, one shared-goal board, one gift mailbox, short paths, and two arrivals. Soft fencing forms the boundary. Compose the barn, full expansion footprint, garden, paddocks, cooperative fixtures, and arrivals in one readable frame.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the cozy pastel farm composition and child-safe visual language are authoritative, while the simple 4x5 expansion grid can be projected from it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is small per farm, directly following the compact single-farm answer. The theme is wholesome, rounded pastel chibi farming. `P3` is required because players move between the exterior farm and the barn interior.
