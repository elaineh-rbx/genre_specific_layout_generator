# Scene prompt
Create a cozy 3D family farm map in a soft pastel, rounded chibi style. Each player has a separate compact farm plot, beginning with one cozy barn, a few animal spaces for mini horses, pygmy goats, teacup pigs, fluffy chicks, round bunnies, and baby cows, plus about 6 flower plots growing sunflowers, tulips, daisies, and roses. Arrange the barn and animal area beside the garden plots with clear open paths for visitors to move between them. Expansion remains within the same single farm area, extending to roughly 20 flower plots and a larger barn holding about 12 mini animals; approved visitors can enter and help around the farm.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The genre is Simulation. Planting, harvesting, animal care, and farm expansion form a repeatable farming-management loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `plot-isolated`: each player owns one separate compact farm that approved visitors can enter without sharing ownership. The preset is none; the available presets do not preserve both personal farm expansion and the specified cooperative caretaking space.

## Config requirements
Use `trigger-task` for visible planting, watering, feeding, and animal-care stations. Use `path-circulation` for broad visitor-friendly paths between barn, pens, and garden. Use `npc-population` for the mini horses, pygmy goats, teacup pigs, fluffy chicks, round bunnies, and baby cows occupying their animal spaces.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional `trigger-task` locations at every flower bed and within each animal-care area. Begin with about 6 flower-plot stations and reserve a regular expansion pattern to roughly 20. Place about 12 animal occupancy or care positions around the fully expanded barn, divided among the six named animal kinds. The functional half of `npc-population` sites those animals in pens beside the barn.

## Layout components
Each compact farm is one bounded plot with a cozy barn on one side, a few adjacent animal spaces, and the flower garden opposite them. Open rounded paths connect the entrance, garden, barn, and pens so visitors can circulate without crossing planted beds. Show about 6 initial plots and clearly reserved ground for expansion to roughly 20; enlarge the barn footprint toward capacity for about 12 mini animals. Use level pastel ground, rounded fences, soft planting beds, and no harsh boundary features.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the chibi farm charm and readable barn-garden relationship lead, and the simple expansion grid follows.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium across multiple compact farms. The theme is soft pastel, rounded, wholesome chibi countryside. Route `P0`; all play remains on one continuous, non-overlapping surface.
