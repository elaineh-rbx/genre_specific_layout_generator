# Scene prompt
Create a 3D supercar driving world set in a compact neon-lit city, sized to drive across in about 2–3 minutes. Lay out interconnected city streets for cruising, with the garage lobby as a distinct accessible hub area where supercars are displayed and acquired.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation. Driving generates currency used to acquire more supercars, but the spatial activity remains open-ended vehicle cruising rather than racing.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, one shared compact city that can be driven in any order. The preset is Vehicle Sim, retaining the open world and vehicle circulation while replacing the unrelated resource field with the requested garage progression hub.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for interconnected supercar-width streets and broad turns. Use `social-hub` for the garage lobby where cars are displayed and acquired. Use `building-interior` because players must access that garage from the city exterior. Use `settlement-density` for a compact neon city rather than scattered structures. The visible garage should include several display positions, although no exact number of supercars was stated.

## Layout requirements
Place player vehicle spawns just outside the garage exit, oriented toward the street with enough launch clearance. Place one player arrival marker in the lobby and interaction markers at each supercar display or acquisition bay. The currency amount is gameplay, not geometry, so the stated ten-dollar driving reward adds no layout component.

## Layout components
Build a compact neon-lit city taking about 2–3 minutes to cross. Use interconnected streets with at least one broad cruising loop, several cross streets, readable intersections, sidewalks, and occasional plazas or parking pockets. Put the garage lobby in a prominent block beside a major road. Its entrance must connect cleanly from interior showroom floor to exterior driveway, with display bays arranged around a central circulation aisle. Surrounding modern building shells and neon signs create a dense night-city frame without crowding vehicle lanes. Close the map with blocked street continuations and skyline facades.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: street continuity, garage access, and the interior-to-driveway connection should be authoritative before neon visual dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, driven by the 2–3-minute crossing time. The theme is playful neon supercar nightlife. Use `P3` because play crosses between the outdoor city and enterable garage lobby; no other modifier is forced.
