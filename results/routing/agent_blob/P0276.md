# Scene prompt
Create a fully 3D, enormous 2000x2000-stud multiplayer horror forest map at night, with an open-world feel that supports several minutes of exploration. Dense giant trees and abundant undergrowth fill the landscape, divided into visually distinct forest zones through varied terrain and atmosphere: narrow winding paths, rocky hills, fallen logs, shallow rivers, small lakes, light fog, deep moonlit shadows, sight-blocking tree cover, open clearings, enclosed thickets, and pockets of near-total darkness. Interweave branching routes, alternate trails, shortcuts, and natural hiding places rather than linear paths. Place key landmarks throughout the forest: an abandoned cabin with a small simple explorable interior, an accessible watchtower with a compact interior, an accessible cave entrance leading into a small simple cave interior, a destroyed campsite, a wooden bridge over water, and scattered abandoned vehicles. Use realistic dark nighttime lighting, moonlight, fog, and optimized Roblox-scale environmental composition.

# Agent decision
### Genre
The dominant genre is Survival because the original brief explicitly designs the forest for multiplayer pursuit and hiding, even though this deliverable is scenery only.

### Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, taken from outside Survival’s usual shape set, for one contiguous forest explored freely rather than a bounded round arena or forced route. Preset is none: 1 vs All suggests the pursuit intent, but replacing its shape and omitting its escape and role-start options leaves too little of that preset intact.

### Config requirements
Use `cover-los` for dense trunks, undergrowth, fallen logs, rocks, thickets, and natural hiding pockets that repeatedly break sightlines. Use `building-interior` for the small explorable cabin, compact watchtower room, and cave interior. Use `water-body` for shallow rivers and small lakes, and `terrain-relief` for rocky hills. Draw branching narrow trails, alternate routes, clearings, fog, the ruined camp, wooden bridge, cave mouth, and abandoned vehicles as scene-specific geometry.

### Layout requirements
Nothing invisible needs placement in this scenery-only request: there are no NPC spawners, role starts, pickups, checkpoints, gates, or scoring volumes.

### Layout components
Build one 2000x2000-stud forest divided into visually distinct atmospheric regions without threat tiers. A web of winding trails and shortcuts links the cabin, watchtower, cave, destroyed campsite, bridge, and scattered vehicles. Rivers and lakes interrupt the wooded ground; the bridge crosses a useful route. Giant trees and enclosed thickets alternate with open clearings. Rocky hills shape sightlines, while fallen logs and rocks create local concealment. Keep the perimeter distant and naturally masked by dense woodland, and compose the landmarks as sparse navigational anchors.

### Render order
Use `isometric`: appearance, scale, moonlit depth, and foliage composition lead, while the top-down plan can be projected from that image.

### Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge, fixed by the stated 2000x2000 studs. The theme is realistic moonlit forest horror with fog and deep shadow. Use `P3` for enterable structures and cave access, `P2` for the cave beneath enclosing terrain, `tiered` for hills without overhang elsewhere, and `CHECK` for traversable water.
