# Scene prompt
Create a compact, military-themed 3D battle arena for a 6-player free-for-all, with practical cover arranged to support fast movement. Build a clear central combat zone with low barriers, crates, and sightline breaks, surrounded by connected side lanes and elevated firing positions for varied assault-rifle, sniper, revolver, shotgun, and pistol engagements.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Shooter: this is a six-player free-for-all deathmatch built around ranged engagements and repeated respawning.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-network` as one compact arena with a central combat lane and connected side lanes that repeatedly feed players back into fights. The preset is Arena Deathmatch, retaining its interconnected routes and elevated positions, dropping its unrequested power-up pickups, and adding practical tactical cover.

## Config requirements
Use `path-loop` for side routes with multiple entrances and no trapping dead ends. Use `cover-elevated` for reachable military firing terraces overlooking selected lanes, with exposed stairs and no surfaces overhanging the arena floor. Use `cover-los` for evenly distributed 3–4 stud low barriers, crates, and 7–8 stud full-body sightline breaks. Use the visible geometry of `spawn-protected` to shield respawn points from direct sniper lines.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` as six respawn positions, one per player capacity, distributed around the perimeter behind cover; each must open onto at least two routes and remain outside direct long-range sightlines. No objective, loot, or NPC placement is needed.

## Layout components
The compact central combat zone occupies the middle, ringed by connected side lanes that return to the centre from several angles. Low barriers and crate clusters break cross-map fire without blocking rapid dashes or crouched movement. A small number of raised firing terraces sit near the outer edge, reached by exposed stairs, while close-range pockets beside cover support shotguns and pistols. Military walls enclose the arena. Frame the whole lane network clearly, with the centre dominant and every perimeter route legible.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, because the interconnected lanes, protected respawn approaches, and balanced sightlines must remain playable; dress the isometric from that plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by six players and a fast 2:30 match. The theme is practical modern military training-ground architecture. Use `tiered` because raised firing terraces add elevation without overhang; no other modifier is forced.
