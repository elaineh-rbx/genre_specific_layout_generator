# Scene prompt

Create a minimal playable coin-collection test fixture centered on a plain grey 30×30 floor at position (40, 3, 0). Arrange five gold Neon spherical coins spaced above the floor, with no additional dressing or surrounding environment.

# Agent decision

## Genre

This is `No Genre`: it is a direct, minimal Studio test fixture rather than a developed game genre. Coin behavior identifies a playable purpose but does not justify inventing a broader adventure or simulation.

## Shape and preset

There is no genre shape. Leave `enclosure`, `verticality`, `zone-count`, `structure`, and `play-space` at their defaults: one exterior, single-surface fixture. The preset is none because all generic place presets would add unrequested scenery or circulation.

## Config requirements

Draw only a plain grey square floor measuring exactly 30×30, centered at world position (40, 3, 0). Keep the image free of dressing, landscape, walls, surrounding environment, and pre-drawn coins because the collectible objects are placed after segmentation.

## Layout requirements

Place `collectible-nodes` as exactly five gold Neon spherical coins named Coin1 through Coin5. Space them distinctly above the floor, all within the 30×30 footprint, anchored and non-colliding. Their award, debounce, hide, restore, leaderstats, and script behavior are gameplay implementation details rather than image or spatial layout requirements.

## Layout components

The sole zone is one flat 30×30 grey floor part at (40, 3, 0). There are no paths, terrain features, surrounding props, or enclosing boundary. The five coin placements form a readable spread over the floor with sufficient separation for individual collection. Frame the whole square and all five positions clearly from above and at an angle.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render the minimal floor first and derive its top-down plan, since collectible placement is simple and no topology can invalidate play.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is small, fixed by the 30×30 test floor. The theme is untextured grey-and-gold test geometry. Use `P0`; this is one unobstructed surface with no transition, overhang, or structure-critical route.
