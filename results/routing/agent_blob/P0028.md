## Enriched image prompt
Create a bright, toon-like 3D forest map for a friendly animal adoption game. Human Roblox characters move through one large open woodland containing friendly forest animals, with a simple central clearing where people can meet, adopt, and care for them. Keep the forest welcoming and navigable, with plain foot trails connecting the clearing to animal gathering areas among the trees.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, specifically human-led pet care rather than an animal-player simulation.

## Shape and preset
Use `wilderness-open`: the map is one open natural forest rather than a town of claimable houses. The Pet Care preset does not remain recognisable after replacing its settlement shape and omitting its unrequested interiors and town streets, so the preset is none.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for the central forest adoption-and-care clearing, sized as the shared meeting point without turning it into a town square. Use `path-trail`, taken from Adventure through the shared catalogue, for plain walkable forest trails linking the clearing to animal areas. Use `npc-population` for the friendly forest animals and the ground they inhabit.

## Layout requirements
The `npc-population` placement distributes friendly animals through several woodland gathering areas and a smaller group around the central care clearing, while keeping trails unobstructed. No hostile spawners are implied by the protective-animal rule.

## Layout components
The central care clearing anchors the composition. Tree-filled woodland spreads around it on all sides, with simple trails radiating to animal gathering areas and reconnecting so people can circulate. The forest edge forms the natural map boundary. Keep individual animals and the central clearing readable in one isometric frame.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the welcoming forest look and placement of animals around the care clearing lead, while the uncomplicated trail network can be projected afterward.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large, preserving a broad surrounding forest while keeping animals legible. The theme is bright, toon-like, friendly woodland roleplay. The pipeline cost is `P0`: one continuous natural surface has no overhang, interior transition, or separate map.
