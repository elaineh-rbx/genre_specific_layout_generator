# Scene prompt
Create a 3D animal-adoption game map set in a huge, bright toon-like forest with a friendly, welcoming atmosphere. The playable space places people among forest surroundings, with friendly animals present throughout the woods as protective companions.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim because human players adopt and care for friendly animals in an open-ended social setting.

## Shape and preset
Use the single `world-open` shape from outside the genre’s usual set: one contiguous forest that people explore in any order. It fits better than `wilderness-open`, whose animal-sim wording assumes players inhabit the space as creatures. Preset is none because Pet Care would add a town hub, enterable venues, and pedestrian streets that the forest-only brief never requests.

## Config requirements
Apply `npc-population` to show friendly forest animals present throughout the woods as protective companions, along with clear ground where they can gather without blocking human movement. Draw a huge bright toon-like forest with welcoming colors, broad clearings, layered tree clusters, soft undergrowth, and readable natural routes, but do not invent buildings or adoption storefronts.

## Layout requirements
Place the `npc-population` across multiple forest clearings and route edges, with several friendly animals in each broad area rather than one dense central crowd. Their placements need adjacent navigable ground for people to approach and care for them. Place human player spawn markers in a generous welcoming clearing with direct access to the forest routes. No hostile spawners, pickups, checkpoints, or completion zone are required.

## Layout components
Build one huge continuous forest zone. A broad starting clearing opens into several loosely connected woodland clearings separated by colorful tree masses and low toon foliage. Gentle dirt paths and visible gaps between trees keep navigation readable while retaining a natural forest feel. Add logs, mushrooms, flowers, rocks, and small resting pockets for the animals. Use thick trees and vegetation as the outer boundary rather than hard walls. Compose the map with the welcoming start clearing in the foreground and successive animal-filled clearings receding through the woods.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down image, because the friendly toon atmosphere and animal-focused composition lead while the open forest has forgiving topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge because the author explicitly requests a huge forest. The theme is bright, colorful, welcoming toon woodland. Use `P0`: one walkable surface, no interiors, overhangs, water volume, or separate maps.
