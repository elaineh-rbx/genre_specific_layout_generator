# Scene prompt

Create a 3D stage-based parkour map as one continuous floating course, with Zone 1 leading directly into Zone 2 and then Zone 3. Zone 1 is a simple floating-platform course lasting about 1–2 minutes, ending at a physical reward-spawner machine displaying common and rare blocky pet figures: a green cactus-block elephant with brown ears, a yellow noob-like figure with blue torso, green legs and flat orange hat, a sunglasses-and-gold-chain character in a blue jacket, and a green gnome-like creature with a long white beard and yellow eyes. Zone 2 is a 2–3 minute harder course with moving platforms and lava hazard blocks, ending at another physical spawner with a blue shark on legs in blue sneakers and a cylindrical wooden totem holding a baseball bat. Zone 3 is a 3–4 minute extreme course of very long jumps and tiny platforms, ending at a secret OG spawner pad for a sparkling bright-red blocky elephant with green leaves on its head; place a visible 3D countdown timer above this pad. Include physical 3D pet and reward stations throughout the map, with room for replay loops back toward earlier stages.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Obby & Platformer because difficulty comes from completing real parkour geometry. Simulation is secondary for the timed pet-collection and repeatable reward loop.

## Shape and preset

Use `course-flat`, one continuous floating point-to-point course divided into three sequential difficulty zones. The Classic Obby preset applies without checkpoints, supplemented by reward stations, lava, and replay circulation.

## Config requirements

Draw `path-track` through Zones 1, 2, and 3. Use `obstacle-jump` throughout, `obstacle-moving` in Zone 2, and `hazard-kill` for its lava blocks. Add `path-circulation` as a safe replay route from the Zone 3 end toward earlier stages. Draw three physical reward stations: Zone 1 displays four specified figures, Zone 2 displays two, and Zone 3 displays one sparkling red elephant on a secret pad beneath a visible 3D countdown timer.

## Layout requirements

Place four pet spawn sockets at the Zone 1 machine and two at Zone 2, each with a 30-second collection window and 1–2-minute respawn cycle. Place one exclusive Zone 3 pet socket, spawning every 5 minutes for exactly 30 seconds, directly under its countdown. Site each machine on a safe end platform clear of hazards. Place a replay return path from Zone 3 to the Zone 1 approach. Timing and follower bonuses are mechanics, but their physical pads and clearance are layout requirements.

## Layout components

Build three linked floating zones: a 1–2-minute simple platform run, a 2–3-minute moving-platform and lava run, and a 3–4-minute extreme run of tiny platforms and very long authored jumps. Each ends on a broad reward deck holding its machine and named pet displays. The final deck includes the secret red-elephant pad and overhead timer. Open fall space surrounds the route; no baseplate is present. A distinct safe loop-back walkway skirts the course to earlier zones. Compose the map diagonally so the escalating zones and all three reward decks read in sequence.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: plan the authoritative route first because all three courses and the replay loop must remain connected and completable, then dress it isometrically.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is large, driven by three courses totaling about 6–9 minutes. The theme is colorful floating brainrot parkour with blocky collectible characters. Use `P6` for valid obstacle construction; the explicitly extreme long jumps require authored validation rather than ordinary baseline spacing.
