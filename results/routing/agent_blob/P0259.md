# Scene prompt

Create a 3D mini-concert performance map with a selectable avatar performing a single song on one of 3 stages combined with any of 3 environments. The default is a compact modern concert club: a small raised stage at the front, a small ambient crowd facing the performer, dramatic colored lighting, and a dark indoor venue arranged for clear recording angles. Each stage and environment pairing should form a distinct performance backdrop while preserving an open view of the performer and audience.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Entertainment: players create and record short performance showcases rather than pursue a spatial objective.

## Shape and preset

Use `venue-stage` from the Performance Venue preset. Every selectable combination preserves one shared layout grammar: performer on a raised stage, a crowd floor facing it, and unobstructed recording views.

## Config requirements

Draw `social-hub` as the compact audience floor facing the performer. Use `vignette-photo` for several clean recording compositions around the frontage, each preserving the performer and audience in frame. Add `npc-population` as a small ambient crowd oriented toward the stage. Provide exactly 3 interchangeable stage designs and exactly 3 interchangeable environment backdrops; every stage must fit every environment without changing the core footprint. The default pairing is a small modern club stage in a dark indoor venue with dramatic colored lighting.

## Layout requirements

Place the layout half of `npc-population` as a small crowd cluster across the audience floor, leaving the recording sightlines open. Place one performer anchor at center stage for the selected avatar. Place recording anchors at clear frontal and three-quarter positions around the immediate frontage, outside the crowd’s densest area.

## Layout components

Build one compact performance footprint with a small raised stage at the front, a shallow open audience floor, and a dark enclosing backdrop. Create 3 swappable stage modules on the same dimensions and 3 swappable environment shells around the same stage-and-crowd plan, supporting 9 possible pairings without overlap. Keep speakers, lighting truss, colored fixtures, and modest club dressing away from the performer sightline. The crowd faces one way toward the stage, while the recording compositions retain both performer and audience.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render the authoritative dressed venue and modular relationship first, then derive the top-down plan because visual recording composition leads.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, driven by a compact club footprint despite its modular variants. The theme is dark modern concert club with dramatic colored light. Use `P0`; all variants share one single-level interior footprint with no separate maps or transitions.
