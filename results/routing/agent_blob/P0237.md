# Scene prompt

Create a compact 3D carnival throwing booth enclosed by a back wall and side walls. A bright red ball sits at the bottom center of the play area, facing three clearly separated horizontal target rows a few metres deep: a foreground row, a middle row, and a background row near the back wall. Arrange wooden targets at varying positions to the left and right of center along each row.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Party & Casual: a short carnival throwing minigame played in one fixed booth.

## Shape and preset

Use `range-directed`, taken from outside the genre’s usual shapes, because the player faces forward from a safe throwing line into a compact target field and does not traverse it. The preset is none; the Minigame preset’s separate lobby, spectator area, and teleports are not present.

## Config requirements

Draw one enclosed carnival booth with a back wall, two side walls, a front throwing position, one bright red ball at bottom center, and wooden targets distributed left and right across exactly three clearly separated depth rows. Keep the foreground, middle, and background rows visually distinct over only a few metres.

## Layout requirements

Place one ball spawn marker at the bottom-center throwing position. Place three row-wide target spawn regions, one on each depth row, allowing replacement targets to shift left or right while remaining on their assigned row. Apply `trigger-scoring` to the target faces so hits register at the three corresponding depths. Nobody walks through the target field.

## Layout components

The booth is one small rectangular interior-like set viewed from its open front. The throwing line occupies the bottom-center foreground. Beyond it, three parallel horizontal target rails or shallow platforms step back toward the rear wall: foreground, middle ground, and background. Wooden target silhouettes vary laterally along each row. Bright painted trim frames the timber back and side walls, while the floor remains flat and uncluttered. The walls form the boundary, and the composition faces straight downrange so all three rows remain readable without overlap.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, then produce the isometric dressing; preserving the three non-overlapping depth bands is necessary for target placement and readable play.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a small scale, matching a real carnival booth only a few metres deep. The theme is colorful fairground timber with vivid red throwing balls. Use `P0` for the single flat bounded set and append `SET` because no avatar traverses the geometry.
