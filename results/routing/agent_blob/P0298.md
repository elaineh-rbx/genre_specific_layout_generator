# Scene prompt
Create a 3D third-person co-op shooter test arena set in one compact ruined futuristic city block, sized for squads of up to four players. The arena is an open central fighting space rather than strict lanes, crossed by broken roads and surrounded or framed by damaged buildings. Arrange abandoned vehicles, concrete barriers, and wreckage throughout as navigable obstacles, with several designated enemy spawn points around the block and clear paths through the cover. Use flickering lights, fog, or atmospheric particles to reinforce the ruined-city setting while preserving open sightlines and room for combat.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Shooter is the dominant and only genre. The authored loop is a cooperative third-person firearm fight against waves, organized around sightlines, cover, and enemy approach directions.

## Shape and preset
Use `open-battlefield` for one compact, contiguous ruined block with a central fighting space and dispersed obstacles rather than lanes. Preset is none: PvE Shooter supplies the wave-spawner concept, but its lane shape, chokepoints, and enterable interiors contradict or exceed this focused open test arena.

## Config requirements
Use `cover-los` for evenly distributed concrete barriers, abandoned vehicles, wreckage, and substantial building corners that break enemy sightlines while retaining readable firing distances. Use `settlement-density` for the single dense futuristic city block framed by damaged urban buildings. Do not add `building-interior`; the buildings surround or frame the block, and no exterior-to-interior play is requested.

## Layout requirements
Place four `spawn-protected` player arrival markers together along one sheltered edge of the arena, screened by solid geometry from every hostile entry direction. Place several `spawner-npc` enemy-wave origins around the outer block, each associated with a clear, readable approach into the central fighting space and never directly behind the squad spawn. No fixed objectives, weapon pickups, or scoring volumes are required.

## Layout components
Build one compact ruined city block around an open central combat zone. Cross it with broken road surfaces and maintain multiple clear walking paths through distributed vehicle wrecks, concrete barriers, and debris clusters. Damaged futuristic buildings define the outer frame without turning circulation into strict lanes. Keep cover varied but low enough that broad sightlines survive across the arena. Bound the play space with ruined facades, rubble, and blocked side streets. Compose the first view from the protected squad edge toward the open center, with enemy approaches visible beyond layered cover. Use flickering practical lights, restrained fog, and atmospheric particles without obscuring targets.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: cover spacing, enemy approaches, spawn shielding, and clear circulation are gameplay-critical and must be established before ruined-city dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small because the answer fixes the arena at one compact city block, sized for up to four players. The theme is a performance-conscious ruined futuristic metropolis with cold lights, fog, and damaged infrastructure. Use `P0`; the arena is one exterior surface with no overhang-dependent routes, interiors, or separate maps.
