# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map showing a small sandstone pyramid in a desert, Egyptian-style setting, built as a short playable climb. A clear upward route runs from the sandy base to the pyramid summit across stepped stone tiers, with spikes, bombs, and other hazardous obstacles placed along the ascent.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Obby & Platformer. The player controls a short upward obstacle-course climb and restarts after lethal failures; increasing speed is a movement mechanic, not a stat-grinder economy.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-terraced`: the route climbs exposed stepped sandstone tiers from the desert floor to the pyramid summit, with strong elevation but no overhanging platforms. The closest preset is Precision Platformer, retaining its terraced shape while replacing its optional moving obstacles and shortcuts with the explicitly requested lethal hazards.

## Config requirements
Use `path-track` for one clear sequential ascent across the pyramid steps. Use `obstacle-jump` for short physics-legal gaps and stepped rises between tiers. Use `hazard-kill` for visible spikes, bombs, and other contact-lethal obstacle surfaces along the route. Use the visible half of `winner-zone` as a safe summit platform marking the end. Do not add `checkpoint-respawn`, because this deliberately short climb restarts from the base after death.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `winner-zone` on the pyramid summit, centered beyond the final hazard. Place one player start and respawn anchor on a safe sandy apron at the pyramid base. Hazard functionality must align exactly to each rendered spike strip, bomb footprint, and other lethal obstacle rather than spilling onto safe tier landings.

## Layout components
Build one small sandstone pyramid rising from a compact desert floor. A single readable route starts at a safe base apron, zigzags upward across broad stepped tiers, and terminates at a flat summit. Distribute spikes, bombs, and a few similarly legible lethal obstacles along the ascent, always leaving an achievable ordered path and safe recovery footing between challenges. Use sand, weathered sandstone blocks, sparse Egyptian-style carvings, and a few distant desert rocks. Bound the course with open sand and an impassable outer perimeter. Frame the pyramid centrally so the complete climb and summit read together.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish a valid sequential hazard route and legal jump spacing first, then dress the authoritative plan as a sandstone pyramid.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by the requested short sprint. The theme is bright desert-Egyptian sandstone with readable deadly traps. Use `P6` for physics-valid course construction and `tiered` for the strong non-overhanging ascent.
