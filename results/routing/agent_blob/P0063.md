# Scene prompt
Create a 3D Roblox R15 fighting arena as a small, simple stylized toon dojo. The compact arena has an open central combat floor with dojo-style boundary walls and clear open space around the player spawn, with 5–10 enemy NPC spawn positions distributed around the perimeter and across the map. Keep the arena uncluttered; the upgrade shop is screen-only and has no physical kiosk.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Action because the playable space is expressly a compact NPC fighting arena built around close physical combat.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded` as a flat, single-level toon dojo arena with a collision-clean combat floor. The preset is none: Boss Raid suggests NPC emitters but also adds destructive cover and ring-out hazards that this intentionally basic, uncluttered arena does not request.

## Config requirements
No canonical image-config option is required. The visible build is the shape itself: a simple dojo floor enclosed by dojo-style boundary walls, with clear open fighting room and no physical shop kiosk, cover clutter, hazards, stands, or breakable structures.

## Layout requirements
Place one player spawn marker in the open central floor, with a clear safety radius around it. Apply `spawner-npc` to place 5–10 enemy NPC spawn positions: distribute them around the perimeter and across the map, keep them out of the player's immediate clear radius, and provide enough separation that the whole group does not converge from one point. These emitters support defeated NPC respawns in place. The screen-only shop creates no map placement.

## Layout components
Build one compact rectangular or lightly octagonal combat zone. Its largest component is an open central dojo floor with a readable flat surface and generous circulation around the central player spawn. Low-detail stylized dojo boundary walls fully enclose the floor, with a few simple posts and trim elements carrying the theme without becoming collision clutter. Reserve perimeter pockets and a handful of evenly spaced floor positions for the 5–10 NPC emitters. Use clean toon materials, warm timber accents, pale wall panels, and a strong boundary silhouette. Compose the arena centrally in frame so the full enclosure, open floor, and spawn distribution remain legible.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then convert it to top-down, because this simple bounded floor has no topology that needs construction-first validation.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by a compact arena for one player and 5–10 enemies. The theme is a lightweight stylized toon dojo. Use `P0`: the map is one flat, enclosed surface with no overhangs, transitions, separate maps, or structurally constrained route.
