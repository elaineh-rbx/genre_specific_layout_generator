# Scene prompt

Create a true 3D boss-battle map viewed from a first-person or over-the-shoulder camera: a dark stone-ruin arena formed as a circular pit, with open central ground for one large, distinctive fearsome boss creature rendered as a detailed custom 3D mesh. The player combat space surrounds the boss within the pit, enclosed by ruin walls.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Action because one player directly fights a mobile boss in a shared clash arena.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-bounded` for one circular, single-level combat pit. The preset is none: although Boss Raid is the nearest family, its destructible clusters and environmental hazards contradict the requested straightforward open central ground.

## Config requirements

Draw one large fearsome boss creature as a distinctive detailed custom 3D mesh in the central pit; it must not be a primitive-block assembly, flat sprite, or interface image. Keep the surrounding dark stone floor open and collision-clean. Draw enclosing ruin walls and restrained broken masonry around the perimeter without turning the center into cover or hazards.

## Layout requirements

Place exactly one `spawner-npc` at the center of the pit for the unique boss, with sufficient clear radius for its movement and attacks. Place one player arrival marker near the arena edge, facing the boss but outside its immediate spawn footprint. The visible boss health bar and victory state are interface and gameplay logic rather than map geometry.

## Layout components

Build one circular stone pit with a broad flat central combat floor, a peripheral player movement ring, and continuous ruined stone walls enclosing the arena. Include one custom boss model at center, one player entry point at the edge, sparse cracked paving, and limited wall rubble kept outside the active floor. The boundary must clearly contain combat while leaving the sky or upper ruin silhouette visible. Center the composition on the boss and preserve readable attack distance in every direction.

## Render order

Render in `isometric` order because the boss silhouette and dark ruin atmosphere are authoritative, while the circular plan is simple.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is medium, driven by the movement radius required for one large boss and ranged combat. The theme is dark, fearsome stone ruin. The route is `P0`: the arena is one flat bounded surface without overhangs or transitions.
