# Scene prompt

Create a 3D slingshot game map set around a lake. A fixed slingshot stands on the lake shore in the foreground, aimed across roughly 35 studs of open water toward a crocodile positioned in the middle of the lake. Leave a clear, unobstructed launch path between the shore and the crocodile.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Shooter because the activity is a fixed-position aiming gallery in which piggy projectiles are fired at one target. Nobody walks through the built space.

## Shape and preset

Use `range-directed` from the Aim Trainer preset. Here it is one firing point on shore facing directly across the lake toward a single crocodile target, with all playable action downrange. Keep the preset while omitting its weapon-swap station because the brief specifies one fixed slingshot.

## Config requirements

Draw `target-practice` as one large crocodile target clearly readable in the lake center, with a visible hit area for piggies. Draw `water-body` as roughly 35 studs of open lake between shore and target. Also draw one fixed slingshot in the foreground and a clear trajectory corridor with no trees, rocks, or structures obstructing it.

## Layout requirements

For `target-practice`, place one scoring target volume tightly against the crocodile’s hittable body in the lake center. Place one projectile launch origin at the slingshot on the near shore, oriented straight toward the crocodile and separated from it by roughly 35 studs. Nothing else needs spawning or collecting in the map.

## Layout components

Build a small lakeside display set with one compact foreground shore pad, one fixed slingshot centered on that pad, a calm open lake, and one crocodile in the middle distance. Preserve an unobstructed 35-stud line from slingshot to crocodile. Low shoreline edging may frame the sides, but nothing should intrude into the launch corridor. Compose the slingshot large in the foreground and the crocodile centered beyond it so the aim relationship reads immediately.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative, then derive the top-down view; the simple straight firing relationship does not require computed topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, driven by the single 35-stud shot. The theme is cute, bright lakeside arcade play. Use `CHECK` for the water volume and `SET` because the scenery is real geometry but the player remains fixed rather than traversing it.
