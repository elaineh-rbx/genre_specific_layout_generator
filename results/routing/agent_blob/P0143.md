# Scene prompt
Create a themed avatar-making space where the entire environment swaps to the selected theme, with three launch themes: Fairy Garden, Lava Island, and Beach. Build the Fairy Garden theme first, presenting the avatar catalog area and overall setting as a cohesive fairy-garden environment.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim because free-form avatar creation is the activity. There is no secondary genre and no contest, scoring, or round structure.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded` for each self-contained avatar-making environment, taking this shape from outside the genre’s usual set because the whole setting is replaced rather than organized as a town, runway, or housing model. Build the Fairy Garden variant first. The preset is none: Dress Up assumes a runway and social-show bundle that this free-form catalog space does not have.

## Config requirements
No loaded canonical option is required beyond the chosen shape. Add free-text visible geometry for a cohesive Fairy Garden avatar catalog: flower-shaped category displays, vine-framed mirrors, mushroom and petal seating, fairy lights, decorative wings, clothing stands, accessory pedestals, and a central avatar-preview area. The catalog fixtures must look native to this theme rather than like generic shop furniture placed in a garden. Preserve three distinct environment variants at launch—Fairy Garden, Lava Island, and Beach—with the same functional setup reskinned and recomposed for each.

## Layout requirements
Place one free-text player spawn at the edge of the Fairy Garden, oriented toward the central catalog and preview area. Site avatar-category interaction positions around the central clearing with enough separation for unobstructed selection and previewing. Theme selection and swapping are systems, not visible layout geometry; no voting, scoring, checkpoints, collectibles, or hostile emitters are needed.

## Layout components
Build the Fairy Garden as a bounded outdoor clearing with a central avatar-preview space and catalog stations arranged in a readable ring. Connect them with short petal or stepping-stone paths through flowers, oversized leaves, mushrooms, vines, and softly glowing fairy decorations. Use gentle garden ground, shallow decorative planting beds, and a foliage boundary that encloses the space without feeling like a wall. Frame the catalog ring and central avatar together. Later Lava Island and Beach variants must replace the whole setting rather than coexist beside it.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the cohesive Fairy Garden identity first, then derive its simple radial plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, driven by one complete catalog environment per theme. The first theme is luminous whimsical Fairy Garden. Use `P4` because the three launch themes are separate whole-environment maps, never simultaneous zones on one surface.
