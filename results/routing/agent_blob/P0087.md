# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map set in a simple warehouse with a broad open concrete floor. Arrange separate player build lanes side by side, each with a starting area of stacked milk crates and room for an independently built tall crate staircase; the route rises to a top turnaround point and descends back to the floor.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation because building a physics crate structure is the central activity. Obby & Platformer is secondary because players then race up and down what they built; its genre-wide route does not lead.

## Shape and preset
Use `plot-isolated` for equal side-by-side player build lanes that cannot overlap. Match the Physics Sim preset with this isolated shape substituted, retaining its physics contraption and circulation concept while omitting an unrequested environmental hazard.

## Config requirements
Apply `physics-rig` to the stackable milk-crate assemblies whose stability and collapse reactions are the entertainment. Apply `path-circulation` to each independent up-to-turnaround-and-back route. Apply `winner-zone` visibly as a clearly marked floor-level finish area reached after descending.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional side of `winner-zone` once per player lane at the return point on the warehouse floor, not at the top turnaround. Each lane also needs one build-start marker beside its initial crate supply and a distinct turnaround marker at the staircase summit. The player count is unspecified, so lane count must remain configurable rather than invented.

## Layout components
Build one simple warehouse interior with a broad open concrete floor. Divide the floor into equal parallel lanes with generous safety gaps so one collapsing crate staircase cannot strike or obstruct a neighbour’s build. Each lane starts with a clearly bounded staging area containing stacked milk crates, extends through an open construction footprint large enough for a tall staircase, rises to a visible top turnaround point, and returns to a marked finish on the floor. Keep structural columns and stored materials at the perimeter, leaving the race lanes unobstructed. Enclose the scene with warehouse walls and frame the parallel stacks so their independent progress is easy to compare.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the tall crate assemblies and their vertical relationship are visually authoritative, while the simple lane plan can be derived afterward.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by multiple independent tall builds inside one venue. The theme is utilitarian concrete warehouse with reusable milk crates. Use `P2` because stacked staircase surfaces overhang lower levels; do not add dominant-genre `P6`.
