# Scene prompt

Create a 3D game map for “Elements Monsters” as one continuous magical forest, beginning at an entrance circle marked “Come in the circle for chapter 1.” Players follow a guide along short, visible forest paths between small objective clearings. The first clearing contains scattered light bulbs for assembling a lamp; ahead, a water-filled crossing forms the water element area, with wood scattered nearby to build a bridge. Beyond the bridge, place a clearing with paint supplies for making a large SOS sign, followed by a flower-filled earth area with scrap scattered for constructing a helicopter. Include hiding boxes along the routes and in objective clearings. End at a helicopter departure clearing, with space for a final scene featuring the fire monster.

# Agent decision

## Genre

Adventure is dominant because this is a story-driven sequence of collection and construction objectives along a journey. Survival is secondary because four elemental monsters pursue or endanger players in those objective areas.

## Shape and preset

Use `route-guided`: one continuous forest route orders the lamp, bridge, SOS, and helicopter stages. Start from the Guided Trail preset, retain its trail and destination-landmark intent, and drop `reveal-exit` because no inside-to-outside cinematic opening is requested.

## Config requirements

Use `path-trail` for short visible forest links; `landmark-focal` for the helicopter departure clearing pulling players onward; `gate-progression` for the water crossing blocked until a bridge is built; `hazard-kill` for the dangerous water and elemental attack areas; `cover-los` for hiding boxes along paths and in clearings; `water-body` for the crossing; and the visible half of `npc-population` for one guide. Draw the entrance circle, lamp assembly station, bridge footprint, large SOS-sign surface, flower-filled earth area, helicopter construction pad, and final fire-monster scene space.

## Layout requirements

Place `collectible-nodes` in four objective groups: light bulbs in the first clearing, wood beside the crossing, paint in the SOS clearing, and scrap in the flower-filled earth area. Place four `spawner-npc` origins, one each for the water, fire, earth, and air monsters, sited in their corresponding progression areas without overlapping arrivals. Place the functional half of `npc-population` for one guide at the entrance and along the initial trail. Put progression triggers at the lamp station, bridge footprint, SOS surface, and helicopter pad, with one arrival marker inside the chapter circle.

## Layout components

Arrange five small clearings in one readable chain: entrance, lamp, water crossing, SOS, earth-and-scrap, ending at the helicopter departure space, with the SOS and earth functions allowed to share adjacent ground. Short forest paths connect each stage. The crossing divides two banks and reserves a straight bridge span. Hiding boxes sit near route edges and inside every dangerous clearing. Dense magical forest forms the outer boundary, while each clearing remains visible across. Frame the chain diagonally with the helicopter landmark at the far end.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: the plan is authoritative so every objective group, monster area, progression barrier, and short connecting path occurs in the intended order.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is medium, made of several small clearings rather than long travel. The theme is colorful magical-forest adventure with distinct elemental accents. Use `CHECK` for the water play envelope; otherwise the continuous grounded map remains `P0`.
