# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] goal — Q: What are you surviving - hunger and the environment, wild animals, a killer chasing you, or other players? A: You are surviving hunger, the harsh rainforest environment, and dangerous wild animals; there is no killer or PvP focus.
- [author] theme — Q: Should the jungle read realistic and oppressive, or bright and stylized? A: Make it realistic and oppressive, with dense foliage, rain, fog, and a dangerous atmosphere.
- [author] scale — Q: One clearing you defend, or a large multi-region jungle you range across? A: Use a small jungle map centered on one clearing, with nearby rainforest areas to explore for survival resources.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a small rainforest survival map centered on one open clearing, with short exploratory routes extending into dense nearby jungle. Surround the clearing with oppressive realistic foliage, heavy rain, low fog, and limited sightlines, while keeping the central ground readable as a safe orientation point. Distribute visible survival-resource patches through the nearby rainforest and reserve dangerous wildlife encounter ground farther from the clearing. Keep the map compact and bounded, with no PvP arena, pursuing killer, or multi-region world.

## Genre
The genre is Survival.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded`, a BoundaryZone (Contained Arena) containing the central clearing and its nearby rainforest ring. The preset is none because Resource Survival's multi-biome world and player base plot would add scope not requested here.

## Config requirements
Use `cover-los` for dense foliage clusters and low jungle cutouts that break animal sightlines, and `zone-graphics` from Entertainment for concentrated rain, fog, and dense vegetation around the rainforest perimeter. The central clearing and short exploration routes are direct scene-specific visual requirements.

## Layout requirements
Distribute `collectible-nodes` Collectibles for food and general survival resources along the short routes outside the clearing. Place `spawner-npc` Spawners for dangerous wild animals in the outer rainforest, set back from one plain SpawnZone with no canonical option ID at the clearing center. Hunger, weather damage, animal behavior, and resource consumption are mechanics.

## Layout components
The build contains one central clearing, several short outward exploration paths, a dense surrounding rainforest ring, resource patches, wildlife-origin areas, and a closed natural boundary. Keep the center open, push the heaviest foliage and fog toward the margins, and preserve enough route visibility that the clearing remains easy to find.

## Render order
Use `isometric` because the compact topology is simple and the oppressive layering of clearing, foliage, rain, and fog leads the scene.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use small scale because resource patches and foliage gaps must remain legible around one clearing. The theme is a realistic, oppressive rainy rainforest with fog and dangerous wildlife. `P0` is sufficient because all play occurs on one contiguous ground surface with no interiors, overhangs, or separate maps.
