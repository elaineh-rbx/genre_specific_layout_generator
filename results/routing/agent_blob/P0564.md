# Scene prompt

Create a small, realistic and oppressive rainforest survival map centered on one clearing, surrounded by nearby dense jungle areas to explore. Thick foliage, wet ground, rain, fog, and tangled vegetation enclose the clearing, with natural routes leading into the surrounding rainforest and a dangerous wild atmosphere.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Survival, focused on resource survival against hunger, harsh rainforest conditions, and dangerous wildlife rather than PvP or a pursuing killer.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-bounded`: one compact jungle play space contains the central clearing and its nearby exploration ring. No preset remains recognisable because Resource Survival assumes a broader biome world and player base-building, while this brief asks only for a small clearing and local gathering.

## Config requirements

Draw one open but exposed clearing enclosed by thick foliage, tangled vines, wet earth, buttress roots, and close-packed rainforest trees. Carve several natural routes through the vegetation, using trunks, roots, rocks, and brush to create readable cover and oppressive sightlines without turning the map into a maze. Rain, ground mist, puddles, and muted realistic materials establish the harsh atmosphere.

## Layout requirements

Place `collectible-nodes` for edible plants, wood, stone, and other basic survival resources along the routes and in small jungle pockets outside the clearing, with enough separation to require short expeditions. Place `spawner-npc` origins for dangerous wild animals in screened perimeter habitat, oriented toward patrol ground rather than directly onto the player start. Put one protected player spawn near the center of the clearing. No hostile-human or PvP spawns are needed.

## Layout components

Build a medium-size central clearing as the navigation anchor, then surround it with a bounded ring of dense rainforest. Run three or four short looping footpaths from the clearing into distinct resource pockets and back, avoiding dead-end traps. Keep terrain mostly ground-level, with shallow puddles and muddy depressions rather than cliffs or caves. Enclose the outer boundary with impassably dense vegetation and large roots. Compose the view so the clearing remains visible through the canopy while the exploration paths feel enclosed.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first as authoritative, then dress the isometric view, because the short exploration loops, wildlife separation, and return routes must remain navigable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is small, set by one clearing and nearby jungle only. The theme is realistic, wet, foggy, and oppressive rainforest. Route `P0`: one bounded ground surface has no overhang, interior transition, or separate map.
