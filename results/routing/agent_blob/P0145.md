# Scene prompt

Create a compact 3D fashion city centered on a massive circular runway plaza. Towering glass buildings, glowing fountains, digital billboards, palm trees, and wide pedestrian streets frame the plaza; arrange the main boutiques, cafés, photo spots, gardens, and portals directly around it so activities are close together. Include themed clothing boutiques for casual, formal, fantasy, sportswear, vintage, beachwear, streetwear, and seasonal styles, with mannequins and display shelves inside.

Place an exclusive luxury district behind elegant golden gates, containing marble floors, crystal chandeliers, indoor waterfalls, floating displays, private runways, luxury dressing suites, and lounges overlooking the city. Access private per-player dressing booth instances from the city; each booth has sliding glass doors, floor-to-ceiling mirrors, adjustable lighting, makeup stations, clothing racks, a rotating platform, and accessory shelves. Make the central runway a giant stage with a long catwalk, audience area, grand winner podium, giant LED screens, moving lights, smoke, fireworks, floating platforms, and dynamic backdrop space.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, specifically a dress-up competition in a walkable fashion-city setting.

## Shape and preset

Use `stage-runway`: the compact city is composed around a central catwalk, preparation spaces, and its audience. The Dress Up preset fits directly, with private booth instances and themed boutiques added from the prompt.

## Config requirements

Use `social-hub` for the massive circular runway plaza and its crowd-sized pedestrian apron; `vignette-photo` for the dedicated photo spots and gardens; `path-street` for compact, wide pedestrian links around the plaza; `building-interior` for all nine themed boutiques, VIP suites, lounges, and other entered venues; and the visible half of `teleporter-link` for portals into private booth instances. Draw the gated luxury district, central catwalk, audience area, winner podium, LED screens, moving lights, smoke and fireworks equipment, floating platforms, and configurable backdrop space.

## Layout requirements

Place `teleporter-link` endpoints at the city booth-access portal and inside each private per-player booth instance. Provide up to ten contestant presentation marks along the runway flow, ending at the grand podium, because rounds support ten players. Keep judging and voting logic out of the geometry.

## Layout components

The circular runway plaza occupies the center, with the long catwalk crossing its focal axis, audience space along both sides, and the podium and screens at its far end. A tight ring of nine specialty boutiques—casual, formal, fantasy, sportswear, vintage, beachwear, streetwear, seasonal, and VIP—plus cafés, gardens, photo spots, and portals surrounds it. Wide pedestrian streets connect this ring without long travel. Golden gates open into the luxury district of marble boutique rooms, indoor waterfalls, private runways, dressing suites, and elevated lounges. Each isolated booth contains mirrors, adjustable lights, makeup stations, racks, one rotating platform, and accessory shelves. Glass towers frame the bounded compact city.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: the authoritative plan comes first so the plaza ring, runway flow, boutique access, and ten presentation positions remain playable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is large despite the compact travel radius, driven by nine boutiques and multiple private instances. The theme is glossy metropolitan fashion with glass, gold, neon screens, palms, and theatrical lighting. Use `P3` for entered buildings and `P4` for isolated per-player booths.
