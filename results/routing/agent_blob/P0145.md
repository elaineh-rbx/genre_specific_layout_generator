# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] scale — Q: How large is the city meant to be - a plaza with shops around it, or a district you spend minutes walking across? A: Build it as a compact central runway plaza with the main boutiques, cafés, photo spots, gardens, and portals arranged around it; it should feel like a fashion city without requiring minutes of walking between activities.
- [author] shape — Q: Are the dressing booths per-player instances or physical rooms in the world, one per slot? A: Dressing booths are private per-player instances, accessed from the city, so every player has their own uninterrupted styling and decoration space.
- [author] goal — Q: How many players per round, and how is the vote resolved into a winner? A: Use up to 10 players per round. After the runway presentations, players vote for other contestants based on the listed criteria, and the player with the highest total votes wins; ties are broken by the highest theme-accuracy score.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a compact, vibrant 3D fashion city centered on a massive circular runway plaza for up to 10 contestants. Ring the plaza with glass towers, glowing fountains, animated billboards, palm trees, wide streets, main boutiques, cafés, gardens, photo spots, and portals. Make the non-VIP shops enterable and display casual, formal, fantasy, sportswear, vintage, beachwear, streetwear, and seasonal clothing on mannequins and shelves. Behind elegant golden gates, form a luxury VIP zone with marble floors, crystal chandeliers, indoor waterfalls, floating displays, designer collections, private runways, luxury suites, and lounges overlooking the city. The central catwalk has moving lights, giant LED screens, smoke, fireworks, floating platforms, dynamic backdrops, an audience area, and a grand winner podium. Each city access point to a private dressing instance leads to a room with sliding glass doors, floor-to-ceiling mirrors, adjustable lighting, makeup stations, clothing racks, rotating platforms, and shelves for shoes, handbags, jewelry, and accessories. Include NPC fashion models, scenic photo compositions, and hidden collectible alcoves while keeping every main activity close to the plaza.
