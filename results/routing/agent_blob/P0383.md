## Clarifications resolved
- [author] goal — Q: Does the road end somewhere, or is it endless with a distance score? A: Make it an endless road with a distance score; the goal is to run as far as possible while jumping over oncoming cars.
- [author] theme — Q: Is this a normal city street, a highway, or something stylized? A: Use a normal city street setting.
- [agent_inferred] movement — Q: Does the runner snap between fixed lanes or steer freely across the road? A: Use free lateral steering because the prompt describes running down a normal road rather than switching among fixed lane slots.

## Enriched image prompt
Create an endless-runner map made from repeating normal city-street segments. The player runs forward along a continuous asphalt road with free lateral space while oncoming cars travel toward them as moving jump obstacles. Use ordinary curbs, sidewalks, street markings, and simple urban facades to frame the road without blocking the forward view. Keep each generated segment aligned to the next and leave clear, speed-appropriate gaps between cars so the road remains readable and jumpable as it extends toward the horizon.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Infinite Runner, canonical slug `infinite-runner`.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-free`, Free Lateral Steering across the street rather than fixed snap lanes. The matched preset is Free Runner, retaining deterministic chunks, jump obstacles, and run-ending hazards.

## Config requirements
Use `chunk-modular` for aligned repeating street segments, `obstacle-jump` for the car-clearing jumps, `obstacle-moving` for oncoming traffic, and `hazard-kill` for collisions that end the run. Use `path-road-vehicle`, taken from the shared catalogue and routed as `P6` because the roadway is the course rather than an ordinary city street.

## Layout requirements
Place one `trigger-despawn` TriggerZone a fixed distance behind the camera to recycle cleared street and traffic segments. No finish trigger is required because the author specified an endless distance run.

## Layout components
Each reusable chunk contains a straight city roadway, curbs, sidewalks, simple street-edge facades, and one or more valid traffic-obstacle arrangements. Chunk exits and origins share one axis with no drift. The visible boundary is the urban street wall, while the route continues indefinitely beyond the horizon.

## Render order
Use `topdown`; modular alignment and speed-valid obstacle spacing must be authoritative before city dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium per rendered chunk so cars and jump spacing remain legible even though the generated route is endless. The theme is a normal modern city street. Use `P6` because chunk joins and elastic obstacle spacing must be valid by construction.
