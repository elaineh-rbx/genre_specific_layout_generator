# Scene prompt

Create a 3D endless-runner map set on a normal city street: a long straight road extending into the distance, with oncoming cars traveling toward the player along the roadway. Frame the street with simple urban roadside surroundings.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant and sole genre is Infinite Runner. The player runs forward endlessly for distance while reacting to approaching traffic.

## Shape and preset

Use `lane-free` as the single shape: the player moves continuously across the width of a city roadway rather than snapping among stated lane slots. Match the Free Runner preset, whose modular path, jump obstacles, and run-ending collisions fit directly, and add the explicitly moving oncoming traffic.

## Config requirements

Use `chunk-modular` for straight road segments whose exit and origin pivots align perfectly along one axis. Use `obstacle-jump` for cars the runner can hurdle, `obstacle-moving` for those cars traveling toward the player, and `hazard-kill` for a vehicle collision that ends the run. Use `settlement-density` for simple built-up city-street edges with sidewalks and modest urban facades, kept visually secondary to the roadway.

## Layout requirements

Place one `trigger-despawn` cleanup volume a fixed distance behind the camera and active road queue. Oncoming-car patterns must leave clear lateral and jump responses, with their spawn spacing derived from current speed and at least half a second of reaction time. No collectibles, power-ups, checkpoints, lap regions, or terminal finish trigger are required.

## Layout components

Build a repeating queue of straight, level city-road chunks extending toward one vanishing point. Each chunk includes a broad asphalt roadway, curb and sidewalk strips, and simple low-rise urban facades along both sides. Arrange oncoming cars in readable forward patterns with enough open road to dodge or jump each one, never creating a full-width unavoidable wall. Keep roadside props outside the running corridor. The street canyon forms the boundary and composition, with no endpoint.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: draw the modular road and valid traffic-clearance plan first, then dress it as an isometric city street. The plan is authoritative because chunk drift or an unavoidable traffic pattern would break the endless run.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale band is small per reusable chunk, assembling into an endless road. The theme is a straightforward contemporary city street. Use `P6` because chunk alignment and speed-aware obstacle spacing must be valid by construction.
