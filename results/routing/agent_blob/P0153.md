## Enriched image prompt

Narrow corridors, doors, obstacles, movable wall sections, and floor traps form the maze; rearranged wall modules keep routes unreliable while every valid state still leads to one structural escape exit. Show the environment almost entirely black, with brief luminous echolocation rings outlining nearby walls, obstacles, doors, traps, and scattered throwable alarm clocks and rocks.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Survival because noise-sensitive monster evasion drives the round, with Puzzle secondary because navigating the rearranging labyrinth and finding its exit is the spatial challenge.

## Shape and preset
Use `puzzle-maze`, contributed by Puzzle, because solvable labyrinth topology is the defining space. The matched Survival preset is Escape, substituting the explicit maze shape while retaining the structural exit and dropping unneeded outside-to-inside shelter assumptions.

## Config requirements
Use `gate-escape` for the single structural exit. Use `hazard-kill` for traps embedded in corridor floors. Use `obstacle-moving`, taken from Obby & Platformer, for wall modules that rearrange corridor routes during a round. The echolocation edge-lighting is a whole-scene visual constraint, not extra geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place `gate-escape` at one reachable maze boundary. Use `collectible-nodes` for throwable alarm clocks and rocks distributed in corridor alcoves. Place one `spawner-npc` hostile origin away from the survivor start, and use `spawn-protected` for survivor starts screened from that initial monster position.

## Layout components
One enclosed floor contains the maze, movable wall modules, narrow passages, doors, obstacle pockets, trap tiles, decoy-item alcoves, separated starts, and one exit. Every rearrangement must preserve a connected start-to-exit route. No exterior or second level is built.

## Render order
Use `authored_plan`: `puzzle-maze` is a supported generated topology, and every initial or rearranged state must remain solvable before the image model dresses it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by one compact floor and readable corridor hazards. The theme is pitch-black underground industrial horror revealed by luminous sound rings. Use `P6` because maze validity must be constructed, not inferred from an image.
