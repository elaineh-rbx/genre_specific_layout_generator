# Scene prompt

Create a realistic 3D outdoor national motocross map built as a small hard-pack dirt lap circuit. The track loops through an open riding area, with sculpted bermed corners, deep ruts, braking bumps, and a varied sequence of motocross jumps arranged along the racing line. Include detailed pit and crowd areas beside the circuit, with terrain and track surfaces designed to visibly change under repeated riding.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Racing. Although the broader project emphasizes simulation realism, this first map is a finite motocross lap circuit supporting races and time trials.

## Shape and preset

Use `route-circuit` for one closed hard-pack dirt loop through an open outdoor area. The preset is Circuit Racing, augmented with motocross recovery, pit, crowd, and deformable-terrain requirements from the brief.

## Config requirements

Draw `spawn-grid` as a dirt-bike starting grid on the main straight, `barrier-guardrail` as low course-edge protection where riders could leave the safe area, and `path-turnaround` as bermed corners that return the course toward its origin. Add `spectator-zone` for detailed crowd areas and pits beside the circuit. The track surface must visibly include deep ruts, braking bumps, hard-pack wear, sculpted berms, and varied motocross jumps, with a deformable appearance under repeated riding.

## Layout requirements

Place `spawn-grid` slots in aligned rows behind the shared start/finish line, each oriented down the opening racing line; no count is fixed by the brief. Place one `trigger-lap` region across that line and additional split regions at spaced points around the loop to prevent course cutting. Place `checkpoint-respawn` recovery points after major jump groups and difficult corner sequences, aligned to the next safe segment with forward orientation and zeroed velocity.

## Layout components

Build a compact start straight, a continuous sequence of bermed corners, rutted straights, braking-bump approaches, and mixed jumps, then close the route back to the start. Put the pit zone beside the start area and crowd zones outside barriers at readable viewing points. Keep the open riding ground around the circuit as runoff, use hard-pack dirt throughout, and frame the entire loop so pits, spectators, terrain deformation, and jump rhythm are visible.

## Render order

Use `authored_plan`: generate the closed circuit in code first, then derive top-down and isometric images. The loop must close and remain continuously rideable without ambiguous crossings.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a small scale for the requested first outdoor national. The theme is photorealistic professional motocross with worn hard-pack terrain and active pits. Apply `P6` for circuit validity and `tiered` for sculpted jumps and berm relief without overhanging surfaces.
