# Scene prompt
Create a bright, stylised 3D tag-game map set in one small open park field that players can cross in a few seconds. Keep the park simple and focused, with clear open ground for running and a simple spectator area raised above or placed beside the field for eliminated players to watch the round.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Party & Casual because this is a timed tag elimination round ending with one surviving player.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded` with preset none. The map is one continuous small park field plus an adjacent out-of-play viewing area; neither the Childhood Game preset’s hiding maze nor the Minigame preset’s isolated stage matches this simple open field.

## Config requirements
Apply `spectator-zone` as a simple walled viewing gallery beside the park, visibly separated from active play while preserving a clear view of the field. Draw the remaining arena as bright stylized grass with sparse park details around the perimeter and uninterrupted open running ground.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` as evenly distributed round-start markers around the field so no player begins within immediate tagging distance. Place `trigger-scoring` as the tag-contact and end-of-timer detection regions needed by the active round. Apply `teleporter-link` in both streams: draw a simple transfer pad at the field edge and place its functional link to send eliminated players into the spectator gallery, with a return marker for the next round. Keep all spectator markers outside the playable field.

## Layout components
Build one small flat park field that can be crossed in a few seconds, bounded by a clear low fence or stylized hedge. Keep the center open, with only a few decorative trees, benches, or lamps held near the perimeter. Put one compact spectator gallery directly beside the boundary, separated by a wall or fence and facing the whole field. A short transfer pad links the field edge to that gallery without becoming a traversable escape route. Frame active play and spectators together in one readable composition.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative because the bright park appearance and immediate field-to-gallery relationship lead, while the topology is simple.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by one quickly crossed field and a compact spectator area. The theme is bright, playful stylized park. Use `P0`; choosing the beside-field gallery avoids overhang or tiered relief, and the build has no interior transition, separate map, or volumetric play.
