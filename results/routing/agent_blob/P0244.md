# Scene prompt

Create a bright, cartoonish party-game map with a separate lobby waiting area leading into a compact large arena sized for 12 players. The main arena is an open hazard floor with clear sightlines, arranged for players to encounter one another, while vivid glowing laser beams spawn at the arena edges and travel across the space at varying paths and heights.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Party & Casual, built as a round-based last-player-standing minigame.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-staged`: the 12-player hazard arena is fully separated from the waiting lobby so waiting players cannot enter or interfere. The preset is Minigame, with the leaderboard and scoring-volume elements omitted because the brief specifies elimination rather than point scoring.

## Config requirements

Use `social-hub` as the separate lobby waiting area, sized for all 12 players without blocking its exit. Draw `teleporter-link` as a clear lobby-to-arena gate. Use `spectator-zone` as a protected out-of-play viewing area with clear sightlines over the arena. Keep the arena itself a broad, flat, uncluttered hazard floor with a vivid perimeter and obvious laser-emitter housings around its edges.

## Layout requirements

Place exactly 12 evenly distributed `spawn-protected` round-start positions on the arena floor, all away from emitter mouths and without positional advantage. Place the functional `teleporter-link` between lobby and arena and a return destination in the lobby. Place laser-emitter markers around multiple arena edges, with travel paths crossing the floor at varied directions and heights; their exact count was not stated. Each moving beam carries its own elimination trigger. Route eliminated players to the `spectator-zone`.

## Layout components

Build two separate spaces: one compact colorful lobby and one large but encounter-dense arena for 12 players. The lobby contains an open gathering floor, the arena gate, and no route directly onto the hazard floor. The arena is a simple bounded rectangle or circle with uninterrupted movement space, low perimeter walls, emitter housings on several sides, and a protected spectator platform outside the active boundary. Avoid fixed cover that would hide incoming beams. Compose the arena so all edges and laser directions read clearly.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative, then derive the top-down plan; the varied laser heights and instantly readable cartoon hazard presentation lead, while the arena topology is simple.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a medium scale, compact but explicitly sized for 12 players. The theme is bright cartoon party-game sci-fi with vivid glowing lasers. Apply `P4` because the lobby and match arena are separate maps.
