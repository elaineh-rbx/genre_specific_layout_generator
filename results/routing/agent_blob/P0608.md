# Scene prompt
Create a low-graphics 3D driving map spanning a few explorable city blocks, optimized for weak devices. Lay out connected roads through textured buildings, with traffic poles, traffic lights, and walls positioned along streets and around the block edges. Include space for drivable cars, jeeps, motorcycles, and other vehicles.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation: this is a free-driving vehicle game with no laps, finish line, or competitive race condition.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, a shared persistent city world in which players can drive and explore in any order. The preset is Vehicle Sim, retaining its open world and road-network idea but dropping the unrelated shared resource field.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for vehicle-width connected streets linking all explorable blocks. Use `settlement-density` for a compact grid of a few textured city blocks rather than scattered buildings. The image should also show low-detail collision props—traffic poles, traffic lights, and perimeter walls—placed visibly along the streets.

## Layout requirements
No canonical post-segmentation option is required. The layout pass should nevertheless place several vehicle spawn markers in broad roadside or parking areas, with enough clearance for cars, jeeps, and motorcycles, and one player arrival marker beside the road rather than in traffic.

## Layout components
Build a medium compact city of a few blocks around a connected road network. Include intersections, short side streets, turning space, and multiple open pull-off or parking areas. Low-poly textured building shells line both sides of the roads; the prompt does not require enterable interiors. Distribute traffic-light assemblies and poles at intersections, add occasional roadside poles, and use walls around outer block edges without sealing road exits. Keep terrain flat and materials simple for weak devices. Frame the whole street grid clearly from above while preserving readable building silhouettes.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: road connectivity and usable turning space should be authoritative, then the isometric view should dress that plan with buildings and collision props.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, driven by the clarified “few city blocks” scope. The theme is low-graphics contemporary urban driving. Route through `P0`; one flat outdoor surface has no overhang, interior transition, separate map, or volumetric play-space cost.
