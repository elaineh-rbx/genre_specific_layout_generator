## Enriched image prompt
Create a low-graphics 3D driving map made from a few contemporary city blocks. Use a simple connected street loop with short cross streets, vehicle-width lanes, intersections, sidewalks, and explorable side areas for cars, jeeps, motorcycles, and other road vehicles. Line the streets with low-detail buildings that use simple baked textures rather than player-editable materials. Include sturdy walls, roadside poles, and traffic lights as clear collision objects. Keep geometry, lighting, props, and texture density optimized for weak devices.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation, specifically free-roaming vehicle operation with no race, laps, or finish condition.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, named Shared Persistent World for Simulation: everyone drives through one connected city area. The matched preset is Vehicle Sim, retaining its explorable road network and dropping the unrequested shared resource field.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` as a street network rather than a race course, so it routes as a P0 city street. Use `settlement-density` for the few built-up city blocks. Represent traffic lights, poles, walls, and low-detail textured building facades as scene-specific collision geometry rather than destructible structures.

## Layout requirements
No post-segmentation option is required. Vehicle selection, collision deaths, and distinct crash animations are mechanics attached to the drivable vehicles and collision objects, not map triggers or pickups.

## Layout components
A connected street loop encloses several small blocks, with short cross streets and intersections providing alternate exploration routes. Textured low-detail buildings sit behind sidewalks; poles and traffic lights mark intersections, and outer walls or turnbacks close the map edge. Road widths accommodate cars, jeeps, and motorcycles without dense decoration.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: connected road widths, turns, and intersections are authoritative for driving, though the simple network does not require procedural track generation.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, corresponding to a few city blocks rather than a drive-for-minutes world. The theme is a low-spec contemporary city with simple textured buildings. The pipeline cost is `P0`: the roads are ordinary streets on one surface, not a racing route.
