# Scene prompt
Create a 3D endless-runner map in a bright, colorful cartoon urban subway setting, viewed from a third-person camera behind and above the runner. A straight track corridor extends along the +Z axis as repeating 100-stud chunks, with three parallel 8-stud-wide lanes centered at X = -8, 0, and +8. Arrange open track sections, train-yard sections with parallel rails, parked subway trains, signals, and simple platform-side props; barrier-run sections with lane-based barriers and signs; lit concrete tunnel sections with overhead signs and low-clearance overhangs spanning the tracks; and ramp sections that lead onto train roofs or raised track. Place coins in straight lines, arcs, and curved trails above or between the lanes. Keep the track corridor readable ahead, with urban subway structures framing the sides and a clear forward route.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Infinite Runner because the avatar auto-advances through an endlessly streamed reaction course.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-snap`: three rigid parallel lanes centered at X = -8, 0, and +8. The preset is Lane Runner.

## Config requirements
Use `chunk-modular` for five kinds of repeating 100-stud segments with matching entry and exit pivots: Empty, TrainYard, BarrierRun, TunnelSection, and RampSection. Use `obstacle-moving` for incoming trains, `obstacle-jump` for barriers and roof ramps, and `hazard-kill` for trains, blockers, and pits that end a run. Use `barrier-horizon` as tunnel mouths and urban framing that conceal chunk creation while retaining a readable forward corridor.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` as lines of 5–10 coins, arcs, and curved jump trails across the lanes. Place `powerup-buffs` about once per 300 studs on valid approach lines. Put one `trigger-despawn` cleanup volume 200 studs behind the runner-facing camera position. Obstacle placement must keep at least one valid action available, with clear spacing scaled to current speed.

## Layout components
Each chunk is 100 studs long and carries three 8-stud lanes. Train-yard chunks contain rails, parked trains, signals, and platform props; BarrierRun chunks use lane-readable blockers and signs; lit concrete tunnels use low overhead clearance; RampSection chunks climb onto 60-stud train roofs or raised track. Urban subway structures enclose both sides, while the corridor remains open along +Z and composes as a strong central vanishing point.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, because lane continuity, obstacle clearability, and aligned chunk pivots must remain valid.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge because the course streams indefinitely. The theme is bright colorful cartoon urban subway. Use `P6` for valid procedural lane and chunk structure and `P2` because tunnels, overhangs, and runnable train roofs create vertically overlapping surfaces.
