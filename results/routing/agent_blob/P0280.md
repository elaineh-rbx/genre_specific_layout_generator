# Scene prompt
Create the first large playable map for a tense horror-lit Prey-versus-Predators survival game: a Big School building under low, moody lighting with deep shadows and unsettling ambience, while routes and interactive objects remain readable. Build a connected school interior with classroom wings and hallways, a gym, cafeteria, rooftop access, lockers for hiding, chokepoints, and multiple escape routes; include physical hiding places such as closets, lockers, dumpsters, and space beneath furniture. Arrange the building as a compact vertical slice with distinct routes between its major rooms and varied spawn locations. Other rotating map layouts include a multi-floor shopping mall with stores, food court, escalators, parking garage, and security rooms; an abandoned outdoor neighborhood of empty houses, backyards, alleyways, and a small park; plus optional hospital, subway station, office building, and summer camp maps. For the menu’s 3D background, show a dark nighttime forest with tall silhouetted trees, thick ground fog, dim moonlight through branches, and a glowing campfire centered in the scene.

# Agent decision
### Genre
The dominant genre is Survival: Prey evade player-controlled Predators through repeated timed role phases.

### Shape and preset
Use `warren-looping` for the Big School, with classroom wings and shared rooms linked by loops so pursuit routes have no trapping dead ends. The preset is 1 vs All, retaining its pursuit topology, hiding cover, and asymmetric starts but omitting an escape gate because “escape routes” here are evasive circulation and the match advances by timer and role swap.

### Config requirements
Use `cover-los` for closets, lockers, dumpsters, under-furniture gaps, hallway corners, and room-side recesses. Draw the school’s classrooms, halls, gym, cafeteria, chokepoints, rooftop access, and readable interactive objects as scene-specific geometry. Also draw the separate menu background as a non-walked dark forest display centered on one glowing campfire. The mall, neighborhood, and optional later themes are rotation context, not additional maps to build in this first artifact.

### Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` starts as varied, screened positions for Prey and Predators across different school sectors, keeping opposing roles out of immediate sightlines. No exit-win trigger is needed. Interactive doors, vents, alarms, and task fixtures may receive interaction markers only where their visible geometry is present; no exact count was supplied.

### Layout components
The playable zone is a large connected school interior: classroom wings feed looping hallways, with the gym and cafeteria as broad junction spaces and several routes reaching rooftop access. Chokepoints should always have a nearby alternate path. Distribute hiding props throughout rooms and corridors. The building shell is the boundary. Separately, compose the menu forest around a central campfire, silhouetted trees, ground fog, and moonlight, with no traversal path.

### Render order
Use `topdown`: loop connectivity, alternative routes, and spawn separation must be valid before horror dressing is applied.

### Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large, driven by a full school with several wings and major rooms. The theme is readable, moody institutional horror plus a sketchy campfire-forest menu set. Use `P6` for the pursuit warren, `P3` for interior-to-rooftop traversal, and `P4` because the menu background is a separate map from the school.
