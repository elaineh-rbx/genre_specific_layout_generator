# Scene prompt

Create a bright, cute cartoon 3D pet-collecting world divided into connected fantasy, jungle, garden, grassland, and ocean zones. The grassland and garden hold areas for land animals, with the garden featuring insect habitats; the jungle contains tropical animal habitats; and the ocean zone includes open water for marine creatures. Within the same map, a distinct but visually cohesive mystical fantasy area contains habitats for magical creatures and a prominent fantasy tree that produces magical fruit. Include a shop structure where players can obtain apples and fish food.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, specifically a pet-collecting and care experience centered on befriending animals across habitats.

## Shape and preset

Use `wilderness-open` for one connected natural world with no residential settlement. Preset is none: Animal Sim assumes players inhabit the world as animals, while Pet Care assumes a town and claimable housing; neither matches habitat-based pet collection.

## Config requirements

Apply `path-street` as natural pedestrian circulation connecting the five zones; `landmark-focal` as the prominent magical-fruit tree anchoring the fantasy area; `npc-population` as ambient land animals, insects, tropical animals, marine creatures, and magical creatures occupying their proper habitats; and `water-body` as the open ocean habitat. Add one visible shop structure supplying apples and fish food.

## Layout requirements

Place the functional half of `npc-population` by habitat: land animals across grassland and garden, insects in dedicated garden micro-habitats, tropical animals in jungle clearings, marine creatures within the ocean volume, and magical creatures around the fantasy zone. Place a magical-fruit production marker directly at the fantasy tree. Keep animal placements off narrow transition paths and give each habitat enough open interaction ground.

## Layout components

Build five connected zones on one map: fantasy, jungle, garden, grassland, and ocean. Link them with readable natural paths and gradual visual transitions. The garden contains multiple insect habitat pockets; grassland has broad land-animal areas; jungle has dense but traversable tropical habitat clearings; ocean has open water and shoreline access; fantasy has magical habitats surrounding the dominant fantasy tree. Place the shop near a central junction. Keep the outer boundary natural and compose the world so all five regions read together.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative; visual habitat identity leads, and the route network is not structurally constrained.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is large, driven by five full habitat zones. The theme is bright, cute cartoon wildlife with cohesive mystical fantasy accents. Use `CHECK` for the marine-creature water volume; no self-occluding underwater layers are requested.
