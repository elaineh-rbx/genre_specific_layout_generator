# Scene prompt

Create a 3D gritty, satirical neon crime-sandbox city as a compact drivable district. A few neon-lit urban blocks connect directly to a waterfront, with broad streets and parking areas suited to sports cars. Along the water, build a continuous accessible shoreline with docks, ramps, and open water fully navigable by jet skis; the waterfront and city blocks remain visually connected through neon-lit roads and promenade edges.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Simulation: this is an open vehicle sandbox centered on operating sports cars and jet skis, not a racing game with laps or a crime game with explicit combat objectives.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open` as one contiguous shared district joining city streets and navigable waterfront. The preset is Vehicle Sim, retaining its open-world shape and vehicle-width routes while dropping its unrequested shared extraction field.

## Config requirements

Draw `path-road-vehicle` as broad, flat city streets sized for sports-car turning, linking every block to waterfront parking and launch access. Add `path-circulation` for the continuous road-and-promenade connection between urban and shoreline activity. Use `settlement-density` for a compact cluster of only a few neon-lit blocks, and `water-body` for fully navigable open jet-ski water. Include parking areas, docks, shoreline ramps, and promenade edges. Do not add banks, police stations, chop shops, or other crime-specific venues.

## Layout requirements

Place scene-specific sports-car spawn markers in parking bays distributed across the city blocks, each aligned to the road network. Place jet-ski spawn markers at the docks and launch ramps, facing unobstructed water with enough clearance to depart. Put the initial player arrival near the visual and circulation seam between city and waterfront so both vehicle modes are immediately legible. No race checkpoints, finish line, combat spawns, or task triggers are needed.

## Layout components

Build a compact grid of several urban blocks with broad streets, intersections, curbside parking, and one larger parking area. Let the road network meet a continuous waterfront promenade at multiple points. Run docks and ramps from that edge into a broad open water zone, keeping pilings and shoreline geometry clear of jet-ski paths. Enclose the landward edge with dense city facades and the waterward edge with a distant skyline or bay boundary. Frame city and water together.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first as authoritative, then dress the isometric image, because vehicle turning paths and uninterrupted road-to-water access must remain valid.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is medium, set by a few drivable blocks plus substantial open water. The theme is gritty satirical Miami neon. Route `CHECK` for the jet-ski play volume over water; otherwise the connected single-surface district needs no modifier.
