# Scene prompt
Create a 3D underwater obby map: one compact, continuous easy-to-medium course that climbs from the dry ocean floor toward the surface. The player walks and jumps along the seabed while ocean water surrounds the course as the backdrop, with checkpoints placed along the ascending route.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Obby & Platformer, with no secondary genre. The activity is a player-paced sequence of jumps on a continuous ascending course.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-terraced`, with the route climbing across seabed shelves toward the surface without stacked overhangs. Apply the Classic Obby preset with its shape replaced by this ascending form; retain its sequential track, checkpoints, jump obstacles, and modest moving obstacles.

## Config requirements
Draw `path-track` as one uninterrupted chain of platforms rising through the underwater scene. Show `checkpoint-respawn` as broad, dry, clearly marked rest pads along the ascent. Use `obstacle-jump` for coral-rock gaps and stepped platforms, and `obstacle-moving` sparingly for easy-to-medium rotating or sliding sea-themed obstacles. Add `water-body` as the surrounding ocean backdrop while keeping the entire traversable seabed course dry. Draw `winner-zone` as the final bright platform near the surface.

## Layout requirements
Place `checkpoint-respawn` markers at the start and at regular difficulty-break intervals along the continuous ascent, each on a broad safe platform. Place one `winner-zone` trigger on the terminal surface-side platform. Keep the required route on dry geometry and validate every jump; the surrounding ocean is scenery, not a swimming play volume.

## Layout components
Begin with a compact dry ocean-floor start basin, then lead upward through a single chain of coral shelves, stone platforms, short ramps, and sunken-ruin fragments. Early jumps are broad and forgiving; later sections become narrower and add simple timed motion while staying easy-to-medium. Checkpoint pads divide the climb visually without turning it into numbered stages. Ocean water surrounds the route, with coral, sea plants, bubbles, and distant fish used as backdrop rather than traversable space. The seafloor and enclosing water define the boundary. Frame the scene diagonally upward so the bottom start, rising route, and sunlit finish are all legible.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish the valid ascending jump chain first, then dress it into the authoritative underwater isometric scene.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small because the course is compact and continuous. The theme is colorful underwater ruins and coral on a dry seabed. Use `P6` for structurally valid obby traversal and `tiered` for strong elevation without overhang; no volumetric-play `CHECK` is needed because players never enter the water.
