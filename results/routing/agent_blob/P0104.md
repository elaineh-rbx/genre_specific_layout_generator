## Enriched image prompt
Create a compact underwater 3D obby that begins on a dry ocean floor and climbs continuously toward the surface at easy-to-medium difficulty. Arrange a clear sequence of walkable and jumpable platforms across ascending seabed terraces, with surrounding ocean water visible as backdrop rather than as the player's movement space. Include three distinct safe checkpoint pads: at the start, around the midpoint, and on the final safe ledge before a clear finish near the surface. Keep the route continuous, readable, and physically jumpable, with no separate numbered-stage maps.

## Genre
The genre is Obby & Platformer.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-terraced`, Path (Ascending Terraces), because the compact course gains height from seabed to surface without asking for stacked overhangs. Preset is none: the request fixes an easy continuous underwater climb and checkpoints but does not choose enough of Classic Obby or Precision Platformer's obstacle bundle.

## Config requirements
Use `path-track` for the single ordered platform route from ocean floor to surface. Use `checkpoint-respawn` for three visible safe pads integrated into the course. Use `winner-zone` for the clear surface-side endpoint inferred from the climbing obby. Use universal `water-body` for the ocean surrounding the dry course as a visible non-walkable backdrop.

## Layout requirements
Place `checkpoint-respawn` as exactly three Checkpoints: one on the starting seabed pad, one on a stable midpoint terrace, and one on the last safe ledge before the finish. Place `winner-zone` as one WinnerZone at the reachable surface endpoint. No swimming placements are needed.

## Layout components
The build is one compact continuous route. Its lowest zone is a dry seabed starting pad; ascending terraces and platform links rise through the middle; a final ledge leads to the finish near the water surface. Surrounding ocean water frames the course but does not cover its dry walkable surfaces. The boundary is open water around the route. Compose the full climb diagonally so its start, three checkpoints, rising path, and surface endpoint all remain visible.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the continuous legal obby route must be established before underwater dressing, while `authored_plan` is not supported for obby courses.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale, corresponding to Block, so individual platforms and checkpoint pads stay readable while the full climb fits one frame. The theme is a dry-seabed underwater course surrounded by ocean blue. `P6` is required for valid course construction, `tiered` captures the non-overhanging ascent, and `CHECK` covers the surrounding volumetric water.
