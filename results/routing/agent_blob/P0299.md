## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation because operating interceptor vehicles to perform a storm-chasing task is the core activity; Survival is secondary because the tornado is a damaging moving hazard.

## Shape and preset

Universal `building-interior` draws multiple enterable houses, while `settlement-density` establishes the compact urban district.
