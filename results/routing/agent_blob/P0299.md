# Scene prompt
Создайте 3D-карту города для погони за торнадо: городские улицы и кварталы с домами, в которые можно заходить, соединены дорогами для управляемых игроками машин-перехватчиков. Оставьте открытые проезды и широкие улицы между зданиями, чтобы торнадо и машины могли двигаться по городу.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Simulation is the dominant and only genre. Players operate interceptor vehicles in a persistent tornado-chasing activity; survival is a hazard within that job rather than the stated goal.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open` for one contiguous shared city that players and the tornado can cross freely. Use the Vehicle Sim preset, retaining its open-world vehicle operation and road-network structure while omitting the unrelated shared resource field.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for broad connected roads sized for player-driven interceptor cars, with open junctions and alternate approaches around city blocks. Use `building-interior` for houses that players can enter from the streets. Use `settlement-density` for coherent urban blocks rather than isolated structures. Use `hazard-kill` for the moving tornado and its visible destructive corridor through the shared city.

## Layout requirements
Place safe player and interceptor-vehicle arrivals at multiple roadside pullouts, clear of building entrances and initial tornado movement. Mark repeated observation and interception locations as scene-specific task zones on wide streets and open junctions, where vehicles can stop without blocking through traffic. Tornado routing must remain dynamic rather than tied to one narrow corridor.

## Layout components
Build one medium-sized city of several connected blocks. Use a legible road grid with broad avenues, open intersections, and occasional cross streets so interceptor cars can pursue, overtake, and reposition around the tornado. Line the roads with houses whose exterior doors connect to generated interior floor plans; keep entrances visible and set back from vehicle lanes. Include open lots or plazas that give the tornado turning room and provide clear observation areas. Bound the map with continued urban skyline, blocked outbound roads, and distant storm haze. Compose the city from a broad avenue looking through successive intersections, with the tornado visible against the skyline and vehicle routes readable around it.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the connected road network, alternate pursuit routes, house access points, and tornado clearances must be valid before the city is dressed.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by the need for a city large enough to support vehicle pursuit without becoming sprawling. The theme is a contemporary storm-threatened city with heavy clouds and realistic emergency-chase atmosphere. Use `P3` because play explicitly crosses between exterior streets and enterable house interiors.
