A: Use a compact battle space sized for a single focused engagement, with opposing forces starting roughly 1–2 minutes of flight apart. This keeps the vertical 3D maneuvering meaningful without long travel downtime.

- [agent_inferred] repair bases — Q: How many repair bases should the initial space contain? A: Use one base for each opposing force at opposite sides so the same initial battlefield supports PvE and PvP; the friendly base contains the planning room.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a compact but fully three-dimensional steampunk-feudal airship battlefield in open sky. Place two opposing repair bases roughly one to two minutes of flight apart, with the friendly base containing an enterable planning and situation room. Between them, distribute a small number of floating islands, larger floating rocks, cloud banks, sky-city structures, and wildlife-threat areas as tactical cover, chokepoints, and candidate objective locations rather than as continuous ground. Arrange several staged-objective sites for attack, capture, defense, or survival operations, and show representative battle airships maneuvering above and between the terrain. Preserve broad vertical flight lanes and clear silhouettes in a single dramatic isometric composition.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Shooter, with Strategy secondary because collaborative planning and staged tactical objectives materially organize the battle space.

## Shape and preset
Use `volume-open-air`, taken from outside Shooter's usual set, because open sky is the primary play volume and islands are discrete terrain within it. It matches the PvE Shooter preset with this shape substitution, retaining hostile origins, chokepoints, and the enterable planning structure.

## Config requirements
Use `spawn-teambase` for the two opposing repair bases, `cover-los` for cloud banks, rocks, and island masses, `choke-bottleneck` for narrow air passes, `building-interior` for the friendly planning room, `island-cluster` for the small number of separated floating landmasses, and `capture-zone` for visible staged-objective sites. Use `tracker-quest`, reached through the shared catalogue from Adventure, for the physical strategy table or briefing wall in the situation room.

## Layout requirements
Place two `spawn-teambase` SpawnZones at opposite repair bases, separated by roughly one to two minutes of flight. Place several candidate `capture-zone` CaptureZones on islands or sky structures, selected per staged operation. Place `spawner-npc` Spawners at the enemy-side approaches and wildlife-threat areas. Place one `tracker-quest` Tracker in the friendly situation room. Matchmaking, objectives, commanders, economy, damage, ship control, and procedural selection are mechanics.

## Layout components
The build contains one volumetric battle space, two opposing repair bases, one linked planning-room interior, a small number of floating islands, cloud banks, floating rocks, sky-city structures, open vertical flight lanes, chokepoints, and several objective sites. Terrain is sparse enough that airships fight primarily in open sky. The flight envelope is the boundary.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, because vertical airship separation and layered cover cannot be expressed by a single flat plan; verify the resulting play volume for self-occlusion.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use the large scale band because capital airships, bases, and floating terrain must fit while remaining only one to two minutes of flight apart. The theme is steampunk feudal warfare among clouds and sky cities. `CHECK` is required for volumetric open-air play and separated floating islands, and `P3` is required for the exterior battlefield to linked planning-room interior transition.
