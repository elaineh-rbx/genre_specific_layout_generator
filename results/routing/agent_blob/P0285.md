# Scene prompt
Create a 3D airship-combat battle map set high above the clouds in a steampunk feudal world at war between two kingdoms. Build a compact open-sky engagement space, with opposing airship forces beginning roughly 1–2 minutes of flight apart. The main combat volume is open vertical airspace, broken by a small number of floating islands, dense cloud banks, floating rock formations, sky-city structures, and larger islands that serve as cover, chokepoints, or operation objectives. Place repair bases for each team at separated sides of the map. Arrange objective locations around defensible structures or islands, with clear aerial approach routes and room for large battle airships, small craft, and multi-ship formations to maneuver above, below, and between the terrain.

# Agent decision

## Genre
Shooter is dominant because ranged team combat, firing approaches, and held objectives organize the battle space. Action is secondary because the same volume supports large PvE clashes and dynamic enemy engagements.

## Shape and preset
Use `volume-open-air`, taken from outside Shooter’s usual shapes: open vertical airspace is the playable volume, with islands and structures as discrete cover rather than continuous ground. The closest preset is PvE Shooter, retaining its enemy-wave and chokepoint logic while replacing its usual lane shape and omitting interiors.

## Config requirements
Use `cover-los` for cloud banks, floating rocks, and sky structures that break long firing lines; `choke-bottleneck` for narrowed aerial passages between obstacles; `spawn-teambase` for two visibly separated, screened repair bases; `capture-zone` for defensible operation objectives; and `island-cluster` for the small number of floating landmasses separated by open air. The bases and objective markers must have visible physical forms.

## Layout requirements
Place both `spawn-teambase` zones on opposite sides, one per kingdom, outside direct opening fire. Place one `capture-zone` at each designated objective structure or island. Site `spawner-npc` origins beyond readable approach vectors around operation objectives so hostile airships enter from intelligible directions. No exact objective count was supplied, so do not invent one.

## Layout components
The compact map is a broad three-dimensional combat volume above a cloud floor. Opposing repair bases anchor its far sides at roughly 1–2 minutes of flight separation. Between them, a small number of large floating islands, rock formations, cloud banks, and sky-city structures create upper, middle, and lower aerial routes, cover pockets, chokepoints, and objective perches. Approaches must accommodate capital airships, small craft, and formations without making the space flat. Open sky bounds the maneuvering area; frame the scene to show both bases and the layered central obstacle field.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, because invalid approach connectivity or cramped flight corridors would undermine the battle even though the vertical dressing is added afterward.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large, driven by capital-ship maneuvering despite the focused engagement footprint. The theme is steampunk feudal warfare above dense clouds. Use `CHECK` because flight occupies a volumetric play-space with layered islands and structures that may self-occlude.
