# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] theme — Q: Should the world look realistic and rugged, or bright and stylized? A: Make it realistic and rugged, with outdoor mountain, trail, and mud terrain.
- [author] scale — Q: How big is big - a few minutes to cross by car, or tens of minutes? A: A few minutes to cross by car is enough for the first build, while still feeling like a big open-world explorer map.
- [author] goal — Q: What does mud gear 10plus mean - ten or more vehicles, off-road tyres, or gear tiers? A: Mud gear 10plus means ten or more off-road vehicles and ride options, including cars and bikes suited for muddy terrain.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a realistic, rugged 3D open-world exploration map that feels large but takes only a few minutes to cross by car. Place a clear spawn and vehicle staging area in the foreground, with muddy off-road routes branching into the world and hiking, walking, biking, and mountain-climbing trails leading toward a prominent mountain. Show at least ten off-road vehicles and ride options, including cars and bikes suited to mud, gathered at the staging area and distributed at useful route access points. Shape the continuous landscape with mountain relief, slopes, trails, and muddy ground while keeping the complete road and trail network readable from above.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure. Vehicles are traversal tools inside the exploration game rather than a separate racing or simulation loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, one contiguous Open World traversed freely. It matches the Exploration preset, retaining the directed landmark and exploration trails while omitting the preset's secret alcoves because none were requested.

## Config requirements
Use `landmark-focal` for the mountain that pulls exploration across the map, `path-trail` for hiking, walking, biking, and climbing routes, the universal `terrain-relief` for the mountain and rugged slopes, and `path-road-vehicle`, reached through the shared catalogue from Simulation, for muddy vehicle-width off-road routes. Route the vehicle path as a P0 roadway rather than a P6 race course. Add the source's free-text image requirement for at least ten off-road cars, bikes, and ride options.

## Layout requirements
Place one free-text SpawnZone at the vehicle staging area, oriented toward the mountain and the first road/trail junction. No race starts, laps, finish triggers, or vehicle-course validation are required.

## Layout components
The map contains one spawn and vehicle staging zone, at least ten off-road vehicles and ride options, a branching muddy road network, narrower hiking and biking trails, a climbable mountain destination, and rugged sloped terrain. Roads and trails connect the spawn area to the mountain and loop through the continuous world without creating separate maps. Natural terrain forms the outer boundary, and the whole network fits in one overhead frame.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, because the vehicle routes and foot trails must connect the spawn area to the mountain before realistic isometric dressing is added.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use the large scale band, following the clarified few-minutes-by-car extent while keeping roads, trails, vehicles, and slopes readable. The theme is realistic rugged mountain wilderness with muddy outdoor terrain. `tiered` is required for strong mountain relief without overhangs; otherwise the continuous map and non-racing roads are baseline `P0`.
