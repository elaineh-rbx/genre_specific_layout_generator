# Scene prompt
Create a realistic, rugged open-world exploration map of outdoor mountain, trail, and muddy terrain, large enough to take a few minutes to cross by car. Place a central spawn area with road access, branching into drivable routes across the landscape; include hiking and climbing paths up the mountain, walkable trails, and muddy off-road tracks. Provide space near the spawn or along the routes for ten or more off-road vehicle and bike ride options.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure. The cars and bikes support free exploration rather than defining a racing, delivery, or vehicle-simulation win condition.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open` for one contiguous rugged region traversed in any order. The closest preset is Exploration, retaining the open shape, trail network, and mountain focal point while omitting unrequested secret alcoves.

## Config requirements
Use `landmark-focal` for the climbable mountain as the main orientation landmark. Use `path-trail` for the hierarchy of roads, muddy off-road tracks, walkable trails, hiking routes, and climbing approaches. Use `terrain-relief` for the mountain, rugged slopes, cuts, and rolling muddy ground. Add visible vehicle staging pads and shelters near spawn and selected routeheads as scene-specific geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place one player spawn marker in the central spawn area, oriented toward the road network and mountain. Place ten or more off-road vehicle and bike ride positions across the staging area and selected routeheads, keeping the majority near spawn for immediate access and reserving a few at distant trail junctions. These are functional vehicle placements rather than baked-in road decoration.

## Layout components
Build a central spawn clearing with direct road access, vehicle staging space, and a clear view toward the mountain. Several drivable roads branch outward and reconnect across the landscape; rough muddy tracks peel away toward steeper or more remote terrain. Narrow walking and hiking trails climb the mountain and connect routeheads, overlooks, and the summit approach. Use realistic mud, rock, dirt, scrub, grass, tire ruts, and weathered track edges. Rugged outer slopes and dense distant terrain enclose the region. Compose the mountain as the dominant background mass, with spawn in the central foreground and route branches readable across the middle ground.

## Render order
Render in `isometric` order because the rugged realism, mountain silhouette, and multi-modal route hierarchy lead; the continuous plan can be derived from that image.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a large scale, sized for a few minutes of driving. The theme is realistic rugged mountain recreation and muddy off-roading. Apply `tiered` for strong climbable relief without required overhangs; no other modifier is forced.
