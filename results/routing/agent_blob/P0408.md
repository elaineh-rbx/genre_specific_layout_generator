# Scene prompt

Create a 3D free-roam mudding map on the American plains: one compact open mud field with varied muddy tracks, rutted paths, and puddles spread across the terrain for driving around.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Simulation: this is free-roam vehicle operation focused on driving through mud, explicitly not Racing because there is no finish line or lap condition.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-bounded`, taken from outside Simulation’s usual shape set, for one compact, clearly bounded, single-level mud field that contains the entire activity. The preset is Vehicle Sim with its normal open-world shape replaced by this compact bounded field; retain its vehicle-route idea but omit the unrequested shared resource area.

## Config requirements

Apply `path-road-vehicle` as broad muddy hauling-style routes rewritten for recreational off-road driving, with several intertwined vehicle-width tracks and churned ruts rather than paved roads. Apply `water-body` to the shallow standing-water puddles distributed across the field. No shops, gates, buildings, or task stations are requested.

## Layout requirements

Nothing needs post-segmentation placement. There are no checkpoints, collectibles, triggers, race markers, or task stations; the mud, ruts, and puddles are visible terrain for the image configuration.

## Layout components

Build one compact oval-to-irregular mud field on flat American plains. Its main zone is open churned earth crossed by several broad looping and intersecting muddy tracks, with alternate lines around the field so drivers can roam rather than follow one course. Vary the ground between deep-looking ruts, softer mud patches, shallow puddles, and short strips of prairie grass. Keep intersections broad and readable for vehicles, avoid a designated start or finish, and use low grassland, sparse fence posts, and a distant prairie horizon as the boundary. Frame the composition wide enough to show the whole field and its route variety at once.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first as the authoritative image, then convert it to top-down; the visual differentiation among mud, water, grass, and ruts matters more than exact route topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a small scale because the author chose one compact field rather than miles of plains. The theme is rugged contemporary American prairie mudding with wet brown earth, shallow reflective puddles, and muted grassland. Use `CHECK` for the puddle play volume, though the water is shallow and should not self-occlude; no other modifier is forced.
