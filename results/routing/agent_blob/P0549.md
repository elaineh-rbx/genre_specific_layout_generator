# Scene prompt
Create a 3D city-building tycoon map with separate city plots for each player, each developed as an individual city. Within every plot, arrange buildings, houses, shops, and shopping malls along a simple street layout.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Simulation, specifically a city-building tycoon. The separate player-owned city plots and rent-and-sales development loop are stronger signals than the urban appearance itself.

## Shape and preset
Use `plot-isolated`: each player receives a massive independent plot on which their city can grow without overlapping a neighbor. Match the Tycoon preset, substituting this isolated-plot shape for its shared team plot because the author explicitly requires separate cities for every player.

## Config requirements
Apply `gate-progression` as visible construction boundaries or blocked development pads that open as each city expands. Apply `path-circulation` as a simple internal street grid connecting homes, shop rows, and shopping-mall sites within every plot. The houses, commercial buildings, and malls should read as revenue-producing city fabric without introducing unrequested enterable interiors.

## Layout requirements
Nothing needs placement after segmentation. Rent, sales income, ownership, and upgrade purchases are gameplay systems rather than visible layout components, and the prompt does not request pickups, task triggers, NPC emitters, or other invisible markers.

## Layout components
Build a repeated field of large, equally sized city plots separated by broad buffer strips so neighboring developments remain visually and physically independent. Within each plot, reserve a clear entrance from the shared outer access path, then lay out a simple connected street pattern with small residential blocks, rows of street-facing shops, several larger generic buildings, and one prominent shopping-mall footprint. Keep undeveloped expansion pads legible around the initial district. Use mostly level paved and grassy ground to support reliable tycoon construction, with curbs and low plot-edge barriers defining ownership. Compose the isometric view so multiple complete plots are visible while one representative city remains readable in detail.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the city-tycoon appearance first and derive the top-down plan from it, because the simple streets need clear circulation but no topology is structurally difficult to validate.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a large scale band, driven by multiple independently expandable city plots and full sets of houses, shops, and malls. The theme is a clean, colorful contemporary Roblox city tycoon. Route through `P0`; all requested geometry occupies one unobstructed surface with no required interiors, overhangs, or separate maps.
