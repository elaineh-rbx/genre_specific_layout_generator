# Scene prompt
Create a soft, stylized woodland map with a warm, approachable atmosphere. Build a small forest loop formed by connected walking trails, with trees and undergrowth enclosing the paths and open clearings along the route for relaxed wandering with a customizable dog.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Use No Genre. The brief describes relaxed wandering in a place and explicitly rejects collection and destination objectives, so it does not supply the focal pull required for Adventure or a roleplay loop. The default axes apply: exterior enclosure, single-surface verticality, one zone, dressed structure, and grounded play-space.

## Shape and preset
No catalogue shape is assigned under No Genre. Use the Explorable Place preset, but omit its orientation landmark because the author explicitly wants no destination; retain its circulation and scenic-place intent.

## Config requirements
Use `path-circulation` for a small connected woodland trail loop; `vignette-photo` for the open clearings composed as calm scenic pauses along that loop; and `boundary-edge` for dense trees and undergrowth that naturally hide the map limit without making the trail feel hostile.

## Layout requirements
Nothing invisible needs placement after segmentation. The brief excludes pickups, objectives, destination markers, and other progression elements, and it does not request a special spawn or teleport system.

## Layout components
Build one small woodland zone around a connected loop of walking trails. The loop should pass several open clearings while returning naturally to its starting area, with no branch presented as a required destination. Use soft forest ground, warm foliage, clusters of trees, and approachable undergrowth; keep the trail readable and comfortably wide for a player walking beside a customizable dog. Dense planting encloses the outer edge and shapes views inward. Frame the overall composition around the complete loop, with clearings distributed as breathing spaces rather than objectives.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the warm stylized woodland look is authoritative, and the uncomplicated loop can be projected into a top-down plan afterward without risking gameplay validity.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by the requested compact woodland loop. The theme is warm, soft-stylized, and approachable. Use `P0`; the scene is one grounded exterior surface with no required overhang, interior transition, separate map, or structure-critical topology.
