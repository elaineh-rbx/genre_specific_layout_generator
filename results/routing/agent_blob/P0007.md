# Scene prompt
Create a stylized anime-inspired island adventure map made of six separate sea maps, each containing four compact islands a few hundred studs across for quick exploration. Each island has distinct boss-fight areas and surrounding minion zones, with routes or docks leading toward the next island; place world-spawn locations for collectible elemental, animal, and legendary fruits across the terrain. Include combat-oriented landmarks such as sword-training or fighting-style areas where appropriate, while the islands progress from earlier to later regions within each sea.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open-biomes`: six separately loaded seas each contain a four-island difficulty progression. The Open World & Survival RPG preset remains recognizable through its regional progression, collectible grounds, and landmarks, but omit player build plots because none were requested.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` emitters in every island’s minion zone and boss area, away from docks and safe arrival paths. Place the functional half of `collectible-nodes` at distributed timed fruit-spawn sites across all 24 islands, including viable sites for rare and mythic fruits. Place linked travel endpoints at each onward dock, preserving the four-island order within every sea and transitions between the six loaded sea maps.

## Layout components
Build six separate sea maps with four islands each. Every island is a few hundred studs across and contains an arrival dock, a quick exploration loop, surrounding minion territory, one distinct boss-fight area, and selected fruit-spawn clearings. Distribute sword-training and fighting-style landmarks where they support orientation without forcing one onto every island. Water encloses each four-island cluster; docks and visible routes establish early-to-late progression. Compose each sea so all four silhouettes and the next destination remain legible.
