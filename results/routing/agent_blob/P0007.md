# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] scale — Q: How big should one island be - a few hundred studs you cross in half a minute, or a full explorable region? A: Use the smaller island scale: a few hundred studs, designed for quick exploration, boss fights, and progression to the next island.
- [author] shape — Q: Is this one continuous world of seas you sail across, or are the seas separate loaded maps? A: Make the six seas separate loaded maps so each sea can stay focused and progression is simple.
- [author] goal — Q: Where do the fruits come from - do they spawn in the world on a timer, drop from bosses, or come from a shop? A: Fruits should spawn in the world on a timer as the main way to find them, including the rare and mythic fruits.

## Enriched image prompt
Create an anime-styled maritime action-RPG world split into six separate sea maps. Each sea contains exactly four compact islands, each a few hundred studs across for quick exploration. Arrange each four-island cluster in a readable progression order across open water, with a distinct open encounter area on every island for its boss and surrounding minions. Increase the visual intensity of the encounter ground from island to island without inventing unrelated settlements or interiors. Keep shorelines, water gaps, and the sequence toward the next island clear in each map. The full build therefore contains six seas and twenty-four islands, with enough open ground on every island for combat.

## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG, canonical slug `rpg`, because combat, bosses, upgrades, rarity tiers, swords, and fighting styles form a character-progression loop. Adventure, canonical slug `adventure`, is secondary because exploration advances across ordered islands and separate seas.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-chaptered`, taken from Adventure's usual set, as a Chaptered Journey containing six genuinely separate loaded sea maps. Preset is none: Action RPG assumes a hub-and-dungeon structure, while the prompt and author answer require seas of ordered islands.

## Config requirements
Use universal `island-cluster` for four compact islands in each sea and universal `water-body` for navigable-looking open water between them. Use RPG `hazard-aggro` for the broad boss-and-minion encounter ground on each island. No shop, town, interior, or physical level gate is added.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` emitters in every island's encounter area: one boss origin and surrounding minion origins per island, totaling twenty-four boss origins plus their local minion groups. Place `collectible-nodes` as timed fruit spawn points distributed across all twenty-four islands, including locations eligible for legendary ice, rare flame, mythic mammoth, and mythic T-Rex fruits; these are post-segmentation Collectible placements, not permanently drawn fruit props.

## Layout components
The top-level container has six map segments named Sea 1 through Sea 6. Each segment is one sea map containing exactly four compact islands, open water, clear shoreline arrival areas, an ordered island-to-island progression, and one boss/minion encounter area per island. All segments share an anime maritime combat theme while increasing in challenge presentation. Keep each island a few hundred studs across and compose each sea so all four islands and their order read clearly.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: draw each sea's four-island progression plan first, then dress it isometrically; the top-down image is authoritative because the ordered island sequence and usable encounter ground must remain connected and readable, but no supported authored blueprint is required.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use large scale for each sea map, composed of four medium, few-hundred-stud islands. The theme is anime maritime fantasy combat. Use `P4` for the six separate loaded seas and `CHECK` for movement across open water between island landmasses.
