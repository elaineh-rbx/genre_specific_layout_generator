## Enriched image prompt
Create a long, dark horror maze that takes a couple of minutes to traverse. Use repeating narrow corridors, disorienting turns, and interconnected loops to make the route feel confusing and dizzying while preserving one valid path from the entrance to a single exit. Keep the maze almost entirely dark, with a very bright character flashlight producing the only strong local illumination. Nothing else needs to be found inside.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Puzzle because the maze's solvable topology and reachable exit are the entire spatial challenge; horror is the visual theme rather than a second game genre.

## Shape and preset
Use `puzzle-maze`: the labyrinth topology itself is the puzzle and must be solvable. The matched preset is Maze / Labyrinth, retaining its loop-back path structure and dropping the unrequested collectible items.

## Config requirements
Use `path-loop` for corridors that repeatedly reconnect and create disorienting route choices without making the exit unreachable. Use `gate-escape`, taken from Survival through the shared catalogue, for the single visible exit at the maze boundary. Keep the very bright flashlight against near-total darkness as a whole-scene lighting constraint.

## Layout requirements
Place the `gate-escape` trigger at the single terminal exit on the outer maze boundary. No collectibles, hostile spawners, checkpoints, or intermediate objective placements are required.

## Layout components
The maze has one entrance, a couple of minutes of narrow repeating corridors, several reconnecting loops, misleading turns, and one reachable exit. Tall dark walls enclose every corridor and the outer perimeter. The full topology remains readable in the plan even though the playable presentation reveals only a flashlight-sized area.

## Render order
Use `authored_plan`: `puzzle-maze` is supported by the procedural blueprint generator, and solvability from entrance to exit is authoritative before horror dressing is applied.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by a couple of minutes of corridors while keeping individual turns legible. The theme is near-black claustrophobic horror with a harsh bright flashlight beam. Use `P6` because the maze must be valid by construction.
