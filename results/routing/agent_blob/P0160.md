# Scene prompt
Create a dark horror maze map with a couple of minutes of confusing, disorienting corridors leading to a single exit. The maze is enclosed by tall walls and kept mostly unlit, with the player’s bright flashlight as the main visible illumination; use a tense, oppressive atmosphere throughout.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Puzzle because navigating a confusing maze to its exit is the entire activity; there is no pursuing threat or survival resource loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `puzzle-maze` for a procedurally solvable labyrinth of enclosed corridors with one reachable exit. The preset is Maze / Labyrinth. Retain `path-loop` to make wrong turns circle back in disorienting ways, but drop the preset’s `collectible-nodes` because the brief explicitly requires nothing to be found before escaping.

## Config requirements
Draw `path-loop` as a mixture of winding loop-back corridors, deceptive near-parallel passages, and repeated junction silhouettes that feel confusing without becoming unplayable. Use tall continuous maze walls, a dark floor, sparse or absent fixed lighting, and deep shadow. The environmental illumination should be restrained so the player’s bright flashlight can remain the primary visible light source. Make the single exit visibly distinct only at close range, preventing it from becoming an immediate long-distance landmark.

## Layout requirements
Place one player start marker inside the maze at the opposite navigational region from the exit. Place exactly one scene-specific winner trigger at the single maze exit, sited beyond the last corridor opening. No keys, collectibles, solve inputs, checkpoints, or enemy spawners are required. Preserve enough clear width at turns and junctions for ordinary avatar movement and flashlight visibility.

## Layout components
Build an enclosed maze containing a couple of minutes of traversable corridors. Use tall walls, repeated junctions, several loop-back branches, and confusing changes of direction, while ensuring every route belongs to a valid solvable topology. The maze wall shell is the hard outer boundary. Keep the ground mostly flat and visually dark, with no distracting props beyond occasional subtle wall wear. Compose the start and single exit in distant plan regions and keep the corridor field visually dominant in both top-down and isometric views.

## Render order
Render `authored_plan` first and make the repository-generated maze blueprint authoritative, then produce top-down and isometric images from it; a sealed route or unreachable exit would make the game unplayable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a small scale, driven by the requested couple-minute traversal despite the maze’s long-feeling corridors. The theme is oppressive flashlight-lit horror. Route with `P6` because maze solvability must be valid by construction.
