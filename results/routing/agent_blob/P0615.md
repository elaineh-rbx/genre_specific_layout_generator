# Scene prompt
Create a 3D open-world car game map set in a present-day modern urban city spanning a few connected city blocks. Lay out a navigable street grid bordered by city buildings, with roads and intersections supporting traffic; populate sidewalks with AI pedestrians and streets with AI cars. The focused city area is designed for free-roam driving.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation. This is pure free-roam urban driving alongside ambient traffic and pedestrians, with no race or task loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, a shared compact city explored in any order. The preset is Vehicle Sim, retaining its open vehicle world and road network while dropping the unrelated shared resource field.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for a navigable street grid with connected intersections and traffic-width lanes. Use `settlement-density` for the focused cluster of a few modern city blocks. Use `npc-population` to draw inhabited sidewalks with ambient pedestrians and the ground they occupy. The image should also show ordinary AI traffic cars on the streets. Do not add enterable interiors, since none were requested.

## Layout requirements
The `npc-population` placement should distribute pedestrian spawn and wandering areas along sidewalks, with crossings only at selected intersections and no pedestrian routes through traffic lanes. Place AI-car emitters and ordered traffic paths on each usable street loop, with lane direction, intersection waypoints, and despawn points at the boundary. Add player vehicle spawns in curbside bays or a small parking area, plus a player arrival marker on the sidewalk.

## Layout components
Build a compact present-day city spanning a few connected blocks. Use a readable grid or modified grid with two-way streets, intersections, sidewalks, marked crossings, and at least one continuous cruising loop. Modern low- and mid-rise building shells define each block, with occasional parking pockets and street furniture that does not obstruct traffic. Reserve sidewalks as pedestrian zones and roads as AI-car and player-car zones. Close the outer edge with blocked continuation streets and distant building facades so the focused map still reads as part of a larger city. Frame the road network clearly from above.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: connected streets, lane continuity, pedestrian separation, and AI traffic paths should be authoritative before the isometric city dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by the clarified few-block scope. The theme is present-day modern urban free roam. Use `P0`; the map is one flat outdoor surface with no required overhang, interior crossing, or separate map.
