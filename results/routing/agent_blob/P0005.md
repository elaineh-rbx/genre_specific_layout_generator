## Enriched image prompt
Build a compact high-fantasy Human starter kingdom for Roblox as an overworld map linked to a separate small dungeon interior. Centre the overworld on one safe medieval village with a modest cluster of homes, shops, and quest-facing NPC ground around a walkable square. A clear trail leaves the village and passes into the nearby forest, where one open encounter clearing shows signs of Hollow creatures attacking. Continue the trail to a prominent dungeon entrance at the forest's far edge. The linked dungeon is a short stone interior ending in one small combat chamber. Keep the village, forest trail, attack clearing, and dungeon entrance readable as the player's first journey, using warm inhabited stone-and-timber fantasy architecture in the village, natural woodland around it, and darker ancient masonry inside the dungeon.

## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG, with Adventure secondary because the scoped build follows a story investigation from village through forest to dungeon.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-hub-dungeon`: the starter village and forest form the safe overworld hub map, which feeds a separate dungeon map. The matched preset is Action RPG, scoped down to one starter region and one dungeon.

Use `safezone-town` for the protected Human starter village. Use `social-hub` for the compact village square with nearby shops and quest-facing ground. Use `settlement-density` for the single modest village cluster rather than scattered kingdom-scale buildings. Draw the visible half of `npc-population` as non-hostile villagers occupying the square and village paths. Use `hazard-aggro` for the forest clearing where Hollow creatures are encountered away from the safe village. Use `building-interior` for the dungeon entrance and its linked interior map. Draw the visible half of `spawn-area`, taken from No Genre's catalogue row, as a composed arrival spot in the village square.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawn-area` SpawnZone in the village square, oriented toward the forest-bound trail. Place `npc-population` Zone occupancy across the square and village paths for ambient villagers. Place two `spawner-npc` Spawners: one in the forest encounter clearing for the attacking Hollow creatures and one in the dungeon's terminal encounter chamber.

## Layout components
There are two map segments. The Starter Kingdom map contains one village at centre, a surrounding nearby forest, a village-to-forest trail, one forest attack clearing, and one dungeon entrance at the route's far end. The Simple Dungeon map is a compact stone interior with a short approach and one final encounter chamber. Village buildings cluster tightly around the square; the forest forms the overworld boundary. The composition should show the village as the safe anchor, the trail as the readable progression, and the dungeon entrance as the distant destination.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the first-quest route from village through forest encounter ground to the dungeon entrance must remain connected before each map is dressed.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is medium because village buildings, NPC ground, the forest trail, and the encounter clearing must remain individually legible; the dungeon is split into its own map to preserve detail. The theme is a high-fantasy Human starter kingdom transitioning from warm medieval village to threatened forest and dark stone dungeon. The pipeline requires `P4` for the overworld and dungeon as separate maps and `P3` for crossing from the forest exterior into the dungeon interior.
