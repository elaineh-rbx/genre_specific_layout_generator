# Scene prompt
Create a 3D high-fantasy starter kingdom region centered on a single human village. The village forms the safe central hub, bordered by a nearby forest where dark Hollow creatures appear, with a simple dungeon encounter set deeper along the forest path.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG (`rpg`), established by the author’s MMORPG progression, combat, quest, and dungeon loop. Adventure (`adventure`) is secondary because the starter quest directs exploration from village through forest to dungeon.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-hub-dungeon`: one village-and-forest overworld feeds a separate small dungeon encounter. The Action RPG preset is the closest fit, scaled down to this starter kingdom slice.

## Config requirements
Draw `safezone-town` as the protected human village; `social-hub` as its compact quest, shop, and equipment center; `hazard-aggro` as Hollow-infested forest clearings set away from village ground; `path-trail` as the readable forest road from hub to dungeon; and `landmark-focal` as the dungeon entrance visible deeper in the woods. The village, surrounding forest, and dungeon architecture should carry a cohesive high-fantasy human-kingdom style.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` emitters only within the Hollow encounter clearings and the final dungeon encounter area, never in the safe village or directly on the connecting trail. Place one player start in the village center, one quest destination along the forest route, and one final objective zone inside the dungeon. These markers follow the single opening quest from hub to forest investigation to dungeon encounter.

## Layout components
Build one small human village at the region’s center, a nearby forest wrapped around its outer side, and one simple dungeon beyond the forest path. A main road leaves the village, narrows into a wooded trail, passes several Hollow clearings, and terminates at the dungeon threshold. The dungeon is a short enclosed encounter space rather than a second sprawling region. Use fields or low walls to separate safe village ground from the forest, and frame the village as the foreground anchor with the darker dungeon landmark glimpsed through trees.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: render the plan first and treat it as authoritative so the safe hub, encounter clearings, and dungeon route remain connected and correctly ordered.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, reflecting a focused first-ten-minutes starter region. The theme is bright high-fantasy civilization giving way to Hollow-corrupted woodland. Use `P4` for the separate dungeon map and `P3` for its linked overworld-to-interior entrance.
