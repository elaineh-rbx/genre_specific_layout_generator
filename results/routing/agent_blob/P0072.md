# Scene prompt
Create a 3D multiplayer grand-strategy map of one fictional continent with a Europe-inspired overall land shape, surrounded by ocean. Lay out 51 uniquely positioned fictional nations with clearly distinct fictional borders, each divided into multiple provinces of cities, farmland, or wilderness; use placeholder country, capital, and city names only. Shape the terrain with frozen northern regions, western offshore islands, broad central plains, major mountain ranges, southern peninsulas, eastern forests, major rivers, and large lakes. Place one walkable contemporary capital city within each nation as its government center and player spawn, containing a government building, barracks, factory, town hall, resource storage, locked airport, rail station, construction yard, and surrounding urban streets. Distribute additional cities, industrial sites, farms, resource areas, harbors, airfields, and defensive structures across provinces according to terrain and coastlines.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Strategy: territorial expansion, resource control, diplomacy, and army movement define the game.

## Shape and preset
Use `terrain-open` from the Real-Time Strategy preset. This is one contiguous continent where forces move dynamically across provinces rather than following fixed lanes.

## Config requirements
Apply `buildzone-plot` as broad national territory supporting distributed construction around each nation’s holdings. Draw `tracker-core` as each nation’s contemporary capital and governmental heart, with 51 such cores. Use `settlement-density` for the capital street grids and additional cities, `terrain-relief` for the major mountain ranges, `water-body` for the surrounding ocean, rivers, and large lakes, and `island-cluster` for the western offshore islands. The image must also show fictional borders and province divisions, frozen north, central plains, southern peninsulas, eastern forests, farmland, wilderness, industrial sites, farms, resource areas, harbors, airfields, and defenses.

## Layout requirements
Place `tracker-core` in both streams: site exactly one capital core inside each of the 51 national territories. Place 51 player spawn markers, one at each capital’s government center. Place `collectible-nodes` across suitable provinces, aligning food and timber with farms and forests and mineral resources with industrial terrain. Preserve distinct national and provincial ownership regions.

## Layout components
Build one huge Europe-shaped fictional continent bounded by ocean, partitioned into 51 nations and multiple provinces per nation. Each nation has one walkable capital urban zone containing a government building, barracks, factory, town hall, resource storage, airport, rail station, construction yard, and streets. Roads and rail connections link capitals, additional cities, farms, industrial and resource sites, harbors, airfields, and defensive positions. Frame the full continent from above with western islands and southern peninsulas legible against the ocean.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative because 51 non-overlapping nations, their provinces, coastlines, and connected strategic geography must remain spatially coherent.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge, driven by the whole continent, 51 nations, and 51 walkable capitals. The theme is contemporary fictional geopolitical strategy. Use `tiered` for mountain relief and `CHECK` for ocean, rivers, lakes, and separated islands; no stacked surfaces, interiors, or separate maps require another modifier.
