# Scene prompt
Create a 3D destruction sandbox map showing one massive, continuous city that takes a long time to cross on foot. Fill it with many dense city blocks and varied building sizes, arranged along a broad street grid with open corridors and districts that accommodate earthquake, tornado, flood, and meteor destruction.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Use Simulation. This is explicitly a destruction sandbox whose activity is realistic building physics, with no score, race, or survival objective.

## Shape and preset
Choose `world-open`, meaning one shared persistent city that can be traversed continuously in any direction. Use the Physics Sim preset, retaining its open-world shape and adapting its physics and hazard features to city-scale destruction.

## Config requirements
Use `physics-rig` for realistically breakable buildings and structural assemblies across varied building sizes. Use `hazard-kill` for visible earthquake damage, tornado paths, floodable low ground, and meteor impact destruction. Use `path-circulation` for the broad connected street grid and open emergency corridors. Use `settlement-density` for many dense urban blocks and distinct districts across the massive city.

## Layout requirements
Place four free-text disaster origin controls, one each for earthquake, tornado, flood, and meteor effects, distributed in four separated districts so every disaster has an unobstructed test area. Site earthquake coverage beneath a dense mixed-height block, the tornado entry at a broad outer corridor, the flood source beside the lowest district, and the meteor target over a central open intersection. Place player arrivals at several street intersections around the city edge and center so the long map remains testable.

## Layout components
Build one massive continuous city of many dense blocks on a broad, connected street grid. Mix low-rise, mid-rise, and tall buildings, with open corridors separating several visually distinct districts. Include a low flood basin connected to streets, a wide tornado approach corridor, a dense earthquake test block, and a large central intersection suitable for meteor impact. Keep all districts linked at ground level with no separate maps or enterable interiors. Bound the city with distant urban continuation. Frame a representative downtown core while preserving the legible street network.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the realistic destructible city and varied skyline first, then derive the authoritative top-down because exact street topology is not a win-condition constraint.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge because the continuous city takes a long time to cross on foot. The theme is super-realistic modern urban disaster destruction. Route `P0`; all play remains on one continuous ground-connected map without required interiors, overhang-dependent traversal, or separate zones.
