# Scene prompt

Create a 3D game map centered on a small stylized cartoon pond designed for easy pedal-boat riding, with room for up to four pedal boats at once. Ducklings and fish occupy the pond water around the boats.

# Agent decision

## Genre

Simulation is the sole genre. Players operate pedal boats freely in a persistent pond with no lap, finish line, or competitive racing condition, so this is a small vehicle-operation simulation.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-bounded`, taken from outside Simulation’s usual set because all activity occurs within one clearly enclosed, single-level pond. Preset is none: Vehicle Sim assumes extraction and hauling, while Vessel Operation assumes the vessel deck itself is the map; neither remains recognisable for free pedal-boat riding on water.

## Config requirements

Draw `water-body` as a small, shallow-looking cartoon pond with an uncluttered riding surface and a readable shoreline. Draw the visible habitat for `npc-population` as ducklings floating on the surface and fish visible beneath or breaking the water. Include four recognizable pedal boats as full 3D props, sized and spaced so all can operate simultaneously without immediately colliding. Add a simple waterside launch edge for boarding, but no unrequested shop or town.

## Layout requirements

Place the functional half of `npc-population` throughout the pond: ducklings on varied surface routes and fish in the water volume, avoiding fixed clusters that block the launch. Their exact counts are not stated. Site four pedal-boat start positions along the launch edge, evenly spaced and facing into open water. Preserve a broad central maneuvering area and clear approach room around animals so the horn interaction can occur ahead of a boat rather than beside its spawn.

## Layout components

The main zone is one compact oval or organically rounded pond. A short shoreline launch zone occupies one edge, while open water fills the center and animal habitat areas loosely ring the perimeter and cross the middle. The water is bounded by a soft grassy bank with simple cartoon reeds and stones; terrain stays low and non-overhanging. Props include exactly four pedal boats plus duckling and fish models in unspecified quantities. Compose from an elevated three-quarter view showing the entire pond, launch, all four boats, and clear shared riding water.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: appearance and readable boat-animal composition lead, then the top-down plan is derived from the authoritative isometric image.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is small, set by one pond and four boats. Theme is playful stylized cartoon aquatics. Use `CHECK` because fish occupy a water volume; verify that the shallow volume does not self-occlude.
