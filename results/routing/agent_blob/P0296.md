# Scene prompt
Create a 3D destruction sandbox map set in a modern city. Lay out streets between dense blocks of skyscrapers, office buildings, shops, and ordinary urban infrastructure, with a broad city skyline extending into the background.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Simulation is the dominant and only genre: the authored activity is an open-ended destruction sandbox with no score, target, race, or combat objective.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, interpreted as one shared, contiguous modern city that all players can roam and operate in. Use the Physics Sim preset because breakable physical reactions are the entertainment, but omit its environmental hazard because none was requested.

## Config requirements
Use `physics-rig` for skyscrapers, offices, shops, street fixtures, and ordinary infrastructure assembled as destructible structures with readable break points and debris behavior. Use `path-circulation` for the connected street network through the city. Use `settlement-density` for dense urban blocks and the broad skyline; this is explicitly a big modern city rather than scattered buildings. Do not assume enterable `building-interior` spaces: the prompt names building types but does not say players enter them.

## Layout requirements
No invisible objectives, scoring volumes, pickups, checkpoints, or task stations are needed. Place only a safe player arrival area at the edge of the street network, outside the immediate collapse footprint of the nearest destructible buildings.

## Layout components
Build one large contiguous city district. Arrange a connected hierarchy of broad avenues and smaller cross streets around dense blocks containing varied skyscrapers, mid-rise offices, ground-level shop fronts, sidewalks, lamps, traffic furniture, and other ordinary urban infrastructure. Preserve generous street corridors between structures so collapsed debris has room to spread without sealing every route. The boundary should read as continued city through skyline buildings and distant streets rather than an abrupt wall. Compose the main view down a broad avenue, with layered block heights producing a wide metropolitan skyline in the background.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: visual massing, destructible silhouettes, and the skyline lead, while the ordinary connected street plan can be projected from that look without requiring computed topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large because the request explicitly calls for a big city with dense blocks and a broad skyline. The theme is contemporary everyday urban realism designed for playful structural destruction. Use `P0`; all play remains on one visible surface, with no required interiors, overhang-dependent floor layout, separate maps, or volumetric traversal.
