# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] theme — Q: Modern city, or something else - a fantasy town, a sci-fi block? A: Use a modern city: streets, skyscrapers, offices, shops, and ordinary urban infrastructure.
- [author] goal — Q: Is there a score or a target, or is it a sandbox you knock down for fun? A: Make it a sandbox where players destroy the city for fun, with no score or required target.
- [agent_inferred] boundary — Q: How much of the big city should fit in one buildable frame? A: Use one large dense city district with several blocks and main roads, not an entire region.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a large modern city destruction sandbox as one dense, contiguous urban district. Lay out several connected street blocks with a mix of skyscrapers, mid-rise offices, street-level shops, and ordinary urban infrastructure. Author the buildings and infrastructure as chunky multi-part assemblies that can break apart, while leaving broad street corridors and open intersections where players can move and observe collapses. Keep the city free-form with no scored target, required destruction order, or finish area.

## Genre
The genre is Simulation.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, a Zone (Shared Persistent World) containing one freely explored city district. The preset is Physics Sim, keeping its physics contraptions and circulation while dropping the unsupported environmental kill hazard.

## Config requirements
Use `physics-rig` for breakable urban assemblies and collapse-ready props, `destructible-cluster` from Survival for multi-part skyscrapers, offices, and shops authored to come apart, `path-circulation` for the connected street network, and `settlement-density` for the dense modern city blocks.

## Layout requirements
No checkpoints, scoring volumes, targets, or progression gates are required. Destruction reactions, debris behavior, and the absence of scoring are mechanics applied to the visible breakable geometry.

## Layout components
The map contains several city blocks, connected main streets, broad intersections, skyscrapers, offices, shops, and ordinary infrastructure distributed along the roads. Building footprints leave safe circulation and collapse space around major structures. The district has a clear outer boundary but no prescribed route through it. Compose the city so tall and low structures are both readable and multiple destruction areas remain visible.

## Render order
Use `isometric` because the deliverable is a free-form destruction set whose building massing, street canyons, and collapse-ready composition lead the result.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use large scale because the smallest essential structure is the city block and main road rather than interior furniture. The theme is a contemporary modern city with ordinary urban infrastructure. `P0` is sufficient because the playable ground is one contiguous surface and no enterable interiors or stacked playable floors were requested.
