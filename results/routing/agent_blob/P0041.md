## Enriched image prompt
Create a compact open world inside one garden bed, composed to feel enormous from an ant’s scale and packed with distinct areas to explore. Use lush, stylised oversized plants, exposed soil, and large stones to form the garden terrain, with the ant colony as a clear home destination. Distribute food to forage across the garden and leave traversable routes back to the colony. Show many other animals throughout the space as visible inhabitants that make the environment feel alive. Keep individual food items and the colony entrance readable while the full garden remains visible as one connected map.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure because exploration and collecting food across an open map provide the activity and destination. Roleplay & Avatar Sim is secondary because the player inhabits an ant within a living animal world.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`: one contiguous garden that can be explored in any order. Match the Scavenger Hunt preset because the ant searches for distributed objective items, while omitting hidden alcoves and a quest-board structure that were not requested.

## Config requirements
Use `den-shelter` from Roleplay & Avatar Sim for the ant colony entrance and surrounding nest mound as the ant’s natural home. Use `npc-population` for the many visible garden animals and the ground they inhabit. No water, interior rooms, settlement, or unrelated landmarks are implied.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` as food pickups distributed among the garden’s distinct exploration areas, with no invented exact count. Place the layout half of `npc-population` in several visible habitat pockets without blocking ant-scale circulation. Add one free-text `TriggerZone` at the colony entrance as the food-delivery destination; no canonical option precisely represents a forage deposit point.

## Layout components
Build one bounded garden-bed zone with varied soil clearings, oversized plant clusters, large stones, and an ant-colony mound or entrance. Arrange several small exploration pockets around the colony and connect each back to it over walkable soil. Spread food pickups and ambient animals across those pockets. The garden-bed edge forms the outer boundary. Frame the colony as a visible anchor while preserving the exaggerated ant-scale height of plants and stones.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the ant-scale visual composition is authoritative, and the open garden’s connectivity is simple enough to derive from it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use small scale because the physical extent is one compact garden bed and individual food items must remain legible, even though it feels vast to the ant. The theme is lush, stylised, cartoonish garden wildlife. Use `P0`: all play remains on one outdoor surface with no forced interior transition, separate map, or validity-by-construction requirement.
