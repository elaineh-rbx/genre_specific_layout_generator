# Scene prompt
Create a 3D open-world map from an ant’s scale: a single compact garden bed that feels enormous, dense with explorable zones. Build a lush stylized garden with towering plants, soil, stones, and many visible animals throughout. Place the ant colony as a distinct home area, with food scattered around the garden for collection and routes weaving between plants, rocks, and earth.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation because foraging and carrying food back to the colony form a repeatable task loop. Adventure is secondary because exploring the oversized garden is also central.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open` for one compact shared garden that can be explored in any order. Start from the Role Sim preset, retaining its task station and circulation while dropping vehicle roads because all movement is ant-scale and on foot.

## Config requirements
Use `trigger-task` for the visible colony food-delivery area; `path-circulation` for ant-scale routes weaving among plants, stones, and soil; `landmark-focal` for a distinct colony mound that remains an orientation anchor; and `npc-population` for habitat spaces visibly occupied by many non-hostile garden animals. Towering stylized plants and oversized stones must make the compact bed feel immense.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` as scattered food throughout the garden, with clusters beside plants, around stones, and in exposed soil pockets; keep every cluster reachable from the colony by at least one route. Place the functional half of `trigger-task` as one delivery trigger at the colony entrance. Place `npc-population` inhabitants throughout suitable habitat patches while keeping the colony arrival and principal paths unobstructed.

## Layout components
Build one compact garden bed with a distinct colony home zone near one edge and several explorable pockets among towering flowers, broad leaves, soil ridges, roots, and boulders. Connect the pockets through branching but readable ant-scale tracks that return to the colony. Scatter food across near, middle, and far zones. Hide the boundary with dense planting, raised bed edges, and impassable earth. Compose from a low ant-scale viewpoint while keeping the colony and route network legible.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: exaggerated ant-scale atmosphere is authoritative, and the garden’s branching circulation does not require computed topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The physical scale is small, but it must read as huge from an ant’s viewpoint. The theme is lush, stylized, lively garden micro-world. Use `P0`; all required play fits one grounded exterior surface.
