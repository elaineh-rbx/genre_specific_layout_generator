# Scene prompt
Create a futuristic tower-defense map on open ground, designed around a start-to-goal enemy route that can dynamically reroute across the terrain when player-built wall barriers block it. Include a hosting area with teleporters for entering game sessions. Populate the playable battlefield with generic sci-fi defensive units and large mech stand-ins, including standard mechs and heavier armored mech forms; avoid direct copies of named game items or units.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Strategy. It is tower defense, but player-built walls make enemies path dynamically over open ground rather than follow a fixed track.

## Shape and preset
Use `terrain-open`: one contiguous battlefield between an enemy start and defended goal, broad enough for alternate routes around changing barriers. The preset is none because the standard Tower Defense bundle assumes a fixed `lane-actor-track`, while this scene deliberately rejects one.

## Config requirements
`buildzone-plot` draws broad, unobstructed placement ground across the battlefield rather than narrow fixed-grid strips. `tracker-core` draws a fortified futuristic goal structure. The hosting area includes visible session teleporters. Dress the field with generic sci-fi defensive emplacements, standard mech stand-ins, and a smaller number of visibly heavier armored mech forms, without reproducing named designs. Keep permanent scenery away from likely rerouting corridors.

## Layout requirements
Place one `tracker-core` objective marker at the defended goal and one `spawner-npc` at the opposite edge as the enemy origin. Place teleport triggers within every hosting-area teleporter. Reserve a connected open navigation field between start and goal so runtime wall placement can redirect enemies without relying on pre-authored lanes; wall build locations must not seal the entire route.

## Layout components
Build a hosting zone at one edge and a large open battlefield beyond it. Teleporters face the battle entrance. The enemy origin and defended core occupy opposite ends, with broad open ground and several generous side passages between them. Use low futuristic surface panels and sparse perimeter machinery, leaving the center readable and flexible. Generic gun emplacements and mech silhouettes provide scale; heavier armored forms anchor the core side. A technological barrier encloses the map, and the elevated composition shows all viable routing ground.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the visual identity leads, while dynamic pathfinding means no single authored route topology must be preserved.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by the reroutable battlefield and separate hosting zone. The theme is original futuristic mech defense with cool-toned metal and energy lighting. Use `P0`; one top-down surface captures all permanent geometry, and changing runtime walls do not add a generation route.
