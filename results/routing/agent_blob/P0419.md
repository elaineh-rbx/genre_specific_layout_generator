## Enriched image prompt

Keep every lot open for a future dream home and a small yard, with clear protected boundaries and no overlap. Connect the lots with simple roads and surround them with grass and scattered trees. Do not prebuild houses on the open plots; the layout should show where players construct them.

## Genre

## Shape and preset
Use `settlement-buildable`, BuildZone (Personalized Plots), as a strict grid of player-owned building footprints. The preset is Home Builder, keeping its buildable-plot shape and suburban road circulation while omitting an unrequested central social hub and prebuilt interiors.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for simple suburban roads sized to reach all eight plots and `settlement-density` for the low-density residential arrangement. Grass, scattered trees, and clearly outlined open lots are scene-specific drawn geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly eight free-text Plot entries, one assigned medium-sized BuildZone per player. Site each plot against the road, leave room within it for one dream home and a small yard, and keep protected plot boundaries disjoint. No house geometry or interior is placed before players build.

## Layout components
One neighborhood zone contains eight equal residential lots arranged along a simple connected road network. Each lot has open grass, a small-yard allowance, a road-facing edge, and a clear ownership boundary. Trees occupy shared verge space without intruding on build footprints. The neighborhood edge remains compact and readable in one frame.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish eight equal, non-overlapping assigned plots and their road access first, then dress the authoritative plan isometrically.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale so all eight plots and their yard boundaries fit while individual building footprints remain legible. The theme is a simple low-density suburban neighborhood. Route `P3` because player-built homes are intended as enterable spaces linked to the shared exterior.
