# Scene prompt
Create a small asymmetrical horror map inspired by a classic Roblox headquarters building, designed for four onion-ring survivors and one cheeseburger killer. Arrange a simple office-like HQ with a central interior space and a few clear looping routes through connected rooms and corridors; include exterior approaches around the building to support alternate movement paths. Keep the layout compact, with recognizable blocky headquarters architecture and open sightlines balanced by corners and room connections.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Survival because the round is asymmetric horror built around evading one killer until a timer expires. Action is secondary because the survivors can briefly fight back.

## Shape and preset
Use `warren-looping`: the compact headquarters and its exterior approaches must form a few connected loops with no dead-end trap corridors. The preset is 1 vs All, with its escape gate omitted because the clarified win condition is survival by timer.

## Config requirements
Draw `cover-los` as corners, low partitions, doorway recesses, and connected office rooms that interrupt long views without making the building cramped. Draw `building-interior` as the enterable blocky headquarters linked directly to exterior paths. Preserve recognizable classic Roblox HQ architecture and a readable central interior.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` as five role-separated starts: four survivor starts distributed around connected rooms and one killer start isolated far enough away to prevent an immediate encounter. Place `trigger-scoring` over the active headquarters and exterior approaches as the round-survival area. No `gate-escape` or objective placement is needed.

## Layout components
The build has one small blocky headquarters, a central office-like interior, several surrounding rooms, and short corridors that reconnect into a few simple loops. Exterior approaches wrap around the building and reconnect through multiple doors, creating alternate movement paths. Open sightlines cross the central room, while corners, partitions, and room connections provide breaks in visibility. A compact outer boundary encloses both building and approaches. Frame the headquarters as the dominant central mass with its loop structure legible around it.

## Render order
Use `topdown` because every pursuit route must reconnect and the no-dead-end topology must be valid before the building is dressed.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, supporting exactly four survivors and one killer. The theme is classic blocky Roblox headquarters with playful food-character horror. Use `P6` for guaranteed looping connectivity and `P3` because play crosses between exterior approaches and the headquarters interior.
