# Scene prompt
Create a bright, stylised 3D team shooter map with expansive grassy fields and several tall, simple towers distributed across the terrain as central contested landmarks. Arrange the towers with open ground and clear approaches between them, providing distinct areas for two opposing teams to converge and fight; include space for accompanying bot characters.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Shooter: two armed teams, including bots, fight to hold towers.

## Shape and preset
Use `open-battlefield` rather than the usual lanes because the scene calls for expansive open grass with several dispersed contested landmarks. The preset is King of the Hill, substituting this open shape while retaining held points and elevated firing positions; its generic cover array is dropped because the brief stresses open ground and clear approaches.

## Config requirements
Use the visible part of `control-zone` to mark each tower’s hold area clearly. Use `cover-elevated` for tall, simple, climbable tower platforms overlooking the grass, with exposed predictable stairs or ramps. Keep broad sightlines and distinct approach space around every tower.

## Layout requirements
Place `control-zone` at every tower, matching one hold volume to each landmark. Place `spawner-npc` as two team-specific bot entry areas near the opposing sides, shielded from direct tower fire and oriented toward the contested field. Include one allied helper-bot spawn beside the player-side entry area, preserving the stated count of one; other team bot numbers are unspecified.

## Layout components
Build one expansive grassy battlefield with opposing team arrival areas on far sides. Distribute several tall towers across the field rather than lining them into strict lanes, leaving open ground and readable approaches between landmarks. Each tower has a simple base, exposed climb route, upper platform, and visually distinct held area. Use gentle grass variation but no major terrain barriers. Sparse stylised rocks or low field details may frame routes without becoming dense cover. The boundary is an open-looking but finite field edge defined by distant low fencing, hedges, or soft impassable terrain. Compose the towers as a balanced constellation visible from both team sides.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative because the tower silhouettes, open-field composition, and vertical contest points define the scene more than intricate topology. Derive the top-down plan from it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large because several towers need separate approach fields. The theme is bright, simple, stylised grassland combat. Use `P2` because tower platforms overhang lower walkable bases and require layered capture.
