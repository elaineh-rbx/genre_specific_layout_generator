# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] goal — Q: Do you catch the fish with a rod from the shore, or swim and grab them? A: Catch the mythical fish with a fishing rod from the shore.
- [author] scale — Q: One lake and a river, or a whole region of water to search? A: Use one lake and a river so the map stays small and easy to explore.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a small, easy-to-explore mythical fishing map built around one lake connected to one river. Shape accessible grassy or rocky banks around both bodies of water, with a simple shoreline trail linking several clear fishing positions from which players can cast rods without entering the water. Keep the lake as the main open focal area and let the river extend through the map as a narrower secondary search zone, using a light magical natural atmosphere without adding unrelated buildings or activities.

## Genre
Adventure is the sole genre because finding mythical fish around the map is a focused scavenger hunt.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, named Open World, for one contiguous lake-and-river map explored in any order. This uses the Scavenger Hunt preset's shape while omitting its unrequested quest board and hidden alcoves.

## Config requirements
Use universal `water-body` for exactly one lake and one connected river. Use `path-trail` for the walkable shoreline route linking readable rod-fishing positions around both waters.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` as mythical fish catch points within the lake and river, each within rod-casting reach of a valid shore position. Distribute them across both bodies of water so players search around the map without needing to swim. No combat spawners, checkpoints, or finish gate are requested.

## Layout components
The central lake forms the broad main zone, and one river connects to it and traverses the secondary zone. Accessible banks ring the relevant fishing edges, with a continuous trail joining several open casting spots while leaving vegetation or rocks outside the casting clearance. The map boundary closes around this compact watershed, and the composition keeps both the lake and river visible as one connected environment.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the magical lake-and-river composition first and derive the top-down view, because the connected waterscape is simple and requires no procedurally solved route.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by one compact lake and river with individual shore fishing positions legible in one frame. The theme is a light magical natural waterscape suited to mythical fish. Use `CHECK` for the real lake and river volumes; no other modifier is forced.
