# Scene prompt
Create a compact fishing map centered on one lake with a river leading away from it, with accessible shorelines and fishing spots around both bodies of water for finding mythical fish.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure: the player explores the map to find mythical fish, while fishing is the collection method rather than a stat, economy, or management simulation.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, a single compact outdoor map where the lake, river, and all fishing positions are freely reachable in any order. The preset is Scavenger Hunt, adapting its objective nodes to shore-caught mythical fish and omitting hidden alcoves and a quest board because they were not requested.

## Config requirements
Use `water-body` for exactly one central lake and one river extending from it. Use `path-trail` as an accessible shoreline route around the lake and alongside the river, with widened banks at visible fishing spots. Keep the water open and readable from shore rather than enclosing fish inside caves or layered water volumes.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` as mythical-fish catch points in the lake and river water, each within fishing-rod reach of a safe shoreline standing area. Distribute nodes across both bodies of water and avoid overlapping catch points. No exact fish count was given, so preserve an unspecified set rather than inventing a quantity.

## Layout components
Build one small outdoor zone organized around a single lake, with one river leaving its edge and continuing toward the map boundary. Wrap a continuous walkable bank around the lake and continue it along both sides of the river where practical. Create several widened, flat fishing spots along both shore systems, each with a clear cast into unobstructed water. Use simple ground relief only to shape the banks; avoid cliffs that would block access. Bound the map with scenic vegetation or low terrain while leaving the lake-and-river composition clear. Frame the overview with the lake as the visual anchor and the river leading outward through the scene.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the mythical fishing atmosphere first and derive the top-down plan from it, because the simple shoreline network has no maze, circuit, or brittle traversal topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, matching the requested compact, easy-to-explore lake-and-river map. The theme is tranquil mythical freshwater fantasy. Use `CHECK` for the water volume; it should pass as a single unobstructed surface with no self-occluding underwater layers.
