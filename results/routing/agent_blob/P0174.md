## Enriched image prompt
Create two separate simple dungeon-themed 3D spaces. The first is a sequence of exactly four connected rooms: a plain entry room, a plain connecting room, a monster room that reads as immediately dangerous, and a final exit room containing a portal to the other world. The second space is a small bounded dungeon-like other world with enough simple cover and branching visibility to conceal one small axis object. Keep both maps compact and show each room connection, the portal transition, and the hidden-object search space clearly.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Survival, with Adventure secondary because the second space is explored to find a hidden objective.

## Shape and preset
Use `rooms-sequence`, taken from outside Survival's usual set, for the dominant four-room dungeon because the rooms are worked through in order. The preset is none; no Survival or Adventure preset preserves both the exact four-room sequence and the separate hidden-object world without adding unsupported features.

## Config requirements
Use `hazard-kill` for the visibly dangerous monster room and `teleporter-link`, taken from Adventure through the shared catalogue, for the portal shown in the exit room and its matching arrival point in the other world. The second segment is a bounded exploration zone rather than another room sequence.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` once as a Spawner inside the monster room. Place `teleporter-link` as a paired Teleporter: one marker at the dungeon exit portal and one at the other-world arrival point. Place one `collectible-nodes` Collectible, using Adventure's objective-item meaning, in a concealed but reachable location in the other world. Entering the monster room causing an explosion and collecting the axis completing the game are mechanics.

## Layout components
Segment one is a separate four-room dungeon map with three door or corridor connections in sequence and a portal in the fourth room. Segment two is a separate small bounded dungeon-themed exploration map with a portal arrival zone and one concealed axis object. The first segment uses `rooms-sequence`; the second uses `space-bounded`. Each has closed dungeon boundaries and a complete overhead composition.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, because the four-room order, portal pairing, monster placement, and reachable hidden object must remain coherent.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use the small scale band because individual rooms and one hidden object must remain legible. The theme is a simple dungeon in both spaces. `P4` is required because the dungeon and other world are separate maps.
