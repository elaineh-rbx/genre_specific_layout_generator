# Scene prompt
Create a 3D finding-game map set in a simple, colorful cartoon fantasy world. Build a small varied map with distinct areas and hiding places distributed around it for 10 collectible characters, including the starter character.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure: the activity is a spatial scavenger hunt in which players explore to find characters. No secondary genre is needed; character advantages and disadvantages are gameplay rules, not additional layout content.

## Shape and preset
Use the `world-open` shape and the Scavenger Hunt preset. This is one small contiguous fantasy map whose distinct areas can be searched in any order, rather than a directed route or separate chapter maps.

## Config requirements
Draw `alcove-secret` as varied hiding recesses integrated into the fantasy scenery, including tucked-away corners behind props and vegetation. Draw the physical face of `tracker-quest` as a colorful character-collection board near the starting area. Do not visualize character abilities, unlock rules, or interface elements.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` for exactly 10 characters: one starter character at the start area and nine unlockable characters distributed one per hiding site across the distinct areas. Each node should sit against a recognizable hiding recess while remaining physically reachable. Place the functional tracker for `tracker-quest` at the starting area beside the starter character.

## Layout components
Build a compact central start clearing with the collection board and starter character, surrounded by several visually distinct cartoon-fantasy pockets such as a bright grove, a small rocky rise, a mushroom patch, and a whimsical ruin garden. Short walkable connections should form a loose loop around the clearing, with side recesses providing the nine remote hiding sites. Use gently varied grassy ground, colorful rocks, oversized plants, and simple fantasy ruins. Keep an obvious soft boundary of cliffs, dense foliage, and decorative fencing around the map. Compose the whole map so the central clearing reads immediately while peripheral pockets remain individually legible.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the colorful look first and derive the authoritative top-down from it, because no topology-critical maze or circuit is required.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by the brief’s compact varied map and 10 total character sites. The theme is colorful cartoon fantasy with playful oversized forms. Use `P0`; the scene is one walkable surface with no required interiors, overhangs, separate maps, or structurally generated course.
