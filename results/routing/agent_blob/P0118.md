# Scene prompt
Create a bright, cartoonish 3D shooter lobby as the central hub. Arrange a welcoming open lobby space with distinct areas for matchmaking, weapon unlock displays, skin boxes, and wrap boxes, plus a clearly marked entrance leading into a separate practice arena. Include the practice arena as an adjacent combat space, and provide connections from the lobby to match maps for team deathmatch and free-for-all.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Shooter: the lobby serves a weapon-based shooter with practice and competitive match destinations.

## Shape and preset
Use `hub-portals`, taken from outside Shooter’s usual shapes, because this scene’s purpose is to route players from a central lobby to separate experiences. The preset is none; no shooter preset describes the requested hub.

## Config requirements
Use `station-loadout` for a visible weapon-unlock display containing about 15 different readable cartoon weapons. Use `target-practice` for static and popping targets at graded distances inside the adjacent practice arena. Also draw distinct skin-box and wrap-box display counters, a matchmaking area, a prominent practice entrance, and two clearly differentiated match-map gateways.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `station-loadout` at the weapon display so players can inspect and swap unlocks. Place `target-practice` targets downrange in the practice arena, with near, middle, and far groups facing one safe firing line. Place interaction triggers at the matchmaking station, skin-box counter, wrap-box counter, practice entrance, and exactly two match gateways: one for team deathmatch and one for free-for-all.

## Layout components
The welcoming open lobby is the central and largest zone. Arrange matchmaking, the 15-weapon unlock display, skin boxes, and wrap boxes as separate perimeter stations around a clear circulation floor. A bold entrance connects directly to an adjacent enclosed practice arena with a safe firing line, target field, and backstop. Two additional gateways lead onward to separate team-deathmatch and free-for-all maps. Use broad walkways from the lobby centre to every station, keep the practice connection visually dominant, and enclose the hub with bright chunky cartoon architecture. Frame all destinations clearly from the arrival point.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, because the hub’s circulation and separation of safe lobby, directed range, and match gateways must remain unambiguous. Dress the isometric from that plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by one hub plus an adjacent practice arena. The theme is bright, playful, toy-like shooter architecture. Use `P4` because the two match gateways lead to genuinely separate maps.
