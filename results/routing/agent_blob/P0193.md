## Enriched image prompt

A single snowbound road begins at a truck staging point, passes exactly three checkpoints, and ends at a cold dark town where a generator core is unloaded. These sockets can interchange near-zero-visibility snow, a frozen-lake crossing, fallen-tree blockages, an avalanche opening or closing a road, a strange radio beacon, repeated abandoned trucks, or an optional abandoned village without making every event appear in one run. Include enterable roadside structures for scavenging, while keeping the final town visible as the journey’s distant orientation landmark. Preserve one continuous driveable point-to-point route in the elevated composition.
