# Scene prompt

Create a 3D cooperative adventure map set on one compact, continuous frozen mountain road crossing a stormy alpine region. A single truck route winds through snowbound slopes and tight mountain passes, with three checkpoints spaced along the journey toward a distant dying town in the background, the delivery destination. Route conditions can include deep snow with very short sightlines, a frozen lake crossing where the road vanishes beneath ice, fallen-tree blockages, and avalanche-altered sections where one path is closed and another is exposed. Place scattered abandoned trucks along the route, each visually identical to the players’ truck, plus occasional strange tyre tracks beside the road. An abandoned village sits off the roadside as an omittable, intermittent location rather than a fixed stop, with small buildings that can be searched. Near the final stretch, the road becomes almost obscured by the intensified storm before descending into the town.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Adventure, with Survival secondary because the storm, ice, and avalanche conditions threaten the journey.

## Shape and preset

Use `route-guided` from the Guided Trail preset: one continuous truck road sequences the journey through three checkpoints to the town. Keep its trail and destination landmark, but omit a separate reveal exit.

## Config requirements

Build `path-trail` as a truck-width mountain road through tight passes, deep snow, a frozen-lake crossing, fallen-tree blockages, and avalanche variants with one closed branch and one exposed branch. Use `landmark-focal` for the distant dying town. Add `terrain-relief` for snowbound alpine slopes, `water-body` for the frozen lake, and `hazard-kill` for visibly dangerous ice and avalanche margins. Reserve a route-side branch for an optional abandoned `settlement-density`, with `building-interior` on its searchable small buildings. Draw scattered duplicate trucks and strange tyre tracks.

## Layout requirements

Place exactly three event checkpoint volumes at safely widened road sections, spaced from start to final descent. Mark mutually exclusive avalanche route branches so every enabled variant keeps one continuous truck path. Reserve one optional village activation area off the road; omitting it must leave the main route intact. No fixed stop belongs inside that branch.

## Layout components

Create one compact region-scale road from a snowy mountain start through three checkpoint clearings to the distant town. Sequence narrow passes, a frozen lake, fallen-tree sites, avalanche forks, and a near-whiteout final descent. Place abandoned duplicate trucks on shoulders without blocking the minimum drive line, with tyre tracks occasionally running beside it. Put the optional village on a short side spur with a few searchable buildings. Bound the map with steep snowy slopes and storm-obscured distance, framing the town as the far destination.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: establish a continuous drivable main road and valid alternate avalanche branches first, then dress that authoritative plan isometrically.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is large, driven by a region-scale road and three checkpoints. The theme is bleak alpine mystery-adventure. Use `tiered` for mountain relief, `CHECK` for the frozen lake, and `P3` for entering the optional village buildings.
