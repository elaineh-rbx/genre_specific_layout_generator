# Scene prompt

Create a 3D rainbow-colored go-kart race map with a simple, non-crossing loop track suspended against an open sky backdrop. Place a starting grid for up to four karts beside a clear start/finish line, with the continuous multicolored road looping back to this point; include visible lane space and safe track edges around the course.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant and sole genre is Racing. The authored three-lap go-kart event has a fixed circuit and simultaneous competitors.

## Shape and preset

Use `route-circuit` for one simple closed loop with no crossings or stacked track sections. Match the Circuit Racing preset: its starting grid, lap detection, and guardrails fit directly, while its 180-degree turnaround is dropped because the road continuously loops back without reversing direction.

## Config requirements

Draw `spawn-grid` as four evenly spaced kart slots beside the line. Draw `startpoint-line` as one prominent combined start/finish stripe, `lane-corridor` as a broad continuous multicolored driving corridor, and `barrier-guardrail` as safe physical edging around the suspended road. Keep the entire road readable as a single rainbow loop against open sky.

## Layout requirements

Place one `spawn-grid` containing four starting positions immediately behind the line, all oriented along the course. Place one `startpoint-line` marker and one `trigger-lap` region across that same stripe, configured spatially to register three complete laps. Place `checkpoint-respawn` recovery points at safe intervals around the loop, each centered on the road and aligned with forward travel.

## Layout components

Build one medium suspended circuit with a start straight and a sequence of gentle, non-crossing bends returning to the same line. The rainbow road is the sole traversable surface, wide enough for four karts to race side by side near the grid and pass around the course. Guardrails enclose both edges; open sky surrounds the track with no ground scenery, bridges, tunnels, or overlaps. Compose the full loop clearly in frame.

## Render order

Use `authored_plan`: generate the closed circuit blueprint first, then produce top-down and isometric images from it. The plan is authoritative because a broken or non-closing loop would make the race unplayable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale band is medium, supporting a complete three-lap kart circuit for four racers. The theme is bright rainbow arcade racing in open sky. Use `P6` because the loop must be connected and valid by construction; the explicit non-crossing design avoids `P2`.
