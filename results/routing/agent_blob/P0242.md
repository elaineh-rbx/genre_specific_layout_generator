# Scene prompt

Create a peaceful, mysterious 3D floating-island cluster called Stars Alike. A central main island serves as a social hub with interactive seating areas, connected by simple bridges to separate compact islands: one for a small obstacle course, one for a target-hitting area, and one for a friendly race track. Scatter hidden secret areas around the islands and along less obvious paths, with no formal entrance or introductory structure so players explore and discover the space themselves.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Party & Casual: three small friend-focused minigames share a peaceful social world.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open`, taken from outside the genre’s usual shapes, because the joined island cluster is one freely explored map with no prescribed order. The preset is none; the Minigame preset’s isolated stage, lobby teleport, leaderboard wall, and spectator area conflict with the seamless exploratory cluster.

## Config requirements

Use `social-hub` as the central island’s open gathering area with interactive seating. Draw `island-cluster` as a small group of distinct floating landmasses joined by simple bridges. Include exactly three compact activity islands: obstacle course, target-hitting area, and friendly race track. Add secret alcoves and subtle side paths around island edges without a formal entrance or tutorial structure. Favor sparse reusable props and simple geometry for low rendering cost.

## Layout requirements

Place safe start markers at each of the three minigames. Add checkpoint and respawn placements at completed sections of the obstacle course, functional hit targets across the target area, and racer starts plus one `trigger-scoring` finish line on the race island. Exact player, checkpoint, target, seat, and secret counts were not stated.

## Layout components

Make one main social island the largest landmass, with clear seating clusters and paths branching toward three bridges. Each bridge reaches one smaller dedicated minigame island. The obstacle island has a short reachable platform chain; the target island has a firing line and unobstructed target field; the race island has a compact closed loop. Tuck secret spaces below sightline breaks, behind rocks, or on narrow side paths, without stacking islands directly over one another. Use open-air boundaries, grassy rock terrain, and a composition centered on the hub with satellites around it.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, then dress the isometric view; bridge connectivity, the obstacle chain, and the race loop must remain playable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a medium scale for one hub and exactly three compact game islands. The theme is peaceful celestial mystery with low-detail floating terrain. Apply `P6` for valid obstacle and racing routes, plus `CHECK` for the open-air island cluster; keep landmasses non-overlapping so the play envelope does not self-occlude.
