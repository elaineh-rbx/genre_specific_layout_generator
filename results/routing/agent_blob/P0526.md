# Scene prompt
Create a bright, toon-style 3D obby map built as one compact tower that fits in a single view, with a short vertical sequence of colorful, simple obstacle-platform stages. Place two clear avatar-choice boxes at the tower’s starting area, each displaying one of two distinct preset avatar outfits.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Obby & Platformer, `obby-platformer`, with Roleplay & Avatar Sim, `roleplay-avatar-sim`, secondary because choosing and temporarily wearing a preset avatar is part of the experience.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-tower` for one compact vertical course visible in a single frame. Tower Obby is the matching preset, simplified for a short climb and extended with two avatar-choice stations.

## Config requirements
Draw one physics-valid `path-track` through a short sequence of colorful stages. Use `obstacle-jump` for simple platform gaps and stepped ascents and `obstacle-climb` for one or two short ladder or truss sections. At the starting area, draw exactly two large avatar-choice boxes side by side, each clearly displaying a distinct preset outfit on a mannequin or full-body icon. Draw the summit as `winner-zone`.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly two avatar-selection trigger volumes, one inside each choice box, bound separately to the displayed temporary outfit. Site both on the start pad before the first obstacle with enough separation to avoid accidental double activation. Place one `winner-zone` on the summit platform. No other post-segmentation placements are required.

## Layout components
Build a broad ground-level starting pad holding the two equal-sized choice boxes, followed by one compact open-sided tower and a short singular route to its top. Arrange bright simple platform stages in a readable upward zigzag with safe landings and legal gaps. The two outfit displays must be visually distinct but equally prominent, with clear approach space in front of each. Use rounded toon geometry, saturated colors, clean outlines, and minimal clutter. Keep the tower’s full base-to-summit path visible in one isometric composition, with open void around the course and a broad celebratory finish pad above.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: valid obstacle spacing and non-overlapping choice triggers must lead, then the isometric render applies the toon styling.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small because the entire short tower fits one view. The theme is bright, colorful, and toon-like. `P6` guarantees a playable course, while `P2` handles stacked platforms over lower stages.
