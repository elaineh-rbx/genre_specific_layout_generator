# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] theme — Q: You want it to look different from Brookhaven — should it read as modern suburban with different props, or a completely different setting such as fantasy or retro? A: Keep it modern suburban, but use different-looking props, buildings, and layout so it has its own look.
- [author] shape — Q: Do players build their own house on an empty lot, or claim and decorate a house that is already standing? A: Players should claim an already-standing house and decorate it; no house-building system is needed.

## Enriched image prompt
Create an original 3D modern-suburban life-roleplay town with a distinct layout, buildings, and props. Divide the town into quiet residential streets lined with already-standing claimable houses and a separate commercial area containing enterable shops, with a connected road and pedestrian network between them. Make the houses varied but clearly readable as homes players can claim and decorate, and give the shops usable entrances and interiors for obtaining tools. Preserve the familiar open suburban roleplay structure without copying another game's exact visual design.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim.

## Shape and preset
Use `settlement-claimable`, Claimable Houses, because players claim finished homes rather than build on empty lots. It matches the Life preset exactly in housing model while the author's clarification requires an original arrangement and appearance.

## Config requirements
Use `district-zoned` to separate residential streets from the commercial shop area, `path-road-vehicle` for broad modern-suburban roads between those districts, `path-street` for walkable access from streets to homes and shops, and `building-interior` for the claimable homes and stores players enter. Tools are shop contents or gameplay items, not a separate map option.

## Layout requirements
Place one free-text TriggerZone for claiming at the entrance of every generated house, tied to that already-standing home. No buildable empty Plot markers are allowed. Decoration systems, tool ownership, and shop transactions are mechanics rather than additional layout placements.

## Layout components
The concrete build is one modern-suburban town with a residential district of multiple finished claimable houses, a commercial district of enterable shops, connecting roads, sidewalks, driveways, and pedestrian paths. The two districts remain visually distinct but part of one continuous map. Building fronts and entrances face the circulation network, and the town edge forms a clear soft boundary within the isometric frame.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, because the original suburban look and relationships among houses, shops, streets, and props lead; the projected top-down only needs to preserve that composition.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use the medium scale band because individual houses, shop entrances, roads, and sidewalks must remain legible in one town frame. The theme is an original modern suburb with different-looking props, buildings, and layout from the named reference. `P3` is required because play crosses between exterior streets and enterable houses and shops.
