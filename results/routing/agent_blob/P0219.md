# Scene prompt
Create a modern suburban roleplay town with a distinct layout and original-looking props and buildings. Arrange claimable, already-standing houses in residential areas, with shops and public town spaces connected by streets and sidewalks; include space for house interiors that players can decorate.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, canonical genre `roleplay-avatar-sim`, because players claim homes, decorate interiors, visit shops, and freely roleplay in a persistent town.

## Shape and preset
Use `settlement-claimable`, a prebuilt suburban town whose already-standing houses are claimed and decorated rather than constructed. The preset is Life, matching claimable homes, separated districts, vehicle-capable streets, and enterable interiors.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for a broad suburban street network, with roads at least 30 studs wide where two-way vehicle traffic is expected, and parallel sidewalks for pedestrians. Use `district-zoned` to separate quiet residential clusters from the busier shop area, `building-interior` for claimable houses and shops that players enter, `settlement-density` for a coherent modern suburban town rather than isolated buildings, and `social-hub` for the requested public town spaces. Props, architecture, and street arrangement must be original-looking rather than a visual copy of the reference.

## Layout requirements
Nothing canonical needs placement after segmentation. The brief does not specify pickups, teleporter shortcuts, NPCs, triggers, or spawn markers; house-claiming and decoration are systems layered onto the visible prebuilt homes and interior spaces.

## Layout components
Lay out several residential areas of detached, already-standing claimable houses around a compact commercial district of shops. Connect all districts with a legible road network and continuous sidewalks, and provide one central public gathering space such as a landscaped civic square or neighborhood park. Each house needs an exterior footprint and linked interior space suitable for decoration. Use flat-to-gently graded suburban ground, original street furniture, landscaping, façades, and roof silhouettes. Bound the town naturally at its outer roads and compose the scene so the public core is central while residential clusters branch outward. No exact house or shop count was stated.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the town’s distinct architectural look first, then derive the top-down plan, since the street network need not be valid by construction.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is large, driven by multiple residential areas, shops, roads, and public spaces. The theme is original contemporary suburbia. Use `P3` because players cross between exterior streets and enterable house and shop interiors.
