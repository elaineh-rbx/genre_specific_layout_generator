# Scene prompt
Create a 3D self-contained pirate-themed escape room: an enclosed room filled with pirate-themed props, with a single exit door as the focal point for the eventual escape route.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Puzzle because this is an escape room, although the present build intentionally stops before puzzle mechanics are specified.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single`, one enclosed room containing the entire future escape experience. The preset is none: applying Escape Room now would invent buttons, clues, and collectible items that the author explicitly deferred.

## Config requirements
Draw `gate-progression` as one closed exit door and make it the room’s visual focal point. Fill the room with pirate-themed geometry only: stacked treasure chests, rope coils, barrels, crates, rolled maps, lanterns, an anchor, ship timbers, and nautical wall dressing. Do not imply specific clue relationships or interactive puzzle inputs yet.

## Layout requirements
Nothing functional needs placement after segmentation in this first environment pass. The exit door is reserved as the eventual solve gate, but its unlock trigger, puzzle inputs, collectibles, and checkpoints remain deliberately undecided until the mechanics brief exists.

## Layout components
Build one medium rectangular enclosed room with a level plank floor, heavy timber walls, and a single exit door centered on the far wall. Leave a clear navigable route from the room’s open center to that door. Arrange pirate props in readable clusters around the perimeter: barrels and crates in storage corners, treasure chests near one wall, maps and nautical objects on a table, ropes and anchor against another wall, and lanterns spaced for warm illumination. Keep enough negative space around every cluster for later puzzle additions. Use the walls as the hard boundary and compose the scene from an elevated corner so the exit and major prop groups appear together.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: topology is simple and the pirate atmosphere, focal door, and prop composition should lead before conversion to a plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by one self-contained room. The theme is stylized wooden pirate treasure hold with warm lantern light. Use `P0`; one flat interior surface captures the complete layout.
