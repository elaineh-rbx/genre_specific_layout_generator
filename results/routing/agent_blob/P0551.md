# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map showing a shared traditional Georgian village made of neighbouring personal farmyards that players can visit. Each compact starting yard is enclosed by wooden fencing and contains a small traditional Georgian house, a modest storage shed, and exactly 24 planting plots arranged in clear rows, with open space reserved for later expansion. Populate the plots with a varied visual mix of grapevines, orchard trees, berry bushes, vegetables, herbs, and melons representing the large collection of Georgian crops. Frame the village with a rural Georgian panorama in the background; do not include animals.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Simulation: a persistent farming and collection game where players grow and sell crops for currency in neighboring personal yards.

## Shape and preset
Use `plot-isolated`: each player has an independent fenced farmyard that remains part of one walkable shared Georgian village. The preset is none; the available Simulation presets do not preserve both the compact farming-station layout and the neighboring social village described here.

## Config requirements
Use the drawn side of `trigger-task` for exactly 24 clearly arranged planting beds in every starting yard. Use `path-circulation` as village footpaths linking the neighboring gates so players can visit one another. Draw each yard’s small traditional Georgian house, modest storage shed, wooden fence, reserved expansion ground, and mixed crops. Do not add animals, and do not imply enterable house or shed interiors.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional side of `trigger-task` exactly 24 times per personal yard, one planting interaction station centered on each visible plot and aligned to its row. These stations support planting and harvesting without encoding crop prices, XP, rarity, or selling rules into geometry. No animal, NPC, or egg spawners are placed.

## Layout components
Form a shared village from neighboring compact rectangular farmyards along a simple walkable lane. In every yard, put the house near the entrance, the shed beside a service edge, exactly 24 planting plots in clear rows through the central working area, and an obvious empty expansion strip behind them. Mix grapevines, orchard trees, berry bushes, vegetables, herbs, and melons across the plots without attempting to show all 300 crop types. Use low wooden fencing and gates between private yards and the common lane. Keep the terrain gently rural but the working beds level, with distant Georgian hills and village scenery framing the boundary. Compose the view to show one complete yard clearly and several neighbors connected around it.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the cultural appearance and readable farm composition lead, with the top-down plan derived afterward because the simple village paths need no procedural topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a large scale band for a shared village containing multiple expandable yards. The theme is warm traditional rural Georgia with wood, stone, vineyards, orchards, and a broad pastoral panorama. Route through `P0`; no requested geometry requires overhang, interior transition, or separate maps.
