# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] theme — Q: Should the forest be realistic and grim or bright and stylized? The prompt gives the setting but not the treatment. A: Use a bright, stylized forest so the shooter is clear and easy to play.
- [author] goal — Q: What ends a round - clearing a wave count, a timer, or is it endless? A: Make it endless; bots keep spawning near the player and the goal is to survive as long as possible.
- [agent_inferred] map boundary — Q: Does endless describe the forest extent or only the enemy loop? A: Use one finite forest combat map; only the bot spawning is endless.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a bright, stylized 3D forest shooter map as one finite open combat space. Arrange tree clusters to break long sightlines while leaving broad clearings and connected ground routes for first-person movement and bot encounters. Keep the terrain readable and uncluttered, with visible ammunition pickups resting on the forest floor. Leave enough open ground around every playable position for bots to appear 20 studs from a player without intersecting trees, and close the outer boundary with a denser tree line.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Shooter, specifically an endless PvE survival shooter.

## Shape and preset
Use `open-battlefield`, one contiguous Open Battlefield with dispersed forest cover rather than lanes. The preset is none: PvE Shooter assumes a lane network and structures, while this prompt explicitly needs an open forest and dynamic nearby spawns.

## Config requirements
Use `cover-los` for bright stylized tree clusters that break firing lines without making the forest difficult to read. Ammunition and bot origins are post-segmentation placements rather than image config.

## Layout requirements
Use `spawner-npc` as a dynamic Spawner rule tied to each active player, selecting valid forest ground exactly 20 studs from that player and rejecting points inside trees or outside the boundary; it repeats endlessly rather than using a fixed bot count. Place `collectible-loot` Collectibles as ammo pickups on visible ground throughout the clearings and routes. Do not place a finish or winner zone. First-person camera, the visible blaster, ammo logic, and endless survival timing are mechanics.

## Layout components
The build contains one finite forest battlefield, several broad clearings, connected walkable ground, distributed tree-cover clusters, ground-level ammunition sites, and a dense perimeter tree line. Open pockets around the route network support the 20-stud bot spawn rule. The composition keeps all playable ground and the natural boundary visible from above.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, because clear routes, tree sightlines, valid 20-stud spawn pockets, and reachable ammo ground must remain functional before stylized isometric dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use the medium scale band because the forest must support sustained movement while individual trees, ammo pickups, and a 20-stud spawn distance remain legible. The theme is a bright, stylized, highly readable forest. Use baseline `P0`: the battle occupies one continuous ground surface with no interiors, stacked levels, or separate maps.
