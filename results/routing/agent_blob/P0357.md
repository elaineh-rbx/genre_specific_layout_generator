# Scene prompt
Create a 3D first-person shooter map set in a bright, stylized forest, with open navigable ground among trees and scattered ammo pickups placed on the forest floor. Frame the view for a visible blaster in the foreground, with clear nearby spaces around the player for enemy approaches.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Shooter, with Survival secondary because endless hostile bots pressure the player to survive as long as possible.

## Shape and preset
Use `open-battlefield`, an open forest combat ground with dispersed approach space rather than fixed lanes. The preset is PvE Shooter, but its lane shape is replaced with `open-battlefield` and its interior option is dropped because the brief specifies an outdoor forest.

## Config requirements
Keep the forest floor open enough to navigate while trees form loose sightline breaks around clear enemy approaches. No tactical cover array is added: the trees already supply the only cover the prompt asks for.

## Layout requirements
Use `spawner-npc` for endless hostile bot origins placed 20 studs from the player’s current start area, distributed around the nearby approach perimeter rather than directly on top of the player. Use `collectible-loot` for ammo pickups scattered on visible forest-floor clearings. Place one central player start with safe immediate footing and readable approaches.

## Layout components
Build one medium forest clearing network: a central player area, several open approach pockets approximately 20 studs away, and navigable ground connecting them among bright stylized trees. Scatter ammo pickup sites across the forest floor while preserving first-person firing lanes. The forest thickens into the outer boundary. Compose from player height with the visible blaster in the foreground and nearby attack routes clearly framed.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the stylized forest appearance leads, and its open-ground plan can be projected afterward without risking playability.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, large enough for repeated nearby approaches but still centered on one player. The theme is bright, clean, stylized woodland combat. Use `P0`; the scene is one outdoor surface with no overhang, interior transition, or separate map.
