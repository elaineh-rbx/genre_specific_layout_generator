# Scene prompt
Create a colourful, cartoony 3D snowy battlefield for two opposing penguin kingdoms. Place a distinct kingdom base at each end of a small map, with open snow-covered ground and several territory capture points distributed between and around them so both sides have areas to contest.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Action, with Shooter secondary for its opposing-base and held-point layout vocabulary. Players directly fight as penguins; the territory structure organizes those clashes without implying a strategy-management game.

## Shape and preset
Use `open-battlefield`, taken from outside Action’s usual set, because the small snowy map has dispersed capture points rather than lanes or one bounded arena floor. No preset remains recognisable: King of the Hill supplies held-point vocabulary, but this scene has several points and omits its elevated firing and cover bundle.

## Config requirements
Use the drawn half of `spawn-teambase` for two distinct penguin kingdom bases at opposite ends, each fully screened from direct attacks across the map. Use the visible half of `control-zone` for several clearly marked territory circles, flags, or icy platforms distributed across central and side ground. Do not add role buildings or weapon stations; the answers explicitly make roles simple loadouts only.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly two `spawn-teambase` start regions, one inside each kingdom base, with mirrored travel distance to the nearest contested ground. Place the functional half of `control-zone` at every visible territory marker, spread so neither side can take all points from one position. “Several” is descriptive rather than an exact count, so preserve that wording without inventing a number.

## Layout components
Build one small, open snow battlefield with a colourful penguin castle or fortified village at each opposing end. Spread several capture areas through the middle and around flanking snowfields. Use broad snowy routes, low ice ridges, snowbanks, frozen rocks, banners, and playful kingdom silhouettes to make each territory readable while keeping movement open. Enclose the play space with decorative ice cliffs and impassable snowdrifts. Compose the two bases as balanced opposing anchors with the contested points visible between them.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the top-down plan is rendered first and authoritative because fair base distances and capture-point distribution determine playability.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small. The theme is colourful cartoon penguin warfare in a snowy kingdom setting. Use `P0`; the scene is one surface with no overhangs, interiors, or separate maps.
