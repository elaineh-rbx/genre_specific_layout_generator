# Scene prompt
Create a 3D modern suburban roleplay town as a compact, easy-to-navigate small town. A single main street runs through the center, lined with a few distinct shops and civic-style buildings; place several selectable residential houses on nearby side streets or lots for players to move into. Include interior-ready spaces in homes and shops for roleplay items such as baby supplies, pet items, and food.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim: players choose ages and avatars, move into existing houses, and use free themed items for open-ended town roleplay.

## Shape and preset
Use `settlement-claimable` for a pre-built town whose several houses can be selected rather than constructed. The preset is Life, matching claimable homes, enterable venues, vehicle-ready streets, and distinct residential and commercial areas.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for one central two-way main street at least 30 studs wide, with short side-street access to the homes. Use `district-zoned` to separate the shop-and-civic frontage from quieter residential lots. Use `building-interior` for the homes and shops players enter, including dedicated baby-supply, pet-item, and food displays. Use `settlement-density` for a compact small-town fabric of a few shops, civic-style buildings, and several houses rather than a sparse landscape.

## Layout requirements
Place one scene-specific house-claim marker at the front entrance or lot boundary of every selectable home, keeping each marker outside the doorway and unambiguous to approaching players. No currency, job, purchase, collectible, or progression markers are needed because the items are free roleplay props.

## Layout components
Run one main street through the center, with a short row of distinct shops and civic buildings facing it. Put several different pre-built houses on nearby side streets or shallow residential lots, each with a visible front path and private boundary. Connect all entrances by sidewalks and short crossings. Keep the commercial core compact and the residential area close enough to navigate on foot despite vehicle-width roads. Furnish the enterable shops with clearly separated baby, pet, and food sections, and make house interiors varied enough for different family roleplay choices. Use modern suburban materials, colorful clean signage, street trees, lamps, lawns, and low fences. Frame the town so the main street organizes the composition.

## Render order
Render in `isometric` order: architectural style and town readability lead, while the straightforward street plan can be projected from the authoritative view.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, as specified by one main street and only a few buildings. The theme is clean modern suburban roleplay. Use `P3` because players cross from outdoor streets into house and shop interiors.
