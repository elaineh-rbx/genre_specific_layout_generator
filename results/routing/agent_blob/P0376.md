# Scene prompt

Create a simple free-drifting map on a small, bare open baseplate with plenty of unobstructed clear space and no circuit, finish line, or themed scenery.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Simulation. This is specifically free-form vehicle operation rather than Racing: the author explicitly rejected laps, a finish line, and a circuit.

## Shape and preset

Use the single shape `world-open`, interpreted here as one shared, contiguous baseplate where vehicles can drift in any direction. The preset is none. Vehicle Sim supplies the right genre boundary and shape, but its hauling roads and shared resource field do not belong in this deliberately empty drifting space, so the preset bundle is no longer recognisable.

## Config requirements

No canonical image-config option applies. The requested baseplate and clear driving surface are the scene’s core geometry, while the prompt explicitly excludes roads, themed scenery, obstacles, progression areas, and a finish structure.

## Layout requirements

Place one vehicle start marker near an edge of the baseplate, oriented toward the open center and leaving a broad clear runout ahead. No checkpoints, lap gates, finish trigger, pickups, task stations, or scoring volumes are needed.

## Layout components

Build one small, flat rectangular baseplate as the sole zone, with a broad unobstructed center and enough margin on every side for wide drifting arcs. Use a plain neutral baseplate material with no terrain relief or water. Add no roads, corners, buildings, barriers within the driving area, decorative props, or scenery. The outer baseplate edge is the visible boundary; frame the whole plate from above with the open center dominant.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render the sparse isometric scene first as the visual authority, then derive the top-down plan from it. The topology is intentionally simple and does not require a constructed plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale band is small, driven by the author’s explicit request for a small open plate. The theme is bare, neutral baseplate minimalism. Use route `P0`; this is one flat, walkable surface with no overhangs, interiors, separate maps, volumetric play, or validity-constrained course structure.
