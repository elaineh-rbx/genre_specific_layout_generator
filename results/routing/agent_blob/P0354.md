# Scene prompt
Create a small sci-fi game map with a fortified futuristic player outpost as the starting base, set within terrain patrolled by strange glowing, distorted creature-like anomalies. Place one secret fortified base a moderate distance away across the map, surrounded by anomalies and with an enclosed interior containing the king’s arena. Scatter at least 10 keys around the map between the starting outpost and the secret base.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Shooter is dominant, with Adventure secondary because ranged base defense leads into a key hunt and a distant boss objective.

## Shape and preset
Use `open-battlefield`: one contiguous small map contains two dispersed fortified points of interest and the traversable ground between them. Preset is none; no standard shooter mode captures the asymmetric starting outpost, collectible route, and boss base together.

## Config requirements
Use `landmark-focal` for exactly one secret futuristic base, visible as the destination a moderate distance from the starting outpost. Use `building-interior` for that secret base’s enterable enclosed king arena. Draw the player’s fortified sci-fi outpost, the secret compound, open intervening terrain, and strange glowing distorted creature-like anomalies patrolling and surrounding both strongpoints. The starting base is shown at its initial built size; growth from kills is gameplay, not a static layout instruction.

## Layout requirements
Use `collectible-nodes` for at least 10 keys, scattered across distinct reachable positions between and around the two bases rather than clustered at either endpoint. Place one unkeyed player spawn inside the starting outpost. Place unkeyed anomaly encounter positions around the starting base, across the route, and outside the secret base, plus one king encounter position at the center of the enclosed arena. The secret-base access condition is scripting, not extra geometry.

## Layout components
The start zone is one fortified player outpost at one side of the map. Open sci-fi terrain and a readable ground route cross the middle toward exactly one secret fortified base on the far side. The destination includes an exterior perimeter, a single entrance, and an enclosed king arena. Low alien terrain and anomaly clusters frame the route without hiding the bases. A hard outer map boundary contains the battlefield. Compose both bases in one diagonal view, with the collectible corridor between them legible.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish the two-base route, reachable key distribution, and linked secret-base entrance first, then dress that authoritative plan isometrically.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, with the bases only a moderate traversal apart. The theme is fortified futuristic sci-fi with luminous distorted creatures. The required modifier is `P3` because play crosses from the outdoor battlefield into the secret base’s enclosed king arena.
