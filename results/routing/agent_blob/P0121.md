# Scene prompt
Create a 3D shooter map set at a small modern city street crossroads. Four long, straight roads extend from the central intersection in each direction, with sniper-range sightlines between their ends; arrange urban buildings and street-side cover along the roads and around the intersection.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Shooter: this is a sniper-focused score match with repeated respawns and a fallback AI opponent.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-network` as four long firing lanes meeting at one central intersection. The preset is none: the free-for-all crossroads does not honestly retain the team bases, power-ups, elevated positions, or objectives of the available shooter presets.

## Config requirements
Use `cover-los` for street-side vehicles, concrete barriers, planters, and full-height urban blockers spaced along all four roads, preserving long sniper sightlines while giving players staged protection. Use the visible geometry of `spawn-protected` for several screened respawn recesses distributed near the four road ends. Urban buildings frame the streets as non-enterable facades; no interior play is requested.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` as multiple random-select respawn positions across all four outer road ends, each behind solid cover and outside direct lines through the intersection. Associate each with a three-second protective force-field trigger after spawning. Place one `spawner-npc` for exactly one medium-difficulty AI opponent when the server has no other player, sited in a protected outer-road recess rather than at the exposed centre.

## Layout components
Build one central four-way intersection with four long, straight roads extending north, south, east, and west. Keep end-to-end sniper-range visibility through the crossing, but rhythmically place street-side cover along each road so movement is possible. Frame the roads with compact modern city blocks, sidewalks, lamps, signs, parked vehicles, barriers, and planters. Buildings remain perimeter facades and hard boundaries rather than accessible interiors. Recess protected spawn pockets behind corners or solid streetscape elements at the road ends. Compose the intersection as the dominant focal point with all four axial roads clearly legible.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative because the four firing axes, spawn screening, and cover spacing must preserve fair sniper routes. Dress the isometric from that plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium: the footprint is a small crossroads, but each of four arms must support long sniper distance. The theme is a clean modern city street. Use `P0` because all play remains on one exterior ground surface with no overhang or interior transition.
