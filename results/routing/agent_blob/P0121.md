## Enriched image prompt
Build two distinct 3D spaces for a sniper shooter. The first is a compact, plain staging lobby. The second is a small modern-city crossroads map: four long straight roads meet at a central intersection, with long sniper-range sightlines between opposite road ends. Modern street-front buildings and sparse street cover frame the roads without closing the firing corridors. Provide several screened spawn pockets around the outer road ends, plus one clearly sited hostile AI entry point for the lone bot used when the server is empty.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Shooter, organized around long ranged sightlines and respawning combat.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-staged`, outside Shooter's usual shapes, as a container for a separate lobby and isolated match map. No Shooter preset remains recognisable after requiring that two-map container and omitting unrequested team bases, powerups, and interiors, so the preset is none.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for the compact lobby staging space. Use `settlement-density` for the built-up modern street fronts around the crossroads. Use `cover-los` sparingly for street objects and building corners that provide recovery cover without breaking the four long sniper corridors. Use `spawn-protected` for visible screened pockets around map starts.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` respawn markers across multiple outer-road pockets so random respawning does not expose a player immediately; the three-second force field is behavior attached to these starts. Place one `spawner-npc` hostile origin on the crossroads map for the single AI opponent used when nobody else is present.

## Layout components
There are two `map` segments: Lobby, a compact staging room with clear departure space; and Crossroads, an outdoor four-road intersection with long opposite-axis sightlines, central conflict ground, perimeter buildings, sparse cover, and several screened spawn pockets. The lobby does not share a surface with the match map.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the four long firing axes and protected random-spawn siting are authoritative, because accidentally blocking a road or exposing a spawn would undermine sniper play.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by one intersection whose four road ends must remain at long sniper range. The theme is a small modern city street. Use `P4` because lobby and crossroads are genuinely separate maps.
