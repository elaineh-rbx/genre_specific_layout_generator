# Scene prompt
Create a compact 3D endless-wave arena under 100 studs, tight enough that attacking purple and pink balls can reliably reach the player. The floor is light blue with a black techno pattern, enclosed by pink walls marked with black stripes. Place a light-blue upgrade kiosk in one corner, usable directly from the arena floor with no separate interior; leave the main arena open for movement and ball approaches.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Shooter: the core activity is aiming a laser gun at attacking targets through endless combat waves.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded` from outside Shooter’s usual shape list. One compact enclosed arena keeps every enemy within reach and contains the complete score chase. Preset is none because the standard PvE Shooter bundle assumes lane chokepoints and an enterable structure absent from this open room.

## Config requirements
Draw a flat light-blue floor with a black techno pattern, fully enclosed by pink walls with black stripes. Add one light-blue upgrade kiosk in a corner as a small floor-facing fixture, not a room. Show both purple and pink spherical enemies with clear color separation and enough open floor around them to approach the player. Keep the remaining arena free of cover and decorative obstructions.

## Layout requirements
Use `spawner-npc` for hostile ball wave origins, distributed along the wall edges away from the kiosk and aimed into the open arena. Place one unkeyed player spawn near the center with clearance on all sides. Place one unkeyed interaction trigger immediately in front of the corner kiosk. Pink and purple dust bursts are effects attached to enemy destruction, not permanent layout geometry.

## Layout components
The build is one compact arena under 100 studs across. Its main zone is an unobstructed patterned floor, with the single upgrade kiosk occupying one corner and a clear interaction apron before it. Enemy approaches run directly from several perimeter origins toward the central movement area. Flat flooring is the only terrain. Tall striped walls form a complete boundary. Compose the image diagonally so the kiosk, both ball colors, techno floor, and enclosing walls are all visible.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the vivid techno palette and simple spatial composition lead, followed by a derived top-down placement plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, explicitly under 100 studs and tight enough for reliable enemy contact. The theme is bright arcade-techno combat in light blue, pink, purple, and black. The route is `P0`; one flat arena surface has no transition, overhang, or separate map.
