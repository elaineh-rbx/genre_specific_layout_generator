# Scene prompt
Create a realistic 3D wheelie-riding game with ten separate selectable maps: a compact city spanning a few connected urban blocks with streets, intersections, and riding spots; a highway; an industrial area; country roads; dirt tracks; a skatepark; parking lots; mountain roads; a beach boardwalk; and forest trails. Build each map around its intended surface and riding environment, with paved, dirt, trail, or boardwalk routes and open areas suitable for bike stunts. Include varied realistic motorcycles, e-bikes, mountain bikes, BMX bikes, road bikes, and dirt jump bikes, with customizable visible components such as plastics, number plates, wheels, handlebars, seats, tires, and rider helmets and riding gear. Use detailed terrain and dynamic environmental presentation including day or night lighting, weather, rain-wet reflective surfaces, dust and dirt on off-road maps, and ground surfaces that can show tire marks and scrape marks.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation, focused on realistic bike handling, tricks, customization, and free riding. Racing is secondary because time trials and competitive races are also explicit modes; it contributes race-specific placements but does not impose its genre-wide route on every map.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-chaptered`, taken from outside Simulation’s usual shapes to represent exactly ten selectable, genuinely separate maps rather than one surface. The preset is none; no single simulation or racing preset covers this ten-environment free-ride and competition bundle.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for rideable streets, highways, country roads, mountain roads, dirt tracks, boardwalks, and forest trails. Use `physics-rig` for stunt ramps and skatepark assemblies. Use `startpoint-line` and `trigger-finish` only in designated challenge or time-trial areas. Use `settlement-density` for the compact city, `terrain-relief` for mountain and uneven off-road maps, and `water-body` for the sea beside the beach boardwalk. Show varied bikes and customizable visible parts without turning upgrades or scoring into geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly ten map-selection destinations, one per named environment. Within each map, place bike spawns on clear level ground. Use `checkpoint-respawn` at the last safe riding position along challenge routes and after major mountain, dirt, or stunt sections, oriented with travel. Place `startpoint-line` and `trigger-finish` pairs only on authored time-trial or race routes, with corresponding split markers where needed.

## Layout components
Build ten separate maps: compact city blocks; highway; industrial yards; country roads; dirt tracks; skatepark; parking lots; mountain roads; beach boardwalk; and forest trails. Give each distinct surfaces, open trick areas, safe spawn space, and a clear boundary. The city is only a few connected blocks. Preserve broad paved stunt space in parking lots, ramps and bowls in the skatepark, elevation on mountain roads, loose terrain off-road, and sea-facing linear space on the boardwalk.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: each riding route, challenge line, and safe recovery path must be coherent, while the ten-map bundle is not a single racing circuit eligible for `authored_plan`.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The overall scale is huge across ten maps, though each is focused. The theme is high-realism contemporary bike culture with dynamic weather. Use `P4` for separate maps, `P6` for race-route validity, `tiered` for mountain relief, and `CHECK` for the coastal water play envelope.
