## Enriched image prompt
Create two separate soft rustic interior maps with matching aesthetics. The lobby is a warm, dimly lit gathering room with fancy usable seats arranged around open social floor space and a clear group departure point. The round map is a small hotel interior that players can sweep within three minutes: one connected shared space made from a handful of distinct rooms and short hallways, with broad sightlines and several routes that let up to ten players move around and observe one another without becoming isolated. Use dim lamps, soft rustic materials, and refined seating or hotel furnishings throughout, preserving open circulation rather than turning the hotel into a maze.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Party & Casual. The game consists of short social deduction rounds staged for a group, while avatar copying, numbered name tags, voting, points, timers, and the results display are mechanics or UI rather than layout.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-staged`: the lobby and hotel round area are fully separate maps. Match the Minigame preset for its isolated round stage and lobby transport, while omitting its spectator and physical scoring features because no player is eliminated and voting is handled through UI.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for the lobby’s full-group gathering floor with dim lamps and fancy sit-able seats. Use `teleporter-link` for the visible lobby departure marker and matching hotel arrival point. The hotel furnishings and handful of rooms are prompt-specific scene geometry; do not add a maze, leaderboard wall, scoring floor, or spectator stand.

## Layout requirements
Place the paired `teleporter-link` endpoints in the lobby and hotel for whole-group round transport. Place ten `spawn-protected` round-start markers across one connected arrival area in the hotel, supporting the stated player numbers 1 through 10 without stacking avatars. Keep all start markers within mutual circulation rather than isolating players in separate rooms. No scoring trigger is placed because correct guesses are UI votes.

## Layout components
There are two map segments. The lobby is an `interior-single` room with dim lamps, fancy seats, an open gathering area, and a departure point. The hotel is also an `interior-single` building containing a handful of rooms connected by short hallways around a shared circulation core. Give the hotel multiple observation positions but no deep dead ends or maze-like branches. Enclosing walls bound both maps.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the soft rustic atmosphere, seating arrangement, and readable shared hotel interior lead, with no generated topology needed.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use small scale for each compact interior, driven by legible seats, lamps, rooms, and short hallways. The theme is soft rustic hotel elegance with dim warm lighting. Use `P4` for the separate lobby and round maps; no outside-to-inside or procedural-validity modifier is forced.
