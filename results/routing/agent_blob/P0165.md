# Scene prompt
Create a 3D journey map made of small harbor scenes, one per chapter, linked by boat travel. Begin at an Israeli harbor with docks and material-gathering areas for building or upgrading a boat, then move through compact Egyptian and Moroccan port stops with coastal waters and patrol boats along each travel route. End at a United States harbor with a clearly visible immigration or visa checkpoint near the arrival docks.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure, with Survival secondary: this is a chaptered mission journey whose travel legs involve gathering supplies and avoiding patrols.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-chaptered` for four genuinely separate harbor chapters connected by boat travel. The preset is Story; its chapter-transition structure remains, while unrelated cinematic exits and one-way drops are omitted.

## Config requirements
Use `gate-chapter` as clearly marked departure docks between harbor chapters, `gate-progression` as the visible immigration or visa checkpoint at the final United States arrival, and `water-body` for the coastal sea around every port and travel route. The patrol boats are visible hostile vessels associated with `spawner-npc`.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` as material pickup points around the Israeli dock and boat-upgrade work area. Place `spawner-npc` origins for patrol boats along each of the three inter-chapter travel legs, away from the departure and arrival safe margins.

## Layout components
Build four small harbor scenes in order: an Israeli starting port with docks, material areas, and a boat work spot; compact Egyptian and Moroccan stopover ports; and a United States arrival harbor. Connect consecutive chapters conceptually by three coastal boat routes rather than one continuous surface. Each port needs a navigable quay, a clear arrival berth, a departure edge where applicable, and surrounding coastal water. Put the final immigration checkpoint beside the United States arrival docks and frame each chapter around its waterfront.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first as authoritative, then derive each top-down, because the harbor character and readable waterfront composition lead and no computed maze or circuit topology is needed.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large overall but each of the four chapter maps is small. The theme is a grounded modern international coastal journey. Use `P4` for separate chapter maps and `CHECK` for the traversed water volume.
