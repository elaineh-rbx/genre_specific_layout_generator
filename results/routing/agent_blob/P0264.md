# Scene prompt

Create a bright, cartoonish 3D zombie-defense map centered on a shared defensible base, with open approaches around it for incoming zombies and space around the base for friends to build turret defenses.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Strategy: friends build turret defenses around a shared core against endless enemy waves. Zombies provide the theme and threat but do not change the tower-defense classification.

## Shape and preset

Use `terrain-open` from the Base Defense preset. The shared base sits at the center of open contested ground and must be defensible from every direction rather than protected by one hard-coded enemy lane. Add free-placement build ground because the brief explicitly asks friends to construct turrets around the base.

## Config requirements

Draw the visible half of `tracker-core` as a chunky, readable central base whose condition can visually represent the team’s remaining health. Draw `buildzone-plot` as broad, flat buildable land radiating around the base, with clear turret footprints and no fixed lane grid. Keep several open zombie approaches around the outer edge, unobstructed enough for dynamic pathing. Omit destructible terrain and dedicated line-of-sight walls because the prompt does not request them.

## Layout requirements

Place the functional half of `tracker-core` at the exact center of the defensible base. Place `spawner-npc` wave origins around the map perimeter, distributed across all major approach directions and outside the central turret-building ground, so endless zombie waves pressure the base from multiple sides. No final winner zone is needed because the session ends when the base falls.

## Layout components

Build one central fortified base surrounded by a wide annulus of flat turret-building ground. Beyond it, create multiple open approach sectors leading inward from the bounded perimeter, separated by bright cartoon ruins, low hills, fences, or stylized debris that never seals a route. Keep a clear circulation ring for cooperating players around the base and reserve generous footprints for constructed defenses. Use playful barricades, oversized warning signs, colorful supply crates, and simplified zombie-apocalypse dressing. Enclose the battlefield with a readable perimeter boundary and compose the base as the central focal point.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render the authoritative bright defense composition first, then derive the top-down plan because enemies path dynamically across open terrain rather than requiring a computed track.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is medium, driven by a shared base, surrounding build area, and several approach sectors. The theme is bright, approachable cartoon zombie defense. Use `P0`; the battlefield is one continuous surface with no overhangs or separate maps.
