# Scene prompt
Create a 3D story-game map set in a focused forest at night. A main woodland clearing serves as the gathering area, with scattered natural resources and space for a campfire and a simple shelter. From the clearing, a clearly marked chase route winds through dense trees toward a city reached as a continuous transition. The route opens into city streets filled with hundreds of silent pedestrians, ending in an urban area that contrasts with the dark forest.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Survival, with Adventure secondary. A hostile guide and forced chase define the play, while the directed forest-to-city sequence carries the story.

## Shape and preset
Use `route-guided`, taken from outside Survival’s usual set, for one continuous route from clearing through forest to city. The preset is Escape with its looping-warren shape replaced by the explicitly directed chase route; keep its escape threshold, sightline-breaking cover, and enterable shelter.

## Config requirements
Draw `buildzone-plot` as one open clearing where a shelter and campfire can be assembled, `cover-los` as dense tree trunks and undergrowth around the chase, and `building-interior` as the simple shelter entered before the nighttime reveal. Draw `gate-escape` as the forest edge opening onto city streets. Add Adventure’s `path-trail` as a winding but unmistakable route marked by arrows. Use `settlement-density` for the contrasting dense urban endpoint and `npc-population` for hundreds of silent pedestrians occupying its streets.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` as scattered natural resources around the clearing, close enough to gather before the storm. Place one `spawner-npc` for Mat’s reveal in the shelter corner and a chase activation immediately outside. Place arrow guidance at every ambiguous bend along the trail. Place the functional `gate-escape` trigger where the forest route opens into the city. Place ambient `npc-population` anchors throughout sidewalks and street crossings, preserving the author’s “hundreds” wording without inventing an exact count.

## Layout components
Build exactly one main clearing with one campfire site and one simple shelter. From it, run one focused chase trail through dense nighttime forest to one continuous city transition. Trees and brush enclose the route without creating misleading branches. The city endpoint contains broad streets, sidewalks, building facades, and hundreds of silent pedestrians, sharply contrasting with the forest. Natural barriers close the forest boundary.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish the clearing, single connected chase route, and city opening first, because a broken or ambiguous route would make the chase unplayable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, balancing a compact clearing, chase corridor, and urban endpoint. The theme is storm-dark story horror shifting into uncanny city normality. Use `P3` for entering and leaving the shelter; do not use `P4`, because forest and city are continuous.
