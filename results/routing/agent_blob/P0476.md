## Enriched image prompt
Create one continuous story map beginning at a focused forest clearing and ending in a city. In the clearing, provide open ground for gathering resources, building a campfire, and raising a shelter. From beside the shelter, lead one clearly marked arrow route through dense nighttime forest, with a direct followable path that opens continuously into a populated city at the far end rather than loading a separate map. Make the city entrance broad enough for a crowd of hundreds of unresponsive pedestrians while keeping the forest route visually connected behind it. Use storm-darkened trees, wet ground, and an increasingly creepy nighttime mood, with the clearing, chase arrows, route, and city transition all readable.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure, with Survival secondary because resource gathering, shelter building, a storm, and a pursuing threat support the linear story journey.

## Shape and preset
Use `route-guided`, the Adventure flavor Guided Corridor, as one continuous directed route from clearing through forest to city. The matching preset is Guided Trail, retaining its trail and distant city landmark while omitting an enclosed reveal exit because the transition remains outdoors.

## Config requirements
Use `path-trail` for the arrow-marked forest chase route; `landmark-focal` for the city skyline pulling the route forward; `buildzone-plot` from Survival for the clearing's campfire-and-shelter ground; `settlement-density` for the city at the route end; and `npc-population` for Mat in the clearing and the hundreds of pedestrians occupying the city.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` as wood and other shelter resources around the main clearing. Place the layout half of `npc-population` with one guide position in the clearing and crowd occupancy for hundreds of pedestrians in the city. Place `spawner-npc` at the shelter-side start of the chase for Mat's hostile story state, facing down the arrow route.

## Layout components
The build contains one gathering clearing, one campfire/shelter build plot, resource sites, one guide position, one chase origin, one arrow-marked forest route, dense trees forming the route boundary, one continuous city entrance, a compact built-up city zone, and crowd ground for hundreds of people. The path has no branch that can bypass or lose the city transition.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish the uninterrupted clearing-to-city chase route first, then dress it in the authoritative nighttime storm atmosphere.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is large, preserving the resource clearing, focused chase route, and city entrance in one continuous frame. The theme is storm-darkened storybook forest horror transitioning into an uncanny city. The pipeline remains `P0`, since every zone shares one outdoor surface and no separate map, interior, or overhang is required.
