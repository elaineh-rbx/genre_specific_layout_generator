Use `cover-los` for cliff folds, desert formations, and town corners that interrupt seeker sightlines; `terrain-relief` for the cliff steps and desert dunes; and `settlement-density` for the compact town's built-up facades and streets. The flat, readable color-matching surfaces are a direct scene-specific visual requirement.

## Layout requirements
Use `spawn-protected` for separated round-start SpawnZones in every map, with hider starts distributed across concealment areas and the seeker start isolated from immediate sightlines. No scoring volume is needed; discovery and the timer are mechanics.

## Layout components
Create three map segments: Cliffs, a tiered `arena-tiered` zone of colored rock faces; Desert, a bounded `space-bounded` zone of dunes and wall-like formations; and Town, a fixed `settlement-static` zone of streets and facades. Each is a separate small map with its own enclosing boundary and connected search circulation.

## Render order
Use `topdown` because each map needs connected, searchable circulation and fair sightline breaks before its color treatment is dressed.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use small scale for each map so wall colors and pose-sized hiding surfaces remain legible. The theme is flat toon-bright cliffs, desert, and town. `P4` is required for three separate maps, and `tiered` is required for the stepped cliff relief.
