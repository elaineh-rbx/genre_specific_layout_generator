# Scene prompt

Create a colorful, fun multiplayer survival game layout with a safe central lobby that connects to three separate random survival maps. Each map is a compact, easy-to-navigate arena that players can cross in about half a minute, with open areas and clear routes suitable for disaster survival and monster encounters. Leave space in the lobby for a shop area and future map or update portals.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Survival, combining disaster survival and hostile-monster encounters within the same round-based structure.

## Shape and preset

Use `hub-portals`, taken from outside Survival’s usual shapes, because the deliverable is a lobby that dispatches players to three genuinely separate arenas. No single preset fits the combined lobby, random-disaster, and monster-map bundle, so the preset is none.

## Config requirements

Draw one safe colorful lobby with a shop alcove, three active destination portals, and reserved wall or floor bays for future portals. Draw `hazard-kill` as broad, readable disaster-prone arena ground rather than one permanently active threat. Keep each of the three survival maps open and compact, with simple cover and multiple clear routes.

## Layout requirements

Place three scene-specific teleporter pairs, each linking one lobby portal to the start area of one survival map. Place `spawner-npc` origins at readable arena edges for dangerous monsters, away from player arrivals. Place coin pickups across safe-to-risky routes and checkpoints at each arena arrival; these are scene-specific placements without canonical IDs. Put the survivors’ victory-reward marker back in the lobby near the shop rather than inside a hazard zone.

## Layout components

Build four zones total: one central lobby and exactly three separate compact survival arenas. The lobby has a protected gathering floor, one shop area, three current portals, and expansion room for later updates. Each arena should be crossable in about thirty seconds, bounded clearly, and composed from an open center plus branching escape routes and edge cover. The maps must not share visible terrain or physical corridors with the lobby or one another. Use bright, playful materials and readable silhouettes throughout.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first as authoritative, then derive isometric views, because portal pairing, compact crossings, and multiple escape routes must remain unambiguous.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is large overall but small per arena, driven by four separately rendered zones. The theme is colorful, playful chaos. Route `P4` because the lobby and three survival arenas are separate maps; no additional modifier is forced.
