# Scene prompt

Arrange dense streets, building interiors, rooftops, alleys, and hidden loot locations across the urban district, leading into the industrial yard with warehouses, loading areas, and usable cover. Scatter valuable loot crates through exposed and concealed locations. Place several fixed extraction points around the map perimeter, each approached through distinct routes, with open combat sightlines and sheltered paths supporting both AI and player encounters.
