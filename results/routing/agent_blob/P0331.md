# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map showing a cosy suburban town with a pre-built set of claimable houses whose interiors can be decorated. Arrange many stores around the town’s central shopping area, with seasonal Christmas, Halloween, Easter, and St. Patrick’s Day decorations focused on store displays and pet-themed areas rather than changing the permanent buildings.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim: pet adoption and trading share the town with claimable, decoratable homes.

## Shape and preset
Use `settlement-claimable` as one map containing finished suburban houses that players claim and personalize internally. The matching preset is Pet Care, expanded with the explicitly requested shopping district.

## Config requirements
Draw `social-hub` as the central pet-oriented shopping square. Use `path-street` for walkable suburban streets connecting stores and homes. Include `district-zoned` to separate the busy commercial center from quieter residential blocks. Use `building-interior` for both decoratable claimed homes and stores players enter. Apply `settlement-density` as a cosy built-up town with many storefronts. Show `npc-population` as adoptable and tradable pets in designated pet areas. Keep permanent architecture suburban while displays use exactly four seasonal motifs: Christmas, Halloween, Easter, and St. Patrick’s Day.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `npc-population` in pet-adoption and trading areas around the shopping center, not across residential roads. Place one claim marker at every house entrance and one decoration anchor set inside each house. The source’s “over 1000 pets” describes the collectible catalog, not 1000 simultaneous map occupants, so it must not become a rendered population count.

## Layout components
The town has a central shopping area lined with many stores, surrounded by connected residential streets and a pre-built set of claimable houses. Pedestrian streets link every block. Interiors are required for stores and homes. Use modest lawns, sidewalks, warm street furniture, and seasonal display clusters concentrated at storefronts and pet areas. A landscaped suburban edge encloses the single map. Compose the shopping square as the focal center with residential blocks stepping outward.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: establish the cosy town and seasonal display language first, then derive the straightforward street plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a large scale band because one map must hold many shops and a residential district. The theme is cosy suburban pet life with four festive display styles. Apply `P3` for exterior-to-store and exterior-to-home transitions.
