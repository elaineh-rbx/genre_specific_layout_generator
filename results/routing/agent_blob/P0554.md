# Scene prompt

Gentle rolling waves form a clear, readable surface for diving, launching, and skimming; white spray, foam trails, and splashes appear along the waterline. Frame the dolphin near the center with open ocean in the foreground and midground, a bright colorful sky above, and an unobstructed horizon in the distance; recycle the visible ocean as the camera follows.
