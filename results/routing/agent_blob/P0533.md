# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] shape — Q: Is the dungeon a separate instance you load into, or a cave on the same map? That choice roughly doubles the build cost. A: Make it a cave on the same map, entered directly from the wilderness; keep the slice compact and seamless.
- [author] scale — Q: How long should crossing the wilderness take on foot, given you want map size traded away for finished systems? A: About 2–3 minutes on foot from the village edge to the far side, with meaningful points of interest along the way.
- [author] goal — Q: Does the vertical slice end when the Shattering intro storyline resolves, or when the dungeon is cleared? A: The vertical slice ends when the dungeon is cleared; that clear provides the introductory Shattering story beat, not a full resolution.
- [agent_inferred] creature habitats — Q: How should six original Wildkin species affect the map? A: Reserve exactly six compact habitat or nest areas across the wilderness and cave, one spatial home per species, without inventing the creature designs.

## Enriched image prompt
Create an original compact fantasy adventure-RPG map with one central village, one large but focused wilderness, and one small cave dungeon entered directly from the wilderness on the same seamless map. Arrange a clear 2–3 minute walk from the village edge to the far side, passing meaningful points of interest and exactly six compact habitat or nest areas for six distinct original creature species. Cluster egg-hatching and crafting spaces in the village, place visible egg nests and gatherable resource sites through the wilderness, and lead the terrain naturally to the cave mouth. Build the cave as a short sequence of underground chambers ending in one final encounter room that concludes the slice. Use subtle signs of magical corruption across the route while keeping the world original, polished, and free of copied creatures, locations, interfaces, characters, or story assets.

## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG, with Adventure secondary because exploration and the introductory journey through wilderness into a dungeon are central.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-underground`, Zone (Surface and Underground Layers), from outside RPG's usual shape list because one seamless surface world descends into a cave beneath it. The matching preset is Action RPG, with its separate hub-and-dungeon shape replaced by the author's same-map cave while retaining village services and encounter grounds.

## Config requirements
Use `safezone-town` for the central village, `social-hub` for its compact egg-hatching and crafting cluster, `hazard-aggro` for wilderness and dungeon encounter grounds, and the drawn form of `collectible-nodes` for egg nests and crafting-resource sites. The cave mouth, underground chamber sequence, six habitat areas, final encounter room, and magical-corruption dressing are scene-specific image geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly six `spawner-npc` Spawners, one against each species habitat or nest area across the wilderness and cave. Place `collectible-nodes` Collectibles at visible egg nests and resource sites; the prompt gives no exact egg or material count. Place one free-text TriggerZone in the final cave encounter room to register the dungeon clear. Battle rules, hatching, party management, crafting, riding, saving, and controls are mechanics.

## Layout components
The top-level `world-underground` container has three zones on one map. Village uses `settlement-static` as a central safe hub with crafting and hatching spaces. Wilderness uses `world-open` as a 2–3 minute explorable region with points of interest and six distributed habitat nodes. Cave Dungeon uses `rooms-sequence` as a small underground chamber chain ending in a final encounter room. A wilderness trail and cave mouth connect all three without loading or portals.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: village circulation, the wilderness crossing, six habitat sites, cave entrance, chamber chain, and final room must connect coherently before fantasy isometric dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is large because a 2–3 minute wilderness crossing approaches district scale, while the map still preserves village, nest, and cave-room detail. The theme is original creature-rider fantasy with subtle magical corruption. Modifier `P2` is required because the cave lies beneath the surface, and modifier `P3` is required for the seamless outside-to-cave transition. No `P4` applies because the author explicitly requires one map.
