# Scene prompt
Create a compact, seamless 3D fantasy creature-collecting adventure map with a central village opening into one large wilderness region. The wilderness takes roughly 2–3 minutes to cross on foot from the village edge to its far side, with clear paths and meaningful points of interest along the way, habitats for six original Wildkin species, and places to find creature eggs and crafting materials. At the far side, a small cave dungeon is entered directly from the wilderness rather than loaded as a separate area; its interior forms the final location of the introductory Shattering story beat.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG, with Adventure secondary: creature party growth, combat, crafting, and equipment drive progression through an explorable story route.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open` from the Turn-based RPG preset: village, wilderness, and cave coexist on one compact seamless map. Keep the safe town and hostile placements, while expressing progression through the far-side cave rather than a separate instance.

## Config requirements
`safezone-town` makes the central village a sanctuary isolated from hostile Wildkin. `path-trail` threads a clear 2–3-minute route from the village edge through wilderness points of interest to the cave. `hazard-aggro` forms six distinct habitat clearings for the six original Wildkin species. `collectible-nodes` draws egg nests and crafting-material patches in and around those habitats. `landmark-focal` makes the corrupted cave mouth a distant navigation target. `building-interior` supplies the directly entered cave interior. `npc-population` draws village residents and story figures.

## Layout requirements
Place `npc-population` markers around the village square and route entrance. Place at least six `spawner-npc` clusters, one within each species habitat and set back from the main path. Place `collectible-nodes` for eggs at sheltered nest sites and crafting materials beside readable natural resources. Place one final story-completion trigger in the deepest cave chamber.

## Layout components
Build one central village with a square, clustered civic and crafting spaces, protected edge, and a clear wilderness gate. Beyond it, one large wilderness region takes 2–3 minutes to cross on foot, with a primary trail, six visually distinct habitat clearings, egg nests, material sites, scenic landmarks, and small optional detours. At the far side, a prominent cave mouth transitions directly into a compact sequence of corrupted chambers ending at the Shattering story location. Natural terrain encloses the map without breaking its seamless surface. Frame the village centrally, the wilderness spreading outward, and the cave as the far compositional anchor.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the lush seamless world composition leads, then the top-down plan is projected from it while preserving the village-to-cave trail.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by a 2–3-minute wilderness crossing plus a small cave. The theme is original painterly fantasy wilderness with creature habitats and magical corruption. `P3` is required for the direct outside-to-cave-interior transition.
