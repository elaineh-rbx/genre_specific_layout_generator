# Scene prompt
Create a small, crossable post-apocalyptic wilderness map, overgrown with vegetation and scattered abandoned remnants. Include natural areas for fishing and hunting, open ground and resource areas for building shelters from wood planks and metal, with zombie-filled approaches throughout the wilderness.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Survival, because gathering, shelter construction, and evasion of zombies define the play loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded` as one compact, clearly bounded wilderness that keeps resources and zombies within walking distance. Start from the Resource Survival preset, but substitute its broad biome-world shape with this bounded map because the brief explicitly asks for one small crossable wilderness; retain the preset’s resource and base-building options.

## Config requirements
Draw `buildzone-plot` as several clear patches of open, defensible ground where plank-and-metal shelters can be raised. Show `water-body` as a fishable pond or short river reach, without layered or self-occluding water. Use the visible side of `npc-population` for wildlife habitat around the hunting area. Add overgrown abandoned remnants—collapsed walls, rusted vehicles, and ruined utility structures—scattered through the wilderness as scene-specific geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` as gatherable wood and metal sources near forest edges and abandoned remnants, distributed so each shelter plot has reachable supplies. Place `spawner-npc` origins for zombies along multiple approaches around the buildable clearings, never directly inside a shelter plot. Place the functional side of `npc-population` as huntable wildlife origins in the natural hunting ground, kept apart from the densest zombie approaches.

## Layout components
The map has a central cluster of shelter-building clearings, an overgrown hunting woodland on one side, and a fishable water feature on the other. Short dirt paths connect these zones through ruined roadside remnants and resource pockets. Use mostly low, uneven wilderness ground with dense vegetation but preserve readable sightlines around build plots. Enclose the small map with impassable tree mass, rubble, and steep natural edges. Frame the central clearings as the compositional anchor, with water and woodland readable behind them.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down plan; the compact open topology does not require a computed plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a small scale, driven by the explicit one-map walking distance. The theme is overgrown post-apocalyptic wilderness with decayed industrial remnants. Route with `CHECK` because the fishable water is a volumetric feature, though its simple unlayered form should not self-occlude.
