# Scene prompt
Create a compact 10-stage 3D obby tower, a vertical course of short, distinct obstacle sections, with a candy-coloured stylized look, bright platforms, and simple playful obstacles.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Obby & Platformer, canonically `obby-platformer`, because the build is a player-controlled vertical obstacle course.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-tower` for one compact ascent with platforms stacked over earlier stages. Tower Obby is the matching preset, with its timed-obstacle option omitted because the brief only commits to simple playful obstacle sections.

## Config requirements
Draw a continuous, physics-valid `path-track` divided into exactly ten short stages. Use `obstacle-jump` for bright stepping platforms and wrap-around jumps, `obstacle-moving` for a small number of readable rotating or sliding pieces, and `obstacle-climb` for ladders, trusses, and short vertical scrambles. Draw the summit payoff platform as `winner-zone`. Keep every obstacle chunky, legible, and playful rather than precision-hard.

## Layout requirements
Place one `winner-zone` at the end of stage ten on a broad summit landing. No checkpoints are required for this compact course, and no other invisible markers or pickups were requested.

## Layout components
Build one ten-stage tower around a narrow central footprint. Start with a generous entry pad, then arrange ten visually distinct obstacle sections in a clear upward sequence, each ending on a small safe landing before the next begins. Alternate jump, moving, and climb sections so adjacent stages do not repeat the same silhouette. Use bright candy-colored platforms, rounded rails, striped accents, and toy-like geometric obstacles. Keep the sides open so the complete climb reads in frame, with an open void beyond the course and a strong celebratory summit cap. The composition should show the base, the zigzagging vertical route, and stage ten at the top without hiding lower surfaces.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: valid stage order and legal gaps must be established first, then the isometric image dresses that playable plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by exactly ten short stages. The theme is candy-colored, stylized Roblox play equipment. `P6` guarantees a valid climbing route, and `P2` handles tower platforms that overhang lower stages.
