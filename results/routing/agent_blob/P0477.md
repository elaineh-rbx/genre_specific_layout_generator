# Scene prompt
Create a 3D comic pirate Caribbean island adventure map in a playful style, with a small set of three riddle locations arranged across the island.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Puzzle is dominant, with Adventure secondary: solving three spoken riddles is the activity, while crossing a pirate island supplies the exploratory journey.

## Shape and preset
Use `puzzle-open`, an open-air island puzzle space whose three riddle stops can be visited across one contiguous landmass. The closest preset is Word / Quiz Puzzle. Keep its open shape and `gate-solve`, but drop its visible clue-facade assumption because the answers appear through dialogue choices rather than writing on a wall.

## Config requirements
Use `gate-solve` to give each riddle location a visibly distinct pirate-themed point of progress, such as a carved idol, lookout shrine, or locked ruin marker. Use `water-body` for the Caribbean sea enclosing the island. The dialogue choices themselves are UI and must not be drawn into the environment.

## Layout requirements
Place three uncatalogued dialogue interaction trigger volumes, exactly one at each riddle landmark and aligned to its approach side. Each trigger starts the corresponding spoken riddle; answer verification remains outside layout. No physical `trigger-solve` receptacle or `button-solve` input is needed.

## Layout components
Build one medium tropical island bounded on every side by bright Caribbean water. Arrange exactly three clearly separated riddle zones across the island, connected by a readable walkable trail: a beachside pirate marker near the arrival edge, a jungle landmark inland, and a small ruined lookout on the far side. Keep each stop open enough for a player to approach and read dialogue without crowding. Compose the island so all three landmarks form a loose triangular journey while remaining visually distinct in the playful comic-pirate style.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down plan; the requested topology is simple and does not require a computed solution.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale, driven by three spaced destinations on one island. The theme is colorful comic Caribbean piracy. Route with `CHECK` because the surrounding water creates a volumetric play envelope that should be checked for self-occlusion; no other modifier is forced.
