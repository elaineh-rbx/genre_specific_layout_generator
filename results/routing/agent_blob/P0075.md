# Scene prompt
Create a 3D playground-sized candy land map for a small hide-and-seek game, with enough themed hiding spots throughout the single compact area.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Party & Casual because the map hosts timed hide-and-seek rounds with one player selected as seeker.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded` from the Childhood Game preset. The single compact candy playground is the complete round space; no separate lobby or isolated stage was requested. Keep the hiding-space concept but omit the preset’s separate social hub and full maze.

## Config requirements
Apply `cover-los` as dense but readable clusters of oversized candy props that break sightlines and create hiding spots: lollipops, gumdrop mounds, candy-cane arches, chocolate blocks, and frosting shrubs. Maintain open circulation between clusters so the seeker can inspect every hiding area.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` as evenly distributed round-start markers around the playground, with one designated seeker start separated from the hider starts to prevent immediate discovery. Place `trigger-scoring` as tag-detection regions used when the seeker reaches a hiding player. Keep every marker on accessible ground and outside enclosed prop geometry. The number of players and therefore the exact spawn count is unspecified, so size the marker set for a small match without asserting a fixed quantity.

## Layout components
Build one playground-sized, single-level candy land zone. Arrange several themed hiding clusters around an open central circulation area, joining them with winding frosting or cookie paths. Use oversized lollipops, gumdrops, candy canes, chocolate slabs, and frosting-like bushes as cover, with gaps and loops that prevent dead ends from becoming traps. Enclose the compact play space with a continuous candy-themed boundary such as a low wafer wall or frosting ridge. Frame the whole arena clearly in one view while preserving visual occlusion at player height.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative because the candy styling and readable concealment clusters define the map, while no maze topology or structurally fragile route needs procedural planning.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, fixed by one playground-sized area for a small match. The theme is bright, whimsical candy fantasy. Use `P0`; this is one visible ground surface with no overhangs, indoor transitions, separate maps, or volumetric traversal.
