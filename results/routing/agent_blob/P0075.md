Arrange clusters of oversized candy-shaped props around the area to form plentiful hiding pockets and break sightlines while preserving clear paths between clusters. Include a simple gathering patch at one edge for players before and between rounds, integrated into the same continuous play space rather than separated as a lobby. Keep the entire colorful candy environment readable in one elevated view.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Party & Casual (`party-casual`), specifically a childhood hide-and-seek game.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded`, with the gathering area and hide-and-seek ground sharing one continuous playground-sized space. Use the Childhood Game preset, but drop `obstacle-maze` because the author asks for themed hiding spots rather than a maze-like warren.

## Config requirements
Use `cover-los` for dense clusters of oversized candy props that create hiding pockets and interrupted views. Use `social-hub` for the small gathering patch inside the same area. No maze, spectator enclosure, or separate stage is requested.

## Layout requirements
Use `spawn-protected` for round starts: place one seeker start at the gathering-side edge behind a sightline blocker and distribute hider starts among the candy clusters, with no start visible directly from the seeker position. The timed-round rule itself adds no fixed geometry.

## Layout components
The map has one bounded candy playground, one integrated gathering patch, several candy-prop hiding clusters, and clear walkable links between them. The clusters ring and subdivide the central play ground without becoming a formal maze. A simple candy-themed perimeter encloses the small map, and the composition keeps all major hiding areas visible from an elevated frame.

## Render order
