# Scene prompt
Create a 3D single-player/co-op friendly dragon platformer world built as a central hub connected by glowing portals to five compact, district-sized themed realm maps: a sunny grassy realm, molten lava caverns, cool blue ice cliffs, a purple spooky night world, and a sky-and-cloud realm. Each hand-composed realm is an open-ish 3D playground with branching routes, hidden alcoves, elevated glide-accessible secrets, charge ramps, breakable gates or chests, scattered gems, rare eggs, captives to free, enemies, and a return portal home stone. Use rounded stylized geometry, warm saturated storybook materials, painterly sky backdrops, atmospheric depth, and strong focal lighting on portals, treasure, and captive creatures. Compose each district with clear foreground traversal space, midground paths and landmarks, and scenic background terrain; use distinct palettes for meadow, orange volcanic cave, icy blue cliffs, haunted purple night, and bright clouds. Include a small dragon character with a nearby dragonfly-style companion, along with chargeable and flammable environmental targets.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Obby & Platformer, matching the self-declared 3D platformer and movement challenges. Adventure is secondary because exploration, secrets, and collectibles shape each realm.

## Shape and preset
Use `hub-portals`, outside the dominant genre’s usual set, for one central hub feeding five separate realm maps. Match the Adventure Exploration preset with this shape substituted; its landmarks, trails, and hidden alcoves remain central.

## Config requirements
Apply `path-trail` to branching traversable routes, `landmark-focal` to each realm’s strongly lit destination, `alcove-secret` to elevated glide-accessible hiding places, and `obstacle-jump` to charge ramps and platform gaps. Draw both ends of `teleporter-link` as five glowing hub portals and five return stones. Apply `hazard-kill` to lava and fatal chasms, `terrain-relief` to cliffs and vertical landforms, and `island-cluster` to separated cloud platforms.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` for scattered gems, rare eggs, and captives across safe routes, skill-gated branches, chests, and hidden alcoves in all five realms. Place the functional endpoints of `teleporter-link` at every hub portal and its matching return stone. Site enemy entry markers near combat clearings without blocking traversal.

## Layout components
Build one compact hub and exactly five district-sized maps: sunny grassland, molten caverns, icy cliffs, spooky purple night, and bright cloud realm. Each needs branching ground routes, elevated glide secrets, charge ramps, breakable gates or chests, a focal landmark, and a return stone. Caverns may overhang; cloud platforms occupy open air. Keep the small dragon and companion readable in foreground compositions.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: portal pairing, branching route connectivity, and physics-legal platform access must be valid before storybook dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge from six spaces total. The theme is saturated rounded storybook fantasy with realm-specific palettes. Use genre-wide `P6`, `P4` for separate portal maps, `P2` for cavern overhangs, `tiered` for cliffs, and `CHECK` for volumetric gliding among clouds.
