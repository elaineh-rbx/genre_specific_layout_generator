## Enriched image prompt

Make each level an open-ish hand-composed playground with branching paths, rounded stylized terrain, hidden alcoves, vertical secrets, glide gaps, charge ramps, breakable gates and chests, enemy encounter ground, and a return-portal home stone. Use focal lighting on portals, chests, gems, and captured creatures. Scatter gems across obvious and risky routes, hide rare eggs behind movement challenges, and place creatures to free at milestone sites. Maintain warm saturated palettes, clean silhouettes, painterly skies, atmospheric depth, and distinct realm moods while keeping every traversal line readable.

## Genre

Adventure, canonical slug `adventure`, is secondary because exploration, portals, collectibles, and secrets structure the journey.

## Shape and preset

The matched secondary-genre preset is Quest Hub, with `hub-portals` substituted for its one-world shape and with no physical quest board added.

## Config requirements
Use `teleporter-link` as separate-map portals from the hub to every realm and a return stone in every level. Use `gate-progression` for creature-gated world thresholds, `path-trail` for branching walkable routes, `alcove-secret` for hidden gems and eggs, `landmark-focal` for portal and collectible landmarks, `obstacle-jump` for glide gaps and vertical platforming, `path-shortcut` for optional charge/glide mastery routes, `hazard-kill` for lava and chasm danger, `terrain-relief` for cliffs and vertical secrets, and `volume-open` for the traversable sky-and-cloud airspace.

## Layout requirements
Place twelve `teleporter-link` Teleporters as six paired links: five hub-to-level portal pairs plus return behavior represented by each level's paired home stone. Place `collectible-nodes` Collectibles as gems throughout normal and risky paths, captured creatures at milestone sites, and rare eggs in hidden challenge alcoves. Place `checkpoint-respawn` Checkpoints at safe landings before major glide, charge, and vertical challenges. Place `spawner-npc` Spawners on dedicated enemy and boss encounter ground, never on arrival pads.

## Layout components
The build has six map segments. The central Hub is a `hub-portals` map with five clearly distinguished realm portals. Grassy Realm is a `world-open` map of sunny branching terrain; Lava Caverns is an `interior-single` cavern playground; Ice Cliffs and Spooky Night World are separate `world-open` maps with vertical secrets; Sky/Cloud Realm is `volume-open-air` with discrete landing surfaces and glide routes. Every level has branching paths, hidden alcoves, milestone collectible sites, enemy ground, and one return stone.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: draw each hand-composed level plan first, then dress it isometrically. The top-down plans are authoritative because branching connectivity, reachable return portals, and physics-legal platforming must survive, while `authored_plan` is unavailable for this non-maze, non-racing structure.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is large, matching the confirmed compact district size for each of six separate maps. The theme is bright, saturated, rounded storybook fantasy with five distinct realm palettes. Use `P4` for the hub plus five separately loaded levels, `P6` from the dominant platformer genre for valid traversal, `tiered` for cliff and vertical relief, and `CHECK` for the sky realm's traversed open-air volume.
