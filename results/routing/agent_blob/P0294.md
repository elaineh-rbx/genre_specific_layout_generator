# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map showing a compact 3-by-3-block vertical cyberpunk concrete metropolis at night, designed as a physics-driven superpower playground with no NPC areas or combat-objective spaces. Dense skyscrapers rise around street canyons, with accessible wet, highly reflective rooftops at varied heights and neon grind pipes spanning between buildings. Streets below contain physics-active traffic cars and loose street elements, while glowing neon billboards cover building faces; heavy rain falls throughout the city.

# Agent decision

## Genre
Simulation is the sole genre because manipulating momentum, gravity, friction, vehicles, and loose environment pieces is the central sandbox loop. The rooftop movement supports that physics play but does not establish a scored or terminating obby.

## Shape and preset
Use `traversal-city`, outside Simulation’s usual shapes: the 3-by-3 metropolis is built to be crossed over roofs, walls, ledges, and pipes, with streets remaining a lower fallback layer. The preset is Physics Sim, replacing its open-world shape while retaining the physics contraptions and circulation; omit its environmental hazard because none was requested.

## Config requirements
Use `physics-rig` for unanchored traffic cars, loose street elements, and breakable or launchable urban props. Use `path-circulation` for connected street, rooftop, wall-running, and grind-pipe movement routes through the nine blocks. Dense concrete skyscrapers, reflective wet rooftops at varied heights, neon billboards, heavy rain, street canyons, and inter-building neon pipes are explicit scene geometry. Add no NPC population, enemy spawners, combat objectives, or lethal regions.

## Layout requirements
After segmentation, assign uncatalogued physics-active properties to every authored traffic car and loose street element so powers can steal, reverse, or impart velocity; keep structural buildings, streets, and rooftop surfaces anchored. Mark each intended neon connector as grindable and align its usable path between safe roof-edge access points. No exact car, prop, or pipe count was supplied, so do not invent one.

## Layout components
Lay out exactly nine blocks in a compact 3-by-3 street grid. Dense skyscrapers form deep street canyons, with accessible wet roofs stepping through varied elevations. Neon grind pipes span selected gaps between neighboring buildings, while suitable walls and ledges support vertical movement. Physics-active cars and loose urban elements populate the streets without blocking all circulation. Neon billboards cover facade focal zones, and rain reads across reflective concrete and rooftop surfaces. Dense towers hide the outer map edge. Compose the view to reveal the nine-block grid, low streets, several rooftop tiers, and cross-building pipe links together.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, because the vertical cyberpunk atmosphere, reflective surfaces, and readable layering lead this non-terminating sandbox; the circulation plan can be derived from that composition.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, fixed by the compact 3-by-3 block footprint but amplified vertically. The theme is rain-soaked concrete cyberpunk at night with saturated neon. Use `P2` because rooftops, facade routes, and grind pipes overhang the streets below.
