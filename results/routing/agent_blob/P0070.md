# Scene prompt
Create a static, table-sized 3D hex-board game map viewed by a fixed angled top-down camera. Arrange a standard 19-tile hexagonal island in five staggered rows of 3, 4, 5, 4, and 3 terrain hexes, with forest, brick-clay, pasture, wheat field, ore, and one central or near-central desert tile; each producing hex has a circular numbered token, with 6 and 8 tokens red. Place a dark robber pawn on the desert at the start. Surround the board edges with 54 settlement/city vertex positions and 72 road slots, visibly integrated along tile corners and borders. Show player-colored settlements, cities, and road bars when built, plus a pair of 3D dice beside the board. Keep the board unobstructed in the middle of the screen, with space around it for a top turn-and-roll HUD and bottom resource/action controls.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Strategy because this is explicitly a turn-based resource-management board game controlled from a fixed camera.

## Shape and preset
Use `board-grid` for a table-sized hex board that users act on rather than traverse. Match the Board & Card Games preset, adapting its regular playing spaces to the specified hex graph.

## Config requirements
Apply `tile-grid` as exactly 19 terrain hexes in staggered rows of 3, 4, 5, 4, and 3. Draw 4 forest, 3 brick-clay, 4 pasture, 4 wheat, 3 ore, and 1 central or near-central desert. Give all 18 producing tiles circular number tokens; color 6 and 8 red and all others black. Include one dark robber pawn, player-color wooden settlements, cities, and road bars, and exactly two 3D dice beside the island.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly 54 unique settlement/city vertex sockets at hex corners and exactly 72 unique road slots along valid borders, deduplicating shared geometry and preserving the precomputed adjacency graph. Place the robber on the single desert tile initially. Associate each number token with its producing hex. Reserve structure sockets for dynamic player-colored builds; do not prefill them as permanent scenery.

## Layout components
Build one table-sized island board centered in the camera-safe middle area. Its 19 equal hexes form the exact five-row silhouette with perfectly aligned shared edges. Place the desert centrally or near-centrally and distribute the five producing terrain families in a readable mix. Number tokens sit at tile centers without hiding terrain identity. Vertex sockets and edge slots remain visually integrated but selectable. Put the two dice just outside the island footprint on the tabletop. Leave generous neutral margin above for turn and roll information and below for resources and actions. Compose the plan symmetrically and keep every tile, token, edge, and corner unobstructed.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, then dress the isometric view from that plan, because the exact 19-tile arrangement and 54-vertex/72-edge graph must be spatially valid.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small and table-sized. The theme is polished colorful resource-board terrain with wooden pieces. Use `P0` with `SET`: nobody walks on the geometry, so traversal and jump-gap checks are skipped.
