# Scene prompt
Create a 3D neon club layout with a small lobby sized for about 10 players, arranged as a social gathering space for approaching other dancers. Connect the lobby to separate instanced dance-battle arenas, with each arena as a dedicated performance space for two dancers and phonk music.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Party & Casual because players challenge one another to short scored battles. Entertainment is secondary because each match is a music-driven dance performance in a club venue.

## Shape and preset
Use `hub-portals`, the Portal Hub, taken from outside Party & Casual’s usual set because one social lobby dispatches accepted pairs into separate instanced arenas and several battles may run concurrently. Use the Minigame preset, substituting the multi-instance hub shape for one isolated stage and retaining match transport and scoring placement.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for a compact neon-club lobby comfortably holding about ten players, with open floor for dancers to approach one another. Use `teleporter-link` as a clearly marked battle-transfer pad or club doorway in the lobby and a matching arrival point in the arena. Give each arena one dedicated two-dancer performance floor, phonk-themed lighting, and a strong face-to-face composition; do not add audience seating or backstage rooms because none were requested.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `teleporter-link` at the lobby transfer marker and matching arrival and return markers in every arena instance. Place exactly two opposing dancer start markers per arena, equally spaced from the performance-floor center. Place one `trigger-scoring` volume over each dance floor to delimit the active battle space. Keep general lobby starts around its perimeter so ten arriving players do not overlap the social center.

## Layout components
Build one small shared lobby map and a reusable separate arena instance. The lobby is an open neon club room with peripheral seating or glowing wall features, broad central circulation, and one unmistakable battle-transfer focal point. The arena is a compact symmetrical stage for exactly two dancers, with opposing start spots, a clear centerline, phonk speakers, neon strips, and no route through the performance floor. Bound both interiors cleanly and frame each arena around the dancers.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the isometric first and make it authoritative because club atmosphere, lighting, and face-to-face stage composition lead the design.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium overall scale: a ten-player lobby plus repeatable two-player instances. The theme is dark neon phonk club energy. `P4` is required because each accepted battle occupies a separate instanced arena map.
