# Scene prompt

Create a purely environmental 3D wasteland desert: a small, walkable stretch a few hundred studs across, designed to be explored on foot.

# Agent decision

## Genre

This is `No Genre`: the author explicitly wants an environmental desert to look at and walk around, with no mechanics or game type.

## Shape and preset

There is no genre shape. Leave `enclosure`, `verticality`, `zone-count`, `structure`, and `play-space` at their defaults, producing one exterior, single-surface walkable area. The preset is none because even Explorable Place would add landmarks, explicit circulation, and composed scenic spots that were not requested.

## Config requirements

Draw a barren 3D wasteland desert extending a few hundred studs across. Use a dry, desolate ground treatment with sparse environmental variation sufficient to read as wasteland, but do not introduce buildings, water, settlements, gameplay hazards, or focal structures absent from the brief.

## Layout requirements

Nothing needs placement after segmentation. There are no requested spawns, collectibles, triggers, checkpoints, hazards, teleports, or NPCs.

## Layout components

The build is one continuous walkable desert zone a few hundred studs across. Its ground should remain broadly level and traversable on foot, with subtle surface irregularity and patches of cracked or wind-scoured earth for visual texture rather than significant terrain relief. There are no formal paths or named props. Use a distant, natural desert edge or fading wasteland horizon as the boundary without adding a conspicuous wall. Compose the view to communicate open emptiness while keeping the full playable stretch legible.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render the desolate surface character and open composition first, then derive the top-down plan because no exact topology is required for play.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is small, following the requested on-foot area of a few hundred studs rather than a vehicle-scale world. The theme is barren environmental wasteland desert. Use `P0`; this is one grounded exterior surface with no transition, overhang, separate zone, volumetric play, or structure-critical route.
