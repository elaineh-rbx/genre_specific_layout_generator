# Scene prompt

Create a 3D game map of a small, explorable castle interior for princess roleplay, with the main expected rooms connected by halls and stairways.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, combining princess transformation with open-ended social play rather than quests or scored rounds.

## Shape and preset

Use `interior-single`: the whole map is one castle interior, freely explored as a connected building with no exterior transition. The preset is Morph Roleplay, but its static-settlement shape is replaced by the castle interior and its outdoor orientation landmark is dropped.

## Config requirements

Use `social-hub` as a generously sized central great hall or ballroom where transformed princess avatars can gather and roleplay. Use `vignette-photo` for richly dressed royal scenes such as a throne room, banquet setting, and elegant chamber. Use `path-street` as the interior system of halls and stairways connecting the expected castle rooms. Render visible transformation mirrors, wardrobes, or magical pedestals as part of a dedicated dressing room.

## Layout requirements

Place uncatalogued princess-transformation interaction triggers at the visible mirrors, wardrobes, or magical pedestals in the dressing room, one per available princess grouping. Site the player start in the great hall near a clear route to that room. No quest markers, runway judging zones, scoring volumes, teleporters, or property claims are required.

## Layout components

Build a small multi-room castle interior organized around a central great hall or ballroom. Connect it by broad halls and readable stairways to a throne room, banquet or dining room, royal dressing chamber, and a few decorative side chambers. Keep routes free-roaming rather than forcing a fixed room sequence, with the great hall acting as the orientation core. Use stone walls, arched doors, banners, chandeliers, ornate rugs, royal furniture, mirrors, and wardrobes. The castle walls fully enclose the play space; no exterior grounds are needed. Compose the building so the great hall remains visually dominant while stairs reveal the upper rooms.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: establish the fairy-tale castle atmosphere and room hierarchy first, then derive the plans; the dressed roleplay setting is authoritative.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, covering one compact castle and its principal rooms. The theme is colorful fairy-tale royalty. Use `P2` because playable upper rooms and stair-connected floors overhang lower interior space.
