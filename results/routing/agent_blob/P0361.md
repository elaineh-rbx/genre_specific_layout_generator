# Scene prompt
Create a 3D dragon flight-racing map with a sequential aerial course of 10 large floating rings, each clearly numbered and arranged as a continuous route through the sky. Frame the course from a third-person view behind and slightly above the flying dragon. Below the course, build a simple high-fantasy landscape with varied terrain forming a visible ground layer beneath the open air route.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Racing: the dragon flies a finite ordered course against a timer.

## Shape and preset
Use `route-point-to-point`, a sky route beginning at the first ring and terminating at the tenth. The preset is Sprint / Drag, adapted from a ground lane to an aerial ring corridor and extended with open-volume flight.

## Config requirements
Use `volume-open` for the flyable sky above one visible ground layer. Use `terrain-relief` for the varied high-fantasy landscape beneath the course. Draw exactly 10 large, clearly numbered floating rings forming one continuous readable sequence. Use `startpoint-line` as the first ring’s starting gate and `trigger-finish` as the visible final ring.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly 10 ordered pass-through trigger volumes inside the 10 visible rings, numbered one through ten. The first also carries the functional `startpoint-line`; the tenth carries `trigger-finish`. Place the dragon start immediately before ring one, oriented toward ring two. No recovery checkpoints are added because crashing is explicitly a loss state.

## Layout components
Build one aerial route of 10 rings that curves through open sky with controlled rises, descents, and turns while remaining visually sequential. A continuous high-fantasy ground plane of hills and valleys sits below as scenery and crash surface. Keep generous clear air around every ring and frame from behind and above the dragon, with the next several rings visible ahead.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: ordered route continuity must be established first, while vertical offsets can be assigned when dressing the plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large, driven by 10 spaced flight gates. The theme is simple high fantasy with a dragon above varied terrain. Use `P6` for valid ordered course structure, `CHECK` for volumetric flight, and `tiered` for the non-overhanging terrain relief below.
