# Scene prompt
Create a 3D grim outdoor zombie-apocalypse shooting range. The player stands at an elevated firing position looking down a short 100-stud range toward a lower platform where stationary zombie targets are arranged.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Shooter. This is explicitly a sniping-practice game rather than a survival encounter: the zombies are stationary targets and there is no round end.

## Shape and preset
Use `range-directed`, a single firing position aimed downrange at targets with no opposing route through the map. The matching preset is Aim Trainer, retaining its practice-target bundle while adapting the targets to zombies.

## Config requirements
Use `target-practice` for the visible stationary zombie silhouettes on the lower target platform, arranged at readable positions within the 100-stud range. Add `cover-elevated` for the raised sniper firing position, reached by a simple exposed stair or ramp and looking over the lower platform.

## Layout requirements
The placed half of `target-practice` must register each stationary zombie as a shootable practice target at its visible model. Place one player start at the elevated firing position behind the firing edge. No finish, score volume, enemy spawner, or weapon station is required because this is free practice and the prompt supplies no loadout area.

## Layout components
Build a compact outdoor range with one raised firing platform in the foreground, a straight 100-stud clear shooting lane, and one broad lower zombie-target platform at the far end. Rough apocalypse debris may frame the lane without blocking sightlines. Enclose the sides and backstop with ruined barriers or earth berms; compose the scene along the sniper’s downward sightline.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the grim range look first, then derive the uncomplicated directed plan from it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by the stated 100-stud shot. The theme is grim outdoor zombie apocalypse. Use `tiered` because the firing position and target deck are on different elevations without overhang; no other modifier is forced.
