# Scene prompt
Create a focused 3D story-game map set inside Bloxdale High, a girls' school with basic uniforms: navy plaid skirts, white collared shirts, and cardigan or jacket layers. Build only the connected spaces the story visits: a classroom for start-of-year and friendship scenes, connecting indoor hallways, a cafeteria or lunch area, and a teacher's office. Arrange a small outdoor school courtyard as the final confrontation space, accessible from the hallways, with enough open ground for the group to face one another. Keep the same school layout throughout the school-year story, using simple day-to-day progression rather than seasonal changes; all ending scenes remain in the courtyard.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Adventure is the sole genre. The player moves through a choice-driven school story, and the map exists to sequence its scenes rather than simulate a whole campus.

## Shape and preset
Use `rooms-sequence`, taken from outside Adventure’s usual shapes, for a focused chain of school rooms connected by halls and ending at the courtyard. No preset fits: the Story preset assumes separate chapter maps, while this brief explicitly reuses one compact layout throughout.

## Config requirements
Use `building-interior` for the connected Bloxdale High classroom, cafeteria, teacher’s office, and hallways, with a real threshold leading to the outdoor courtyard. Keep the school visually simple and consistent: functional institutional rooms, basic furnishings, and no seasonal redressing. Include uniformed student figures only as scene dressing, with navy plaid skirts, white collared shirts, and cardigan or jacket layers.

## Layout requirements
Place five uncatalogued story trigger volumes: one in the classroom, one in a principal hallway gathering point, one in the cafeteria, one in the teacher’s office, and one in the courtyard. The courtyard trigger must support all three endings in exactly the same confrontation space. Site the initial player start in the classroom and keep dialogue choices and branching logic outside layout.

## Layout components
Build exactly one classroom, one cafeteria or lunch area, one teacher’s office, a compact set of connecting indoor hallways, and one small outdoor courtyard. Arrange the classroom and cafeteria off the main hall, place the office on a quieter short branch, and terminate the route at doors opening into the courtyard. Give the courtyard enough clear ground for four characters to face one another, with the school facade enclosing one side and a simple fence or wall enclosing the others. Frame the plan as a small, readable story set rather than a complete school.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, then dress the isometric image; every named story room must connect cleanly to the shared final courtyard.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use small scale because only five focused spaces are required. The theme is an ordinary contemporary girls’ school with restrained navy-and-white styling. Route with `P3` because play crosses between the school interior and outdoor courtyard.
