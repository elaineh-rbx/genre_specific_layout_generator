# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map showing a bright tropical beach cove from a hermit crab’s ground-level perspective. Build one compact, dense stretch of warm sand bordered by shallow clear water, palms, and colourful shell-strewn tide pools, with varied shells scattered throughout the cove for exploration.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation because collecting increasingly beautiful shells drives endless growth and inventory progression. Adventure is secondary because exploration and discovery are the immediate activity.

## Shape and preset
Use the single `world-open` shape as one compact shared cove explored in any order. The closest preset is Scavenger Hunt from the secondary genre, retaining its open-world collectible structure but omitting the unrequested quest tracker and deliberately hidden alcoves.

## Config requirements
Apply `water-body` to draw shallow clear coastal water and colourful tide pools as real parts of the cove. Warm sand, palms, rock-edged pools, and visible shell-strewn patches provide dense ground-level detail. The shells themselves are functional collectibles and therefore are placed after segmentation rather than baked into the image configuration.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` as varied shell pickup points throughout the traversable sand and around the dry rims and shallow edges of tide pools. Distribute them densely enough for continuous discovery, with multiple possible routes through the cove and no final shell or winner zone. Keep nodes reachable at hermit-crab scale and avoid placing them beneath opaque water or blocking rocks.

## Layout components
Build one small crescent-shaped sandy cove. The main beach forms a continuous roaming zone, with several colourful tide pools embedded along one side and shallow clear sea bordering the outer curve. Palm clusters frame the landward boundary while rocks subdivide small search pockets without creating dead ends. Scatter visible decorative shells among the functional pickup sites, vary their size and silhouette, and preserve clear crab-width passages. Compose from a very low ground-level viewpoint, with nearby shells prominent and the palms and water enclosing the background.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down view; the tropical look and crab-scale composition lead, while the compact open topology is forgiving.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small because the brief specifies one compact, dense cove. The theme is bright tropical shoreline with colourful tidal detail. Use `CHECK` for the shallow `water-body`; its single exposed surface should not self-occlude. No other modifier is forced.
