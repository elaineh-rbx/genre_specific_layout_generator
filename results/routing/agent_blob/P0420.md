# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map showing a small, sunny tropical island that takes only a few minutes to cross. Beaches ring the shoreline with palm trees, while rocky cliffs and simple jungle areas form the interior; hide 10 treasures across these island zones.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure because the player freely explores a compact island to discover a complete set of hidden objectives.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, one contiguous island that can be searched in any order. The matched preset is Scavenger Hunt, retaining its open exploration shape, hidden-object placement, and secret discovery spaces while omitting a physical quest board that was not requested.

## Config requirements
Use `alcove-secret` for concealed pockets among jungle growth, rocks, and cliff edges. Use `water-body` for the sea enclosing the island and `terrain-relief` for the rocky cliffs rising through the interior. The visible environment should have a beach ring, sunny tropical foliage, palm trees, simple jungle patches, and small natural hiding recesses.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly 10 `collectible-nodes` as hidden treasures distributed across the named zones: some around beaches and palms, some within jungle pockets, and some against reachable rocky cliff features. Every treasure must be reachable, individually discoverable, and not visible from the same approach. No physical tracker or quest-board placement is required.

## Layout components
Build one small island that takes only a few minutes to cross. A continuous sandy beach wraps the shoreline; palms punctuate the coast, while simple jungle occupies the middle and rocky cliffs add raised search areas. Connect the zones through naturally walkable gaps rather than a forced trail. The ocean is the outer boundary. Compose the island as a readable whole while preserving ten distinct hiding sites.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down view; the tropical look and varied hiding composition lead, and no computed topology is required.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, set by a few-minute crossing time and 10 treasures. The theme is bright, sunny tropical adventure. Use `tiered` for cliffs without overhang and `CHECK` for the surrounding water volume; a single surface still represents the traversable island.
