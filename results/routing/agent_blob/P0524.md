# Scene prompt
Create a 3D parkour map built as a small tower with around 10 stacked stages, using clean stylized Roblox blocks in bright, easy-to-read colors and a clear route to the top.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Obby & Platformer, canonically `obby-platformer`, because the requested map is a self-paced parkour climb.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-tower` for a single route rising through stacked stages. Tower Obby is the matching preset, kept small and visually simple.

## Config requirements
Draw a continuous physics-valid `path-track` through around ten stages. Use `obstacle-jump` for short platform gaps, wrap-around blocks, and stepped ascents; `obstacle-moving` for a few simple rotating or sliding pieces; and `obstacle-climb` for short ladders and truss sections. Draw the top landing as `winner-zone`. Omit timers, leaderboards, lethal surfaces, and hidden routes because the brief explicitly asks only for a clear route to the top.

## Layout requirements
Place one `winner-zone` at the summit, on a broad safe platform immediately after the final obstacle. No checkpoints, pickups, or additional invisible placements are needed for this small course.

## Layout components
Build a small open-sided tower with a generous ground-level start pad and around ten compact obstacle stages arranged in one clear upward spiral or zigzag. Give each stage a distinct but simple block arrangement, separated by readable safe landings. Preserve legal gaps and ensure no decorative element resembles a false route. Use clean rectangular Roblox blocks, saturated contrasting colors, crisp edges, and minimal trim. Lightweight vertical framing defines the tower boundary while leaving the ascent visible; open void surrounds the platform chain. End with a broad brightly marked summit pad. Compose the tower so its entry, complete stacked path, and finish can all be understood in one isometric view.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: stage sequence and legal parkour spacing must be authoritative before the isometric render adds color and tower framing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by around ten compact stages. The theme is clean, bright, stylized Roblox blocks. `P6` guarantees a valid obstacle chain, and `P2` captures stacked platforms that overhang lower stages.
