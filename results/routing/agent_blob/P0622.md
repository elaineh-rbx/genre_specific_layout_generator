# Scene prompt

Create a plain grey test-place baseplate with open, unobstructed space for a drivable car to move around.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Simulation because operating a car and earning coins by driving is the game loop. It is not Racing: there is no course, lap, opponent, or finish condition.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open`, a minimal Shared Persistent World consisting of one freely drivable baseplate. Match the Vehicle Sim preset only at its open-world shape level; omit its hauling route and shared resource field because the author asks for unobstructed test space rather than roads or extraction.

## Config requirements

No canonical image option is needed. Draw one simple drivable car on a plain grey baseplate, leaving the surrounding surface deliberately empty and unobstructed. Do not add roads, shops, scenery, gates, resources, buildings, water, relief, NPCs, or decorative districts that the brief did not request. Coin earning is a gameplay rule and must not become visible coin pickups unless separately requested.

## Layout requirements

Place one vehicle spawn marker near one edge of the baseplate with enough clear space around it for the car to appear safely and begin moving. Place one player entry position beside the car. No checkpoints, race starts, finishes, scoring zones, collectible nodes, task stations, or enemy emitters are required; distance-driven rewards are runtime logic rather than segmented layout placement.

## Layout components

Build one flat rectangular grey baseplate as the entire zone. Keep its central and peripheral driving area clear, with no paths, obstacles, elevation changes, water, structures, or props other than the single car. The baseplate edge is the visible boundary of the play space. Compose the car off-center so both the vehicle and a broad expanse of usable driving room are obvious in frame, while preserving the intentionally plain test-place appearance.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down plan. Appearance and open-space readability lead, while the topology is trivial and cannot invalidate the activity.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is small: one car and one simple baseplate with enough turning room. The theme is a plain grey Roblox test place. Route `P0` because the map is a single flat surface with no overhang, interior transition, separate space, volumetric play, or structural-validity constraint.
