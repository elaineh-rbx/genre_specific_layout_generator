# Scene prompt
Create a 3D dress-up hangout with a soft, pretty pastel and cosy aesthetic, arranged as a welcoming space for players to gather and change outfits together.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim. This is an open-ended social dress-up hangout with no contest, voting, or round structure, and it needs no secondary genre.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single` for one welcoming enclosed dressing lounge that is the entire map. Keep the Dress Up preset and its social and photo-ready options, but substitute `interior-single` for the usual `stage-runway` because the brief explicitly asks for a shared hangout rather than a fashion contest.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for a roomy central lounge where players can gather, compare outfits, and circulate without crowding. Use `vignette-photo` for several polished pastel corners designed as outfit-showcase and screenshot backdrops. Add free-text visible dressing geometry around the lounge: coordinated outfit racks, mannequins, mirrors, vanity stations, changing alcoves, soft seating, rugs, plants, and cosy decorative shelving. Keep the alcoves visually open enough that the map remains social rather than becoming a sequence of private rooms.

## Layout requirements
Place one free-text shared avatar spawn area near the lounge entrance, oriented toward the central gathering space and outfit displays. Site outfit-change interaction positions evenly beside the racks, mannequins, mirrors, and changing alcoves so several players can dress at once. No runway marks, judging stations, scoring volumes, checkpoints, collectibles, or hostile emitters are required.

## Layout components
Build one enclosed pastel dressing lounge with a broad central social zone, outfit displays distributed around its perimeter, and multiple shallow changing alcoves opening back toward the group. Connect every station with generous level-floor circulation. Cluster soft seating near the center and place several distinct photo-ready corners at the edges without blocking movement. Use pastel walls, rounded furniture, warm diffuse lighting, plush textiles, floral details, and cute accessories. Bound the whole play space within this single room and frame the view to show the central gathering area and the surrounding dressing choices together.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the cosy aesthetic and decorative composition first, then derive the simple top-down lounge plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by one shared room with capacity for several simultaneous dress-up stations. The theme is soft pastel, cosy, pretty, and welcoming. Use `P0`; the single-level interior has no exterior transition, overhang, separate map, or constrained topology.
