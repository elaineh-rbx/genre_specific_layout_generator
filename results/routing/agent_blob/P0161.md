# Scene prompt
Create a first-person 3D arcade escape map for six players, built as a compact maze of interconnected arcade rooms. Each room contains arcade machines, corridors, doorways, and misleading routes that hint at or trick players searching for the exit; place the true exit beyond the room network. Support multiple distinct arcade map layouts for players to choose from.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Party & Casual, with Puzzle secondary. Six players compete in a quick round and choose among maps, while maze navigation and deceptive clues provide the spatial challenge.

## Shape and preset
Use `puzzle-maze`, taken from outside Party & Casual’s usual shape set, for each compact but procedurally valid arcade labyrinth. The preset is Minigame, with its isolated-stage shape replaced by the maze and its leaderboard omitted because none was requested. Retain round transport, completion detection, and a waiting area for players who have already escaped.

## Config requirements
Draw `obstacle-maze` as interconnected arcade rooms, corridors, and doorways filled with machine banks that form misleading routes. Use `facade-clue` for visible signs, wall motifs, and framed hints positioned in natural first-person sightlines; some may deliberately misdirect while preserving a discoverable true route. Draw `social-hub` as a compact staging room with a clear area for choosing among the available arcade layouts. Draw the visible half of `teleporter-link` as map-selection portals or pads. Provide `spectator-zone` as an out-of-play waiting area for escaped players.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly six `spawn-protected` round starts, evenly distributed so no player begins closer to the exit. Place one `trigger-scoring` completion volume at the true exit of each map; exited players continue outside the active maze while the last remaining player loses. Place the functional half of `teleporter-link` at each lobby map choice and its matching stage arrival. No collectible or solve-item placement is needed.

## Layout components
Build a small lobby plus multiple distinct arcade maze maps, each intended for a one-minute search. Every map contains interconnected arcade rooms, machine-lined corridors, deceptive doorways, false routes, and one true exit beyond the room network. Vary the arrangement and visual arcade motifs between layouts while keeping equivalent scale and fairness. Arcade walls form hard boundaries. Compose each variant around dense machine silhouettes and readable junctions, with the exit concealed from the starts.

## Render order
Render `authored_plan` first for each maze and make the computed blueprint authoritative, then derive top-down and isometric images; every variant needs a valid route to its exit.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a small scale per map, driven by the one-minute round, while the full set is medium in aggregate. The theme is colorful but deceptive first-person arcade interiors. Route with `P6` for valid maze topology and `P4` because the lobby and multiple selectable maps are separate spaces.
