# Scene prompt
Create a 3D soccer map with a clean, simple modern grass pitch. Place two clear goals at opposite ends of the rectangular field, with open space across the pitch for freely running and kicking a ball.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant and only genre is Sports because freely running and kicking a soccer ball is the map’s activity, even though this build intentionally has no teams, match, or scoring system.

## Shape and preset
Use the single `space-bounded` shape for the rectangular grass pitch. The preset is Court Sport, with its scoring-trigger option dropped because the author explicitly wants unscored free play; its bounded field and play-start placement still fit.

## Config requirements
Draw `trigger-bounds` as the clear outer pitch lines and surrounding safety edge. Draw `startpoint-play` as a simple center spot for the ball and a nearby neutral player entry mark. Add exactly two visible soccer goals, centered at opposite ends, as ordinary image geometry rather than scoring targets. Keep the grass surface broad, clean, modern, and completely open.

## Layout requirements
Place one `trigger-bounds` perimeter along the outer pitch edge so the ball can be returned if it leaves the usable map. Place `startpoint-play` markers at the neutral player entry and at midfield for the ball. Place no `trigger-scoring` volumes: both goals are visual free-play equipment only, with no score or match logic attached.

## Layout components
Build one flat rectangular grass pitch as the whole playable zone. Paint crisp sidelines, end lines, a halfway line, center circle, and center spot without adding team-specific areas or decorative stadium complexity. Center one netted goal on each short end and leave uninterrupted open grass between them for free running and shooting. Add a narrow, level safety apron outside the lines. Use a simple low perimeter or empty background to contain the scene while keeping every part of the field visible. Compose the field symmetrically around its long axis.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: render the field plan first and make it authoritative so the two goals, markings, and open kicking space remain aligned; derive the dressed 3D view from it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by one uncomplicated practice pitch. The theme is clean modern grass football with minimal dressing. Route through `P0`; all play occurs on one flat, unobstructed surface.
