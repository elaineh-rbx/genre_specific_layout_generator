# Scene prompt
Create a realistic first-person supernatural action map across three worlds: first, a real-size sealed modern school classroom where the opening encounter takes place. Arrange polished tile flooring, white walls, large windows, LED ceiling lights, rows of wooden desks and chairs, a front whiteboard and teacher desk, shelves, cabinets, backpacks, books, and a few worn broken chairs; one wall is broken open by an armless low-grade curse, while flickering lights and the cramped desk-filled room create the only combat space. Second, build a realistic forest sprint environment with a fast route through dense trees, branches, leaf-covered ground, and sunlight filtering through the canopy. Third, create a small original cursed-energy combat area inspired by supernatural anime, designed as a compact arena. Use immersive realistic materials, natural lighting, and detailed physical props throughout.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Action is the dominant and only genre. Combat and physical first-person movement define the authored game; the forest traversal is one act within that action structure rather than a separate Adventure genre.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-chaptered`, taken from outside Action’s usual shapes, because the prompt explicitly requests three distinct worlds. Use the Boss Raid preset with its shape replaced: hostile entry and destructible props remain recognizable, while unsupported ring-out hazards are dropped.

## Config requirements
Use `destructible-cluster` for the classroom breach, worn chairs, and other explicitly physical props that can fracture into debris. Use `arena-lockin` for the sealed classroom boundary and the compact cursed-energy encounter. Draw a clear, fast forest sprint route as scene-specific geometry. Keep the realistic classroom obstacles readable and do not add lethal edges.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` once in each of the three worlds, with the classroom start away from the breached wall and monster entry. Place one `spawner-npc` at the single broken classroom wall for the armless curse and additional hostile entry points around the third arena only if needed by that encounter. No pickups or checkpoints were requested.

## Layout components
World one is one real-size sealed classroom: polished tile, white walls, large windows, LED fixtures, rows of desks and chairs, front teaching area, shelves, cabinets, books, backpacks, and one broken wall. Preserve narrow but continuous movement lanes among the desks. World two is a fast directed forest route through dense trees and low branches over leaf-covered ground. World three is one small bounded supernatural combat arena with original cursed-energy motifs. Each world has a hard boundary and no traversable connection to the others. Frame the classroom from its spawn toward the breach, the forest along its route, and the final arena around its center.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: desk circulation, the forest sprint line, sealed boundaries, and compact combat clearances must be established before realistic dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium overall because it contains three focused maps; each individual map is small. The theme is photoreal modern supernatural anime with natural light, flickering classroom illumination, and cursed-energy effects. Use `P4` because the three worlds are separate maps.
