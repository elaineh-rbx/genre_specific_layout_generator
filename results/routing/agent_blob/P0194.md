# Scene prompt
Create a realistic 3D deserted-Earth map covering one small ruined town and its nearby abandoned countryside. The town forms the central exploration zone, with empty streets, damaged buildings, and scattered notes, recordings, and environmental objects left behind; surrounding fields, roads, and rural terrain extend outward as a focused outer zone.

Use `settlement-density` for one small, coherent built-up town of damaged buildings and empty streets rather than scattered standalone ruins.

Site several along the town streets and damaged-building edges and several beside rural roads or field landmarks, with enough separation to reward exploration; no exact count was specified.

A broader outer zone of abandoned fields and realistic rural terrain surrounds it. Roads leave the town in several directions, branch into worn tracks, and reconnect the countryside to the town without forming separate maps. Use weathered asphalt, dirt, grass, and neglected field boundaries. Enclose the focused region with distant tree lines, impassable collapsed roads, and natural horizon terrain. Frame the town as the central compositional mass, with countryside forming a spacious ring around it.
