# Scene prompt

Create a 3D basketball World Cup competition map centered on a realistic televised basketball court inside a small full stadium. The court sits at the center, surrounded by crowd stands on all sides, with arena lighting and broadcast-style framing.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Simulation because the author’s primary loop is repeatedly rolling players and assembling a team. Sports is secondary because that team then competes in basketball matches.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-bounded` from Sports’ usual set: one regulation-style basketball court forms the rigid central play space inside its stadium perimeter. The preset is Full Stadium, augmented with the court-play requirements that its dressing-focused bundle omits.

## Config requirements

Apply `spectator-bleachers` as tiered crowd stands on all four sides, `barrier-perimeter` as the enclosing arena shell, and `spectator-zone` as team benches and sideline areas outside the court. The visible forms of `trigger-bounds`, `startpoint-play`, and `trigger-scoring` are respectively the painted court perimeter, center-court reset markings, and two basketball hoops with backboards. The rolling interface remains UI and creates no physical draft room.

## Layout requirements

Place one `trigger-bounds` detection perimeter exactly on the court’s painted boundary. Place one `startpoint-play` at center court for match resets. Place two `trigger-scoring` volumes, one aligned through each hoop cylinder at opposite ends of the court. These functional volumes must register play without appearing in the render.

## Layout components

Build one central realistic basketball court with two opposing hoops, center circle, key areas, sidelines, and baselines. Surround it with narrow runoff space, opposing team benches, courtside broadcast positions, and tiered seating rising on every side. Close the stadium with a compact arena wall and roof-height lighting rig; no exterior or concourse is needed. Use strong overhead broadcast lighting, competition signage, and balanced televised framing centered on the full court.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first as authoritative, then derive the top-down image. The known basketball markings can be placed parametrically, while the televised stadium look and sightlines should lead the composition.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a medium scale: a complete court and surrounding stands, but explicitly a small stadium. The theme is a realistic televised international basketball championship. Apply `tiered` because the bleachers rise in steps without overhanging the court; no other pipeline modifier is forced.
