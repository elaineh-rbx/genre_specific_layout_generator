# Scene prompt
Create a compact 3D scary forest map a few hundred studs across, with dense woodland, hiding spots throughout, and items scattered across the forest.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Survival because one AI monster hunts players through hiding space. Action is secondary because the clarified round ends by defeating that monster with found items.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded`, a compact forest arena a few hundred studs across that keeps the monster and players in meaningful proximity. The preset is none: the mode combines asymmetric pursuit with scattered combat pickups and does not retain a complete existing bundle.

## Config requirements
Draw `cover-los` as dense tree clusters, broad trunks, tangled roots, mossy boulders, fallen logs, and brush pockets that create frequent hiding places and short breaks in visibility. Keep enough clear ground between clusters for the monster to patrol and for players to flee without snagging on decorative clutter.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawner-npc` at a concealed edge of the forest for the single AI-controlled monster, with navigable patrol ground reaching every hiding cluster. Place `spawn-protected` player starts away from that origin and screened by terrain. Place `powerup-buffs` as the unspecified combat items scattered across distinct cover clusters, spaced so players must move through the forest rather than collect everything in one area.

## Layout components
The map is one compact woodland zone a few hundred studs across. Dense trees and undergrowth fill the interior, broken into several readable cover clusters by narrow natural paths and small clearings. Hiding props include broad trunks, hollow or fallen logs, boulders, roots, and thick brush. Scattered combat-item sites occupy visible pockets beside paths and inside risky clearings. A dense tree wall and rough rock boundary enclose the play space. Compose the forest with a darker center and layered tree silhouettes, while preserving readable movement corridors.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the atmospheric hiding-space composition leads, and no maze, circuit, or otherwise strict topology requires a generated plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, set by the stated few-hundred-stud footprint. The theme is a dark, dense monster-haunted forest. Use `P0`; the build is one bounded surface with no requested interiors, separate maps, or overhanging layers.
