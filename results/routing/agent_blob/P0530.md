# Scene prompt
Create a 3D obby map built as a small, plainly playable tower—a quick vertical course rising through stacked platform sections—with a silly bright candy-colour look.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Obby & Platformer, centered on a short player-controlled vertical platform course.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-tower` from the Tower Obby preset: a compact stack of platforms forming one quick climb. Keep the preset’s ordered track and end reward area, but omit its specific timed and climbing obstacle options because the brief asks only for a simple, plainly playable tower.

## Config requirements
`path-track` is a continuous ascending chain of brightly colored platforms with physics-legal horizontal and vertical gaps. `winner-zone` is a small, clearly readable candy-colored payoff platform at the top. No specific gag props, lethal surfaces, moving obstacles, or checkpoints are added; the short length makes a full restart reasonable.

## Layout requirements
Place one `winner-zone` trigger across the top platform, centered away from its edges. Nothing else needs placement after segmentation: there are no requested pickups, checkpoints, teleporters, or invisible hazard volumes.

## Layout components
The map has a small ground-level start pad, a quick sequence of stacked platform sections wrapping upward through the tower footprint, and one top reward platform. Connections alternate between short horizontal jumps and modest vertical steps while remaining plainly readable and playable. Use simple geometric platforms in bright candy pink, lemon yellow, aqua, lime, and lavender, with clean contrast between adjacent jumps. Open air surrounds the climb, and a low perimeter barrier encloses only the start and finish pads rather than the active jump path. Compose the view from an elevated three-quarter angle that shows the complete start-to-finish ascent in one frame.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish the legal jump chain first because an impossible gap would make the obby unplayable, then dress the isometric image from that plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by the requested quick climb rather than a long endurance tower. The theme is silly through bright candy colors and chunky low-detail geometry, without invented novelty props. `P6` is required because the platform sequence must be valid by construction. `P2` is also required because the stacked platforms overhang and occlude lower sections.
