# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map progression of 10 increasingly challenging realistic gym environments. Each world is a compact, walkable single gym-floor-sized area, beginning with a realistic gym and advancing through larger commercial gyms and elite training facilities. Arrange training areas, a boss area, and a pet-purchase area within each gym floor.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation (`simulation`) because raising strength through repeated training and multipliers is the core loop. Action (`action`) is secondary because boss and goon fights gate access to later gyms.

## Shape and preset
Use `tier-ladder`: ten gym worlds form a clear rising sequence, each unlocked after its preceding boss. The Stat Grinder preset is the direct fit, retaining training stations, progression gates, and the upgrade storefront; omit its rebirth pad because rebirth was not requested.

## Config requirements
Draw `station-training` as dense sets of realistic weight racks, machines, punching stations, and strength-training equipment in every gym; `gate-progression` as a clearly marked locked exit to the next world; `social-hub` as each gym’s pet-purchase area; and `path-circulation` as open walkways linking training, pet, boss, and exit zones. Draw each boss area as a collision-clean gym-floor arena with enough open room for combat.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `station-training` on each usable equipment station throughout all ten worlds. Place `spawner-npc` emitters for boss goons and one boss within each gym’s combat area, separated from training and pet-purchase traffic. Place one linked travel endpoint at every world exit, activating only after the local boss threshold is met. Place the functional side of the pet storefront in one purchase area per gym.

## Layout components
Build exactly ten separately loaded, compact, walkable gym-floor worlds. Each contains a dense training zone, one pet-purchase zone, one boss arena, and one exit gate, joined by short broad circulation paths. Progress visual quality and scale from a modest realistic gym through larger commercial gyms to elite training facilities while keeping every world grounded in realistic fitness architecture. Use walls and equipment rows to define zones without creating mazes, enclose each floor as a complete interior, and frame the training floor as the main visual focus with the boss gate visible beyond it.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render each gym’s realistic interior first and treat it as authoritative, then derive its top-down because the routes are simple and topology is not structurally fragile.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large overall because there are ten worlds, though each floor is compact. The theme is increasingly prestigious realistic gym design. Use `P4` for ten separate loaded worlds; no additional modifier is forced within an individual single-level floor.
