## Enriched image prompt
Create a compact, continuous high-fantasy action-RPG world whose walkable route crosses nine ordered regions: the Beginner Plain, Mist Forest, Ice Canyon, Lava Volcano, Golden Desert, Abyssal Sea, Sky City, Shadow Kingdom, and Black Moon Castle. Make each region visually distinct but close enough to reach on foot, with clear transitions and dedicated entrances leading from each region to separate small dungeon and raid maps. Use hostile encounter clearings away from the main travel route, regional boss arenas sized for immense creatures, and the Black Moon Castle as the final distant landmark and arena. Show icy crystal formations for the 15-metre Frost Titan, volcanic ground and open air for the 60-metre lava dragon, and a cosmic throne-like court for the floating 30-metre Astral Kaiser. The world has dramatic AAA-style cosmic high-fantasy dressing inspired by a shattered black moon, with regional terrain, water, and elevation kept readable in one isometric composition.

## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG. Its stat progression, monster and boss combat, quests, dungeons, raids, loot, and level-banded regions make the classification explicit.

## Shape and preset
Use shared shape `world-hub-dungeon`, named Hub and Dungeons: one compact continuous overworld feeds separate dungeon and raid maps through region-specific entrances. The matched preset is Action RPG, retaining its encounter-ground and hostile-emitter structure while omitting unsupported town and economy-ring features.

## Config requirements
Use `hazard-aggro` for broad monster clearings and boss grounds set back from safe travel paths. Use `landmark-focal` to keep Black Moon Castle visible as the final regional waypoint. Use `teleporter-link` for the visible dedicated dungeon and raid entrances, with the separate-map interpretation. Use `water-body` for the Abyssal Sea and `terrain-relief` for the ice canyon, volcano, desert relief, and elevated Sky City.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` emitter groups inside each of the nine regional combat grounds, with additional emitters inside every separate dungeon, raid, and boss arena; none belongs on the through-road. Place the functional half of each `teleporter-link` directly against its visible regional entrance and pair it with the corresponding small instanced map.

## Layout components
The main map is one walkable overworld containing nine sequentially connected regional zones, beginning at the plain and ending at Black Moon Castle. Roads and terrain passes connect every adjacent region; the sea crossing and ascent to Sky City remain visually explicit. Each region has encounter ground and a dedicated entrance to one or more subordinate dungeon or raid maps. Major set pieces are the Frost Titan arena in the Ice Canyon, lava-dragon arena at the volcano, and Astral Kaiser court in the castle. Open terrain bounds the early world, while cliffs, water, city edges, kingdom walls, and the castle frame later zones. Compose the nine-zone journey as a readable foreground-to-background progression.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: draw the connected overworld plan first so all nine regions, their walking route, and their dungeon entrances remain coherent; dress the isometric view from that plan. The top-down plan is authoritative.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is medium, driven by the need to read individual monsters, entrances, roads, and boss arenas while honoring the author's compact first build. The theme is cosmic high fantasy with elemental regions and an ominous shattered-black-moon finale. `P4` is required for the separate dungeon and raid maps, `P3` for their linked overworld-to-interior transitions, `tiered` for the canyon, volcano, and elevated city relief, and `CHECK` for the swimmable or volumetric Abyssal Sea and Sky City spaces.
