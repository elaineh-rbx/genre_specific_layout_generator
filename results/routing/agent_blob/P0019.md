# Scene prompt
Create a compact, continuous 3D fantasy RPG world that players can walk across, progressing through nine connected regions: Beginner's Plain, Mist Forest, Ice Canyon, Lava Volcano, Golden Desert, Abyssal Sea, Sky City, Shadow Kingdom, and Black Moon Castle. Arrange the regions as a clear journey from open grassy plains through foggy woodland, icy ravines, volcanic terrain, desert, a deep-sea-themed area, a city suspended in the sky, and a dark kingdom, ending at the Black Moon Castle beneath the looming remnants of an exploded black moon. Place dedicated entrances within each regional map leading to separate small dungeon and raid maps. Feature region-appropriate monsters and imposing boss encounter spaces, including an icy titan arena, a volcanic dragon lair, and a final cosmic throne or fortress arena within the castle.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG. Levels, stats, monsters, bosses, quests, dungeons, raids, and a fixed regional progression are one coherent role-playing loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-hub-dungeon`: one compact continuous overworld feeds separate small dungeon and raid maps through regional entrances. No preset remains recognisable because the scene lacks the sanctuary and economy bundle of Action RPG and the social hub of Dungeon Crawler.

## Config requirements
Use `hazard-aggro` for monster grounds and boss arenas set away from connective routes; `gate-progression` for visible thresholds between successively harder regions; `landmark-focal` for Sky City, the exploded black moon, and Black Moon Castle; `building-interior` for entered dungeons, raid maps, and castle chambers; `terrain-relief` for icy ravines, volcanic slopes, and the ascent to Sky City; and `water-body` for the Abyssal Sea region. Draw dedicated dungeon or raid entrances in every region.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` emitters in each of the nine regions using region-appropriate monsters, plus boss emitters in the icy titan arena, volcanic dragon lair, and final cosmic fortress arena. Place exactly nine transition markers, one at each dedicated regional dungeon or raid entrance, linking to its separate small map.

## Layout components
Build exactly nine connected overworld regions in the stated order: Beginner’s Plain, Mist Forest, Ice Canyon, Lava Volcano, Golden Desert, Abyssal Sea, Sky City, Shadow Kingdom, and Black Moon Castle. Use walkable passes, bridges, ramps, and gates to keep the entire compact overworld traversable. Give each region a distinct ground, climate, silhouette, encounter ground, and dungeon entrance. The black moon remnant and final castle anchor the end of the composition; three major boss spaces occupy ice, volcano, and castle destinations.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the top-down plan is rendered first and authoritative so all nine regions connect in order and every instance entrance remains reachable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is large overall despite compact regions, driven by nine overworld zones and their small instances. The theme is dramatic AAA-style cosmic fantasy. Use `P4` for separate dungeon and raid maps, `P3` for linked interior transitions, `tiered` for non-overhanging regional relief, and `CHECK` for the Abyssal Sea play envelope.
