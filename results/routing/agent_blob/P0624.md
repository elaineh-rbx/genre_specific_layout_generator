## Enriched image prompt
Create one small realistic open-air beach spelling-game set. Arrange a few beach chairs together on a compact stretch of sand, all facing a shared physical word-display board while retaining a clear outlook across the sea. Keep the seating close enough that everyone participates in the same prompt at once, with a short open arrival area behind the chairs and no long shoreline route or unrelated beach attractions. Use natural sand, realistic chairs, calm water, and an unobstructed horizon.

## Genre
Puzzle is the dominant genre.

## Shape and preset
Use `puzzle-open`, Open-Air Puzzle Space, because the spelling activity happens on an unenclosed beach set. Use the Word / Quiz Puzzle preset, omitting a solve gate because correct typed answers award points rather than opening a physical path.

## Config requirements
Use `facade-clue` for the shared physical word-display board placed in every seated player's natural view. Use `water-body` for the real sea and horizon beyond the beach. The few beach chairs, sand, and compact arrival ground are direct scene components rather than extra catalogue options.

## Layout requirements
Nothing needs post-segmentation placement. The spoken word, typed response, correctness check, shared timing, third-person camera, and point award are mechanics and UI rather than physical volumes. The beach chairs and shared board are visible geometry drawn with the scene.

## Layout components
The map is one small bounded beach zone. A few chairs form a compact seating cluster on the sand, all oriented toward one shared display board and the sea outlook. A short clear arrival strip sits behind the seating. The shoreline runs across the front of the composition, with calm sea extending to the horizon as the visual boundary. There is no extended walk, separate room, stage, gate, or second map.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the realistic beach atmosphere, chair orientation, shared display, and sea outlook should lead, with the simple top-down arrangement derived afterward.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
