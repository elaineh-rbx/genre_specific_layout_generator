# Scene prompt

Create a realistic third-person 3D beach setting: one small stretch of shoreline with a clear sea outlook, sandy ground, and a few beach chairs arranged facing the water. Keep the area compact and focused around the seated beach-chair viewing area.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Puzzle. The original brief is specifically a spelling game in which everyone answers the same prompted word, matching the genre’s Word / Quiz Puzzle family; the beach is its physical setting rather than a Party game classification.

## Shape and preset

Use `puzzle-open`, an Open-Air Puzzle Space reduced to one compact beach seating area. Match the Word / Quiz Puzzle preset, but omit `gate-solve` because correct spelling awards points rather than opening a physical route. Also omit `facade-clue`: the word and correctness feedback are delivered through voice and screen UI, not a clue wall in the scene.

## Config requirements

Use `water-body` for the sea filling the players’ outward view and acting as the far visual boundary. Draw sandy shoreline ground and a few realistic beach chairs in a shallow group facing the water. Do not add gates, key-item receptacles, buttons, clue facades, collectibles, buildings, NPCs, settlements, islands, or extra terrain features. The spoken word, typed answer, correctness feedback, and points are audio, UI, and game rules rather than image geometry.

## Layout requirements

Place one shared player spawn area on the landward side of the chair group so arrivals do not block the sea outlook. Associate one seating interaction point with each segmented beach chair, facing seaward. No solve-input slot, progress gate, collectible, checkpoint, enemy emitter, or scoring volume belongs in the layout; all players receive and answer the same non-spatial prompt simultaneously.

## Layout components

Build one small, level strip of sand with a clean shoreline along its seaward edge. Arrange a few beach chairs close together as the central viewing and play area, all oriented toward an uninterrupted sea horizon. Leave a short clear approach behind the chairs and keep the sides visually open. The water and horizon form the outward boundary, while the compact sand patch limits the landward extent. Compose the frame from a realistic third-person-friendly angle that clearly shows the chairs, sand, shoreline, and sea outlook without unrelated beach clutter.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down plan, because visual realism and the shared seated outlook lead while topology is trivial.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is small, driven by one focused seating cluster. The theme is a realistic, quiet beach with a clear sea view. Use `CHECK` for the sea’s volumetric play-space representation; no overhang, interior transition, separate map, or validity-by-construction pass is needed.
