## Enriched image prompt
Create one continuous, walkable cartoon-animal world containing six adjoining biomes in progression order: Jungle as the starting zone, then Snow, Desert, Lake, Volcano, and the Dark Lands as the final zone. Give each biome a distinct ground treatment, food-orb distribution area, animal encounter clearings, mission space, and a large boss encounter site. Link every region with visible land paths and readable transitional borders rather than portals or loading gaps. Make the Volcano strongly elevated and the Lake a real water basin, while the Dark Lands forms the most imposing final area. Use bright stylized Roblox-friendly scenery and powerful but non-realistic creature scale.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation, with RPG secondary for combat, quests, bosses, and progression through increasingly difficult biomes.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open-biomes`, an Open World with Biomes taken from RPG's shape set. Override its usual `P4` route because the author explicitly requires one continuous map with walkable transitions. The preset is none; neither Stat Grinder nor Open World & Survival RPG preserves this exact food-orb evolution loop and six-zone world without adding unsupported plots or rebirth structures.

## Config requirements
Use `path-circulation` for the continuous route between all six regions, `hazard-aggro` for biome-specific animal and boss encounter clearings, `terrain-relief` for snowy rises and the elevated Volcano, and `water-body` for the Lake basin. The distinct Jungle, Snow, Desert, Volcano, and Dark Lands treatments are direct scene-specific requirements.

## Layout requirements
Distribute `collectible-nodes` for distinct food and evolution orbs within every biome. Place `spawner-npc` Spawners for each biome's exclusive animals and giant boss, set back inside its encounter clearing. Menus, shop purchases, inventory, EXP, evolution logic, abilities, PvP, saving, and rewards are mechanics.

## Layout components
The world contains six contiguous zone segments: Jungle, Snow, Desert, Lake, Volcano, and Dark Lands. Each has a connected traversal path, collectible ground, ordinary encounter clearing, and boss arena. Jungle anchors the start; the remaining zones form an escalating readable sequence, with the tiered Volcano and final Dark Lands visually dominant. No segment is a separate map.

## Render order
Use `topdown` because all six biome connections and progression order must remain continuous before distinct terrain and creature-themed dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use large scale to show six substantial biome zones while retaining clearings and transitions. The theme is bright stylized cartoon creature evolution with imposing non-realistic animals. `tiered` is required for the Volcano's strong relief, and `CHECK` is required for the traversable Lake. `P4` is explicitly not used because the world is continuous.
