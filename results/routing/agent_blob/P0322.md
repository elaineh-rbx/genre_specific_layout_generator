# Scene prompt

Crea un mapa 3D continuo de evolución animal con estilo cartoon brillante y estilizado, apto para Roblox, donde seis biomas conectados se recorren a pie. La Jungla ocupa la zona inicial y conduce por transiciones claras hacia una región nevada, un desierto, un lago, un volcán reservado para criaturas poderosas y, al extremo final del mundo, las Tierras Oscuras como destino del Dragón de la Oscuridad. Cada bioma presenta terreno, vegetación o formaciones propias, comida u orbes repartidos por el suelo, animales exclusivos, áreas de misión y un espacio abierto para un jefe gigante; coloca criaturas cada vez más imponentes hacia el volcán y las Tierras Oscuras.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is RPG: experience collection, leveling, quests, bosses, and final creature evolutions organize the journey.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open-biomes` for six regions whose threat and reward rise toward the final destination. Start from the Open World & Survival RPG preset, omit its unrequested player base ground, and override the shape’s normal separate-map route because the author explicitly requires one continuous walkable world.

## Config requirements

Apply `collectible-nodes` to visible food and evolution orbs scattered across each biome; `landmark-focal` to the volcano and distant Dark Lands; `hazard-aggro` to open boss grounds; `terrain-relief` to the volcanic and regional landforms; and `water-body` to the lake biome. Give every region distinct terrain, vegetation, formations, mission areas, and increasingly imposing creatures.

## Layout requirements

Place the functional half of `collectible-nodes` across traversable ground in all six biomes, with distributions specific to each region. Place `spawner-npc` origins for exclusive animals and one giant boss within each biome’s open boss area, set away from transition corridors. Keep the final Dark Lands encounters reachable from the continuous route.

## Layout components

Build one continuous sequence of six walkable zones: starting Jungle, snowy region, desert, lake, volcano, and terminal Dark Lands. Use broad natural transition corridors rather than portals. Each zone has distinct ground, flora or formations, an orb-and-food field, a mission area, and an open boss arena. Raise the volcano as a late-world landmark and place the Dark Lands at the farthest end. Frame the world to show the full danger gradient.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative so all six biomes connect and the progression route reaches the final Dark Lands.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is huge, driven by six complete biomes. The theme is bright stylized cartoon creatures becoming imposing toward a dark finale. Use `tiered` for relief and `CHECK` for the lake; explicitly do not use `P4`, because all regions share one continuous surface.
