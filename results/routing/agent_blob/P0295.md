# Scene prompt
Create three separate, sequential 3D maps for a respectful Indian mythological epic battle: Act 1 is the Shores of Lanka, a calm golden-hour beach tutorial space with ocean behind it; Act 2 is the Golden Gates, a gauntlet leading through monumental gilded palace gates where a flying lotus chariot can pass overhead; Act 3 is a compact rooftop-sized Sky Arena above Lanka Palace, crossed in seconds, with open sightlines for the ten-headed demon king’s flying lotus chariot, palace towers far below, and gathering storm clouds. Enclose the rooftop with low mythic stone parapets and magical light barriers. Place banners, Hanuman statues, and scattered glowing dharma shrines around the arenas; use golden energy and light effects rather than graphic combat imagery. End the final arena in a restorative wash of light, with a distant gentle crash off-screen and Sita’s silhouette reunited with Ram.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Action is dominant because the authored game is a boss battle and combat gauntlet; Adventure is secondary because the three acts form a stated sequential story journey.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-chaptered` for three genuinely separate maps progressing from shore to gates to rooftop. This shared shape comes from outside Action’s usual set and matches the accepted separation requirement. The closest preset is Story, supplemented with the combat requirements.

## Config requirements
Use `gate-chapter` for the monumental Golden Gates as the visible threshold into the second act, `arena-lockin` for focused act encounters, and `water-body` for the ocean bordering the Shores map. Depict banners, Hanuman statues, glowing dharma shrines, magical rooftop barriers, palace towers, and golden restorative light as scene-specific geometry and dressing. Do not add `hazard-kill`: the final rooftop is explicitly protected from falls.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` arrival markers once in each of the three maps, screened from immediate enemy fire. Place `spawner-npc` emitters along the Golden Gates gauntlet and around the Sky Arena perimeter for Rakshasa waves. Place `powerup-buffs` as scattered glowing collectible-orb points around combat floors, and site shield functionality at each visible dharma shrine. Link the three acts in order through chapter-transition destinations.

## Layout components
Map one is a bounded golden-hour beach tutorial strip with ocean behind the playable sand. Map two is a directed gauntlet terminating at monumental gilded gates, with clear overhead airspace for the lotus chariot. Map three is one compact, flat palace rooftop with open sightlines, low stone parapets, magical light barriers, and distant towers below. Keep props away from central dodge lanes. Frame each map around its forward objective, with the rooftop sky and chariot flight volume dominant in the finale.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the separate-map progression, gauntlet route, protected rooftop boundary, and clear combat circulation must be valid before visual dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large overall because there are three maps, though the final arena itself is small. The theme is respectful modern Indian mythological epic, saffron-gold at first and storm-dark at the climax. Use `P4` because the acts are separate maps, plus `CHECK` for the Shores ocean and the boss’s open-air flight volume.
