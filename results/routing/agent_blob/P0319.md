# Scene prompt

Create a large 3D open-world survival map with multiple distinct loot areas spread across the terrain. Include civilian locations containing common civilian supplies, Soviet-themed locations containing Soviet equipment, military sites containing military loot, and a few secluded or heavily defended locations holding very rare special loot. The map supports open approaches and encounters between players and zombie/AI threats; initial weapon pickups include an M1911 pistol and a hunting knife.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Survival, with Shooter secondary. This is a persistent permadeath world about surviving players and zombie AI while scavenging weapons and supplies.

## Shape and preset

Use `open-battlefield`, taken from outside Survival’s usual shapes, for one large contiguous world with dispersed loot locations and open approaches rather than lanes. Preset is none: Battle Royale shares scattered loot but contradicts the persistent, non-round structure, while Resource Survival assumes gathering and base building not requested here.

## Config requirements

Create geographically distinct civilian, Soviet-themed, military, and secluded high-value site families, each visually communicating its loot tier. Preserve broad open approaches and enough broken terrain, walls, wreckage, and structures for encounters without turning the world into fixed combat lanes.

## Layout requirements

Place `collectible-loot` throughout the map by site identity: common civilian supplies at civilian locations, Soviet equipment at Soviet sites, military equipment at military sites, and super-rare special loot at a few secluded or heavily defended locations. Include initial M1911 pistol and hunting-knife pickups. Place `spawner-npc` origins for zombie/AI threats around navigable site edges and defended special-loot areas, never directly atop pickups.

## Layout components

Build one large terrain map containing multiple separated points of interest. Civilian districts should be the most approachable and common; Soviet-themed compounds and military sites should be stronger landmarks with wider defensive perimeters; a few special-loot sites should sit remotely or behind naturally dangerous approaches. Connect sites with informal roads and wilderness routes while preserving alternate cross-country approaches. Use an open outer boundary treatment that still clearly limits the playable region. Compose the map so loot-site families remain readable at regional scale.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative; the space is appearance-led and does not require a computed maze or circuit.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is large, driven by many dispersed loot regions. The theme is grounded post-apocalyptic open-world survival. Use `P0`; one top-down surface can represent the requested geometry without extra capture passes.
