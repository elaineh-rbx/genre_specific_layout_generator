## Enriched image prompt
Create an original large post-apocalyptic open-world survival map with several connected loot regions, without copying a named game’s exact terrain, settlements, or structures. Separate the world into visually distinct civilian, Soviet-era, military, and super-rare special loot sites. Use overgrown residential streets and shops for civilian areas, weathered industrial depots for Soviet-era areas, fortified compounds for military areas, and one remote high-risk landmark for special loot. Make the major buildings enterable and connect all regions with readable roads and wilderness paths while leaving open ground for player-versus-player encounters and zombie patrols. Use abandoned vehicles, broken fencing, vegetation, and decay to support the persistent survival atmosphere. Keep the outer terrain naturally bounded and show every major region in one coherent plan.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Survival, with Shooter secondary because firearms, PvP sightlines, and distributed weapon loot materially shape the map.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, an Open World shape taken from outside Survival’s usual set, because this is one persistent contiguous world rather than separate biome maps or a round arena. No preset fits persistent PvP-and-zombie looting without adding bases or a shrinking boundary, so the preset is none.

## Config requirements
Use `building-interior` for enterable loot structures connected to the exterior. Use `settlement-density` for the built-up civilian area and `landmark-focal` for the remote special-loot destination. Add scene-specific visual zoning for civilian, Soviet-era, military, and special rarity regions because no catalogue option represents loot rarity as architecture. The M1911, hunting knife, permadeath, and loot probabilities are mechanics or inventory data rather than map geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-loot` as Collectible anchors throughout every region, with civilian-tier placements most common, Soviet-era placements in depots, military placements inside fortified compounds, and super-rare placements only at the remote landmark. Place `spawner-npc` as Zombie Spawner origins across roads, settlements, and compound approaches, away from initial arrival ground.

## Layout components
The contiguous map contains several connected regions: civilian streets, Soviet-era depots, military compounds, one special-loot landmark, enterable buildings, roads, wilderness paths, open encounter ground, and decayed natural boundaries. Every major site has at least one traversable connection to the network.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: make regional connectivity, roads, and exterior-to-interior links authoritative, then dress the isometric apocalypse world from that plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is large so multiple loot regions fit while doors and cover-scale details remain legible. The theme is an overgrown, gritty post-apocalyptic landscape. The modifier is `P3` because players enter loot buildings from the open world.
