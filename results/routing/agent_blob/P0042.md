# Scene prompt

Create a small, cute pink shop plaza focused on browsing avatar decals, with storefront display areas arranged around a simple central walkway. Keep the plaza compact and uncluttered, with decals visibly presented for purchase and no runway, hangout area, or minigame spaces.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim because avatar customization and decal shopping are the entire activity.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-bounded`, taken from outside the genre’s usual shape set: the whole experience occupies one compact, single-level plaza. The preset is none. Dress Up is contradicted by the explicit removal of its runway and social areas.

## Config requirements

Use `path-street` as a short, uncluttered pedestrian walkway connecting the storefront display areas. Draw cute pink storefront façades and clearly visible avatar-decal display boards around that path as scene-specific geometry. Do not add interiors, a social hub, runway, or unrelated venues.

## Layout requirements

After segmentation, place one purchase interaction marker at every decal display position, aligned to the front of its board and reachable from the central walkway. No spawns, checkpoints, scoring volumes, or other gameplay markers are implied by the brief.

## Layout components

Build one small plaza with a central straight or gently curved walkway and compact storefront display bays facing inward around it. Each bay should present decals toward the walking route with enough clear standing room to browse without clutter. Use flat pink paving, pastel storefront trim, simple signs, and a low decorative perimeter boundary. Keep the composition centered and readable in one frame, with no rear districts or unused side areas.

## Render order

Render in `isometric` order: establish the cute storefront look first, then derive the simple top-down plan from that authoritative image because the topology is uncomplicated.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, driven by the requested single compact plaza. The theme is cute pastel pink avatar retail. The pipeline route is `P0`: one flat exterior surface has no overhang, interior transition, separate map, or construction-valid topology requirement.
