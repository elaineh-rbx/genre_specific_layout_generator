# Scene prompt
Create the core 3D launch map for a multiplayer fashion runway game: a Luxury Mall themed environment for 10 players. Arrange a comfortable central lobby that holds all players, with a standard runway-show layout featuring a long catwalk, model entrance, audience/viewing area, and a top-three winners’ podium. Surround the show space with mall shopping and styling zones for clothing, body, hair, makeup, accessories, and shoes, plus a designer studio and photo studio. Luxury apartments are separate instanced home spaces, not part of the active mall map. Future maps are distinct themed environments but reuse this consistent runway layout.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, with Party & Casual secondary because the dress-up activity runs as repeated multiplayer voting rounds.

## Shape and preset
Use `stage-runway`: one standard show layout forms the core of the Luxury Mall and will remain reusable across later themes. The matching preset is Dress Up.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for a central lobby comfortably holding all 10 players and for the audience gathering space. Use `vignette-photo` for the dedicated photo studio and polished outfit backdrops. Use `path-street` as wide mall circulation connecting clothing, body, hair, makeup, accessories, shoes, designer studio, model entrance, and lobby. Add visible free-text geometry for each named styling zone, the long catwalk, audience area, and a three-place winners’ podium.

## Layout requirements
Place 10 free-text player start positions around the lobby and preparation zone without blocking styling stations. Use `trigger-scoring` for the runway presentation/voting area and results handoff. Place exactly three winner positions on the podium, ordered first at center, second and third to either side. Site one model-entry trigger at the head of the catwalk and sequential presentation marks along it. Apartments and future themed maps are out of scope for this active scene.

## Layout components
Build a luxurious indoor mall around one central lobby and one long runway hall. Put the model entrance at one end, the catwalk through the center, audience viewing along both sides, and the three-place podium at the focal end. Arrange distinct shopping and styling zones for clothing, body, hair, makeup, accessories, and shoes around broad mall walkways, with separate designer and photo studios nearby. Keep all 10 players’ circulation clear and frame the map to reveal the lobby, styling ring, and runway axis.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the luxury fashion identity first, then derive the clear single-level plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is large, driven by 10-player capacity and nine named support zones around the runway. The theme is glamorous luxury-mall fashion. Use `P0`; this launch map is one continuous indoor surface, while separate apartments and future maps are not included.
