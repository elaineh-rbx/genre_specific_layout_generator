# Scene prompt
Create a peaceful cottagecore outdoor setting with a cottage as scenery nestled in a forest full of pastel flowers. Keep the cottage exterior-only, surrounded by walkable forest ground and flower-filled clearings for viewing the cottage, trees, and blooms.

# Agent decision

## Genre
This is `No Genre`: it is an environment showcase for peaceful walking and looking, with no scoring, opponents, rounds, or other genre-owned activity.

## Shape and preset
Use the Explorable Place preset. No canonical genre shape applies, so keep all five no-genre axes at their defaults: exterior enclosure, single-surface verticality, one zone, dressed rather than structure-critical topology, and grounded play-space. The cottage is the visual anchor within one contiguous forest setting.

## Config requirements
Use `landmark-focal` for the distinct cottage exterior, visible through framed gaps in the trees. Use `path-circulation` for gentle walkable forest paths connecting the clearings and cottage viewpoints. Use `vignette-photo` for composed flower-filled clearings that frame the cottage, pastel blooms, and tree canopy. Do not use `building-interior`, because the cottage is scenery only.

## Layout requirements
Nothing must be recovered as functional layout placement after segmentation. The brief calls for no checkpoints, pickups, trigger volumes, teleporters, or gameplay spawns.

## Layout components
Build one medium woodland zone with the exterior-only cottage nestled near its visual center, offset enough to create layered approach views. Thread soft, curving dirt paths through pastel flower clearings and around clusters of trees, with several wider viewing pockets facing the cottage. Keep the ground continuously walkable and mostly gentle, using grass, moss, leaf litter, and dense pastel blooms. Trees and undergrowth form a natural outer boundary without making the setting feel boxed in. Compose the cottage as the dominant focal point, revealed between foreground trunks and flowers.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the atmospheric cottagecore view first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down plan because visual composition matters more than topology here.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a medium scale, large enough for a short peaceful walk and multiple clearings but still intimate around one cottage. The theme is soft pastel cottagecore woodland. Route through `P0`; one exterior, single-surface map requires no additional pipeline modifier.
