# Scene prompt
Create a 3D elemental-fantasy world divided into four distinct nations, each representing a different natural element. Build them as one connected, traversable map, with all four nations kept at roughly the same small scale for a balanced playable world.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Action, with Adventure secondary. Bending combat is the active challenge, while travel through and discovery of four nations gives the fight space an exploratory world structure.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, taken from outside Action’s usual shape set, as one contiguous world split into four equally small elemental nations. The preset is none: Open World Action assumes stacked urban combat geometry, while this prompt specifies balanced elemental regions and does not ask for cover or chokepoints.

## Config requirements
Use `path-trail` for a continuous traversal network joining all four nations without portals or separate maps. Use `spawn-protected` as a visible neutral arrival court screened from immediate bending combat. Beyond those necessities, do not invent settlements, interiors, NPC populations, hazards, or water merely because particular elements might suggest them. Each nation’s visible geometry should express its own natural element while preserving equal footprint and traversal value.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `spawn-protected` once at a neutral central junction, outside direct combat sightlines but connected evenly to all four regions. No enemy emitters, power-up pickups, checkpoints, or objective volumes are established by the brief, so none are placed. Preserve four comparable region footprints and ensure every nation can be reached from the shared connection network without crossing another nation as a mandatory gateway.

## Layout components
Build one connected, bounded world divided into exactly four small nations. Arrange the nations as four balanced regions around a neutral center, with a clear path network linking adjacent regions and direct routes back to the central arrival. Give each region a distinct elemental terrain language, silhouette, ground treatment, and landmark-scale focal feature, but keep combat surfaces readable and free from incidental trip geometry. Use transitional border strips where neighboring elemental treatments meet rather than detached islands or teleport seams. Enclose the whole first map with a coherent distant natural boundary. Compose the view so all four regions have comparable visual weight and the central connection is immediately legible.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: equal regional area, complete connectivity, and balanced approaches should be fixed in the authoritative plan before the elemental isometric treatment is added.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium: four individually small nations combine into one balanced first world. The theme is original stylized elemental fantasy, avoiding copied franchise architecture or characters. Use `P0`; the brief requires one connected surface and does not force overhangs, interiors, separate maps, or volumetric play.
