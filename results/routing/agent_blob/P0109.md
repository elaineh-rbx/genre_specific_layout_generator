# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map set inside a single large, exaggerated cartoony supermarket called Tops Supermarket. The focused interior is one open shop floor, with long grocery aisles and product shelves arranged in rows, clear cross-aisles between them, and open areas around the store perimeter for shoppers.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Shooter, with no secondary genre: the author describes shoppers shooting one another until all opponents are down.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single`, taken from outside Shooter’s usual shape set because the entire map is one supermarket interior and nobody goes outside. The preset is none; swapping to a single interior while omitting team bases and objective zones leaves no published shooter preset recognisable.

## Config requirements
Use `cover-los` by treating long grocery shelves as alternating waist-high end displays and full-height sightline blockers. Use `path-loop` for cross-aisles and perimeter lanes that give every aisle at least two exits rather than creating dead-end shelf canyons. Show `spawn-protected` as screened shopper entry pockets around the perimeter, concealed by endcaps and promotional displays. Include the “Tops Supermarket” name prominently over the checkout-side wall.

## Layout requirements
Place functional `spawn-protected` markers in several separated perimeter pockets, each facing into an open cross-aisle and shielded from direct views from other entry pockets. No team bases, capture volumes, loot placements, or hostile NPC emitters are required. Keep all gameplay on the single shop-floor surface.

## Layout components
Build one large rectangular supermarket hall with parallel grocery aisles, regularly spaced cross-aisles, and a continuous circulation lane around the perimeter. Include checkout counters and the Tops Supermarket sign along one end, colorful product shelving through the center, broad produce and promotional islands near the front, and open shopper circulation areas around the walls. Shelf rows provide cover but never seal movement; cross-aisles reconnect them into loops. Solid exterior walls enclose the entire play space, with no stockroom or parking-lot extension. Compose from a raised diagonal view that shows the store name, aisle grid, cross-connections, and perimeter openings.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the connected aisle network, sightline breaks, and separated spawn pockets must be established before the authoritative cartoony isometric dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by one large shop floor rather than multiple store zones. The theme is exaggerated, bright, cartoony supermarket retail. Use `P0`: this is one enclosed, single-level surface with no exterior transition or overhang.
