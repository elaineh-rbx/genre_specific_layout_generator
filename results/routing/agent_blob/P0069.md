# Scene prompt
Create a 3D chess game scene centered on a standard 8x8 chessboard in a classic wood-and-marble style. Each square is a clear raised tile, with two opposing full chess armies arranged in their standard starting ranks; every piece is a Roblox-style avatar dressed and shaped to clearly read as its chess role. Place the board in the middle of a small, simple room with open space around all sides for spectators, keeping the board as the visual focus.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Strategy because users command chess pieces by selecting board squares rather than navigating avatars through the scene.

## Shape and preset
Use `board-grid` for a tabletop-scale chess board that players act on without walking through it. Match the Board & Card Games preset exactly, with its regular playing grid specialized to standard chess.

## Config requirements
Apply `tile-grid` as exactly 64 clear raised tiles in a standard 8-by-8 alternating pattern. Draw exactly 32 Roblox-avatar chess pieces in two opposing armies, each dressed and shaped to read immediately as king, queen, bishop, knight, rook, or pawn while remaining an avatar. Use classic wood for the set and restrained marble for the room and board accents. Keep the surrounding room simple and the board visually dominant.

## Layout requirements
Place all 32 avatar pieces on their exact standard starting squares: eight pawns and eight back-rank pieces per side, with correct mirrored role order and opposing colors. Each of the 64 tiles must expose one selectable destination socket centered on the square. Leave a clear spectator ring around all four sides of the board, but add no avatar player spawn or walking route because gameplay is cursor-directed.

## Layout components
Build one small, simple enclosed room around a centrally placed square chessboard. The board consists of an exact 8-by-8 array of raised, equally sized tiles with clean shared edges and no gaps. Arrange the two complete armies across their standard two starting ranks at opposite ends. Maintain open floor on every side as spectator clearance, with plain walls and minimal decor that frame rather than compete with the set. Use a symmetrical composition, center the board in both plan and frame, and keep every square and piece silhouette readable from an elevated camera.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, then dress the isometric view from that plan, because an exact 8-by-8 grid and correct starting-square placement are essential to chess.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by one board and its spectator clearance. The theme is classic wood-and-marble chess with Roblox-avatar pieces. Use `P0` with `SET`: the scene is operated from outside rather than traversed, so traversal and jump-gap checks are skipped.
