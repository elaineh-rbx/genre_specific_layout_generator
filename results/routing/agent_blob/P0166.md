Use visible key-item receptacles, pressable controls, clue surfaces, and successive locked barriers to structure forward progress. The final main gate is the unmistakable escape exit and opens outward beyond the map. Keep the concrete, steel bars, security doors, and institutional lighting clean and contemporary, without horror fog, flicker, or decay.
