# Scene prompt

Create a 3D social music map as a small, single-room modern neon nightclub. Arrange dance areas, a stage, and sofa seating within the same open club space, with colorful neon lighting creating a lively social music atmosphere.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Entertainment: this is a social music hangout organized around dancing and a stage, without a competitive objective.

## Shape and preset

Use `venue-stage` from the Performance Venue preset. Adapt it to one intimate open nightclub room where the dance areas face the stage and sofa groups sit around the edges. Omit raked audience seating because informal sofas and standing dance space are explicitly requested.

## Config requirements

Draw `social-hub` as the nightclub’s open central gathering floor, divided visually into dance areas without walls that fragment the room. Draw the visible half of `spawn-first-reveal` as an entrance composition looking across the neon dance floor toward the stage. Include colorful neon strips, club lights, a compact raised stage, and several sofa groupings along the perimeter, keeping all social areas within the same room.

## Layout requirements

Place the layout half of `spawn-first-reveal` at the club entrance, oriented toward the stage and central dance floor rather than toward a side wall or seating back. No song-rate limits, audio moderation, interface controls, scoring volumes, checkpoints, or teleport markers belong in the spatial layout.

## Layout components

Build one small enclosed club room with a compact raised stage along one wall, open dance areas across the center, and sofa seating grouped around the remaining perimeter. Maintain wide walking lanes between the entrance, dance floor, stage frontage, and sofas so the room remains social rather than compartmentalized. Use low tables, speaker stacks, ceiling light bars, colored spotlights, and neon wall graphics as supporting props. Keep the floor level and the boundary fully enclosed. Compose the room so the stage anchors the far side while the sofas frame the dance activity without blocking sightlines.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render the authoritative neon atmosphere and room composition first, then derive the top-down plan because exact topology is simple and not gameplay-critical.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, specified as one club-sized room. The theme is modern neon nightclub with colorful social lighting. Use `P0`; this is a single-level interior with no outside transition, overhang, or separate map.
