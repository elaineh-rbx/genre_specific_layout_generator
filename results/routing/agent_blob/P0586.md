# Scene prompt
Create a simple test-place map on a small baseplate, with a small giant keyboard centered on it. Arrange about 12 raised, clickable keycaps in a compact keyboard grid.

# Agent decision
## Genre
This is `No Genre`: the author describes a simple interaction test place, not a game with scoring, progression, rounds, enemies, or a finish.

## Shape and preset
No genre shape is assigned. Keep all routing axes at their defaults: exterior, single-surface, single-zone, dressed structure, and grounded-surface play. The raised keycaps are objects on the baseplate rather than a separate stacked play level. The preset is none because paths, landmarks, social gathering areas, boundaries, and collectibles would pad the deliberately simple test.

## Config requirements
The image config contains a small plain baseplate and one small giant-keyboard model centered on it. The keyboard has about 12 raised keycaps arranged in a compact, orderly grid, with clear gaps and individual key outlines so segmentation can recover each cap separately. Add no desk scenery, buildings, terrain, vegetation, or unrelated props.

## Layout requirements
Place about 12 freeform clickable interaction targets, exactly one aligned to the top surface of each segmented keycap. Each target must also respond when the player physically touches its keycap. No scoring volume, checkpoint, pickup, spawner, teleporter, or other gameplay marker is needed.

## Layout components
Build one small square baseplate with a flat, uniform surface. Center a compact keyboard body on it and arrange about 12 evenly sized raised keycaps in a small rectangular keyboard grid. Keep the keyboard low enough to remain approachable from the surrounding baseplate, with open walking room on every side. Use a plain outer baseplate edge as the boundary. Compose the keyboard centrally from a high three-quarter angle so all keycaps remain distinct and countable.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the authoritative raised keyboard form first, then derive the straightforward top-down placement plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by the small baseplate and about 12 keys. The theme is a clean, plain Studio test environment. The route is `P0` because all geometry fits on one exterior surface without topology-critical structure.
