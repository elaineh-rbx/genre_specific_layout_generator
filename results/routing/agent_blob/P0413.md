# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] scale — Q: How far is the walk from town to the forest edge - a few seconds, or a real journey? A: A few seconds on foot; the forest should begin just beyond the town via the dirt path so the woodcutting loop is immediately playable.
- [author] theme — Q: Medieval is stated for the buildings; should the forest read the same storybook way, or more naturalistic? A: Use a polished, naturalistic pine forest that complements the medieval town, with appealing stylized Roblox detail rather than an exaggerated storybook look.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a compact polished 3D resource-gathering map with two contiguous zones: a small medieval-style town center and a nearby naturalistic pine forest. A clear dirt path leaves the town and reaches the forest edge within a few seconds on foot. The forest contains at least 20 detailed pine-tree positions across grass and gently uneven terrain, with rocks and bushes between them. Each pine has a brown textured trunk, several layers of green branches, a sturdy ground contact, and slight variation in height, width, and rotation. Keep the town and forest visually distinct but close, with appealing stylized Roblox detail. Do not add shops, mines, trading, combat, quest areas, or other systems.

## Genre
The genre is Simulation because the repeatable shared woodcutting and persistent resource-gathering loop defines the game.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, the Simulation shape Zone (Shared Persistent World), because the town and forest are contiguous common ground with no personal plots. Preset is none: Mining & Extraction would imply the explicitly forbidden mining system and vehicle hauling routes.

## Config requirements
Use `resource-shared` for the multiplayer pine forest where all players act on the same trees. Use `path-circulation` for the short dirt path connecting town spawn to the forest edge. Use universal `settlement-density` for the compact medieval town center. Use universal `terrain-relief` for the forest's gentle uneven ground. Keep the at-least-20 tree models scene-specific.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` as at least 20 tree resource nodes distributed through reachable forest ground, each with one fixed location reused after its 30-second respawn. Keep enough clearance around every trunk for players to approach and swing an axe. Place one free-text SpawnZone in the town center facing the dirt path. The held axe, UI, hit timing, health, rewards, sounds, particles, and persistence are mechanics rather than map placements.

## Layout components
The top-level contiguous map contains two zone segments: Town Center, a compact medieval spawn settlement, and Pine Forest, a larger natural resource field. One dirt path connects them directly. The forest uses grass, rocks, bushes, gentle relief, and at least 20 varied pine trees. The boundary remains compact, with the forest beginning just beyond town. Frame both zones, the short path, and individual tree spacing in one readable view.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the polished naturalistic forest and medieval-town composition lead, and the direct path and open resource field need no computed topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale, corresponding to Block, because trees and their approach clearances must remain legible while town and forest fit together. The theme is a polished medieval town beside a naturalistic stylized pine forest. Use `tiered` for gentle uneven terrain without overhang; no other modifier is forced.
