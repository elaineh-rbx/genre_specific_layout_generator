# Scene prompt

Create a polished 3D Roblox map for a woodcutting game loop. A small medieval-style town center forms the spawn area, clearly separated from a naturalistic pine forest; a dirt path leads from town to the forest edge only a few seconds away on foot. The forest contains at least 20 detailed pine trees distributed across grassy, uneven terrain with rocks and bushes, with each tree grounded naturally and varied slightly in height, width, and rotation. Pine trees have brown textured trunks and layered green branches. Place the visible axe tool and woodcutting interaction space around the trees; keep the town and nearby forest as the complete playable area, with no mines, shops, combat zones, trading spaces, or quest areas.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Simulation because the map supports a repeatable multiplayer woodcutting job loop in a persistent shared world.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open` in its Simulation wording: one compact common map where every player shares the town and forest, with no personal plots or instanced areas. The preset is Role Sim, retaining its repeatable task stations and circulation while dropping vehicle roads.

## Config requirements

Apply `trigger-task` to the visible chopping spaces around the pine trees, `resource-shared` to the communal forest, and `path-circulation` to the short dirt path from spawn town to resource ground. Apply `settlement-density` as a small medieval town center, not scattered buildings, and `terrain-relief` as gently uneven grassy forest ground. Do not add the explicitly excluded mines, shops, trading, combat, or quest areas.

## Layout requirements

Place one player spawn marker in the town center beside the visible axe tool. Place at least 20 `trigger-task` interaction areas and matching `collectible-nodes`, each aligned to one segmented pine-tree base with clear standing room around it. Distribute them throughout the shared forest rather than the town or path. The same tree positions serve all players.

## Layout components

Create two adjacent zones: a small medieval spawn town and a larger naturalistic pine forest, separated clearly but connected by one short dirt path only a few seconds long. The forest contains at least 20 pines with brown textured trunks, layered green branches, sturdy grounded bases, and slight variation in height, width, and rotation. Spread them across gently uneven grass with rocks and bushes while keeping walkable chopping clearances. Keep the axe visible near spawn. Bound the complete map with denser trees and low natural slopes, and compose the scene so town, path, and forest progression read together.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first as authoritative, then derive top-down; environmental polish and the immediate visual relationship between town and forest lead.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a small scale because the complete loop is intentionally compact, despite requiring at least 20 trees. The theme is polished medieval Roblox architecture beside a naturalistic stylized pine forest. Apply `tiered` for gentle uneven terrain with no overhangs; no other modifier is forced.
