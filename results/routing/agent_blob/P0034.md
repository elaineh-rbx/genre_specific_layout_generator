## Enriched image prompt
Create a focused survival map spanning a few connected city blocks, seen at the scale of a cat. The city has been abandoned for decades: vines and trees overtake cracked streets, facades decay, and debris breaks up the roads. Build a continuous network of streets, alleys, courtyards, fence gaps, and low ground-floor cutouts with no dead ends, so a small cat can keep moving while evading stray dogs and other predators. Distribute sheltered hiding recesses that break sightlines and place readable food and water scavenging spots away from the most exposed intersections. Keep the playable space at street level, bounded by blocked roads and dense ruined structures, with no full building interiors. Frame all connected blocks and their looping escape routes clearly.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Survival.

## Shape and preset
Use `warren-looping`, a Path (Looping Warren), because predator evasion requires interconnected routes with zero dead ends. No preset cleanly combines ongoing resource survival with a compact urban chase network, so the preset is none.

## Config requirements
Use `cover-los` for cat-sized recesses, broken wall cutouts, parked debris, and vegetation that interrupt predator sightlines. The hunger, thirst, and indefinite survival loop are mechanics; their physical footprint is handled by scavenging placements below.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` as Collectible food and water scavenging points distributed across every block, with some in sheltered side routes and none concentrated at one location. Place `spawner-npc` as separated Spawner origins for stray dogs and other predators along exposed outer streets, away from the cat’s initial safe recess.

## Layout components
The map contains a few connected blocks, a zero-dead-end street and alley network, several courtyards and fence gaps, cat-sized hiding cutouts, overgrown facades, cracked roads, debris, and natural blocked-road boundaries. Every block has at least two route connections so a fleeing cat cannot be trapped by topology. Keep the full network readable in frame.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: make the route plan authoritative because a dead end would make predator evasion unfair, then dress the isometric view from that connected plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is large because several blocks must fit while cat-sized hiding and scavenging locations remain legible. The theme is a decades-abandoned, heavily overgrown urban ruin. The modifier is `P6` because the looping route network must be valid by construction.
