# Scene prompt
Create a small, colorful toy-battlefield map reused across waves, with a central open build area where one player constructs a simple base from paper-like blocks. Leave open ground on every side of the base so tan toy soldiers can approach from different angles. Arrange the battlefield as a compact tabletop-scale outdoor play space with clear sightlines, toy-style terrain, and room for placed toy soldiers and traps around the defenses.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Strategy. This is open-ground base defense: the player builds a defensive position while enemy waves path dynamically toward it, rather than following a fixed tower-defense lane.

## Shape and preset
Use `terrain-open`, with a central defensible territory and unobstructed approach ground around it. The closest preset is Base Defense, retaining its open-terrain shape and wave-origin concept while omitting fixed destructible scenery and line-of-sight blockers that would contradict the requested clear sightlines.

## Config requirements
Use `buildzone-plot` for broad, flat, open land around the center where the player can place paper blocks, toy soldiers, and traps. Use the visible half of `tracker-core` as the central base position and make its toy-block construction visually legible without prebuilding the player's final defense. No extra terrain relief, water, interiors, or ambient population is requested.

## Layout requirements
Place `tracker-core` at the exact center as the defended objective anchor. Place four `spawner-npc` wave origins just beyond the playable perimeter, one on each side, with clear routes across open ground toward the center; the four-sided arrangement concretely realizes attacks from different angles without a fixed lane.

## Layout components
Build one compact square battlefield with a broad central base-building zone occupying roughly the middle third. Surround it with an open approach ring extending evenly to all four edges. Keep the ground mostly flat, colorful, and tabletop-like, with toy-grass or play-mat materials and only low decorative edging that preserves sightlines. Reserve clear placement room immediately around the center for paper-block walls, toy defenders, and traps. Enclose the map with a low toy-box or game-board rim, while keeping enemy entry points readable at the four sides. Frame the whole battlefield as a clean, centered toy diorama.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the colorful toy-battlefield look first, then derive the top-down plan, because the open approach geometry does not require a procedurally valid lane.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by the single-player, single-map brief. The theme is colorful high-quality toy warfare with paper blocks and tabletop materials. The route is `P0`; one flat, contiguous surface preserves all required geometry in a single top-down capture.
