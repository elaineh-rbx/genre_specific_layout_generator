# Scene prompt
Create a small stylized tabletop strategy board, about 5 hexes across, viewed from an elevated top-down/isometric camera. Arrange colorful terrain hexes in a compact hexagonal grid, with distinct resource terrain types and 3–4 visibly graded production tiers expressed through increasing hex height, brightness, glow, or particle density; high and rare tier hexes are sparse. Place buildable junctions at hex corners and routes along shared edges for simple wooden-style settlement, city, and road pieces. Keep the board clean and readable as a single-player-visible prototype, with a neutral tabletop surround framing the terrain.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Strategy: this is a real-time resource, settlement, and road-building board game governed by spatial adjacency.

## Shape and preset
Use `board-grid` for a tabletop-scale hex board that players operate from an elevated camera rather than walk through. Match the Board & Card Games preset, adapting its playing spaces from squares to hexes as the prompt explicitly requires.

## Config requirements
Apply `tile-grid` as a compact hexagonal playing board about 5 hexes across, with evenly aligned colorful terrain cells. Distinguish each resource terrain type clearly and show 3–4 production tiers through progressively stronger brightness, glow, and restrained particle density; keep high and rare tiers visibly sparse. Draw simple wooden-style settlement, city, and road pieces as the board's build vocabulary, and include a clean neutral tabletop surround.

## Layout requirements
Place a buildable junction socket at every unique hex corner and a road socket along every unique shared hex edge, preserving correct adjacency and eliminating duplicates where neighboring cells meet. Associate each hex with its resource type and one of the 3–4 visible production tiers. No avatar spawn, traversal checkpoint, NPC emitter, or walkable trigger volume is needed because interaction occurs from the elevated board view.

## Layout components
Build one compact hexagonal board zone about 5 hexes across. Its terrain cells form one clean contiguous cluster centered on a neutral tabletop. Every hex edge aligns exactly with its neighbor; corner junctions remain unobstructed for wooden settlement and city pieces, while shared edges have continuous road routes. Vary terrain color and iconographic surface treatment by resource, then use glow and particle intensity rather than major height changes for tier readability. Keep rare-tier cells sparse and distributed. Leave a consistent tabletop margin around the board and compose it for immediate top-down legibility.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, then dress the isometric view from that plan, because valid hex adjacency, shared edges, and corner junctions are essential to play.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, fixed by a board about 5 hexes across. The theme is colorful stylized tabletop terrain with wooden pieces. Use `P0` with `SET`: it is real geometry nobody walks on, so traversal and jump-gap checks are skipped.
