# Scene prompt

Create three distinct military-themed combat maps for players to choose between: one open map with broad sightlines and sparse cover, one close-quarters map built from tightly connected structures and narrow passages, and one map with elevated areas and multiple vertical routes.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Shooter, specifically a two-team timed deathmatch.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-staged`, with three isolated match stages selected from outside the active arena. This comes from outside Shooter’s usual shape list because the three-map vote and teleport structure matters more than forcing all choices into one combat topology. The preset is Team Deathmatch, retaining its balanced team-base and combat-cover logic while substituting the multi-stage shape.

## Config requirements

Use `spawn-teambase` for two visually protected military deployment bases on every map, `cover-los` for sparse field cover on the open map and denser cover elsewhere, `choke-bottleneck` for the close-quarters passages, `path-loop` for multiple connections through its structures, and `cover-elevated` for reachable towers, platforms, and firing nests on the vertical map.

## Layout requirements

Place both sides of `spawn-teambase` on each map: six team spawn zones total, one opposing pair per stage, each behind sightline-breaking geometry. No pickups, scoring volumes, or NPC emitters are needed because weapons and kill scoring are supplied by gameplay rather than world placement.

## Layout components

Build three separate medium combat maps. The open map is a broad training field with long sightlines, sparse barricades, sandbag lines, and opposing bases at the far ends. The close-quarters map is a compact military compound of tightly connected structures, narrow passages, looped junctions, and contested pinch points. The vertical map has a ground combat area plus elevated platforms, towers, stairs, ramps, and several alternate routes between levels. Enclose each stage with military perimeter walls or terrain berms and frame each as a complete standalone battlefield.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render each battlefield’s dressed military composition first, then derive its top-down plan; visual distinction between the three choices is authoritative.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The overall scale is large because it contains three medium maps. The theme is modern military training grounds and fortified compounds. Use `P4` because the choices are separate maps and `P2` because the vertical stage has overhanging elevated routes.
