# Scene prompt
Create a 3D story-adventure map set in Brightview, a compact, walkable few-block town with a cheerful cyan, magenta, and yellow visual identity, colorful performance imagery, broadcast motifs, and recurring 13-pointed stars. Arrange an arcade, theater, shopping district, school, museum, observatory, rooftop routes, and a central broadcast tower close together along connected streets and plazas; make the arcade, theater, school, museum, and observatory compact, enterable interiors built as focused spaces. Place maintenance-tunnel entrances around town, leading into deeper maintenance tunnels and an underground Network beneath Brightview. Include hidden rooms, secret dream-like areas, a rare black void room with a single red tree, and a concealed room containing only one chair. Later, reuse this same town layout as a distorted collapsed variant with floating buildings, broken textures, glitches, altered traversal, and a sky filled with CMY 13-pointed stars around the activated broadcast tower.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Adventure is dominant, with Puzzle secondary. This is a chaptered story journey through one recurring town, with secrets and environmental puzzles supporting discovery.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-underground`, taken from outside Adventure’s usual shapes, because Brightview’s surface, rooftop routes, maintenance tunnels, and deep Network form linked vertical layers. Start from the Exploration preset, substituting this layered shape while retaining its landmarks, trails, and secrets.

## Config requirements
Use `landmark-focal` for the central broadcast tower, `path-trail` for connected streets and rooftop routes, `alcove-secret` for hidden rooms, and `building-interior` for the five named enterable buildings. Add `facade-clue` for recurring stars, broadcast symbols, and environmental puzzle surfaces. Draw the black void room with one red tree, the one-chair room, dream-like secret areas, and a distorted reuse of the same town geometry with floating buildings.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly 13 `collectible-nodes` across streets, interiors, rooftops, tunnels, and secret rooms. Place `checkpoint-respawn` markers at major chapter thresholds and before deep Network sections. Site uncatalogued transition triggers at tunnel entrances, compact interiors, and secret rooms while preserving correspondence between the normal and collapsed town variants.

## Layout components
Build a compact few-block town centered on the broadcast tower. Cluster the arcade, theater, shopping district, school, museum, and observatory along short connected streets and plazas; link five compact interiors to their facades. Add rooftop paths above, multiple maintenance entrances below, and branching tunnels descending to the Network. Conceal dream rooms, the red-tree void, and the single-chair room off secondary routes. The collapsed variant must retain this plan while lifting buildings and breaking traversal.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, then dress the isometric view; linked surface, interior, rooftop, and underground routes must remain navigable across both variants.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use large scale despite the compact footprint because it includes many interiors and layered networks. Theme: cheerful CMY performance town degrading into CRT-glitch unease. Route with `P2` for overhanging layers and floating buildings and `P3` for outside-to-interior transitions.
