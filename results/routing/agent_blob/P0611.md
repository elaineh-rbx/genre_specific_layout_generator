# Scene prompt
Create a compact royal-themed driving simulator map set in a small palace and its grounds, sized for about one minute of driving from edge to edge. Arrange the palace as the central landmark with drivable paths and open grounds around it, and include three cars placed in the map.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation. It is a free-driving vehicle simulator, with no stated lap, finish line, or competitive race.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, a small shared palace estate that players may drive around in any order. The preset is Vehicle Sim, retaining its open vehicle world and route network while dropping the unrelated shared resource field.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for vehicle-width palace drives connecting the arrival area, forecourt, gardens, and outer grounds. The image must include the palace as the central landmark and exactly three distinct cars positioned in usable open areas. Do not add `building-interior`, because the brief asks for the palace and grounds but never says players enter the building.

## Layout requirements
No canonical post-segmentation option is required. Place exactly three vehicle spawn markers, one aligned to each visible car position, with road clearance and forward orientation. Put one player arrival marker in the palace forecourt near the cars but outside their driving path.

## Layout components
Build a compact royal estate that takes about one minute to cross by car. Set one palace centrally, fronted by a broad formal forecourt. A connected loop and several short branching drives pass through open lawns and simple formal garden areas before returning to the palace. Use gates, low walls, hedges, and a few statuary or fountain accents to establish the royal theme without cluttering the driving surface. Place the three cars together near the forecourt or distribute them among clearly reachable parking bays; preserve the exact count of three. Enclose the map with an estate wall and gates, and compose the palace as the dominant center of the frame.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the royal visual composition leads, and the simple connected drives can be projected into the authoritative top-down layout afterward.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by the one-minute edge-to-edge travel time. The theme is bright, readable royal palace grounds. Use `P0`; this is one outdoor, mostly flat, continuous surface without required interiors, overhangs, or separate maps.
