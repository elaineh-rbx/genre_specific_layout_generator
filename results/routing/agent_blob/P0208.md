# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map as a small, walkable black box room. Place lights in the top corners, with reachable light switches on the walls. The left wall contains a donation display area for game passes.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Simulation, specifically an idle incremental game with a deliberately minimal walkable set.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single`, taken from outside Simulation’s usual shape set, because the entire map is one enclosed room and the player never goes outside. Preset is none: Incremental Simulator would add storefront progression, circulation, and purchase gates that contradict the intentionally endless no-unlock brief.

## Config requirements
Use `trigger-task` in its visible form for reachable wall-mounted light switches, the only physical interaction in the room. Draw four corner lights at ceiling height, one in each top corner of the black box. Add one donation display area to the left wall for the author’s game passes, presented as a readable bank of panels rather than an enterable shop.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly four `trigger-task` interaction zones, one aligned with each reachable light switch, and associate each with its corresponding corner light. Keep every switch at player interaction height and reachable from the open floor. Place one ordinary player spawn marker on the floor facing into the room, clear of the left-wall donation display. No finish, unlock gate, collectible, checkpoint, or progression-zone placement is needed.

## Layout components
Build a small rectangular room with a flat black floor, four black walls, and a black ceiling enclosing the entire play space. Keep the central floor open so the player can walk among all walls. Put one light high in each of the four corners and one corresponding switch below at reachable height. Reserve the left wall for a single organized donation display area without blocking its switch or circulation. The room is fully bounded, sparse, and composed so an isometric cutaway can show the floor, four light positions, switches, and donation wall together.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative, then derive the top-down view, because the simple topology cannot fail and the important decision is the readable relationship between ceiling lights, wall switches, and donation display.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use small scale because the brief explicitly asks for a compact room the player can walk around. The theme is stark minimalist black-box idle space with practical corner lighting. Use `P0`: one enclosed room on one level requires no outside transition or occlusion pass.
