# Scene prompt

Create a stylized low-poly alpine snowboarding map: one long authored descent from a snowy summit to a warm chalet base village, assembled from stitched terrain chunks for varied pacing. A wide groomed corduroy piste forms the forgiving green main line, while blue and black routes branch into progressively riskier powder tree runs, steeper shortcuts, banked walls, cliff drops, cat-track gaps, and feature routes with kickers, boxes, rails, and a halfpipe section before rejoining lower on the mountain. Use near-white snow with cool blue-lavender shadows, chunky conifers capped with separate rounded snow geometry, and layered blue mountain silhouettes fading into a clean bright gradient sky. Place the village in the lower midground/base area with ochre wooden chalets, red-brown snow-capped roofs, amber windows, and chimney smoke; frame the descent with long soft blue-tinted low-sun shadows. Compose for a third-person chase view behind a chibi rider in a bright red-orange snowsuit, oversized helmet and goggles, riding a blue snowboard.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Racing: the rider controls speed down a finite mountain route toward a terminal base. The trick scoring does not change the spatial classification.

## Shape and preset

Use `route-point-to-point` for the summit-to-village descent, with branching green, blue, and black lines that repeatedly rejoin the same mountain route. The preset is Downhill Descent, expanded with the explicitly requested terrain-park features.

## Config requirements

Draw `hazard-kill` as cliff edges and dangerous off-piste boundaries, `marker-distance` as visible green, blue, and black route signs, and `terrain-relief` as one strongly descending alpine surface without overhangs. Include the groomed corduroy piste, powder pockets, snow-capped conifers, banked walls, cat-track gaps, kickers, boxes, rails, halfpipe, and warm chalet village exactly as described.

## Layout requirements

Place `checkpoint-respawn` recovery points at the summit start, before each major branch, and after each branch rejoins, aligned downhill to valid snow. Place one functional `trigger-finish` across the route at the base village entrance. These placements must support all three difficulty lines without introducing dead-end branches.

## Layout components

Build a summit start zone, a long descending mountain zone assembled from varied chunks, and a village finish zone. The green piste is the wide main spine; blue and black shortcuts leave it through powder, trees, steeper drops, and feature sections before reconnecting below. Keep terrain-park props beside or across their intended lines, bound the course with forest, cliff, and mountain silhouettes, and frame the full descent behind the chibi snowboarder.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: establish the authoritative downhill route and every branch/rejoin first, then dress the isometric view, because disconnected shortcuts would make the run invalid. This is not a circuit or maze, so it does not use an authored plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a large scale for one long mountain descent. The theme is low-poly alpine winter with cool blue-lavender snow and warm village accents. Apply `P6` for valid racing connectivity and `tiered` for strong descending relief with no overhanging surfaces.
