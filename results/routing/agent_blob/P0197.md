# Scene prompt
Create a 3D adventure map set inside a small, bright and cheerful kindergarten building. Arrange a handful of connected kindergarten rooms with colorful classroom furnishings and wall-mounted buttons that can be reached by a controllable drone. Include an unsettling monster presence within the otherwise cheerful interior.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure, as declared by the author, with Puzzle secondary because remote-controlled drone buttons are the spatial interaction around which the rooms are arranged.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single`, taken from outside Adventure’s usual shape set, because the entire map is one enclosed kindergarten building and the player never needs an exterior transition. No preset remains recognisable: this is a compact freeform interior rather than an open exploration world, guided journey, or full escape-room sequence.

## Config requirements
Use `button-solve` for colorful wall-mounted buttons positioned where a drone can approach them. Use `npc-population` for one non-hostile but unsettling monster occupying the cheerful kindergarten. Add the explicitly requested colorful classroom furniture and child-friendly wall decoration as scene-specific visible geometry without a canonical option.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `button-solve` at each wall-mounted button, sited with an unobstructed drone route and a clear viewing line from the adjoining player area; no exact button count was given. Place the functional half of `npc-population` once for the monster, in a room or connecting sightline where it can watch the player without blocking circulation.

## Layout components
Build a handful of connected rooms within one small kindergarten: colorful classrooms, a shared activity room, and short linking corridors. Doorways connect every room into an easily readable interior rather than a maze. Furnish the rooms with low tables, small chairs, toy shelves, play mats, cubbies, bright posters, and wall-mounted drone buttons. Flat floors and generous door openings preserve both player and drone movement. Exterior walls form a complete enclosed boundary. Compose the view to show several cheerful rooms at once while reserving a deeper doorway or corridor for the monster’s unsettling silhouette.

## Render order
Render in `isometric` order because the bright visual contrast and readable room dressing lead, while the simple connected plan can be derived from the image.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a small scale, driven by only a handful of rooms. The theme is saturated, cheerful kindergarten color with one uncanny monster presence. Route through `P0`; it is a single, flat interior with no outside transition, stacked geometry, or separate map.
