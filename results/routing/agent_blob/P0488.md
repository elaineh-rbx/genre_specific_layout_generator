# Scene prompt
Create a creepy game map that opens in a small, focused portion of a bedroom visible from the bed, where the player wakes at 1 a.m. during a phone call from Jasmine. After the call, transition into a bright flower field under a blue sky with fluffy clouds, contrasting the unsettling premise. The field is the central and only ending area, with Angel greeting the player there: Angel wears a pastel flower crown, oversized white hoodie, short pink skirt, arm warmers, and light purple socks. Include a beautiful koi pond within the flower field for a small feeding task; all five ending presentations take place in this same field rather than separate locations.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure because the game is a story-led sequence with choices, tasks, and multiple endings.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-chaptered`: the opening bedroom and the in-game flower field are genuinely separate maps joined by a narrative transition. The closest preset is Story, retaining its chapter-separation concept while omitting its unrelated one-way drops and cinematic exit.

## Config requirements
Use `gate-chapter` for the transition from the bedroom scene into the flower game. Use `npc-population` to draw Angel in the flower field with one pastel flower crown, oversized white hoodie, short pink skirt, arm warmers, and light purple socks. Use `water-body` for the beautiful koi pond. The field needs dense flowers, blue sky, fluffy clouds, and a deliberately bright pastel appearance; the bedroom remains a tightly framed, creepy 1 a.m. fragment visible from the bed.

## Layout requirements
Place one initial player spawn at the bed-facing edge of the bedroom fragment. Place one transition trigger at the game-start interaction, linked to the flower field. Place Angel's single character marker near the field arrival point, one koi-feeding interaction marker at the pond edge, and one shared ending-presentation zone in the field supporting five ending states rather than five separate locations.

## Layout components
Build two maps: a small bedroom vignette containing the bed, phone-call context, and game-start setup, and a medium open flower field. In the field, place Angel near arrival, one accessible koi pond beside a short flower-lined path, and one clear central presentation area for all five endings. The bedroom is enclosed and tightly composed; the field has an open scenic boundary beneath the sky.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative, then derive top-down views, because atmosphere and the contrast between creepy bedroom and pastoral field lead the design.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium overall: one tiny vignette plus one central field. The theme is pastel dreamscape with creeping digital horror. Use `P4` for the two separate maps and `CHECK` for the koi pond's water play volume.
