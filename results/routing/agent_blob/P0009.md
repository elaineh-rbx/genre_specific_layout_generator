# Scene prompt
Create a compact 3D city map made of a few connected city blocks for free movement with powers and NPCs. Arrange streets and sidewalks between city buildings, with accessible rooftops and vertical routes designed for flight-based exploration; populate the ground-level city spaces with non-hostile NPCs.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The genre is Adventure (`adventure`): free exploration and powered traversal through the city are the activity, with no combat, PvP, or hostile NPC loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `traversal-city`, taken from Adventure’s typical shapes: a compact city crossed at street, rooftop, and airborne levels, with streets as the fallback route. The preset is none because the prompt specifies a traversal space but does not request the landmarks, secrets, collectibles, or story gates bundled by the available Adventure presets.

## Config requirements
Draw `path-trail` as a connected traversal network spanning sidewalks, streets, roof edges, exterior stairs, and vertical transitions between buildings. Draw `npc-population` as non-hostile city residents occupying ground-level public space. Apply `settlement-density` to form a compact cluster of a few connected urban blocks rather than isolated buildings. Also draw accessible flat rooftops, ledges, and clear aerial gaps that support flight-based exploration without adding combat arenas or hazards.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `npc-population` along sidewalks, corners, plazas, and building-front gathering areas at ground level, leaving launch, landing, and roof-edge routes unobstructed. No enemy spawners, scoring volumes, objectives, or PvP zones are needed.

## Layout components
Build a few connected city blocks with streets and sidewalks forming a continuous ground network between closely spaced buildings. Give each block reachable rooftops, exterior vertical connectors, ledges, and open gaps broad enough for flight. Link roof routes across neighboring blocks while preserving streets below as reliable fallback paths. Keep ground-level NPC gathering pockets beside the main circulation rather than inside it. Enclose the compact map with a simple city-edge boundary, and frame it obliquely so both street continuity and rooftop layering are visible.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the layered urban look first as the authority, then derive the top-down, because this is free exploration rather than topology whose validity determines a race, maze, or directed course.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by only a few compact blocks. The theme is a clean contemporary powers playground with readable traversal surfaces. Use `P2` because rooftops and elevated routes overhang streets, plus `CHECK` because flight creates a volumetric play space around those stacked surfaces.
