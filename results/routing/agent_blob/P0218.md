# Scene prompt
Create a 3D prison map with a walled central prison complex for guards and prisoners, including escape routes and weapon areas. Include a small exterior area beyond the perimeter walls for escapees to run through.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Shooter, canonical genre `shooter`, because armed guard-versus-prisoner conflict and team positioning define the requested loop. Roleplay & Avatar Sim, `roleplay-avatar-sim`, is secondary because the two sides inhabit distinct prison roles.

## Shape and preset
Use `open-battlefield` as one contiguous prison and exterior escape map with dispersed functional areas rather than mirrored lanes. The preset is none: the asymmetric prison, escape objective, and outside run area do not honestly match a standard deathmatch or tactical-raid bundle.

## Config requirements
Use `building-interior` for the prison buildings entered from the yard, `cover-los` for defensible barriers and cover in armed confrontation areas, `choke-bottleneck` for controlled doorways, gates, and escape breaches, and `station-loadout` for visible weapon racks in secured weapon areas. Use `spawn-teambase` for visually distinct, protected guard and prisoner starting areas, deliberately asymmetric rather than opposite mirrored bases.

## Layout requirements
Place two `spawn-teambase` zones: one shielded guard spawn beside a secure armory and one prisoner spawn inside the cell-block side of the compound. Place `station-loadout` interaction volumes directly on the weapon racks, restricted to their secure rooms. No exact weapon-rack or escape-route count was stated.

## Layout components
The central zone is a walled prison complex containing a prisoner cell block, guard-controlled area, yard, weapon areas, and connecting interior corridors. Multiple readable escape routes should lead from inside the compound through or beneath the perimeter to one small exterior run area beyond the walls. Gates and narrow thresholds create contested chokepoints, while yard fixtures, barriers, and corners provide cover without blocking navigation. The exterior should be visibly distinct but compact, bounded beyond the prison perimeter, and composed so the prison remains the dominant mass with the escape area readable behind it.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the prison’s visual hierarchy first, then derive the top-down plan; escape connectivity is important but does not require a computed maze or circuit.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, driven by one central compound plus a small exterior. The theme is grounded institutional prison realism. Use `P3` because play crosses between prison interiors and the yard and exterior.
