# Scene prompt

Create a 3D free-roam driving map set in a small, condensed Roblox-inspired New Zealand South Island suburban city, loosely inspired by eastern Christchurch around Buckleys Road without replicating real locations. Build a right-hand-traffic street network of broad suburban roads, intersections, side streets, parking areas, low-rise commercial buildings, homes, and generic city-edge spaces, with locations rearranged into an original layout. Include open roadside and parking areas suitable for optional NPC and player street-race meetups, plus a giant motorhome displayed as a notable vehicle/engine-related prop. The drivable vehicle roster includes copyright-free Iron Lion and Blue Wolf performance variants, utility vehicles, and starter sedan/coupe options, with visible detailed interiors for driver seating, dashboard controls, gear shifters, indicator stalks, pedals, and manual-transmission layouts.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Simulation, with Racing secondary because free-roam vehicle operation leads while optional NPC and player street races remain an explicit activity. Simulation therefore owns the shape and avoids applying Racing’s genre-wide route automatically.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open`, one condensed Shared Persistent World with freely navigable suburban streets. Match the Vehicle Sim preset, omit its unrelated resource field, and adapt its vehicle circulation to an urban road network. Add only the secondary Racing options needed for optional street-race routes.

## Config requirements

Use `path-circulation` for broad right-hand-traffic roads, intersections, side streets, parking areas, and race-meetup circulation. Use `settlement-density` for the compact low-rise suburban-commercial city fabric. Use `npc-population` for NPC racers gathered at roadside and parking meetups. Draw several meetup areas, `startpoint-line` markings on selected streets, terminal `trigger-finish` markings, and the giant motorhome as a focal vehicle prop. Show the copyright-free vehicle roster with detailed visible cabins; vehicle interiors do not constitute enterable building transitions.

## Layout requirements

Place NPC racer positions at every meetup. Pair each `startpoint-line` with one `trigger-finish` on a clear point-to-point route through existing streets, and place `checkpoint-respawn` recovery points at safe roadside pull-offs along those routes. Keep ordinary free-roam vehicle spawns in parking areas, separated from race launch slots.

## Layout components

Build one small, original city district inspired by eastern Christchurch but not replicating it. Broad arterial roads connect rearranged commercial strips, homes, side streets, parking lots, and city-edge open ground. Race meetups occupy generous roadside lots without becoming a separate circuit. Place the giant motorhome in a prominent display area. Vehicles include Iron Lion and Blue Wolf performance variants, utility vehicles, and starter sedan/coupe choices, with dashboards, shifters, stalks, pedals, and manual layouts visibly modeled. Keep all traffic geometry right-hand-drive oriented and frame the road hierarchy clearly.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, then dress the isometric image, because optional race routes must remain continuous through a functioning street network.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is small and condensed. The theme is a generic New Zealand South Island suburban city with performance-car culture. Use `P6` for the selected race starts, finishes, and recovery routes; no overhang, outside-to-inside building transition, or separate map is required.
