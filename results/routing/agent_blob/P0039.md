# Scene prompt
Create a 3D free-roaming wild horse simulator map set in a small Yellowstone-inspired wilderness, with natural terrain boundaries reached after about 2–3 minutes of riding.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Simulation because the author explicitly frames the experience as a wild horse simulator, even though it deliberately has no survival, breeding, taming, or herd progression loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open` for one shared persistent wilderness that the horse can roam in any direction. No Simulation preset matches free animal roaming without introducing vehicles, tasks, resource extraction, progression gates, or construction, so the preset is none.

## Config requirements
Use `terrain-relief` for broad Yellowstone-inspired slopes and low ridges that shape the riding ground and become natural-looking map limits without overhangs. The visible config otherwise stays intentionally simple: open grazing terrain, patches of conifer woodland, and readable natural gaps suited to horse movement. Do not add task stations, roads, shops, resource fields, or upgrade structures.

## Layout requirements
Nothing needs placement after segmentation. The brief explicitly excludes herd, survival, breeding, and taming systems and names no pickups, objectives, checkpoints, teleports, or repeatable task triggers.

## Layout components
Build one compact wilderness zone sized so a horse reaches its edge after about 2–3 minutes of riding. Arrange broad open meadows as the primary movement ground, with scattered conifer stands and gentle rolling slopes that preserve long, readable riding lines. Form the outer boundary from denser forest, rising ridges, and impassable rocky terrain rather than artificial walls. Keep the center open enough for free roaming and avoid narrow corridors. Compose the view wide and low, emphasizing the horse-scale expanse while allowing the complete natural enclosure to remain understandable.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the Yellowstone-inspired atmosphere and natural terrain composition are authoritative, and free roaming does not require structure-critical topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, specifically a 2–3-minute ride to the edge. The theme is approachable Yellowstone wilderness for a wild horse. Use `tiered` for visible terrain relief with no overlapping surfaces.
