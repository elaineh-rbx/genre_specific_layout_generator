## Enriched image prompt
Create a 3D free-roaming wild-horse map inspired by Yellowstone, sized so a horse reaches its natural-looking edge after about 2–3 minutes of riding. Compose one contiguous natural landscape of open grazing ground, sparse woodland, and gently raised terrain, with a visible ridge serving as orientation and the terrain itself closing the playable boundary. Keep it open for unrestricted exploration, without settlements, herd facilities, breeding areas, taming pens, or survival shelters.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, specifically Animal Sim because the player explores as a horse.

## Shape and preset
Use `wilderness-open`: the entire map is an open natural world with no settlement or personal property. The matched preset is Animal Sim, retaining its wilderness form and orientation landmark while dropping den and social-hub features contradicted by a solitary free-roaming horse experience.

## Config requirements
Use `terrain-relief` for natural rolling ground and the raised boundary ridge, with no cliffs or overhangs. Use `landmark-focal` for that distant ridge as the plain visual anchor across the open range.

## Layout requirements
No post-segmentation placement option is required; the brief specifies free roaming without task, herd, survival, breeding, or taming systems.

## Layout components
Open grazing terrain occupies the center, bordered by sparse woodland and gently rising ground that forms a natural edge instead of an artificial wall. The ridge remains visible from most of the map for orientation. Circulation is unrestricted across one continuous surface, and the whole 2–3-minute riding extent is composed in one isometric frame.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the natural landscape and its readable horizon lead, and free roaming does not require an authored route or maze topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large, driven by a 2–3-minute mounted crossing while preserving broad terrain features rather than small props. The theme is naturalistic Yellowstone-inspired wild range. The modifier is `tiered` for the rolling relief and raised boundary ridge; nothing overhangs, so no `P2` is needed.
