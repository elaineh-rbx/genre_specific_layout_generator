# Scene prompt
Create a bright, colorful, cute cartoon-style Pet Factory Tycoon hub with a central spawn plaza containing a tutorial NPC, daily-reward point, shops, trading plaza, leaderboards, and a portal area leading to ten separate world maps. Arrange 8 compact, easy-to-read player factory plots around or beyond the hub; each plot begins with a small conveyor and pet incubator area and has room for expanded machines, storage, upgrade stations, and collectors. Build the separate portal-accessed worlds as distinct named environments: Grassland, Desert, Snow, Volcano, Space, Cyber City, Fantasy, Heaven, Void, and a Secret Dimension, all retaining the same colorful cartoon visual style. Include themed egg-hatching areas or egg displays appropriate to the worlds, from starter and forest eggs through ice, lava, galaxy, mythic, and secret eggs.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The genre is Simulation. Factory automation, pet production, upgrades, rebirth, and world unlocks make this an incremental tycoon.

## Shape and preset
Use `hub-portals`, outside Simulation’s usual shapes, because one central plaza links ten genuinely separate maps. The preset is Incremental Simulator, with its shape replaced while preserving the shared progression hub, gates, and circulation.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for spawn plaza, shops, trading plaza, daily reward, leaderboards, and portal court. Use `gate-progression` for ten world portals unlocked through progression, `path-circulation` for routes from the hub to exactly 8 factory plots, and `trigger-task` for visible incubators, upgrade stations, collectors, and egg-hatching areas. Use `npc-population` for the tutorial NPC and its occupied teaching space.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly 8 factory claim and spawn positions, one at each compact plot entrance. The functional halves of `trigger-task` place incubator, upgrade, collection, and egg-hatching interactions against their visible stations. The functional half of `npc-population` places one tutorial NPC near spawn. Place ten paired portal departure and arrival markers, one for each named world.

## Layout components
Center a bright spawn plaza containing the tutorial point, daily reward, shops, trading space, leaderboards, and a readable ten-portal court. Arrange 8 compact factory plots around or just beyond it; each begins with a small conveyor and incubator and reserves orderly expansion space for machines, storage, upgrades, and collectors. Build ten separate maps—Grassland, Desert, Snow, Volcano, Space, Cyber City, Fantasy, Heaven, Void, Secret Dimension—with themed scenery and appropriate starter, forest, ice, lava, galaxy, mythic, or secret egg displays.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: exact plot count, hub circulation, portal pairing, and expansion footprints should lead before cartoon dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge because a hub and ten separate worlds accompany 8 plots. The theme is bright, cute, colorful cartoon pet-factory fantasy. Route `P4` because all ten worlds are separate portal-accessed maps.
