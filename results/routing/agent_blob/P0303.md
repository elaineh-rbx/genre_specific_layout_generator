# Scene prompt
Create a cozy, compact 500-stud pastoral village inspired by an idyllic fantasy hobbit settlement, nestled in a green valley with gentle grassy hills and surrounding nature. Arrange around 12 existing rounded earthen player burrows built into the hillsides, each with a small customizable garden and interior, connected by winding footpaths through vegetable plots and shared open village space. Keep the village dense, walkable, and inviting for social exploration.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Roleplay & Avatar Sim is dominant because the authored experience is an open-world social roleplay where players claim and personalize homes. Simulation is secondary because tending and trading garden produce supplies a defined farming task loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `settlement-claimable` for around 12 pre-built burrows that players claim rather than construct. Use the Life preset, keeping its claimable-home model and enterable interiors while replacing vehicle roads and commercial zoning with pedestrian village circulation.

## Config requirements
Use `building-interior` for each rounded earthen burrow’s customizable interior. Use `path-street` for winding pedestrian footpaths connecting homes, gardens, vegetable plots, and shared space. Use `social-hub` for the central open village gathering and vegetable-trading area. Use `trigger-task` in drawn form for each player’s small planting garden. Use `terrain-relief` for the gentle green valley and grassy hillsides, and `settlement-density` for a compact cluster of around 12 burrows.

## Layout requirements
Place around 12 claim markers, one at each existing burrow entrance, with ownership extending to that home’s garden and linked interior. Place the functional half of `trigger-task` once in each of those around 12 garden plots, sited beside its burrow and reached directly from the footpath. Place player arrivals in the shared village space without blocking trade or foot traffic.

## Layout components
Build one compact 500-stud valley village. Set around 12 rounded earthen burrows into gentle hillsides, each with one visible round entrance, a small garden, and an interior linked across the threshold. Wind narrow footpaths among vegetable plots and converge them on one welcoming shared green used for gathering and trade. Use grass, hedges, cultivated beds, flowers, and surrounding pastoral nature; avoid vehicle-scale roads. Bound the village with rising green slopes, trees, and denser natural vegetation. Compose the initial view across the central green toward layered hillside burrows, keeping multiple paths and round doors visible.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: all around 12 homes, gardens, footpaths, shared space, and access points must fit clearly inside the 500-stud village before scenic dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, fixed by the compact 500-stud footprint and around 12 homes. The theme is cozy pastoral fantasy with realistic earthen materials and idyllic greenery. Use `P3` for outside-to-burrow interior transitions and `tiered` for gentle hillside relief without stacked overhangs.
