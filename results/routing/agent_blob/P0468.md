# Scene prompt
Create a stylized, game-like 3D rugby arena with a compact rugby field about half the length and width of a regulation pitch. Keep the clear marked pitch, goal areas, and goalposts close together so play stays tightly contained.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The genre is Sports. The game is a compact team rugby match built around passing, tackling, and scoring on a marked field.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded` as a single flat rugby pitch with a rigid touchline perimeter. The preset is Field Sport, scaled down to the requested half-length and half-width arena.

## Config requirements
Draw `trigger-bounds` as clearly painted sidelines and dead-ball boundaries around the compact pitch. Draw `startpoint-play` as a prominent halfway line and center restart mark. Draw `trigger-scoring` as two in-goal areas with H-shaped posts at opposite ends. Use `marker-distance` for restrained rugby field markings that preserve an immediately readable playing surface.

## Layout requirements
Place one functional `trigger-bounds` perimeter directly on the painted field edge. Place one `startpoint-play` reset at the field center. Place four `trigger-scoring` functions: two broad try-detection zones covering the in-goal areas and two narrow kick-scoring planes between and above the crossbars, one pair at each end. These placements must be mirrored so neither team gains a spatial advantage.

## Layout components
Build one compact rectangular grass field about half the length and width of a regulation rugby pitch. Include two touchlines, a halfway line, two shallow in-goal zones, and two H-shaped goalposts placed close enough to keep play concentrated. Surround the field with a simple stylized arena apron and low enclosing structure, but keep the playing area visually dominant and free of decorative obstacles. Compose the view to show the complete pitch, both scoring ends, and the intentionally compressed distances.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the stylized arena first and derive its top-down plan afterward, since the field is a simple known rectangle and does not depend on complex topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, set by the explicit half-regulation dimensions and frequent-contact intent. The theme is clear, colorful, game-like rugby rather than photorealism. The route is `P0`; the layout is one flat exterior surface with no overhangs, interiors, or separate maps.
