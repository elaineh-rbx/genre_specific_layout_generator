# Scene prompt
Create a 3D pet-collecting adventure map set in a small, rugged mountain range. Three themed cave entrances are spaced a short walk apart across the mountains: a crystal cave, an icy cave, and a lava cave, each entrance visually hinting at its interior theme. Inside the caves, arrange explorable passages and chambers containing rare crystals to discover, with darker areas in the deeper sections.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure, with Simulation secondary because crystal extraction feeds the pet-hatching and upgrade loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-underground`: one shared mountain surface connects to explorable underground passages and chambers. This shape comes from outside Adventure’s usual set because the defining space spans surface and cave layers. The closest preset is Scavenger Hunt, retaining its discovery structure while substituting the underground shape.

## Config requirements
Use `path-trail` as a short mountain walking route connecting all three entrances. Add `alcove-secret` as darker side chambers that reward cave exploration. Draw `terrain-relief` as a compact rugged mountain range. Include `hazard-kill` as clearly bounded lava inside the lava cave. The three cave identities must remain visually distinct: luminous crystal, icy blue, and volcanic lava.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` as rare crystal deposits in chambers and discovery alcoves across all three caves, including deeper dark sections; no crystal count was specified. Keep deposits reachable from each entrance and avoid placing them within lethal lava. No other invisible placement is required.

## Layout components
Build one small mountain surface zone with exactly three entrances a short walk apart. Each entrance leads to its own themed passage-and-chamber network: crystal, ice, and lava. A compact exterior trail links all three mouths. Use rocky ridges and stepped slopes above ground, enclosed tunnels below, glowing formations in the crystal cave, frost and ice in the icy cave, and lava channels in the volcanic cave. The mountain perimeter forms the boundary. Compose the entrance trio as readable surface landmarks while cutaway depth preserves the darker inner chambers.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: establish the mountain and themed cave look first, then derive linked surface and underground plans from that authoritative image.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a medium scale band: the map is explicitly small, but three cave networks add depth. The theme is rugged fantasy geology with crystal, ice, and lava contrasts. Apply `P2` for tunnels and chambers beneath overhanging mountain surfaces, `P3` for exterior-to-cave transitions, and `tiered` for mountain relief.
