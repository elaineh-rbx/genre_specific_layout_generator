# Scene prompt
Create a 3D first-person clay shooting map set at a modern outdoor clay range. A fixed central shooting station overlooks open range ground, with clay-pigeon launchers positioned at varied points around the station so targets can fly in from multiple unpredictable directions; keep clear sightlines and open sky above the range.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Use Shooter. This is a first-person target-shooting round with timed scoring, no opposing team, and no player traversal.

## Shape and preset
Choose `range-directed`: one fixed firing station faces a broad safe target field, with launchers distributed across the downrange semicircle rather than behind the shooter. Use the Aim Trainer preset, adapting its practice targets from static plates to moving clay pigeons and dropping the unrequested weapon bench.

## Config requirements
Use `target-practice` for visible clay-pigeon launchers at varied distances and bearings, their moving clay targets, and a clearly marked central shooting station. Keep all target flight against open sky or uncluttered range ground, with no cover, buildings, team bases, or route through the field.

## Layout requirements
The layout half of `target-practice` places a few launchers irregularly across the forward and side sectors around the station, each with a distinct launch origin and safe flight arc; no launcher sits behind the firing direction. Place one fixed player spawn at the central station, facing downrange. Associate the launch origins with a round-wide timed target schedule, but add no visible timer geometry or scoring volume.

## Layout components
Build one compact modern outdoor clay range: a fixed central concrete shooting station, a broad fan-shaped open target field, a few low launcher housings distributed at varied distances and angles, and a safe earthen perimeter berm beyond them. Preserve clear sightlines from the station and completely open sky over every flight arc. Use short grass and restrained modern range markings, with no traversal path into the active field. Compose the station low and central in frame with the launcher fan and skyward firing volume spreading outward.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the visual relationship between station, launchers, open field, and sky first, then derive the authoritative top-down because the target arcs do not require computed topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by one focused clay range. The theme is clean, modern outdoor sport shooting. Route `P0` plus `SET`: the range is real geometry, but the player remains fixed, so traversal and jump-gap checks are skipped.
