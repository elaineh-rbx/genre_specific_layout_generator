# Scene prompt
Create a realistic cottagecore indie-style three-story house showcase, designed as a walk-through home with fully detailed, accessible interiors across all three stories. Surround the house with a small, detailed cottage garden, keeping the plot focused tightly on the home and its immediate landscaped grounds.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Entertainment because the house and garden are a detailed environment showcase explored for their visual compositions, without scoring or another game loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `route-guided` for a curated walk beginning in the cottage garden, entering the house, and progressing through its three stories. Match the Showcase preset, with the cottage itself as the hero build.

## Config requirements
Use `landmark-focal` for the three-story cottage as the composition’s dominant subject. Use `spawn-first-reveal` to present the house and immediate garden together on arrival. Use `vignette-photo` for distinct detailed compositions in the garden and selected interior rooms. Use `building-interior` for the fully accessible home, explicitly preserving all three interior stories and the outside-to-inside transition.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawn-first-reveal` marker near the garden entrance, oriented toward the cottage façade. Link the exterior front door to the ground-floor interior, and ensure the internal stair landings align between all three stories. No collectibles, triggers, or gameplay stations are required.

## Layout components
Build one realistic three-story cottage centered on a tight landscaped plot. Give the ground floor a welcoming entrance and connected living spaces, the second floor additional detailed rooms, and the third floor attic-level rooms beneath the roof, all joined by a coherent staircase. Surround the home with a small cottage garden of dense flowers, shrubs, a short path, rustic fencing, and handcrafted details, without extending into a wider landscape. The stated count is exactly three accessible stories. Use the garden edge as the boundary and frame the opening view so layered planting leads the eye to the house.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the three stacked floor plans and their stair connections must be established before the cottagecore dressing, since disconnected levels would break the walk-through.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small because the build is only one house and its immediate garden. The theme is realistic cottagecore with indie handmade warmth and dense domestic detail. Use `P3` for the exterior-to-interior transition and `P2` because three accessible stories overlap vertically and require separately captured floor geometry.
