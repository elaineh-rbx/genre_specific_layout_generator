# Scene prompt
Create a simple multiplayer game map as a compact modern outdoor park plaza with green grass and a central stone path. In the middle, place three distinct round tables, clearly separated but close together, each surrounded by four simple chairs. Leave enough open lawn around the table sets for players to circulate, using clean, functional park furnishings.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant and only genre is Party & Casual because the original concept hosts short multiplayer word-chain sessions at shared tables.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded`, the Continuous Play Space, because the compact park plaza is both the gathering area and the entire playable space, with no separate stages. The preset is none: the Quiz preset’s answer grid does not describe seated word play, and retaining only its social area would not be recognizable.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for the compact modern plaza where players gather and choose a table. Draw exactly three distinct round tables close to the center, each with exactly four matching simple chairs, for a total of twelve chairs. Keep the table sets clearly separated from one another while preserving open grass circulation. Include the central stone path and only clean, functional park furnishings; do not add a leaderboard, answer tiles, or spectator area.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly twelve seat interaction points, one aligned to each chair and associated with its table. Define exactly three table occupancy groups, each containing its four chair markers, so two different seated players at the same table can initiate that table’s session. Reserve one floating interface anchor above the center of each table. Place general player starts along the central path, outside chair interaction bounds. No scoring, checkpoint, pickup, or hostile spawn placement is needed.

## Layout components
Build one small outdoor plaza on flat green lawn. Run a central stone path through the space and cluster three round four-chair table sets in the middle without blocking the path. Maintain comfortable walking loops around every chair and enough lawn outside the cluster for circulation. Add sparse benches, low lights, or bins only at the perimeter. Use low planting or a neat park border to define the boundary, and frame the three table sets as the clear central composition.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the isometric first and make it authoritative because table-and-chair readability and an inviting park presentation lead this simple layout.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use small scale, driven by three nearby table sets and modest circulation. The theme is clean, simple modern park furniture on bright grass. Use `P0`; the whole space is one flat, unobstructed surface.
