# Scene prompt

Create a cozy, stylized toon-coloured adventure map set in a single compact, dense forest grove that can be crossed in about half a minute. Layer winding walkable paths through closely packed trees and environmental obstacles, with small exploration pockets and scattered resource-gathering spots throughout the grove.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Adventure because the core activity is exploring a forest and gathering resources from discoveries.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open` from the Exploration preset, compressed into one compact grove that can be wandered in any order rather than a long guided route.

## Config requirements

Build `path-trail` as a dense network of winding, walkable forest paths with several short loops through the grove. Add `alcove-secret` as small pockets tucked behind close tree groupings, bushes, logs, and rocks. Draw scattered resource-gathering spots as visible clusters of harvestable plants, branches, stones, and similar natural materials. Environmental obstacles should be cozy traversal interruptions—fallen trunks, thick roots, boulders, and dense undergrowth—rather than lethal hazards.

## Layout requirements

Place `collectible-nodes` at resource-gathering spots throughout the main routes and exploration pockets, with some directly readable from paths and others just out of sight in alcoves. Space them across the whole grove so no single edge holds all gathering value. No other invisible markers are required.

## Layout components

Build one compact, roughly circular forest grove bounded by an impassably dense outer wall of trees and undergrowth. Wind several narrow dirt paths from a clear entrance through a central woodland pocket and around smaller peripheral discovery pockets, reconnecting them into loops rather than dead ends. Pack stylized trees closely while preserving readable walking width. Break sightlines with shrubs, rocks, fallen logs, raised roots, and small changes in ground cover. Scatter visible gathering clusters beside trails and inside tucked-away pockets. Use gently uneven mossy earth without major slopes, water, buildings, or separate zones. Frame the map from above so the path loops remain legible beneath the canopy.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: establish the cozy toon forest look and dense layered vegetation first, then derive the top-down plan from that authoritative image.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small because the entire grove should be crossed in about half a minute. The theme is cozy, stylized, toon-coloured woodland. Use `P0`; it is one continuous ground surface with no overhang, interior transition, or separate map.
