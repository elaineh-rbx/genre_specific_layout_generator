# Scene prompt
Create a cinematic 3D sequence beginning in a rainbow, party-like world with a portal as the focal transition point. Beyond it is a huge sealed concert arena rendered with realistic 3D lighting, materials, and effects while retaining rainbow colors throughout; fill its seating with toy-like human audience figures, with colorful disco balls scattered overhead and around the venue. Center the stage on Void, a tall black singer holding a microphone, with a pure-black face partly white around one side and the creepy smiling mouth, long dark hair from the shoulders, and flame-like hair or fire emerging from the head. Include a realistic crashed car near the stage. After the concert, cut to a separate small funeral space containing a coffin labeled “Sleepy Void.”

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Entertainment: this is a cinematic live-event sequence meant to be watched and traversed rather than won.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-chaptered`, taken outside Entertainment’s usual shape set, because the rainbow arrival world, sealed concert arena, and small funeral room are three genuinely separate built scenes shown in order. Keep the Performance Venue preset, substituting this chaptered shape while retaining its audience-oriented venue composition.

## Config requirements
Use `spectator-bleachers` for raked seating facing the concert stage, `social-hub` for the huge audience floor, `landmark-focal` for the portal and Void’s stage, and `spawn-first-reveal` for composed entrances into each scene. Use `teleporter-link` for the visible rainbow portal. Use `npc-population` for the toy-like seated audience and Void. Also draw scattered disco balls, the realistic crashed car near the stage, and the labeled coffin in the funeral room.

## Layout requirements
Place both sides of `teleporter-link`, connecting the rainbow world to the sealed arena. Place one `spawn-first-reveal` at the opening rainbow vista, one inside the arena facing Void, and one in the funeral room facing the coffin. Place `npc-population` seating anchors throughout the bleachers and a central performer anchor onstage.

## Layout components
Build a bright rainbow arrival zone around one focal portal; a huge enclosed concert zone with a centered raised stage, broad audience floor, raked seating, overhead disco-ball rigging, and crashed car beside the stage; and a separate compact funeral room centered on the “Sleepy Void” coffin. Keep the arena boundary sealed and frame each scene as a deliberate cinematic reveal.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the chapter sequence, portal endpoints, one-way audience orientation, and separated spaces must be established before dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge because of the concert arena, with two smaller supporting scenes. The theme is rainbow party spectacle rendered in realistic 3D, turning ominous at the funeral. Use `P4` because the three chapters are separate maps; `P3` is unnecessary because no continuous outside-to-inside traversal is requested.
