# Scene prompt

Create a bright boutique-style avatar customization lobby as one compact hall. Arrange about eight distinct doors along the walls or in small alcoves around the lobby, with open floor space at the center; no runway or display stage.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, centered on avatar customization through a walkable lobby loop.

## Shape and preset

Use `interior-single`: one compact enclosed lobby hall is the entire map, and the customization doors belong to that room rather than separate enterable venues. The preset is none because Dress Up’s runway and social-showcase bundle is explicitly contradicted by the brief.

## Config requirements

Use `path-street` as clear pedestrian circulation around the central open floor and between the door alcoves. Draw about eight visually distinct boutique doors spaced along the perimeter, each with a readable color, icon, sign, or surrounding display that identifies it as a different avatar-design choice. Keep the central floor free of obstructions and omit any runway or stage.

## Layout requirements

Place about eight uncatalogued avatar-customization trigger volumes, one across the threshold of each door, so passing through activates that door’s choice. Site every trigger flush with its visible doorway and keep activation regions from overlapping adjacent alcoves. A single player start should face into the central open floor with all door groups easy to discover. No teleporter destinations, judging zones, or property markers are required.

## Layout components

Build one small rectangular or gently rounded boutique hall with an open central floor. Arrange about eight doors around the walls, using shallow alcoves where needed to give each threshold breathing room while preserving a continuous circulation loop. Add colorful trim, signs, mirrors, mannequins, and compact fashion props around the perimeter without blocking the door approaches. Keep all doors on the same level and make each approach wide enough for players to enter, turn, and continue to the next choice. Enclose the map with bright cartoon boutique walls and frame it so the central floor and nearly every doorway read in one view.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render the colorful boutique hall and its door identities first, then derive the top-down plan; the visual differentiation of choices leads.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, fixed by one compact hall and about eight doors. The theme is a bright cartoon avatar boutique. Use `P0`; all play occurs on one flat interior surface with no exterior crossing or overhanging level.
