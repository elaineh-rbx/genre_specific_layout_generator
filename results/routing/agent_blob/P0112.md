# Scene prompt
Create a simple low-polygon 3D first-person shooting arena as a true cubic combat volume, with vertical depth roughly equal to its width and length. Arrange platforms, corridors, cover structures, and suspended sections throughout the full XYZ space so the map extends upward and downward as much as across, rather than functioning as a flat arena.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Shooter, with no secondary genre. This is a solo first-person arena against respawning bots, not bomb defusal or a competitive match.

## Shape and preset
Use `volume-open-air`, taken from outside Shooter’s usual set because movement and combat occupy a true cubic volume with suspended landing surfaces rather than a ground network. The preset is none: Aim Trainer assumes targets downrange, while this scene places hostile bots throughout the volume.

## Config requirements
Use `cover-los` for simple polygonal walls, blocks, shields, and platform undersides that break firing lines at multiple heights. Draw a sparse but complete network of platforms, corridors, and suspended sections across the full width, length, and equal vertical depth. Do not add bomb sites, team bases, rounds, loadout stations, or decorative realism.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` as a distributed set of bot-spawn candidates throughout the cubic volume, attached to broad platforms or sheltered corridor pockets at low, middle, and high elevations. Spawn sites must avoid immediate player sightlines while still feeding enemies back into combat from varied directions. No objective, capture, team-spawn, or loot volumes are required.

## Layout components
Build one cubic arena volume with a central open void and low-, mid-, and high-level clusters of polygonal platforms. Connect local clusters with short corridors, vertical shafts, suspended bridges, and free-space gaps intended for gravity-free movement. Spread blocky cover across platform faces and corridor mouths, and include enclosed pockets without turning them into separate building interiors. The arena’s outer cube is the boundary. Use simple flat-shaded geometry and a restrained color code to distinguish altitude bands. Compose the authoritative view from a cutaway diagonal angle so upward and downward depth is as visually prominent as horizontal span.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the true XYZ composition must be authoritative because a top-down plan would collapse the arena’s equal vertical depth and hide its defining spatial relationships.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by a cubic combat volume large enough for several elevation clusters. The theme is simple, clean, low-polygon sci-fi test geometry. Use `CHECK` because the volumetric play space self-occludes, and `P2` because suspended playable surfaces overhang lower ones.
