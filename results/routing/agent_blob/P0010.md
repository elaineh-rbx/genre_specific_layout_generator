# Scene prompt
Create a 3D space-themed game world called Clicker Phyco, formed as one continuous route through 15 sequentially unlockable areas. Players run between distinct planets and lunar zones arranged in order, with each new region visibly separated by a gateway or boundary and increasing in scale and value along the progression path. Place themed pet areas within each zone, and reserve prominent encounter spaces for bosses.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation (`simulation`) because clicking, multipliers, upgrades, rebirth, pets, and sequential stat gates define the game. Action (`action`) is secondary because bosses provide combat encounters within that progression.

## Shape and preset
Use `tier-ladder`: exactly 15 planetary and lunar areas form one readable continuous progression route, each blocked until the required power or currency threshold. The Stat Grinder preset is the direct match.

## Config requirements
Draw `station-training` as dense click-and-power training grounds within every area; `gate-progression` as a themed gateway or boundary between every adjacent pair of zones; `social-hub` as each zone’s pet and armor storefront area; `path-circulation` as the continuous run route linking all 15 regions; and `arena-lockin` as a distinct boss encounter space where a zone requires a fight. The environments should visibly increase in prestige, scale, and Lunar value along the route.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `station-training` throughout each of the 15 zones. Place one `trigger-rebirth` pad at the far end of the highest currently reachable tier, with safe space for Lunar upgrades nearby. Place `spawner-npc` emitters inside each reserved boss area and keep them off the main circulation path. Place one functional pet-purchase point in every zone, for exactly 15 pet areas total, and link each progression gate to its corresponding unlock threshold.

## Layout components
Build one continuous linear or gently spiraling route through exactly 15 distinct space-themed areas. Every area contains training ground, one themed pet area, a boss space, and a visible onward gate. Connect neighboring regions with broad run-ready causeways or planetary transition paths rather than teleporters. Use low boundaries and strong material changes to separate zones while preserving a single surface. Frame the earliest modest lunar region in the foreground and let increasingly grand planetary regions rise toward the final tier.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: render the plan first and make it authoritative because all 15 zones, gates, and the single progression route must remain correctly ordered and reachable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large, driven by 15 sequential regions. The theme is colorful lunar and planetary clicker progression. Use `P0`; the continuous single-surface tier ladder has no overhang, separate map, or volumetric traversal requirement.
