# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map set along a short, endless train rail line. Begin at a small rail stop, with a handful of buildings spaced along the track; each building contains an old-fashioned firearm. A train travels along the rail and provides a rideable moving platform. Place open ground and intermittent large green zombies beside and along the route, with the repeating rail corridor extending beyond view.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Use Shooter. Firearms distributed along a zombie-populated route define the activity; the train is traversal rather than a racing or vehicle-simulation objective.

## Shape and preset
Choose `route-guided` from outside Shooter’s usual set: one repeating rail corridor sequences the player from the starting stop past a handful of weapon buildings and zombie encounter ground. Use the PvE Shooter preset with this substituted shape, retaining hostile origins and enterable structures while dropping lane chokepoints.

## Config requirements
Use `building-interior` for the handful of small 1890s trackside buildings that players enter from open ground. Draw visible old-fashioned firearm pickups for `collectible-loot`, one inside each building. Include the small starting stop, a continuous rail, a rideable period train with a clear deck, open ground, and intermittent large green zombie encounter areas.

## Layout requirements
Place one `collectible-loot` firearm node inside every building. Resolve each node as a 1890s sawed-off double-barrel shotgun with 60 percent probability, a 1890s rifle with 30 percent probability, or a 1890s bolt-action rifle with 10 percent probability. Place `spawner-npc` zombie origins intermittently beside the rail and on open ground, away from the initial stop and outside building interiors. Place one player spawn at the stop and a train boarding marker beside its platform. The route has no winner zone because the ride is endless.

## Layout components
Build a short repeating rail corridor beginning at one small stop, then passing a handful of spaced trackside buildings and open zombie ground before extending beyond view and looping its content seamlessly. Connect every building to the rail edge by a short walkable dirt path. Include one rideable train, one continuous track, a simple platform, flat open terrain, and sparse period scenery. Keep the train deck and all boarding edges clear. Bound the sides with distant scrub while hiding the repeat at the forward horizon. Compose along the rail’s strong receding axis.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: draw the authoritative repeating rail plan first, then dress the isometric, because the train path must remain continuous and every stop, building, and encounter area must connect to it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium: the authored segment is short but supports an endless repeated ride. The theme is eerie 1890s frontier rail horror. Route `P2` because the playable train deck moves above the rail surface, plus `P3` because players cross between open ground and enterable weapon buildings.
