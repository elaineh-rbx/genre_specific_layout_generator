## Enriched image prompt
Create a small 1890s railway-horror scene built as a short repeatable section of an endless line. Begin with a simple stop and boarding platform beside one continuous train rail, then place a handful of small enterable buildings at intervals along the track, each with a clear interior pickup surface. Include a period train aligned to the platform and preserve safe space for players to hop aboard. Make both ends of the rail and surrounding ground align seamlessly with the next streamed section, with no final station or ending. Keep the route open enough for occasional large green zombie encounters without adding crowds of humanoid enemies.

## Genre
Shooter is the sole genre because firearm pickups and shooting the zombie are the defining playable activity.

## Shape and preset
Use `vehicle-deck`, named Vehicle Deck, from outside Shooter's usual shape set because players can board the train and ride while the world continues past it. This uses the PvE Shooter preset with the train shape substituted and the unrequested chokepoint removed.

## Config requirements
Use `building-interior` for the handful of small trackside buildings players enter. Use shared `chunk-modular`, taken from outside Shooter's shortlist, for railway sections whose rail, terrain, and travel envelope align into an endless sequence. The stop, boarding platform, rail, train, and pickup surfaces are direct scene components.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-loot` as one weapon pickup inside each generated trackside building. Each pickup chooses an 1890s sawed-off double-barrel shotgun at 60 percent, an 1890s rifle at 30 percent, or an 1890s bolt-action rifle at 10 percent. Place `spawner-npc` Spawners only in occasional streamed sections, offset from the rail and buildings, and configure them to emit the requested big green zombie rather than constant crowds.

## Layout components
Each active section contains one continuous rail corridor, a stop and boarding platform when the run begins, enough train clearance, and a handful of spaced enterable buildings with simple interiors. The rail centreline, grade, and open travel envelope match at both chunk boundaries. Buildings sit close enough to reach from the line while leaving the train unobstructed. The runtime repeats compatible sections indefinitely, so there is no final station, finish gate, or dead-end track.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish the aligned railway and repeatable section boundaries before dressing the isometric view, because a broken rail connection would make the endless train ride unplayable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by a short line with a handful of individually legible buildings. The theme is 1890s frontier railway horror with an occasional green zombie. Use `P6` because streamed rail sections must connect validly by construction and `P3` because players cross from the exterior track into building interiors.
