# Scene prompt

Create a 3D bright modern mall-style social hangout with a central gathering area for players to meet and show off outfits, surrounded by wardrobe and outfit-making spaces.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, centered equally on social hanging out and unscored avatar customization.

## Shape and preset

Use `interior-single`: one enclosed mall-style venue is the entire walkable map, with no exterior transition. The preset is none. Dress Up would impose a runway, while the town and housing presets would expand a compact social wardrobe into a settlement the author did not request.

## Config requirements

Use `social-hub` for a deliberately spacious central gathering area where players can meet and display completed outfits without blocking circulation. Use `path-street` as broad mall walkways connecting that hub to the surrounding wardrobe and outfit-making spaces. Draw open wardrobe bays, mirrors, mannequins, clothing displays, creation counters, lounge seating, and a few polished outfit-showoff backdrops, all visible from the central space.

## Layout requirements

Place uncatalogued outfit-selection and editing interaction points at each wardrobe or creation station, directly in front of its visible mirror, mannequin, or counter. Keep these interaction areas recessed from the main walkways. Place the player start at the edge of the central gathering hub with immediate sightlines to both social seating and outfit-making bays. No judging zones, score volumes, runway markers, teleporters, or NPC placements are needed.

## Layout components

Build one bright modern mall interior around a large central social lounge or atrium. Arrange wardrobe and outfit-making bays along the perimeter, connected by a continuous open walkway and short aisles. Furnish the center with grouped seating, planters, low decorative features, and clear standing space for players showing outfits. Give each customization bay mirrors, racks, mannequins, shelves, and uncluttered approach space. Add a few stylish backdrop walls near the hub for informal outfit displays without creating a formal stage. Use a flat polished floor, bright neutral architecture, colorful fashion accents, and a sealed mall boundary. Frame the central hub as the immediate visual anchor with the wardrobe options clearly surrounding it.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render the welcoming mall atmosphere and social-to-customization relationship first, then derive the top-down circulation plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is medium, driven by one central social hub and several surrounding wardrobe bays. The theme is bright contemporary mall fashion. Use `P0`; all activity shares one flat interior surface without exterior crossings or overlapping levels.
