# Scene prompt

Include a six-car starting grid, a start/finish straight, narrow turns, and trackside damageable barriers and structures.

Use continuous barriers to separate the driving surface from buildings and spectators, and frame the compact circuit from above so its recognizable winding footprint and six-car grid are both prominent.
