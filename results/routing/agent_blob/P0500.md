# Scene prompt
Create a polished medieval-meets-technology Tower Defense world with a large scenic lobby surrounded by trees, rocks, flowers, mountains, and rivers. Place a central fountain as the lobby focal point, with distinct nearby areas for a tutorial NPC, shop, codes, achievements, and player leaderboard; line several matching elevator bays around the lobby for Easy, Normal, Hard, and Nightmare matches. Generate seven compact match maps whose entire main enemy route is visible at once: a forest, castle, city, snow, volcano, laboratory, and desert map. Each map has a clear winding path from a spawn entrance to a defended base at the end, with open build areas along the route and terrain or structures framing the path: woodland and river scenery for the forest, fortress walls for the castle, streets and buildings for the city, icy terrain for snow, lava and rock for volcano, futuristic-medieval technological rooms for the laboratory, and dunes and stone formations for the desert.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Strategy, specifically tower defense.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-chaptered`, outside Strategy’s usual set, because the lobby and seven named battlefields are genuinely separate maps. Retain the Tower Defense preset with this substituted shape: every battlefield still has the preset’s continuous lane, build ground, defended core, and wave origin.

## Config requirements
`buildzone-grid` draws open tower-placement ground alongside each route. `tracker-core` draws one defended base per match map, seven total. `water-body` draws lobby rivers and the forest river. `terrain-relief` supplies surrounding lobby mountains and the volcano’s raised rocky terrain. `npc-population` gives the tutorial NPC a visible station. The lobby also draws one central fountain, five service areas—tutorial, shop, codes, achievements, leaderboard—and four labeled elevator bays. Each battlefield receives its named biome treatment while keeping its full route visible.

## Layout requirements
Place seven `tracker-core` markers and seven `spawner-npc` origins, one paired set per battlefield. Place one tutorial NPC at its lobby station and triggers inside the four Easy, Normal, Hard, and Nightmare elevators. Keep every enemy route continuous from its origin to its core, with build areas reachable from the player arrival point.

## Layout components
The large scenic lobby centers on the fountain, with service areas nearby and four elevator bays around its edge. Trees, rocks, flowers, rivers, and mountains frame it. Separate compact maps cover forest, castle, city, snow, volcano, laboratory, and desert themes. Each contains one winding start-to-base lane, flanking build ground, and biome-specific framing: woods and river; fortress walls; streets and buildings; ice; lava rock; technological medieval rooms; or dunes and stone. Each map has a clear bounded edge and an overview composition.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: all seven routes must be valid and fully readable before their isometric dressing passes.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge because it includes one lobby and seven maps, despite each match being compact. The theme is polished medieval fantasy fused with technology. Use `P4` for separate maps, `P6` for valid enemy lanes, `tiered` for mountains and volcanic relief, and `CHECK` for the represented rivers.
