# Scene prompt
Create a 3D zombie chase course in a small abandoned city. A forward-running route follows empty streets through city blocks, with ramps leading onto rooftops and simple parkour obstacles along the escape path. Place an escape exit at the far end of the course.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Survival because the central pressure is escaping one pursuing zombie. Obby & Platformer is secondary because the escape route uses player-controlled ramps and parkour.

## Shape and preset
Use `traversal-city`, taken from outside Survival’s usual shape set, because the course deliberately crosses streets, ramps, rooftops, ledges, and gaps through one small city. Preset is none: Escape assumes a looping warren, hiding cover, and enterable shelter, while this brief explicitly describes a directed outdoor course.

## Config requirements
Use `path-track` for the ordered physics-legal escape route across streets and roofs, `obstacle-jump` for simple gaps and parkour barriers, `gate-escape` for the visible terminal exit, and `settlement-density` for a compact abandoned city-block grid. Draw broad ramps from street level onto rooftops and preserve the streets below as fallback ground.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawner-npc` origin for the zombie directly behind the player’s course start, with patrol navigation covering the full escape route. The layout half of `gate-escape` places one win trigger within the far-end exit. No checkpoints are added because the brief defines being caught as losing the chase rather than resuming mid-course.

## Layout components
Build one forward route through several empty city blocks. Begin on a broad abandoned street, climb ramps to a connected rooftop run, alternate simple barriers and clearable roof gaps, and return to street or lower roofs where needed before the final escape gate. Derelict facades and sparse debris line the route without creating accidental dead ends. Keep one continuous chase path and make the far exit readable from the final approach.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, because the zombie navigation, parkour chain, and reachable escape route must all remain continuous.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, matching the requested compact abandoned city. The theme is readable stylized urban decay with a tense chase mood. Use `P6` for valid parkour spacing and pursuit connectivity, plus `P2` because playable rooftops and ramps overhang the fallback streets below.
