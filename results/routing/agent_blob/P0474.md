# Scene prompt
Create a 3D narrative walking map formed from five stitched life-chapter path segments, all physically present on one continuous expanding surface. The player progresses forward through Childhood, Adolescence, Early Adulthood, Middle Age, and Later Life, while the accumulated route stretches behind them; early sections remain close and readable at first, then recede into distant glowing landmarks and lights by old age. Each chapter contains three to five compact, active memory vignettes arranged directly along or beside the route, with transition walls, hallways, or doorways separating chapters.

Childhood includes a warm kitchen with a parent cutting fruit, imaginative play space, a family campsite, and the original family photograph. Adolescence moves through a campsite with friends, a practical-skill scene with a parent, and a packed childhood bedroom leading out of home. Early adulthood contains a first shared home, hospital farewell room, child’s first-steps space, and sleepless nursery. Middle age includes a family kitchen, departing child’s room or doorway, aging parent’s home, and quiet dinner setting. Later life includes a campsite with younger family, photograph-sorting room, a home marked by a lost partner’s absence, and a final multi-generation gathering around a photograph. A teddy bear and family photograph recur visibly across these spaces. At each chapter transition, place a physical 90-row by 52-square week chart that becomes increasingly faded and framed with age. End in a hospital room, then a quiet childhood kitchen, with the completed path visible far below from above as individual memory scenes glow.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The genre is Adventure. This is a directed narrative journey through five life chapters, with walking and observation as the core spatial activity.

## Shape and preset
Use `route-guided` for one continuous forward path containing all five stitched segments while preserving the accumulated route behind. The preset is none: Story assumes separate maps and one-way progression, while this prompt explicitly requires one co-present surface and backward views.

## Config requirements
Draw `path-trail` as the expanding life route; `gate-chapter` as four transition walls, hallways, or doorways between five chapters; `building-interior` for kitchens, bedrooms, homes, hospital rooms, and nursery spaces entered from the route; and `npc-population` as active family and friends inhabiting the memory vignettes. Draw the recurring teddy bear and family photograph in their specified scenes. Each of the four transitions carries one physical chart containing exactly 90 rows, 52 squares per row, and 4,680 squares.

## Layout requirements
Place four chapter-transition triggers, one at each chart between adjacent segments. Place ambient `npc-population` anchors within every active vignette rather than on the path. Place one terminal marker in the final quiet childhood kitchen after the hospital sequence. Nothing should teleport or remove earlier chapters.

## Layout components
Build five continuous ordered zones: Childhood with four vignettes, Adolescence with three, Early Adulthood with four, Middle Age with four, and Later Life with four, for 19 principal memories. Connect them by one walkable route and four occluding thresholds. Interiors open directly beside or across the path; campsites provide exterior relief. Preserve long reverse sightlines so earlier memories become distant glowing landmarks. End with a hospital room, then the quiet childhood kitchen, and compose the complete path for a far-above credits view.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: author the continuous chapter sequence and all threshold connections first, because a disconnected segment would break the only narrative route.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge, driven by five segments and 19 memory spaces. The theme is warm, energetic, bittersweet, and increasingly faded with age. Use `P3` for repeated exterior-to-interior traversal; do not use `P4`, because every chapter must remain on one surface.
