# Scene prompt
Create a compact 3D treasure-hunt map on a weathered photoreal coastal lighthouse island, small enough to cross in under a minute. A storm-damaged lighthouse stands as the central landmark, with its beacon at the top; arrange three signal parts at distinct reachable locations around the island. Show visible storm damage along the shoreline and around the lighthouse, with surrounding ocean enclosing the island.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure because the player explores an island for objective items and brings them to a focal destination.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, one compact island searched in any order. The matched preset is Scavenger Hunt, retaining its open exploration structure and collectible placement while replacing unrequested secret alcoves and a physical tracker with the lighthouse objective.

## Config requirements
Use `landmark-focal` for the storm-damaged lighthouse that dominates the island and draws players toward its beacon. Use `water-body` for the enclosing ocean and `building-interior` for the accessible lighthouse route needed to reach the beacon at its top. Draw weathered photoreal coastal materials, visible storm debris and damage around the structure and shoreline, and three distinct reachable search areas.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly three `collectible-nodes` as signal parts at separate reachable locations around the island. Place one beacon-repair interaction at the lighthouse’s top, aligned to the beacon geometry. Use one central `checkpoint-respawn` at safe ground near the lighthouse base so falling into the ocean returns the player quickly without repeating a long search.

## Layout components
Build one small island, crossable in under a minute, enclosed by ocean. Put the lighthouse at the center as the tallest landmark, with an accessible interior route from base to beacon. Create three distinct part locations around its perimeter and intermediate ground. Storm damage should affect the shoreline, lighthouse surroundings, and scattered debris without blocking reachability. Frame the island so lighthouse, shore, and all search sectors read together.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative, then derive the top-down image; the lighthouse silhouette, damage, and coastal realism lead.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, set by the under-one-minute crossing. The theme is weathered photoreal storm coast. Use `P3` for movement from island exterior into the lighthouse interior and `CHECK` for the surrounding ocean volume.
