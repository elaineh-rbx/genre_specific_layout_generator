# Scene prompt
Create a 3D chill hangout map set in a soft, stylized cartoon woodland. A peaceful forest clearing provides the central social space for player avatars, surrounded by trees and open walking areas, with birds flying around the canopy and sky. Place a few simple seating spots throughout the clearing and along the forest edges.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Entertainment: this is a walkable hangout environment meant to be enjoyed rather than won.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open` for a freely explored woodland clearing with no prescribed route. The preset is Free-Roam Showcase, but omit its landmark and collectible options because the brief asks for a low-key forest and explicitly rules out collectibles; retain the free-roam shape and curated arrival.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for the broad central clearing where avatars gather. Use `spawn-first-reveal` to draw a welcoming forest-edge arrival facing into the clearing. Use `npc-population` for stylized birds occupying the canopy and open sky. Add a few simple benches, logs, or stump seats as scene-specific visible geometry, distributed without turning the clearing into a formal plaza.

## Layout requirements
Place the `spawn-first-reveal` marker at one edge of the forest, oriented toward the peaceful clearing and its seating rather than toward the outer boundary. Place the `npc-population` bird activity across the upper canopy and nearby open sky, avoiding a single dense flock. Nothing collectible, scored, triggered, or progression-based is placed.

## Layout components
Create one generous central clearing ringed by soft cartoon trees, shrubs, and open woodland floor. Keep broad walking gaps between tree clusters and connect the arrival edge to the clearing and peripheral seating with informal dirt paths. Scatter a few seating spots through the center and along quieter forest edges. Use gently uneven grassy ground, warm foliage, rounded rocks, flowers, and a visible canopy opening for the birds. Dense trees form a soft natural boundary. Compose the frame around the open social center, with layered forest edges and visible sky above.

## Render order
Render in `isometric` order: the welcoming woodland mood and foliage composition are authoritative, and the simple open plan can be derived from that image.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by one clearing and its immediate forest edge. The theme is soft, cheerful cartoon woodland. Use `P0`: the map is one open walkable surface with no forced overhang, interior transition, separate map, or validity-construction pass.
