# Scene prompt
Create a realistic 3D Roblox-style war shooter map: a single medium-sized battlefield arena designed for fast 5v5 firefights, with opposing team sides and clear combat space between them. Arrange cover, barriers, and varied fighting positions across the arena, with routes connecting the two sides and no graphic blood or gore.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Shooter: the original brief specifies a realistic 5v5 kill-count match with opposing teams.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-network` for parallel combat routes joining two opposing sides across one arena. The preset is Team Deathmatch, matching the team bases, distributed cover, and contested transitions requested.

## Config requirements
Use `spawn-teambase` to draw two balanced, visibly distinct military staging bases at opposite ends, each fully screened from direct enemy sniper fire. Use `cover-los` for evenly distributed waist-high barriers and full-body cover across every route. Use `choke-bottleneck` for a few narrow lane junctions that focus firefights without reducing the whole battlefield to one corridor.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-teambase` as exactly two team spawn zones, one at each opposite end, sized for five players and sited behind solid sightline-blocking geometry. No capture point, pickups, NPC emitters, or other invisible placements are requested.

## Layout components
Build two opposing base zones with clear combat ground between them. Connect the bases through a readable central route and flanking side routes, with each route offering varied engagement distances. Distribute concrete barriers, military crates, sandbag-like low cover, and taller solid blockers so players can advance in stages. Tighten selected route junctions into contested chokepoints while preserving alternate approaches. Keep the ground largely level and the arena enclosed by robust military perimeter walls or impassable battlefield edges. Compose the plan symmetrically enough for fair 5v5 starts, but vary individual cover silhouettes and route bends for a natural war-zone appearance.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, because balanced team routes, shielded bases, and uninterrupted lane connections determine whether the match plays fairly. Dress the isometric from that plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, accommodating ten simultaneous players while keeping firefights fast. The theme is realistic, non-graphic modern military combat in Roblox 3D. Use `P0`: one level battlefield, one walkable surface, no interiors, overhangs, or separate maps.
