# Scene prompt

Create a realistic first-person dirt BMX trail in the woods. A packed-earth bike track winds through natural woodland, with jumps and drop-ins placed along the route amid trees and natural surroundings.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant and sole genre is Simulation. This is free-form bicycle operation with no race, lap, finish, score, or opponent, so it is not Racing.

## Shape and preset

Use `route-guided`, taken from outside Simulation’s usual shape shortlist, for one directed woodland trail that sequences riders through its jumps and descents. Match the Physics Sim preset with the shape substituted: its circulation path and physics contraptions describe the ride, while its unrelated environmental hazard is dropped.

## Config requirements

Use `path-circulation` for the packed-earth riding trail winding through the woods. Use `physics-rig` for realistic dirt takeoff ramps, landing slopes, and drop-ins whose geometry supports bicycle momentum. Use `terrain-relief` for the natural rises and dips that shape the trail without overhangs. Trees and woodland ground cover should flank rather than obstruct the route.

## Layout requirements

Place one rider-and-bike start marker at the broad trail entrance, oriented into the first easy section with clear runup. No `trigger-lap`, `trigger-finish`, scoring volume, checkpoint chain, task station, or pickup is required because the experience is continuous free-riding rather than a timed run.

## Layout components

Build one medium woodland zone containing a continuous packed-earth trail. Begin with a forgiving entry clearing, wind between stands of trees, and sequence several dirt jumps and drop-ins with adequate straight runups and broad landing zones. Use natural soil, leaf litter, rocks, shrubs, and dense trees beyond the riding corridor. Keep every branch and prop out of the bicycle envelope. The forest forms the soft visual boundary, while the trail remains readable in the composition.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: establish the realistic woodland look and ride-feature silhouettes first, then derive the top-down plan. The route is simple and need not be procedurally authored.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale band is medium, driven by a useful sequence of bike features with safe approach and landing space. The theme is realistic packed-earth BMX woodland. Use `tiered` because the rises and drop-ins require elevation on one continuous surface; no geometry overhangs, so `P2` is unnecessary.
