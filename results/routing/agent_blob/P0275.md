# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] scale — Q: How big should the village be - a few blocks a Runner can cross in half a minute, or a full town? A: Un pueblo compacto de unas pocas manzanas, que un Runner pueda cruzar en alrededor de medio minuto; debe priorizar rutas, atajos y edificios explorables sobre tamaño.
- [author] goal — Q: If no Runner is caught before the timer runs out, do the Runners simply win, or is there somewhere they have to reach? A: Si termina el tiempo y queda al menos un Runner sin eliminar, los Runners ganan; no hace falta una zona de extracción.
- [author] sewers — Q: Should the sewers be a walkable underground network, or just short connector tunnels between two manholes? A: Las alcantarillas deben ser una red subterránea transitable y compacta, con accesos por algunas alcantarillas y conexiones útiles como atajos.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a compact abandoned village at night, only a few blocks across and traversable in about half a minute. Build a looping network of streets, alleys, multiple routes, and shortcuts around houses, one school, one church, one gas station, one shop, one police station, and one park. Make the named buildings enterable through readable doors and windows, with interior hiding spots and exterior cover. Beneath the streets, add a compact walkable sewer network reached through several manholes and reconnecting distant parts of the village as useful shortcuts. Keep every chase route connected with no trapping dead ends. Add a separate small interactive lobby for waiting players. Use wind-swept, flickering-light nighttime atmosphere without background-music imagery.

## Genre
The genre is Survival.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-staged`, taken from outside Survival's usual set, as the top-level Lobby and Isolated Stage container. The village match segment uses `warren-looping` for fair zero-dead-end chase circulation. The matching preset is 1 vs All, with its escape gate removed because surviving the timer requires no extraction zone.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for the interactive lobby; `settlement-density` for the compact abandoned village; `cover-los` for interior and exterior hiding spots; `building-interior` for the school, church, gas station, shop, police station, and houses; `path-flank-tunnel` from Shooter for the sewer shortcuts; and `teleporter-link` for the lobby-to-match transition.

## Layout requirements
Place the layout half of `teleporter-link` between the lobby and village. Place `spawn-protected` as separated Runner and Chaser start areas with no immediate sightline between roles. Do not place `gate-escape`. Sewer entrances must align with manholes and connect back to useful street locations.

## Layout components
There are two map segments: a Lobby shaped as `interior-single`, and the Abandoned Village match shaped as `warren-looping`. The village contains a few blocks, houses, one school, church, gas station, shop, police station, park, looping streets and alleys, doors, windows, hiding places, several manholes, and a compact underground sewer network. Every above- and below-ground route reconnects.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish the village, building, shortcut, and sewer connectivity first, then dress the authoritative plans as a nighttime abandoned settlement.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is medium, preserving individual interiors and hiding spots across a half-minute village. The theme is an abandoned nighttime town with flickering practical lights. `P4` is required for lobby and match maps, `P6` for zero-dead-end chase topology, `P2` for the underground sewer layer, and `P3` for outside-to-building and street-to-sewer transitions.
