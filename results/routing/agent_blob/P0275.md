# Scene prompt
Crea un mapa 3D de un pueblo abandonado nocturno, compacto y de unas pocas manzanas, cruzable en alrededor de medio minuto. Organiza casas y callejones conectados por calles con rutas múltiples, atajos, ventanas, puertas y escondites; incluye edificios explorables como una escuela, una iglesia, una estación de servicio, una tienda y una comisaría, además de un parque. Bajo el pueblo, construye una red de alcantarillas transitable y compacta, accesible desde varias tapas de alcantarilla y conectada con zonas útiles del pueblo como atajo. Añade un lobby físico separado para el inicio de las partidas. La composición debe sentirse oscura y tensa, con iluminación nocturna tenue, luces parpadeantes y un oscurecimiento aún mayor hacia el final de la ronda.

# Agent decision
### Genre
The dominant genre is Survival: an asymmetric Chaser-versus-Runner pursuit game where surviving the timer matters more than reaching an exit.

### Shape and preset
Use `warren-looping`, making streets, alleys, interiors, and sewer shortcuts form interconnected loops with no trapping dead ends. The closest preset is 1 vs All, retaining its pursuit topology, hiding cover, and separated role starts but dropping its escape gate because the brief explicitly has no extraction.

### Config requirements
Use `cover-los` for windows, doorways, alleys, and compact hiding recesses that break Chaser sightlines. Use `building-interior` for the explorable school, church, gas station, shop, police station, and houses. Use `settlement-density` for a compact abandoned village of a few blocks. The walkable sewer network and its manhole entrances must also be drawn as scene-specific geometry, as must the physically separate lobby.

### Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` role starts in separated positions: Runner starts distributed through safe village locations and Chaser starts screened far enough away to prevent immediate contact. No `gate-escape` or winner zone is needed because surviving Runners win in place when time expires.

### Layout components
The main zone is a compact village crossed in roughly thirty seconds, with streets connecting dense houses, narrow alleys, a central park, and the five named civic or commercial buildings. Doors and windows provide alternate passages. A compact underground sewer layer links several useful surface areas through multiple manholes. The lobby is isolated from the active round. Bound the town with dark, impassable ruined edges and frame it as a readable few-block plan despite low nighttime light.

### Render order
Use `topdown`: the looping chase network, multiple shortcuts, and sewer access connectivity must be valid before the isometric image dresses the plan.

### Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by a few dense blocks rather than a full town. The theme is abandoned nocturnal civic horror with dim, flickering light. Use `P6` for guaranteed loop connectivity, `P2` because the sewer layer lies beneath the surface, `P3` for exterior-to-interior transitions, and `P4` for the isolated lobby map.
