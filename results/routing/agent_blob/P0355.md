# Scene prompt
Create a 3D zombie arena with a dark, foggy, rotting horror atmosphere and a defensible central area with approaches from multiple sides.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Survival because players hold a position against endless zombie waves.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded`: one contained arena keeps the zombies and defenders in meaningful proximity. The preset is Horde Defense, retaining its bounded shape and hostile wave origins while omitting destructible structures and specialized hiding cutouts because the brief does not request them.

## Config requirements
Draw a defensible central area surrounded by open attack ground and several clearly readable approaches. Build the arena from dark, damp, rotting horror materials with broken masonry, decayed timber, dead vegetation, and low fog used as atmosphere rather than a solid obstacle. Keep the main movement lines open and do not add an escape gate, since this is endless survival rather than an escape round.

## Layout requirements
Use `spawner-npc` for zombie wave origins placed at multiple separated points around the arena perimeter, one aligned with each approach toward the center. Keep every origin outside immediate player visibility and far enough from the defended area to provide a readable warning. Place one unkeyed player spawn within the central defensible zone, oriented toward the broadest incoming path.

## Layout components
The map has one central defense zone occupying the middle of a small bounded arena. Several ground-level paths approach it from different sides through open, foggy decay, allowing hordes to converge without a single mandatory lane. Sparse ruined props define the edges of those approaches but do not form enterable buildings or overhangs. Uneven-looking rotten ground remains one traversable surface. A high ruined wall, collapsed fence line, or darkness-obscured barrier closes the outer boundary. Compose the central holdout as the focal point, with at least three approach directions visible around it.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the horror atmosphere and defensive composition first, then derive the straightforward radial top-down layout from that image.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, keeping endless waves close to the defended center. The theme is outright dark, foggy, rotting zombie horror. The route is `P0`; all play remains on one ground surface with no enterable interior, overhang, separate map, or procedural topology.
