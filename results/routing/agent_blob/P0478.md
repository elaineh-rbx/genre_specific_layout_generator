# Scene prompt
Create a 3D multiplayer mystery island with a tropical, pirate-flavoured atmosphere. The playable island has a central treasure mystery, with environmental clues and puzzle locations distributed across its tropical landscape.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Adventure is dominant, with Puzzle secondary. The experience is an explorable treasure mystery, while environmental solves provide the way players uncover it together.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, one contiguous island explored in flexible order. The closest preset is Scavenger Hunt: retain its open-world structure, `collectible-nodes`, and `alcove-secret`, but omit `tracker-quest` because the brief does not ask for a quest board. Add the Puzzle options needed for physical clue sites.

## Config requirements
Use `alcove-secret` for concealed clue pockets in foliage, rock recesses, and pirate ruins. Use `facade-clue` for environmental symbols and patterns placed directly in natural sightlines, and `gate-solve` for a final treasure approach visibly blocked until the clues are resolved. Use `water-body` for the tropical sea enclosing the island. Include a hidden pirate treasure chamber or cache as the focal payoff geometry.

## Layout requirements
Use `collectible-nodes` for several clue pieces distributed among the puzzle locations, each sited at a visible pedestal, ruin niche, or landmark rather than loose in empty terrain. Place uncatalogued interaction trigger volumes at each environmental puzzle and one unlock trigger at the final treasure gate. Multiplayer cooperation rules remain outside layout.

## Layout components
Build one medium tropical island surrounded by turquoise sea. Distribute puzzle locations around the coast, jungle interior, and weathered pirate ruins, with walkable trails connecting them to a central locked treasure site. Include hidden clue alcoves, readable carved facades, distinct landmark silhouettes, and a concealed treasure cache behind the central solve gate. Keep the island boundary open to the water and compose the major clue landmarks around the central mystery so players can orient themselves without a rigid sequence.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative, then derive the top-down view; visual discovery and atmosphere lead, while the open exploration order does not require computed topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale for multiple distributed clue sites on one island. The theme is playful tropical pirate mystery. Route with `CHECK` because surrounding water adds volumetric traversal that must not self-occlude; no separate-map or interior transition is required.
