# Scene prompt
Create a 3D medieval tycoon map with separate player plots, each containing a buildable fortress. Arrange space around each fortress for roughly a dozen defending soldiers and clear approaches for attacking waves.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation because each player incrementally builds a fortress on a personal tycoon plot. Strategy is secondary because roughly a dozen commanded soldiers defend it from enemy waves.

## Shape and preset
Use `plot-isolated`: every fortress occupies an independent player-owned footprint with protected build space and its own attack approaches. The preset is Base Defense from the secondary Strategy genre, with its open-terrain shape replaced by personal plots while retaining the defended core, wave origins, and readable attack ground.

## Config requirements
Use `tracker-core` for each buildable fortress as the visible heart of its plot. Use `cover-los` for fortress walls, towers, and sparse approach obstacles that give defenders useful positions without blocking every route. Use `gate-progression` for fortress sections that appear through the tycoon build. Keep broad clear ground around each stronghold for the requested army and waves.

## Layout requirements
The functional half of `tracker-core` places one fortress objective per player plot. Use `spawner-npc` for several wave-origin points along the outer edge of each plot, all facing clear approaches toward that fortress. Place roughly 12 defender muster or guard positions around each completed fortress, distributed across the gate, walls, and immediate courtyard rather than represented as ambient population.

## Layout components
Repeat separate medieval plots, each centered on one buildable stone fortress with a gate, walls, towers, and a compact courtyard. Reserve clear phased construction footprints within the plot. Around the fortress, provide broad approach lanes from the plot perimeter, sparse cover, and enough open ground for roughly a dozen soldiers to form defensive lines without obstructing attackers. Low terrain boundaries separate neighboring tycoons. Compose several plots in view while making one fortress and its converging approaches the focal structure.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: wave approaches, defender positions, and plot separation should be readable before medieval dressing, although no hard-coded enemy lane is required.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large because multiple plots each hold a fortress, about 12 defenders, and wave staging ground. The theme is sturdy medieval stone tycoon warfare. Route `P0`; all geometry is on one continuous surface with no stated interiors or overhangs.
