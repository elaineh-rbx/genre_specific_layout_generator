# Scene prompt
Create a polished 3D asymmetric-horror experience with an original, whimsical dark-fantasy visual register: a detailed central lobby that blends a cozy small-town area, castle-like streets, snowy woodland, glowing cavern elements, and warm golden-flower gardens. Arrange wooden paths, benches, decorative NPC figures, glowing crystals, magical effects, and cozy pools of light around a central gathering space. Build a separate large 1v4 chase map as one seamless, highly detailed location: dark forests transition into castle-town streets, snowy woods, blue-water caverns, purple-brick ruins, abandoned laboratories, and mysterious corridors. Connect these areas through multiple elevations, looping routes, plazas, narrow hallways, underground passages, caves, bridges, decorative buildings, hidden shortcuts, and short hiding pockets branching from loops rather than trapping dead ends. Scatter physical objective landmarks such as magical generators, ancient rune pillars, and dark fountain structures across the map, with obstacle-like puzzle elements and numerous hiding places. Use tense but readable lighting, volumetric fog, flickering lamps, glowing plants, magical particles, and environmental storytelling throughout.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Survival, specifically 1v4 asymmetric horror centered on evasion and surviving a six-minute timer.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-staged` because the central lobby and chase arena are separate maps. The preset is 1 vs All, replacing its usual warren shape with the staged structure and omitting the escape gate because survivors win by outlasting the timer.

## Config requirements
Draw `cover-los` as vegetation, ruins, street corners, laboratory partitions, cavern rocks, and short hiding pockets. Draw `npc-population` as decorative lobby figures, `water-body` as glowing blue cavern water, `terrain-relief` as traversable elevation changes, and `building-interior` as laboratories and corridors entered from outdoor regions. Also draw the named magical generators, rune pillars, dark fountains, obstacle puzzles, lobby countdown display, crystals, benches, and golden-flower gardens.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly five `spawn-protected` starts in the arena: four survivor positions distributed across loops and one isolated killer start. Place interaction triggers at every rendered generator, rune pillar, and dark fountain. Place lobby-to-arena and return transport markers at the central gathering point and safe arena arrivals. Site ambient sound emitters by biome and objective.

## Layout components
The lobby is a separate cozy gathering map blending small-town, castle-street, snowy woodland, cavern, and flower-garden motifs around wooden paths. The large seamless chase map transitions among dark forest, castle streets, snowy woods, blue-water caverns, purple ruins, laboratories, and mysterious corridors. Plazas, hallways, caves, bridges, underground passages, hidden shortcuts, and short pockets branch from interconnected loops across multiple elevations. Natural and architectural edges enclose the arena. Keep objectives and glowing landmarks readable through fog.

## Render order
Use `topdown` because interconnected pursuit loops, shortcuts, objective access, and safe 1v4 starts must be valid before visual dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge: a detailed lobby plus a large multi-region arena. The theme is whimsical dark fantasy with pixel-inspired magical motifs. Use `P4` for separate maps, `P2` for caves, bridges, and underground overlap, `P3` for outdoor-to-laboratory transitions, `tiered` for relief, and `CHECK` for cavern water.
