# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map with a town-sized, high-detail lobby hub themed around clock people versus couches. Arrange teleport platforms around the hub as gateways to separate maps. Include a summon area within the lobby. One destination is a base-defense city: a defended city base sits at the end of a single clear approach path, with open ground along the route for incoming couch attackers and defensive placement around the city.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Strategy. Couch enemies attack a defended city in waves along a fixed path, making this tower defense despite its unusual theme.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-hub-dungeon`, taken from outside Strategy’s usual set: a safe town-sized lobby feeds a separate instanced base-defense city. Retain the Tower Defense preset with this substituted overall shape; its placement ground, defended core, enemy origin, and continuous lane still define the match.

## Config requirements
`buildzone-grid` draws open defensive placement zones along the approach and around the city edge. `tracker-core` draws the defended city-heart at the lane endpoint. `settlement-density` draws the detailed clock-person lobby town and the compact defended city destination. The lobby also draws a distinct summoning pavilion and several teleport platforms arranged as obvious gateways, with playful clockwork-versus-upholstery motifs throughout.

## Layout requirements
Place one `tracker-core` objective marker on the city-heart, representing the base whose 200 health is handled by gameplay. Place one `spawner-npc` at the far end of the fixed path for couch waves. Place one teleport trigger on every lobby gateway platform, with difficulty-selection markers covering the available choices and Nightmare identified as the hardest. Place the match arrival point near the defended city but outside the enemy path.

## Layout components
Build a huge, high-detail lobby town with a central circulation plaza, surrounding clockwork buildings, a prominent summon pavilion, and gateway platforms distributed around the hub edge. The separate city-defense map has one clear approach path from an outer couch spawn to the fortified city base, with broad deployment ground flanking the lane and additional defensive pads around the city perimeter. Roads connect lobby landmarks; hard map boundaries separate both spaces. Compose the lobby as an expansive hub and the match from high enough to reveal the full route.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the fixed enemy lane must connect spawn to city without ambiguity before isometric dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge because the lobby is town-sized and the battle is a separate map. The theme is whimsical high-detail clockwork people versus animated couches. Use `P4` and `P3` for the hub-to-instanced-map transition, plus `P6` for the valid enemy route.
