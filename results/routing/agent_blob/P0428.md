# Scene prompt
Create a bright, cartoony dinosaur-and-egg themed 3D game map with a compact plot-based layout for 8 players. Arrange eight separate player plots around a shared central area, each plot providing space for dinosaur egg displays and hatched dinosaurs.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation: players operate personal collection plots, hatch eggs, and raid other plots rather than following a race, combat arena, or exploration route.

## Shape and preset
Use `plot-isolated`, with exactly eight independent, equally sized player plots arranged around common ground. The preset is Sandbox, retaining its isolated plots and shared central activity while adapting the resources to stealable dinosaur eggs.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` as the compact shared central area tying all eight plots together, themed with oversized egg and dinosaur motifs. Use `path-circulation` for a clear ring and radial walkways connecting the center to every plot. Each plot must visibly include egg display fixtures and open pens or pads for hatched dinosaurs; these scene-specific structures have no separate canonical option.

## Layout requirements
Place one player spawn-and-claim marker in each of the eight plots, centered near that plot's entrance. Place stealable egg interaction points on the display fixtures within each plot, but do not assume an egg count because none was stated. Keep every display reachable from the circulation route and leave enough clear floor around it for stealing interactions.

## Layout components
Build eight separate player plots in an even ring around one shared central zone. Make the plots equal in area, clearly bounded, and inward-facing, with one entrance from the ring path apiece. Within every plot, reserve distinct areas for dinosaur egg displays and hatched dinosaurs. A continuous outer ring links all plots, while eight short radial paths connect their entrances to the central area. Keep the ground flat and compact, use bright cartoony materials and dinosaur-and-egg props, and enclose the whole small map with a readable decorative boundary. The overview composition should make the eightfold radial symmetry immediately legible.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: establish the cheerful themed plots first, then derive the authoritative top-down arrangement, since exact symmetry matters but no generated maze or circuit is required.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, as requested for eight compact plots and short player-to-player routes. The theme is bright cartoony dinosaur nursery. Use `P0`; all play stays on one flat, contiguous exterior surface with no overhangs or interiors.
