# Scene prompt
Create a 3D obby map built as a small butter-themed tower, a short plainly playable vertical climb rising through stacked platform sections to a summit.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Obby & Platformer, canonically `obby-platformer`, because the requested play is a self-paced vertical obstacle climb.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-tower` for one short ascent with platform sections stacked above each other. Tower Obby is the matching preset, simplified for a plainly playable one-sitting course.

## Config requirements
Draw one continuous physics-valid `path-track` from the tower base to the summit. Use `obstacle-jump` for butter-stick and butter-cube platforms with forgiving gaps, and `obstacle-climb` for short stacks and stepped vertical sections. Draw the summit landing as `winner-zone`. Do not add timers, leaderboards, checkpoints, moving hazards, or lethal surfaces: none are required by this short brief.

## Layout requirements
Place one `winner-zone` on a broad safe summit pad immediately beyond the final climb. No other post-segmentation markers or pickups are required.

## Layout components
Build a small open-sided tower with a generous start pad, several short stacked platform sections, safe intermediate landings, and one clearly visible summit. Shape traversable pieces as oversized sticks, pats, and cubes of butter, with occasional toast-like support slabs, while preserving clean collision silhouettes and legal jumps. Give surfaces a soft glossy yellow finish, rounded corners, pale highlights, and small melting drips that do not obscure walkable edges. Keep the route singular and readable from bottom to top, without branches or hidden correct paths. The surrounding boundary is open void framed by a lightweight tower scaffold. Compose the isometric view to show the base, every stacked section, and the summit payoff in one compact silhouette.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: legal jump spacing and uninterrupted vertical progression must be established first, then the isometric image applies the butter styling.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small because this is a short one-sitting climb. The theme is playful, glossy, oversized butter. `P6` is required for a valid obstacle chain, and `P2` handles stacked platforms that overhang lower sections.
