# Scene prompt

Create a large, manageable free-roam off-roading map in sunlit rural farmland, roughly double the original map size. Lay out muddy trail networks through open fields and wooded sections, with multiple distinct areas sized for cars, utes, and trucks to drive across.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Simulation, specifically free-roam vehicle operation rather than Racing: the author explicitly rules out events and finish lines, so driving and mudding are the activity.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open`, a Shared Persistent World consisting of one contiguous rural map that drivers can explore in any order. Match the Vehicle Sim preset, but omit its extraction-field element because this brief asks for off-roading rather than resource hauling; retain its open-world vehicle circulation.

## Config requirements

Use `path-road-vehicle` for a broad network of muddy vehicle-width trails linking the fields, wooded sections, and distinct off-road areas. The trails must accommodate cars, utes, and trucks, with wider junctions and passing space for the largest vehicles. No additional universal option is required: the woods are part of the environment, and the prompt does not require enterable buildings, water, settlements, NPCs, islands, or pronounced relief.

## Layout requirements

Place non-drawn vehicle spawn markers in a spacious arrival clearing near the map edge, with separate clearances for cars, utes, and trucks. No checkpoints, finish markers, scoring volumes, pickups, or enemy emitters are needed because the experience is free-roam.

## Layout components

Build one large rural zone, roughly double the referenced original’s map area, divided into several readable off-road districts. Open farm fields should occupy broad sunlit clearings; dense wooded sections should frame and separate them. Muddy trails should branch, reconnect, and form multiple exploration loops rather than terminate as race routes. Include broad churned-mud areas, narrower forest tracks, and generous truck-capable clearings. Use grass, packed earth, mud, and tree cover, with an open rural perimeter and a composition that makes the trail network legible across the frame.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down plan; visual terrain character leads, and no strict competitive topology must be validated.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is large because the map is roughly double the original and supports several vehicle districts. The theme is bright sunlit rural farmland with muddy tracks and green woodland. Route `P0`: one continuous, walkable surface has no forced overhang, interior transition, separate map, or validity-by-construction requirement.
