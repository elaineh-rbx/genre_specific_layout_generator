# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] scale — Q: Is this one island you can cross in a few seconds, or a spread of islands with distance between them? A: Use one compact central floating island that players can cross in a few seconds, with smaller nearby decorative islands in the background.
- [author] shape — Q: Do players walk inside the inventory and shop buildings, or interact from outside? A: Players interact with the inventory and shop from outside via clearly marked building entrances or prompts; no interior spaces are needed.
- [author] goal — Q: Do the glowing portals actually take players somewhere, or are they decoration? A: The glowing portals are decorative for this first build and do not take players anywhere.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a colorful futuristic cosmic lobby on one compact central floating island that players can cross in a few seconds. Arrange a central spawn area facing a prominent roll platform, with exterior-only inventory and shop buildings nearby, each having a clearly marked entrance prompt but no interior. Decorate the island with floating crystals, nonfunctional glowing portals, clouds, particles, and a large skybox filled with planets and stars. Place smaller floating islands only as unreachable background scenery, keeping the active island open, polished, and easy to navigate.

## Genre
The genre is Simulation because repeated random rolls, luck progression, collection, inventory, pity, currency, and upgrades form an incremental RNG loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open` in its shared-persistent-world sense: one compact island holds every walkable activity. Preset is none because the Incremental Simulator bundle would add progression gates and extended circulation that this single-lobby build does not request.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for the compact colorful lobby and `station-training` for the visible roll platform as the repeatable interaction that drives progression. The exterior-only inventory and shop façades, floating crystals, decorative portals, clouds, particles, planets, stars, and unreachable background islands are scene-specific image requirements. Do not use `building-interior`, `teleporter-link`, or `island-cluster`, because the author ruled out interiors, portal travel, and playable secondary islands.

## Layout requirements
Place one free-text SpawnZone at the central spawn area, oriented toward the roll platform. Place the `station-training` TriggerZone on that platform. Place two free-text TriggerZones at the marked inventory and shop entrances to open their interfaces from outside. Random rarity generation, aura effects, pity, luck, saving, leaderboard data, UI, sounds, and platform compatibility are mechanics rather than map placements.

## Layout components
The build contains one compact playable floating island, one central spawn, one roll platform, two exterior-only service buildings, decorative crystals and portals, cloud and particle dressing, a planetary skybox, and several smaller unreachable background islands. Keep all three interaction points close together and compose the roll platform as the island's focal center.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the cosmic floating-island look and focal composition lead, while the compact single-surface plan is simple to project.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small because the island is crossed in a few seconds and its roll, shop, and inventory interactions must remain immediately legible. The theme is colorful futuristic cosmic fantasy. `P0` applies because only one island is playable, the portals are decorative, the buildings are façades, and no requested geometry forces an extra pass.
