# Scene prompt
Create a polished 3D futuristic cosmic RNG lobby on one compact central floating island that players can cross in a few seconds. Place the central spawn area and a prominent roll platform near the middle, with separate clearly marked inventory and shop buildings around its edge; these buildings have exterior entrances or interaction prompts only, with no interiors. Arrange floating crystals, decorative glowing portals, clouds, and particle effects around the island, with smaller decorative floating islands in the background. Surround the scene with a vast starry skybox containing distant planets.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation because the repeatable RNG roll, collection, luck growth, and shop economy form an incremental progression loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open` as a small shared persistent world where every player uses the same compact island. Match the Incremental Simulator preset, retaining its central bazaar and circulation while dropping `gate-progression` because the brief specifies no locked tiers or physical progression walls.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for the roll platform and exterior-facing inventory and shop buildings arranged around it. Use `path-circulation` for short, readable walkways connecting spawn, rolling, inventory, and shopping. Use `trigger-task` for the prominent physical roll platform, with its visible platform drawn into the scene. Draw floating crystals, decorative nonfunctional portals, clouds, particles, background islets, planets, and stars as scene-specific geometry and dressing; do not treat the portals as teleporters or the buildings as enterable interiors.

## Layout requirements
Place one central player spawn facing the roll platform. Place the functional half of `trigger-task` over that single platform so rolling occurs at the visible station. Site exterior interaction markers at the inventory and shop entrances. Nothing is placed on the decorative portals or background islands.

## Layout components
Build one compact, walkable central island with a middle spawn plaza and prominent roll dais. Ring it with two distinct edge buildings, one for inventory and one for the shop, connected by short paths. Cluster floating crystals and glowing decorative portals around the usable island without obstructing circulation. Place several smaller inaccessible islands, clouds, and particles behind the play space, all enclosed visually by a deep starfield skybox with distant planets. Keep the playable boundary clear at the island rim and compose the roll platform as the focal point.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the visual hierarchy and cosmic atmosphere lead, while the compact hub topology is simple and does not need computed construction.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small because the playable island is crossable in seconds, despite the vast-looking backdrop. The theme is polished futuristic cosmic fantasy with vivid glow effects. Use `P0`; the background islands are scenery, nobody flies between layers, no interiors are entered, and one top-down surface captures all playable geometry.
