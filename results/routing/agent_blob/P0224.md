# Scene prompt
Create a stylized 3D haunted-school roleplay map set primarily inside a small, detailed school building. Arrange a compact network of corridors connecting a handful of classrooms and hidden secret rooms, with a dedicated VIP-only area and a shop space within the school. Use a bright, polished presentation while retaining a haunted atmosphere, with the interior designed for exploration and clear room-to-room sightlines.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, with Survival secondary because the midnight monster creates repeatable survival rounds inside the roleplay setting.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single`: the entire playable map is one compact school interior, with no outside-to-inside transition. The closest preset is Mascot Horror, retaining its enclosed horror setting and hostile origin but omitting its escape gate because winning means outlasting the round rather than leaving.

## Config requirements
Use `path-street` for broad, readable school corridors linking the classrooms, shop, VIP area, and secrets. Add the visible forms of a clearly marked VIP-only room, an in-school defense-and-flashlight shop, and several concealed secret rooms as scene-specific geometry. The haunted dressing should remain bright and polished, with supernatural details, strong room identities, and clear sightlines rather than murky clutter.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawner-npc` for the monster in a concealed central service area, positioned so it can reach the main corridor network without appearing directly beside players. No escape trigger is needed; survival is timer-based. Reserve access boundaries for the VIP room and shop counter after segmentation.

## Layout components
Build a small central corridor loop with a handful of classrooms branching from it, a compact shop near the most legible junction, and a VIP room off a quieter side hall. Hide secret rooms behind believable wall panels or classroom storage, while preserving navigable links and room-to-room views. Use polished school flooring, bright practical lights, haunted accent lighting, lockers, desks, noticeboards, and restrained supernatural props. The building shell is the hard boundary, and the composition should show the corridor network and key rooms in one readable cutaway-like frame.

## Render order
Render in `isometric` order: the stylized haunted look leads, and the top-down plan can be projected from the authoritative isometric image because no procedural topology is required.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by the requested handful of rooms. The theme is bright polished haunted school. Use `P0`: one interior surface and no exterior transition, overhang, separate map, or construction-valid structure adds cost.
