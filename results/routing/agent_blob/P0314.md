# Scene prompt
Create a bright tropical ocean map with three separate sea regions, each containing five small-to-medium islands visible from one another across the water. Arrange tropical island settlements and quest areas around the shores, with fruit spawn spots throughout the islands; include a central fruit shop with a fruit-rolling NPC and permanent-fruit displays, plus dedicated transformation and trading rooms. Use distinct gateway or dock areas with a guide NPC to connect the first sea to the second and the second to the third, making each new sea feel like a separate destination. Mystical fruits, including vapor, yeti, kitsune, werewolf, dragon, and nacho fruit, stand out with special visual effects.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG, with Adventure secondary. Island quests, fighting, equipment-like fruit powers, shops, and gated progression between seas define the RPG, while sailing and fruit hunting supply exploration.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-chaptered`: First, Second, and Third Sea are three genuinely separate maps, each containing five mutually visible islands. The preset is Action RPG, with its hub, hostile areas, and emitters retained but its hub-and-dungeon shape replaced by the explicitly chaptered seas.

## Config requirements
Use `safezone-town` for tropical shore settlements and quest-service ground protected from combat. Use `social-hub` for the central fruit shop, rolling NPC, permanent-fruit displays, and access to transformation and trading rooms. Draw `hazard-aggro` as fight areas outside settlements. Use `teleporter-link` for gateway or dock structures joining First to Second Sea and Second to Third. Add `island-cluster` with five islands per sea, `water-body` for each ocean, `npc-population` for quest givers, fruit roller, and sea guides, and `building-interior` for the shop and two dedicated rooms.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` emitters in island combat and quest areas, buffered from shops and arrivals. Place `collectible-nodes` as fruit spawn markers throughout all fifteen islands, with rare mystical nodes distributed farther from safe hubs. Place paired `teleporter-link` triggers at the two progression connections and their destination arrivals. Place `npc-population` markers at services and guide points. Give vapor, yeti, kitsune, werewolf, dragon, and nacho fruits six distinct highlighted display or spawn treatments.

## Layout components
Build exactly three sea maps with five small-to-medium islands each, for fifteen islands total. Within every sea, space islands across open ocean but keep all five mutually visible. Each cluster includes shore settlements, quest areas, combat ground, fruit-search terrain, docks, and readable sailing corridors. The primary service island holds one fruit shop with permanent displays organized into common, uncommon, epic, legendary, and mystical tiers, one fruit-rolling NPC, one transformation room, and one trading room. Distinct docks and guide stations gate the later seas. Compose each sea as a coherent tropical archipelago with clear destination silhouettes.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: island spacing, visible sailing links, safe-to-combat separation, and the ordered sea transitions must be fixed in authoritative plans before tropical isometric dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is huge, driven by three seas and fifteen islands. The theme is bright tropical anime-pirate fantasy with high-impact mystical fruit effects. Use `P4` for separate sea maps, `P3` for entered service rooms and linked transitions, and `CHECK` for ocean traversal and island clusters.
