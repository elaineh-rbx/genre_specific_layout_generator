# Scene prompt
Create a 3D third-person roleplay hangout scene on a hill at sunset. The player spawn sits on the hillside in the foreground, looking downhill toward a road running across the lower area; across the road stands a massive IKEA-style store shown as an exterior only. Frame the scene around the open hill, road, large store facade, and warm sunset view.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim: the author explicitly describes an open-ended third-person roleplay and hangout without a shopping or job loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, taken from outside this genre’s usual shape set, because the scene is one contiguous outdoor hillside that players can roam freely. The preset is none; the housing, pet, dress-up, and town-life presets do not describe this focused scenic hangout.

## Config requirements
Use `terrain-relief` for the broad hill descending toward the road. Use `landmark-focal` for the massive IKEA-style exterior across the road, sized and positioned as the scene’s dominant orientation anchor. Add the cross-slope road, open grass, and warm sunset sky as scene-specific image geometry. Do not use `building-interior`: the store is explicitly a facade-only exterior that players never enter.

## Layout requirements
Place one scene-specific player spawn on the upper foreground hillside, oriented downhill so the road, store facade, and sunset view form the first composition. No shop interaction zones, job markers, interior links, or vehicle systems are required.

## Layout components
Build one open grassy hill occupying the foreground and middle ground, falling toward a road that runs laterally across the lower slope. Put the massive IKEA-style store on level ground beyond the road, with a broad blue-and-yellow facade, entrance-looking frontage that remains sealed, and enough setback to read at full scale. Keep the hill mostly open for walking, with sparse shrubs or rocks that do not block the view. The outer scene boundary can dissolve into distant terrain and sunset haze. Compose the map from the hillside spawn downward, using the road as a horizontal divider and the store as the large background anchor.

## Render order
Render in `isometric` order: the authored sunset composition and relative visual scale are authoritative, while the simple open plan can be projected afterward.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, driven by the massive store and the viewing distance across the road. The theme is warm sunset suburban surrealism. Use `tiered` because the hill has strong relief without overhanging surfaces; no `P3` applies because the store is exterior only.
