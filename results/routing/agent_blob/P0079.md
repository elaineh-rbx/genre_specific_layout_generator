# Scene prompt
Create a compact indoor hide-and-seek map set entirely inside a small house for up to 8 players. Lay out clear connected rooms with distinct hiding spots throughout the house interior.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Party & Casual because the house is built specifically for hide-and-seek rounds.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single`, taken from outside the genre’s usual shape list, because the entire map is one small house interior and players never cross to an exterior. Use preset none: replacing the Childhood Game preset’s open bounded shape and dropping its maze and social hub leaves only the general concealment concept.

## Config requirements
Apply `cover-los` as room-specific hiding clusters: wardrobes, beds, sofas, cabinets, curtains, and other large furniture that interrupt sightlines while leaving clear walkable gaps. Draw a compact set of distinct connected rooms with readable doorways and no exterior environment.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` as exactly eight possible round-start markers for the stated maximum of eight players, distributing them among reachable rooms without placing anyone inside furniture. Reserve one suitable marker for the seeker, separated by walls and travel distance from the hider starts. Place `trigger-scoring` as tag-detection regions for found hiders. Keep every marker connected through the house’s doorway network.

## Layout components
Build one compact house interior as the full bounded play space. Arrange a central hall connecting a living room, kitchen or dining area, bedrooms, bathroom, and storage spaces, with clear doors and circulation loops rather than isolated rooms. Give each room distinct furniture-based hiding spots while retaining enough open floor for pursuit. Solid exterior walls enclose the map; there is no playable yard or outside transition. Compose the cutaway house so all rooms, connectors, and major hiding clusters remain legible from above.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative because room connectivity, doorway access, and the distribution of hiding spaces must remain coherent, though no procedural maze blueprint is needed.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by one compact house and a maximum of eight players. The theme is a familiar, readable domestic hide-and-seek interior. Use `P0`; the map is interior-only rather than an outside-to-inside transition, and it has one non-overlapping floor with no separate maps or volumetric traversal.
