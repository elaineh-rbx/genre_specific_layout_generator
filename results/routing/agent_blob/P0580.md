# Scene prompt
Create a compact, bright stylized high-fantasy sky-island world with a grand colorful stone castle on the central floating island. Up to twenty nearby floating islands connect by short bridges, forming a welcoming explorable cluster; hanging vines trail beneath the islands. Surrounding islands hold small village housing, farms, swimming areas, trees, and jungle vegetation, with open sky visible around and below the archipelago.

# Agent decision
## Genre
This is `No Genre`: the author explicitly wants peaceful exploration and hanging out, without a game loop.

## Shape and preset
No genre shape is assigned. Keep the default exterior enclosure, single-surface verticality, one-zone count, dressed structure, and grounded-surface play space; the bridges make the islands one walkable cluster rather than separate maps, and players are not asked to fly. Use the Explorable Place preset.

## Config requirements
Use `landmark-focal` for the grand colorful stone castle on the central island. Use `path-circulation` for short bridges linking the cluster, and `vignette-photo` for composed castle, village, farm, jungle, and swimming-area views. Add `island-cluster` for up to twenty nearby floating landmasses, `settlement-density` for small village housing, and `water-body` for swimmable pools. Use `social-hub` for welcoming open gathering ground near the castle. Hanging vines, farms, trees, and dense jungle vegetation are visible freeform geometry.

## Layout requirements
Nothing needs post-segmentation placement. There are no gameplay checkpoints, pickups, triggers, spawners, scoring areas, or teleporters.

## Layout components
Build one large central island with the castle and social plaza, surrounded by up to twenty smaller islands at short bridge-walk distances. Group village homes and farms on several calmer islands, swimming pools on accessible low islands, and thick jungle on greener outer islands. Connect every inhabited island into a clear bridge network with no isolated required destination. Trail long vines beneath island edges and leave open sky visible through gaps and below the archipelago. Keep the cluster compact in frame, with the castle centered and outer islands forming an irregular welcoming ring.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: establish the bright fantasy composition first, then derive the top-down layout from it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large, driven by up to twenty islands despite compact spacing. The theme is bright, colorful, welcoming high fantasy. Use `CHECK` because swimmable water and open-air gaps around floating islands require confirming the represented play volume does not self-occlude.
