## Enriched image prompt
Create a small 3D hide-and-seek map entirely inside one ordinary house, sized for about six players. Arrange a compact set of connected normal rooms with short circulation between them, and furnish each room with recognizable household objects that players can disguise themselves as. Keep furniture dense enough to break sightlines and offer hiding choices, but preserve clear search routes so the house remains easy to navigate and players remain findable.

## Genre
The genre is Party & Casual.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single`, a Zone (Single Interior) taken from outside Party & Casual's usual shape set because the complete map is one compact house interior. The preset is none: Childhood Game suggests a lobby and maze-like warren that would contradict the requested small, easy-to-search house.

## Config requirements
Use `cover-los` for room-specific clusters of normal furniture that break sightlines and double as disguise forms. No lobby, maze segment, spectator area, or exterior is requested.

## Layout requirements
Use `spawn-protected` for six player SpawnZones distributed among the connected rooms without clustering at one doorway. Use `spawner-npc`, taken from the shared catalogue, for one Spawner representing the grandma's entry point, sited in the central circulation area so every room is reachable without an immediate dead end.

## Layout components
The house contains a compact group of normal connected rooms, short halls or door openings, furniture clusters in every room, and clear paths around those clusters. The playable boundary is the interior shell of the single house. Compose the cutaway so all major rooms and the routes between them are readable in one frame.

## Render order
Use `topdown` because connected circulation and fair access from the grandma's starting area to every hiding room should be established before furnishing and visual dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use small scale because individual furniture forms must remain legible as disguises in a room-detail map. The theme is a normal cozy domestic house. `P0` is sufficient because this is one connected interior with no exterior transition, overhanging playable floor, or procedural maze.
