# Scene prompt
Create a 3D hide-and-seek map set inside a small single house sized for about 6 players. Arrange normal furnished rooms with everyday household furniture and objects that provide hiding spots throughout the house.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Party & Casual because this is a house-based hide-and-seek game in which players disguise themselves as household props to evade a grandma seeker.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single`, taken from outside the genre’s usual shape list, because one enclosed house interior is the entire map and nobody crosses an exterior boundary. Use preset none: the Childhood Game preset’s open shape, maze, and social hub do not describe this compact furnished house.

## Config requirements
Apply `cover-los` as normal household furniture and object clusters that create concealment in every room: sofas, chairs, tables, cabinets, beds, lamps, boxes, and similar everyday props. Provide enough distinct objects for player disguises without packing rooms so densely that movement or visual inspection becomes impossible.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` as round-start markers sized for about six players, distributed across reachable rooms and clear of furniture footprints. Give the grandma seeker one separated start position with no immediate line of sight to the hiding starts. Place `trigger-scoring` as catch-detection regions associated with the seeker-player contact logic. Mark eligible disguise objects across the rooms for prop selection, while keeping that transformation behavior out of the rendered geometry.

## Layout components
Build one small single-house interior with a connected living room, kitchen or dining room, bedrooms, bathroom, hall, and storage spaces. Use clear doorways and at least one circulation loop so pursuit does not collapse into a single chokepoint. Furnish every room with recognizable household props at varied sizes, leaving walkable lanes around them and multiple believable hiding positions. Solid exterior walls bound the play space, with no playable outside. Compose the house as a readable cutaway showing room connections and furniture clusters.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative because door connectivity, circulation, and disguise-object distribution must remain playable, though the house is not a procedural maze.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by one house for about six players. The theme is ordinary domestic hide-and-seek with a playful prop-disguise tone. Use `P0`; this is one interior-only, single-level surface with no outside transition, overhang, separate map, or volumetric play.
