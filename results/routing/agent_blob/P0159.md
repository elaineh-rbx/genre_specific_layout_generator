# Scene prompt
Create a 3D multiplayer escape map set across a dense cluster of connected city blocks. Arrange buildings along intersecting streets, with a central or side park, tunnels linking parts of the block network, scattered obstacles, and accessible hiding spots within the urban layout. Include a clear starting point and multiple checkpoints distributed through the streets, buildings, park, and tunnels.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Survival. The endless distance loop is driven by evading a powerful pursuing NPC, not by auto-running or reaching a finish line.

## Shape and preset
Use `warren-looping` for a few interconnected city blocks whose streets, park routes, interiors, and tunnels form continuous chase loops with zero dead ends. The preset is none: the endless urban distance chase contradicts the exit-based Survival presets and is best configured directly.

## Config requirements
Draw `cover-los` as accessible hiding recesses inside buildings, behind park structures, around alley corners, and in tunnel alcoves, each at least four studs deep on its entry axis. Use `building-interior` for the named accessible building spaces crossed from the streets. Add scattered urban obstacles—barriers, debris, parked vehicles, and construction clutter—without sealing the looped routes.

## Layout requirements
Place one clear player start marker at a protected edge of the street network. Place `spawner-npc` origins for Invincible around the connected block network, positioned out of direct sight but able to enter every chase loop. Place multiple scene-specific checkpoint and respawn markers across streets, accessible building interiors, the park, and tunnels; distribute them by region rather than clustering them near the start. There is no exit trigger or winner zone because the route is explicitly endless and resets a caught player to the starting point.

## Layout components
Build a dense cluster of a few connected city blocks with intersecting streets, lined buildings, alleys, one central or side park, and tunnels linking separated parts of the network beneath surface routes. Connect all major areas into loops suitable for continuous pursuit. Include accessible hiding spots and obstacle clusters while retaining readable clearances. Bound the compact city with blocked streets and continuous facade edges. Compose the park as a visual relief area within the denser street grid, with tunnel entries and major intersections clearly legible.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, then derive the isometric image; broken loops or accidental dead ends would undermine an endless chase.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a medium scale because the brief specifies only a few dense, replayable blocks. The theme is colorful Roblox-style urban action horror. Route with `P6` for valid loop topology, `P3` for street-to-building transitions, and `P2` because tunnels run beneath surface streets.
