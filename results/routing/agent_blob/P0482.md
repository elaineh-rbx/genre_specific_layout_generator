# Scene prompt
Create a small, focused 3D cartoon town trapped inside a notebook world, rendered as uncoloured, wonky pencil-like drawings on paper. Arrange around five or six simple town buildings along short paths in a compact central cluster, with static little cartoon people placed as non-moving props nearby. Include an escape-focused key-making area or workshop within the town as the central destination, surrounded by the buildings that can be unlocked and upgraded. Frame the map with notebook paper ground, sketchy linework, and page-like edges or margins.
