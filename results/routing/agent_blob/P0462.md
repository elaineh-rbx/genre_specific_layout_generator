# Scene prompt
Create a 3D basketball park centered on one full court, with a small walkable area immediately surrounding it and the rest of the park serving as a backdrop. Use a sunny daytime setting.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant and only genre is Sports, centered on play across one outdoor basketball court.

## Shape and preset
Use the single `space-bounded` shape for the rigid full-court perimeter and its immediate walkable apron. The preset is Court Sport, with no stadium additions.

## Config requirements
Draw `trigger-bounds` as the full rectangular court boundary and painted basketball markings. Draw `trigger-scoring` as two visible hoop-and-backboard assemblies centered at opposite ends. Draw `startpoint-play` as the center-circle start and balanced player reset marks. Frame the court with a compact paved walkable area, then transition quickly into a sunny park backdrop of grass, trees, and distant park furnishings.

## Layout requirements
Place one `trigger-bounds` detection perimeter exactly along the court lines. Place two `trigger-scoring` cylinders through the two rims, one at each end. Place `startpoint-play` markers at center court and in mirrored positions for opposing sides. Keep all markers inside the court and reserve the surrounding apron for circulation rather than play logic.

## Layout components
Build one standard-proportioned full outdoor basketball court as the central zone. It includes two painted halves, center line and circle, sidelines, baselines, free-throw lanes, arcs, and three-point lines. Install exactly two hoops with backboards on opposite baselines. Wrap the court in a narrow, continuous paved apron that players can walk around, with clear access at the sides and corners. Beyond that apron, use non-playable park backdrop: level lawn, scattered shade trees, and restrained distant paths or benches. Keep the boundary visually open and the court dominant in frame under bright daytime sunlight.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: render the plan first and make it authoritative because court symmetry, line geometry, hoop placement, and trigger alignment must be exact; dress the sunny park view from that plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by one full court plus only a narrow walkable surround. The theme is a lively, clean urban basketball park on a sunny day. Route through `P0`; the court and its apron form one flat, unobstructed surface.
