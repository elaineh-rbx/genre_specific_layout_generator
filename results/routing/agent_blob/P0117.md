# Scene prompt
Create a detailed 3D PvP arena as a compact fantasy colosseum for up to 8 players. A broad central combat floor sits inside circular stone walls, with repeating arches around the perimeter and tiered spectator stands rising behind them.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Action: the brief describes a shared free-for-all clash space rather than ranged firing lanes or a formally scored sports field.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded` as one collision-clean circular combat floor enclosed by the colosseum. The preset is none: the prompt specifies a colosseum and spectator architecture but does not choose the hazards, cover arrays, or chokepoints that would keep an Action preset recognisable.

## Config requirements
Use `spectator-zone` for raised ringside stone seating behind the arena arches. Use the visible geometry of `spawn-protected` for discreet recessed spawn alcoves around the perimeter, screened so returning fighters do not appear directly in an opponent’s attack line. Keep the active combat floor broad, flat, and free from incidental tripping geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` as eight evenly spaced respawn positions, one for each supported player, inside the screened perimeter alcoves. Orient each toward the central floor and keep every spawn clear of immediate combat risk. No NPC emitters, buffs, or scoring volumes are requested.

## Layout components
The central zone is a broad circular stone combat floor sized for up to eight fighters. A ring of repeating arches defines its edge, with shallow protected alcoves incorporated between selected arch bays. Tiered spectator stands rise behind the arch ring, remaining visually prominent but outside the active fight surface. Heavy circular stone walls enclose the entire colosseum. Use worn masonry, carved trim, banners, stair aisles through the seating, and strong radial repetition for detail without cluttering the collision-clean floor. Compose the arena concentrically, with the combat floor as the unmistakable focal point and the tiered stands framing it.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, because the simple bounded floor does not need computed topology and the fantasy architecture should lead the composition. Derive the top-down plan from it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by a maximum of eight players in one arena. The theme is a detailed medieval-fantasy stone colosseum. Use `tiered` for the rising spectator stands without overhang; no further modifier is forced.
