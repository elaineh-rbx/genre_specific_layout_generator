# Scene prompt
Create a detailed modern city neighbourhood map with a focused, fully walkable area. Arrange streets between varied buildings, with parks and interior-style decorative detail throughout; do not place labels, signs with text, or ground markings that function as labels anywhere in the map.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Simulation: the original game is a clicker with rebirth progression, while the city is its walkable setting rather than a separate roleplay game.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open` as one contiguous neighbourhood players may explore in any order. Preset is none: Incremental Simulator matches the clicker mechanics, but its physical storefronts and purchase gates are absent, leaving only ordinary circulation and no recognizable preset bundle.

## Config requirements
Use `path-circulation` for the fully walkable street and sidewalk network connecting the neighbourhood. Use `settlement-density` for a compact modern urban district of varied buildings and parks rather than isolated structures. Interior-style decorative detail should enrich facades, windows, lobbies visible through glass, street furniture, and sheltered frontage without creating enterable interiors. Do not draw labels, text signs, named zones, or label-like ground markings anywhere.

## Layout requirements
Place one ordinary player spawn marker in a safe open part of the street network, clear of traffic geometry and with immediate access to a park and main street. No physical `station-training` or `trigger-rebirth` is needed because tapping, rebirth, and the owner-only admin panel are screen interfaces rather than map geometry. No checkpoints, collectibles, shop triggers, or labeled wayfinding markers are requested.

## Layout components
Build a neighbourhood-sized district with several connected streets, sidewalks, varied modern building masses, and multiple small parks woven between blocks. Streets should form a legible connected network rather than a single corridor, with alleys and pedestrian cut-throughs adding detail without trapping players. Use trees, benches, lamps, planters, glass frontage, facade recesses, and interior-like visible dressing as props. Enclose the focused play area naturally with perimeter building fronts and blocked side streets. Compose the isometric view to show the street hierarchy, parks, and facade variety without any readable text.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative, then derive the top-down plan, because architectural detail and urban composition lead while no exact gameplay topology is required.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale for the explicitly neighbourhood-sized area. The theme is highly detailed contemporary city with polished parks and decorative storefront frontage. Use `P0`: all traversal remains on one connected outdoor surface, with no enterable interiors or overhang-dependent play.
