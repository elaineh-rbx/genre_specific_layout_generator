# Scene prompt
Create a compact open-world storm-chasing map centered on a large El Reno–style city. The central city has grid-block streets, one-story suburban houses, gas stations, and a complex highway interchange. Long gray asphalt rural roads extend a few minutes’ drive from the city through broad, flat crop fields, isolated farms, and distant power transmission towers, leaving wide open intercept space around tornado funnels. Use gentle terrain hills around and beyond the city to break sightlines while preserving the vast plains feel. Include a lobby or garage area with a physical vehicle-shop point, plus space for heavy armored interceptor vehicles and fast probe cars to access the road network.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation. This is a storm-chasing vehicle and field-work simulation whose scoring comes from deploying probes, not a race or primarily surviving disasters.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, one shared persistent road-and-plains world explored freely. The preset is Vehicle Sim, retaining its open world and vehicle routes while replacing its unrelated resource field with storm-intercept space.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for the connected city grid, highway interchange, and long rural roads. Use `trigger-task` for a visible scientific probe-deployment setup. Use `hazard-kill` for tornado danger layered over open fields. Use `social-hub` for the lobby garage and physical interceptor shop. Use `settlement-density` for the large central suburban city, and `terrain-relief` for gentle hills that break sightlines without losing the plains character.

## Layout requirements
The `trigger-task` placement is a set of probe-scoring deployment zones in wide fields beside rural roads, concentrated ahead of likely funnel approaches rather than inside city blocks. Place one garage shop interaction marker in the lobby, vehicle spawns for heavy interceptors and fast probe cars beside the garage, and safe player arrival markers clear of traffic.

## Layout components
Center the map on a large rectilinear city grid with one-story houses, several gas stations, and a complex but connected highway interchange. Roads radiate through broad crop fields to isolated farms and distant transmission towers, providing a few minutes of driving before the boundary. Reserve multiple broad, obstacle-light tornado intercept fields with roadside access. Put the garage at the city edge near a major road. Gentle hills ring and partly screen the city; the outer boundary remains open-looking but finite.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: road connectivity, interchange access, and unobstructed intercept fields must lead before the isometric view adds architecture and storm atmosphere.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is large but compact enough for mobile streaming. The theme is low-detail storm-country realism with gray asphalt and vast plains. Use `tiered` because gentle hills alter one continuous surface without overhang; otherwise this remains the `P0` baseline.
