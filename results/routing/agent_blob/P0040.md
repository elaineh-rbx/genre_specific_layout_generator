# Scene prompt
Create a naturalistic, stormy 3D migration map for a duck traveling from New Zealand toward Canada in several staged flight legs. Each compact ocean segment shows rough seas below and threatening storm fronts ahead, with open sky and distant horizon framing the route.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Adventure: the experience is a directed migration journey through sequential locations rather than endless automatic running or survival in one arena.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-chaptered` for several genuinely separate flight-leg maps that do not coexist on one surface. No Adventure preset remains recognisable without adding story gates, one-way drops, quest targets, or fast travel, so the preset is none.

## Config requirements
Use `hazard-kill` for dangerous storm cells and the rough ocean below, both presented as regions the flying duck must avoid. Each segment also requires a readable open-air route, layered storm clouds, a visible distant horizon, and ocean surface geometry; these are direct scene requirements without a more specific loaded option.

## Layout requirements
Place one non-visual stage-transition trigger at the far end of every flight leg except the final one, sited beyond the segment’s storm route so reaching it advances the migration. Each compact segment needs one safe aerial arrival volume at its near horizon. Keep a continuous navigable flight envelope between arrival and transition, with hazards distributed around rather than completely sealing that envelope.

## Layout components
Build several compact ocean segments, each consisting of rough sea below, open flight space, storm fronts ahead, and distant horizon framing. Shape the playable route through gaps among dark cloud banks, rain curtains, and turbulent zones while leaving enough vertical and lateral room for a duck to maneuver. Use horizon and weather to conceal every segment boundary; no artificial walls should appear. Compose each map as a forward-looking journey image, with safe entry behind, hazard layers in the middle, and the transition horizon ahead.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish a continuous hazard-free route through each storm field first, then dress the authoritative plans as naturalistic isometrics.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium overall because it comprises several compact legs. The theme is naturalistic, stormy, threatening ocean migration. Use `P4` for separate flight maps and `CHECK` for volumetric aerial play above water.
