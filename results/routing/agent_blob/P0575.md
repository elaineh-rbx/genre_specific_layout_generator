# Scene prompt

Create a compact 3D concert arena for a slick modern stadium show, with a main stage accessible to all players and a prominent performance microphone at centre stage. Arrange audience space facing the stage within the compact arena.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Entertainment: this is a participatory concert venue consumed and performed in rather than won. No secondary genre is needed.

## Shape and preset

Use `venue-stage`, a compact arena whose stage and audience are oriented toward one another. Match the Performance Venue preset because its audience space, gathering floor, and arrival composition fit the brief.

## Config requirements

Use `spectator-bleachers` for compact raked audience seating that preserves rear sightlines toward the stage, and `social-hub` for an open standing audience floor immediately in front. Use the visible part of `spawn-first-reveal` as a clean arena entrance facing the show rather than backstage. Draw a slick modern main stage, an accessible route onto it, and one prominent microphone at centre stage.

## Layout requirements

Place `spawn-first-reveal` once at the audience-side entrance, oriented so arrivals see the stage first. Add one uncatalogued microphone interaction placement precisely at centre stage so any player can use it. Keep the stage access open to the audience floor; no performer-only gate or backstage restriction is needed.

## Layout components

Create three aligned zones: the raised main stage at one end, the standing audience floor in front, and compact raked seating behind or around the rear of that floor. Connect the floor to the stage with a clearly visible player-accessible stair or ramp. Place exactly one microphone at stage center. Arena walls define the boundary, and every seating row and open standing area faces the performance. Frame from the audience side so the microphone anchors the composition.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render the modern concert look, stage focal point, and converging audience sightlines first, then derive the straightforward plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is medium but compact for an arena, driven by one stage and one concentrated audience bowl. The theme is slick modern stadium concert. Use `tiered` over baseline `P0` because the raked seating has elevation without overhanging surfaces; no `P2`, `P3`, or `P4` is required.
