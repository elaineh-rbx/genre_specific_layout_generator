# Scene prompt
Create a realistic cinematic horror pizzeria roleplay map based on a fictional animatronic restaurant film setting. Build the full pizzeria interior with its main dining area, stage, service and back-of-house spaces, connected corridors, and other recognizable restaurant rooms, plus a small exterior parking lot and surrounding street framing the building.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim: players select characters and act within a cinematic horror location, with no stated chase, survival round, or combat loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded`, taken from outside the genre’s usual shape set, for one contained restaurant property encompassing the pizzeria, parking lot, and framing street. The preset is Morph Roleplay, but its usual settlement shape is replaced because this is one venue; retain its social gathering, cinematic vignette, and focal landmark options.

## Config requirements
Use `building-interior` for the full pizzeria entered from the exterior lot. Use `social-hub` for the main dining room, sized as the central roleplay gathering space. Use `vignette-photo` for highly detailed cinematic corners in the stage, dining, corridors, and back-of-house rooms. Use `landmark-focal` for the recognizable pizzeria facade anchoring the exterior. Add the performance stage, kitchen and service spaces, offices, storage rooms, connected corridors, parking lot, and street as scene-specific image geometry.

## Layout requirements
Nothing additional must be placed after segmentation. The all-character morph picker and gamepasses are GUI and economy systems, not map geometry, so they do not create display rooms, character stands, triggers, or shop zones.

## Layout components
Center the property on a complete pizzeria building facing a small parking lot and short surrounding street segment. Inside, connect a large dining room and raised character stage to service counters, kitchen, offices, storage, utility rooms, and back corridors. Give public rooms broad circulation while making staff areas narrower and more atmospheric, but keep every route readable for roleplay. Outside, include marked parking spaces, curbs, lamps, loading access, and restrained roadside context. Use realistic worn restaurant finishes, cinematic practical lighting, signage, tables, booths, kitchen equipment, posters, cables, and maintenance props. The street and property edge form the hard boundary, and the composition should reveal both the facade and the interior organization.

## Render order
Render in `isometric` order: cinematic realism and recognizable room dressing are authoritative, while the bounded restaurant plan can be projected afterward.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, driven by a full restaurant interior plus a small exterior lot. The theme is realistic cinematic animatronic horror. Use `P3` because play crosses between the street-side exterior and the pizzeria interior.
