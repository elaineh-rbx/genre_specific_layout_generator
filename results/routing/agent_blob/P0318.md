# Scene prompt

Create a premium 3D mining world with three separate lobby places connected by portals, centered on a high-tech surface Main Hub. The Main Hub contains portal stations, leaderboard displays, and personal ore display cases. A separate Cave Arena serves as the mine entrance: a sprawling, dark multi-tiered chasm roughly 300 studs deep, visually descending through five playable cave-depth tiers. The upper caves have dusty volumetric air, rock walls with compacted dirt and copper, and torchlight; each lower tier grows darker and more atmospheric, progressing through deep iron, sunken gold, and frost sapphire, with metallic, emissive, or vaporous ore veins embedded in the walls. A hidden separate black-market lobby is a dim alleyway containing the upgrade forge and altar. Deep caves transition toward ethereal glowing mist and cosmic visual accents, while Levels 6–10 remain unbuilt for later progression.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Simulation: mining, extraction, selling, equipment upgrades, and gated depth progression form the core loop.

## Shape and preset

Use `hub-portals`, taken from outside Simulation’s usual shapes, because the Main Hub explicitly routes players to separate lobby places. Start from the Mining & Extraction preset, substitute this portal-hub shape for `world-underground`, and drop vehicle hauling roads while retaining the mine and ore-node structure.

## Config requirements

Apply `social-hub` to the high-tech Main Hub with leaderboard displays and personal ore cases. The Cave Arena is a 300-stud multi-tiered chasm with five descending playable tiers, torches, dusty air above, and increasingly dark fog and cosmic accents below. The black-market alley contains the visible forge and altar. Portal stations visibly anchor all inter-place links.

## Layout requirements

Place `collectible-nodes` as ore veins against mineable wall segments by tier: compacted dirt and copper in the upper cave, then deep iron, sunken gold, and frost sapphire across the five built depths. Place portal endpoints in the Main Hub, Cave Arena entrance, and black-market alley, with every remote endpoint returning to the Main Hub.

## Layout components

Build three separate places: one high-tech Main Hub, one hidden dim alley lobby, and one Cave Arena. The arena descends roughly 300 studs through five clearly separated tiers linked by ramps or mine passages. Rock walls, ledges, torch stations, and ore-bearing faces define each tier. Keep Levels 6–10 absent. Frame the hub around its portals and the arena to reveal the full vertical descent.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, because portal connectivity and the five-tier descent must remain spatially coherent.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is large, driven by three places and a 300-stud mine. The theme is premium high-tech mining descending into cosmic darkness. Use `P4` for separate maps, `P2` for vertically overlapping chasm tiers, and `P3` for the surface-to-interior mine transition.
