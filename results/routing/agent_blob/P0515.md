# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] theme — Q: What kind of building - a city office block, a fantasy tower, something cartoonish? A: Use a small modern city office block with a clear exterior climbing route and a rooftop.
- [author] goal — Q: Should the jump from the top be survivable, or is the fall meant to kill and reset you? A: The jump should be survivable so players can keep having fun jumping from the top; use a safe landing area at the base.
- [agent_inferred] route loop — Q: How does the player begin another climb after jumping? A: Put the climb entrance beside the safe landing area so the rooftop jump naturally loops back to the route start.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a small modern city office block with a clear exterior climbing course rising to an accessible rooftop. At the roof edge, provide an obvious jump-off point aligned over a broad safe landing area at the base, with the climb entrance immediately beside that landing so players can repeat the ascent and jump.

## Genre
The genre is Obby & Platformer because player-controlled climbing and jumping along a designed route are the core activity.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-tower`: the route wraps or stacks up the office exterior to the rooftop above. Preset is none because the Tower Obby bundle would add timed obstacles and a winner reward that the simple repeatable climb-and-jump loop does not request.

## Config requirements
Use `path-track` for the ordered physics-valid exterior ascent and `obstacle-climb` for the façade climbing geometry. Add a scene-specific broad padded or otherwise visibly safe landing area at the base, directly below the rooftop jump point and beside the route entrance.

## Layout requirements
Place one free-text SpawnZone beside the safe landing area and climbing entrance. Place no lethal fall zone, terminal winner trigger, or checkpoint; the survivable rooftop jump physically returns players to the beginning of the loop.

## Layout components
The build contains one small office block, one exterior climbing route, one accessible rooftop, one clear roof-edge jump point, one broad safe landing area, and one adjacent route entrance. Keep the vertical route continuous, align the landing area beneath the jump, and compose the full building height and return loop clearly in frame.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: physics-valid climb spacing and jump-to-landing alignment must be authoritative before the modern office isometric dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium because the whole office block must fit while individual exterior holds, the rooftop edge, and the landing area remain legible. The theme is a small modern city office building. `P6` is required for valid-by-construction climbing geometry, and `P2` is required because the stacked route and rooftop overhang lower playable areas.
