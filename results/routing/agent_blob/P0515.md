# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map centered on a small modern city office block with a clear exterior climbing route leading from the ground to the rooftop. At the base, place a broad safe landing area directly below or beside the rooftop jump-off point, with open space around the building for repeated jumps.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Obby & Platformer. Players complete an exterior climbing route, then repeatedly jump from the rooftop to a survivable landing area.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-tower`: the route stacks upward along the outside of one compact office block, with climbing surfaces above lower portions of the course. The preset is Tower Obby, retaining the tower, sequential ascent, climbing obstacles, and rooftop endpoint while omitting timed hazards that were not requested.

## Config requirements
Use `path-track` for one clear ordered exterior route from street level to the roof. Use `obstacle-climb` for ladders, trusses, handholds, and narrow facade ledges, plus `obstacle-jump` for short physics-legal transfers between them. Use the visible half of `winner-zone` as the open rooftop reached after the climb. Draw a broad, visibly soft safe landing area directly below or beside the chosen rooftop jump edge, with an unobstructed fall corridor between them.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `winner-zone` across the rooftop beyond the final climbing element. Put one initial spawn at the ground-level route entrance. The safe landing requires no death or respawn trigger: it must be ordinary survivable geometry, kept clear of props and aligned with the rooftop jump-off edge so players can land and immediately return to the climb.

## Layout components
Build one small modern office block as the central zone, with a compact street-level apron around it. Run a continuous exterior climbing route up one or two connected facades using ledges, ladders, wall grips, and short platform jumps. Make the rooftop broad, flat, and uncluttered, with one visually obvious jump edge. Place one large padded plaza, awning-like landing deck, or other safe impact surface at the base, directly within the fall line. Keep neighboring city structures low and distant so repeated jumps have open space. Use glass, concrete, steel trim, and minimal street props. Frame the roof-to-landing relationship clearly.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish a reachable ordered climb and aligned jump-to-landing corridor first, then dress the office block and city setting.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by one compact office building. The theme is clean modern urban architecture with a playful safe-jump facility. Use `P6` for physics-valid climbing structure and `P2` because exterior route surfaces stack and overhang lower sections.
