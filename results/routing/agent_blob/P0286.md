# Scene prompt
Create a 3D open-world trampoline map set in a dreamy pastel skyscape, with soft clouds, a calm airy atmosphere, and floating-looking platforms. Spread large trampolines across the ground-level play space and rising aerial routes, leading upward through multiple elevation bands toward a few-thousand-stud-high upper sky zone. Place collectible stars and appearance-changing power-ups in the air above trampoline paths, with the best power-ups concentrated near the highest elevations; leave ground areas beneath the aerial course for resets and landing space.

# Agent decision

## Genre
Obby & Platformer is the dominant genre because player-controlled trampoline jumps and chained aerial landings are the movement challenge. The open-world wording describes route freedom, not a separate Adventure loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `volume-open-air`, outside the genre’s usual shape list, because playable space extends through open air among discrete landing surfaces rather than along one ground course or tower stack. Preset is none: Precision Platformer is the nearest family, but replacing its terraced shape and dropping its moving-obstacle and shortcut bundle makes it unrecognisable.

## Config requirements
Use `obstacle-jump` for the large trampolines and rising boost routes, with landing surfaces arranged for successively higher launches. Floating-looking platforms and soft cloud masses provide visual elevation landmarks. Do not add lethal surfaces, a shop hub, or conventional checkpoints: touching the ground intentionally resets the combo rather than constituting course failure.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` as stars along risky airborne lines above the trampoline network, becoming denser or more valuable at upper elevation bands. Place appearance-changing power-ups as uncatalogued collectible geometry along those same aerial routes, reserving the best rewards for the highest reachable zone. Their exact count is unspecified. The ground itself is the broad reset and recovery area, so no respawn checkpoint placement is required.

## Layout components
A spacious pastel ground field forms the lowest band, with large trampolines distributed across it and ample clear landing areas between them. Multiple branching aerial routes rise through floating platforms and soft cloud layers to a focused upper-sky reward zone a few thousand studs high. Each route needs readable trampoline-to-platform relationships, enough lateral alternatives for chaining landings, and increasingly demanding vertical transitions. Stars and power-ups hang visibly above intended launch arcs. The boundary is open skyscape around a finite playable height. Compose the frame vertically so the ground reset field, intermediate bounce network, and distant reward tier are simultaneously legible.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, because the trampoline landing network must remain physically connected and playable before the extreme vertical presentation is dressed.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge because the map rises a few thousand studs. The theme is dreamy pastel cloudscape with calm, airy forms. Use `P6` because jump and landing relationships must be valid by construction, plus `CHECK` because the layered open-air play volume can self-occlude.
