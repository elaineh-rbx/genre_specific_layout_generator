# Scene prompt
Create a detailed 3D open-world city map with a realistic, lived-in street grid. Arrange claimable pre-built detailed houses in residential neighborhoods, with stores and other city buildings along commercial streets. Include broad drivable roads for cars throughout the city and a dedicated racing-street circuit with a visible start/finish line and lap route. Compose dense urban foreground streets leading through housing and shop districts toward a larger city backdrop.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, with Racing secondary because the dedicated timed lap circuit is a real activity rather than merely a fast road.

## Shape and preset
Use `settlement-claimable` for a realistic open city whose pre-built houses players claim. The preset is Life, retaining its claimable homes, city roads, zoned districts, and enterable venues; racing options are added without taking a second shape.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for a broad interconnected city grid with two-way roads at least 30 studs wide. Use `district-zoned` for residential neighborhoods and denser commercial streets. Use `building-interior` for claimable homes and stores players enter. Use `settlement-density` for dense foreground blocks and a larger urban backdrop. For the racing streets, draw `startpoint-line`, `lane-corridor`, `trigger-finish`, `spawn-grid`, and `barrier-guardrail`, creating a visibly continuous lap circuit integrated into but clearly separated from ordinary traffic roads.

## Layout requirements
Place the `startpoint-line` and `trigger-finish` together at the visible start/finish stripe. Place one `spawn-grid` spanning the racing road immediately behind it, with evenly spaced car slots. Place `trigger-lap` detection at the start/finish and key circuit intervals, and `checkpoint-respawn` recovery points after major turns, oriented along race flow. Put one scene-specific claim marker at each selectable house entrance.

## Layout components
Build dense foreground streets leading through commercial blocks and claimable residential neighborhoods toward a larger city skyline. Connect every district with broad drivable roads, while reserving one guarded loop as the racing circuit. Give the circuit a clear start/finish straight, legible turns, lane paint, barriers, and no ambiguous crossings. Line commercial streets with detailed stores and urban props; give homes distinct facades, yards, driveways, and interiors. Use realistic asphalt, sidewalks, signals, lamps, parked cars, signs, utility details, and street trees. The city edge continues visually into the backdrop, while the race loop remains fully visible and connected in plan.

## Render order
Render in `authored_plan` order: the repository-generated circuit blueprint is authoritative so the lap closes and remains unambiguous, then the city top-down and isometric views dress that route.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is huge, driven by multiple city districts and a full racing loop. The theme is highly detailed contemporary realism. Use `P6` for the construction-valid circuit and `P3` for transitions into homes and stores.
