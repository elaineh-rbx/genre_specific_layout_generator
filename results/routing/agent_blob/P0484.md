# Scene prompt
Create a 3D murder mystery map set inside a dim, gothic mansion with interconnected interior rooms and corridors.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Survival is the sole genre. This is an asymmetric killer round in which the murderer hunts the other players until caught or until everyone else is eliminated.

## Shape and preset
Use `warren-looping`, an interconnected mansion plan built on loops with zero dead ends so fleeing players are not unfairly trapped by the architecture. The closest preset is 1 vs All. Keep its hiding cover and separated role starts, but drop `gate-escape` because the stated round endings do not involve escaping through a door.

## Config requirements
Use `cover-los` for gothic wardrobes, curtained recesses, alcoves, and low wall cutouts that let players briefly break sightlines. Keep all play inside one dim mansion, with shadowed rooms, heavy interior walls, and corridors arranged into readable loops. No exterior grounds or outside-to-inside transition are required.

## Layout requirements
Use `spawn-protected` to place one murderer start apart from the survivor starts, with intervening rooms and no immediate direct sightline between roles. Put the survivor starts together or in a balanced nearby cluster rather than scattering them into isolated dead ends. The murderer, evidence, accusation, and elimination rules are gameplay systems and need no additional geometry.

## Layout components
Build one bounded gothic mansion interior organized around a central circulation room, with multiple surrounding rooms and corridors reconnecting on at least two sides. Every side passage must return to another corridor or room; no route may terminate in a trapping dead end. Distribute wardrobes, curtains, columns, and recessed alcoves where they interrupt long sightlines without blocking movement. Enclose the full play space with mansion walls and keep the composition compact enough that players repeatedly cross paths, while giving each loop more than one retreat direction.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, then dress the isometric image. The looped circulation must be valid for fair chase play, and an appearance-first view cannot guarantee zero dead ends.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale, balancing repeated encounters against enough rooms for pursuit and concealment. The theme is dim gothic mansion horror. Route with `P6` because the looping, dead-end-free structure must be valid by construction.
