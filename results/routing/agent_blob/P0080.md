# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map with a bright, colorful park as the chase ground, with simple paths, trees, and open areas for ducks and geese to run around. Include a separate lobby area with a visible obby course.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Party & Casual for the timed duck-and-goose chase rounds, with Obby & Platformer secondary because the lobby contains a playable obstacle course.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-staged` from the Minigame preset. The bright park match area is separate from the lobby so waiting players cannot interfere, while the lobby contains its own visible obby.

## Config requirements
Apply `social-hub` as the colorful gathering lobby surrounding the obby entrance. Apply `path-track` as a short sequential platform route through that lobby and `obstacle-jump` as simple, physics-legal jumps suited to waiting players. Apply `teleporter-link` in both streams as visible lobby-to-park transfer pads. Draw the chase park with simple paths, trees, and broad open running areas.

## Layout requirements
Place `teleporter-link` markers between lobby and chase ground and a return marker for round end. Place `spawn-protected` as separated round starts for ducks and the initial goose, avoiding immediate tags. Place `trigger-scoring` across the active park for tag conversion and end-of-timer round resolution. Place `winner-zone` in both streams at the end of the lobby obby as a visible payoff pad and functional completion region.

## Layout components
Build two separate zones: a bright park chase ground and a colorful lobby. The park has open grass, simple looping paths, sparse tree clusters, and a clear outer boundary. The lobby has a gathering floor, transfer pads, and a short visible platform course terminating at a reward pad. Keep the obby route distinct from lobby circulation and preserve legal jump spacing. Compose each zone as a self-contained map, with the park optimized for pursuit and the lobby obby readable at a glance.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative because the obby’s ordered jump chain must be valid and the lobby-to-stage functions must remain separated; this is not a maze or circuit.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by one chase park plus one lobby and short obby. The theme is bright, playful, colorful park fantasy. Use `P4` for the separate lobby and match maps and `P6` because the obby route and jumps must be valid by construction.
