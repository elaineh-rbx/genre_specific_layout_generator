# Scene prompt

Create a 3D fighting simulator map made of six compact, connected ground zones that players walk through in sequence, each with an NPC combat area, a training space, and a clear stat-gated entrance to the next zone. Begin at a safe lobby and Beginner Arena with weak training dummies, then lead through City Streets with street fighters, a Dojo with ninja opponents, a Lava Arena with lava monsters, a Space Zone with space warriors, and an endgame God Arena containing the final boss. Keep separate PvP arenas apart from the main progression route. Arrange the zones as distinct arena-sized environments connected by visible paths or gateways, with the later zones progressively more imposing in the background.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Simulation, with Action secondary. Stats, training, unlock barriers, upgrades, and rebirth drive progression; fighting supplies the activity within that simulator loop.

## Shape and preset

Use `tier-ladder` from the Stat Grinder preset. It is a six-tier connected ground sequence whose arena-sized zones become stronger and more imposing, with a stat wall between each consecutive pair.

## Config requirements

Use `social-hub` as the safe starter lobby with upgrade storefronts. Draw `station-training` clusters in each of the six zones, themed to that tier. Use `gate-progression` for five visible stat-locked gateways between the six sequential zones. Use `path-circulation` as one continuous walkable route through the full ladder. Separate PvP arenas branch from the lobby and remain outside NPC progression spaces.

## Layout requirements

For `station-training`, place one dense training cluster in each of the six zones. Place six `spawner-npc` origins: weak dummies in Beginner Arena, street fighters in City Streets, ninjas in the Dojo, lava monsters in Lava Arena, space warriors in Space Zone, and the final boss in God Arena. Place one `trigger-rebirth` pad beyond the final God Arena encounter. PvP arenas receive opposed player starts, but their count is unspecified and must not be invented.

## Layout components

Build exactly six compact arena-sized environments in order: Beginner Arena with a safe lobby, City Streets, Dojo, Lava Arena, Space Zone, and God Arena. Connect each neighboring pair by a visible ground path and one gateway, totaling five progression gateways. Give every zone separate NPC-combat and training subareas. Place the PvP branch beside the lobby so it never interrupts the main route. Keep all ground contiguous and non-overlapping, with later arenas visible as increasingly imposing background landmarks. Bound the chain with themed walls and inaccessible scenery.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, then dress the isometric view; the six-zone order, five gates, side PvP branch, and continuous route must remain playable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is large, driven by six compact arenas plus separate PvP space. The theme escalates from beginner grounds through urban, martial-arts, lava, cosmic, and divine settings. Use `P0`: all zones share one connected ground surface with no overhang or interior transition required.
