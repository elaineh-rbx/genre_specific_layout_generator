# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] scale — Q: How big is gigantic - a few square kilometres of forest, or something a player could cross in a couple of minutes? A: Quero um mapa grande, mas que o jogador consiga atravessar em alguns minutos, com várias áreas de floresta, vegetação e rios para manter o jogo leve e jogável.
- [author] goal — Q: What do the animals do - survive, hunt each other, roleplay a pack? A: O foco é sobrevivência na natureza, com os animais podendo explorar o mapa e atacar outros animais quando necessário.

## Enriched image prompt
Create an ultra-realistic 3D wilderness survival map for controllable animals. Build one large but lightweight continuous natural landscape that can be crossed in a few minutes, divided into several forest and vegetation areas and threaded by several rivers. Keep the forests varied through density and plant cover while leaving readable clearings and riverbanks for animal movement and encounters. Show several realistic 3D animal species distributed across the habitats, and compose the whole traversable map clearly in one isometric frame with lifelike terrain, vegetation, water, and textures.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Survival.

## Shape and preset
Use `wilderness-open`, an Open Natural World with no settlement, organized as a continuous animal habitat of forests, clearings, and rivers. This shape comes from outside Survival's usual set because it matches a natural animal world better than the threat-scaled or arena shapes. The preset is none: Resource Survival would add harvesting and base-building that the author did not request.

## Config requirements
Use the universal `water-body` option for several real rivers crossing the habitat. Add a free-text image requirement for several realistic 3D controllable animal species in the forest zones because no canonical option represents player-controlled animal avatars; do not reinterpret them as ambient NPCs.

## Layout requirements
Nothing invisible must be placed after segmentation. Animal controls, attacks, running, jumping, and joystick buttons are mechanics rather than map placements.

## Layout components
The build is one broad continuous wilderness containing several forest zones, lower-density vegetation areas, open clearings, riverbanks, and several rivers. Natural passages connect every habitat so animals can traverse the full map within a few minutes. The boundary is a soft natural edge rather than a constructed wall. Forest masses and waterways should form distinct, readable bands in the full-map composition while representative animals remain visible in open habitat pockets.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, because the realistic habitat composition and visual relationships lead while no generated topology is required.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use the large scale band, following the author's clarified request for a broad map with several areas that remains crossable within minutes. The theme is ultra-realistic forest wilderness with lifelike vegetation, animals, rivers, terrain, and textures; “ultra-realistic” is a fidelity constraint rather than the place name. Use `CHECK` for the river play volume; otherwise the continuous ground is baseline `P0`.
