# Scene prompt
Create a 3D animal-survival map with a large but traversable wilderness that can be crossed in a few minutes. Divide the landscape into several forested areas with dense natural vegetation, connected by multiple rivers running across the map; leave varied open ground between forest zones for exploration and animal encounters. Populate the environment with controllable 3D animals and realistic natural textures.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Survival: the animals explore a natural wilderness and attack one another when needed while surviving in the wild.

## Shape and preset
Use the single `world-open` shape from outside Survival’s usual set, because the brief asks for one contiguous wilderness crossed in minutes rather than separate danger-scaled biome maps. Preset is none: Resource Survival would add gathering and base-building geometry that the author did not request.

## Config requirements
Apply `water-body` to draw multiple natural rivers crossing the wilderness. Draw several distinct forest zones with dense realistic vegetation and broad open ground between them. Controllable animal models belong to the avatar system rather than ambient `npc-population`, so no unrequested NPC herd is added to the map image.

## Layout requirements
Place multiple player-animal spawn markers on navigable open ground across the map, including starts near different forest areas, without putting opposing animals directly on top of one another. Spawn sites must have clear routes to forest cover, riverbanks, and central encounter ground. No scripted hostile spawners, resource collectibles, bases, escape gates, or winner zones are needed.

## Layout components
Build one large continuous wilderness that remains crossable in a few minutes. Arrange several irregular forest zones around a broad network of clearings and natural animal trails. Run multiple rivers across the map, with shallow fordable sections or open-bank crossings so they create landmarks without fragmenting traversal. Use dense trees, shrubs, ground plants, rocks, mud, grass, and realistic soil textures, while retaining enough open terrain for running and encounters. Enclose the map with denser woodland and natural terrain at the outer boundary. Compose broad forest masses around open central ground, with river curves readable from above.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down image, because natural realism and vegetation massing lead while the continuous map has no hard topology puzzle.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large, constrained to a wilderness traversable within several minutes. The theme is ultra-realistic temperate wildlife forest. Use `CHECK` for the multiple `water-body` rivers; keep their playable water volumes shallow and non-layered. No other modifier is forced.
