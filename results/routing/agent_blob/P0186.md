## Enriched image prompt
Create one continuous whimsical fantasy adventure world divided into forest, mountain, and water-based regions around a gated central castle. Connect all three regions and the castle with clear trails. Place three simple puzzles in each biome, nine total: include block-shape puzzles with nearby visual clues, short sequence obstacle puzzles, and small lock-in monster arenas. Make one key puzzle in each biome visually important because the three together open the castle; use the other six for token, item, or heart rewards. Place one small shop in each biome. Add friendly-looking goblin-like and fantasy-creature patrol areas bounded away from major paths. Distribute swords, axes, bows, arrows, tokens, and healing items on reachable ground and in hidden spots, with some rewards inside breakable bushes, pots, barrels, or similar clusters. Shape the mountain with strong relief, give the water biome a real water body, and make the central castle an enterable boss destination.

## Genre
Adventure is dominant, with Puzzle secondary.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, Open World, for the single continuous landmass containing all biomes and the central castle. Use the Exploration preset, omitting unrequested secret alcoves and adding the explicit puzzle network.

## Config requirements
Use `path-trail` for continuous routes linking every biome to the castle and `landmark-focal` for the central fortress. Use `facade-clue`, `trigger-solve`, and externally sourced `path-track` for the clue walls, block-input puzzles, and short ordered obstacle puzzles. Use `arena-lockin` for small enclosed monster challenges and `gate-progression` for the castle gate. Use `building-interior` for the castle and three shops, `terrain-relief` for the mountain, `water-body` for the water biome, and `destructible-cluster` for breakable reward containers.

## Layout requirements
Use `spawner-npc` for bounded monster patrol origins and the castle boss area. Use `collectible-nodes` for three biome keys plus token, healing, and heart rewards from the other six puzzles and world props. Use `collectible-loot` for swords, axes, bows, and arrows on paths or in hidden reachable locations. Place exactly one key reward per biome and bind all three to the central castle gate.

## Layout components
Forest, Mountain, and Water are three zones of one map, each with three puzzles and one shop. The mountain uses tiered ground; the water zone contains traversable shoreline and water; the forest uses wooded land. Trails connect all zones radially to the Castle at center. The castle is an enterable fourth zone with the boss encounter beyond its gate. Hostile patrol areas stay within marked land pockets. Breakable bushes, pots, and barrels furnish accessible reward sites. The outer world edge is the boundary.

## Render order
