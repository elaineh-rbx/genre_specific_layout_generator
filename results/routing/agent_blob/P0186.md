# Scene prompt

Create a 3D third-person fantasy adventure map inspired by classic storybook action-adventure worlds, built as one continuous walkable landmass with a central castle. Arrange a forest biome, a mountain biome, and a water-based biome around the castle, each containing three simple puzzle locations; one prominent key-puzzle site in each biome opens the castle gate, while the other puzzle sites reward tokens, equipment, or extra-heart pickups. Place gentle goblin-like and other non-scary fantasy creature patrol areas along routes, with visible boundary terrain. Spread small shops evenly across the world, and scatter swords, axes, bows, arrows, tokens, healing items, breakable bushes, pots, and barrels along paths and in hidden alcoves. The central castle is the visual destination, with a gated entrance, interior puzzle barriers, and a final boss chamber.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Adventure, with Puzzle secondary because exploration contains nine defined puzzle sites and three of them gate the destination.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open` from the Exploration preset: one continuous landmass freely explored around a central castle. Keep the preset’s landmark, trails, and hidden alcoves, then add the stated puzzle gating.

## Config requirements

Use `landmark-focal` for the central castle, visible as the destination from the surrounding regions. Thread `path-trail` through all three biomes and add `alcove-secret` for hidden equipment and item caches. Build three prominent `gate-solve` key-puzzle sites and six smaller puzzle sites, three puzzle locations per biome and nine total. Use `gate-progression` for the castle entrance and interior barriers. Add `building-interior` for the entered castle, `water-body` for the water region, and `terrain-relief` for the mountain region. Draw evenly distributed small shops, bounded creature patrol clearings, breakable bushes, pots, and barrels.

## Layout requirements

Place three `collectible-nodes` key objectives, one at each biome’s prominent puzzle, and six reward sets at the remaining sites. Distribute swords, axes, bows, arrows, tokens, healing items, and extra-heart pickups along trails and in alcoves. Place bounded hostile patrol volumes beside routes, plus one final boss encounter marker in the castle chamber.

## Layout components

Arrange forest, mountain, and water-based regions as three connected sectors around the central castle. Give each sector three distinct puzzle clearings linked by trails. Use ridges and slopes to define the mountain, water channels and walkable shores for the water region, and dense trees with hidden cutouts for the forest. Ring the castle with converging approaches, place its gate toward the outer regions, and sequence interior puzzle barriers before the boss chamber. Keep the outer boundary natural and frame the castle as the central visual anchor.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: establish connected biome routes, all nine puzzle sites, and converging castle access first, then dress the authoritative plan isometrically.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is large, driven by three biomes and nine puzzle sites. The theme is friendly storybook fantasy. Use `P3` for outside-to-castle traversal, `tiered` for mountain relief, and `CHECK` for the traversed water region.
