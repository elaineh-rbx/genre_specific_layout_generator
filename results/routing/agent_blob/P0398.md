# Scene prompt

Create a large 3D map shaped as a complete, realistic computer keyboard scaled up so players can walk along its full length. Arrange oversized keys in their standard keyboard layout as the main traversable surface, with a clear route progressing from the first keys toward the final key at the far end. Place physical obstacles across and between sections of keys along this route.

# Agent decision

This comes from outside Simulation’s usual shapes because the scene explicitly describes a finite obstacle route. Use the Stat Grinder preset with `course-flat` substituted for `tier-ladder`; retain its stat-training concept and omit unrequested rebirth, bazaar, and progression-wall geometry.

The complete standard keyboard layout itself is additional image geometry, brightly stylized while preserving recognizable rows and key proportions.

## Layout requirements

Place the functional triggers for `station-training` on the designated pressable keys along the route. Place `checkpoint-respawn` at safe transitions between the keyboard’s major row or key-group sections; no exact count is stated. Place one `winner-zone` on the final key at the far end.

## Layout components

The single main zone is one huge complete keyboard, with oversized alphanumeric, modifier, navigation, arrow, and numeric-keypad groups arranged in their standard relative positions. The traversable route starts at one end, crosses key tops and short gaps through successive groups, and ends at the farthest final key. Physical obstacles interrupt the route without obscuring the recognizable keyboard plan. A low keyboard casing encloses the footprint; the surrounding area remains visually open. Frame the whole keyboard diagonally so its complete silhouette and end-to-end progression are legible.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: the authoritative plan comes first so the standard key arrangement and continuous obstacle route remain valid, followed by the dressed isometric view.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
