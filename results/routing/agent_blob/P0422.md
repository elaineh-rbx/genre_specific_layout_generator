# Scene prompt
Create a small 3D collectible exploration world in a bright, stylized look, with gems placed around the traversable environment.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure because the player roams a three-dimensional world to find collectibles. No secondary genre is needed; mobile and desktop controls do not change the spatial genre.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, one small contiguous environment that can be explored freely and searched in any order. The matched preset is Scavenger Hunt, retaining its open-world shape and collectible objective placement while omitting secret alcoves and a physical tracker because the prompt does not request either.

## Config requirements
No named image-config option is required. Draw a bright, stylized, uncluttered traversable world with clear open ground and a few visually distinct areas so scattered gems can be found from movement and observation. Do not add shops, buildings, water, relief, NPCs, or other plausible extras that the brief never names.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` as gems around the traversable environment, distributing them across the distinct visible areas and along reachable movement space rather than clustering them at the start. The prompt gives no gem count, so preserve an unspecified collection rather than inventing a number. No checkpoint, tracker, spawn family, or completion volume is implied by the provided space.

## Layout components
Build one compact walkable zone with a simple continuous ground plane, a readable central area, and several shallow peripheral pockets that invite short exploration without becoming hidden maze branches. Gems are the only named props and should be spread across these reachable areas. Use a clear outer boundary appropriate to a small game world. Compose the view so the central circulation and peripheral collectible spaces are all legible.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down view; the bright stylized presentation leads and the simple open topology does not require a plan-first pass.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, as stated by the brief. The theme is bright, stylized collectible exploration. Use `P0`; one continuous single-level walkable space forces no extra capture route.
