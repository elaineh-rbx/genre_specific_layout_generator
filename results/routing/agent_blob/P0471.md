# Scene prompt
Create a 3D soccer game layout with a separate match lobby and a real-looking stadium map. The lobby contains distinct mode-selection boxes for 1v1, 2v2, 3v3, 4v4, and 5v5, arranged clearly for players to enter before teleporting to matches. The match takes place on a smaller arcade-sized soccer pitch inside a realistic stadium, with normal goals at opposite ends, marked field lines, and surrounding spectator stands; the field supports Spain-versus-France team matches from 1v1 through 5v5.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The genre is Sports. The game is a soccer match suite with a separate mode-selection lobby and team field play.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-staged`, taken from outside Sports’ usual shapes, because the waiting lobby and stadium match are genuinely separate maps connected by teleportation. The preset is Full Stadium with its shape replaced to honor that separation; its stadium dressing remains intact.

## Config requirements
Draw `spectator-bleachers` around the arcade-sized pitch, `barrier-perimeter` as the realistic stadium shell, and `spectator-zone` along the team sidelines. Add `trigger-bounds` as marked touchlines, `startpoint-play` as the center spot and mirrored team positions, and `trigger-scoring` as two normal goal mouths. In the separate lobby, draw exactly five clearly labeled mode-selection boxes for 1v1, 2v2, 3v3, 4v4, and 5v5. Use Spain and France color accents without altering field symmetry.

## Layout requirements
Place five mode-entry trigger volumes, one per selection box, each linked to its match destination. Place one `trigger-bounds` perimeter on the pitch. Place ten maximum-capacity `startpoint-play` positions, five mirrored per team, plus one ball reset at center; lower-player modes use subsets. Place two `trigger-scoring` planes inside opposing goals.

## Layout components
Map one is an uncluttered lobby with five evenly spaced selection boxes and clear circulation around them. Map two is a realistic stadium with a small arcade soccer pitch, two goals, marked lines, team-side areas, tiered stands, and enclosed outer structure. Keep every mode playable on the same fast, compact field and compose both maps as distinct authoritative spaces.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render each map’s appearance first, then derive its simple plan; neither space requires generated topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large overall, combining a modest lobby and stadium map. The theme is realistic Spain-versus-France soccer. Use `P4` for separate maps and `tiered` for spectator seating.
