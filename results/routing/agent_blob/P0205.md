# Scene prompt
Create a bright, stylized 3D jungle map with an immersive, layered presentation. During the peaceful exploration area, winding jungle paths pass through dense trees, foliage, and animal gathering areas, with simple feeding spots distributed around the map. The jungle has a clear escape route leading toward its outer edge; at night, the same paths and vegetation become a tense, darkened route to escape, while remaining non-graphic and suitable for Roblox.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Survival, with Adventure secondary: the round culminates in escaping hostile animals, while its opening phase is free jungle exploration.

## Shape and preset
Use one `warren-looping` shape: interconnected jungle paths with no dead ends until the deliberate outer-edge escape. The closest preset is Escape, but omit its unrequested shelters and built hiding cutouts while retaining the chase-readable looping route and exit.

## Config requirements
Draw `gate-escape` as a clear structural opening at the jungle boundary. Draw `path-trail` as winding, repeatedly reconnecting jungle tracks. Use `npc-population` for the peaceful animal gathering grounds, and include simple visible feeding stations as uncatalogued scene geometry distributed beside those grounds.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` origins and patrol ground for the animals at their gathering areas, allowing those same inhabitants to become the nighttime threat. Place the functional half of `gate-escape` as one win trigger at the outer-edge opening. Place `npc-population` anchors at each animal gathering area during the peaceful phase. Add interaction markers to every visible feeding station so each can accept food.

## Layout components
Build one bounded jungle with an entry/exploration area, several animal clearings, and a single exit at the outer edge. A braided network of winding paths should connect the clearings and feeding spots through dense stylized trees and foliage, with loops that provide alternate nighttime escape routes and no accidental dead ends. The vegetation forms the boundary. Compose the map so the bright daytime gathering areas remain legible while the same route can read as tense under dark night lighting.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, then dress the isometric view from it, because a broken loop or unreachable exit would make the chase unfair and the escape impossible.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale for a two-minute peaceful exploration. The theme is bright stylized jungle shifting to tense, non-graphic Roblox night horror. Route through `P6` because the looping warren and reachable escape must be valid by construction.
