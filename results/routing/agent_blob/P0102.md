# Scene prompt
Create a 3D obby map as a plain, neutral classic colorful Roblox-style course: a finite 20-stage platform route that progresses from easier sections to noticeably more difficult sections, ending at a finish area. Keep the overall course compact and plainly playable, sized for roughly 10–15 minutes to complete.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Obby & Platformer, with no secondary genre: player-paced platform traversal and rising jump difficulty define the game.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-flat`, a compact ground-spread route of 20 sequential stages, from the Classic Obby preset. Keep that preset’s track, checkpoints, jump obstacles, and moving obstacles, and add the explicitly requested finish area.

## Config requirements
The image config needs `path-track` as one continuous ordered chain of physics-legal platforms, `checkpoint-respawn` as clearly visible safe pads separating the stages, `obstacle-jump` for progressively longer or more exacting jumps, `obstacle-moving` for later rotating, sliding, and conveyor challenges, and `winner-zone` as a colorful payoff platform at the end. Do not add contact-lethal surfaces merely from the word “hazards”; ordinary falling and moving obstacles satisfy the brief.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `checkpoint-respawn` at 20 stage starts, including the initial start pad, so each completed stage becomes the next recovery point. Place one `winner-zone` trigger on the terminal finish platform after stage 20. The critical route must preserve increasing but legal jump spacing throughout.

## Layout components
Build a compact start pad followed by 20 clearly divided platform stages in a readable horizontal sequence. Early stages use broad blocks and short gaps; middle stages introduce narrow paths, wraps, conveyors, and rotating bars; late stages combine smaller landings, longer legal gaps, and tighter moving-obstacle timing. Connect every stage directly to the next with no branches. Use simple brightly colored Roblox materials over a neutral void or failure floor, with low visual clutter and a clear enclosing course footprint. Frame the full progression diagonally so start, escalating middle, and finish remain legible.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the playable 20-stage topology and legal critical path must be established first, then dressed into the authoritative isometric view.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by the finite 10–15 minute course despite its exact 20-stage count. The theme is plain, neutral, and classic colorful Roblox. Route through `P6` because every platform connection and gap must be valid by construction.
