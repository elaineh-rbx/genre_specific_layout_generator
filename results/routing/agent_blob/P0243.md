# Scene prompt

Create a bright, colorful 3D game map for young kids centered on a small lake. A single fixed giant slingshot stands at the lake edge, aimed across the water, while a friendly cartoon crocodile runs around the shoreline. Keep the lake close enough that a normally aimed launched piggy crosses it in about one second, with an open, unobstructed shooting area between the slingshot and the crocodile’s path. Use rounded shapes, cheerful colors, and big smiling character designs.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Shooter, specifically a fixed-position, kid-friendly target gallery rather than combat.

## Shape and preset

Use `range-directed`: one fixed firing position faces an open target area with no opposing team and no player route through it. The preset is Aim Trainer.

## Config requirements

Use `station-loadout` as the single giant slingshot fixed at the near lake edge, with the next pink piggy visibly supplied at the firing point. Use `target-practice` as one large, smiling cartoon crocodile presented clearly along its shoreline circuit. Draw `water-body` as one small lake, sized so an ordinary shot crosses it in about one second. Preserve a completely unobstructed firing corridor between slingshot and crocodile path.

## Layout requirements

Place exactly one functional `station-loadout` interaction and player position at the slingshot. Place one projectile supply marker on the sling cradle. Bind the functional `target-practice` hit target to the crocodile and place one continuous actor route around the shoreline, without hidden or unreachable sections. The hit target itself registers scoring; no additional score volume or player traversal markers are needed.

## Layout components

Build one compact, rounded lake with a continuous flat shoreline loop. Put the single giant slingshot on a small near-bank pad facing across the water. Keep the lake center and sightline empty, while the one crocodile follows the perimeter at readable range. Use low rounded grass banks and sparse soft-edged decorations outside the firing corridor. The outer ground edge encloses the set, and the composition looks from behind the slingshot across the lake so the piggy trajectory and crocodile are immediately legible.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, then dress the isometric view; the crocodile’s shoreline circuit must be continuous and remain exposed to the fixed firing position.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a small scale, driven by the one-second shot distance. The theme is bright, rounded, smiling preschool cartoon. Apply `CHECK` for the water surface and append `SET` because the player remains fixed and does not traverse the built space.
