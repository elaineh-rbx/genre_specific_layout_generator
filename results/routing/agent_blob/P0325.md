# Scene prompt
Create a bright, cartoon-cute 3D pet-game town with a large central town area and pre-built houses distributed around it for players to claim.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim: players adopt and care for pets in an open-ended town setting.

## Shape and preset
Use `settlement-claimable`, with a large communal center and finished homes around it that players claim rather than construct. The matching preset is Pet Care.

## Config requirements
Draw `social-hub` as an oversized central town square suitable for pet-and-owner gatherings. Use `path-street` for clear pedestrian streets and walks from the square to every home. Include `building-interior` because claimed homes are usable pet-care spaces rather than facades. Use `settlement-density` to make the requested town read as one substantial built-up place, not scattered houses. Show `npc-population` as friendly adoptable pets occupying the central adoption area and nearby public ground.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `npc-population` at a clearly legible adoption area adjoining the central square, with additional pets distributed only on safe public ground. Place one claim interaction marker at the entrance of every pre-built house; the house count is not stated, so it should follow the rendered residential capacity rather than inventing a number.

## Layout components
The central zone is a broad town square and pet-adoption gathering area, surrounded by a residential ring of bright pre-built houses. Pedestrian streets radiate from the square and form a simple connected loop past all homes. Ground should be mostly flat, with colorful paving, lawns, small trees, and cartoon-cute pet-scale details. The boundary is a soft town edge of landscaping and distant decorative buildings. Frame the square as the visual focus while keeping each house entrance visible and reachable.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the cheerful town look first, then derive the top-down plan because the simple connected street pattern does not require computed topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a large scale band, driven by the explicitly large town and distributed housing. The theme is bright, cartoon-cute suburban pet care. Apply `P3` because play crosses between the town exterior and enterable claimed homes.
