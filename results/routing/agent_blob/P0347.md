# Scene prompt
Crie um mapa de jogo de tiro 3D em uma arena aberta, pequena e compacta, com chão branco plano e uma mureta azul extremamente alta fechando todo o perímetro. O avatar do jogador ocupa a área central: um personagem careca, alto e largo, estilo strongman, com cerca de 2,20 metros de altura, usando uniforme bege do exército com “Forças Armadas” escrito nas costas e portando uma pistola. Soldados inimigos de altura equilibrada surgem em hordas ao redor da arena e avançam pelo espaço aberto; alguns carregam pistolas, metralhadoras, shotguns ou granadas. Mantenha todos os inimigos dentro do alcance de pistola, sem grandes áreas para recuo ou estruturas adicionais.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Shooter is dominant, with Survival secondary because the activity is an endless horde holdout.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded` from outside Shooter’s usual shape list: one compact, enclosed ground-level arena keeps every fight within pistol range. The preset is Horde Defense, with its bounded shape and hostile origins retained; destructible structures and hiding cover are dropped because the brief explicitly requires an open floor with no additional structures.

## Config requirements
The visible config includes a flat white arena floor, an extremely high blue perimeter wall, one bald strongman player avatar approximately 2.20 metres tall in a beige army uniform marked “Forças Armadas,” and varied enemy soldiers visibly carrying pistols, machine guns, shotguns, or grenades. No tactical cover, interior, or retreat zone is added.

## Layout requirements
Use `spawner-npc` for hostile wave origins distributed around the perimeter, outside immediate overlap with the central player position but close enough to keep enemies in pistol range. Place one unkeyed player spawn at arena center, oriented into the open floor.

## Layout components
The map is a single small rectangular or circular combat zone with an uninterrupted white floor. The towering blue wall fully seals its boundary. The center is reserved for the player; a ring of enemy entry points occupies the outer edge, with clear radial approaches and sightlines between every edge and the center. There are no paths beyond the open floor and no extra props or structures.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the stark color-blocked arena and character presentation lead, while the simple radial plan can be derived afterward.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small and compact. The theme is minimalist military combat with white ground, saturated blue walls, and beige uniforms. The route is `P0`; all play occurs on one visible surface without overhangs, transitions, or separate maps.
