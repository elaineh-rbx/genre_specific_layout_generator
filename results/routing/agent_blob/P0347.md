## Enriched image prompt
Create a small open 3D combat arena whose entire floor is white and whose outer edge is sealed by an extremely high blue wall. Keep the interior flat, uncluttered, and compact enough that hostile soldiers remain within pistol range from anywhere in the arena, with no large fallback distance. Reserve clear opposing ends for the player and continuous enemy hordes, while preserving open sightlines across the whole floor. Use a stark white-and-blue military training-ground aesthetic and do not add buildings, lanes, elevated positions, cover arrays, hats, UI, or weapon-system graphics.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Shooter because firearms, automatic targeting, varied hostile soldiers, and continuous enemy waves define the activity. The endless survival rule does not add a second layout genre; its spatial consequence is an enemy-wave origin.

## Shape and preset
Use the shared shape `space-bounded` from outside Shooter’s usual set: one clearly bounded, single-level arena contains the whole fight. Preset is none because the PvE Shooter preset assumes lanes, chokepoints, and an enterable structure that conflict with the requested small open floor.

## Config requirements
Use `barrier-perimeter` from the shared catalogue for the extremely high blue wall enclosing the white arena floor. No cover, building interior, elevated firing position, lane network, loot, or shrinking boundary is requested.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawner-npc` Enemy Wave Origin against the far side of the blue perimeter wall, with open navigable ground toward the player side and enough spawn clearance for machine-gun, shotgun, pistol, and grenade soldiers. Add one free-text `SpawnZone` for the player against the opposite side of the arena. Wave timing, increasing resistance, weapon recoil, knockback, avatar proportions, uniforms, sound, and auto-aim are mechanics rather than map placements.

## Layout components
Build one flat rectangular or circular white-floor combat zone bounded continuously by one extremely high blue wall. Keep the centre open and maintain a direct readable axis between the player start and hostile origin. The wall is the hard boundary and the arena contains no additional rooms or routes. Frame the entire compact floor and its enclosing wall in one view.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the stark arena appearance and full-floor sightline are authoritative, and the geometry has no topology requiring a plan-first route.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use small scale because the player and all enemies must stay within pistol and auto-aim range with little retreat space. The theme is a minimal white-and-blue military combat arena. Use `P0`: one flat bounded surface holds the entire fight without interiors, overhangs, separate maps, or procedural-validity requirements.
