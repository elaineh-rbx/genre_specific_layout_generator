# Scene prompt
Create a 3D endless free-flight map set in a small modern city. Arrange similar-looking buildings along compact streets, with checkpoint boards mounted on rooftops and building sides at varied heights for vehicles to fly through.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The genre is Simulation, specifically aircraft operation with endless free flight and repeatable score stations rather than a race or fixed route.

## Shape and preset
Use `volume-open-air` with the Aircraft Operation preset. Keep the airborne play-space and task-station concept, but drop its social upgrade hub and progression gate because the brief is an endless free-flight score loop. The city and airspace are one continuous map.

## Config requirements
Use `trigger-task` in its visible form for bold boards marked “CP,” mounted on rooftops and building sides at varied heights. Use `settlement-density` for a compact modern city of deliberately similar-looking buildings along small streets. Keep broad airborne gaps around every board so different flying vehicles can approach without clipping nearby architecture.

## Layout requirements
Use `trigger-task` in its placed form for a score-detection volume aligned exactly with each CP board opening or strike face. Distribute these checkpoint interactions across multiple rooftops and façades at varied heights, with no required order so players can revisit them endlessly from either sensible approach direction. No finish trigger, lap counter zone, or race gate is needed.

## Layout components
Build one medium city zone spanning a few compact blocks. Arrange repeated modern building masses along a simple street grid, varying their heights while preserving a coherent similar-looking concrete-and-glass style. Mount CP boards on rooftop edges, above roof surfaces, and on open-facing building sides, always leaving a wide flight corridor before and after each. Use streets and courtyards as low-altitude channels and the gaps above roofs as higher routes. Keep the outermost buildings lower or backed by a clear skyline boundary so players can read the usable airspace. Compose an aerial three-quarter view that shows several CP boards at distinct elevations without one hiding another.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the authoritative vertical arrangement and visible CP approaches first, then derive the top-down plan because varied board heights are central to the experience.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a medium scale for a small city that supports repeated free-flight loops. The theme is clean modern urban aviation with bold readable checkpoint signage. Apply `CHECK` because movement occupies a 3D air volume; ensure the checkpoint arrangement does not self-occlude.
