# Scene prompt

Create a 3D drifting map on a bare baseplate with one short, compact course laid out using simple pavement markings and barriers. The course fits comfortably on the baseplate and winds through several turns designed for drifting.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Simulation, specifically vehicle simulation rather than Racing: the player drifts freely for style score, with no laps or finish condition.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-bounded`, taken from outside Simulation’s usual shape list, because the entire activity fits inside one compact, single-level baseplate area. The preset is none. Vehicle Sim is the closest family, but its open-world shape and shared resource field do not describe this focused drift course.

## Config requirements

Use `path-road-vehicle` as one short vehicle-width paved course with several linked turns. Draw simple pavement markings that make the intended drifting line readable and low physical barriers that contain cars without decorating the otherwise bare baseplate. Do not add shops, progression gates, resources, scenery, or terrain that the brief did not request.

## Layout requirements

Place one scene-specific vehicle spawn marker at the course entrance, centered on a straight section and oriented toward the first turn. No lap, split, checkpoint, or finish triggers are needed because this is free drifting rather than a race. Style scoring is gameplay logic and does not introduce additional geometry or placement volumes into this handoff.

## Layout components

Build one compact paved drift zone comfortably inside the baseplate boundary. Route the course through several varied turns, including broad arcs and tighter direction changes, with enough straight pavement between them for setup. Mark the pavement edges and intended line clearly, place continuous simple barriers along unsafe outer edges, and leave generous bare runoff between the course and the baseplate perimeter. Keep the ground perfectly flat and the composition sparse, with the complete course visible against the undecorated baseplate.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render the isometric view from text first and treat it as authoritative, then convert it to top-down. Exact race topology is not a win condition here, and the simple course can be validated from the resulting plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a small scale because the single short course must fit comfortably on one baseplate. The theme is a bare daytime driving test ground with plain markings and barriers. Apply `P0`; the map is one flat, walkable surface with no overhang, interior transition, separate map, or structurally constrained race route.
