# Scene prompt
Create a low-graphics 3D mech combat map set across Ayush Forest, an extremely large forested planet that forms the whole playable world. The team begins after ejecting from a warship, landing at a small safe landing-zone base near the forest edge, with space for the squad and a dropship pad. About six distinct locations are spread widely through the hostile forest and connected by long ground routes, including a main early-story location near the starting base; the dropship travels between unlocked landing areas. Dense woodland, uneven natural terrain, and scattered resource sites fill the spaces between locations, while red-and-black alien forces occupy hostile areas.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure, with Shooter secondary. A long investigation, side quests, and widely spread destinations drive the journey, while ranged mech combat determines hostile-space needs.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`: all of Ayush Forest is one contiguous explorable world. No preset remains recognisable; the Quest Hub bundle is closest, but the prompt asks for dropship travel rather than a physical quest board or progression blockade.

## Config requirements
Use `path-trail` for long ground routes between locations; `landmark-focal` to identify destinations across dense woodland; `cover-los` for trunks, rocks, and wreckage that break plasma-fire sightlines; `terrain-relief` for uneven forest ground; `npc-population` for exactly three AI squadmates and adequate formation space; and the drawn half of `teleporter-link` as dropship pads at unlocked landing areas. Draw one small safe landing-zone base near the forest edge and scattered visible resource sites.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` emitters for red-and-black aliens inside hostile locations and along selected approaches, facing readable defense directions. Place exactly three `npc-population` companion starts with the player at the base. Place `collectible-nodes` at scattered resource sites between destinations. Place the functional half of `teleporter-link` on the base dropship pad and each destination landing area, linking only established travel stops.

## Layout components
Build about six distinct locations across one extremely large forest, including the starting landing-zone base and an early-story destination nearby. Long ground routes branch through dense woodland and uneven terrain, with resource clearings between them. Give each location a landmark silhouette and a nearby open landing area. The base contains squad staging space and one dropship pad; hostile sites use alien plasma emplacements, wreckage, and defensive cover. The forest perimeter forms the boundary, and the composition should emphasize vast spacing between six destinations.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the top-down plan is rendered first and authoritative so the six-location route network, landing areas, and hostile spacing remain coherent.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is huge, driven by long travel between about six locations. The theme is low-graphics military science fiction in a hostile forest. Use `tiered` for uneven natural relief without overhangs; otherwise use `P0`.
