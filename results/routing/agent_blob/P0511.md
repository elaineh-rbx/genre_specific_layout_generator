# Scene prompt
Crie um mapa 3D original de Tower Defense em estilo cartoon brilhante, compacto e legível, para até 4 jogadores defenderem uma base compartilhada. Um caminho inimigo claramente visível atravessa o cenário em cerca de 8–10 curvas ou pontos de passagem, começando em uma entrada distante e terminando na base destacada no fim do trajeto. Disponha áreas abertas e próprias para posicionar torres ao longo dos dois lados do caminho, com visibilidade clara sobre trechos retos e curvas; mantenha o cenário bonito, otimizado e com iluminação e efeitos simples.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Strategy. It is a multiplayer Tower Defense map centered on a shared base, fixed enemy movement, and tower placement beside the route.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-actor-track`: one continuous enemy path crosses the compact map from a distant entrance to the shared base without branching or ambiguous self-crossings. The preset is Tower Defense.

## Config requirements
Use `buildzone-grid` as broad, visually distinct tower-placement ground along both sides of the route, positioned to give useful sightlines over straight sections and bends. Use the visible half of `tracker-core` as the prominent shared defended base at the path endpoint. Keep decoration original, sparse enough for performance, and outside the legal placement ground.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawner-npc` at the distant route entrance. Place the functional half of `tracker-core` on the shared base at the opposite end. The placement stage must preserve tower-valid zones on both sides of the lane and exclude the enemy path, boundary, and decorative obstacles. Up to 4 player starts should occupy a small safe apron beside the base without blocking either the route or tower sites.

## Layout components
Build one compact bounded battlefield around a single highly visible path with roughly 8–10 curves or waypoints. Alternate readable straight runs and broad turns, leaving open tower pads on both sides and clear views across several nearby route sections. Put the enemy entrance at the far edge and make the shared base the strongest landmark at the endpoint. Use flat or gently shaped cartoon ground, simple colorful trees, rocks, fences, and low structures as edge dressing only. Enclose the play space with a bright stylized boundary and compose it so most of the complete route is visible in one frame.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish the continuous 8–10-turn enemy route and legal placement margins first, then dress that authoritative plan isometrically.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by the compact route and support for up to 4 players. The theme is bright, accessible, original cartoon Tower Defense with simple lighting and effects. The route is `P6`, because the fixed enemy track must be valid by construction.
