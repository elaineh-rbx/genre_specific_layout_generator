# Scene prompt

Create a polished semi-realistic Roblox 3D survival map set in a genuinely seamless, large Antarctic open world. A sheltered starting area supports an individually owned, expandable base: it begins as a tiny emergency shelter and can grow into connected insulated rooms with foundations, walls, roofs, storage, generators, heaters, solar panels, wind turbines, garages, greenhouses, workshops, laboratories, and lookout towers. Surround the base with nearby snowy terrain and common salvage areas, then extend outward through ice fields, glaciers, frozen lakes, mountain ranges, caves, ice tunnels, abandoned research stations, crashed aircraft, and shipwrecks. Place increasingly remote hidden facilities and scientific-expedition remnants across the continuous landscape, with the furthest regions requiring vehicle travel; include routes suited to snowmobiles and snowcats, cargo-sled access, and late-game helicopter landing areas. Show driftwood, scrap, stone, fuel caches, food supplies, hides, electronics, and rare scientific equipment distributed farther from shelter. Include penguin and seal areas along coasts and frozen water, offshore orcas, and birds overhead. The landscape is exposed and harsh, with blizzards, whiteouts, high winds, deep snow, sunset light, night darkness, and auroras contrasting with the warm, cozy illuminated base.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Survival, specifically resource and base survival against the Antarctic environment.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-biomes` for danger and reward increasing with distance, but override its usual separate-map route because the brief explicitly requires one seamless world. The preset is Resource Survival.

## Config requirements

Draw `buildzone-plot` as one individually owned sheltered base footprint. Add `building-interior` for the expandable warm shelter and enterable abandoned stations, `terrain-relief` for glaciers and mountains with caves and ice tunnels, `water-body` for frozen lakes and offshore water, `hazard-kill` for exposed storm-swept regions, and the visible habitat half of `npc-population` for penguins, seals, orcas, and birds.

## Layout requirements

Place `collectible-nodes` for driftwood, scrap, stone, fuel, food, hides, electronics, and rare scientific equipment, grading rarity outward from the shelter. Place the functional `npc-population` groups along coast, frozen-water, and offshore habitats. Site one player start at the emergency shelter and vehicle access points along snowmobile, snowcat, cargo-sled, and helicopter routes.

## Layout components

Anchor the warm base in a protected starting basin. Radiate continuous expedition routes through nearby salvage snowfields, glaciers, frozen lakes, mountain passes, caves, tunnels, research stations, wreck sites, and remote facilities. Reserve connected expansion ground for rooms, garages, greenhouses, workshops, laboratories, towers, and utilities. Put landing areas in the furthest regions. Bound the huge world naturally with impassable mountains, ocean, and ice.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first as authoritative, then dress the isometric view, because all regions and vehicle routes must remain continuously connected.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is huge, driven by vehicle-distance regions in one world. The theme is semi-realistic Antarctic austerity contrasted with warm shelter light and auroras. Route `P2` for caves and overlapping tunnels, `P3` for exterior-to-interior travel, and `CHECK` for water and offshore play. The explicit seamless requirement overrides `P4`.
