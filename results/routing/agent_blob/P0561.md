## Enriched image prompt
Create one huge seamless semi-realistic Antarctic open world centered on individually owned shelter-building grounds. Place common-resource ice fields near the starting base region, then grade the continuous landscape outward through glaciers, frozen lakes, mountains, caves, ice tunnels, abandoned research stations, crashed aircraft, and coastal shipwrecks toward remote vehicle-dependent regions with rarer resources and harsher exposure. Provide broad snowmobile, snowcat, and cargo-sled routes between landmarks and sparse late-game helicopter landing areas. Let each tiny emergency shelter expand into connected furnished rooms with storage, heating, generators, insulation, a garage, greenhouse, workshop, laboratory, lookout tower, solar panels, and wind turbines. Include campfire ground, enterable research structures, offshore water and ice, penguins, seals, birds, and distant orcas. Use snowstorm, sunset, and aurora lighting while keeping expedition routes and distance tiers readable.

## Genre
The genre is Survival, specifically resource survival with permanent personal shelter building.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-biomes` because danger and reward rise with distance from base. The Resource Survival preset fits directly. Override the shape’s normal separate-map route: the author explicitly requires one seamless continuous world.

## Config requirements
Use `buildzone-plot` for independent player shelter grounds, `building-interior` for enterable shelters and abandoned research stations, `terrain-relief` for glaciers, mountains, and ice formations, `water-body` for frozen lakes and offshore water, and `npc-population` for penguins, seals, birds, and offshore orcas. From the shared catalogue, use Simulation’s `path-road-vehicle` routed as `P0` circulation for snowmobiles, snowcats, and cargo sleds rather than a race course. Caves, ice tunnels, wrecks, landing areas, and all named shelter-expansion structures are direct requirements.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` by distance tier: nearby driftwood, scrap metal, ice, stone, fabric, fuel, animal hides, and food supplies; farther-out electronics, rare scientific equipment, blueprints, journals, and recordings. Place the functional half of `npc-population` on appropriate coast, ice, air, and offshore grounds. Place one player `SpawnZone` with no canonical option ID at each owned shelter plot and vehicle `SpawnZone` markers against snow routes and remote helicopter landing areas.

## Layout components
One continuous world contains a start region, personal shelter plots, outward difficulty bands, glaciers, lakes, mountains, caves, tunnels, stations, aircraft wrecks, shipwrecks, resource sites, wildlife grounds, snow-vehicle routes, and remote landing areas. Each plot supports foundations, walls, floors, roofs, connected rooms, furniture, storage, utilities, garage, greenhouse, workshop, laboratory, tower, solar panels, and wind turbines. Ocean and extreme outer ice form the boundary.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: seamless region connectivity and distance-based progression must lead before atmospheric dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge. The theme is semi-realistic Antarctic survival with cozy bases and harsh expeditions. Use `tiered` for relief, `P2` for caves and ice tunnels, `P3` for exterior-to-interior play, and `CHECK` for water and helicopter volumes. Do not use `P4`; seamless continuity explicitly overrides it.
