# Scene prompt
Build a medieval castle dungeon with stone corridors lit by torches, connecting three distinct rooms in sequence. Room 1 is a tight roughly 40-stud chamber containing small skeleton enemies; Room 2 is another tight roughly 40-stud puzzle chamber with floor pressure plates leading to an iron gate. Beyond the gate, Room 3 opens into a larger boss hall, with a giant chest at the far end as the dungeon finish.

# Agent decision

The dominant genre is Action (`action`) because two of the three chambers are enemy and boss combat spaces. Puzzle (`puzzle`) is secondary because the middle chamber’s pressure-plate solve controls forward progress.

## Shape and preset
Use `rooms-sequence`, a linear chain of three enclosed castle chambers joined by stone corridors. The Hack & Slash preset is the closest fit, retaining its room-chain structure and hostile-emitter logic while adding the explicit puzzle gate; omit breakable structures and combat lock-in because neither was requested.

## Config requirements
Draw `button-solve` as floor pressure plates in Room 2 and `gate-progression` as the iron gate they open. The stone corridors, torch sconces, three chambers, giant finish chest, and medieval masonry are scene-specific drawn geometry without canonical option IDs.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` emitters for small skeleton enemies within Room 1 and for the boss within Room 3, kept beyond each chamber entrance. Place the functional side of `button-solve` on the visible pressure plates in Room 2 so their trigger state opens the gate. Place one collectible reward marker at the giant chest at the far end of Room 3. The prompt implies exactly three rooms, one puzzle trigger group, one boss, and one finish chest.

## Layout components
Build three rooms in strict order. Room 1 and Room 2 are each roughly 40 studs across; Room 3 is a larger open boss hall. Short torch-lit stone corridors connect Room 1 to Room 2 and the iron gate to Room 3. Put pressure plates on Room 2’s floor, the gate on its forward wall, and the giant chest centered against the boss hall’s far end. Enclose the whole dungeon in castle masonry with no exterior play area, and frame the composition lengthwise so the start-to-chest progression reads clearly.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: render the plan first and make it authoritative because the three-room sequence, gated transition, and reachable finish must remain valid.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by two 40-stud chambers and one larger hall. The theme is torch-lit medieval castle dungeon. Use `P0`; the map is one enclosed surface with no outside transition, overhang, or separate map.
