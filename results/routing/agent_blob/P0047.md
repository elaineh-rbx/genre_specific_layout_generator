## Enriched image prompt
Create two separate spaces for a first-person last-standing shooter: a compact waiting lobby and a small modern military-base battlefield for up to eight players. Keep the battle map open and readable with evenly distributed exterior military structures and cover that supports explosive-projectile combat, while excluding enterable building interiors and ground weapon pickups. Separate the lobby fully from the active match so waiting players cannot see or enter the battlefield. Compose the battlefield around a clear central final-combat area with protected starting pockets around its perimeter.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Shooter, specifically a compact last-standing battle.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-staged`, named Lobby and Isolated Stage, taken from outside Shooter's usual shapes because the prompt explicitly separates the waiting lobby from the battle map. Preset is none: Battle Royale supplies the last-standing boundary idea, but its scattered loot and enterable-building bundle conflicts with the author answers.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub`, taken from the shared catalogue, for the compact waiting lobby. Use `cover-los` for evenly distributed exterior military-base cover. Use the visible side of `spawn-protected` for eight shielded starting pockets around the battlefield. Do not use `collectible-loot`; weapons are purchased rather than found on the ground.

## Layout requirements
Use `spawn-protected` to place up to eight player starts, one in each perimeter pocket with no direct opening sightline. Use `boundary-shrinking` for the invisible closing play area, centered so the final safe region contracts toward the battlefield's central combat ground.

## Layout components
The Lobby is one separate compact map sized for eight waiting players. The Battlefield is a second small map: an exterior modern military base with a central open combat zone, surrounding cover, eight protected perimeter starts, and a bounded match edge. Military structures remain facades and solid cover rather than interiors. No weapon pickups occupy the battlefield.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish balanced cover, eight separated starts, and the central closing-boundary target first, then dress the authoritative plan as a military base.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is medium because eight-player sightlines and individual cover elements must remain legible in a compact match. The theme is a small modern military base. Use `P4` because lobby and battlefield are separate maps; no `P3` is needed because buildings are exterior-only.
