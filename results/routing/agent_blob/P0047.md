# Scene prompt

Create a 3D first-person combat map with a lobby area leading into a small modern military base battlefield designed for up to 8 players. The battlefield is a compact base with multiple cover-filled combat zones and clear routes between them, suitable for grenade-launcher fights; players enter it from the lobby for each round. No ground weapon pickups are present.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Shooter: this is a first-person last-player-standing combat round.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-staged`, taken from outside Shooter’s usual set, because the waiting lobby and active battlefield must be isolated so lobby players cannot interfere with a round. The preset is none; neither Battle Royale nor Arena Deathmatch fits after removing world loot and other unrequested mode features.

## Config requirements

Use `cover-los` for robust waist-high and full-body military cover distributed across several combat zones, with enough spacing for grenade arcs. Use `spawn-protected` to draw solid sightline-breaking barriers around round-entry positions. Draw clear ground routes between the zones, but do not include weapon racks, loot, or ground pickups.

## Layout requirements

Place one lobby arrival region sized for up to 8 players and one transition trigger that sends the round roster into the battlefield. Place exactly 8 battlefield spawn positions, distributed around the map rather than clustered, each behind the geometry for `spawn-protected`. No `collectible-loot`, `powerup-buffs`, or weapon placement is needed because every player starts equipped and upgrades are purchased outside the map layout.

## Layout components

Build a compact lobby and a separate small military-base battlefield. Divide the battlefield into multiple cover-filled yards linked by clear roads, alleys, or gated passages. Use concrete blast walls, crates, barriers, low service structures, and restrained military signage, leaving open pockets large enough for grenade-launcher fights. Enclose the battlefield with secure base fencing or walls and compose the combat zones as one readable compact arena.

## Render order

Render in `isometric` order because the map’s military look and cover rhythm lead, while its routes do not require computed topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, explicitly designed for at most 8 players. The theme is a modern military base built for explosive combat. Use `P4` because the lobby and match arena are separate spaces.
