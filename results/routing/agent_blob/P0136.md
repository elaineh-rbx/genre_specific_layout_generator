# Scene prompt
Create a bright stylized-toon 3D fashion dress-up map with private bedroom-and-closet dressing suites for each player. Each suite begins at a bed and opens into a personal closet area displaying outfit options including Y2K, cute-core, emo, and large puffy dresses. Connect the dressing area to a glossy fashion-show runway, with a clear catwalk and viewing space arranged around it for spectators.

# Agent decision

The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim. Dressing an avatar and presenting the result is the core activity; the timed round and star voting do not add a separate spatial genre.

## Shape and preset
Use `stage-runway`, organized as private preparation suites feeding one shared catwalk and spectator area. The matching preset is Dress Up, with its preparation area changed from shared booths to the private bedroom-and-closet suites explicitly requested.

Use `vignette-photo` for polished runway poses and outfit-showcase backdrops. Use `path-street` for broad indoor circulation from the suite exits to the runway entrance. Add free-text visible geometry for repeated private suites, each containing one bed, one personal closet, and displays grouped into four named outfit categories: Y2K, cute-core, emo, and large puffy dresses.

## Layout requirements
Place one private player spawn marker at the bed in each dressing suite, with the total matching the eventual player-capacity setting. Place one free-text runway-entry trigger at the merge point from the dressing corridor and evenly spaced presentation marks along the catwalk for the voting phase. The five-minute timer and star-voting rules are systems, not image geometry.

## Layout components
Arrange repeated private bedroom-and-closet suites along a shared corridor, keeping each suite visually screened from the others. Every suite starts at its bed and opens directly into its closet displays. Merge the corridor into one glossy fashion-show hall with a straight catwalk, a clear runway entrance, a focal endpoint, and spectator viewing space along the sides. Keep the floor level, all circulation walkable, and the entire map enclosed. Frame the composition to show both the repeated suite pattern and the runway destination.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the bright toon fashion identity first, then derive the top-down plan, because exact suite count is capacity-driven and the connections are straightforward rather than structurally constrained.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, driven by multiple private suites plus one shared runway hall. The theme is bright stylized-toon Fashion Week with glossy, cute dressing displays. Use `P0`; all spaces occupy one level in one continuous interior without overhangs or outside-to-inside transitions.
