# Scene prompt
Create a 3D hide-and-seek map as a small, compact grass maze under dark lighting, with a few branching paths, enclosed turns, and hiding corners throughout the maze.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Party & Casual because the maze hosts rounds of hide-and-seek rather than spatial riddles.

## Shape and preset
Use `puzzle-maze`, taken from outside the genre’s usual shape list, because the grass maze must have a generated, solvable topology. Retain the Childhood Game preset but substitute this maze shape and omit its social hub; its concealment and maze options still describe the requested play.

## Config requirements
Apply `obstacle-maze` as a compact warren of branching grass-walled paths, enclosed turns, and hiding corners. Apply `cover-los` as thick grass or hedge clusters at selected bends that block seeker sightlines without sealing passages. Use dark, low-key lighting while preserving enough edge contrast for every corridor and junction to remain readable.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` with one seeker start near the maze entrance and hider starts distributed among distinct reachable corners, preventing immediate sightlines between them. Place `trigger-scoring` as tag-detection regions for found hiders and a round-completion region that can recognize when all hiders are found. The backup timer is gameplay logic and adds no geometry. Keep all start markers on valid maze cells and out of wall footprints.

## Layout components
Build one small, bounded grass maze with a clear entrance, a connected network of a few branching paths, multiple loops, enclosed turns, and several hiding corners. Use tall dark-green grass or trimmed hedge-like walls over a simple grassy floor, with a continuous outer boundary preventing escape. Avoid inaccessible pockets and excessive scale so a seeker can search the full maze. Compose the maze as one compact footprint with its entrance and major branches legible from above despite the dark atmosphere.

## Render order
Render `authored_plan` first and make its generated blueprint authoritative because a maze must be solvable by construction; dress that plan in grass and dark lighting afterward.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by the explicitly compact maze and few branches. The theme is dark, tense grass-maze hide-and-seek. Use `P6` because valid connected maze topology is structural to play; no overhang, interior transition, separate map, or volumetric movement is required.
