# Scene prompt

Create a small modern suburban social hangout house with shared living spaces and two private bedrooms. Arrange a central living room and kitchen as the communal area, with the two lockable bedrooms and lockable bathrooms positioned off separate short hallways for privacy.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim: the house exists for open-ended social interaction and private roleplay, with no task or progression loop.

## Shape and preset

Use `interior-single`, with the entire playable map contained inside one suburban house. No genre preset is recognisable after narrowing the deliverable to one fixed interior and omitting town, roads, plots, and a central public hub, so the preset is none.

## Config requirements

Draw a central communal living room and kitchen, then two separated private bedroom suites reached by different short hallways. Make each bedroom and bathroom doorway visually distinct and capable of taking a lock. Do not add `building-interior`: players never cross between an exterior map and this house, because the interior is the whole scene.

## Layout requirements

Place four lock trigger volumes, one across each of the two bedroom doorways and one across each corresponding bathroom doorway. Site each trigger tightly to its own door geometry so locking one room cannot block its hallway or the other suite. Place the initial player spawn in the communal living area, outside all lockable rooms.

## Layout components

Build one small modern suburban house interior. The central living room is the primary gathering zone, with an open kitchen adjoining it. From opposite sides of this shared core, run two separate short hallways, each ending at one private bedroom. Give each bedroom access to its own lockable bathroom, for exactly two bedrooms and two bathrooms total. Keep circulation outside the door swings and preserve a clear communal route between living room and kitchen. Use warm contemporary furnishings, clean drywall, wood flooring in shared spaces, and tile in bathrooms. The building shell fully encloses the map. Frame all shared and private zones legibly from above.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, then dress the isometric view from it, because correct separation of the two private branches and their lockable thresholds is essential.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is small, set by the two-bedroom brief. The theme is modern suburban social comfort. Route `P0`: one interior floor has no exterior transition, stacked surface, or separate map.
