# Scene prompt

Create a 3D free-roam dress-up map set inside a bright cartoon boutique interior. Arrange clear, easy-to-explore sections for body-shape choices and clothing options, with open walkable space connecting the areas and interactive boutique displays.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, with free-roam avatar customization as the primary activity.

## Shape and preset

Use `interior-single`: one boutique interior is the complete walkable map, with no exterior transition. The preset is none because Dress Up assumes a judging runway and social-showcase spaces, while the author explicitly requested purely free-roam dressing and exploration.

## Config requirements

Use `path-street` as broad, open pedestrian circulation connecting the customization areas. Draw one clearly marked body-shape section with three distinct display stations for skinny, muscular, and fat body choices, plus separate clothing sections organized around colorful boutique racks, shelves, mannequins, mirrors, and interactive display counters. Keep the displays visible across the room and leave generous walking space between them.

## Layout requirements

Place exactly three uncatalogued body-selection interaction triggers, one at each skinny, muscular, and fat display station. Place outfit-selection interaction points directly in front of each clothing display grouping, aligned outside the circulation lanes so browsing players do not obstruct movement. No runway scoring, teleporters, vehicles, NPC population, or property markers are needed.

## Layout components

Build a single bright cartoon boutique interior with a central open floor and an easy loop of walkable space around the customization displays. Put the three body-type stations together in one highly legible zone, each with its own sign and avatar silhouette. Arrange clothing displays in neighboring themed sections around the perimeter, using racks, shelves, mannequins, mirrors, and colorful counters. Connect every section through wide unobstructed aisles, with no enclosed back rooms or dead-end browsing pockets. Use a flat polished floor and colorful rounded retail architecture, enclosing the entire play space within the boutique walls. Compose the room so body choices and clothing areas are visible at a glance.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: establish the cheerful boutique presentation first, then convert it into the top-down circulation plan; visual clarity is authoritative.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, suited to one freely explorable boutique. The theme is bright, colorful cartoon fashion retail. Use `P0`; all play occurs on one flat interior surface with no outside-to-inside crossing or overlapping levels.
