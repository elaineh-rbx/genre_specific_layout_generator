## Enriched image prompt
Create a short fantasy valley visible at a glance from a player overlook on a foreground hill. Run one continuous rocky dirt lane from a distant enemy entrance through a few gentle bends to a clearly defended base, with flat tower-placement ground along both sides. Surround it with rolling green hills and scattered pine trees. Show sturdy low-poly fantasy tower silhouettes along the route: wooden archer posts, stone cannon turrets, and crystal-topped mage spires. Use warm saturated colours, chunky hand-painted textures, golden-hour light, and soft shadows; goblin-like enemies in earthy greens and browns enter only at the lane mouth.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Strategy, specifically Tower Defense. Coins, waves, tower effects, camera behavior, and increasing difficulty are mechanics; their spatial requirements are the lane, placement ground, enemy origin, and defended base.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-actor-track`: one unbroken winding enemy lane runs from spawn to the core with no forks, dead ends, or ambiguous self-crossings. Match the Tower Defense preset.

## Config requirements
Use `buildzone-grid` as flat structural tower-placement ground along both sides of the dirt lane, adapted to the fantasy valley rather than shown as a literal modern grid. Use `tracker-core` for the defended fantasy base at the terminal end. Use `choke-bottleneck` at the path’s gentle bends where towers can concentrate fire. Use `terrain-relief` for the rolling hills and elevated player overlook.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawner-npc` Wave Origin at the distant mouth of the dirt lane, oriented toward the base. Place the layout half of one `tracker-core` exactly at the lane’s terminal end. Add one free-text `SpawnZone` on the overlooking hill, facing the full valley. Keep all tower placement ground outside the hard-coded lane and leave the lane continuously navigable.

## Layout components
Build one short valley with a foreground lookout hill, one winding dirt lane with a few gentle bends, paired tower-placement strips, one enemy entrance, one defended base, rolling grassy relief, and scattered pines. The valley sides form the boundary. Compose the complete spawn-to-core route in one view from the elevated player position.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish a continuous, non-forking enemy route and legal placement ground first, then dress the isometric fantasy valley; `authored_plan` is not available for tower-defense lanes.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale because the short valley must fit at a glance while individual towers and enemies remain distinguishable. The theme is warm low-poly stylized fantasy at golden hour. Use `P6` for the structurally valid lane and `tiered` for rolling relief with no overhang.
