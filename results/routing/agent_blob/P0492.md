# Scene prompt
Create a 3D tower defense battlefield viewed from an elevated first-person hillside vantage, overlooking a compact fantasy valley. A single short, winding rocky dirt lane with a few gentle bends crosses between rolling green hills, starting in the background and leading toward the player base in the foreground. Leave clear build spaces along both sides of the path for distinct sturdy fantasy towers: wooden archer posts, stone cannon turrets, and crystal-topped mage spires. Scatter pine trees across the hillsides, with goblin-like enemy figures marching along the lane. Use a low-poly, hand-painted stylized look with warm saturated colors, golden-hour lighting, and soft shadows.

Site tower-placement markers on the flat `buildzone-grid` pads along both sides, preserving clear targeting arcs and never blocking the route.

## Layout components
Build one compact valley zone containing a single short rocky dirt lane with a few gentle bends. The lane begins high in the background, crosses between rolling grassy hills, narrows once, and terminates at the player base in the foreground. Flat build pads flank both sides; pine trees scatter across non-buildable slopes. Keep the outer hills as the boundary.
