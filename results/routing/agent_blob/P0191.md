## Enriched image prompt
Create a bleak snowy mountain adventure map focused on one continuous ascent from a lower approach to a clearly visible summit. Cut a connected climbing trail across steep snow-covered slopes and exposed ridges, with the summit landmark pulling the route upward. Place one lockable, enterable tent shelter on a flat protected campsite along the trail, providing a clear refuge from the surrounding whiteout. Keep the tent and path readable against the severe snow terrain while preserving the dangerous, cold, low-visibility atmosphere.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure, with Survival secondary because the route includes shelter from dangerous cold.

## Shape and preset
Use `route-guided`, a Guided Corridor that directs the player from the lower slope to the summit. The preset is Guided Trail, dropping `reveal-exit` because there is no enclosed threshold and adding the stated mountain relief and shelter.

## Config requirements
Use `path-trail` for the connected snowy ascent, `landmark-focal` for the visible summit destination, `terrain-relief` for the steep mountain slopes and ridges, and Survival's `building-interior` for the lockable tent that players enter from the exposed trail.

## Layout requirements
No invisible post-segmentation placement is required. The summit, trail, campsite, and tent are visible geometry; tent locking and cold protection are mechanics.

## Layout components
The map contains a lower approach zone, one continuous climbing trail, several readable slope and ridge sections, one protected flat campsite with a single enterable tent, and a summit endpoint. Open snowy mountain edges bound the route. Compose the ascent diagonally through frame so the shelter remains identifiable below the summit and no trail section is visually lost.

## Render order
Use `topdown` because the lower-to-summit trail must remain continuously walkable before steep relief and whiteout dressing are applied.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale to preserve tent and trail detail while showing one complete mountain ascent. The theme is a bleak survival whiteout in dangerous snowy mountains. `tiered` is required for strong mountain elevation without overhangs, and `P3` is required for movement between the exposed mountainside and the tent interior.
