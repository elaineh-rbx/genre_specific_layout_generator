# Scene prompt

Create a 3D mountain adventure map in a bleak survival whiteout: a snow-covered ascent leading toward a high mountain summit, with a tent shelter positioned along the route as a safe stop from the cold.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Adventure, with Survival secondary because dangerous cold makes the enterable tent a functional shelter along the journey.

## Shape and preset

Use `route-guided` from the Guided Trail preset: one directed snowbound ascent from the mountain foot to the summit. Keep the exploration trail and summit landmark, while omitting the preset’s cinematic reveal exit because the brief does not describe one.

## Config requirements

Build `path-trail` as a continuous climb through snowy slopes, wind-scoured switchbacks, and narrow but walkable mountain ledges. Use `landmark-focal` for the high summit, visible often enough to orient the ascent. Add `terrain-relief` for the mountain’s strong vertical rise without stacked or overhanging surfaces. Use `building-interior` for one lockable expedition tent that players genuinely enter to shelter from the cold, placed on a safe level shelf beside the route.

## Layout requirements

Place one safe-shelter volume inside the tent, entirely enclosed by its footprint so protection begins only after entering. Put a player start at the broad base of the ascent and one destination marker on stable ground at the summit. No collectible, enemy, or additional checkpoint placement is implied.

## Layout components

Build one snow-covered mountain ascent beginning on a broad lower shelf and climbing through switchbacks toward a distinct high summit. Shape the path with snowy banks, exposed dark rock, sparse frozen shrubs, and occasional marker poles that remain readable in a whiteout. Midway up, widen the trail into a level campsite shelf holding one sturdy enclosed tent, with a clear entrance facing the route. Continue the path beyond the shelter through steeper but non-overhanging terrain to a compact summit platform. Use cliffs and storm-obscured distance as the outer boundary. Frame the composition diagonally upward from the mountain foot, through the tent, to the summit.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: establish the bleak whiteout mood, visible ascent, and summit hierarchy first, then derive the top-down route from that authoritative image.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is medium, driven by a full mountain ascent with one midway shelter. The theme is bleak survival whiteout. Use `tiered` for strong mountain relief and `P3` because play crosses from the exterior route into the tent interior.
