## Layout components
The entire playable map is one compact mansion floor containing several oversized rooms, connecting corridors, simple puzzle-gated thresholds, and a final exterior door. Corridors loop back around the main rooms instead of terminating in chase dead ends. Furniture and knockable household objects line room edges while leaving kitten-width escape routes clear. Checkpoint pads occupy sheltered thresholds outside immediate pursuit lines. The building shell is the boundary, and no playable upstairs or exterior district is included.

## Render order
