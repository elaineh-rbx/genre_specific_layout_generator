# Scene prompt
Create two small, separate 3D maps in a classic old Roblox dark-fantasy style, built from simple blocky parts with visible studs, medieval structures, dark lighting, fog, and a void-like atmosphere. The first is The Soul Highway, a compact dark staging space between ruined realms, centered on one large Crossing portal with role-selection space around it.

The Crossing leads to a compact ruined medieval fortress mission map, roughly 300–500 studs across. The outer area is a defensible courtyard enclosed by damaged stone walls, with a damaged main gate on one approach, barricade positions around the courtyard, and open lanes for incoming attackers. Behind the courtyard, place an inner keep with a clear retreat route from the gate and courtyard; its interior is the final defended space, with a second breached entrance. Include rooftop access on fortress structures, small positions for friendly soldier squads, and a larger approach area outside the walls for enemy waves. Keep the composition readable and prototype-like, using placeholder blocky characters and simple medieval props rather than realistic terrain or detailed assets.

# Agent decision
### Genre
The dominant genre is Survival: this is explicitly a cooperative wave-survival mission whose spatial progression is an outer defense followed by a final holdout.

### Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded` for the fortress mission, a contained combat space encompassing the outer approach, courtyard, and keep. The preset is Horde Defense, retaining its hostile wave origins and defensible cover while omitting collapsing structures because the brief asks for repairable damage, not destruction.

### Config requirements
Use `cover-los` for blocky barricade positions and damaged wall recesses around the courtyard. Use `building-interior` for crossing from the courtyard into the final keep. Use `npc-population` for visible friendly soldier squads and the ground they occupy. Draw one large Crossing portal in The Soul Highway, the damaged main gate, second breached entrance, battlements, rooftops, and simple medieval props as scene-specific geometry.

### Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` hostile origins across the large exterior approach lanes, outside the defended walls and facing the gate or breach. Place the functional teleport marker within the one Crossing portal. The layout half of `npc-population` sites small friendly squads at several courtyard barricades and fallback positions inside the keep. Provide protected starting room for 1–5 players around role selection in the separate lobby.

### Layout components
The Soul Highway is a compact void staging room centered on one portal with role-selection space around it. The fortress map is 300–500 studs across: exterior enemy approaches lead to a damaged gate and enclosed courtyard, barricades line open defense lanes, and one clear retreat route reaches the inner keep through a second breached entrance. Rooftop access links fortress structures. Damaged stone walls bound the mission, while the exterior approach remains visible beyond them.

### Render order
Use `isometric`: the blocky old-Roblox silhouette and readable courtyard-to-keep composition lead, while the simple bounded circulation can be projected afterward.

### Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, fixed by the 300–500-stud fortress plus a small lobby. The theme is classic studded Roblox dark fantasy with fog and void atmosphere. Use `P4` for two separate maps, `P3` for courtyard-to-keep traversal, and `P2` because accessible rooftops overhang interior and ground surfaces.
