## Enriched image prompt
Create a short finite sci-fi runner corridor that scrolls toward a player fixed at the near end and moving only left or right. Divide the corridor into exactly two parallel lanes: one carrying incoming enemy-drone encounters and the other carrying breakable upgrade walls. Build the route from aligned modular sections, keep both choices visible ahead of the player, and place a clear level-completion gate at the far end. Use clean futuristic floor panels, strong lane dividers, drone-themed danger markings, and glowing upgrade-wall accents without adding extra lanes.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Infinite Runner, with Shooter secondary because the player fires forward at drones and upgrade walls while the world auto-scrolls.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-snap`, Fixed Lane Snap, configured for exactly two lanes and lateral-only player movement. The matching preset is Lane Runner, keeping modular scrolling chunks and moving lane content while omitting unrequested collectible trails.

## Config requirements
Use `chunk-modular` for short aligned corridor segments whose pivots stay on one scrolling axis; `obstacle-moving` for incoming lane content timed against the scroll; `destructible-cluster`, reached from Action through the shared catalogue, for breakable sci-fi upgrade walls; and the image half of `trigger-finish` for a visible completion gate at the end of each finite level.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` origins down the enemy lane for incoming drones. Place `powerup-buffs` at the upgrade-wall positions in the second lane so destroyed walls yield their upgrades. Place `trigger-despawn` a fixed distance behind the player-facing camera to clear passed chunks, drones, and walls. Place the layout half of `trigger-finish` exactly at the terminal gate of each short level.

## Layout components
The build contains one directed corridor, exactly 2 fixed lanes, one enemy lane, one upgrade-wall lane, repeated aligned modular chunks, clear lane dividers, drone origins, breakable upgrade walls, upgrade placements, one rear cleanup volume, and one terminal finish gate. The player position remains fixed at the near end while all route content approaches along the same axis.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish the two-lane chunk alignment and finite sequence first, then dress it as a sci-fi corridor, because a drifted or ambiguous lane would make the runner unplayable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is medium, preserving individual drones and upgrade walls across a short complete level. The theme is clean science fiction with enemy drones and glowing upgrade barriers. `P6` is required for valid lane, chunk, and finish sequencing.
