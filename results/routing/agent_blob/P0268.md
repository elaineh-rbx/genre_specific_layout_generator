# Scene prompt
Create a small, playable wilderness survival arena inspired by New Zealand landscapes, sized for 12 starting players. Arrange rugged natural terrain with open ground, forested areas, rocky slopes, and water features distributed across the arena.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Shooter because the requested Hunger Games format is a last-player-standing battle royale organized around arming players across an open combat map. Survival is secondary through its wilderness survival framing.

## Shape and preset
Use `open-battlefield`, one contiguous natural arena with dispersed terrain regions rather than lanes. The preset is Battle Royale, with its enterable-building option omitted because this scene is wholly wilderness.

## Config requirements
Draw `cover-los` as forest edges, boulders, ridgelines, and broken rocky ground that interrupt long views while leaving open fighting areas. Draw `terrain-relief` as rugged New Zealand-inspired slopes and low ridges, and `water-body` as distributed streams or small ponds that serve as readable barriers rather than a layered swimming volume.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` as exactly 12 evenly distributed tribute starts, each screened from immediate direct sightlines by terrain or forest. Place `collectible-loot` as weapon and equipment pickups spread among open ground, forest pockets, rocky slopes, and waterside sites. Place one `boundary-shrinking` region centered over the arena so its contracting edge compresses all surviving players toward playable terrain.

## Layout components
The arena is one small wilderness basin with a broad open center, several forested pockets around it, rugged rocky slopes along portions of the perimeter, and streams or ponds distributed between regions. Natural paths connect every region without formal lanes. Boulders, tree stands, and ridge folds create cover while preserving readable approaches. The enclosing boundary is formed by steeper rock and dense woodland. Compose the open center as the visual anchor, ringed by varied New Zealand-inspired green slopes, dark forest, exposed stone, and reflective water.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the scenic wilderness composition leads, while the simple contiguous arena does not require a computed maze or circuit.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, explicitly sized for 12 starting players. The theme is rugged, lush New Zealand wilderness. Use `tiered` for the rocky slopes and `CHECK` for the water play envelope; no separate maps or interiors are required.
