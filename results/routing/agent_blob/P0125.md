# Scene prompt

Create a small, simple grey Roblox-style baseplate arena, approximately 100x100 studs, enclosed by plain walls. Arrange colorful oversized hoops and assorted simple obstacles throughout the space as cover and movement markers, leaving short-to-medium open sightlines for pistol training and dashing. Place a clear opposing bot position within the arena.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Shooter: this is a solo combat-training arena built around aiming and movement against one hostile bot.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-bounded`, taken from outside Shooter’s usual shape list because the whole session occurs inside one compact, single-level box rather than along a firing lane or team-lane network. The preset is none: Aim Trainer assumes a directed target field and weapon bench, while this prompt asks for an opposing bot moving or firing inside an arena.

## Config requirements

Use `cover-los` as scattered simple barriers sized to create readable short-to-medium pistol sightlines. Use `spawn-protected` for visible geometry shielding the player’s starting corner from an immediate bot shot. The oversized colorful hoops and assorted blocky obstacles are additional image geometry, distributed as aiming references, dash gates, and cover without turning the floor into a maze.

## Layout requirements

Place one `spawn-protected` player spawn behind the starting-corner shield. Place one `spawner-npc` at the clear opposing bot position across the arena, with an unobstructed initial training direction but intermediate obstacles available once the drill begins. The arena needs exactly one hostile origin and one player start; dashing and pistol assignment are gameplay mechanics, not layout placements.

## Layout components

Build one approximately 100-by-100-stud square grey baseplate enclosed by four plain walls. Put the player start near one edge and the bot position toward the opposite side. Scatter several colorful oversized hoops across the center and side lanes, mixed with simple low and full-height rectangular obstacles. Preserve multiple short-to-medium sightlines and broad enough gaps for lateral dashes. Keep the floor flat, the boundary sealed, and the composition sparse enough that every hoop, bot position, and cover edge reads clearly from above.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render the readable grey-box training look first, then derive the top-down plan; no computed topology is required.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, fixed by the approximately 100x100-stud footprint. The theme is a plain Roblox grey-box arena with bright arcade training markers. Use `P0`; one flat bounded surface loses no information in a single top-down capture.
