# Scene prompt

Build a 3D modern, contemporary small dwelling with a simple clean residential look. Include a small outdoor yard leading to a clear front entrance, with an interior limited to essential rooms including a bedroom for sleeping and a bathroom with a hand-washing area.

# Agent decision

## Genre

This is `No Genre`: the author asks for a house and its essential rooms but gives no roleplay, building, scoring, or other game loop.

## Shape and preset

There is no genre shape. Set `enclosure` to transition because the build crosses from the outdoor yard through the front entrance into the dwelling. Leave `verticality`, `zone-count`, `structure`, and `play-space` at their defaults. The preset is none; the request is a specific compact residential build rather than one of the generic place presets.

## Config requirements

Use `building-interior` to draw a small modern house that is entered from its yard and contains the requested bedroom and bathroom. Draw a clean, simple residential exterior, a clear front door, a bed and sleeping area, and a bathroom sink or basin that visibly provides the hand-washing area. Keep every room essential and compact.

## Layout requirements

Nothing invisible or functional needs placement after segmentation. There are no requested spawns, triggers, pickups, checkpoints, or scoring volumes; sleeping and washing are represented by the visible bed and sink geometry only.

## Layout components

Create two linked zones: a small outdoor front yard and one compact dwelling interior. A short walk leads across the yard to the front entrance, then interior circulation connects the entrance to a bedroom and bathroom. The bedroom contains one bed with modest clearance around it. The bathroom contains a sink or hand basin and only essential fixtures. Use level ground, a simple yard surface, and a modest property edge as the boundary. Frame the house and yard together while keeping the room arrangement readable.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render the contemporary house, yard, and room character first, then derive the top-down plan, since no topology-critical game structure is involved.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is small, driven by one dwelling, one yard, and two essential rooms. The theme is clean modern residential. Use `P3` because play crosses a real outside-to-inside boundary and linked exterior and interior top-downs are required.
