# Scene prompt
Create a 3D toon-comedy social-deduction map with three exaggerated, colorful themed challenge rooms, sized for all 6–12 players to occupy together one room at a time. Each room contains simple physical task spaces and prominent goofy environmental hazards such as trapdoors, light controls, reversed conveyor belts, synchronized floor tiles, glowing battery-carrying routes, and giant moving obstacles. Connect the rooms through simple transition routes to a dramatic central courtroom voting arena, arranged with a discussion floor and a large pit beneath the accused position; the pit may be a lava drop, deep void, or giant toilet. Frame spaces with clear spectator-like sightlines and cinematic focal points for chaotic falls and accusations.

# Agent decision

The dominant genre is Party & Casual, matching the author’s social-deduction and chaotic-party framing. Survival is secondary because players must evade or endure environmental threats.

## Shape and preset
Use `rooms-sequence`, the Room Sequence, taken from outside Party & Casual’s usual set because the whole server advances through exactly three discrete challenge rooms before reaching the courtroom. Use the Minigame preset, substituting this connected sequence for its isolated stage and dropping lobby transport and leaderboard features.

Use `spectator-zone` as clear out-of-play viewing edges around the challenge action and courtroom accusation focal point. Draw battery-carrying routes and light-control stations as scene-specific physical task geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place `trigger-scoring` volumes at each synchronized-tile solution, battery delivery endpoint, and completed room exit. Place exactly three room-progression triggers, one after each themed challenge. Place battery pickups along their carrying routes, sabotage activators against trapdoors, lights, and conveyors, and one voting/drop trigger at the accused position above the courtroom pit. Site starts for 6–12 players together at each active room’s entry.

## Layout components
Build exactly three colorful challenge rooms, each large enough for 6–12 players, connected by short transition corridors to one central courtroom. Distribute the named tasks and hazards across the rooms rather than repeating every device in each. Give the courtroom a discussion floor, accused position, audience sightlines, and one oversized pit below. Use exaggerated rounded props and strong cinematic axes toward falls, moving hazards, and accusations. Enclose the whole sequence as a coherent toon-comedy facility.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the isometric first and make it authoritative because comedic staging, sightlines, and the vertical courtroom reveal drive the composition.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use large scale for three server-sized rooms plus courtroom. The theme is exaggerated colorful toon comedy. `P2` is required because the courtroom floor and accused position overhang the pit below.
