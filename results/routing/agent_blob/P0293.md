Fill it with futuristic skyscrapers, glowing neon billboards, dark rainy streets, and elevated highway platforms. Make every building contribute accessible ledges and rooftops, and distribute neon pipes, narrow traversable edges, ramps, and clear grapple-facing surfaces so movement can chain continuously between nearby structures. Use readable rooftop-to-rooftop gaps and several vertical route choices rather than a sprawling skyline. The street layer remains visible below the overlapping roof and highway layers, but there is no finish line, race route, combat arena, or objective destination.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Action because the game is a fast physical traversal playground built around active movement skills.

## Shape and preset
Use `traversal-city`, Zone (Traversal City): rooftops, ledges, and gaps are sized as the primary movement surface while streets form the fallback layer. The matching preset is Parkour Traversal; keep its explicit city-traversal shape while omitting combat cover.

## Config requirements
Use `hazard-kill` for clearly dangerous open drops between skyscraper routes. Use `choke-bottleneck` for selected narrow neon pipes and ledges that focus traversal before routes fan out again. Accessible roofs, grapple-facing ledges, elevated highways, billboards, and rain-slick streets are required scene-specific visible geometry.

## Layout requirements
Nothing additional needs post-segmentation placement. Sandevistan, Dash, Slide, Grapple, cooldowns, particles, hitbox changes, camera effects, and mobile UI are mechanics; the geometry they require is already expressed as surfaces, ledges, gaps, pipes, and ramps.

## Layout components
One city contains a street layer, several skyscraper masses, accessible rooftop zones, ledges around every building, neon pipe crossings, and elevated highways spanning blocks. Multiple nearby route choices connect horizontal and vertical levels, with grapple-visible surfaces maintaining continuity throughout the playground.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the interconnected traversal routes should be planned before dressing so each rooftop and vertical layer remains reachable, although no single finish path is required.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use large scale because a few complete city blocks must fit while ledges and pipes remain readable. The theme is dark rainy cyberpunk with saturated neon. `P2` is required because rooftops and elevated highways overhang streets, and `CHECK` applies to grapple movement through the layered open-air volume.
