# Scene prompt
Create a massive, dense vertical cyberpunk city movement playground spanning a few richly layered city blocks. Towering futuristic skyscrapers form a compact rooftop-to-rooftop traversal space, with accessible ledges, rooftops, façade platforms, and neon pipes closely and deliberately spaced between buildings. Dark rainy streets run far below, while elevated highway platforms weave through the mid-levels; glowing neon billboards, illuminated building surfaces, and cables frame the vertical skyline. Compose clear routes across roofs, along ledges, and between high-rise structures for seamless fast movement, with the city extending upward in dense layers rather than outward into a sprawling skyline.

# Agent decision

## Genre
Obby & Platformer is the sole genre because traversal skill and chaining movement abilities across discrete surfaces are the game, even though this playground has no terminating course.

## Shape and preset
Use `traversal-city`, taken from outside the genre’s usual shapes: a compact city is built to be crossed over via rooftops, ledges, facade platforms, and gaps, with streets as the lower fallback layer. Preset is none because the open movement playground does not preserve the sequential structure or obstacle bundles of the available obby presets.

## Config requirements
Use `obstacle-jump` for deliberate roof-to-roof gaps, facade platforms, wraparound ledges, and neon-pipe transitions that support dashes and boosted jumps. Towering skyscrapers, elevated highways, neon billboards, facade lighting, cables, rain-darkened streets, and the layered rooftop surfaces are explicit uncatalogued image geometry. Do not add a winner zone, checkpoints, lethal surfaces, or a start hub because the brief defines free traversal rather than failure-and-retry progression.

## Layout requirements
Place uncatalogued grapple-target properties on the aimed ledges, rooftop lips, neon pipes, billboard frames, and elevated highway edges required to bridge intended route gaps. Every major rooftop cluster needs multiple reachable grapple targets, but no exact count is supplied. Site these targets against segmented structural edges rather than floating in open air. Nothing else needs placement after segmentation.

## Layout components
Build a few dense city blocks with streets at the lowest level, elevated highway platforms through the middle, and closely interlinked skyscraper roofs above. Each building needs accessible roof edges, facade ledges, neon pipes, and clear landing surfaces. Gaps and vertical offsets must support seamless combinations of dash, slide, boosted speed, and grapple movement, with several cross-links rather than one linear route. Dark rainy streets provide a recoverable lower layer. Dense high-rises form the outer boundary. Compose the frame vertically, exposing low street, mid-level highway, and upper rooftop networks at once.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, because unreachable ledges or broken movement links would make the playground unplayable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large, driven by richly layered verticality across a few blocks rather than outward sprawl. The theme is dark rainy cyberpunk with saturated neon. Use `P6` because traversal connections must be valid by construction and `P2` because roofs, ledges, and highways overhang lower streets.
