# Scene prompt
Create a 3D marble-and-glass hotel lobby designed as a social hangout space, with a water fountain as the central focal point. Arrange the lobby openly around the fountain, with no round teleporters or queue area.

# Agent decision
## Genre
This is `No Genre`: it is a social hangout place with no rounds, minigames, scoring, or other game loop.

## Shape and preset
No genre shape is assigned. Set `axis-enclosure` to interior-only because the entire scene is one hotel lobby and no outside-to-inside transition is needed. Verticality remains single-surface, zone count is single, structure is dressed, and play space is grounded. Use the Social Space preset.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for the open gathering floor arranged around the fountain. Use `landmark-focal` for the central marble fountain, whose flowing water is the lobby’s visual anchor. Use `spawn-area` for a clearly composed hotel entrance edge and arrival sightline facing the fountain. The marble floors, glass walls, hotel columns, lounge seating, planters, and reception-like architectural dressing are visible freeform geometry. Do not add teleporters or a queue area.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawn-area` arrival zone just inside the main lobby entrance, oriented so arriving players face the fountain and can step into the surrounding social floor. Nothing else needs post-segmentation placement: there are no triggers, queues, checkpoints, pickups, spawners, or scoring volumes.

## Layout components
Build one broad rectangular interior lobby with a circular fountain exactly at its center. Wrap a generous ring of walkable gathering space around it, then arrange seating groups and planters along the perimeter without blocking circulation. Place the main entrance on one short edge and balance the remaining edges with glass wall bays, marble columns, and hotel-lobby alcoves. Keep the floor level and the boundary fully enclosed. Compose the fountain as the dominant center of the frame, with symmetrical marble-and-glass architecture supporting it.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the material-rich hotel interior first, then derive the simple top-down plan from that authoritative composition.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, as the entire map is one lobby. The theme is polished marble-and-glass hospitality. The route is `P0`: one enclosed interior on a single surface needs no exterior transition or extra capture.
