# Scene prompt
Create a 3D open-world pirate adventure map beginning in the First Sea with a small, playable Starter Island. Arrange a central starter settlement with quest-giving NPCs, shops, and a dock with boats, surrounded by beginner enemy areas and a distinct boss location. The island sits in a broad ocean with clear open water routes toward distant future islands. Place portals or dedicated NPC entry points in the overworld for separate instanced dungeon and raid areas. The larger world is an ocean spanning dozens of islands, including a pirate town, marine base, jungle, desert kingdom, sky, frozen, volcano, samurai, ninja, demon, soul, hero, ancient, raid, arena, and end-game islands, with later seas beyond the First Sea.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG, with Adventure secondary. Levelling through quests, enemy areas, bosses, shops, dungeons, and raids defines the RPG; ocean exploration supplies the adventure layer.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-hub-dungeon`: the Starter Island is the safe overworld hub and its dungeon and raid entrances lead to separate instanced maps. The preset is Action RPG, retaining its sanctuary, social services, hostile zones, and enemy emitters while adding maritime access.

## Config requirements
Use `safezone-town` for the central settlement, protected from beginner enemies. Use `social-hub` for tightly grouped quest NPCs and shops. Draw `hazard-aggro` as beginner enemy clearings and one distinct boss arena outside town. Use `teleporter-link` for visible dungeon and raid entry points. Add `npc-population` for quest givers, shopkeepers, and entry NPCs; `water-body` for the broad First Sea; and a dock with boat berths as explicit maritime geometry. Do not build the later islands yet.

## Layout requirements
Place `npc-population` markers at quest, shop, dungeon, and raid services inside safe ground. Place `spawner-npc` emitters within beginner combat areas and a separate boss marker at the single boss location, all buffered from the settlement and dock. Place `teleporter-link` endpoints at dedicated overworld entrances and paired instanced dungeon or raid arrivals. Place player spawn in town and boat spawns along the dock, facing unobstructed sea routes.

## Layout components
Build one small Starter Island in the First Sea: a central settlement, shops and quest stands, dock and boat slips, beginner enemy clearings, and one landmark boss area. Roads connect town to combat ground and dock without crossing enemy patrol space. Surround it with a broad ocean and clear outward navigation corridors. Reserve distant sightlines and route stubs for dozens of future islands, including the named pirate, marine, biome, culture, raid, arena, and end-game destinations and later seas, but do not render those destinations in this first build. Dungeon and raid instances remain separate from the island.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: safe circulation, combat buffers, dock access, and linked instance entrances must be planned first; that plan is authoritative before isometric dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The current build is medium, focused on one compact vertical-slice island while reserving a huge future world. The theme is vibrant original anime-pirate seafaring. Use `P4` for separate dungeon and raid maps, `P3` for their linked transitions, and `CHECK` for navigable open water.
