# Scene prompt

Create a massive, seamless open-world map under perpetual night, with a large glowing pale-blue moon, dark volumetric clouds, light fog, and moonlight across the landscape. Build extremely uneven wild terrain of rolling hills, valleys, small mountains, and deep gorges, blanketed in dense swaying grass. Place 10–11 massive dark gothic castles far apart across the world, with distances long enough that horseback travel is essential and crossing on foot takes a long time. Castle interiors may be separate loaded zones; each contains complex rustic wooden rooms with aged furniture, furnished kitchens, medieval bathrooms, and secret chambers. Scatter small houses throughout the wilderness, along with high-value treasure areas guarded by tall frightening robed wizard-like creatures. Add small attractive shop huts scattered across the map, illuminated with warm light against the dark landscape.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is RPG: weapon progression, loot, bosses, vendors, exploration, and open-world combat define the game.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open` for the genuinely seamless exterior wilderness. Castle interiors are linked auxiliary maps rather than a different exterior shape. Preset is none because no bundle matches this seamless gothic wilderness plus numerous separately loaded castles without substantial contradiction.

## Config requirements

Apply `landmark-focal` to the 10–11 distant gothic castles; `terrain-relief` to rolling hills, valleys, mountains, and deep gorges; `building-interior` to each castle’s rustic rooms, kitchens, bathrooms, and secret chambers; `hazard-aggro` to high-value treasure clearings guarded by wizard bosses; and `collectible-nodes` to visible treasure-chest sites. Add scattered small houses, warm-lit shop huts, dense swaying grass, pale-blue moonlight, clouds, and fog.

## Layout requirements

Place `spawner-npc` emitters inside each guarded treasure area, set back from major travel lines. Place the functional half of `collectible-nodes` at the chest sites within those guarded zones. Keep both placements paired so every treasure site has an associated defender without spilling onto ordinary routes.

## Layout components

Build one huge exterior world with 10–11 castles distributed far enough apart to require horseback travel. Connect them through broad valleys and readable wilderness routes, with scattered houses and illuminated shop huts between destinations. Give each castle a separate complex interior zone of rustic wooden rooms, aged furniture, furnished kitchen, medieval bathroom, and secret chambers. Deep gorges, hills, and small mountains dominate the ground; open wilderness encloses the playable region. Compose the exterior around distant castle silhouettes beneath the oversized blue moon.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative so the huge seamless exterior, castle spacing, routes, and guarded zones remain coherent.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is huge. The theme is ultra-realistic gothic nocturne. Use `tiered` for extreme relief, `P3` for exterior-to-castle transitions, and `P4` because castle interiors may be separate loaded maps.
