# Scene prompt
Create a realistic 3D dragon-riding map with five distinct islands spaced across open sea and reachable by flight. Each island provides terrain for finding different dragon species, while rare, powerful alpha dragons occupy prominent remote areas of the island group.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Adventure. Exploration by dragon flight and finding a collection of species define the activity; the brief does not establish RPG stats, combat progression, or open-ended roleplay strongly enough to add another genre.

## Shape and preset
Use `volume-open-air`, with the navigable sky between five islands acting as real play space and each island providing landing surfaces and search terrain. The preset is none: Exploration assumes a contiguous ground world, while this brief is explicitly an airborne island search and does not ask for trails or secret alcoves.

## Config requirements
Use `island-cluster` for exactly five distinct landmasses separated by flyable open sea. Use `water-body` for the ocean beneath and between them. Use `npc-population` for 100 different findable dragon species distributed through terrain suited to their habitats. Add `landmark-focal` as prominent remote alpha-dragon territories that can be spotted from the air and pull riders toward the edges of the archipelago. Shape each island with visibly distinct realistic terrain rather than repeating one landform.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `npc-population` as dragon habitat and encounter markers across all five islands, covering exactly 100 distinct species in total while avoiding uniform distribution. Reserve rare alpha-dragon markers for isolated, elevated, or outermost territories with clear landing approaches and substantial separation from common habitats. Place one safe initial rider arrival on an accessible island and ensure every other island lies within a practical flight chain.

## Layout components
Build five realistic islands across open sea, each with a distinct silhouette, terrain character, habitat zones, landing clearings, and coastal boundary. Maintain broad flyable corridors between them and enough open sky for mounted turning and descent. Common dragon habitats occupy varied island terrain; alpha habitats sit on prominent remote ridges, sea stacks, or far coasts and act as visual anchors. Include no bridges or forced ground connections, because flight is the intended traversal. Compose the archipelago so all five islands read from an aerial isometric view without collapsing into one mass.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: aerial composition, island silhouettes, and realistic atmosphere lead, with the top-down projected afterward because no maze, circuit, or otherwise exact topology must be computed.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is large, driven by five flight-separated islands and habitats for 100 dragon species. The theme is realistic rugged maritime dragon wilderness. Use `CHECK` because open-air flight is volumetric and could self-occlude around tall island terrain; the ocean and island cluster reinforce the same check without adding another route.
