# Scene prompt

Build a small stylized kart racing circuit with Puma logos placed throughout the map and on trackside racing boards.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Racing. Karts complete three laps around a finite closed track.

## Shape and preset

Use `route-circuit`, a compact closed loop that returns every kart to the same start and finish area. The preset is Circuit Racing.

## Config requirements

Draw `spawn-grid` as an orderly kart launch grid beside the shared start/finish line, `barrier-guardrail` as physical track-edge protection around the loop, and `path-turnaround` as broad stylized bends that carry the course back toward its origin. Add trackside racing boards around the circuit and place Puma logos throughout the map and prominently on those boards, preserving the requested branded visual treatment.

## Layout requirements

Place the functional `spawn-grid` slots in evenly spaced rows immediately behind the start/finish line, all oriented along the first track segment. Place one `trigger-lap` detection region across the full track width at that line to count each crossing toward the stated three-lap race and detect completion after lap three. Add split detection regions only where needed to prevent shortcutting, especially on the far side of the loop and before the final return bend.

## Layout components

Build one small kart circuit enclosing a simple central infield. The route must leave the launch straight, pass through a sequence of broad readable bends, reach a far-side segment, and reconnect cleanly to the shared start/finish straight without ambiguous crossings. Line both sides with guardrails and branded racing boards, distribute additional Puma logos across the infield and perimeter scenery, and use a low open boundary that keeps the entire lap legible. Compose the circuit as a colorful stylized arena with the starting grid in the foreground.

## Render order

Use `authored_plan`: generate the closed circuit blueprint in code first, then derive the top-down and isometric images from it. A racing circuit must close without breaks or ambiguous self-crossings, and this is one of the two shapes supported by authored plans.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a small scale because the brief explicitly asks for a compact kart circuit. The theme is bright stylized motorsport with pervasive Puma branding. Apply `P6` because the closed race structure must be valid by construction; no additional modifier is forced.
