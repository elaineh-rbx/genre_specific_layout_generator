# Scene prompt

Create a 3D kart racing map with a bright toy-kart look: a colorful compact looped race circuit with a clearly marked start/finish line, simple cheerful surroundings, and continuous safety barriers or walls enclosing the course so karts cannot leave the track.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Racing. Players drive karts for a set number of laps around a finite closed track.

## Shape and preset

Use `route-circuit` as one compact loop returning to a shared start/finish line. The preset is Circuit Racing, which directly fits the lap structure, launch area, returning bends, and physical barriers.

## Config requirements

Draw `spawn-grid` as an orderly toy-kart launch grid behind the line, `barrier-guardrail` as continuous colorful safety walls enclosing both edges of the course, and `path-turnaround` as cheerful broad bends that carry the track back toward its origin. Make the start/finish line highly visible across the pavement. Keep the surroundings simple and bright, using playful shapes and colors without adding complex destinations or terrain that the brief does not request.

## Layout requirements

Place `spawn-grid` slots in aligned rows behind the start/finish line, all facing along the opening straight; no kart count is fixed. Place one `trigger-lap` detection region across the entire track width at the visible line so each crossing records a completed lap and the final required crossing ends the race. Add split detection regions around the loop where needed to ensure a kart followed the full course rather than reversing or cutting across the infield.

## Layout components

Build a compact start/finish straight, a continuous sequence of readable turns, a far-side segment, and a final bend reconnecting cleanly to the straight. Enclose the whole driving surface with uninterrupted walls or guardrails so no kart can enter the void. Put low, cheerful toy-like scenery beyond the barriers and a simple decorative infield inside the loop, preserving clear sightlines to the route. Compose the circuit from an elevated angle with the start line in the foreground and the complete loop legible.

## Render order

Use `authored_plan`: generate the closed circuit blueprint in code first, then derive top-down and isometric images. The racing loop must close exactly and have no broken or ambiguous connections.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a small scale for the requested compact circuit. The theme is bright, colorful toy-kart racing with simple cheerful scenery and bold safety walls. Apply `P6` because the lap circuit must be valid by construction; no other modifier is forced.
