# Scene prompt
Create a 3/4-view 3D tactical board-game diorama of a small modern mansion on a compact 8x8 tile grid. Present model-like mansion rooms and corridors as a readable cutaway layout, with the target and guard pieces placed on clear tile paths; guards occupy simple fixed patrol routes through the space.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Strategy because play consists of planning constrained movement around fixed enemy patrols on a board.

## Shape and preset
Use `board-grid` from the Board & Card Games preset. The scene is a literal tabletop-scale mansion diorama whose pieces act on a compact grid rather than a space avatars walk through.

## Config requirements
Apply `tile-grid` as an exactly 8-by-8 board of evenly divided, clearly readable movement spaces. Draw the small modern mansion as model-like cutaway rooms and corridors, preserving unobstructed visibility of tile paths. Include distinct miniature pieces for the player, the target, and guards, while leaving their functional positions to layout placement.

## Layout requirements
Place one target piece on a reachable mansion tile and one player start marker at a different clear tile. Place guard pieces on simple fixed patrol routes, with every patrol represented as an ordered sequence of adjacent traversable tiles that leaves at least one viable approach to the target. The exact guard count is unspecified, so use a small readable set rather than inventing a hard quantity. The 10-move allowance is a gameplay rule and does not add geometry.

## Layout components
The single small zone is an 8x8 mansion board bounded by a clean diorama base. Arrange a compact sequence of modern rooms and corridors across the grid, using low cutaway walls, door gaps, and floor changes in material to keep paths legible from three-quarter view. Tile paths connect the player start, patrol circuits, and target position without hidden spaces. Compose the mansion centrally on the board with miniature pieces visually separated from walls and furniture.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, then dress it as the requested three-quarter diorama. Grid adjacency and viable patrol paths must remain playable, but this is neither a maze nor a racing circuit, so it does not use an authored blueprint.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, set by the exact 8x8 grid and single mansion level. The theme is a clean modern espionage board-game miniature. Use `P0` with `SET`: the geometry is real, but avatars do not traverse it, so traversal and jump-gap checks are skipped.
