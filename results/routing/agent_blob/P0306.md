# Scene prompt
Create a polished, vibrant 3D anime-inspired pirate RPG map centered on one detailed Starter Island, compact enough to cross on foot in about 1–2 minutes. Arrange a coastal island with clear beach approaches, a small central settlement or quest hub containing quest-giver NPC locations, scattered treasure chests across the island, and dedicated outdoor training areas populated by bandit enemy locations. Use lush stylized terrain, ocean surrounding the island, and a clean, readable layout with paths connecting the shore, settlement, training zones, and elevated or peripheral exploration areas; leave the surrounding sea open for future modular islands.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG, with Adventure secondary. Levelled combat, quest givers, and bandit training define the RPG, while paths and peripheral discoveries make exploration meaningful.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open` as one compact contiguous Starter Island. The preset is Action RPG, but its usual hub-and-dungeon shape is replaced with `world-open` because the brief explicitly asks for one island on one surface; its sanctuary, hostile areas, and enemy emitters remain intact.

## Config requirements
Use `safezone-town` for the small central quest settlement, isolated from bandit aggression. Draw `hazard-aggro` as dedicated outdoor bandit training clearings set away from safe paths. Add `path-trail` as readable routes from beaches through the settlement to training and elevated exploration ground. Use `npc-population` for visible quest-giver positions in the hub. Include `water-body` as the open ocean around the island and `terrain-relief` as lush elevated peripheral terrain. Draw scattered treasure chests as visible interactable props.

## Layout requirements
Place `npc-population` markers for quest givers inside the central hub. Place `spawner-npc` emitters only within the outdoor training clearings, buffered from the settlement and shore arrivals. Place treasure-chest pickup markers at every drawn chest, distributed across paths, beach edges, training outskirts, and elevated peripheral discoveries. Place a player arrival at a clear beach approach facing the route into town.

## Layout components
Build one coastal island traversable in one to two minutes. Its central zone is a small quest settlement; several beach approaches occupy the shore, bandit training clearings sit beyond town, and elevated exploration pockets ring the periphery. A branching but readable trail network connects shore, hub, training zones, and high ground. Use lush grass, stylized rock rises, sandy beaches, surrounding ocean, quest-giver stands, bandit grounds, and scattered treasure chests. Keep the outer sea unobstructed for future islands. Compose the island with town as the visual anchor and all major routes legible.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the vibrant island look leads, and the authoritative top-down is projected from that image because no generated circuit, maze, or exact structural chain is required.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, driven by the stated one-to-two-minute crossing. The theme is polished modern-anime pirate adventure in saturated green, sand, blue, and gold. Use `tiered` for elevated terrain without overhang and `CHECK` for the surrounding swimmable ocean; no separate-map cost applies yet.
