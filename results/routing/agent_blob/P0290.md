# Scene prompt
Create a massive floating stone combat arena suspended in an infinite cosmic void, with all playable ground confined to the main arena and open edges for ring-outs. Surround it with smaller floating celestial platforms used only as backdrop, glowing nebulae, shifting dimensional colors, and high-tech energy pillars framing the arena. Use dramatic lighting to make the space feel boundless, hyper-cosmic, and transcendent.

# Agent decision

## Genre
Action is the sole genre because the map is a shared physical clash space built around ring-out combat.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded` as a Flat Arena: one massive, single-level stone surface contains the entire playable fight despite appearing suspended in boundless space. The preset is Battlegrounds, retaining ring-out edges and protected fighter starts while dropping line-of-sight pillars from the playable floor so the celestial framing remains backdrop.

## Config requirements
Use `hazard-kill` for the open perimeter, where crossing the stone edge becomes a ring-out into the cosmic void. Use `spawn-protected` for visually screened or set-back arrival positions that cannot immediately throw a newly arrived fighter over the edge. The smaller celestial platforms, nebulae, dimensional colors, and high-tech energy pillars are uncatalogued visual geometry outside the combat floor; they must never read as reachable platforms or interior cover.

## Layout requirements
For `spawn-protected`, place fighter starts across the safe interior of the main arena, evenly separated from one another and from the ring-out boundary. Use one start per supported fighter slot, but do not invent a slot count because none was supplied. The visible spawn treatment belongs in the image config and the functional spawn markers belong here. No pickups, enemy emitters, checkpoints, or scored objective volumes are required.

## Layout components
The build centers on one massive floating stone arena with a broad, collision-clean, single-level combat surface. Its perimeter remains continuously open and readable for ring-outs, with no railings or smaller playable ledges. High-tech energy pillars stand beyond or around the arena to frame it without obstructing combat. Smaller celestial platforms float at clearly unreachable distances as backdrop only. Glowing nebulae and shifting dimensional color fill the infinite void boundary. Compose the scene symmetrically around the main platform, keeping its complete edge visible and dominant while backdrop elements establish impossible cosmic scale.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, because the visual impact, dramatic lighting, and clear distinction between playable arena and unreachable backdrop lead the simple bounded plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large, driven by the massive tournament floor, while the playable topology remains simple. The theme is hyper-cosmic stone and luminous high technology. Use `P0`: all play occurs on one unobstructed surface, and unreachable floating scenery adds no traversal cost.
