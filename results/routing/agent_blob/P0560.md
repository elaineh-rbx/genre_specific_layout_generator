# Scene prompt

Create a 3D top-down strategy map for one playable monster civilization base hidden in a suburban backyard, viewed at tiny-creature scale. Center the layout on a Monster Heart, with clear buildable territory branching outward for monster nests and housing, resource extractors and storages, laboratories, breeding caves, spell towers, defensive walls, turrets, traps, acid pools, slime launchers, poison flowers, Venus flytraps, and a future fortress footprint. Surround the base with oversized backyard terrain and landmarks: gigantic mushrooms, tall grass, trees and tree forts, slime rivers, a sprinkler-fed water channel, garden-hose bridges, toy blocks, abandoned bicycles, broken toys, soda cans, old tools, and a sandbox forming a small desert biome. Use height changes, mud, water, cover-providing trees and grass, and open approach routes to shape the terrain. Keep this yard as a focused standalone base map; neighboring player yards are separate destinations rather than visible plots in the same scene.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Strategy, with Simulation secondary for the persistent building, extraction, and upgrade loop.

## Shape and preset

Use `terrain-open`: units move dynamically across one backyard rather than following a hard-coded lane. Match the Real-Time Strategy preset, retaining its open terrain and core/base-building options while adapting it to one defensible player base.

## Config requirements

Draw `buildzone-plot` as broad buildable territory radiating from the Monster Heart. Draw the visible `tracker-core` as that central heart. Add `terrain-relief` through modest backyard height changes without overhangs, and `water-body` as slime rivers plus the sprinkler-fed channel. Include open attack approaches, walls, turrets, traps, acid pools, slime launchers, poison flowers, Venus flytraps, nests, housing, extractors, storages, laboratories, breeding caves, spell towers, and a reserved fortress footprint. Oversized mushrooms, grass, trees, tree forts, hose bridges, toys, bicycle, cans, tools, and the sandbox establish tiny-creature scale.

## Layout requirements

Bind the functional `tracker-core` to the single Monster Heart at map center. Place `collectible-nodes` as thematic Mush, Goo, Bones, Electricity, and Scrap sites near matching backyard materials, outside the innermost defenses but reachable from build territory. No neighboring-yard placement belongs in this map.

## Layout components

Center one base core and fan buildable sectors around it. Ring these with defensive structures while preserving several open perimeter-to-core approaches. Put the sandbox desert on one edge, wet slime and sprinkler channels across another, and hose bridges at crossings. Use raised dry ground for long-range defenses, mud and water in low ground, and trees or tall grass as sight blockers. The yard boundary encloses the standalone scene. Compose it as a readable 3D top-down base plan.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, then dress the isometric view, because build sectors, approaches, resource access, and defensive sightlines must remain tactically legible.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is large for one complete backyard base. The theme is whimsical monster civilization amid colossal suburban debris. Route `tiered` for non-overhanging height changes and `CHECK` for playable water channels; no `P4` applies because other yards are deferred destinations, not maps in this build.
