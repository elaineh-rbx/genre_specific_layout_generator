# Scene prompt
Create a realistic modern tactical-shooter map set in a compact industrial complex for squad-versus-squad operations. Arrange warehouse buildings, factory interiors, loading areas, service corridors, and fenced outdoor yards into interconnected close-quarters combat zones, with doors and windows for breaches, ladders and elevated catwalks for vertical routes, and destructible barricades, furniture, cover, fences, lights, and selected walls throughout. Include open sightlines between exterior sections alongside tighter interior rooms and corridors, with space for military trucks, armored vehicles, utility vehicles, helicopters, or boats where the industrial site meets suitable access routes. Use a detailed, weather-responsive atmosphere with day or night lighting, rain, wind, and storm conditions affecting visibility.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Shooter: this is a realistic competitive tactical shooter for coordinated squads, not an arcade deathmatch.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-network` for several exterior and interior routes joining the two squad sides through the complex. The preset is Tactical Shooter, substituting `lane-network` for its sequential breach shape because the prompt explicitly requires interconnected zones, while retaining breachable structures and tactical cover.

## Config requirements
Use `building-interior` for warehouses and factory buildings entered from fenced yards through breachable doors and windows. Use `cover-los` for industrial machinery, barriers, and full-height sightline breaks. Use `path-loop` so rooms and service corridors reconnect through multiple exits rather than dead-ending. Use `cover-elevated` for ladders and catwalk firing positions, `destructible-cluster` for selected barricades, furniture, fences, lights, walls, and cover, and `spawn-teambase` for two screened squad staging areas. Draw vehicle-access areas for trucks, armored vehicles, utility vehicles, a helicopter pad, and a waterside loading berth for boats.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-teambase` as exactly two squad staging zones on opposite protected edges, sized collectively for the stated maximum of 64 players and shielded from opening fire. No AI spawners are needed. The first map has no single fixed objective volume because the brief names several possible tactical modes without selecting one.

## Layout components
Build a dense industrial compound of warehouse interiors, factory rooms, loading aprons, fenced yards, and service corridors. Interconnect close-quarters rooms through doors, windows, and alternate passages; connect exterior yards with wider roads and open firing lanes. Catwalks cross above selected factory floors and ladders connect them. Place breakable barricade clusters at breach points. Reserve road bays for military and utility vehicles, one armored staging pad, one helicopter landing area, and a dock edge where an industrial canal meets the site. Hard fences and industrial walls bound the complex. Compose it as a compact footprint with enough staging and route width to support up to 64 players.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative because interior/exterior route connectivity and squad flanks must remain valid. Dress the isometric from that plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge because the map must support up to 64 players despite its dense footprint. The theme is realistic modern industry under variable rain, wind, storm, and day/night light. Use `P3` for exterior-to-interior play, `P2` for overhanging catwalks, and `CHECK` for the playable dock water volume.
