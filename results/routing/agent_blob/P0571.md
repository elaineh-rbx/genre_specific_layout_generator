# Scene prompt

Create a bright, natural 3D forest exploration map with a forested roadside running through dense trees. Place street lights along the road, with ropes attached between or hanging from the street lights, and leave walkable ground around the roadside and through the surrounding woods.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Adventure, specifically simple freeform exploration. No secondary genre is needed because the brief adds no survival, combat, collection, or platforming loop.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open`, one contiguous forest-and-roadside map that players may explore in any order. The preset is none: Exploration would add a focal landmark and hidden alcoves that the author did not request, while the open-world shape and requested trail remain appropriate.

## Config requirements

Use `path-trail` for the forested road and the walkable ground branching beside it into the woods. Draw a bright natural forest with dense tree groups, roadside street lights, and ropes visibly attached between lamps or hanging from them. Keep enough clear ground around each lamp and among the trees for walking.

## Layout requirements

Nothing must be placed after segmentation. The prompt asks for visible environmental geometry only and does not establish collectibles, checkpoints, quest trackers, teleports, triggers, or spawn markers.

## Layout components

Build one continuous forest zone organized around a road crossing the map. Line the roadside at intervals with street lights, using ropes both between selected neighboring lights and hanging from others. Extend traversable clearings and informal footpaths from the roadside into dense surrounding woods without turning them into a directed course. Use gently varied natural ground but no major cliffs or overhangs. Dense tree bands form the soft outer boundary. Compose the road as the main visual spine while preserving readable openings into the forest.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render the bright forest atmosphere, lamp rhythm, ropes, and road composition first, then derive the top-down plan because exact topology is not structure-critical.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is medium, allowing a meaningful roadside segment and surrounding walkable woodland. The theme is bright natural forest with slightly unusual rope-strung street lights. Use `P0`; the map is one continuous grounded surface with no interiors, overhangs, separate maps, or topology that must be constructed algorithmically.
