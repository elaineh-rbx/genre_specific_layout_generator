# Scene prompt
Create a small, minimalist 3D ASMR studio interior with soft pastel lighting and a calm, uncluttered composition. A single central work station holds relaxing breakable materials and objects—soap, kinetic sand, slime, fruit, and soft blocks—arranged neatly on a clean tabletop or display surface, with open space around it for the player. Use gentle visual effects and subtle relaxing particles around the station; no physical shop area.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation because the repeatable cutting, crushing, and breaking loop is the game activity. No secondary genre is needed.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single`, taken from outside Simulation’s usual shape list, because the entire small map is one enclosed ASMR studio and the player never needs an exterior transition. The preset is none: Physics Sim contributes the destructible idea, but its broader hazard-and-circulation bundle does not fit this single calm workstation.

## Config requirements
Use `physics-rig` for the neatly arranged breakable soap, kinetic sand, slime, fruit, and soft blocks whose physical reactions are the attraction. Use `trigger-task` for the visible central tabletop workstation where the repeatable ASMR interaction occurs. Soft particles should remain localized around this station rather than filling the room.

## Layout requirements
The `trigger-task` functional placement is one interaction zone aligned to the central tabletop and its object display, reachable from the surrounding standing space. The five material groups should each have a distinct usable area on that one station. No physical shop placement is required because the shop is explicitly screen-only.

## Layout components
Build one compact pastel studio room with a central clean worktable, open player circulation on all sides, and five neatly separated object groups: soap, kinetic sand, slime, fruit, and soft blocks. Keep walls and floor minimal, the boundary fully enclosed, and the frame centered on the workstation with soft illumination and subtle particles.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down view; visual mood and the clear workstation composition lead, while topology is simple.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by one room and one station. The theme is minimalist pastel ASMR with soft light and calm visual effects. Use `P0`; one single-level interior has no overhang, outside-to-inside transition, separate map, or validity constraint.
