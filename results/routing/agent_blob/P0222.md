# Scene prompt
Create a 3D roleplay map set in a small, modern suburban town with a friendly, slightly stylized Roblox look. Arrange about a dozen large, move-in-ready homes along neighborhood streets, with several shops grouped in a compact town commercial area; connect the residential and shopping zones with clear roads and sidewalks.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, canonical genre `roleplay-avatar-sim`, because players move into finished homes and socialize across a suburban town and its shops.

## Shape and preset
Use `settlement-claimable`, a prebuilt town of large houses that players claim rather than build. The preset is Life, matching claimable homes, vehicle roads, residential-versus-commercial zoning, and enterable interiors.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for clear two-way neighborhood roads connecting every home and shop area, with sidewalks alongside. Use `district-zoned` to separate the quiet residential streets from the compact commercial cluster, `building-interior` for move-in-ready homes and shops that players can enter, and `settlement-density` for a complete small suburban town. Carry the stated quantity of about a dozen large homes into the settlement request; retain “several” for the shops without inventing a numeric count.

## Layout requirements
Nothing canonical needs placement after segmentation. The brief does not specify task triggers, NPC populations, teleporter shortcuts, pickups, or spawn markers; house claiming is a system attached to the visible prebuilt lots and interiors.

## Layout components
Build a residential zone containing about twelve large detached homes distributed along a few connected neighborhood streets. Each home should occupy a generous individual lot, have a clear front path from the sidewalk, and provide an enterable, move-in-ready interior. Place several shops together in one compact commercial area rather than scattering them among the homes. Roads and continuous sidewalks must directly connect the two zones. Use mostly level suburban terrain, lawns, street trees, simple parking, and friendly slightly stylized façades. Bound the town at its outer roads with landscaped edges and compose the scene so the residential neighborhood occupies most of the frame while the commercial cluster remains plainly identifiable.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the town’s friendly visual identity first, then derive the top-down plan, because its ordinary street network does not need constructed topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, driven by about a dozen large homes plus several shops. The theme is modern suburban with a friendly, lightly stylized Roblox finish. Use `P3` for exterior-to-interior traversal in houses and shops.
