# Scene prompt

Create a bright, cartoon-style 3D third-person climbing map with a clear vertical ascent route. Arrange jumpable platforms, ledges, and elevated sections so that higher areas form progressively taller tiers and visually signal greater rewards. Include a physical shop area beside or near the starting zone, represented as a colorful 3D shop structure for training and energy upgrades.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Simulation: money, energy training, and upgrades determine how high the player can jump, so the rising number is the progression. Obby & Platformer is secondary because that progression is expressed through jumping across physical platforms.

## Shape and preset

Use `tier-ladder`, a readable vertical sequence of increasingly high training tiers, each representing greater jump capability and reward. The preset is Stat Grinder.

## Config requirements

Draw `station-training` as physical jump-training pads and apparatus near the starting tier. Use `gate-progression` as height-marked barriers or elevated ledges that visually separate successive jump-power tiers. Draw `social-hub` as a colorful three-dimensional shop beside the start, with friendly counters and upgrade displays rather than a screen-only menu. Use `obstacle-jump` for the platforms and ledges forming the ascent. Make the higher tiers visibly more valuable through stronger color, coin motifs, and increasing elevation.

## Layout requirements

Place the functional `station-training` triggers densely around the start so players can train without searching. Place the `gate-progression` thresholds at each transition to a taller tier, aligned with the ledge or platform height that tests the upgraded jump. Inside the physical `social-hub`, place distinct purchase spots for energy upgrades, including one clearly identified option that grants 50 energy and room for stronger options beyond it. Coins, energy consumption, button-triggered jumping, and upgrade arithmetic are mechanics; only their physical stations and spatial thresholds belong here.

## Layout components

The map begins with a broad safe plaza containing the colorful shop and clustered training pads. From it, a clear vertical ascent of bright block platforms, ledges, and elevated tier decks rises in a line or open spiral. Each tier has a stable landing area, a visible height gate to the next tier, and enough separation for the increasing jump requirement to read clearly. Keep the climb open-air with a simple reset floor below and no overhanging hidden routes. Compose the scene from a low three-quarter angle that keeps the shop prominent while showing the reward gradient toward the highest tier.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render the visual ascent and shop-led progression first, then derive the top-down plan because the tier sequence is simple and its look communicates the game.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is medium for one focused training plaza and vertical tier ladder. The theme is bright, friendly cartoon progression with colorful shop models and coin accents. Use `P0`; the single connected tier layout requires no separate map, interior transition, overhang capture, or authored topology.
