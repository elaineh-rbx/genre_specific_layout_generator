# Scene prompt

Create a 3D obby map in a futuristic abstract sci-fi setting: a compact vertical tower of clean floating platform sections suspended in a dark void, with routes visibly crossing above and below one another. Arrange glowing doors and floor buttons along the climb, with open gaps and clear sightlines between stacked sections so translucent, distinctly glowing ghostly player echoes can be seen retracing paths throughout the tower. Design the tower as an extendable sequence of procedurally generated stages.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Obby & Platformer, with Puzzle secondary because past-run echoes operate physical buttons and gates to enable progress.

## Shape and preset

Use `course-tower`, a compact vertical course whose floating routes stack and cross overhead. The Tower Obby preset is the closest fit, retaining its climb structure while adding explicit puzzle inputs and gates.

## Config requirements

Draw `path-track` as a connected ascending chain of floating sci-fi platforms, with `obstacle-jump` gaps and `obstacle-climb` vertical transitions. Use `button-solve` for glowing floor pressure plates and `gate-progression` for luminous doors opened by earlier echo actions. Include translucent, distinctly glowing player echoes along visible replay lines; later stage imagery may show 10–50 echoes without treating them as static map geometry. Keep open sightlines between stacked sections.

## Layout requirements

Place the functional half of each `button-solve` directly on the route where an echo can hold or cross it while the current player approaches its linked door. Pair every button with one clearly readable `gate-progression` barrier farther along the climb. Dynamic echo recording, playback, adaptive generation, and difficulty changes are gameplay systems rather than placed layout objects. Each generated stage begins at a stable landing platform and ends at a stable handoff platform from which another stage can extend.

## Layout components

Build a compact tower segment suspended in a dark void. A stable lower platform begins an upward chain of clean floating ledges, gap jumps, ramps, and small vertical climbs. Several routes pass visibly above and below one another, with glowing doors dividing progression and floor buttons positioned on earlier ledges. Preserve open gaps so replaying echoes remain visible across levels. There is no baseplate; the void is the boundary and fall field. Compose the tower in a three-quarter cutaway view that exposes the whole vertical weave and leaves its top visibly extendable.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: generate the authoritative multi-level plan first because the jump chain and every button-to-door route must remain completable, then dress it isometrically.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is medium for each compact generated tower stage. The theme is abstract futuristic sci-fi with clean geometry, luminous controls, translucent ghosts, and a dark void. Use `P6` for valid obby construction and `P2` because playable routes overhang one another.
