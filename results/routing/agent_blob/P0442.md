# Scene prompt
Create a 3D fairy ASMR simulator map as a short, replayable nature loop that takes a couple of minutes to walk. A small winding path passes through natural scenery and leads through one compact fairy village, with magical ambient sound-effect locations placed along the route; the walk itself is the experience.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Entertainment. Although the source calls it a simulator and references a stat-grinder game, the completed brief explicitly removes collecting, unlocking, stat progression, and a destination; walking and listening are the entire experience. It is therefore an environment showcase rather than Simulation.

## Shape and preset
Use `route-guided` with the Showcase preset. The shape is a short winding route that loops back to its beginning while sequencing visitors through nature and the fairy village.

## Config requirements
Use `landmark-focal` for the compact fairy village as the route’s principal visual destination. Use `vignette-photo` for several composed natural moments along the path, such as glowing flowers, tiny bridges, and mushroom clusters. Draw the visible arrival framing for `spawn-first-reveal` at the loop entrance. Apply `settlement-density` specifically as one compact miniature fairy village, not scattered full-size buildings.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawn-first-reveal` arrival marker at the loop entrance, oriented toward the first magical nature vignette and a glimpse of the distant village. Place magical ambient sound emitters at distinct route moments: near the opening grove, around the village center, beside a small bridge, and near the return stretch. Site them far enough apart that each soundscape has a readable local zone. No collectibles, gates, checkpoints, or destination trigger should be placed.

## Layout components
Build one small winding footpath that leaves a sheltered start, passes through luminous woodland scenery, enters a compact fairy village, crosses a tiny decorative bridge, and returns to the start. The village should contain clustered miniature homes, a little central green, lanterns, and oversized flowers. Use soft grassy ground, mossy stones, mushrooms, glowing plants, and shallow decorative stream edges. Dense enchanted foliage forms the outer boundary. Compose the route as a succession of intimate ASMR vignettes rather than a goal-driven course.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: visual atmosphere and vignette composition lead, with the top-down derived from that look.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by a couple-minute loop and one village. The theme is gentle enchanted fairy nature with luminous storybook detail. Use `P0`; the loop occupies one continuous walkable surface without required overhangs or transitions.
