Keep circulation simple and replayable, and do not add collectibles, unlock gates, progression barriers, or a finish area.

Magical sound effects are audio mechanics and do not add image geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly one `spawn-first-reveal` SpawnZone at the loop's start, oriented so the first view shows the nature path leading toward the fairy village. No collectible, gate, finish, unlock trigger, or other post-segmentation placement is needed.

## Layout components
The build contains one short looping nature path, soft woodland and clearing zones, exactly one small fairy village, and one curated starting area. The path passes through the village and returns to its origin without a terminal destination or dead-end branch. Natural edges enclose the compact walk, and the isometric composition centres the village within the loop.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the peaceful fairy-village composition and natural atmosphere lead, while the simple walking loop can be projected into top-down afterward.

The theme is peaceful enchanted nature and fairy-village fantasy. Modifier `P0` applies because the loop is one continuous surface with no overhang, interior transition, separate map, or validity-constrained competitive route.
