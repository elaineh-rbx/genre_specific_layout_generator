# Scene prompt
Create a 3D tower-defense map as a bright, colorful outdoor garden with a small, neat planting-plot layout and simple cozy farm props. A single winding path runs through the garden toward a central defended base area, with farm plots and open spaces arranged alongside the path for tower placement.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Strategy. RNG determines which towers players receive, but the spatial activity is a conventional tower-defense lane.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-actor-track`: a single continuous winding garden path carries every enemy from the outer entrance to the central defended base. The preset is Tower Defense, retaining its lane, build ground, core, and wave origin while omitting the unrequested bottleneck.

## Config requirements
`buildzone-grid` draws neat planting-plot-shaped tower placement areas on both sides of the path, with clear soil rectangles and open pads that read as deployable ground. `tracker-core` draws a cozy fortified garden structure at the center as the defended base. Add simple farm props—small fences, watering cans, wheelbarrows, baskets, flowers, and compact sheds—without obstructing the route or placement plots.

## Layout requirements
Place one `tracker-core` objective marker on the central base and one `spawner-npc` at the outer mouth of the winding path. Keep the enemy route continuous and one-way, and preserve walkable access from the player arrival point to every tower-placement plot. RNG rolls and tower inventory are mechanics and add no placement geometry.

## Layout components
Build one small bounded outdoor garden. A winding path enters at the perimeter, curves between orderly soil plots, and terminates at the central base. Farm plots and open deployment pads flank each bend, while cozy props cluster at plot corners so sightlines remain clear. Use level grass and soil with colorful flower borders and a low white fence as the boundary. Compose the map from an elevated three-quarter angle that keeps the whole short route and central objective readable.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the winding enemy route must be connected and playable before the isometric garden dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, reflecting the requested easy first map and single short route. The theme is bright, colorful, cozy planting garden. Use `P6` because the enemy lane must be valid by construction.
