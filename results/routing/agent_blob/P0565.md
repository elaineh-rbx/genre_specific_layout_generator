# Scene prompt

Create a 3D survival island map with a separate lobby spawn area connected by a walkable path to a game-options area containing five distinct selection points for 1-, 2-, 3-, 4-, and 5-player sessions. The playable island is small and tropical, with sandy beaches around its edge, palm trees and rocks scattered across the shore and inland, and a simple forest interior.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Survival, specifically cooperative hunger-and-weather survival without hostile animals, PvP, or a round timer.

## Shape and preset

Use `hub-portals`, taken from outside Survival’s usual shapes: the lobby and its game-options area dispatch groups into a separate playable island session. No preset remains recognisable because Resource Survival would add base-building ground the brief never requests.

## Config requirements

Draw a compact safe lobby, a walkable path to one options area, and exactly five clearly separated selection points labeled for one, two, three, four, and five players. Draw `water-body` as the tropical sea fully surrounding the playable island. The island itself has a complete sandy beach ring, scattered palms and rocks, and a simple forest interior. Do not add wildlife threats, defensive structures, or PvP cover.

## Layout requirements

Place one lobby player spawn in the safe area. Put five distinct group-selection trigger volumes on the five visible selection points, each bound to its stated capacity: 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 players. Link every selection point to the island-session arrival area, preserving the chosen capacity for scripting. Place `collectible-nodes` for basic food, fresh water, and weather-survival materials across beach and forest zones, since hunger and weather are the stated threats.

## Layout components

Build two separate spaces. The lobby map contains a spawn zone, one broad walking path, and the five-pad options area with room for small groups to queue without overlap. The survival map is one small tropical island with beach around the entire edge, a sparse rocky shore, denser forest at center, and short paths between resource pockets. Open sea forms the island boundary. Compose both plans so selection capacities and the island’s beach-to-forest structure read immediately.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first as authoritative, then dress the isometric views, because five non-overlapping queue points and reliable island resource routes depend on clear topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is medium overall: one compact lobby plus one small island instance. The theme is bright tropical survival. Route `P4` for separate lobby and session maps, plus `CHECK` for the surrounding water.
