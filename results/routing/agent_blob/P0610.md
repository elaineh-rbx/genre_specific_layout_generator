# Scene prompt
Create a realistic Indonesian railway map with a single point-to-point rail line running roughly 10,000 studs end to end. Place the CC201 locomotive on the route, with roads alongside portions of the track, roadside power lines, level crossings, and moving cars on the adjacent roads. Arrange the large map as a continuous rail corridor with varied surrounding terrain and settlements along the line.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation: the player freely operates a CC201 locomotive, with no race, required objective, or competitive finish.

## Shape and preset
Use `route-point-to-point`, taken from outside Simulation’s usual shape list because the prompt explicitly requires one terminating railway line rather than a freely branching world. The preset is none; the available train-operation preset assumes the vehicle itself is the walkable map and does not describe this 10,000-stud rail corridor.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for roads that parallel selected stretches of railway and connect settlements. Use `settlement-density` for Indonesian towns distributed along the line, and `terrain-relief` for varied surrounding landforms. The image must show the continuous railway, roadside power lines, level-crossing infrastructure, the CC201 locomotive, and cars on adjacent roads.

## Layout requirements
Place one primary CC201 train spawn at an endpoint with a clear forward orientation along the rails. Place moving-car route markers and vehicle emitters on the parallel roads, stopping their paths before each level crossing so road traffic can interact safely with the railway. No score, checkpoint, or winner zone is needed because this is free-roam.

## Layout components
Build one continuous railway approximately 10,000 studs from endpoint to endpoint. Give both ends adequate track approach and turnaround or reset staging, with a sequence of rural and settled stretches between them. Roads run beside portions of the railway, crossing it at several clearly marked level crossings. Power poles follow the roadside at consistent spacing. Place small and medium settlements along the corridor, separated by varied open terrain. Keep the rail grade practical and avoid disconnected track segments. Frame the entire corridor as a long readable diagonal or gentle sequence of bends.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the point-to-point rail line must be continuously connected and road crossings must align, while this is not a maze or circuit eligible for `authored_plan`.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is huge, driven by the stated 10,000-stud line. The theme is realistic Indonesian railway countryside. Use `P6` because the route must be valid by construction, plus `tiered` for varied relief without overhanging surfaces.
