# Scene prompt
Create a beautiful, highly detailed animal roleplay world in a soft stylised visual style, with ten separate teleport-accessed biome maps: City, Forest, Jungle, Desert, Beach, Plains, Farm, Mountain, Snow place, and Rain forest. Each biome is a self-contained, immersive environment designed for animal-scale exploration and roleplay, with distinct terrain, vegetation, natural landmarks, and appropriate built structures where relevant; the City includes streets and buildings, while the Farm includes cultivated fields and farm structures. Keep the biomes separate rather than physically connected.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, specifically open-ended animal roleplay and exploration.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-chaptered`: ten genuinely separate biome maps reached by teleport, not one connected surface. This comes from outside the genre’s usual shapes and replaces the Animal Sim preset’s `wilderness-open`, while retaining the Animal Sim preset because its animal-scale wilderness roleplay remains the clear concept.

## Config requirements
Use `teleporter-link` for visually readable arrival landmarks in every biome; `landmark-focal` for a distinct natural or built orientation anchor in each map; `path-street` for animal-scale circulation, becoming city streets, farm tracks, and natural trails elsewhere; `settlement-density` for the City’s built-up street-and-building fabric; `terrain-relief` for the Mountain and other biome-specific landforms; and `water-body` for the Beach shoreline. Teleport links are a both-stream requirement.

## Layout requirements
Place ten `teleporter-link` arrival endpoints, exactly one in a safe central entry area of each named biome, with each endpoint paired to the shared teleport destination system. No other invisible gameplay markers are required by the brief.

## Layout components
Build ten self-contained maps: City, Forest, Jungle, Desert, Beach, Plains, Farm, Mountain, Snow place, and Rain forest. Each needs a central arrival area, readable animal-scale paths, distinct ground and vegetation, and a memorable orientation landmark. The City uses streets among dense exterior buildings; the Farm uses cultivated field blocks, tracks, and farm structures; the Beach meets a real shoreline; the Mountain carries the strongest elevation. Each map has a closed scenic boundary suitable to its biome, and each composition frames its arrival point against its landmark.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render each biome’s styled isometric first and derive its top-down plan from that authoritative image because no single-map topology is structurally constrained.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is huge because it contains ten detailed maps. The theme is soft-stylised, beautiful, detailed nature at animal scale. Use `P4` because the biomes are separate maps, `tiered` for mountain relief without required overhangs, and `CHECK` for the Beach water play envelope.
