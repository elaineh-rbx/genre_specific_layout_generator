## Enriched image prompt
Create ten separate, richly detailed animal-roleplay maps in a soft stylised visual style: City, Forest, Jungle, Desert, Beach, Plains, Farm, Mountain, Snow Place, and Rain Forest. Each destination is its own finite environment rather than part of one connected terrain. Give each map a clear central arrival clearing with surrounding room for animal avatars to gather and explore. Distinguish them through their stated landforms and ground cover: urban blocks for City; woodland for Forest; dense tropical growth for Jungle and wetter layered vegetation for Rain Forest; dunes for Desert; sand meeting open water at Beach; broad grassland for Plains; fields and farm structures for Farm; strong rocky elevation for Mountain; and snow-covered terrain for Snow Place. Do not include closet, animal-selection, settings, quality, or teleport UI in the physical scenes.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim (`roleplay-avatar-sim`), specifically animal roleplay.

## Shape and preset
Use a described shape rather than a catalogue ID: ten separate finite biome maps selected through UI. Set `axis-enclosure` to `exterior`, `axis-verticality` mostly to `single-surface` with tiered Mountain terrain, `axis-zone-count` to `multi-zone`, `axis-structure` to `dressed`, and `axis-play-space` to `grounded-surface`. Reject `world-chaptered` because these are not ordered story chapters, and reject `hub-portals` because the author specifies a button rather than a physical portal hub. The preset is none; Animal Sim’s single-wilderness shelter bundle does not express ten separately loaded destinations.

## Config requirements
Use `settlement-density` for the City’s built-up urban ground, `water-body` for the sea edge at Beach, and `terrain-relief` for the Mountain’s strong natural elevation. All ten named environments are required scene-specific image content. Clothing, customization controls, and interface buttons are not geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place one free-text SpawnZone arrival marker in the central clearing of each of the ten maps and associate it with the corresponding Teleport-button destination. No physical `teleporter-link` is drawn or placed because travel is explicitly menu-driven. No NPC population is inferred; the animals are player avatars.

## Layout components
There are exactly ten separately loaded maps, one for each named destination. Each has one central arrival space and outward exploration ground, with City blocks, forest trees, jungle growth, desert dunes, a beach and sea, plains, farm fields and buildings, mountain slopes, snowy terrain, or rain-forest vegetation as applicable. Boundaries are finite and naturally screened. Each destination receives its own complete elevated composition.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render each destination’s distinctive soft-stylised environment first, then derive its uncomplicated top-down plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium per independently rendered destination, preserving roleplay-scale vegetation, structures, and arrival spaces; the build contains ten such maps. The theme is soft stylised, beautiful, and highly detailed. Use `P4` for separate maps, `tiered` for Mountain relief, and `CHECK` for the Beach water edge.
