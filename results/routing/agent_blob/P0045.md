# Scene prompt

Create two separate 3D battle-royale spaces for up to 100 players: a distinct starting island and a much larger main match island reached by a flying battle bus. The starting island is a compact waterfront staging area. The main island is surrounded by water and filled with original, varied colorful cartoon-style battle-royale buildings, with weapon chests and ammunition boxes distributed through the built areas. Include a visible storm-zone boundary enclosing the playable island.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Shooter, specifically a Zero Build battle royale.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-staged`, taken from outside Shooter’s usual set, because a compact starting island is fully separate from the main match island. Keep the Battle Royale preset with that shape substitution.

## Config requirements

Use `building-interior` for original enterable buildings across the match island, `settlement-density` for varied built-up combat areas, and `water-body` for open water surrounding both islands. Draw one flying battle bus as the transition focal prop. Keep buildings colorful and original rather than reproducing referenced assets.

## Layout requirements

Place a staging spawn region on the starting island with capacity for up to 100 players. Site one bus departure trigger at its waterfront launch area and connect it to the main island’s aerial drop sequence. Distribute `collectible-loot` as weapon chests and ammunition boxes throughout the built areas, including accessible interiors. Place `boundary-shrinking` around the main playable island and align its visible storm effect to that boundary.

## Layout components

Build two maps: a compact waterfront starting island with an open 100-player staging pad and bus launch edge, and a much larger main island enclosed by sea. The main island should contain multiple distinct clusters of varied cartoon buildings, open ground between clusters, readable roads or paths, and a continuous coastline. Include one battle bus, weapon chests, ammunition boxes, and the visible storm perimeter. Frame each map separately, with the main island readable as one complete battlefield.

## Render order

Render in `isometric` order because visual identity leads and the open battlefield does not require computed topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is huge, driven by the 100-player capacity and large match island. The theme is colorful original cartoon battle royale. Use `P4` for separate islands, `P3` for exterior-to-building traversal, and `CHECK` for surrounding water as volumetric play-space.
