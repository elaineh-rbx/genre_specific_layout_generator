- [author] scale — Q: Is the lab a couple of rooms or a whole facility to explore? A: Make it a small lab with a couple of connected rooms to explore.
- [agent_inferred] room count — Q: How many lab rooms should “a couple” mean? A: Use exactly two connected ruined lab rooms beyond the starting chamber.
- [agent_inferred] theme — Q: What visual register should the destroyed lab use? A: Use a dim abandoned industrial laboratory with restrained, non-graphic horror.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a small story-driven map entirely inside a ruined laboratory. Begin in one sealed spawn chamber facing a breakable damaged door or wall. Beyond it, connect exactly two abandoned lab rooms in sequence, filled with visible structural damage, scattered equipment, debris, and several non-graphic fallen-body props indicating that something destroyed the facility. Keep one clear route through both rooms to a prominent final escape door at the outer end of the lab. Use dim industrial lighting and a restrained horror atmosphere without adding an active monster.

## Genre
The genre is Adventure.

## Shape and preset
Use `rooms-sequence`, a Zone (Room Sequence) taken from outside Adventure's usual set because the story moves through one chamber and exactly two connected lab rooms in order. The preset is none; Adventure's chaptered Story bundle would incorrectly split this small facility into separate maps.

## Config requirements
Use `destructible-cluster` for the breakable chamber barrier and damaged lab assemblies, `path-trail` for the single directed interior route, `spawn-first-reveal` from Entertainment for the chamber start facing the breakout point, and `gate-escape` from Survival for the final structural exit. Debris, ruined equipment, and non-graphic fallen bodies remain direct visual requirements.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawn-first-reveal` SpawnZone inside the starting chamber, oriented toward the damaged breakout barrier. Place one `gate-escape` Gate at the far end of the second lab room as the story-ending escape trigger. Narrative cutscenes and escape completion are mechanics.

## Layout components
Build three sequential spaces: Starting Chamber, Ruined Lab Room One, and Ruined Lab Room Two. Short damaged thresholds connect them, with no branches or alternate bypasses. The final escape door terminates the route. Distribute debris, equipment, structural damage, and fallen-body props without blocking the only walkable path.

## Render order
Use `topdown` because the chamber-to-two-room escape sequence and unobstructed route must be fixed before ruined-lab dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use small scale because the compact rooms, debris, and bodies must remain legible. The theme is dim abandoned industrial laboratory horror. `P0` is sufficient because the requested map is one connected interior sequence with no separate exterior scene or stacked floor.
