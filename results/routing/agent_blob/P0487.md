# Scene prompt
Create a 3D story-game map set in a small abandoned laboratory facility. The character begins in a sealed chamber that opens into a couple of connected, damaged lab rooms leading toward an escape route. Show destruction throughout the facility, with broken equipment and debris, and dead people lying in the rooms; the final path leads out of the lab.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure: this is a short, story-driven escape journey through an explorable facility.

## Shape and preset
Use `rooms-sequence`, taken from outside Adventure's usual shape list, because the map is a small ordered chain of enclosed rooms and corridors. The preset is none: Story suggests separate chapters and Guided Trail suggests an outdoor-style route, while this brief specifically calls for one compact laboratory.

## Config requirements
Use `gate-progression` for the sealed starting chamber opening onto the route. Use `building-interior` for the damaged laboratory rooms and their final inside-to-outside escape threshold. Broken lab equipment, scattered debris, structural damage, and dead bodies must be visibly distributed through the rooms.

## Layout requirements
Place one player spawn marker inside the sealed chamber, clear of the opening door. Place one escape objective trigger at the final exterior threshold after the last lab room. No collectibles, NPC spawners, or intermediate checkpoints are required for this short route.

## Layout components
Build one small sealed spawn chamber, then a couple of connected damaged laboratory rooms arranged in a clear sequence, ending at one escape exit. Short corridors and breached doorways connect every room without branches. Floors are level industrial concrete; broken benches, smashed equipment, debris piles, and several dead bodies create obstruction while preserving a readable walking path. The facility walls bound the route until the final opening outdoors. Frame the composition lengthwise so the chamber-to-exit progression reads immediately.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, then dress the isometric view from it, because the ordered room connections and reachable escape route must remain valid.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by the requested chamber and couple of connected rooms. The theme is abandoned industrial horror with severe laboratory destruction. Use `P3` because play crosses from the laboratory interior to the exterior escape point.
