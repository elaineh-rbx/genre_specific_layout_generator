# Scene prompt
Create a focused urban BASE jumping map centered on modern city skyscrapers. A tallest tower a few hundred studs high provides the main jump point, rising above surrounding city rooftops and streets with open airspace below for a dramatic descent and landing area.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Use Simulation as the dominant genre: the author explicitly asks for a BASE jumping simulator centered on realistic operation and aerodynamic control rather than a race.

## Shape and preset
Choose `volume-open-air`, outside Simulation’s usual shape set, because the playable space is the descent volume between a skyscraper launch and the ground. It comes from the Aircraft Operation preset, adapted from aircraft to a human aerial descent and stripped of its unrelated task, shop, and progression-gate options.

## Config requirements
Use `settlement-density` for a compact modern city cluster of skyscrapers, surrounding rooftops, and visible streets. The tallest launch tower must stand a few hundred studs high above a clearly readable open descent corridor and ground landing area. No enterable interiors are requested.

## Layout requirements
Place one free-text player launch spawn on the roof edge of the tallest tower, facing the unobstructed descent corridor. Place one free-text landing target volume on open ground below, offset enough from tower walls and adjacent rooftops to permit a controlled approach. Place a free-text failure volume across hard-impact street and rooftop surfaces outside the safe landing target.

## Layout components
Build one dominant modern skyscraper a few hundred studs tall, a small supporting cluster of lower city towers, visible roof planes, and streets at ground level. Preserve a broad, obstacle-free air column from the main roof edge to a generous landing area below. Connect the city visually with a simple street grid, while keeping buildings outside the descent corridor. The city edge forms the bounded backdrop. Compose the launch tower high in frame with the landing area clearly legible beneath it and the vertical drop emphasized.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the dramatic vertical city composition first, then derive the authoritative top-down because exact route topology is not the game constraint.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large, driven by the tower’s few-hundred-stud height despite the focused footprint. The theme is realistic contemporary urban BASE jumping. Route `CHECK` because players traverse a volumetric open-air descent; verify that surrounding towers do not make the play volume self-occluding.
