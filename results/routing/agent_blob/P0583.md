# Scene prompt
Create a compact 3D layout with three or four clean modern towers, each marked by distinct colored lines across its exterior. Players enter the buildings to reach slide entrances inside, with large slides emerging through the tower walls and descending into the surrounding open area; arrange the towers close together as a small playable block.

# Agent decision
## Genre
This is `No Genre`: the author wants a playable slide environment but specifies no race, obstacle challenge, scoring, rounds, or other genre-defining game loop.

## Shape and preset
No genre shape is assigned. Set `axis-enclosure` to transition because players cross from the open block into tower interiors. Set `axis-verticality` to stacked because elevated indoor slide entrances and descending slides occupy levels above the ground. Zone count stays single, structure stays dressed, and play space stays grounded-surface. The preset is none; the build is defined by its towers and slides rather than a generic exploration bundle.

## Config requirements
Use `building-interior` for three or four enterable modern towers whose interiors provide access to elevated slide entrances. Add freeform image geometry for distinct colored line patterns across every exterior and for large slides piercing the tower walls and descending into the shared open area. Keep each tower’s line color and slide visually distinguishable.

## Layout requirements
Nothing invisible needs placement after segmentation. The brief does not establish checkpoints, triggers, scoring volumes, pickups, spawners, or teleporters; the slide structures themselves are drawn geometry.

## Layout components
Build one compact block containing three or four towers clustered around a shared open landing court. Give each tower a ground-level entrance, an interior route up to a clearly readable elevated slide mouth, and one large slide that passes through its exterior wall before descending into the court. Separate slide landing areas enough to avoid collisions while keeping short walking connections back to every entrance. Use flat paving at ground level and contain the block with low urban edges. Compose the towers as a tight group while preserving all slide silhouettes.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the vertical tower-and-slide relationships first and treat that image as authoritative, then derive the top-down view.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by three or four close towers. The theme is clean modern architecture with bold colored accents. Use `P3` for linked exterior and interior spaces and `P2` because slides and elevated interior surfaces overhang the ground.
