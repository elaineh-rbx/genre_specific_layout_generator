## Enriched image prompt
Create one compact three-floor mall interior that players can cross in about 20 seconds. Start in a ground-floor entrance vestibule with a paper lying beside the inner door. Arrange two small restaurants around one shared dining area on the ground floor. Place the furniture-store anchor on the second floor, with bed displays that can serve as the group’s sleep area. Keep the third floor clearly accessible by a central stair and give it prominent windows overlooking the interior and outside darkness. Use a readable central atrium so all floors stay oriented.

Build clean normal and progressively paranormal dressing states on the same layout: later states add unexplained dirty dishes, displaced objects, messes, and subtle architectural oddities without changing the core circulation. Story and Endless modes reuse this mall; loading screens, lobby creation, player count, buttons, dialogue choices, day text, and mode selection are UI or mechanics rather than separate physical spaces.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure because story-driven exploration of the mall leads the experience, with Survival secondary for the repeating paranormal Endless mode.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single`, a Zone (Single Interior), because the whole game map is one enclosed mall. No Adventure or Survival preset fits a compact three-floor story mall without inventing exits, monsters, or chapters as separate maps, so the preset is none.

## Config requirements
Use the visible side of `trigger-task` for the second-floor bed displays and two restaurant counters. Use `zone-graphics` for localized paranormal dressing clusters that can intensify without replacing the base map. Add the entrance paper as scene-specific image geometry with no canonical option ID.

## Layout requirements
Place `trigger-task` as three TriggerZones: one sleep zone among the furniture-store beds and one eating zone at each of two restaurant counters. Place one free-text TriggerZone with no canonical ID on the entrance paper for reading its rules.

## Layout components
The mall has three stacked floors, one entrance vestibule, one central atrium, two restaurants and shared dining on floor one, one furniture store on floor two, prominent third-floor windows, and stairs linking every level. Normal and paranormal states share this exact plan.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: make all three floor plans and stair alignments authoritative, then dress normal and paranormal isometric variants.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is medium because storefront, paper, counters, beds, and stairs must remain legible in a 20-second mall. The theme is ordinary enclosed retail space turning into restrained paranormal horror. Use `P2` because three walkable floors overhang one another.
