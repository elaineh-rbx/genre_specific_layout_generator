# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map of a compact two-anchor shopping mall with an ominous paranormal atmosphere, small enough to cross in about 20 seconds. Place the main entrance at one end, leading into a central interior with clearly accessible stairs or escalators to the second and third floors. Put a furniture store on the second floor as a prominent overnight area, and group restaurants and food counters in a dining area with countertops and tables. Include upper-floor windows on the third level, especially a visible window area, and scatter small environmental details such as a paper on the floor near the entrance and dirty dishes left on restaurant counters. The same mall supports both a week-long story visit and an endless repeat setting.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Survival is dominant, with Adventure secondary. The same paranormal mall supports a finite story visit and an endless mode that continues while the lobby survives, with exploration carrying the story.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single`, one enclosed mall that is the entire map. No preset fits: the brief asks for ominous repetition and environmental incidents but names no pursuing monster, hostile origin, escape gate, or collectible loop.

## Config requirements
No canonical genre option is required. Draw the requested three-floor mall directly: a main entrance, central circulation, stairs or escalators, second-floor furniture store, restaurant dining area, third-floor windows, entrance paper, and dirty dishes. The mall must remain visually identical for story and endless modes; event scripting changes, not the base layout.

## Layout requirements
Place one uncatalogued paper interaction marker on the floor just inside the entrance, aligned to the visible rules sheet. Place one sleep interaction volume in the second-floor furniture store, against a usable bed display. Place food interaction points along the restaurant counters. Day progression, lobby size, mode selection, paranormal events, and survival timing remain outside layout.

## Layout components
Build one compact two-anchor mall on exactly three stacked floors, crossable end to end in about 20 seconds. Put the entrance at one end of the ground floor and organize the interior around a short central concourse. Make stairs or escalators clearly visible from that concourse and connect all three levels. Give the second-floor furniture store a prominent open frontage and bed-display area. Group restaurants, counters, and dining tables into one readable food zone. Reserve a conspicuous third-floor window area as a focused ominous landmark. Scatter one rules paper near the entrance and dirty dishes on food counters. The exterior is not playable.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first, with an authoritative plan for each floor, then dress the isometric image; stacked circulation must align vertically and remain obvious.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale because the footprint is compact but spans three floors. The theme is ordinary retail space under subtle paranormal dread. Route with `P2` because upper floors overhang lower playable surfaces.
