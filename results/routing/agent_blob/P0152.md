# Scene prompt

Create a 3D space-survival map set in open space around a dedicated, expandable base platform. A small connected shop platform holds four distinct NPC shop stations. Surround the base with a compact reachable field of drifting real salvage items and craftable materials, with more valuable items floating slightly farther away as the space opens outward; include scattered platforms and player-built structures that can serve as movement anchors. Keep the environment focused on exposed space traversal and resource gathering, with no hostile enemy areas.

# Agent decision

## Genre

Survival is dominant because limited air and gathering resources to endure through day 100 define the run. Simulation is secondary because players expand a persistent base and upgrade crafting, air, solar, and movement systems.

## Shape and preset

Use `volume-open-air`, taken from outside Survival’s usual set, because open space itself is the traversed volume and discrete platforms provide anchor surfaces. Start from the Resource Survival preset, substitute this airspace shape for `world-biomes`, and retain its resource-node and player-build-ground bundle.

## Config requirements

Use `buildzone-plot` for the dedicated expandable base platform and its reserved construction footprint; `resource-shared` for the common drifting salvage field; the visible half of `trigger-task` for crafting tables handling air, solar, and special builds; and the visible half of `npc-population` for four distinct shopkeepers and their four differentiated stations on the small connected platform. Draw the central base, its shop-platform connection, solar-build areas, scattered grapple anchors, and only recognizable salvage and materials rather than generic filler shapes.

## Layout requirements

Place `collectible-nodes` throughout two radial bands: common salvage and craftable materials in the compact inner field reachable with starting air, and more valuable real items in a slightly farther outer field reachable after upgrades. Preserve item-type variety and avoid inaccessible placements. Place the functional half of `trigger-task` at crafting tables on the central base, and the functional half of `npc-population` as exactly four NPC shop positions, one per station on the connected shop platform. Place one player arrival marker on the safe central base and reserve expansion sockets around its perimeter.

## Layout components

The expandable base platform is the central safe zone and largest solid surface. A short rigid connector leads to one smaller shop platform divided into four stations. Around them, an inner spherical salvage field holds recognizable drifting machinery, tanks, panels, crates of parts, and craftable materials. A looser outer band holds rarer valuable items. Scattered small platforms and player-built anchor structures punctuate both bands for grapple movement. Open space is the boundary, with increasing distance and air cost replacing walls. Compose the scene radially around the base while keeping vertical offsets legible rather than heavily stacked.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: the placement plan is rendered first and remains authoritative so distance bands, grapple anchors, shops, and expansion clearances stay playable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is medium, balancing a compact starting reach against a modest upgraded outer band. The theme is exposed improvised orbital survival with practical industrial salvage, solar equipment, and no hostile zones. Use `CHECK` because players traverse a 3D open-space volume; verify that vertical offsets do not self-occlude.
