# Scene prompt
Create a 3D find-and-search map set in a colorful cartoon city. Build three small connected areas on one walkable map, with streets linking rooftops, alleys, and small shops; place distinctive decorative pizza props in visible and tucked-away search spots throughout the areas.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure: this is a find-and-search scavenger hunt across an explorable city, with no separate genre loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `traversal-city`, a connected city designed to be searched at both street and rooftop levels, with streets available as the lower fallback route. The preset is Scavenger Hunt, retaining collectible objectives and tucked-away discovery spots while omitting the unrequested quest board.

## Config requirements
Use `settlement-density` for a compact colorful cartoon city made of three small districts. Use `path-trail` as the connected street-and-alley circulation between all three. Use `alcove-secret` for tucked-away pizza search niches in alleys and rooftop corners. Use `building-interior` for small shops that players can enter and search, linked clearly from the streets.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` as distinctive pizza finds across all three districts, mixing visible street-level nodes with tucked-away alley, rooftop, and shop nodes. No pizza count was given, so do not invent one. Every node must be reachable, with rooftop nodes set on broad safe surfaces and interior nodes assigned to the linked shop plans.

## Layout components
Build exactly three small city districts on one continuous map. Connect them with a primary street spine, secondary alleys, and clear stairs or ramps from street level to searchable rooftops. Give each district a street frontage, an alley pocket, rooftop search space, and at least one small enterable shop, while varying color and prop dressing enough to make the districts recognizable. Keep the rooftops directly above their buildings and preserve streets below as alternate circulation. Enclose the city with stylized perimeter buildings rather than an abrupt wall. Compose the overview so all three districts and their street links read clearly, while the isometric view reveals upper and lower search layers.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the layered cartoon-city character first, then derive the top-down plan, because the map is exploratory rather than a maze, circuit, or mandatory jump chain.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, comprising exactly three small connected districts. The theme is colorful cartoon pizza city. Use `P2` because rooftops overhang playable streets, and `P3` because play crosses from outdoor streets into shop interiors.
