## Enriched image prompt
Create two separate compact school-themed spaces. The main space is a bright, cute classroom for animal students, with simple rows of desks, a subject board at the front, and clear arrival positions. The failure space is a dark transformed school corridor zone arranged as one interconnected loop around a central block, with localized recesses and sightline breaks but no dead ends. Give the dark creature a separate origin away from the player's entry, and provide a clear return point that leads back to the classroom after the chase.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Party & Casual because subject questions form a repeated quiz activity; Survival is secondary because wrong answers launch a creature chase.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-staged`, because the bright classroom and dark chase area are isolated maps used at different moments. Preset is none: the standard Quiz preset cannot express the separate chase zone, and the Minigame preset would import unrelated leaderboard and spectator features.

## Config requirements
`social-hub` draws the compact classroom as the recurring gathering and quiz space, bent to rows of desks and a front subject board. Secondary Survival contributes `cover-los`, drawing localized recesses and block corners in the dark school loop where the player can break the creature's sightline. Shared `teleporter-link` draws the transition entry into the isolated chase map and its return point back to the classroom. The dark corridor transformation is scene-specific geometry rather than a second generic party stage.

## Layout requirements
Place classroom player starts facing the subject board. Place one paired `teleporter-link` from the wrong-answer transition into the dark zone and from the completed end of the chase back to the classroom. Place one `spawner-npc` creature origin on navigable ground out of sight of the dark-zone entry, and one `spawn-protected` player entry opposite it. No physical quiz-answer tiles are required.

## Layout components
The `space-staged` container has exactly two separate maps. Classroom is an `interior-single` bright room with desks, subject board, and arrivals. Dark Chase Zone is a `warren-looping` school-corridor map with a central block, sightline recesses, player entry, creature origin, and return point. The classroom walls and dark-zone outer wall bound their maps independently. Render each space as its own authoritative plan.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the dark chase loop must have zero dead ends, and the classroom-to-chase-to-classroom transitions must be explicit before either visual state is dressed.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is small for each map, inferred from the repeating classroom-and-chase loop. The theme contrasts a bright cute animal school with a dark threatening school transformation. `P4` is required because the classroom and chase zone are separate maps, and `P6` is required because the dark chase loop must be valid by construction.
