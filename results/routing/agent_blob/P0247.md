# Scene prompt
Create a 3D school game map for cute animal students, beginning in a classroom with desks arranged toward a teaching area. Connect the classroom to a contrasting dark escape zone: a shadowy route with space for a dark creature to pursue the player, leading back to the classroom.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Party & Casual because answering school-subject questions drives the round. Survival is secondary because a wrong answer starts an actor-driven chase.

## Shape and preset
Use `warren-looping`, the Looping Warren, taken from outside Party & Casual’s usual shapes. For this scene it is a dark chase route that leaves the classroom and reconnects to it without dead ends where the creature could unfairly corner the player. The preset is none: neither the Quiz preset nor a Survival preset remains recognizable after honoring this classroom-to-chase loop.

## Config requirements
The classroom is a bright, cute-animal school interior with student desks facing one teaching area. Use `gate-escape` for the clearly readable final doorway from the dark route back into the classroom. Keep the chase zone shadowy and strongly contrasted from the welcoming classroom. No extra lockers, hiding cutouts, or answer-tile geometry are added because the brief does not request them.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawner-npc` hostile origin deep enough inside the dark route that the pursued player has an initial lead, with its patrol ground covering the whole loop. Place the functional half of `gate-escape` across the return doorway so crossing it ends the chase and returns the survivor to classroom play. Place player starts among the classroom desks, facing the teaching area.

## Layout components
Build two named zones: a compact classroom and a larger dark chase warren. Arrange rows of desks toward a teacher-facing wall or board. Connect the classroom exit into a branching but fully looping shadow route, then reconnect that route through one conspicuous return door. Use school architecture that becomes distorted and dim in the chase section. Bound the entire map as one interior and compose the classroom as the visual anchor with darkness receding behind its exit.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: render the top-down first and make it authoritative because every chase branch must reconnect and the return route must remain navigable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale, balancing a readable classroom against enough chase distance. The theme shifts from cute animal school to stylized dark pursuit. `P6` is required because the looping route must have valid, dead-end-free topology.
