## Enriched image prompt
Build a small 3D suburban family home as a fully breakable exterior assembly in a compact flat yard. Leave clear walkable and operating space on every side so the house can be destroyed from outside, and do not generate an enterable interior. Construct the exterior, roof, walls, and visible fixtures as separable pieces that can collapse until the house is flattened, with a simple suburban palette and an unobtrusive yard boundary.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation, canonical slug `simulation`, because the physical destruction and collapse reaction of the house is the game's central activity.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded`, taken from outside Simulation's usual shape list, for one compact exterior yard containing the entire activity. Match the Physics Sim preset with this bounded shape substituted for its open-world default; retain the physical destruction rig and circulation around it while dropping the unrequested environmental hazard.

## Config requirements
Use `physics-rig` for the house as a breakable multi-part physical assembly whose collapse is the entertainment. Use `path-circulation` for clear exterior movement around all four sides. Do not use `building-interior`, because the author explicitly limits destruction to the outside.

## Layout requirements
Nothing must be placed after segmentation; the prompt provides no spawn, pickup, trigger, checkpoint, or scoring volume. The timed flattening goal is gameplay logic rather than a spatial marker.

## Layout components
Create one small flat yard zone, one small suburban family house at its center, clear operating ground on every side, and a simple outer boundary. Split the roof, walls, and exterior structural pieces into a readable destructible assembly while showing no enterable rooms. Keep the house dominant in frame and preserve enough surrounding space for external destruction.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the house's exterior form and destructible part relationships first, then derive the top-down view; the isometric image is authoritative because the compact yard has no topology-critical path.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use small scale, driven by one small house and the prop-level detail needed for a readable collapse. The theme is an ordinary suburban family home and yard. Use `P0`; the build is a single flat exterior space with no inside transition, overhang-dependent walkable layer, or separate map.
