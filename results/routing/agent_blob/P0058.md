# Scene prompt

Create a 3D destruction game map centered on a small suburban family home, viewed and attacked from the exterior only. Place the house on a simple residential lot with clear open space around it for explosions and an unobstructed view of the structure.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Action because the activity is fast physical destruction: the player attacks a house and tries to flatten it as quickly as possible.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-bounded` as one compact residential demolition lot with the house at its center and the player circulating outside it. The preset is none; no Action preset describes a single exterior-only destructible building without combatants, waves, or arena hazards.

## Config requirements

Use `destructible-cluster` for one small suburban family home assembled from breakable multi-part walls, roof sections, windows, porch pieces, and structural connections so it can collapse under explosions. Draw a simple residential lot with a level lawn or paved ground and generous clear space on every side. Keep the house sealed and facade-only: no rooms, enterable doors, interior circulation, or outside-to-inside transition should be generated.

## Layout requirements

The layout stage needs no scene-specific triggers, collectibles, enemy emitters, or scoring volumes placed after segmentation. The destruction timing and damage logic are gameplay systems rather than spatial markers; the ordinary player start may use the pipeline baseline at the outer edge of the clear lot.

## Layout components

Build one small bounded residential lot with exactly one family home centered on it. Surround the house with an even open blast apron wide enough to view and attack every facade without obstruction. Use a low curb, fence, or simple lot edge as the outer boundary, but leave the playable side of that boundary uncluttered. The house should read as a complete suburban exterior with a pitched roof and modest porch while remaining structurally divisible for destruction. Avoid trees, sheds, neighboring homes, vehicles, and decorative clutter that would hide the target. Compose the house as the dominant central focal object with its full footprint visible.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative, then derive the top-down image; the visible destructible construction and clear exterior presentation lead, while the lot topology is trivial.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, driven by one house and its surrounding blast space. The theme is clean suburban domestic architecture staged for demolition. Use `P0` because all play remains on one exterior ground surface and the house is never entered.
