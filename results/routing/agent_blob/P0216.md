## Enriched image prompt

Name the houses Emberowl, in crimson and gold with an owl-over-flame emblem; Tidehart, in deep blue and silver with a wave beneath a stag emblem; and Mosswyrm, in emerald and bronze with a small coiled dragon around a leaf emblem. Give each quarter clear matching banners, insignias, and dorm-room entrances. Keep the few key buildings close enough that students can walk independently from the courtyard into their scheduled activity.

## Genre

## Shape and preset

## Layout requirements
Place `trigger-task` TriggerZones at each potion workstation and at the broom lesson's launch and return positions. Class timing, house assignment, currency, room upgrades, and egg purchases are mechanics; do not turn them into extra layout volumes.

## Layout components

The courtyard connects directly to all other zones and includes the small shop. The classroom and each house quarter have enterable interiors; every quarter includes identifiable dorm-room doors. The broom lawn opens into a clear flight volume without crossing pedestrian roofs or the courtyard.

## Render order
Use `topdown` because campus circulation, interior entrances, and separation of broom flight from pedestrian space must be established before magical-academy dressing.
