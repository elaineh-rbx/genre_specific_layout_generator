# Scene prompt
Create a bright, cartoony 3D exploration map sized for about a five-minute full sweep, with 10 brainrot collectibles distributed across the map in distinct reachable locations.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure: the activity is a map-wide search for a complete collectible set, with no combat, progression stats, or timed race to introduce another genre.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, one contiguous walkable map whose locations can be searched in any order. The preset is Scavenger Hunt. Keep its collectible objective but omit hidden discovery alcoves and a physical quest board because neither was requested.

## Config requirements
No additional image-config option is required. The render should establish a bright, cartoony environment with ten visually distinct, reachable destination settings, but it should not draw the collectible placements themselves.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` after segmentation: exactly 10 brainrot collectibles, one at each distinct destination. Distribute them across the full map footprint, keep every node reachable from the walkable surface, avoid clustering, and reserve one final node at the far side of the sweep so completing the set uses the whole map.

## Layout components
Build one medium open exploration zone sized for roughly five minutes to sweep end to end, divided visually into ten recognizable destination pockets without hard gates or separate maps. Connect the pockets through continuous walkable ground with multiple readable approaches so players may search in any order. Use simple cartoony terrain and bold color changes or prop groupings to distinguish each destination. Enclose the outer edge with a clear scenic boundary while keeping the interior open. Frame the whole footprint clearly in the overview, with all ten destination pockets legible and none hidden behind overhangs.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the bright cartoony look first, then derive the authoritative top-down plan from it, because this open search map does not depend on a computed maze, circuit, or fragile traversal sequence.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, driven by the requested five-minute full sweep and 10 distributed objectives. The theme is bright, playful, and cartoony. Use `P0`; one contiguous surface with no interiors, overhangs, separate maps, or structurally constrained topology adds no pipeline modifier.
