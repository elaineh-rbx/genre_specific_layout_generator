# Scene prompt
Create a 3D digital, glitchy circus hub with a central circus interior where players arrive, surrounded by a digital lake; a theme park sits across the water on the opposite side. Include a visible void bordering or opening beyond the hub. Separate travel routes from the circus lead to distinct adventure locations: a spooky area, a bright fun area, and an action-focused area, each retaining the surreal digital circus visual style while having its own atmosphere. Provide a headset-themed arrival or transformation space near the circus entrance, sized for varied player forms that remain neither tiny nor giant.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure because players leave a central hub for separate goal-driven places where they find an objective or survive a challenge.

## Shape and preset
Use the single `hub-portals` shape from outside Adventure’s usual set: the circus hub sends players to three genuinely separate adventure maps. The closest preset is Quest Hub, with its shape replaced to honor the separated destinations and its unrequested quest board and progression gates omitted.

## Config requirements
Use `building-interior` for the circus interior connected to the exterior lakefront. Apply `water-body` to the digital lake. Draw `teleporter-link` as three visibly distinct glitchy travel gateways at the circus, each visually keyed to its spooky, bright-fun, or action destination. Render the visible void as `hazard-kill`, a digital abyss framing the hub boundary. Include the headset-themed arrival chamber near the entrance and the theme park across the lake.

## Layout requirements
Place both ends of three `teleporter-link` pairs: one hub gateway and one arrival point in each adventure map. Place player spawn markers in the headset transformation space, with clearance for varied medium-scale forms. Give each destination one clearly sited objective or completion trigger at its far focal area; the exact gameplay rule may differ, but every adventure needs a spatial endpoint.

## Layout components
The main map contains the circus interior, entrance transformation space, lakefront exterior, theme park across the water, three gateway bays, and a void-edge boundary. The three separate destination maps are a spooky digital environment, a bright playful environment, and an action arena. Maintain readable routes from circus entrance to lake, park, and gateways. Compose the hub around the circus as the dominant center, with the lake and park forming the opposite visual anchor.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, then dress the isometric, because the hub-to-three-destination connectivity must remain unambiguous.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge across four separate maps. Theme is surreal glitchy digital circus with destination-specific moods. Use `P4` for separate maps, `P3` for the circus inside-to-outside transition, and `CHECK` for the lake.
