# Scene prompt
Create a complete Roblox-style low-poly Aura Tower: a sealed vertical tower of 20 stacked floors. Each floor is a compact 40x40-stud enclosed stone chamber with four walls, a ceiling, and one exit door leading upward. Every chamber has a dark mystical aura theme with purple lighting, glowing particles, dark fog, and a giant floating hand-shaped aura centered in the room. Arrange physical hazards across the stone floor, including lava sections, spike traps, and removable platforms; make the hand larger on every fifth floor. Keep the tower fully enclosed, with sparse checkpoint areas at selected floors.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Action, with Obby & Platformer secondary: defeating the giant hand gates each room, while platform hazards complicate movement.

## Shape and preset
Use `rooms-sequence` from the Hack & Slash preset, expressed as 20 vertically stacked combat chambers connected in strict order. Keep its locked-room structure and hostile placement, omit unrequested destructible cover, and add the tower hazards.

## Config requirements
`arena-lockin` gives every chamber one upward exit door that remains closed during its fight. `hazard-kill` draws lava and spike zones across each stone floor. `obstacle-timing` draws removable platforms that vanish on a three-second cycle. `checkpoint-respawn` draws sparse safe checkpoint pads at selected floor entrances. Each room contains one giant floating hand aura, with visibly larger hands on floors 5, 10, 15, and 20.

## Layout requirements
Place 20 `spawner-npc` markers, one at the exact center of each chamber, for one hand aura per floor. Place sparse `checkpoint-respawn` markers at selected floor-entry safe pads, with death returning to the current floor’s start. Associate each room’s locked door with that room’s central hostile marker.

## Layout components
Build 20 enclosed 40x40-stud stone chambers in a single vertical stack. Every floor has four walls, a ceiling, a central hand position, distributed lava, spikes, removable platforms, a protected entry pad, and one exit door feeding a short stair or shaft to the next chamber. Floors 5, 10, 15, and 20 reserve more central clearance for larger hands. The tower has no outdoor play space; its exterior is a solid low-poly shell. Frame a cutaway isometric composition that reveals the repeating stacked rooms while preserving their enclosure.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish each hazard-safe entry, central combat footprint, exit, and floor-to-floor connection first so all 20 rooms remain traversable, then dress the isometric cutaway.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large because 20 chambers of 40x40 studs stack vertically. The theme is dark low-poly mysticism with purple light, glowing particles, fog, and stone. `P2` is required because floors and ceilings overhang lower chambers and a single top-down would hide them.
