# Scene prompt
Create a small decorative tower made of simple blocks, with no interior or climbing route. The tower is 9 wall sections high: three red walls at the base, three orange walls in the middle, and three yellow walls at the top.

# Agent decision

This is `No Genre`: the request is for a decorative object to look at, with no activity, objective, or game loop.

## Shape and preset
No genre shape is assigned. Keep the routing axes at exterior, single-surface, single-zone, dressed structure, and grounded-surface defaults. The nine vertically stacked wall sections describe the object’s construction, not stacked traversable play surfaces. The preset is none because the explorable and social presets would introduce unrequested circulation, gathering space, or landmarks beyond the tower itself.

## Config requirements
The image config contains one freeform subject: a small tower made entirely from simple block wall sections, with exactly nine sections arranged as three red at the bottom, three orange in the middle, and three yellow at the top. It must have no doorway, interior, stairs, ladder, platforms, or climbing route. Add no scenery or decorative props that compete with the color-banded construction.

## Layout requirements

Keep the silhouette simple and readable, with clean horizontal boundaries between the three color bands. The surrounding space is empty and only large enough to frame the full tower. Compose the camera to show its complete height and block construction clearly.

Use `isometric`: render the authoritative three-dimensional tower first, then derive its uncomplicated top-down footprint.

Scale is small. The theme is plain geometric blocks with bold red-orange-yellow bands. Use `P0` with `SET`: the geometry is real, but nobody walks through or on it, so traversal and jump-gap checks are skipped.
