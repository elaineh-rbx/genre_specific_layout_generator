# Scene prompt

Create a 3D game map set in a small modern city, with streets and cars distributed around the playable area. Arrange city buildings along the streets with accessible rooftops and a few elevated walkways, creating upper-level hiding and exploration spaces as well as ground-level routes between buildings.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Action, with Party & Casual secondary because twelve players receive asymmetric fighter, giant, and shapeshifter roles and hiding is central to the round.

## Shape and preset

Use `traversal-city`, taken from Action’s usual shapes instead of the Open World Action preset’s generic stacked arena. Keep the Open World Action preset because its cover and chokepoint features remain recognizable, but substitute the city-crossing shape to honor the accessible rooftops and walkways.

## Config requirements

Use `cover-los` as city buildings, parked cars, rooftop structures, and street corners that let shapeshifters hide and fighters disengage. Use `choke-bottleneck` for a few narrow elevated walkways and street gaps connecting larger movement areas. Use `settlement-density` as a small modern city of compact blocks rather than isolated buildings. Draw cars throughout the streets as throwable props, and keep roofs visibly accessible.

## Layout requirements

Place twelve `spawn-protected` player starts across screened street and rooftop positions, outside immediate lines of sight. Place coin pickups throughout the whole map, divided between ground routes, rooftop routes, and walkway approaches; the stated 50 is a new-player carrying limit, not a requested world-placement count. No geometric tutorial area is required because the requested tutorial is a gameplay flow rather than a space.

## Layout components

Build a small connected city with several street blocks, intersections, parked cars, and buildings arranged around ground-level loops. Every playable building needs a clear rooftop route reached by exterior stairs, ramps, or ladders, while a few elevated walkways bridge selected roofs and cross above streets. Preserve multiple ways around each block and provide hiding pockets without dead-ending exploration. Enclose the map with sealed outer facades. Frame both street and rooftop networks clearly.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, then dress the isometric view from it; the linked ground and rooftop movement network must remain coherent and playable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is medium, sized for twelve players in a small city. The theme is modern urban superpowered combat with transformable street clutter. Use `P2` because rooftops and walkways overhang playable streets.
