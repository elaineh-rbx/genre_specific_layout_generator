## Enriched image prompt
Create a true 3D first-person wave-survival battlefield a few hundred studs across, explorable on foot. Place one defended player pocket near the centre and feed a few continuous vehicle-width roads toward it from different outer edges, using road junctions and narrowing approaches to keep incoming waves readable. Protect the central pocket with solid road barriers and cover while preserving clear firing views down each approach. Keep the surrounding world dark, creepy, and minimally dressed so the unique detailed enemy vehicles remain the focus. All enemies are three-dimensional undead-themed cars, tanks, or other vehicles driving through the world; do not include humanoid zombies, walking characters, flat sprites, or GUI enemies.

## Genre
Shooter is dominant because first-person ranged wave combat and firing approaches organize the map. Survival is secondary because the player holds out against repeated enemy waves.

Use `cover-los` for solid barriers around the defended pocket without blocking every down-road firing view. Use `spawn-protected` for the visible central arrival enclosure. Use shared `path-road-vehicle` for continuous vehicle-width enemy roads; it routes to `P6` here because each road is a required wave course rather than an ordinary street.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawn-protected` SpawnZone inside the central defended pocket, screened from the road mouths. Place `spawner-npc` Spawners at the outer mouth of each approach road, oriented inward so every wave follows a readable route toward the player. These emit only unique, detailed creepy undead vehicle models such as cars and tanks, never humanoid or walking enemies.

## Layout components
The central zone is one compact defended pocket with a protected player start, cover ring, and clear firing gaps. A few roads begin at separated perimeter mouths, pass through distinct chokepoints, and converge on the pocket without crossing or terminating early. The outer boundary lies a few hundred studs across and keeps all routes within an on-foot first-person exploration scale. Reserve full vehicle clearance along every road and keep non-road dressing sparse.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish the continuous approach-road network before dressing the isometric view, because every spawned enemy vehicle must have a valid route to the defended pocket.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, fixed by the defended pocket a few hundred studs across and first-person foot exploration. The theme is dark 3D undead-vehicle horror. Use `P6` because the enemy vehicle roads must be continuous and valid by construction.
