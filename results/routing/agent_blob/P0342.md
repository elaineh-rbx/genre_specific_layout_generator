# Scene prompt
Create a true 3D first-person wave-survival map set in an explorable defended pocket a few hundred studs across. Place the player’s defensive position near the center, with a few distinct roads funneling inward from the surrounding edges for approaching creepy undead-themed vehicle enemies; leave the ground and roadside areas walkable for on-foot exploration. The scene is fully volumetric and viewed from within the world, with no top-down, 2D sprite, or screen-based presentation.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Use Shooter as dominant and Survival as secondary. First-person shooting against enemy waves defines the activity, while enduring the undead-vehicle horde supplies the survival structure.

## Shape and preset
Choose `lane-network`: a few roads begin at separated outer edges and channel hostile vehicle traffic toward the central defense. Use the PvE Shooter preset, retaining its lane shape, hostile origins, and funneling chokepoints while dropping unrequested enterable buildings.

## Config requirements
Use `choke-bottleneck` for the road approaches where the few lanes narrow toward the central defensive pocket. Keep the roadside ground walkable for first-person exploration and preserve clear views of incoming vehicles. The enemy asset brief is a varied set of unique, detailed creepy undead cars or tanks; never depict humanoid zombies, walking characters, sprites, or flat GUI targets.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` origins at the outer endpoint of each inbound road, all oriented toward the central defense and connected by vehicle-width navigable pavement. Associate each origin with undead vehicle actors only. Place one player arrival within the central defensive position, facing the road fan, while leaving safe on-foot clearance around it. No escape gate is needed because the activity is wave survival in place.

## Layout components
Build one defended pocket a few hundred studs across with a central open defensive zone and a few distinct vehicle-width roads radiating inward from different map edges. Add walkable shoulders and connected roadside ground so the player can explore on foot instead of remaining fixed. Keep intersections broad enough for creepy cars and tanks to turn without clipping. Use low roadside scenery that does not conceal the incoming lanes, and close the outer boundary beyond the spawn endpoints. Compose the central defense as the visual anchor with every road approach readable.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: draw the road plan first and make it authoritative, then dress the isometric scene, because every enemy road must connect cleanly from its edge origin to the central defense.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by the stated few-hundred-stud defended pocket. The theme is fully 3D creepy undead-vehicle survival. Route `P0`; the walkable play-space is grounded, single-surface, continuous, and exterior, despite the scene’s volumetric camera presentation.
