# Scene prompt
Create one small PvP sniper arena with a modern military shooter look, inspired by a compact suburban test-town layout: two opposing houses at either end, a central street and open sightlines between them, with low cover and accessible interiors framing the battlefield. Exclude the 2D home screen from the 3D map layout.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Shooter: this is a sniper-only PvP arena. The 2D home screen is explicitly outside the spatial build.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-network` for a central street and flanking routes linking two opposite ends of one compact arena. The preset is Team Deathmatch, with its opposing bases and cover retained and enterable houses added from the prompt.

## Config requirements
Use `spawn-teambase` to draw protected opposing start areas associated with the two end houses. Use `cover-los` for sparse low street cover and taller blockers that create deliberate sniper sightline breaks without making the street unreadable. Use `choke-bottleneck` where side routes narrow beside the houses. Use `building-interior` for exactly two accessible suburban military-test houses entered from the exterior, each with useful windows and more than one route back outside.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-teambase` as two team spawn zones, one behind each end house, fully screened from the opposing sniper line. No targets, weapon pickups, objectives, or NPC emitters are requested.

## Layout components
Place two opposing houses at the short ends of the battlefield and run one broad central street between them. Add narrow side yards and perimeter passages as flank routes, reconnecting near each house. Keep the street open enough for long sniper engagements while using low concrete barriers, parked military-test props, and a few tall blockers to interrupt uninterrupted end-to-end shots. Each house has a compact accessible interior, windows overlooking the street, and exterior doorways connected to the side routes. Enclose the small arena with suburban fencing and hard map-edge structures. Compose it as a readable bilateral confrontation with the central street dominant.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative so both interiors, exterior routes, protected spawns, and sniper sightlines connect fairly. Build the isometric from that plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, matching the single compact arena. The theme is a clean modern military suburban test town. Use `P3` because play crosses between the street and both house interiors; no additional modifier is forced.
