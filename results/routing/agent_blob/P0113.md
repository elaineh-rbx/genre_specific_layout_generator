# Scene prompt
Create a 3D military-themed FPS arena enclosed by huge hollow square outer walls. Inside, build a tight, compact combat space with clear interior routes and cover arranged for fast firefights, while the tall square perimeter walls dominate the background.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Shooter, with no secondary genre. The same compact arena must support free-for-all, team deathmatch, and capture the flag.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-network`, arranging multiple clear combat routes between opposite ends inside the square enclosure. Start from the Capture the Flag preset, but drop its subterranean flanker tunnel because the brief requests a compact interior arena and does not require underground or overhanging play.

## Config requirements
Use `capture-zone` for exactly two visible flag stands, `spawn-teambase` for two opposing military base areas, `cover-los` for evenly distributed waist-high barriers and full-height blockers, and `choke-bottleneck` where routes converge around central contested ground. Add `spawn-protected` as screened individual entry pockets around the perimeter for free-for-all mode. The huge hollow square wall is the dominant visual boundary. Weapons, gun slots, matchmaking, and timers are gameplay systems rather than layout geometry and should not be drawn as map components.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly two `capture-zone` volumes, one deep within each opposing base. Place exactly two `spawn-teambase` markers at opposite ends, shielded from direct fire and suitable for team deathmatch or capture the flag. Place several `spawn-protected` free-for-all markers in separated perimeter pockets, each screened from immediate sightlines and facing a viable lane. Keep the same cover and route network fair across all three modes.

## Layout components
Build one compact square combat zone inside four huge, tall, hollow perimeter walls. Put opposing military base pads at east and west ends, a central contested yard, and side-route pockets along the north and south edges. Connect them with a direct middle lane and two reconnecting flank lanes, using concrete barricades, sandbags, crates, short walls, and solid utility structures as cover. Keep all routes on one ground surface and leave the towering outer walls unbroken as the enclosure. Frame the arena from a raised diagonal angle so the square boundary dominates while lanes, bases, and central cover remain readable.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: mode-compatible lane circulation, protected spawns, and balanced access to both flag zones must be resolved before authoritative isometric military dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small despite the visually huge walls, because the playable interior is explicitly tight and compact. The theme is clean military training-ground architecture. Use `P0`: all play is on one bounded level with no overhang, interior transition, or separate map.
