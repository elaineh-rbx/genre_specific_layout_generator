# Scene prompt
Create a 3D endless colourful parkour tower that rises through small repeating vertical segments, each about 100 studs tall, generating continuously above the player. Stack increasingly difficult climbing sections upward with wall-hop routes, staircases that vanish after being stepped on, and hazardous kill-brick obstacles; provide platforms or landing areas along the ascent for retry points. Emphasize the tall vertical composition and an uninterrupted climb disappearing into the distance above.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Obby & Platformer: player-controlled parkour and climbing are the entire activity.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-tower` from the Tower Obby preset, interpreted as an endlessly repeated vertical tower rather than a finite summit course. Retain the preset’s sequential track, timed obstacles, and climbing obstacles, drop its finite `winner-zone`, and add the explicitly requested hazards and retry pads.

## Config requirements
`path-track` forms the uninterrupted upward chain through repeating 100-stud segments. `obstacle-climb` supplies wall-hop routes and other vertical climbing transitions. `obstacle-timing` supplies staircases that vanish after being stepped on. `hazard-kill` supplies clearly marked kill-brick surfaces. `checkpoint-respawn` draws broad, flat landing pads where players can safely resume. Difficulty increases within each segment and across successive generated segments through tighter, more demanding obstacle arrangements.

## Layout requirements
Place `checkpoint-respawn` markers on the designated landing or retry pads distributed through every generated segment, including one at each segment boundary; falling should return the player to the latest pad reached rather than to a tower base. No finite winner trigger is placed because the ascent never ends.

## Layout components
Build one reusable vertical module about 100 studs tall, with a bottom entry pad, an ordered ascent of colorful platforms, wall-hop faces, vanishing stair runs, kill-brick obstacles, intermediate landing pads, and a top handoff aligned to the next generated module. Stack copies directly upward without a visible ceiling, while varying obstacle dressing and increasing challenge. The tower is bounded closely around its climbing path but remains visually open to the surrounding sky. Compose the isometric view looking sharply upward so repeated modules disappear into atmospheric distance.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish the valid platform chain and retry-pad connectivity first, then dress the isometric tower from that plan. The wall-hop portions rely on undocumented movement technique and must use author-specified spacing rather than ordinary jump validation.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge in runtime extent, built from small 100-stud repeating modules. The theme is vivid, playful, and increasingly intense. `P6` is required for a connected obby structure; `P2` is required because vertically stacked platforms overhang and hide lower surfaces.
