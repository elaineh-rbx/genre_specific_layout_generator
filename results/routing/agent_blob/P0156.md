# Scene prompt
Create a super-realistic 3D horror escape map set in a small 1985 neighborhood centered on Fred's Pizzeria. The pizzeria is the main playable area, with a ball pit serving as the time-travel entrance and escape route; the player emerges from it into the past and must return through it before it closes. Include the yellow, ten-foot-tall bunny monster in a worn spring-style mascot suit moving through the pizzeria and nearby neighborhood spaces, initially appearing as a guide before pursuing the player. The non-playable opening is a cinematic drive to the pizzeria.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Survival because the playable objective is escaping a hostile pursuing monster before the route closes.

## Shape and preset
Use `warren-looping` as a connected chase space spanning Fred’s Pizzeria and its immediate neighborhood, with looped routes and zero dead ends. The preset is Escape, adapted so its escape gate is the ball pit rather than a structural door.

## Config requirements
Draw `cover-los` as kitchen counters, arcade cabinets, dining partitions, service alcoves, parked cars, and short exterior walls that break the bunny’s sightlines. Use `building-interior` for the enterable pizzeria connected to surrounding streets and yards. Draw the visible side of `gate-escape` as the ball pit that serves as both arrival point and closing return portal.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawner-npc` origin for the hostile ten-foot yellow bunny in a pizzeria area where its initial guide appearance can occur, then give it access to both interior loops and nearby exterior routes. Place the functional side of `gate-escape` as one timed escape trigger in the ball pit. Keep the player arrival beside that trigger, while routing the search-and-chase movement away through the building before returning to it.

## Layout components
Center the map on Fred’s Pizzeria, with dining room, arcade, kitchen, service halls, and the ball-pit room connected by multiple loops. Outside, add a compact 1985 neighborhood fragment of streets, small yards, storefront edges, and parking areas that reconnect to more than one pizzeria entrance. The neighborhood perimeter is blocked by buildings, fences, and road closures. Compose the pizzeria as the dominant central landmark and keep the ball pit visually identifiable inside. Do not build the cinematic drive; it is non-playable.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the isometric image, because broken interior-exterior loops would make the monster chase unfair.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a medium scale for a compact neighborhood plus a detailed main building. The theme is super-realistic 1985 family-pizzeria horror. Route with `P6` for valid zero-dead-end chase topology and `P3` for linked exterior and pizzeria-interior captures.
