# Scene prompt
Create a 3D high-speed arcade speedrun course as one continuous linear route of three distinct themed levels flowing directly into each other. Each level is a compact 45–60 second run at super-speed, with a few clearly spaced obstacle sections and glowing green checkpoint pads at the start of each new section. Level 1 is a neon cyberpunk highway with a fast, open forward route framed by moving grid walls, glowing laser-beam barriers, and booster pads. Level 2 transitions into a futuristic factory containing rotating electric rollers, timed vertical laser gates, and toxic-waste pits along the path. Level 3 becomes a crumbling space station with floating platforms, swinging electric pendulums, and a finish line at the far end. Scatter spinning glowing gold coins throughout all three levels, especially along routes, jumps, and obstacle sections.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Racing because this is a timed, finite super-speed run to a finish. Obby & Platformer is secondary because discrete jumps and timing hazards shape the route.

## Shape and preset
Use `route-point-to-point`, one continuous start-to-finish course through 3 themes. The preset is Obstacle Race.

## Config requirements
Use `hazard-kill` for lasers, electric obstacles, toxic-waste pits, and fall-away space edges. Use `trigger-finish` for the terminal finish structure, `checkpoint-respawn` for glowing green recovery pads, `obstacle-moving` for grid walls, rollers, and pendulums, and `obstacle-timing` for laser gates and disappearing platforms. Use `powerup-buffs` for booster pads. The three themed environments remain contiguous rather than separate maps.

## Layout requirements
Place one `startpoint-line` at the cyberpunk highway entrance and the layout half of `trigger-finish` at Level 3’s far end. Place `checkpoint-respawn` pads at each level entrance and at the start of every major obstacle section, restoring the nearest safe point. Place `collectible-nodes` as spinning gold coin lines along all 3 routes, jumps, and higher-risk obstacle approaches. Place `powerup-buffs` on clear highway acceleration lines.

## Layout components
Build 3 consecutive 45–60-second levels. Level 1 is an open neon highway with grid walls, laser bars, and boosters; Level 2 is a factory corridor with rollers, timed gates, and waste pits; Level 3 is a crumbling station route of floating disappearing platforms and swinging pendulums. Use direct transition thresholds, not teleports. Keep the critical path centered and unmistakable from start to finish.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, because the complete route, jumps, hazards, and checkpoints must remain continuously clearable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge, driven by 3 super-speed levels lasting 45–60 seconds each. The theme progresses from neon cyberpunk to industrial electric factory to ruined space station. Use `P6` because the race and obstacle chain must be valid by construction.
