## Enriched image prompt
Create a large Taishō-era rural-fantasy action-RPG world for demon hunters. One continuous overworld connects six readable regions by natural trails: a safe-by-day Starting Village with a family home, character-start area, quest board, shops, tutorial obby, and nearby earth terrain; a foggy moonlit forest with hunt clearings and caravan routes; Butterfly Mansion with training grounds and healer space; a compact swordsmith village and forge; a snowy mountain pass with boulder parkour and a hidden trainer cave; and a haunted rural road with ambush and defense clearings. The forest, road, and mountain routes converge on a physical entrance to the separately instanced Spider Demon Caves, whose twisting tunnels lead to a boss arena. A later mountain threshold leads to the separately instanced Endgame Demon Fortress, an icy summit raid complex with miniboss and final encounter grounds. Distribute at least ten distinct trainer sites across houses, shrines, gardens, mountain caves, and mansion grounds, with four beginner sites near early regions and harder sites deeper in the world. Use low-detail mobile-friendly forms, strong regional landmarks, Japanese rural architecture, fog, rain, moonlight, and clear day/night-readable travel routes.

## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG, canonical slug `rpg`, with Adventure (`adventure`) secondary for the connected quest journey and exploration.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-hub-dungeon`: the six-zone overworld is the shared hub surface, while Spider Demon Caves and Endgame Demon Fortress are party instances. The matched preset is Action RPG, retaining its sanctuary, economy hub, hostile encounter ground, and mob-origin structure.

## Config requirements
Use `safezone-town` for the Starting Village’s protected daytime hub; `social-hub` for clustered quest, forge, healer, trainer, and merchant facilities; `hazard-aggro` for forest, haunted-road, cave, and fortress encounter grounds; `path-trail` for natural overworld links; `landmark-focal` for the mansion, mountain, forge village, and summit fortress; `gate-progression` for mountain and fortress thresholds; `building-interior` for the family home, mansion, shops, forge, and dungeon interiors; `terrain-relief` for passes, peaks, and caves; `tracker-quest` for physical village boards; `npc-population` for villagers, guards, merchants, healers, blacksmiths, and at least ten trainers; and `path-track` plus `obstacle-jump` for the movement tutorial and mountain parkour.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` Spawners inside encounter clearings, tunnel nests, road ambush grounds, and fortress arenas, away from safe travel roads. Place `tracker-quest` Trackers in the Starting Village and Forge Village. Place `npc-population` Zones at each settlement and trainer site, including at least ten trainer placements. Place `checkpoint-respawn` Checkpoints through the tutorial obby and at safe approaches to the two instances.

## Layout components
Use three map segments: the Continuous Overworld map containing Starting Village, Foggy Forest, Butterfly Mansion, Forge Village, Snowy Mountain Pass, and Haunted Road; the Spider Demon Caves instance with twisting tunnels and boss arena; and the Endgame Demon Fortress instance on an icy summit. Roads and trails form the stated loop and branching links among overworld zones. Physical cave and fortress thresholds connect the hub to their instances.

## Render order
Use `topdown`; regional connectivity, encounter separation, and tutorial traversal must be authoritative before the visual dressing, while this is not a supported maze or racing blueprint.

The theme is low-detail Taishō-era rural demon-hunter fantasy with earth, foggy forest, snowy alpine, cave, and icy fortress registers. Use `P4` for the two party instances, `P3` for outside-to-interior transitions, `P2` for cave and tunnel overhangs, `P6` for valid tutorial and mountain parkour, and `tiered` for alpine relief.
