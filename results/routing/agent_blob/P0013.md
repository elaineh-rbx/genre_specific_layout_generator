# Scene prompt
Create a 3D open-world action RPG map in a Taishō-era fantasy setting, built as one continuous world with natural transitions across eight zones. Begin in a rural earth-biome Starting Village: a daytime-safe hub with family homes, a small tutorial obstacle course, and paths leading into surrounding woods. A foggy, moonlit Cursed Forest encircles the village and connects to the Butterfly Mansion training grounds, a forge village, and a snowy mountain pass with alpine trails, boulder parkour, and a hidden trainer cave. A woodland route becomes a dark Haunted Road, continuing toward the forge village and its blacksmith area. The forge village and mountain pass each lead to the Spider Demon Caves, a twisting tunnel dungeon instanced per party for boss encounters. Beyond the caves, high icy mountains rise to an Endgame Demon Fortress, also instanced per party, with raid chambers for final bosses. Include a return route from the fortress back toward the starting village. Use day-to-night lighting, rain, fog, and moonlight to reshape visibility across roads, forests, and settlements.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG, with Action secondary, because character progression, quests, trainers, and boss raids organize a combat-driven open world.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-hub-dungeon`: one connected overworld feeds the party-instanced Spider Demon Caves and Endgame Demon Fortress. The matched preset is Action RPG, retaining its sanctuary, economy cluster, hostile encounter grounds, and hostile emitters.

## Config requirements
Use `safezone-town` for the Starting Village; `social-hub` for its homes and the forge village’s blacksmith and training services; `hazard-aggro` for forest clearings, Haunted Road ambush ground, caves, and raid chambers; `npc-population` for villagers, trainers, and blacksmiths; `building-interior` for the Butterfly Mansion, hidden trainer cave, caves, and fortress chambers; `terrain-relief` for the snowy pass and icy mountains; and `landmark-focal` so mansion, forge, mountain, and fortress guide travel. Include the visible small tutorial obstacle course and boulder parkour.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` emitters in hostile clearings, along Haunted Road, inside cave encounter rooms, and in fortress raid chambers, never in the Starting Village. Place the layout half of `npc-population` at homes, training grounds, forge stations, and the hidden cave. No exact population count is stated.

## Layout components
Build exactly eight named zones: Starting Village, Cursed Forest, Butterfly Mansion, forge village, snowy mountain pass, Haunted Road, Spider Demon Caves, and Endgame Demon Fortress. Roads and woodland paths form the stated branching graph; both forge and mountain routes converge on cave entrances, the caves lead to the fortress, and a return route closes toward the village. Use earth, forest, snow, rock, tunnels, chambers, fog, rain, and moonlit sightline shifts. Keep the overworld continuous while caves and fortress are separate party instances.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: connectivity, route convergence, and valid obstacle traversal must lead; the top-down is authoritative.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge, driven by eight zones plus two instances. The theme is Taishō-era demon fantasy. Use `P4` for instanced maps, `P3` for exterior-to-interior transitions, `P2` for tunnels and overlapping chambers, and `P6` for the playable tutorial and boulder obstacle chains.
