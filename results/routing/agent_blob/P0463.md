# Scene prompt
Create a 3D soccer map in a modern floodlit stadium with a clear Roblox-avatar scale and style. A full soccer pitch sits at the center, bordered by sidelines and goal areas, with opposing goals at each end and spectator stands rising around the field for team-versus-team play.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant and only genre is Sports, built around opposing player-controlled soccer teams on a formal field.

## Shape and preset
Use the single `space-bounded` shape for the full pitch within its stadium enclosure. The preset is Full Stadium, supplemented with the field boundaries, start positions, and scoring goals required for team-versus-team soccer.

## Config requirements
Draw `spectator-bleachers` as rising stands surrounding the pitch. Draw `barrier-perimeter` as the modern stadium’s enclosing outer wall and railings. Draw `spectator-zone` as team bench sectors along opposite sides. Draw `trigger-bounds` as crisp sidelines, goal lines, and marked field regions; draw `startpoint-play` as the center spot and mirrored team positions; draw `trigger-scoring` as two netted goals. Size all geometry clearly for blocky 3D Roblox avatars and illuminate the field with floodlight towers.

## Layout requirements
Place one `trigger-bounds` perimeter exactly on the pitch edge. Place two `trigger-scoring` volumes inside the two opposing goal mouths. Place `startpoint-play` markers at midfield and in balanced mirrored formations on each team half. Keep all functional markers clear of the bench sectors and stand circulation.

## Layout components
Center one full rectangular soccer pitch with halfway line, center circle, sidelines, goal lines, penalty areas, and two opposite goals. Wrap it with a safety strip and four connected banks of tiered seating. Put opposing team bench areas beside midfield, outside the boundary. Enclose the stadium with a clean modern shell, rails, and evenly distributed floodlights, leaving no visual obstruction over the playing surface. The field is the composition’s focal plane, with stands rising evenly around it and all dimensions readable at Roblox-avatar scale.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: render the plan first and make it authoritative because pitch symmetry, goal alignment, team starts, and trigger locations must be exact; derive the floodlit stadium view from that plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is large, driven by a full pitch and surrounding stands. The theme is a modern floodlit soccer venue in clean Roblox proportions. Use `tiered` because the stands rise in steps without overhanging the play surface.
