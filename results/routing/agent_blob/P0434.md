# Scene prompt
Create a 3D volcanic wasteland map on a small, dense 100-by-100-stud area of rugged generated rock terrain. Cover the ground with patches of differently colored lava, with bridges and doors crossing or dividing parts of the terrain; place buttons throughout the landscape beside these features. Scatter crystals across the ground in every direction at roughly one-meter spacing.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure because gathering every crystal is the run's objective. Puzzle is secondary: buttons alter lava, bridges, and doors to change access through the search space.

## Shape and preset
Use `puzzle-open`, taken from outside Adventure's usual shapes, for one open-air volcanic puzzle field whose controls and barriers are distributed across rugged terrain. The preset is Scavenger Hunt, retaining dense collectible objectives while substituting the open-air puzzle shape and adding the requested controls.

## Config requirements
Use `terrain-relief` for rugged generated rock across the exact 100-by-100-stud footprint. Use `hazard-kill` for differently colored contact-lethal lava patches. Draw `button-solve` controls beside the features they affect, and use `gate-solve` for lockable doors and movable bridges that block access until operated. Bridges must visibly span lava or broken ground.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` as crystals across safe ground in every direction at roughly one-meter spacing; retain the stated spacing rather than inventing a total. Place the functional half of each `button-solve` on safe standing ground beside its associated lava control, bridge, or door, with an unambiguous one-to-one spatial relationship. Ensure every crystal becomes reachable through some valid button state.

## Layout components
Build one dense 100-by-100-stud volcanic zone of jagged rock terraces. Tile much of the ground with separated multicolored lava patches while preserving a connected network of safe rock. Add several bridges across lava cuts and several doors dividing terrain pockets, with buttons distributed beside their controlled features. Cover safe ground with dense crystal positions at the requested spacing. Bound the map with high volcanic rock walls. Compose the overview to expose the safe-ground network and control relationships, while the dressed view emphasizes colored lava and crystals.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: draw the authoritative safe-ground, bridge, door, and button plan first, then dress it isometrically, because inaccessible crystal pockets or broken control routes would make completion impossible.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, fixed at 100 by 100 studs, but very dense. The theme is a rugged multicolored volcanic wasteland. Use `tiered` for rock relief and `P2` where bridges overhang lava surfaces; no interiors or separate maps are present.
