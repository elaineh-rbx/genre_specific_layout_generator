# Agent decision

- [author] theme — Q: What should the world look like - a volcanic wasteland, a bright fantasy landscape, something alien? A: A volcanic wasteland: rugged generated rock terrain with multicolored lava patches, bridges, doors, and crystals placed across it.
- [author] scale — Q: How large is the terrain? Crystals every meter means the size decides the count. A: Keep it to a small, dense terrain map, roughly 100 by 100 studs, so crystals can be placed about every meter without making the collection count excessive.
- [author] goal — Q: Does gathering every crystal end the game, or unlock something? A: Gathering every crystal ends the game; no additional unlock or story beat is needed.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a dense 3D volcanic wasteland roughly 100 by 100 studs, using rugged generated rock terrain with objects placed on top. Cover the accessible ground in irregular patches of differently colored lethal lava while preserving connected safe rock routes. Add multiple movable bridges over dangerous gaps and several structural doors embedded in the terrain. Distribute physical buttons throughout the map beside the features they control: some toggle lava patches, some move bridges, and some lock or unlock doors. Reserve reachable crystal placement points across the walkable ground at approximately one-metre spacing in every direction, including areas exposed by button-controlled routes. Keep the terrain compact enough that the complete hazard network, controls, bridges, doors, and dense collection field read together.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure because the player explores the terrain to gather every crystal. Puzzle is secondary because buttons alter hazards, bridges, and doors to change which ground is reachable.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, named Open World: one compact terrain map can be explored in any order as its controls change. The matched preset is Scavenger Hunt, retaining its dense collectible targets while replacing generic secret alcoves with explicit button-gated terrain and omitting a physical quest board.

## Config requirements
Use `hazard-kill` for every multicolored lava patch. Use `terrain-relief` for the rugged generated rock ground. Use the visible half of `button-solve` for the repeated terrain-control buttons, and use `gate-solve` for doors and bridge crossings whose availability changes after the correct control is used. Moving bridges are visible scene geometry with safe landing ends. Crystals are placed after segmentation rather than drawn into the terrain.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` crystals across all reachable walkable ground at the author's density of about every metre in every direction; preserve that stated spacing rather than converting it to an invented exact count. Place each `button-solve` functional input beside a safe standing area and link it to one explicit lava patch, moving bridge, or door state. Validate that every crystal is reachable under some coherent sequence of button states. Collecting all crystals ends the game globally, so no finish volume is needed.

## Layout components
The map is one roughly 100-by-100-stud bounded terrain zone. Rugged safe rock regions weave between multicolored lava patches; movable bridges join separated safe sections; doors block selected passages; and control buttons sit near readable approach paths. Crystal sites densely cover every reachable ground section, including gated areas. Hard volcanic rock edges contain the map. Compose the terrain so controls and their affected features can be visually associated without making the solve sequence obvious.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish safe-ground connectivity, lava coverage, bridge endpoints, door gates, button relationships, and the dense reachable crystal field before dressing the isometric wasteland. The top-down plan is authoritative because an unreachable crystal or control state would make completion impossible.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is medium, driven by the explicit roughly 100-by-100-stud extent and metre-spaced crystal detail. The theme is a rugged volcanic wasteland with multicolored lava and mechanical terrain controls. `tiered` is required for the generated relief without overhangs, and `P6` is required to validate that all collectible ground remains reachable through the button-controlled bridge, lava, and door state graph.
