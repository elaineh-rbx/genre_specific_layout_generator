# Scene prompt
Create a 3D animal roleplay map as a modern suburban shared town. Lay out drivable roads connecting simple enterable houses, with space for animal players and cars to move around the neighborhood; all houses are open for shared use.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, centered on animal avatars sharing a suburban town, driving, and using houses together.

## Shape and preset
Use `settlement-static`: this is a fixed, pre-built neighborhood with no claimable or player-built property. Start from the Life preset but substitute `settlement-static` for its claimable-house shape and omit its unrequested commercial-versus-residential districting; its road and interior requirements still match the brief.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for a flat suburban road network at least 30 studs wide on two-way streets, with turns and junctions that car meshes can navigate without clipping. Use `building-interior` for simple shared houses that animal players can enter from the neighborhood rather than merely viewing as facades.

## Layout requirements
Nothing additional needs placement after segmentation. The brief does not request spawn markers, claims, teleports, checkpoints, pickups, or scoring volumes; every house remains shared rather than receiving ownership markers.

## Layout components
Build one modern suburban neighborhood with a connected two-way road network, sensible junctions, and turning space for cars. Arrange simple detached houses along both sides with short approaches from the roads and enough open ground for animal avatars to circulate safely around vehicles. Every house needs a linked interior plan and a clear exterior entrance, but no private plot boundary or claim pad. Use flat maintained terrain, lawns, verges, and restrained suburban planting. Enclose the town with a natural or developed neighborhood edge rather than abrupt walls. Compose the isometric to show the road network, repeated houses, and several visibly open entrances at once.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the modern suburban look and shared neighborhood composition are authoritative, and the ordinary connected road network does not require a computed plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, large enough for driving while remaining one shared neighborhood. The theme is clean, modern, simple suburban animal roleplay. Use `P3` because play crosses between the outdoor roads and enterable house interiors; no other modifier is forced.
