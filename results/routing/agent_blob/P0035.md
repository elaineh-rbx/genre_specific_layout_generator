- [author] shape — Q: Do players claim a house each, or is it a shared town where any house can be entered? A: Use a shared town where any house can be entered, so players can easily roleplay together.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a 3D animal roleplay map as a shared modern suburban town. Arrange simple houses along a connected road network with enough open street space for animal players to drive cars around the neighborhood. Make the houses enterable from the streets rather than private claimable plots, and keep the town compact enough that individual homes, roads, and entrances remain clear in one isometric view.

## Genre
The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim. Animal avatars are the roleplay subject, while shared houses and driving define a social town rather than the wilderness used by the Animal Sim preset.

## Shape and preset
Use `settlement-static`, a fixed shared town with prebuilt houses that any player may enter. Start from the Life preset, substitute its claimable-housing shape with the author-confirmed shared-town shape, and omit the unrequested commercial districting while retaining its road and interior structure.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for wide, connected suburban roads that cars can turn through between houses. Use `building-interior` for simple homes entered directly from those roads.

## Layout requirements
No additional post-segmentation placement option is required; the request does not establish private plots, checkpoints, pickups, emitters, or scoring volumes.

## Layout components
Build one compact residential town zone containing rows of simple prebuilt houses, their visible entrances and interiors, and a connected network of vehicle-width roads. Roads loop through the neighborhood without isolating any house. The outer edge is a clear suburban map boundary, and the composition keeps the shared town centered with streets and entrances legible.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the inhabited suburban look first, then derive the top-down plan because the road network does not require generated race-course topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale, driven by the need to read individual houses, entrances, and roads. The theme is modern suburban. The required modifier is `P3` because play crosses from outdoor streets into house interiors.
