# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map as a plain, functional park-like grassy arena for testing. The space is a small yard-sized field enclosed by simple boundary walls, with basic obstacles scattered across the grass and one or two NPC characters moving slowly within the enclosed area.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Party & Casual because this is a simple physical game of tag.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded` from the Childhood Game preset: one continuous yard is both the gathering and play space. Keep the preset’s bounded shape but omit its maze and separate social-hub assumptions because the brief asks for a plain testing arena.

## Config requirements
Apply `cover-los` in a restrained form as basic freestanding obstacles scattered across the grass, spaced for easy pursuit rather than dense hide-and-seek clutter. Apply `npc-population` to show one or two slow-moving test NPCs and the open ground they inhabit. Draw simple continuous walls around the yard.

## Layout requirements
Place `npc-population` in the layout stream as one or two NPC movement agents constrained to the enclosed grassy field, with broad routes around the obstacles and no inaccessible pockets. Place `trigger-scoring` as a tag-detection region associated with the back of each NPC so contact can register the intended tag. Place one player start marker near an arena edge, separated from the initial NPC positions but with a clear route into the field.

## Layout components
Build one small, flat, yard-sized grassy zone. Enclose it on all sides with plain functional boundary walls and no exits that allow accidental escape. Scatter a modest set of simple block, barrel, or low-wall obstacles across the interior while retaining wide circulation loops between them. Keep the central field readable and unobstructed enough for a player to catch one or two slow NPCs. Frame the whole test arena clearly from above with the wall perimeter visible.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative because the simple single-level composition and obstacle appearance lead, while exact topology is not structurally fragile.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by the yard-sized test area and one or two NPCs. The theme is a plain, functional grassy park prototype. Use `P0`; the field is one flat visible surface with no overhangs, transitions, separate maps, or volumetric play.
