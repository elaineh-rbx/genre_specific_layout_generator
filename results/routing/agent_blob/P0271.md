## Enriched image prompt
Create two separate interior maps for an original asymmetric horror game: a large lobby and the first medium compact chase arena, an abandoned school. The lobby has an open gathering floor with NPC space, a shop area, mission and ranking boards, a training corner, and a prominent queue portal. The school uses classrooms, offices, and corridors arranged as interconnected loops with multiple alternate routes and no dead ends. Distribute visible task stations through different school rooms, add localized lockers and low hiding cutouts, and place a clearly identifiable exit gate that unlocks after the tasks. Use dynamic low lighting, fog, particles, and unsettling ambient composition in the school while keeping routes readable for a three-minute chase.

## Genre
The genre is Survival, canonical slug `survival`, because up to six survivors complete tasks while evading one killer and trying to unlock an exit.

## Shape and preset
Use top-level `space-staged`, Zone (Lobby and Isolated Stage), from outside Survival's usual shapes because the lobby and arena are confirmed as separate maps. The matched preset is 1 vs All, with the Abandoned School arena segment using `warren-looping` for zero-dead-end chase routes.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for the separate lobby gathering floor, `npc-population` for lobby NPCs, `tracker-quest` for the physical missions board, `tracker-leaderboard` for ranking and statistics walls, `station-training` for the practice corner, and `teleporter-link` for the visible queue portal. In the school use `cover-los` for lockers and crawlspaces, `trigger-task` for visible task stations, and both the visible forms of `gate-escape` and `teleporter-link` for the unlocked exit and lobby-to-arena transfer.

## Layout requirements
Place `npc-population` Zones at the lobby service areas, one `tracker-quest` Tracker at the missions board, one `tracker-leaderboard` Tracker on a prominent wall, one `station-training` TriggerZone in the practice corner, and paired `teleporter-link` Teleporters between lobby queue and school staging. In the school, place `spawn-protected` SpawnZones for exactly one killer and up to six survivors, separated and screened from immediate contact. Spread `trigger-task` TriggerZones across distinct loop sectors and place one `gate-escape` Gate at the outer school boundary.

## Layout components
There are two `map` segments. Lobby uses `interior-single`: gathering floor, NPC stations, shop, boards, training corner, and queue portal. Abandoned School uses `warren-looping`: classrooms, offices, looping corridors, alternate routes, task stations, hiding cutouts, separated starts, and one exit. Laboratory, Destroyed City, Factory, and Mansion are deferred from this first build.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: plan the lobby and school separately, validating the school's zero-dead-end loops, task spread, starts, and exit before dressing both isometrically. The top-down plans are authoritative because an unfair dead end or unreachable objective would break the chase.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium per map, driven by the confirmed compact three-minute school chase while keeping rooms and task stations legible; the lobby is large relative to that frame. The theme is modern asymmetric horror, with the first arena as a foggy abandoned school. Use `P4` for the separate lobby and arena maps and `P6` for the school's mandatory zero-dead-end loop topology.
