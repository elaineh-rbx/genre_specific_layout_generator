# Scene prompt
Create a 3D asymmetrical horror game layout for Shadow Escape, with a separate large lobby area containing NPC stations, a shop, inventory, missions, settings, rankings, statistics, a training area, and a portal leading into the match queue. Build the first arena as a compact, medium-sized abandoned school designed for a three-minute chase: interconnected corridors, classrooms, and enough alternate routes to prevent the space from feeling empty. Use dark atmospheric lighting, fog, particles, ambient sound sources, and worn school architecture to support the horror mood.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Survival, specifically asymmetric horror for one killer against up to six survivors.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-staged`, taken from outside Survival’s usual shapes, because the large lobby and abandoned-school match arena are genuinely separate spaces. The preset is 1 vs All, with its usual warren shape replaced while retaining pursuit cover, role-separated starts, and an escape exit.

## Config requirements
Draw `cover-los` as classroom corners, lockers, partitions, desks, and corridor turns. Draw `gate-escape` as the school’s locked final exit. Draw `npc-population` as staffed lobby stations. The separate lobby must visibly include shop, inventory, missions, settings, rankings, statistics, training, and queue-portal stations; the school must include visible task workstations.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly seven `spawn-protected` role starts in the school: six survivor positions distributed among classrooms and one isolated killer position. Place interaction triggers at every rendered task station and the functional half of `gate-escape` at the final school exit. Place queue transport markers at the lobby portal and school arrival point. Site ambient sound emitters through both maps.

## Layout components
The lobby is a large separate hall organized around NPC service stations, a training zone, and a prominent queue portal. The arena is one compact medium abandoned school with interconnected corridors, classrooms, alternate doorways, lockers, desks, worn walls, and distributed task stations. Routes reconnect around classroom blocks and lead ultimately to the exit. Solid school walls bound the arena. Compose both spaces with dark lighting, fog, particles, and focused landmark illumination.

## Render order
Use `topdown` because school route connectivity, alternate pursuit paths, starts, tasks, and the reachable exit must be settled before atmospheric dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large overall: a large lobby plus a medium three-minute arena. The theme is worn abandoned-school horror. Use `P4` because lobby and arena are separate maps.
