# Scene prompt
Create a compact 3D siege map with a small ice town enclosed by a grey defensive wall. Inside the wall, arrange a handful of ice buildings as the town center; leave approach space outside the wall for torch-bearing attackers to rush the defenses.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Action: players directly assault and defend one bounded clash space, with attackers physically breaking the town’s defenses.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded` for one compact siege arena containing the outer approach, enclosing wall, and defended town. The preset is none because the asymmetric destructible-town siege does not retain enough of any standard Action preset.

## Config requirements
Use `destructible-cluster` for the grey defensive wall and the handful of ice buildings, making the fortifications and town structures visibly breakable under torch attacks. Use `settlement-density` for a compact small town centre rather than isolated unrelated props. Use the visible geometry of `spawn-protected` for two screened staging areas: an attacker start outside the wall and a defender start within the town.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` as exactly two team spawn zones. Site the torch-bearing attackers at the far edge of the exterior approach behind cover from the wall, and the ice defenders behind the inner buildings, out of immediate attacker reach. Mark every ice building as part of the town’s destructible objective set so the scene contains no untracked structure whose survival state is ambiguous. The prompt says a handful rather than an exact building count, so preserve that wording without inventing a number.

## Layout components
Build a compact outer approach zone leading directly to a continuous grey defensive wall. Inside, cluster a handful of ice buildings around a small central lane or square, leaving defenders room to circulate between structures. The wall forms the main boundary between attackers and the town, while a hard outer arena edge encloses the approach. Keep the ground mostly level and snowy or frost-covered. Use chunky grey masonry for the wall and translucent blue-white ice architecture for the town, with visible breakable seams and damage-ready sections. Compose the defended town as the clear centre, framed by the wall and the attacker approach in front.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative because the contrast between wall, ice town, and assault approach defines the scene, while the topology is simple and bounded. Derive the top-down plan from it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by the compact match area and handful of buildings. The theme is a bright elemental ice siege against grey fortification. Use `P0`: one exterior surface with no overhang, interior transition, or separate map.
