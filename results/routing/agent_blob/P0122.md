# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] goal — Q: How does a round end - a timer the defenders survive, or the whole town melted? A: A round ends when the attackers melt the whole town; the ice team wins if they keep any of the town standing until the round timer expires.
- [author] scale — Q: Is the town a handful of buildings or a full settlement inside the wall? A: Make it a small town with a handful of ice buildings inside the grey wall, so the match area stays compact and playable.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a compact asymmetric team battlefield centered on a small ice town. Place a handful of distinct ice buildings close together inside a continuous grey defensive wall, with enough internal lanes for the ice defenders to move between buildings. Outside the wall, provide open approach ground for the torch-carrying attacker team, leading directly toward the defenses from a separate staging edge. Treat the grey wall and every ice building as visibly meltable or breakable parts of one distributed town objective. Contrast cold translucent ice architecture with warm torchlight and scorched attack paths, keeping all buildings and wall faces readable in one view.

## Genre
Strategy is dominant because the map is organized around attacking and defending a base objective. Action is secondary because both teams directly fight over the town.

## Shape and preset
Use `terrain-open`, named Open Contested Terrain, with the ice town as one defended base and attacker approaches across the surrounding ground. This uses the Base Defense preset but drops its hostile-NPC wave origin because the attackers are players.

## Config requirements
Use `destructible-cluster` for the grey wall and handful of ice buildings that can be broken or melted. Use `tracker-core` in visible form for the distributed ice-town objective, where each remaining building visibly contributes to the town still standing. Use universal `settlement-density` for the compact handful of ice buildings grouped as a small town rather than scattered structures. Use `spawn-protected` for visible, separated defender and attacker staging pockets.

## Layout requirements
Place one `tracker-core` Tracker over the whole ice-building cluster, bound to the remaining town structures rather than a single invented core. Place two `spawn-protected` SpawnZones: one sheltered inside the grey wall for the ice defenders and one outside the wall at the far edge of the attacker approach, screened from immediate combat.

## Layout components
The central defended zone is a small town containing a handful of ice buildings enclosed by one grey perimeter wall. Short internal paths link the buildings and wall interior. The attack zone occupies the outer approach ground, connecting the attacker staging edge to multiple exposed wall faces without introducing unrelated objectives. The defender spawn sits inside the town; the attacker spawn sits beyond torch range at the opposite edge. Frame the entire compact siege area so the town, wall, and approach remain legible together.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the ice-versus-fire siege composition first and derive the top-down view, because the map has straightforward connected ground and no topology that must be solved procedurally.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by individual buildings inside a compact playable town. The theme is stylized elemental ice-versus-fire fantasy with a plain grey defense wall. Use `P0` because all combat occupies one continuous surface with no required overhangs, interiors, separate maps, or structural generator.
