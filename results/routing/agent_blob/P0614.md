# Scene prompt
Create a 3D driving map set in a small, polished modern real-world city spanning a few connected blocks. Lay out interconnected paved streets for cruising, bordered by city buildings, sidewalks, streetlights, and traffic-style roadside details.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation. Cruising is the complete activity, with no lap, delivery, police chase, or other goal that would change the layout genre.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, a shared compact city whose connected blocks may be driven in any order. The preset is Vehicle Sim, retaining the open vehicle world and road network while dropping the unrelated shared resource field.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for interconnected paved streets wide enough for comfortable cruising and turning. Use `settlement-density` for a small, polished cluster of a few modern city blocks rather than isolated buildings. The image should include sidewalks, streetlights, intersections, and restrained traffic-style roadside details. Do not use `building-interior`, because the prompt never asks players to enter the buildings.

## Layout requirements
No canonical post-segmentation option is required. Place several vehicle spawn markers in marked curbside bays or a small parking area, oriented toward the street and clear of intersections. Place a player arrival marker on the adjacent sidewalk. No checkpoint, task trigger, capture area, or winner zone is needed for free cruising.

## Layout components
Build a compact grid or modified grid of a few connected blocks, with at least one route looping back so drivers are not forced to reverse at dead ends. Use paved two-way streets, clear intersections, turning room, sidewalks, and readable curbs. Line the blocks with modern low- and mid-rise building shells, leaving simple alleys or parking pockets where helpful. Distribute streetlights consistently along sidewalks and add traffic signals, signs, barriers, or hydrants as sparse roadside detail without narrowing the driving lanes. Keep terrain flat and enclose the map with a visually plausible continuation of buildings or blocked streets. Compose the street network as the clear central structure.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the connected block network and usable intersections should be authoritative before the isometric view dresses the city.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by the clarified few-block scope. The theme is polished modern real-world urban. Use `P0`; one flat outdoor map requires no overhang, interior transition, separate map, or structural generator.
