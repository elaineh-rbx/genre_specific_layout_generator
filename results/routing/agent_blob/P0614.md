# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] scale — Q: How big a city - a few blocks to cruise, or a full open-world map? A: A few connected city blocks is enough for a small, polished cruising map.
- [author] free — Q: Is there anything to do while driving - deliveries, races, police chases - or is cruising the whole game? A: Cruising around the city is the whole game; no extra activities are needed.
- [author] theme — Q: Modern real-world city, or something more stylized? A: Use a modern real-world city style with roads, buildings, streetlights, and traffic-style details.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a small, polished modern real-world city driving map made from a few connected blocks. Build a continuous vehicle-width street network that loops and reconnects through the district, with believable intersections, surrounding building facades, streetlights, curbs, and traffic-style road details. Keep the streets open for relaxed cruising with no racecourse, delivery destination, police-chase route, or other activity zone.

## Genre
Simulation is the sole genre because operating a vehicle and cruising freely are the entire game.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, named Shared Persistent World, for one contiguous city district explored freely. This uses the Vehicle Sim preset while dropping its unrequested extraction-resource field.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for the connected city street network, routed to `P0` because the roads are ordinary free-roam streets rather than a racecourse. Use universal `settlement-density` for a compact urban district of several connected blocks. Buildings, streetlights, curbs, and traffic-style details are direct scene components. The request to source models from the Roblox store is an implementation constraint, not a layout option.

## Layout requirements
None. Cruising needs no start grid, lap or finish trigger, delivery station, police spawner, collectible, or objective marker.

## Layout components
The map contains a few city blocks arranged around a connected road loop with cross streets and readable intersections. Building facades line the outer sidewalks, while streetlights, curbs, and traffic-style details define the roadway edges without narrowing vehicle turns. Every street reconnects into the cruising network rather than ending at an activity destination. The outer boundary closes around one small district, and the full road pattern remains readable in frame.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the modern city-block composition first and derive the top-down view, because the free-roam street network is simple and no exact competitive route must be generated.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by a few connected blocks with individual buildings, intersections, and streetlights kept legible. The theme is polished modern real-world city. Use `P0` because the build is one exterior surface with no interiors, overhang requirement, separate maps, or structurally constrained course.
