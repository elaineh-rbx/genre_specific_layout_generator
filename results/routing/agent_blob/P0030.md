- [author] goal — Q: Is there anything to complete, or is it open-ended living-as-an-animal roleplay? A: It is primarily open-ended living-as-an-animal roleplay; quests, treasures, and rare-animal discoveries are optional activities rather than a final completion goal.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a dense open natural world for animal roleplay, with major biomes about a minute's walk apart. Arrange forests, flower meadows, snowy mountains, rivers, and waterfalls as connected regions of one explorable map. Work caves, claimable natural dens, hidden tunnels, abandoned ruins, and concealed treasure alcoves into the terrain, with peaceful gathering clearings between the wilder discovery areas. Populate the landscape with forest spirits and rare animals, and scatter natural food and treasures to discover. Keep the dens suitable for decoration with natural items. Frame the whole connected wilderness clearly while preserving readable entrances, trails, watercourses, elevation, and secret side spaces; the visual register is realistic, beautiful, adventurous, and gently mystical.

## Genre
The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, canonical slug `roleplay-avatar-sim`, because open-ended living and social play as animals is the primary activity.

## Shape and preset
Use `wilderness-open`, Zone (Open Natural World), for one contiguous biome-rich wilderness without a town. The matched preset is Animal Sim.

## Config requirements
Use `den-shelter` for claimable caves, hollows, and burrows used as animal homes. Use `terrain-relief` for snowy mountains, waterfall drops, caves, and tunnelled landforms; the caves and hidden tunnels require the overhanging form. Use `water-body` for connected rivers and waterfalls. Use `social-hub` for peaceful meadow clearings where animals gather, `landmark-focal` for the mountains, waterfall, and ruins as navigation anchors, `npc-population` for forest spirits and rare animals, and Adventure's `alcove-secret` for concealed ruin, cave, and tunnel discoveries.

## Layout requirements
Place `npc-population` Zones for forest spirits and rare-animal encounters on suitable ground away from the dens. Place `collectible-nodes` Collectibles, taken from the shared catalogue, as food across the biomes and treasures in the hidden alcoves. Mark each authored den as an unnumbered claimable Plot with free-text placement, keeping its entrance connected to the wilderness and enough interior floor for natural decoration.

## Layout components
The single map contains connected forest, meadow, snowy-mountain, river, and waterfall regions; natural trails between them; multiple caves, dens, hidden tunnels, abandoned ruins, secret alcoves, peaceful clearings, food nodes, treasure sites, spirit encounter ground, and rare-animal habitats. Major biomes remain about one minute of walking apart. Open terrain forms the outer boundary, and the composition keeps cave mouths, waterways, relief, and major landmarks legible.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the wilderness, elevation, waterfall, ruins, and cave-mouth composition first, then derive linked plans for exposed and covered spaces. The isometric image is authoritative because the prompt prioritizes a beautiful dense world and does not require computed maze or racing topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is large, driven by the confirmed roughly one-minute walk between several major biomes while retaining dens and discoveries. The theme is realistic mystical wilderness across seasonal forests, meadows, ruins, rivers, and snowy mountains. Use `tiered` for mountain and waterfall relief, `P2` for caves and hidden tunnels beneath overhanging terrain, `P3` for crossings between open wilderness and enclosed dens or tunnels, and `CHECK` for traversable rivers and waterfall water volumes.
