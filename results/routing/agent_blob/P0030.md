# Scene prompt
Create a huge but dense realistic wilderness roleplay map, with major biomes about one minute of walking apart. Forests form the central connective landscape around rivers and waterfalls, leading to caves, snowy mountains, flower meadows, abandoned ruins, and hidden tunnel routes. Scatter claimable animal dens throughout sheltered terrain, with interiors or small clearings suited for natural decorations; include peaceful secluded areas, collectible food locations, hidden treasures, and hard-to-find spots for rare animal discoveries. Arrange the world as an open-ended exploration space for wolves, foxes, bears, deer, rabbits, owls, raccoons, and lynxes, with natural paths and secret connections between biomes.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim because living as customizable animals is the open-ended core. Adventure is secondary, supplying optional secrets, treasures, quests, and discovery.

## Shape and preset
Use the single `world-open` shape from outside the dominant genre’s usual set: one contiguous wilderness whose biomes can be explored in any order. Preset is none because Animal Sim’s simpler wilderness bundle does not cover the explicit multi-biome exploration, collectibles, claimable dens, and secret routes.

## Config requirements
Draw `den-shelter` as scattered natural dens, with `building-interior` for caves and dens entered from outside. Apply `water-body` to rivers and waterfalls, `terrain-relief` to snowy mountains and the valley structure, `alcove-secret` to concealed ruins and discovery pockets, and `landmark-focal` to recognizable waterfalls, peaks, and abandoned ruins. Show `npc-population` as forest spirits and rare discoverable animals occupying secluded ground; player animal species are avatars, not NPC crowds.

## Layout requirements
Place `npc-population` markers in hard-to-find clearings and ruin pockets. Place `collectible-nodes` for food and hidden treasures across every major biome, weighted toward side paths and secret areas. Add one claim marker for each den, sited at its entrance or decoration clearing. Place player spawns in a peaceful central-forest clearing, with routes wide enough for bears and readable to smaller animals.

## Layout components
Build a dense continuous world with central forest linking river corridors, waterfalls, caves, snowy mountains, flower meadows, ruins, and hidden tunnels. Keep major biomes about one minute apart. Natural paths branch between visible landmarks while tunnels provide secret secondary connections. Scatter communal clearings and claimable dens through sheltered edges. Use layered vegetation, rock, snow, flowers, shallow crossings, natural decoration props, and a forest-and-mountain perimeter. Compose the central forest as connective foreground with major landmarks readable beyond it.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, then dress the isometric, because every biome, tunnel, and optional secret must remain connected and reachable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge but dense, with one-minute biome spacing. Theme is realistic, beautiful enchanted wilderness. Use `P2` for caves and hidden tunnels, `P3` for outside-to-den and cave transitions, `CHECK` for rivers and waterfalls, and `tiered` for exposed mountain relief.
