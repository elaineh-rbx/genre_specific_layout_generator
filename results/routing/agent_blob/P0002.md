# Scene prompt
Create a 3D creature-collecting fantasy adventure map with one central village and a compact, dense wilderness region designed for roughly 10–15 minutes of exploration. The village serves as the main settlement, with paths leading outward into the wilderness; arrange clear landmarks, creature-egg gathering nodes, Wildkin encounter areas, and rideable traversal routes throughout the surrounding terrain. Place the entrance to a small instanced dungeon within the wilderness, visually tied to the magical Shattering corruption, with a focused interior route for the introductory investigation. Populate the world with six distinct original Wildkin species and spaces suited to finding, hatching, and traveling alongside them.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG (`rpg`), because creature-party progression, crafting, equipment, and turn-based combat define the game. Adventure (`adventure`) is secondary because exploration and the introductory investigation materially organize the world.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-hub-dungeon`: one compact village-and-wilderness overworld feeds one small instanced Shattering dungeon. The Action RPG preset is the closest fit; retain its hub, encounter grounds, and enemy-emitter logic, while adding the requested egg-collection and exploration features.

## Config requirements
Draw `safezone-town` as the protected central Warden village; `social-hub` as its tightly grouped quest, crafting, and equipment services; `hazard-aggro` as Wildkin encounter clearings set away from safe travel paths; `collectible-nodes` as visible egg-gathering sites; `path-trail` as readable walking and riding routes; and `landmark-focal` as strong wilderness waypoints including the corrupted dungeon entrance. Use `npc-population` for six visually distinct original Wildkin species and the village inhabitants.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` emitters inside each designated Wildkin encounter area, away from roads and village limits. Place the functional half of `collectible-nodes` at the authored egg sites along exploration routes and near landmarks. Place the functional half of `npc-population` across species-appropriate wilderness habitats and non-hostile village ground, preserving the explicit count of six Wildkin species.

## Layout components
Build one central village, one compact dense wilderness sized for 10–15 minutes of exploration, and one small separate dungeon. Several outward trails should loop between encounter clearings, egg sites, riding routes, landmarks, and the dungeon threshold. The dungeon follows one focused interior route through Shattering-corrupted chambers. Keep the wilderness boundary natural and frame the village centrally with the dungeon landmark visible deeper in the region.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the atmospheric fantasy isometric first and treat it as authoritative, then derive each top-down, because no maze or circuit requires computed topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by one dense 10–15-minute overworld plus one small dungeon. The theme is original creature-rider fantasy with luminous magical corruption. Use `P4` for the separate dungeon map and `P3` for the linked outside-to-interior transition.
