## Enriched image prompt
Create an original fantasy creature-collecting RPG map with one central Warden village inside a compact, dense wilderness region sized for roughly 10–15 minutes of exploration. Cluster the village’s quest, egg-hatching, crafting, and equipment spaces around a safe central square. From it, thread clear riding and walking trails through encounter clearings, egg and crafting-resource sites, and distinct landmarks for six original Wildkin species. Place a conspicuous corrupted dungeon entrance in the wilderness as the route to a separate small instanced dungeon. Inside that dungeon, guide the route through a short sequence of encounter spaces to a final Shattering-investigation chamber. Keep all creature designs, architecture, landmarks, and environmental motifs original rather than copying any existing franchise.

## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG, with Adventure secondary because exploration and investigation materially organize the wilderness.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-hub-dungeon`, Hub and Dungeons: the village and wilderness share one overworld, while the small dungeon is a separate instance entered from it. The closest preset is Turn-based RPG, with its usual contiguous shape replaced by the author-requested hub-and-instanced-dungeon shape.

## Config requirements
Use `safezone-town` for the central Warden village. Use `social-hub` for its clustered hatching, crafting, equipment, and quest spaces. Use `path-trail` for readable walking and riding routes. Use `landmark-focal` for wilderness wayfinding and the corrupted dungeon entrance. Use the visible side of `collectible-nodes` for egg and crafting-resource sites, and `hazard-aggro` for Wildkin encounter clearings.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` across at least six wilderness sites, one discoverable egg-node group associated with each original Wildkin species, with additional crafting resources along trails. Place `spawner-npc` in six separated encounter clearings and at controlled points inside the dungeon, never inside the village safe zone.

## Layout components
There are two map segments: the overworld map contains the central village and one surrounding wilderness zone; the second map is the small instanced dungeon. Trails connect village, six species territories, landmarks, resource sites, and dungeon entrance. The dungeon uses a short room sequence ending in a corruption-investigation chamber. Natural terrain bounds the compact wilderness and the whole overworld remains compositionally readable.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: make the plan authoritative so village-to-trail-to-dungeon connectivity and six separated encounter territories remain playable, then dress both isometric scenes from their plans.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is large, driven by 10–15 minutes of dense exploration while keeping egg sites and landmarks readable. The theme is original high-fantasy wilderness under crystalline magical corruption. The modifiers are `P4` for the separate dungeon map and `P3` for the linked wilderness-to-dungeon interior transition.
