# Scene prompt

Create a focused circus-themed roleplay map with one main indoor big-top tent and a small surrounding entrance area. Inside the tent, arrange an open central gathering space beneath the canopy, with a distinct VIP room set off to one side; keep the space suited to character-themed roleplay and hanging out, with no show or minigame areas.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, specifically open-ended morph roleplay and social hanging out.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-bounded` from outside the genre’s usual shape set, because the whole experience is one focused venue containing a small exterior entrance and an indoor big top rather than a town or housing model. Start from the Morph Roleplay preset, substituting this shape while retaining its social gathering and focal-setting intent.

## Config requirements

Draw `social-hub` as the oversized open gathering floor beneath the big-top canopy, clear enough for character groups to mingle without bottlenecks. Draw `landmark-focal` as the visually dominant circus tent, legible from the small entrance forecourt. Add `building-interior` for the playable tent interior reached from outside, including its canopy, central floor, perimeter details, and side VIP room. The VIP room should be distinct and enclosed without becoming a separate map.

## Layout requirements

Place one uncatalogued VIP-access trigger at the VIP room doorway, aligned precisely to its threshold so access control does not affect the main gathering floor. No checkpoints, collectibles, scoring volumes, or minigame markers are needed.

## Layout components

Build a compact entrance forecourt leading directly through the tent opening into one broad central gathering zone. Set a single VIP room against one side of the tent with a short doorway connection and enough separation to read as exclusive. Use circus-patterned flooring, stylized tent supports, hanging lights, themed lounge props, and character-friendly photo backdrops without creating a show ring or minigame stations. Enclose the map with the tent envelope and a modest decorative boundary around the entrance area. Frame the tent as the exterior focal point and the central floor as the interior focal point.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: establish the authoritative circus look and connected exterior/interior composition first, then derive the top-down plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, driven by one tent and a compact entrance. The theme is surreal colorful digital circus roleplay. Use `P3` because play crosses from the outdoor entrance area into the tent interior.
