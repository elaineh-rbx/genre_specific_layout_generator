# Scene prompt

Create a bright, cartoony summer-river map with a flowing river forming the main downstream route for friends floating together. Place slush blops along both shorelines as targets, with a clear finish area at the downstream end of the river.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Shooter: the round is organized around firing water guns at visible targets while moving through the scene.

## Shape and preset

Use `route-guided`, taken from outside Shooter’s usual shapes, because the river carries players along one continuous downstream route past each target grouping. The preset is Aim Trainer, retaining its target-gallery logic but substituting the moving river route for `range-directed` and omitting the stationary weapon bench.

## Config requirements

Use `target-practice` as bright slush-blop targets clearly presented along both banks at readable water-gun range. Draw `water-body` as the broad, continuously flowing river that is both the route and the play surface. Add a conspicuous downstream finish structure spanning or framing the river.

## Layout requirements

Place the functional `target-practice` hit targets along alternating shoreline positions, facing the river and never hidden behind bank geometry. Their exact count was not stated. Place a shared player-and-float start region at the upstream end and a finish trigger across the full navigable river width at the downstream marker. Target scoring belongs to the targets themselves rather than a separate terrain volume.

## Layout components

Build one continuous river corridor running from an upstream launch pool to a downstream finish. Curve it gently enough that upcoming target groups remain readable. Low grassy banks border both sides, with clusters of cartoon slush blops set on clear shoreline pads. Keep the water channel wide enough for friends to float abreast and leave unobstructed firing sightlines to both banks. Use simple summer vegetation, rounded rocks, and sandy edges without enclosing the route. Frame the composition downstream so the river leads visually toward the finish.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative, then derive the top-down plan; the cheerful river scenery and target visibility lead, and the route is a simple continuous channel rather than computed topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a medium scale, long enough for several target encounters but still one round-length river. The theme is bright, playful cartoon summer. Apply `CHECK` because players travel on a water play surface; verify that the navigable envelope does not self-occlude.
