# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game layout with a large separate spawn-building lobby. To the player's left is a summoning area, with an inventory area nearby for equipped units, and a teleporter sized for up to five players that leads into the match. The match is a tiny city built as one small street: a central pathway runs between the Camera Base on one side and the toilet enemy spawn side opposite it, forming a single cooperative lane. The lane supports ten advancing wave sections, ending at the far spawn end with a boss encounter area.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Strategy: this is a cooperative tower-defense game built around placing units against ten enemy waves.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-actor-track`, with one continuous, unbranching street from the toilet wave origin to the Camera Base. The preset is Tower Defense, retaining its lane, placement ground, core, and wave origin but omitting the unrequested bottleneck.

## Config requirements
`buildzone-grid` draws flat unit-placement strips along both sides of the city street. `tracker-core` draws the Camera Base at the defended end. The large spawn-building lobby must visibly contain a summoning area on the left, a nearby inventory area, and a five-person teleporter; the separate match map is a miniature city streetscape.

## Layout requirements
Place one `tracker-core` objective marker on the Camera Base and one `spawner-npc` at the opposite end of the lane. Place one player arrival group for up to five players near the Camera Base, one teleporter trigger in the lobby teleporter, and ten ordered wave-section markers along the street, with the tenth at the boss encounter area.

## Layout components
Build two separate zones: a large enclosed spawn-building lobby and a tiny one-street city match. In the lobby, the summoning area sits immediately left of spawn, inventory space is adjacent, and the teleporter accommodates five players. In the match, a straight central pathway links the Camera Base to the toilet spawn, with buildable sidewalks on both sides and ten readable advancing sections. Buildings form the street boundary; the composition keeps the entire lane legible from an elevated three-quarter view.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the valid, continuous enemy route is authoritative, then the isometric view dresses that plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium because the large lobby and small match occupy two maps. The theme is stylized miniature Skibidi-inspired urban defense. Use `P6` because the enemy lane must be valid by construction, plus `P4` because the lobby and city are separate maps.
