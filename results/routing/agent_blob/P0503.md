# Scene prompt
Create a simple cartoon-style 3D tower defense map with a small, compact play area. A single unbranched lane forms a short winding track across the map, with clear open ground alongside the route for placing 3D unit towers; keep the path and units visually readable.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Strategy, specifically a straightforward tower-defense game.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-actor-track`: one short, continuous, unbranched enemy lane winds across the compact map. The preset is Tower Defense, retaining the placement ground, core, and wave origin while omitting the preset’s unrequested bottleneck.

## Config requirements
`buildzone-grid` draws clear, flat tower-placement strips and pads along both sides of the winding track, sized for readable 3D unit towers. `tracker-core` draws a simple cartoon defended base at the route’s endpoint. Keep scenery sparse and low so enemies, placed units, and the entire path remain visually distinct; tower costs and upgrades are mechanics rather than geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place one `tracker-core` objective marker on the defended base and one `spawner-npc` at the opposite end as the wave origin. Place the player arrival point beside the route without blocking it. Keep every segment of the enemy lane connected, unambiguous, and separated from the tower pads, and make all placement ground reachable from the arrival area.

## Layout components
Build one small, clearly bounded arena on level ground. The enemy lane enters at one edge, makes a few broad readable bends, and terminates at the base on the opposite side. Open deployment ground follows both sides without narrowing the route. Use simple grass or colored ground, rounded rocks, low shrubs, and a few stylized signs as sparse props. A low wall or soft terrain rim encloses the play space. Frame the isometric composition high enough to show the complete short route and all major placement areas at once.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the enemy lane must be valid and continuous before the isometric cartoon dressing is applied.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small because the first build is intentionally compact with a short track. The theme is simple, bright cartoon 3D with strong silhouettes and clean color separation. Use `P6` because the enemy route must be valid by construction.
