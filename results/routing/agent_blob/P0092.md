# Scene prompt

Create a very long, flat 3D obby course that extends forward through successive tiger-themed sections. Arrange a continuous route of platforming areas along the course, including wall-hop passages, moving and disappearing platforms, narrow tightropes, and conveyor sections, with tiger-themed scenery and props framing the path.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Obby & Platformer. Player-controlled traversal through discrete movement challenges is the entire game.

## Shape and preset

Use `course-flat`, a very long point-to-point platform course extending horizontally through successive themed sections. Start from the Classic Obby preset, retaining its sequential track, checkpoints, and moving obstacles, while adding the explicitly requested timed and climbing obstacle families.

## Config requirements

Draw `path-track` as one continuous, ordered platform chain. Use `obstacle-climb` for wall-hop passages and narrow tightropes, `obstacle-moving` for moving platforms and conveyors, and `obstacle-timing` for platforms that disappear after activation or contact. Tiger-striped surfaces, jungle-orange-and-black scenery, paw motifs, and tiger props should frame the route without obscuring its readable critical path. Draw the visible pads for `checkpoint-respawn` and a clearly celebratory endpoint for `winner-zone`.

## Layout requirements

Place `checkpoint-respawn` markers on flat safe pads at regular section boundaries throughout the unusually long course, with each marker returning a failed player to the latest completed section. Place one `winner-zone` at the far end of the final tiger-themed section. Wall-hop geometry is author-supplied because glitch-style wallhopping cannot be validated against the normal jump baseline.

## Layout components

The build is a single narrow, forward-running course made of many successive tiger-themed platforming zones. Connect them directly in order: wall-hop passages lead into moving-platform runs, disappearing-platform sequences, tightropes, and conveyor sections, with safe checkpoint pads separating demanding clusters. Keep all ordinary critical-path gaps within obby physics limits, while treating the requested wallhops as bespoke authored obstacles. The ground treatment is a void or non-playable fall field beneath the track; tiger scenery and props flank rather than block it. Frame the course diagonally so its extreme horizontal length and ordered progression remain visible, with the finish readable in the distance.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: render the top-down plan first because an invalid or disconnected obstacle chain would make the course unplayable, then dress the authoritative plan isometrically.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is huge because the author repeatedly requests a very long course. The theme is bold tiger stripes, jungle accents, and playful feline props. Use `P6` because platform spacing and continuous route validity must be guaranteed by construction; the bespoke wallhops remain an explicit validation exception.
