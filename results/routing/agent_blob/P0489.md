# Scene prompt
Create a bright, cartoony 3D story game lobby as a small plaza hub. Arrange several simple, welcoming buildings around the plaza, with open paths between them and a clear central gathering area for players before a shared story round.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure because this is explicitly the lobby for one shared story journey, not a minigame collection.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded`, taken from outside Adventure's usual list, because the requested build is one compact, single-level plaza containing the entire lobby activity. The preset is none; Adventure's Story preset describes the journey itself rather than this simple pre-round hub.

## Config requirements
Use `gate-chapter` for one visually obvious departure gate or doorway that marks entry into the shared story round. Draw several simple cartoony building facades around an uncluttered central plaza, with broad open paths and welcoming signage. Do not add multiple portals, chapter choices, quest boards, or interiors because the brief specifies one story and a simple lobby.

## Layout requirements
Place player spawn markers around the perimeter of the central gathering area, facing inward without blocking paths. Place one shared round-queue or departure trigger directly before the single story gate. Site one central gathering volume in the plaza with enough clear floor for the whole group; no separate chapter-selection markers are needed.

## Layout components
Build one small plaza as the main zone, with a clear central gathering circle and several simple buildings ringing its edges. Short, wide paved paths connect each facade to the gathering area and converge on the single story-round gate. Keep the terrain flat, use bright paving and trimmed cartoon landscaping, and enclose the lobby with the surrounding facades and a soft decorative boundary. Frame the plaza from a raised three-quarter view with the gathering circle central and the departure gate immediately legible.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative, then derive the top-down view, because the welcoming visual composition matters more than constrained topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, as requested for a simple plaza hub. The theme is bright, welcoming, and cartoony Roblox story-game architecture. Use `P0`; the lobby is one flat, contiguous outdoor surface with no forced extra capture pass.
