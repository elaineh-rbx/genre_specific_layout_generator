# Scene prompt
Create a massive seamless 3D open-world RPG map on one continuous surface, with four distinct mega-biomes naturally connected around The Nexus Palace at the center. The neutral central hub is a majestic stone-and-marble palace with subtle gold accents, visually distinct from every surrounding biome. Each biome feels large but remains compact enough to cross on foot in roughly 3–5 minutes.

Around the hub, build a dense neon futuristic metropolis with towering skyscrapers, holographic advertisements, elevated hover-car lanes, rain-dark asphalt streets with reflective puddles, and accessible underground slums of neon signs and rusty pipes; place a colossal robot boss arena in the city center. Connect it to a mystical forest of giant ancient trees with glowing purple leaves, neon-blue bioluminescent rivers, glowing mushrooms, and fog between trunks. Include a diesel-punk desert wasteland of sand, rusted shipwrecks, half-buried mechanical gears, and iron-sheet scavenger outposts. The fourth region is a celestial kingdom of sky islands, white marble castles with gold trim, Greek-style temples, floating waterfalls dropping into open sky, cloud platforms, and broad aerial space around the sky castles for a massive flying dragon encounter. Use changing daylight and biome-specific atmospheric weather to reinforce the city rain, forest magic storms, desert sandstorms, and kingdom thunderstorms.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG (`rpg`), driven by levels, loot, skills, quests, and combat progression. Action (`action`) is secondary because traversal combat and four world-boss encounters materially shape the space.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open-biomes` from the Open World & Survival RPG preset, but omit its player build plots and override its usual separate-map route: the author explicitly requires one seamless continuous surface. Four radial biome sectors surround the central Nexus Palace.

## Config requirements
Draw `safezone-town` as the neutral palace hub; `social-hub` as its compact quest and equipment core; `hazard-aggro` as broad enemy and boss grounds set off safe routes; `landmark-focal` as the palace, skyscrapers, shipwrecks, and sky castles; `building-interior` as accessible underground slums; `water-body` as forest rivers and floating waterfalls; `terrain-relief` as raised islands and biome transitions; and `island-cluster` as the celestial kingdom. Use `cover-los` around combat grounds and `hazard-kill` at exposed sky-island edges.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` points inside each biome’s enemy areas and one boss encounter center in each of the four biomes. Place the functional side of `island-cluster` nowhere separately; its islands are drawn geometry. No build plots are required.

## Layout components
Build one central marble palace and four connected mega-biomes, each a 3–5-minute crossing. Radial roads and traversal routes connect the hub to metropolis, forest, wasteland, and sky kingdom without portals. Include city streets, elevated lanes, underground slums, a city-center robot arena, glowing forest rivers, desert outposts and wrecks, and aerial islands with castles, temples, waterfalls, cloud platforms, and dragon airspace. Keep an outer world boundary beyond all four sectors and compose the palace as the central visual anchor.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: render the connected plan first and make it authoritative, because all four biome routes must join one hub without breaking seamless traversal.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge, driven by four large regions and a central hub. The theme is a sci-fi/fantasy multiverse with strong biome contrast. Use `P2` for underground slums and elevated sky structures, `P3` for street-to-slum transitions, and `CHECK` for volumetric sky-island and waterfall play. Do not use `P4`; seamlessness explicitly overrides it.
