# Scene prompt
Create a 3D rainbow obby map as a small, flat ground-level course about a minute long, ending in a simple winner area that clearly marks completion.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant and only genre is Obby & Platformer because the author explicitly requests an obby.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-flat` for a short course progressing horizontally at ground level. Preset is none: the brief does not choose the Classic Obby preset’s moving obstacles or checkpoints, so adding that bundle would overstate a deliberately simple map.

## Config requirements
Apply `path-track` as one short, sequential chain of physics-legal rainbow platforms leading directly to the end. Draw `winner-zone` as a simple, unmistakable completion area beyond the final platform. Use a clear rainbow colour progression across successive course sections without inventing unrelated scenery or obstacle families.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional side of `winner-zone` once at the terminal end of the route, on a safe flat pad that the player reaches only after clearing the final platform. No checkpoint placement is needed for an approximately one-minute course because the author did not request checkpoints and the short length makes a full restart reasonable.

## Layout components
Build one small, flat, ground-level obstacle course with a safe start pad, a concise sequence of walkable and jumpable platforms, and one simple winner area at the far end. Arrange the platform colours in an orderly rainbow progression so forward movement is visually obvious. Keep the route largely horizontal, with only minor platform-height changes needed to make the jumps readable; do not turn it into a hill or tower. Every critical gap must stay within supported avatar jump limits, with enough landing depth to recover before the next jump. Use a clean outer boundary or contrasting non-play ground to separate the course from its surroundings. Compose the full one-minute route in a single readable frame with start and finish clearly opposed.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the short platform chain and legal gap spacing must be valid before rainbow materials are applied.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, explicitly set by an approximately one-minute run. The theme is clean, bright rainbow colour blocking. Use `P6` because even this simple obby must be valid by construction; no additional modifier is forced.
