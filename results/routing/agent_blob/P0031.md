## Enriched image prompt

Keep the animals' world entirely natural, with no settlement, camp, town, roads, or human buildings. Shape the valley with varied terrain and broad exploration space so it feels expansive without becoming a 2000-plus-stud region. Include natural dens or nests tucked into the valley edges, a memorable natural formation visible across the valley as an orientation landmark, and one broad natural clearing where animal characters can gather without making it look like a built social plaza. Use rising valley sides and layered ground relief while keeping the central floor and routes between natural features open and walkable.
