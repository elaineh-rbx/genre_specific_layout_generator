# Scene prompt
Create a 3D role-play map of a detailed single fantasy valley that feels expansive through varied terrain and open exploration space. The valley is a wild landscape for customizable fantasy animals, with no settlement or town; arrange distinct natural areas across the valley with traversable terrain, framed by surrounding slopes or mountains.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim because players customize fantasy-animal avatars and inhabit the landscape for open-ended roleplay.

## Shape and preset
Use the single `wilderness-open` shape: one natural valley with no settlement, housing, or personal plots, freely explored by animal players. It comes from the Animal Sim preset, adjusted to omit unrequested dens, formal gathering hub, and fixed landmark.

## Config requirements
Apply `terrain-relief` to create the varied valley floor and surrounding slopes or mountains, keeping all exposed terrain on one non-overhanging surface. Draw several visually distinct natural areas across the valley through changes in ground material, vegetation density, color, and elevation, while preserving broad open exploration space. Do not add settlement buildings, roads, shops, or NPC animals.

## Layout requirements
After segmentation, place several player spawn markers on broad, navigable ground in different parts of the valley so customizable fantasy-animal forms can enter without overlapping. Each spawn needs a clear route into the central open terrain and enough clearance for varied avatar body shapes. No pickups, hostile spawners, checkpoints, claim markers, scoring volumes, or winner zone are needed because the roleplay has no fixed objective.

## Layout components
Build one detailed valley composed of a broad central floor, gently raised side areas, several natural subareas, and mountain or slope boundaries. Connect the subareas with wide traversable terrain and subtle natural trails rather than formal roads. Use fantasy vegetation, grasses, flowers, rocks, small ridges, and scattered tree groups to create variety without closing the sightlines or fragmenting the map. Keep the perimeter enclosed by steeper surrounding slopes or mountains. Compose the scene with open valley ground in the foreground and layered terrain rising toward the enclosing skyline so the single map feels expansive.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down image, because visual terrain variety and expansive fantasy composition lead while the open valley topology is forgiving.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large despite being one valley, driven by the requested sense of expansive exploration rather than a 2000-plus-stud region. The theme is wild, colorful fantasy nature. Use `tiered` for non-overhanging valley relief; no other modifier is forced.
