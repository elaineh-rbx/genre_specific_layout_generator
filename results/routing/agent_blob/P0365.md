# Scene prompt

Build a compact lap circuit, designed for roughly thirty-second laps, looping along the edge of a mountain and passing a waterfall that flows off the cliffside. Place a clear four-kart-wide starting grid on the track; arrange the course with mountain terrain on one side and open cliffside views on the other.
