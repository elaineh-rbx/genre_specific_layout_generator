# Scene prompt
Create a beautiful, compact arcade-themed game map for up to 8 players. A central lobby serves as the spawn area before matches, with a visible shop area for aura purchases. Connect the lobby to a separate arcade arena containing three quick mini-game zones: a coin-collecting race course, an obstacle-course race, and a target-hitting game area, arranged as distinct colorful arcade attractions within the arena.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Party & Casual because players rotate through quick score-based minigames. Racing is secondary because two attractions are finite competitive courses.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-staged`, the Lobby and Isolated Stage, because the central lobby and arcade arena must be separate match spaces. Use the Minigame preset, retaining lobby-to-stage transport and scoring placement while omitting unrequested leaderboard and spectator structures.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for the compact eight-player lobby and its visible aura shop. Use `teleporter-link` for the distinct lobby-to-arena connection. In the arena, use `lane-corridor` to make both race routes visually unmistakable, `startpoint-line` at each course entrance, and `trigger-finish` as each course’s visible finish gate. Draw exactly three colorful attractions: coin race, obstacle race, and a directed target-hitting bay with targets safely facing away from circulation.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional halves of the paired `teleporter-link` in the lobby and arena. Place one `trigger-scoring` completion region in each of the three minigame zones. Place exactly two `startpoint-line` markers and two `trigger-finish` volumes, one pair per race. Add eight `spawn-grid` slots at each race start, `checkpoint-respawn` markers along the obstacle race, coin pickups along risky but reachable lines in the coin race, and target-hit detectors throughout the target bay.

## Layout components
Build two separate maps: a compact lobby with central spawn, aura shop, and arena transfer point; and one arcade arena divided into exactly three adjacent attractions. Give each zone a distinct color identity and entrance marquee while preserving shared circulation around them. Keep both race courses continuous and non-crossing, and orient the target bay downrange behind a safe firing line. Enclose the arena like a polished arcade hall and frame all three attractions together.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: render the top-down first and make it authoritative because both race routes must remain connected, distinct, and playable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale for eight players, a lobby, and three compact attractions. The theme is vibrant polished arcade spectacle. `P4` is required for separate lobby and arena maps, and `P6` for valid race routes.
