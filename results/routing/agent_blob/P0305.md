# Scene prompt
Create a 3D cosmic multiverse map with each player beginning in a small private floating sanctuary garden under pink skies: lush green fields and floating crystal pillars surround a central Portal Temple. The temple serves as the hub connection to five distinct outer planets and three alternate universes—Alpha, Chaos, and Omega—each presented as a corrupted cosmic planet with fractured floating terrain, dark energy cracks, hostile presences, and a central Temporal Fracture. Differentiate all eight destinations through simple color palettes and terrain variations.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG, with Adventure secondary: rank progression and repeated combat-zone clearing define the game, while the multiverse journey supplies its exploratory structure.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-hub-dungeon`, interpreted as one private sanctuary hub feeding eight separate corrupted destination maps. The closest preset is Action RPG; its hub, hostile areas, and emitters remain recognisable, with the prompt’s portal and floating-world details added.

## Config requirements
Draw `safezone-town` as the enemy-free sanctuary garden around the Portal Temple, despite its compact non-urban form. Use `hazard-aggro` for hostile ground on every corrupted planet, keeping danger away from arrival points. Show `teleporter-link` as eight clearly differentiated portal connections within the temple. Use `island-cluster` for fractured floating landmasses divided by open air. Include eight central Temporal Fractures as dark-energy objectives, one visually dominant in each destination.

## Layout requirements
Place `teleporter-link` endpoints so the central temple has eight destinations, with one matching arrival marker on each outer map. Place `spawner-npc` emitters within the hostile terrain of all eight destinations, set back from arrivals and clustered around approaches to their central fractures. No additional ambient population is required.

## Layout components
The hub is a small circular floating garden: a central Portal Temple, lush green field, scattered floating crystal pillars, and a clean outer edge dropping into pink-sky void. It connects to five outer planets and three alternate universes named Alpha, Chaos, and Omega. Each destination is a separate corrupted cosmic-planet arena made from fractured floating terrain, dark energy cracks, hostile approach ground, and one central Temporal Fracture. Give all eight maps distinct but simple palette and terrain treatments. Frame the hub around its temple and each destination around its fracture.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: topology comes first so every arrival, hostile approach, and central objective remains connected on each fragmented map; the top-down plan is authoritative before isometric dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is huge because the build contains a hub plus eight destinations. The theme is luminous sacred-cosmic sanctuary against corrupted fractured worlds. Use `P4` for genuinely separate maps, `P3` for linked hub-to-destination transitions, and `CHECK` because layered floating islands and flight may self-occlude.
