# Scene prompt
Create a 3D exploration map on a tropical terrain island bounded by water. Sandy beaches ring lush grassy interior terrain with palm trees, rocks, and light jungle foliage. Hide 10 unique ornate custom-modeled treasure chests across the island terrain, including among foliage and around natural land features.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure because the player searches an open island until every hidden treasure chest is collected.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, one contiguous tropical island searched in any order. The matched preset is Scavenger Hunt, retaining its open exploration shape, collectible placement, and secluded discovery spaces while omitting a physical quest board because the compass and counter are UI.

## Config requirements
Use `alcove-secret` for small concealed pockets among foliage, palms, rocks, and natural land features. Use `water-body` for the sea that bounds the island. As a free-text image requirement, draw exactly 10 ornate custom-modeled 3D treasure chests, each visibly unique rather than a recolored plain block, while keeping their placements partially concealed.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly 10 `collectible-nodes`, each aligned to one of the 10 rendered chest props. Distribute them across beach edges, grassy interior ground, palm clusters, rocks, and light-jungle pockets; every chest must be reachable and interactable, and no single viewpoint should reveal the whole set. The nearest-chest compass and found counter are UI and require no physical placement.

## Layout components
Build one compact tropical terrain island with a complete sandy beach ring around a lush grassy interior. Scatter palm trees, rocks, and light jungle foliage to form ten distinct but fair hiding contexts. The enclosing ocean is the hard outer boundary. Keep circulation open enough to search freely without adding a formal trail. Compose the frame to show the whole island character while foliage and natural features still screen individual chest sites.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down view; chest craftsmanship, tropical dressing, and concealment lead, while the island topology is uncomplicated.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small to medium, driven by 10 well-spaced chest sites. The theme is lush tropical treasure exploration with ornate handcrafted props. Use `CHECK` for the surrounding water volume; the island itself remains one continuous surface with no extra route.
