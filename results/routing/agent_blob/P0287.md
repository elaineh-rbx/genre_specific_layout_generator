# Scene prompt
Create a 3D gritty, realistic urban city map for free roaming across a few connected city blocks. Lay out intersecting streets bordered by sidewalks, with building facades lining each block and open pedestrian paths along the street edges for NPC foot traffic.

# Agent decision

## Genre
No Genre. The requested foundation is a free-roam city and ambient pedestrian population, with no missions, scoring, combat, jobs, or other loop that would establish a game genre.

## Shape and preset
There is no genre shape. Keep all five routing axes at their defaults: exterior enclosure, single-surface verticality, one coexisting zone, dressed rather than topology-critical structure, and grounded-surface play. Preset is none because Explorable Place is closest but its landmark and scenic-vignette bundle was not requested.

## Config requirements
Use `path-circulation` for the connected street-and-sidewalk network, with pedestrian movement given an obvious grain around each block. Use `settlement-density` for a compact urban fabric of contiguous building facades across a few blocks. Use `npc-population` to depict non-hostile pedestrians and the sidewalk space they inhabit. Use `boundary-edge` to close the small test district behind believable continuation streets or dense facade edges rather than an exposed void.

## Layout requirements
For `npc-population`, place pedestrian occupancy and walking routes only along the sidewalks bordering each street, continuing through corners and crossings without routing NPCs through the roadway or facade walls. The prompt gives no pedestrian count, so preserve a natural sparse-to-moderate city presence without inventing an exact number. The same option appears in the image config for visible pedestrians and here for their occupied ground.

## Layout components
Build a small grid of a few connected city blocks with intersecting roads, continuous curb lines, and sidewalks on both sides. Gritty realistic facade rows define each block edge, while corners remain open enough for readable pedestrian turns and crossings. Streets connect through the district rather than terminating abruptly. The terrain is level paved urban ground with distinct asphalt, curb, and sidewalk materials. Buildings are exterior facades only; no enterable interiors are requested. Frame the district from an oblique aerial view that clearly shows the block network and multiple uninterrupted sidewalk loops.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, because visual city character and facade composition lead while the straightforward street plan can be projected from them.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by the requested few-block prototype. The theme is gritty, realistic contemporary urbanism. Use `P0`: one exterior surface and ordinary circulation require no special capture pass.
