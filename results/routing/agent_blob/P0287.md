# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] theme — Q: Should the city read as gritty and realistic, or bright and stylized? A: Use a gritty, realistic urban city look to match the grand theft auto-style premise.
- [author] scale — Q: How large should the first city be - a few blocks to test movement on, or a full district? A: Start with a few connected city blocks for a small, playable movement and NPC foundation.
- [author] goal — Q: Once the city exists, what do players do in it - missions, free roam, jobs? A: Free roam for now; the prompt only calls for the city and walking NPC foundation, with gameplay added later.

## Enriched image prompt
Create an original gritty, realistic urban free-roam foundation spanning a few connected city blocks. Lay out a compact street grid with continuous sidewalks on both sides, safe corners and crossings, alleys between buildings, and enough open pavement for a basic avatar to move comfortably. Line the blocks with varied realistic city facades and street-level details, while keeping buildings exterior-only for this first pass. Populate the sidewalk space with ordinary pedestrian areas distributed throughout the blocks, leaving roads clear. Use weathered concrete, asphalt, brick, muted signage, streetlights, utility details, and restrained urban greenery. Frame the complete connected road-and-sidewalk network as a coherent small city without copying a named game’s exact city, characters, or assets.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Action because the author explicitly frames this as the city foundation for an open-world crime-action game, although the current slice is free roam only.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, an Open World shape taken from outside Action’s usual set, because the first build is one contiguous cluster of city blocks explored in any order. No Action preset fits the mechanics-free city foundation without adding combat geometry, so the preset is none.

## Config requirements
Use `settlement-density` for a compact built-up group of several city blocks. Use `path-street`, taken from the Roleplay & Avatar Sim catalogue, for continuous pedestrian sidewalks, crossings, and alleys. Use the visible side of `npc-population` for ordinary pedestrians occupying those sidewalks. Missions, combat, vehicles, and jobs are deferred rather than inferred.

## Layout requirements
Place `npc-population` as pedestrian Zone placements along sidewalks on every block, concentrated near corners and storefront frontage but excluded from roads and alleys too narrow for safe circulation. No hostile spawners or mission triggers are required.

## Layout components
The map contains a few connected city blocks, a compact street grid, continuous sidewalks, crossings, alleys, exterior-only building facades, streetlights, signs, utilities, and pedestrian occupancy zones. Roads and sidewalks connect across every block, while blocked outer streets form the boundary. Keep the whole small-city foundation visible and readable.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: make the connected street and sidewalk plan authoritative for reliable free roaming and pedestrian circulation, then dress the gritty isometric city from it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is large because several blocks must fit while sidewalks, crossings, and NPC areas remain legible. The theme is gritty realistic contemporary urbanism. The pipeline cost is `P0`: the build is one exterior surface with no enterable interiors, overhang route, or required procedural topology.
