# Scene prompt
Create a 3D zombie survival map centered on a lived-in, modern house, with open approaches around the building for attacking zombies.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Survival is dominant, with Shooter secondary because the player fights zombie waves using guns while holding the house.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded`: one house lot contains the entire wave-survival round, keeping the defenders and incoming zombies close. The preset is Horde Defense. Its bounded arena and hostile origins are retained, while destructible structures and specialized hiding cutouts are dropped because they were not requested.

## Config requirements
Use `building-interior` for a lived-in modern house that players can enter from the surrounding yard and use as shelter during the attack. Draw the furnished, intact home as an active residence rather than an abandoned ruin. Surround it with open ground on multiple sides, keeping the exterior approaches broad and readable rather than cluttered with extra defenses or props.

## Layout requirements
Use `spawner-npc` for zombie wave origins around the outer edge of the lot, with several separated approach directions leading toward the house. Place one unkeyed player spawn inside the house near the main living area, with a direct route to the primary exterior door. Hostile origins must remain outside and out of immediate sight of the spawn.

## Layout components
The central zone is one compact modern house with a usable interior, front door, and a small set of connected lived-in rooms. An open yard wraps around the building as the exterior combat zone. Direct ground-level paths connect each side of the lot to doors or windows around the house, allowing zombies to approach from multiple directions. Flat grass or suburban ground forms the terrain, and a simple property edge bounds the map without blocking approach lanes. Compose the scene with the house dominant in the center and open attack space visible around it.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the intact modern-home appearance and readable surrounding approaches are authoritative, followed by linked top-downs for exterior and interior placement.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, one house and its lot. The theme is a lived-in modern suburban home under active zombie siege. The required modifier is `P3` because play crosses between the exterior yard and enterable house interior.
