# Scene prompt

Create a 3D music-performance scene focused tightly on a punk rock star performing on a stage, viewed by a camera locked toward the stage and its immediate frontage. The star stands center stage with a large background screen directly behind them, while equipped show effects such as VFX, laser beams, and fireworks frame the performance around the stage.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Entertainment: the performance itself is the content, presented as a non-walkable 3D show set.

## Shape and preset

Use `set-display` from the Display Piece preset. The stage is arranged around the punk rock star as its central subject and is operated from a locked external viewpoint rather than traversed by an avatar.

## Config requirements

Draw `landmark-focal` as the compact hero composition of one punk rock star centered on a raised stage with a large background screen directly behind them. Use `zone-graphics` for dense but isolated show-effect clusters: VFX framing the performer, lateral laser beams, and fireworks positioned around and above the stage without obscuring the star or screen. Add `npc-population` for the single non-hostile performer occupying center stage.

## Layout requirements

Place the layout half of `npc-population` as exactly one performer marker at center stage, facing the locked camera and standing in front of the background screen. No avatar spawn, traversal markers, checkpoints, or audience volumes are required because the player operates the show through the interface.

## Layout components

Build one tight performance zone consisting of a raised punk stage and its immediate frontage. Center one performer mark on the stage; mount one large screen directly behind it. Reserve clear effect bands at the left and right edges for laser emitters and VFX, and an overhead or rear safety band for firework emitters. Use rugged speakers, truss, cables, dark decking, and punk-rock dressing as supporting props without expanding into a full venue or backstage. Enclose the composition with a dark neutral set boundary and frame it symmetrically from the locked frontal camera.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: create the authoritative dressed 3D set first, then derive the top-down placement reference, since the composition rather than traversable topology leads.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, limited to one stage and immediate frontage. The theme is high-energy punk rock with neon lasers and pyrotechnics. Use `P0` with `SET`: the scene is real geometry, but nobody walks through it, so traversal checks are skipped.
