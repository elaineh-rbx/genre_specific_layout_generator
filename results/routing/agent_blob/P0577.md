# Scene prompt

Create a fully walkable 3D ocean-liner map named RMS Willow of the Sea, a polished Edwardian-style ship in good condition with a red-and-white ferry-liner appearance. The vessel is a few hundred studs long, with a long hull, two red funnels, two masts, and 75 lifeboats arranged along the exterior deck edges. Include walkable exterior decks from bow to stern, plus a simple interior layout containing cabin areas and the bridge near the forward upper deck.

# Agent decision

## Genre

This is `No Genre`: the request defines a walkable ship environment but no game loop, scoring, enemies, or objective.

## Shape and preset

There is no genre shape. Set `enclosure` to transition because players move between exterior decks and interior cabins and bridge. Set `verticality` to stacked because multiple decks and upper structures overlap. Leave `zone-count`, `structure`, and `play-space` at their defaults. The preset is none.

## Config requirements

Use `building-interior` for simple cabin areas and the bridge linked from the exterior decks. Use `water-body` for open sea surrounding the vessel. Draw the RMS Willow of the Sea as a polished red-and-white Edwardian ferry liner a few hundred studs long, with exactly two red funnels, two masts, and exactly 75 lifeboats distributed along the exterior deck edges. Keep bow-to-stern deck circulation visibly clear.

## Layout requirements

No invisible gameplay placements are requested. Do not add spawns, checkpoints, triggers, pickups, or teleports. The linked exterior and interior captures must preserve doorway and stair alignment so every represented deck area remains walkable.

## Layout components

The primary zone is the long ship, surrounded by open sea. Exterior paths run continuously from bow to stern and connect by stairs between stacked decks. Interior paths branch into simple cabin areas and the forward upper-deck bridge, then reconnect to exterior access points. Place two funnels along the centerline, two masts fore and aft, and 75 lifeboats in orderly rows along both deck edges. The hull and railings bound movement. Frame the full profile while exposing deck organization.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: establish connected deck, stair, cabin, and bridge circulation first, then dress the isometric, because disconnected access would violate the fully walkable requirement. This is not a maze or circuit, so `authored_plan` does not apply.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is large, driven by a liner a few hundred studs long and 75 lifeboats. The theme is polished Edwardian red-and-white ocean liner. Use `P2` for overlapping decks, `P3` for exterior-to-interior transitions, and `CHECK` for the surrounding water volume; it should remain non-self-occluding.
