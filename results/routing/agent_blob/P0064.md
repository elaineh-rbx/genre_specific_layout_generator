# Scene prompt
Create a true 3D boss-battle arena with an open ground-level combat space, composed for a first-person or over-the-shoulder view. A single large, unique fearsome boss monster stands and moves within the arena as a detailed custom 3D mesh creature, with clear space around it for ranged combat.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Action, with Shooter secondary because the encounter is a physical boss fight whose arena must also support sustained ranged sightlines.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded` for one open, ground-level clash space with room to circle a large target. Match the Boss Raid preset, retaining its bounded arena and NPC emitter while dropping its breakable-cover and ring-out-hazard options because the brief asks for clear ranged-combat ground and names neither feature.

## Config requirements
The image config must include one large, unique fearsome boss as a detailed custom 3D mesh creature, not primitive blocks or a flat sprite. Give it a distinctive silhouette and keep the surrounding combat floor visually open so first-person and over-the-shoulder aiming remains readable. No additional canonical image option is required.

## Layout requirements
Apply `spawner-npc` as exactly one boss spawn marker near the far-central portion of the arena, with a broad unobstructed movement radius around it. Place one player spawn at the opposing side, outside the boss's immediate attack radius but with a direct view into the arena. No waves, pickups, scoring volumes, or additional enemy emitters are requested.

## Layout components
Build a single broad arena floor sized around one large moving boss and one mobile ranged player. The central and near-central ground stays open, with no narrow passages or small collision clutter. A strong enclosing wall or natural arena rim defines the boundary and keeps the encounter readable, while leaving generous lateral space for dodging and circling. Put the boss's initial position beyond the centerline and the player's approach at the opposite edge. Use durable, large-scale floor and boundary forms that frame the custom creature without competing with it. Compose the isometric image to show the entire combat envelope and preserve a clear eye-level firing relationship between the two starting positions.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive top-down, because creature presentation and arena scale lead while the single open floor has no fragile topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, driven by clearance for one oversized boss and ranged evasive movement. The theme is fearsome cinematic monster combat. Use `P0` because the encounter occupies one flat, bounded surface without overhangs or transitions.
