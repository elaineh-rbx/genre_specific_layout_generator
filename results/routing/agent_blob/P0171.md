# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map showing a compact, clear cartoon-adventure escape tower with three stacked floors, each a visually distinct themed challenge room. Players begin on the first floor; each floor has a readable objective area and a physical elevator or stair route that opens upward to the next floor. The third floor leads to a top-level exit. Include at least one physical damage hazard and a single revival-item pickup placed somewhere inside the tower. Design the tower for multiple players to occupy and attempt simultaneously, with enough open circulation space around each floor’s challenge area and vertical connections.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Puzzle: players clear three ordered challenge rooms to unlock upward progression and escape.

## Shape and preset
Use `rooms-sequence` for exactly three enclosed rooms worked through in order, vertically stacked inside one tower. The preset is Escape Room.

## Config requirements
Use `facade-clue` for readable objective displays, `button-solve` for a first-floor jungle-rune tile mechanism and second-floor cartoon workshop levers, `trigger-solve` for third-floor sky-temple item sockets, and `gate-progression` for the elevator or stair barriers between floors. Draw one clearly visible physical damage hazard in the second-floor workshop.

## Layout requirements
Place `button-solve` interactions on the first two floors and `trigger-solve` sockets on the third. Place one `collectible-nodes` revival-item pickup in a visible side alcove on floor two, reachable without blocking progression. Place damage volumes against the workshop hazard. Provide one start area on floor one, one upward unlock per floor, and one completion trigger at the floor-three top exit, all with room for simultaneous players.

## Layout components
Build three stacked floors, each with one open challenge area and perimeter circulation. Floor one is a green jungle-ruin room with rune tiles; floor two is a bright mechanical workshop with levers and a damage hazard; floor three is a blue-and-gold sky-temple room with sockets leading to the rooftop exit. Link floors physically by elevators or stairs, and enclose the tower with a clear outer shell.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first as authoritative using a separate plan for each floor, then dress the isometric, because vertical stacking would hide lower-floor circulation and unlock paths.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, fixed by three compact multiplayer floors. The theme is clear cartoon adventure with strongly differentiated rooms. Use `P2` because the three playable floors overhang one another.
