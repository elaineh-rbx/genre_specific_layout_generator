# Scene prompt

Create a compact 3D tropical pirate island map, sized to cross in a few minutes, with explorable terrain and 10 hidden, unique ornate custom-modeled treasure chests placed across the island. Arrange varied island areas with clear paths and secluded hiding spots for the chests, framed by surrounding ocean.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Adventure because free exploration and discovering all hidden treasure chests form the complete activity.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open` from the Scavenger Hunt preset: one contiguous island searched freely for 10 objective items. Keep the collectible objectives and discovery alcoves, while progress tracking remains UI rather than a physical quest board.

## Config requirements

Build `path-trail` as clear pirate-island footpaths linking varied terrain areas without revealing every hiding place. Add `alcove-secret` as secluded pockets behind palms, rocks, ruined walls, and shoreline formations. Draw exactly 10 unique ornate custom-modeled treasure-chest props, each with a distinct silhouette or decoration and enough surrounding clearance for interaction. Use `water-body` as the ocean visibly framing the island.

## Layout requirements

Place exactly 10 `collectible-nodes`, one bound to each ornate chest. Distribute them across the island’s major areas rather than clustering them, mixing readable near-path finds with secluded alcove placements. Every chest must remain reachable from a clear path or short side route, and none may sit outside the traversable island boundary. No physical compass or counter object is needed because those functions are UI.

## Layout components

Build one compact tropical island with a readable central path network branching toward beach, palm grove, rocky rise, ruined pirate camp, and sheltered cove. Reconnect major branches where possible so exploration does not require excessive backtracking. Create 10 chest settings across these areas: some beside landmarks, others in shallow recesses behind foliage, rocks, or ruins. Surround the landmass with open ocean and use beach, vegetation, rocks, and weathered pirate remnants to distinguish areas. Keep the outer shoreline as the natural boundary. Frame the island isometrically so its varied regions and enclosing water are immediately legible while the hidden chest locations are not all exposed.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: establish the tropical pirate atmosphere, terrain variety, and bespoke chest settings first, then convert that authoritative image to top-down.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is medium because the compact island takes a few minutes to cross and contains 10 distinct search locations. The theme is colourful tropical pirate adventure. Use `CHECK` for the surrounding ocean; otherwise the island is a single uncomplicated ground surface.
