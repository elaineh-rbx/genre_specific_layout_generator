# Scene prompt

Create a very large, bright cartoonish makeup studio with a fun, welcoming social salon layout. Arrange colorful makeup stations throughout the open main room, with mirrors, counters, chairs, and space for players to gather and work on each other’s makeup.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, built as a purely social avatar-makeup salon with no judging or competition.

## Shape and preset

Use `interior-single`: one very large studio room is the complete walkable map, without an exterior or separately entered venues. The preset is none because Dress Up’s runway and judging context is explicitly absent, and the other presets concern towns, homes, pets, or wilderness.

## Config requirements

Use `social-hub` for an oversized open central salon floor capable of holding many players without crowding. Use `path-street` as broad pedestrian aisles between colorful makeup stations. Render each station with a large mirror, makeup counter, paired or closely arranged chairs, bright product props, and enough clear floor for one player to work on another. Add comfortable gathering and waiting areas around the edges without introducing a stage.

## Layout requirements

Place an uncatalogued makeup interaction area at every station, aligned with its mirror, counter, and chairs. Each area should have two clear avatar positions: one seated or centered as the makeup recipient and one beside or behind the counter as the makeup artist. Keep these paired positions out of the main aisles. No judging zones, scoring volumes, runway markers, teleporters, or NPC placements are needed.

## Layout components

Build one very large, open makeup-studio interior with a spacious central gathering floor and multiple colorful salon stations distributed in orderly clusters around it. Separate clusters with wide aisles and preserve direct routes from the entrance area to every station. Furnish stations with mirrors, counters, chairs, lights, brushes, palettes, and stylized cosmetic displays. Add lounge seating and decorative photo-friendly corners along the perimeter while keeping the middle welcoming and uncluttered. Use a flat polished floor, bright walls, oversized cheerful signage, and a sealed studio boundary. Compose the room so the social center and repeated paired-work stations are immediately readable.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render the colorful salon identity and station groupings first, then derive the top-down circulation plan; appearance and social readability lead.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is large because the author explicitly requests a very large studio with room to gather. The theme is bright, welcoming cartoon salon design. Use `P0`; the studio is one flat enclosed surface without overhangs or an outside-to-inside crossing.
