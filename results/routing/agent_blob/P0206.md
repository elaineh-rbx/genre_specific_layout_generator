# Scene prompt
Create a small, closed, bright low-poly fantasy room with a stone floor and fantasy walls. Arrange five increasingly impressive glowing treasure chests in a clear left-to-right row along one wall: Wooden, Silver, Golden, Platinum, and Diamond, with sparkles around the more expensive chests. Mount a sign above each chest showing its name, coin price, and possible sword rarities. Leave an open central walking area facing the chests, with a player spawn area positioned opposite the Wooden Chest; use simple sword display models and a colorful fantasy atmosphere.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Simulation, specifically an idle incremental collector whose progression is expressed through a compact physical chest room.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single`, taken from outside Simulation’s usual shape set, because the entire walkable map is one closed fantasy room. The closest preset is Incremental Simulator, substituting this interior shape and dropping physical progression gates while retaining a central upgrade storefront and simple circulation.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for the single chest wall that contains all five progression purchases. Use `path-circulation` for the clear central walking floor between spawn and the chest row. Use `trigger-task` in its drawn form for exactly five increasingly ornate chest stations: Wooden, Silver, Golden, Platinum, and Diamond, each with a mounted name, price, and rarity sign. Include simple sword display models and escalating glow and sparkles toward the expensive end.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly five `trigger-task` interaction zones, one on each chest, aligned to the corresponding visible model and reachable from the central floor. Place one player spawn marker opposite the Wooden Chest, oriented toward it. No combat, enemy, quest, rebirth, pet, trade, or multi-world placements belong in this first version.

## Layout components
Build one small rectangular fantasy room with stone floor, colorful low-poly walls, an unobstructed central walking area, and one long presentation wall. Arrange the five chests left to right in the stated order with enough spacing for separate prompts. Mount exactly five signs above them and place sword displays as simple supporting props without blocking circulation. The room boundary is fully closed. Frame the isometric composition from the spawn side toward the complete chest progression.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down view, because the simple room topology is forgiving while visual hierarchy across the five chests is primary.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use small scale, driven by the explicitly minimal one-room build. The theme is bright, colorful, low-poly fantasy with glowing treasure and stone architecture. Use `P0`: this is one visible interior surface with no outside transition or overhang.
