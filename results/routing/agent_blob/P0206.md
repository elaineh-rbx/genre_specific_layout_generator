## Enriched image prompt
Create one small closed fantasy room in a bright, colorful, low-poly style, using a simple stone floor and fantasy walls. Arrange exactly five glowing treasure chests in a straight left-to-right row: Wooden Chest, Silver Chest, Golden Chest, Platinum Chest, and Diamond Chest, with each chest looking progressively more impressive and the expensive chests surrounded by stronger sparkles. Place a clear sign above each chest showing its name, price, and possible rarities: Wooden—100 Coins, Common/Uncommon/Rare; Silver—500 Coins, Common/Uncommon/Rare/Epic; Golden—2,500 Coins, Uncommon/Rare/Epic/Legendary; Platinum—12,500 Coins, Rare/Epic/Legendary/Mythic; Diamond—50,000 Coins, Epic/Legendary/Mythic. Leave a simple open approach aisle in front of the row and do not add combat, enemies, pets, rebirths, trading, quests, multiple worlds, or monetization.

## Genre
The genre is Simulation. Automatic income, increasingly expensive chest interactions, randomized sword collection, equipment upgrades, and offline earnings define an idle incremental simulator.

## Shape and preset
Use shared shape `interior-single`, outside Simulation's usual shape shortlist: the entire walkable game is one enclosed chest room. Preset is none because the Incremental Simulator bundle assumes an open shared world, circulation, and progression gates that this deliberately minimal room does not need.

## Config requirements
Use `trigger-task` for the five visible chest-opening stations and their signs, bending the option to this repeated idle-collector interaction. No other canonical image option applies; the ordered chest row and simple closed room are the complete first-version scene.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly five `trigger-task` TriggerZones, one on each chest in the authored left-to-right order, with proximity interaction space in the aisle and no overlap between prompts. Place one free-text SpawnZone opposite the Wooden Chest and orient it toward that first station. Coin generation, loot chances, inventory, equipping, offline earnings, UI, sounds, saving, security, and animations are mechanics rather than placements.

## Layout components
The build contains one closed room, one stone floor, fantasy perimeter walls, one clear approach aisle, five named chests, and five corresponding overhead signs with the exact prices and rarity bands above. Keep all chests simultaneously visible, make each tier visually stronger than the previous one, and frame the Wooden-to-Diamond progression clearly.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the chest hierarchy, glowing materials, and readable row composition lead, while the rectangular room plan is trivial to derive.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small because this first version is a single room whose chests, signs, and approach spaces must be readable. The theme is bright, colorful, low-poly fantasy. `P0` applies because the build is one walkable interior surface with no outside transition, overhang, separate map, or procedural topology.
