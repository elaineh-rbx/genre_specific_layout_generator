# Scene prompt

Create a bright, colorful stylized cartoon battle-royale lobby as one compact central plaza, readable at a glance and sized for players to gather while retaining their own character appearances. The plaza serves as the shared staging hub for two match modes and includes a clear departure area with a battle bus as the key focal prop; both modes lead into the same match map.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Shooter: Battle Royale and Zero Build are two rule variants of the same battle-royale shooter.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-staged`, taken from outside Shooter’s usual set, because the compact lobby is a staging space separated from one shared match map. Keep the Battle Royale preset, substituting this shape to honor the explicit lobby-to-match structure.

## Config requirements

Draw the compact plaza and one battle bus as its central focal prop. Retain `building-interior` from the Battle Royale preset for enterable structures on the shared match map. Keep the lobby visually open and uncluttered so player avatars remain prominent; shops, purchase menus, currencies, and the battle pass are interface or economy systems rather than map geometry.

## Layout requirements

Place two adjacent departure triggers at the battle bus, one for Battle Royale and one for Zero Build, both linking to the same match map. In that match map, place `boundary-shrinking` around the playable battlefield and distribute `collectible-loot` across accessible exterior and interior locations. The prompt implies one bus and two mode triggers.

## Layout components

Create one compact central plaza with broad gathering pavement, short circulation around a single battle bus, and a clearly framed departure edge. Separate it from one shared open match map containing traversable outdoor ground and enterable structures. Do not duplicate the map for the second mode. Frame the lobby so the bus dominates the view and the whole plaza reads at a glance.

## Render order

Render in `isometric` order because the stylized visual identity leads and neither the lobby nor open battlefield requires computed topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is large overall because a compact lobby feeds a full match map. The theme is bright, colorful stylized cartoon battle royale. Use `P4` for the separate lobby and match spaces and `P3` for outside-to-inside traversal in match structures.
