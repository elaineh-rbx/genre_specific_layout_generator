# Scene prompt
Create a bright, stylized 3D obstacle-tower map with an original colorful look. Build a tall vertical tower of stacked platforming sections rising from a central base, with the goal area at the summit; keep the permanent structure non-volcanic, while allowing open lower space for a temporary rising-lava effect.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Obby & Platformer, with Action secondary because the requested Slap Tower-style gameplay implies physical player conflict during the climb. Obby remains dominant and owns the tower shape and structural route.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-tower`: a tall chain of colorful platforming sections stacks and wraps upward from one central base to a summit. The preset is Tower Obby, retaining its vertical sequential course, climbing challenges, and top goal while keeping the visual design original.

## Config requirements
Use `path-track` for one ordered, physics-legal ascent through the stacked sections. Use `obstacle-jump` for varied platform transfers and `obstacle-climb` for vertical connectors where sections change height. Use `hazard-kill` only as the event-ready open lower void that can visually receive rising lava; do not render permanent volcanic terrain or permanent lava. Use the visible half of `winner-zone` as a clear summit goal platform. Keep collision edges clean and readable for both movement and slap interactions.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `winner-zone` across the summit beyond the final obstacle. Place protected player starts on a broad central base outside immediate conflict. Add one temporary rising-lava hazard plane origin below the lowest playable floor, sized to cover the tower footprint and able to rise through the intentionally open lower volume during the Volcano Event. Global speed, jump, gravity, fog, darkness, rain, and wind events are gameplay state rather than additional map geometry, so they add no permanent placements.

## Layout components
Build one broad central start base and a tall vertical sequence of stacked colorful platform sections leading to one summit goal. Use bright original blocks, beams, ramps, ladders, and open jump gaps, with enough lateral variation for players to pass or contest one another. Preserve a clear vertical volume beneath and around the course so temporary lava can rise without intersecting decorative terrain. Keep the permanent base cheerful and non-volcanic, use open sky as the outer boundary, and avoid background clutter that disguises playable edges. Compose the tower so its base-to-summit progression reads as one coherent climb.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish the valid ordered ascent and collision-clean platforms first, then dress that authoritative structure with the original colorful style.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is large because the tower is explicitly tall. The theme is bright, polished, colorful obstacle play with temporary event effects. Use `P6` for a valid climb and `P2` because stacked platforms overhang and occlude lower sections.
