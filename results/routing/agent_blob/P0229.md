# Scene prompt
Create a 3D post-storm city roleplay map that is mostly wrecked and actively being rebuilt. Arrange a few compact neighbourhoods around a damaged central town area, with storm debris, broken roads, damaged buildings, flooded patches, and incomplete construction visible throughout. Include separate homes and buildable residential plots, surviving or repairable commercial spaces for cafés, gas stations, and clothing stores, plus civic emergency-service buildings for police, paramedics, and firefighters. Place a small farm and rural outskirts area beyond the neighbourhoods, connected by damaged roads; include distinct safe community areas and physically separated high-risk combat zones.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, with Survival secondary. The map supports open-ended jobs, homes, businesses, and rebuilding, while recurring disasters and dangerous zones make survival a genuine second concern.

## Shape and preset
Use `settlement-buildable` for uniform residential plots where players build and decorate their own homes within a damaged shared city. The preset is Home Builder, expanded with post-disaster civic, commercial, farm, safe-area, and risk-zone requirements.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for damaged but vehicle-passable roads connecting the neighborhoods, center, and farm. Use `district-zoned` to separate residential plots, commercial repair sites, emergency services, rural outskirts, and combat areas. Use `building-interior` for accessible cafés, gas stations, clothing stores, and police, paramedic, and fire-service buildings. Use `social-hub` for a protected rebuilding commons. Use `destructible-cluster` for visibly storm-damaged multi-part structures, and `water-body` for flooded patches that interrupt low ground.

## Layout requirements
Place scene-specific safe-roleplay volumes over the community hub and residential areas, and separate PvP-enabled volumes inside clearly bounded high-risk zones. Put one ownership/build marker on every residential plot. Keep these functional boundaries aligned to visible barricades, road breaks, or flood edges rather than invisible arbitrary lines.

## Layout components
Arrange a damaged central town hub with a few compact neighborhoods around it and a small farm at the outer edge. Mix surviving homes with flat buildable plots, and cluster repairable cafés, a gas station, clothing stores, and emergency buildings near broken main roads. Connect all districts by cracked, debris-strewn routes with detours around flooded patches. Mark safe community ground with repaired lighting and barriers; put combat zones beyond stronger barricades amid heavier ruins. Use collapsed facades, scattered debris, exposed framing, incomplete construction, floodwater, muddy verges, and storm-bent vegetation. The outer boundary transitions into damaged rural land, and the composition should show rebuilding radiating outward from the protected center.

## Render order
Render in `isometric` order: the contrast between wreckage and active rebuilding is authoritative, while the connected district plan can be derived from it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is large, driven by several neighborhoods plus farm outskirts. The theme is hopeful post-storm urban recovery. Use `P3` for outdoor-to-interior play and `CHECK` for traversable flooded water; no separate-map route is required.
