# Scene prompt

Create an ultra-realistic 3D open-world forest map spanning over 2,000 studs, designed for third-person exploration as a controllable bear. Fill the large wilderness with varied types of trees, dense ground vegetation, grass, brush, and shrubs, with broad rivers running through the forest and dividing or connecting exploration areas. Compose a natural, expansive landscape with forested foreground, midground, and distant wooded horizons, using lighting that supports both day and night conditions.

# Agent decision

## Genre

Survival is dominant, with Roleplay & Avatar Sim secondary. The clarified goal is surviving while exploring as a controllable bear, while the self-declared bear simulator contributes the animal-avatar framing.

## Shape and preset

Use `wilderness-open`, taken from the secondary genre’s usual set because the scene is one contiguous natural world with no settlement. Preset is none: Resource Survival would add unrequested gathering and base plots, while Animal Sim would add dens, landmarks, and a social hub absent from the brief.

## Config requirements

Draw `water-body` as multiple broad natural rivers that divide and reconnect exploration areas while remaining crossable at selected shallow banks. The remaining image geometry is scene-specific: an ultra-realistic forest with several visibly different tree species, dense grass, brush, shrubs, and layered woodland depth. Lighting and material response must support both daytime and nighttime presentation. Do not add shelters, towns, resource fields, or hostile spawners that the scene never specifies.

## Layout requirements

Place a third-person player start marker for the controllable bear on stable, open forest ground near a river but outside dense vegetation; no loaded canonical option covers an ordinary solo start. No collectible, enemy-spawner, escape-gate, or building-interior placement is justified by the brief. Keep the bear’s traversable ground continuous between forest areas and provide reachable river crossings wherever waterways would otherwise isolate a region.

## Layout components

Build one open wilderness spanning more than 2,000 studs. Organize it into broad forest masses separated by winding rivers, clearings, and narrow vegetated transition bands rather than hard zones. Informal animal trails connect foreground clearings, river crossings, deeper woods, and distant exploration areas. Terrain is natural but avoids overhangs; riverbanks provide the principal relief. Props include varied trees in large unspecified quantities plus dense grass, brush, and shrubs. The map edge remains an open wooded horizon. Compose with a forested foreground, readable river midground, and distant tree-covered skyline.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: the authoritative image establishes realism, vegetation density, and landscape depth first, then the top-down plan is derived from it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is huge, explicitly over 2,000 studs. Theme is ultra-realistic temperate wilderness across day and night. Use `CHECK` because broad rivers introduce a water play volume that must be checked for self-occlusion.
