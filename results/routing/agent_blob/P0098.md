# Scene prompt

Create a 3D game map for Jim’s Playground, a whole park centered on a large playground area. Include a food store with an arcade inside or attached within the playground grounds, plus a prominent monkey statue beside the playground holding a burger. Place two kids in the playground area, and connect the park to an obby section elsewhere in the same map, reached by flying across to it; the obby route returns to the playground when complete.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Adventure because the park supports an NPC burger-delivery objective and movement between points of interest. Obby & Platformer is secondary for the separate completable course section.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open`, one contiguous map containing both park and obby with a flyable gap between them. Preset is none because no Adventure preset preserves this specific park, interior arcade, NPC objective, and obby combination.

## Config requirements

Use `landmark-focal` for the prominent burger-holding monkey statue beside the playground. Draw `npc-population` as exactly two kids occupying the playground. Include one food store and `building-interior` for its enterable attached arcade. Draw `path-track` as a connected obby route, with a visible `winner-zone` at its end and the visible return marker of `teleporter-link`. The two burgers for the children are `collectible-nodes`; their actual pickup placement remains a layout task.

## Layout requirements

Place exactly two `npc-population` characters in separate conversational spots within the main playground. Place exactly two `collectible-nodes` burgers at accessible counters in the food store. Place one `winner-zone` at the end of the obby and pair it with one `teleporter-link` return marker that sends players to a safe arrival pad in the playground. The arcade machines are physical props; their playable minigames are mechanics.

## Layout components

The main zone is a whole-park playground with broad play equipment, paths, seating, the monkey statue, and the food store along one edge. The store has an attached enterable arcade with a clear outside-to-inside doorway. Across open air, place a distinct obby zone reached by flight, with one valid sequential platform route and a final return pad. Keep both zones in the same map and maintain a clear visual bearing between them. Use grass and paved park paths under the playground, open air beneath the crossing, and a finite landscaped park boundary. Frame both park and obby diagonally, with Jim’s Playground dominant and the airborne destination visible beyond.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: the plan must first guarantee the obby chain, store transition, and clear relationship between park and airborne section, then the isometric pass dresses it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is large for a whole park plus a remote obby. The theme is cheerful neighborhood playground whimsy. Use `P6` for the obby’s valid construction, `P3` for the store-to-arcade interior transition, and `CHECK` for the flyable open-air play volume.
