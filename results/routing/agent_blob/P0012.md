# Scene prompt
Create three small, bright cartoon horse-themed farm maps, each sized for a single round with clear, easy-to-navigate routes. Include farm settings with horse areas and other animals as environmental features, keeping the spaces open and readable.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Party & Casual: the source asks for Murder Mystery 2-style round maps, while the horses and farm animals are the theme rather than a second gameplay genre.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-staged`, interpreted here as three isolated round stages selected one at a time. This preserves separation between maps and prevents players in one round space from seeing or entering another. The closest preset is Minigame, but it is no longer recognisable after omitting its unrequested lobby transport, leaderboard, scoring volume, and spectator area, so the preset is none.

## Config requirements
Use `npc-population` for visible horses and other friendly farm animals, together with the paddocks and ground they occupy. No dense hiding maze is requested: all three maps should remain open, legible, and easy to navigate.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` round-start markers evenly within each map, with multiple fair starts per stage and none enjoying a dominant sightline. Also place the layout half of `npc-population` at the horse areas and secondary animal enclosures in every map so ambient animals stay within their intended farm zones.

## Layout components
Build exactly three small, separate farm maps. Each is a bounded round arena with a clear looping route around a central horse paddock, secondary animal pens near the edges, sparse barns, fences, hay bales, troughs, and broad readable openings. Vary the arrangement between the three maps while preserving comparable scale and navigation clarity. Low farm fencing encloses each play boundary. Frame each map so its main route, horse area, and major landmarks read together from above.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the top-down plan is rendered first and is authoritative because fair starts and clear round routes matter more than a look-first composition.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small per map, with exactly three maps overall. The theme is a bright, approachable cartoon farm. Use `P4` because the three round stages are separate maps; no other geometry forces a modifier.
