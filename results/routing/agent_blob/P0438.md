# Scene prompt
Create a large 3D medieval rustic village with a big, explorable layout. Arrange village buildings, streets, and outdoor areas throughout the space, with about 20 collectible objects randomly placed around the village.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure because finding collectible objects through exploration is the central activity. No secondary genre is needed; the village is the scavenger-hunt setting rather than a roleplay system.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open` with the Scavenger Hunt preset. The village is one contiguous map searched in any order, with streets and outdoor spaces providing free circulation.

## Config requirements
Draw `alcove-secret` as discoverable outdoor niches between houses, behind market stalls, beside woodpiles, and along garden walls. Draw the physical board for `tracker-quest` in the village square as a rustic notice board for the collection activity. Apply `settlement-density` to create a large, coherent medieval village rather than a few scattered buildings. Buildings remain exterior shells because entering them was not requested.

## Layout requirements
Place about 20 `collectible-nodes` throughout the village after segmentation, randomizing their exact positions among a larger set of valid outdoor sites while keeping every item reachable. Spread sites across streets, courtyards, gardens, stall edges, wall recesses, and landmark approaches rather than clustering them near the start. Place the functional `tracker-quest` marker on the notice board in the central square.

## Layout components
Create a central market square, several residential clusters, a smithy yard, a small chapel green, garden plots, and an outer livestock common. A branching network of broad dirt streets and narrow foot lanes should connect all districts without imposing a fixed order. Use packed earth, rough stone foundations, timber-frame facades, fences, carts, barrels, stalls, wells, and woodpiles to establish the rustic medieval theme. Enclose the large village with a low palisade, hedges, and natural tree lines, with clear gate openings. Compose the map around the square while preserving legible neighborhood pockets and many outdoor hiding edges.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: establish the village’s architectural character first, then derive the authoritative top-down plan from that image.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is large, driven by the requested big village and roughly 20 distributed collectibles. The theme is rustic medieval timber, stone, earth, and muted natural color. Use `P0`; this is one outdoor, walkable, non-overhanging map with no required interior transitions.
