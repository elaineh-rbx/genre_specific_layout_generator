# Scene prompt
Create a cartoonish, colourful beach game map with a bright sandy shore and a chair where a girl sits near the beach. Arrange several simple, friendly-looking shops nearby, including a clothing shop and a separate fashion-show venue that the ticket grants entry to.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure because a character gives an outfit-making quest whose ticket reward unlocks the next destination. There is no secondary genre.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open` for one contiguous beach district where the shore, shops, quest character, and fashion-show venue can all be visited on foot. The preset is none: Quest Hub suggests the progression gate, but its board and fast-travel bundle do not describe this compact bespoke quest.

## Config requirements
Use `path-trail` for a clear sandy and paved walking route connecting the beach chair, clothing shop, other shops, and fashion-show entrance. Use `landmark-focal` to make the fashion-show venue the visible destination beyond the shop cluster. Use `gate-progression` for its conspicuous ticket-controlled entrance. Use `npc-population` for the friendly girl seated by the beach and visible shop characters. Use `building-interior` for the clothing shop and fashion-show venue players enter. Use `water-body` for the bright sea bordering the sandy shore.

## Layout requirements
The `npc-population` placement puts one quest-giving girl at the single chair near the beach, facing the approach from spawn. Place `collectible-nodes` as one ticket reward position associated with her after the clothing task. Site one free-text outfit-making interaction station inside the clothing shop and one access trigger at the `gate-progression` entrance to the fashion-show venue.

## Layout components
Build one bright sandy beach beside a compact row of several simple shops. Place one chair and one seated girl in a readable quest vignette near the shore. Make the clothing shop and separate fashion-show venue enterable, with the venue farther along the route as the destination; keep the remaining shops simple friendly facades unless needed for circulation. Connect everything with a legible promenade and bound one side with colourful storefronts and the other with sea and beach. Frame the map so the chair, clothing shop, and locked show entrance read in sequence.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the colourful beach-town composition first, then derive its uncomplicated top-down plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, driven by the beach, several shops, and two enterable destinations. The theme is bright cartoon beach fashion. Use `P3` for outdoor-to-indoor transitions and `CHECK` for the playable shoreline water volume.
