# Scene prompt

Create a 3D racing map set across a few city blocks, centered on a closed street circuit that loops through the urban streets. Place a walk-in car dealership along the route as a distinct accessible building, with surrounding city blocks framing the track.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Racing. Players complete laps on a closed urban street circuit; the dealership is a supporting destination rather than a separate simulation genre.

## Shape and preset

Use `route-circuit` for one closed loop through a focused cluster of city streets. The preset is Circuit Racing, with its normal circuit structure plus the explicitly requested enterable dealership.

## Config requirements

Draw `spawn-grid` as an orderly launch area on a wider city straight, `barrier-guardrail` as physical separation between racing traffic and sidewalks, and `path-turnaround` as urban bends that return the route to its origin. Use `settlement-density` for the few close-set city blocks framing the course. Use `building-interior` for one distinct walk-in car dealership, showing both its street-facing exterior and accessible sales-floor interior.

## Layout requirements

Place `spawn-grid` slots in aligned rows behind the shared start/finish line, all oriented down the first street; no car count is stated. Place one `trigger-lap` detection zone across that line and split regions at separated points around the loop to prevent block-cutting. Site the dealership entrance on a sidewalk outside the race barrier, with a safe pedestrian connection from the public edge of the circuit rather than an opening onto the driving line.

## Layout components

Build a start/finish straight, several connected streets around a few compact blocks, clear corner sequences, and a final segment that closes the circuit. Place the dealership along one prominent straight as the route landmark, with a linked interior plan behind its facade and a forecourt separated from the track. Fill the remaining blocks with exterior-only urban buildings, bound the driving surface with barriers, and compose the map so the complete street loop and dealership are both easy to identify.

## Render order

Use `authored_plan`: generate the closed street-circuit blueprint first, then derive top-down and isometric images. The track must close without ambiguous intersections; the dealership interior is linked after the authoritative exterior plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a medium scale for a circuit spanning only a few city blocks. The theme is focused contemporary urban motorsport centered on a polished car dealership. Apply `P6` for valid circuit construction and `P3` because players cross from the street into the dealership interior.
