# Scene prompt
Create a compact 3D car-crash map centered on one medium-tall, roughly 10-story building that serves as both the vehicle spawn point and crash area, with surrounding open ground for wreckage.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation, specifically a physics simulation in which vehicle destruction is the entire activity.

## Shape and preset
Use `arena-stacked` as one compact multi-level crash structure whose roughly 10 floors overhang one another and the wreckage ground below. This shape comes from outside Simulation’s usual set because the building’s vertical stack defines the play space. The preset is Physics Sim, retaining its destructible contraptions and hazards while substituting the stacked shape.

## Config requirements
Use `physics-rig` for breakable ramps, barriers, glass, and structural crash props along the building’s vehicle route. Include `hazard-kill` as clearly staged explosive crash points rather than a roaming environmental event. Use `path-circulation` for a broad vehicle-safe route from the spawn location through or down the structure to the open wreckage ground. Include `building-interior` because the crash route begins within the building and exits outdoors. The building must be one structure of roughly 10 stories, not a city block.

## Layout requirements
Place one vehicle spawn marker inside the upper starting area of the building, aligned with the first drivable crash route. Place destruction and explosion triggers against the rendered breakable barriers and impact zones. Reserve a broad debris-clear volume on the surrounding ground so wreckage can spread without blocking respawn. “Millions of pieces” describes the destruction effect, not millions of pre-placed layout props.

## Layout components
Center the map on one medium-tall, roughly 10-story building with stacked crash floors, vehicle-width ramps or openings, breakable barriers, and a clear final exit to open ground. Surround it with a compact flat wreckage apron and a strong perimeter wall. Keep permanent props sparse so dynamic debris has room. Compose the tower as the sole vertical focal point with the open crash ground visible below.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish the vehicle route and impact clearances first, then dress the authoritative multi-level plan isometrically.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a medium scale band, driven by the 10-story height despite the small footprint. The theme is realistic industrial crash testing. Apply `P2` for stacked overhanging floors and `P3` for the interior-to-exterior vehicle route.
