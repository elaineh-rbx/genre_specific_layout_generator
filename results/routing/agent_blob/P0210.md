# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map as a bright, cartoony chill park that a Roblox character can freely walk around. Arrange open grassy paths and simple park scenery around a central physical rebirth button that players can walk up to; the button has no text label. Include a pet egg as a visible park prop or interaction point, also without ground text, signs, or on-screen labels.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Simulation: a clicker with rebirth and pet progression set in a freely walkable park.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open` as one compact contiguous park that players can roam in any order. The closest preset is Incremental Simulator, retaining open circulation and a physical progression hub, dropping its unrequested purchase gates, and adding the explicitly requested rebirth and egg interactions.

## Config requirements
Use `path-circulation` for broad grassy park paths looping through the scenery and back to the center. Use `social-hub` for the central progression clearing containing the rebirth control and pet egg. Use `trigger-task` in its drawn form for one prominent pet egg station. Also draw one central physical rebirth button as uncatalogued geometry. Neither object may carry text, and the park must contain no ground labels, sign text, or label-like markings.

## Layout requirements
Place one `trigger-rebirth` activation zone precisely on the central physical button. Place one `trigger-task` interaction zone aligned to the visible pet egg. Keep both reachable from the path network with open standing room around them. Place one ordinary spawn marker on a nearby path facing the central clearing. The owner admin panel and global communication controls are screen systems, so they require no map placement.

## Layout components
Build a small cartoony park centered on one open circular clearing. Put exactly one unlabeled rebirth button at its center and exactly one visible pet egg interaction prop nearby without crowding it. Loop several grassy walking paths through lawns, rounded trees, flower beds, benches, shrubs, and small decorative park features before reconnecting to the clearing. Use a soft hedge or tree line as the outer boundary. Compose the isometric frame around the central clearing while preserving relaxed open space and readable loops. No text should appear in the rendered environment.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative, then derive the top-down plan, because the park’s cheerful visual composition leads and its simple looping paths do not require procedural topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use small scale for a focused chill park. The theme is bright, friendly, cartoony Roblox parkland. Use `P0`: all walkable geometry lies on one open surface without interiors, overhangs, or separate maps.
