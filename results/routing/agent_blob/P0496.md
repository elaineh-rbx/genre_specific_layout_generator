# Scene prompt
Create a 3D tower defense map as a short, fully visible grassy field crossing. A single unbranched dirt path runs from a portal at one end of the field to a castle at the opposite end. Place five pre-positioned arrow towers spaced near the road and distributed along its length, with clear coverage over the entire route. Use generated meshes for the portal, castle, towers, and enemy creatures, including goblin-, spider-, wolf-, ogre-, knight-, and dragon-like forms.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Strategy, specifically tower defense.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-actor-track`: one short, fixed, unbranched lane carries all waves from portal to castle. The preset is Tower Defense, with `buildzone-grid` omitted because the five towers are pre-positioned rather than deployed into build areas, and with no unrequested chokepoint.

## Config requirements
Use `tracker-core` to draw the castle as the defended endpoint. Draw one generated-mesh portal at the lane entrance and exactly five generated-mesh arrow towers with strong, readable silhouettes beside the road. Include generated-mesh forms for six enemy archetypes: goblin, spider, wolf, ogre, knight, and dragon. Keep the rest of the field grassy and uncluttered so the complete route and tower coverage remain visible.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawner-npc` at the portal and bind all enemy types to the same lane centerline. Place the `tracker-core` objective at the castle entrance. Place exactly five fixed tower markers near the road, staggered from portal to castle and alternating sides where useful, with overlapping firing coverage across the entire route. No tower-placement slots or player build markers are required.

## Layout components
Build one small grassy field bounded by a simple low edge or distant tree line. A single dirt path crosses from the portal at one end to the castle at the opposite end without branches. Five arrow-tower foundations sit near the path and spread along its full length, leaving the lane unobstructed. Keep terrain flat, props sparse, and both endpoints prominent. Compose from a raised three-quarter view that fits the portal, full path, all five towers, and castle in one frame.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, then dress the isometric view, because the route must remain continuous and all five towers must cover it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by the requested short fully visible crossing. The theme is bright readable fantasy field defense with generated-mesh creatures and structures. Use `P6` because the fixed enemy lane must be valid by construction.
