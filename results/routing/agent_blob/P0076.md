# Scene prompt
Create three bright, stylized 1v1 game maps that rotate between rounds: a forest setting, a desert setting, and a snowy setting, each designed as a compact playable arena.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Party & Casual for the tag rounds, with Action secondary for the separate 1v1 duel mode. Party & Casual owns the shape and wording.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded` from the Childhood Game preset for each of the three maps. Each biome is a compact continuous arena, but the forest, desert, and snow settings are separate maps rotated between rounds.

## Config requirements
No canonical image option is required by the brief. Draw three collision-clean bounded arenas with distinct bright stylized biome dressing: trees and grassy ground in the forest, dunes and rock forms in the desert, and snowbanks with icy or evergreen accents in the snow map. Keep decorative geometry away from primary movement space so strict 1v1 pursuit and combat remain readable.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` with exactly two evenly matched round-start markers in each map, positioned on opposite sides with neither player receiving a sightline or distance advantage. Place `trigger-scoring` over the playable arena as needed for tag contact and round completion. Keep the two starts and scoring regions consistent across all three biome variants despite their different dressing.

## Layout components
Build three separate compact arenas: one forest, one desert, and one snowy. Each has a clear central contest area, a few broad circulation routes around biome props, and a continuous visible boundary preventing escape. The forest uses stylized tree clusters, the desert uses rounded dunes and rocks, and the snow arena uses banks and evergreens; none should create tight traps or hidden unreachable pockets. Compose each map as a self-contained, similarly sized 1v1 field suitable for automatic round rotation.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative because the bright biome identity and clean arena composition lead, while the simple bounded topology is not structurally fragile.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium overall, comprising exactly three compact two-player maps. The theme is bright, stylized forest, desert, and snow. Use `P4` because the rotating biomes are genuinely separate maps; no elevation, overhang, interior transition, or volumetric play adds another modifier.
