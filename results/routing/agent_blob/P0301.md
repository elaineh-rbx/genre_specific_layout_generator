# Scene prompt
Create a 3D comic-book-stylized modern city spanning a few dense city blocks for web-swinging. Arrange streets and intersections at ground level between clusters of shorter buildings, with several taller landmarks rising up to about 20 stories and varied rooftops, façades, and alleyways creating different swing heights and routes. Keep street-level spaces clear enough for small mugging and robbery encounters.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Action is the dominant and only genre. Web-swing traversal and quick street-level crime fights are both physical action activities within the same city.

## Shape and preset
Use `traversal-city`: the city is designed to be crossed through air and over rooftops, with streets below as the fallback route. Use the Parkour Traversal preset because its defining shape fits the web-swinging traversal, while unsupported lethal edges are omitted.

## Config requirements
Use `choke-bottleneck` for selected alley mouths and narrow side-street transitions that create readable, brief crime encounter spaces without blocking the broader swing network. Use `cover-los` for building corners, parked street objects, and substantial façade projections that provide momentary breaks during ground fights. Use `settlement-density` for the requested few dense modern city blocks. Keep roofs, façades, and building spacing varied enough to provide multiple web-anchor heights.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` at one elevated starting roof screened from immediate street encounters, with safe access to both the rooftop route and ground level. Place several `spawner-npc` crime-event origins only at clear street-level pockets such as alley mouths, shopfront setbacks, and small intersection corners, leaving pedestrian-scale fighting room around each. Keep these origins away from major landing zones and road crossings.

## Layout components
Build a few contiguous city blocks around a connected grid of streets, intersections, and alleys. Cluster shorter buildings around several landmarks, with the tallest buildings about 20 stories high. Vary rooftop elevations, setbacks, façade projections, and gaps to form overlapping swing choices while keeping streets continuously traversable below. Reserve small clear pockets for mugging and robbery encounters at ground level. Bound the map with continued skyline massing and blocked outbound streets. Compose the opening view from an elevated roof across several swing-height layers toward a tall landmark, while keeping the street grid legible beneath.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: connected streets, safe roof access, encounter pockets, and coherent traversal corridors must be established before comic-book architectural dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium because the answer limits the map to a few blocks but requires meaningful vertical traversal. The theme is a stylized modern comic-book metropolis with bold readable silhouettes. Use `P2` because rooftops, façade projections, and elevated traversal surfaces overhang streets and lower roofs.
