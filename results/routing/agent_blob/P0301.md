## Enriched image prompt
Build a compact comic-book-stylized modern city of a few connected blocks for a web-swinging superhero. Use a varied skyline with the tallest buildings about 20 stories high and shorter buildings around them, creating frequent swing anchors, changing arc heights, accessible rooftops, ledges, and clear gaps. Lay out streets, sidewalks, alley mouths, and small corner plazas below, with several readable street-level pockets suitable for brief muggings and robberies. Keep rooftop and street circulation connected so the player can swing across the district, drop quickly to a crime site, and resume traversal without large set-piece arenas.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Action because web traversal and stopping street crimes are the core physical activities.

## Shape and preset
Use `traversal-city`, Zone (Traversal City): the district is crossed over rooftops and ledges, with streets as the fallback and crime layer. The matching preset is Parkour Traversal, adapted specifically for web swinging rather than ordinary running.

## Config requirements
Use `choke-bottleneck` for alley mouths and small corner-plaza approaches that concentrate each quick street encounter. The varied building heights, swing-facing facades, ledges, accessible rooftops, streets, sidewalks, and compact crime pockets are required scene-specific visible geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` Spawner markers at several separated street-level crime pockets, sited in alley mouths or small plazas with readable approaches from above and along the street. Do not create a spawner for the player-controlled hero.

## Layout components
One few-block district contains streets and sidewalks at ground level, alleys and corner plazas for crimes, shorter buildings throughout, and landmark towers reaching about 20 stories. Rooftops and facade ledges create continuous swing-facing structure above every street, and all crime pockets remain visible from nearby approach routes.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: street circulation, crime pockets, rooftop accessibility, and vertical connections should be planned before comic-book dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use large scale because multiple city blocks and 20-storey towers must coexist while street crime sites remain identifiable. The theme is a comic-book-stylized modern city. `P2` is required because rooftops and ledges overhang the street layer, and `CHECK` applies to web-swinging through the layered open-air volume.
