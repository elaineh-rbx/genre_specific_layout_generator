# Scene prompt
Create a realistic, modern 3D Tower Defense experience with a detailed central lobby: a main waiting plaza with several match-entry elevators arranged around it. Build exactly five compact battle maps, each designed so players can see most of its enemy lane at a glance, with a clearly marked enemy entry point, a defended base endpoint, designated tower-placement ground beside the lane, and blocked or unsuitable placement areas along paths and obstacles. Give every map a distinct route and environmental layout: a modern city with streets weaving between urban structures; a forest with a trail around trees and natural obstacles; a desert with a route through dunes and rock formations; an industrial area with lanes between factories, pipes, and machinery; and a military zone with a defended route through fortified structures and barriers. Use detailed models, quality textures, clear lighting, environmental cover, and readable combat space without obstructing visibility.
