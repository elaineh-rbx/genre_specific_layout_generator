# Scene prompt
Create a bright, toon-like 3D pet-care neighbourhood centered on a player’s already built, customizable house. Place a pet park and a veterinary clinic a short walk from the house, connected by simple neighbourhood paths; leave the house interior and exterior arranged for decoration and customization.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, centered on adopting and caring for a pet through home, park, and veterinary spaces.

## Shape and preset
Use `settlement-claimable`, reduced to one small neighborhood centered on one finished house that the player claims and decorates. The matching preset is Pet Care.

## Config requirements
Use `path-street` as short, simple pedestrian paths linking the house, pet park, and veterinary clinic. Draw `social-hub` as the pet park: an open gathering lawn sized for owners and animals without crowding. Include `building-interior` for the customizable house and the veterinary clinic, both entered from the neighborhood. Use `vignette-photo` for a friendly park corner suited to pet roleplay and screenshots. Keep house rooms and the immediate exterior yard open enough for decoration rather than filling them with fixed props.

## Layout requirements
Place one claim marker at the entrance of the single pre-built player house. Place decoration anchors throughout its interior rooms and in its exterior yard, leaving circulation clear. Place a veterinary interaction point at the clinic reception or treatment area. No ambient pet population is required because the player’s adopted pet accompanies them rather than spawning as scenery.

## Layout components
Build exactly one small neighborhood with three primary zones: the customizable house and yard at the center, a nearby pet park, and a compact veterinary clinic. Two short paths connect the home directly to the park and vet, with no long road network. Use flat lawns, rounded hedges, bright paving, playful trees, a few pet-safe park props, and simple toon-like building forms. A low neighborhood fence and landscaping enclose the map. Compose all three destinations within one readable frame so each is visibly a short walk from the house.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the friendly neighborhood composition first, then derive the simple top-down paths and building footprints.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a small scale band because the brief explicitly asks for one compact neighborhood. The theme is bright, friendly toon-style pet care. Apply `P3` because play crosses from outdoor paths into both the house and veterinary clinic.
