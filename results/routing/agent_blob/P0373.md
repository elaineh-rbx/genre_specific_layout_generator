# Scene prompt

Create a simple 3D gray-box kart driving test map with a small, perfectly flat oval race track sized for roughly ten-second laps. The loop is a clear, continuous driving surface with minimal surrounding ground and no decorative scenery.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Racing. The kart drives repeated timed laps around a finite closed test loop.

## Shape and preset

Use `route-circuit` as a small, perfectly flat oval that returns to its starting point. The preset is none: Circuit Racing is the closest preset, but its multi-car grid, physical guardrail treatment, and full race furniture would contradict this minimal single-kart gray-box tuning map.

## Config requirements

Draw `lane-corridor` as a clearly marked, consistently wide kart-driving surface and `path-turnaround` as two broad 180-degree oval ends connected by short straights. Keep every surface gray-box simple, perfectly flat, and undecorated. The surrounding ground should be only large enough to frame the loop and provide clean runoff; do not add scenery, stands, pits, terrain relief, or branded props.

## Layout requirements

Place one scene-specific kart spawn marker on a straight segment, centered in the lane and oriented along the driving direction. Place one `trigger-lap` detection region across the entire track width immediately ahead of that spawn to time repeated laps and verify that the kart completed the loop. No additional split triggers are necessary for this tiny, unobstructed oval.

## Layout components

Build one continuous oval track with two short parallel straights and two smooth semicircular ends, proportioned for roughly ten-second laps. Surround it with a narrow strip of plain flat base ground and an open outer boundary. Use simple pavement-edge markings to distinguish track from runoff, maintain a constant driveable width throughout, and avoid intersections or branches. Compose the map from a slightly elevated angle that makes the complete oval and the lone start position immediately readable.

## Render order

Use `authored_plan`: generate the closed oval blueprint in code first, then derive its top-down and isometric views. Even a gray-box circuit must close exactly and remain continuously driveable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a small scale, driven by the requested roughly ten-second lap. The theme is a neutral gray-box vehicle-physics test course. Apply `P6` because the racing circuit must be valid by construction; no elevation, overhang, interior, separate map, or other modifier applies.
