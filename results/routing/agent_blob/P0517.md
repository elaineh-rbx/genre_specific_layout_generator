# Scene prompt
Create a 3D obby layout of five separate themed towers, progressing in order through teleporters at the completed top of each tower. Butter Land occupies the first 50-stage tower with butter-stick jumps, melting butter slides, a butter-cube maze, toast platforms, pancake-stack climbs, and a butter waterfall. NeeDoh World follows with giant squishy balls, stretchy bridges, launch pads, bubble tunnels, color-changing platforms, and bounce rooms. Dumpling Village uses steamed dumplings, chopstick balance beams, soy-sauce lakes, bamboo steamers, giant bao jumps, and noodle swings. Mochi & Candy includes mochi trampolines, jelly cubes, marshmallow clouds, gummy-bear parkour, lollipop poles, and a cotton-candy climb. The final Ultimate Squishy Factory tower contains squishy-making machines, conveyor belts, giant presses, rainbow slime rivers, an escape route past a squishy monster, and a neon rainbow finale. Include hidden rooms, a VIP area, and a separate bonus-stage area with the Rainbow Tower, Lava Squish, Ice Squish, and a secret developer room.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Obby & Platformer, canonically `obby-platformer`: player-paced jumping and climbing define the game.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-tower` for five separately rendered vertical courses. Tower Obby is the matching preset, extended with inter-tower teleporters, regular checkpoints, and a mid-course maze.

## Config requirements
Draw a physics-valid `path-track` through all 250 main stages; `obstacle-jump` covers food platforms, launch pads, trampoline jumps, and balance beams; `obstacle-moving` covers slides, swings, poles, conveyors, and presses; `obstacle-timing` covers the safe-timing presses and changing platforms; `obstacle-climb` covers pancake stacks and cotton candy; and `obstacle-maze` forms the butter-cube maze. Draw safe pads for `checkpoint-respawn`, visible gateways for `teleporter-link`, the neon finale as `winner-zone`, and enterable hidden, VIP, and developer rooms as `building-interior`.

## Layout requirements
Place `checkpoint-respawn` every five stages, totaling 50 main-course checkpoints, on enclosed landing pads. Place four sequential `teleporter-link` pairs from each completed tower top to the next tower entrance, plus links into the separate bonus area. Place one `winner-zone` at the fifth tower’s neon finale.

## Layout components
Build five distinct 50-stage towers in sequence, each with its named obstacle set and a protected base, ascending critical path, and completed-top portal. Keep soy-sauce lakes, the butter waterfall, and slime rivers contained within their themed towers. The separate bonus area contains Rainbow Tower, Lava Squish, Ice Squish, and the secret developer room. Hidden rooms branch briefly without interrupting the main route; the VIP area sits off the shared arrival space. Frame each tower to show its base-to-summit progression.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the valid ordered jump chain and portal progression must lead, then the isometric view dresses that plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge because the build contains five 50-stage towers plus bonus stages. The theme is bright, tactile squishy toys and foods. `P6` guarantees legal course and maze topology, `P2` handles vertically stacked tower surfaces, `P3` handles enterable secret and VIP rooms, and `P4` handles five separate tower maps and the bonus area.
