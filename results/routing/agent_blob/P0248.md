# Scene prompt
Create a bright, colourful cartoon-style 3D minigame map titled “Mini Game.” Build a small, neat central lobby hub with clearly separated entrances leading to three simple activity zones: an obby/parkour course, a short race track, and a basic combat arena. Arrange the lobby as the connecting center, with the three game areas visible around it and clear paths between them.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Party & Casual because this is a hub-based collection of short minigames. Obby & Platformer is secondary because one of the three activities is explicitly a parkour course.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded`, the Continuous Play Space, because the compact lobby and all three visible activity zones occupy one connected map rather than isolated stages. The preset is none: Minigame assumes an isolated match stage and lobby transport, while this brief explicitly requires direct paths and mutual visibility.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for the small, neat central lobby and its three clearly marked entrances. Use `path-track` for a sequential, physics-legal platform chain in the obby zone. Use `obstacle-jump` for simple readable parkour jumps. Give the short race track a clearly marked start, broad ground route, and finish, and make the basic combat arena an open bounded floor without adding unrequested cover or weapons.

## Layout requirements
Place one `trigger-scoring` completion volume at the end of each of the three activity zones. Place `checkpoint-respawn` at the obby entrance and safe midpoint, with the drawn pads included in the image config. Place balanced `spawn-protected` starts around the combat arena perimeter and a starting row plus finish trigger on the race track. Keep lobby starts centered and outside all activity volumes.

## Layout components
Build exactly four named spaces: one compact central lobby and three surrounding zones for obby, short race, and combat. Connect each zone directly to the lobby by a short, wide path and keep all three visible from the center. Separate zones with colorful low barriers and distinct floor palettes. Enclose the outer map cleanly and compose it as a tidy three-lobed arrangement around the hub, with the “Mini Game” title facing arrivals.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the top-down image is rendered first and authoritative so the three activities remain separated, connected to the hub, and playable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale for three compact activities plus lobby. The theme is bright, polished Roblox cartoon color. `P6` is required by the obby’s physics-valid `path-track`; no separate-map modifier is needed.
