# Scene prompt

Create a bright, cartoony underground mining map with a surface entry area leading into a vertical excavation that descends through four distinct depth tiers, reaching 1,000 blocks down. Layered breakable terrain transitions from stone and coal near the upper levels to increasingly colorful rock strata, rare ore deposits, and glowing crystal formations in deeper biomes. Include clear routes back toward the surface and open mining spaces within each tier, with deeper zones visually separated by their higher-tier minerals and richer crystal-filled surroundings.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Simulation because mining resources, selling them, upgrading tools, unlocking deeper tiers, and collecting pets form a persistent progression loop.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-underground`: a surface area connected to stacked excavation layers descending beneath it. The preset is Mining & Extraction, retaining the shared resource field and extractable resources but omitting vehicle-width hauling roads because the brief specifies player excavation and return routes, not vehicles.

## Config requirements

Apply `resource-shared` as the common breakable mine across four depth tiers, `gate-progression` as clear physical thresholds into higher-tier mineral layers, `path-circulation` as readable routes from every tier back toward the surface, and `social-hub` as the compact surface entry where selling, tool upgrades, and pet hatching are spatially grouped. Make each tier visibly distinct through stone, coal, colorful strata, rare ores, and increasingly rich glowing-crystal formations.

## Layout requirements

Place `collectible-nodes` throughout all four tiers, aligned to visible ore deposits and crystal-bearing blocks, with common resources concentrated above and rarer resources deeper down. Keep extraction markers out of the return routes and preserve clear open mining space in every tier. No vehicle routes or combat spawners are needed.

## Layout components

Build one compact surface entry and a vertical excavation reaching 1,000 blocks down through exactly four distinct depth tiers. Connect the surface to tier one and link each successive tier with readable descending shafts, ramps, or openings, while providing an unmistakable return path upward from every layer. Give each tier an open mining chamber surrounded by layered breakable blocks. Begin with stone and coal, then transition through more colorful rock, rare ore deposits, and dense glowing crystals in the deepest biome. Separate tiers vertically so each reads as its own progression band while remaining part of one mine. The surrounding unbreakable rock shell forms the boundary.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first as authoritative for each level, then dress the isometric views, because routes through all four occluding layers must connect reliably back to the surface.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a huge scale, driven by four tiers and a stated depth of 1,000 blocks. The theme is bright, cartoony simulator mining with readable ores and saturated glowing crystals. Apply `P2` because underground tiers overhang one another and `P3` because play crosses between the surface and underground interior.
