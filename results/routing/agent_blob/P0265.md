# Scene prompt
Create a 3D Monster Tag / Hide & Seek map set in a compact but giant-scale, overgrown forest that can be crossed in roughly 1–2 minutes. Dense woodland cover fills the play area: scattered giant mossy rocks, hollow fallen trees, tall grass, elevated ruined-stone platforms, and hidden tunnels create unpredictable sightlines and vertical escape routes. Giant ruined stone structures rise through the midground and background, while oversized furniture provides additional hiding spaces beneath and around it. Use a moody twilight sky, low ground-hugging volumetric fog, and glowing mushrooms or lanterns to mark concealed paths. Place a small separate lobby outside the forest as the round staging area, connected to the main map by a clear teleport entry point.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Party & Casual, specifically round-based tag and hide-and-seek; Survival is secondary because one asymmetric hunter pursues the remaining players.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-staged`, with a compact forest match area isolated from the small between-round lobby. The Minigame preset fits the round staging and transport structure, extended with hide-and-seek cover.

## Config requirements
Draw `social-hub` as the small lobby, `cover-los` as dense giant rocks, hollow logs, grass, ruins, and oversized furniture that repeatedly break sightlines, `teleporter-link` as a visible lobby entry portal, and `spectator-zone` as an out-of-play viewing area. The concealed tunnels and elevated platforms must remain clearly navigable.

## Layout requirements
Place `teleporter-link` markers at the lobby portal and a safe forest arrival zone. Place `spawn-protected` as one isolated Monster start and a distributed set of Hider starts, separated by cover. Place `trigger-scoring` across the active forest as the tag-detection region. The spectator area needs an out-of-play return marker.

## Layout components
The small lobby sits apart from the main forest. The forest is a compact giant-scale arena crossable in 1–2 minutes, with looping concealed paths through dense trees, giant mossy rocks, hollow logs, tall grass, oversized furniture, ruined-stone structures, elevated platforms, and hidden tunnels. Glowing mushrooms and lanterns mark subtle escape paths. Bound the arena naturally with dense woodland and ruins; frame the oversized cover against a moody twilight sky and low fog.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: fair route connectivity, distributed starts, and tunnel access must be established before the atmospheric isometric dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by the stated 1–2 minute crossing time. The theme is giant-scale overgrown twilight ruin. Use `P4` for the isolated lobby and match map, plus `P2` because tunnels and elevated platforms create overhanging surfaces.
