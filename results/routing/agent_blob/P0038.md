# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map set in a prehistoric wilderness where players appear as animals and dinosaurs, with open terrain suited to hunting and fighting.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Action because players hunt and fight one another physically; Simulation is secondary because victories feed a stat-and-power upgrade grind. Upgrades remain menu-driven and therefore add no map station.

## Shape and preset
Use `open-battlefield`, taken from outside Action’s usual shapes, for one contiguous prehistoric wilderness with dispersed hunting and fighting ground rather than lanes or stacked arena floors. No preset fits without replacing the requested open wilderness with a bounded ring, urban stack, or room chain, so the preset is none.

## Config requirements
Use `cover-los` sparingly as large prehistoric trees, boulders, fern clusters, and low ridges that let an animal retreat and break combat lock while preserving broad open hunting terrain. The map also requires collision-clean clearings and natural sightlines sized for animals and dinosaurs of different scales. Powers, stats, the owner panel, and upgrade menus are mechanics or UI and do not enter the image config.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` start zones around separated outer portions of the wilderness, screened by major rocks, tree stands, or shallow ridges so newly appearing players are not immediately visible from central hunting ground. Provide one spawn position per supported player slot within those distributed safe zones. No training station, power pickup, NPC emitter, or fixed objective is required.

## Layout components
Build one open prehistoric wilderness with a broad central hunting plain, scattered secondary clearings, and loose natural routes between them. Break long sightlines with sparse boulders, giant trees, fern stands, and gentle ridges without forming formal lanes. Use rugged soil, grass, and prehistoric vegetation, with a natural cliff, dense jungle, or impassable rock boundary around the playable area. Compose the scene wide so the central clash ground reads immediately while protected edge pockets remain visible.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the prehistoric atmosphere and combat readability lead, and the dispersed open terrain has no structure-critical topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium. The theme is bold prehistoric wilderness for animal and dinosaur combat. Use `P0`; one grounded exterior surface captures the complete layout.
