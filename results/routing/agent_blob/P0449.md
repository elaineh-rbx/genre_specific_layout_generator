# Scene prompt
Create a small, manicured park map with bright grass covering the ground. Place a clean fountain at the central spawn point, encircled by a compact neat paved ring path. Around the outside of the path, arrange two houses and two simple shops, each with an accessible interactive interior, facing inward toward the central garden.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Entertainment: the core experience is browsing user-generated videos, while the three-dimensional park is a walkable social setting rather than a scored game.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open` as a compact free-roam garden with all exterior destinations available in any order. Match the Free-Roam Showcase preset, keeping its composed focal landmark and first reveal but dropping `collectible-nodes` because the prompt asks for no hidden finds.

## Config requirements
Use `landmark-focal` for the central fountain as the garden’s visual anchor. Use `spawn-first-reveal` so arrival is composed directly around the fountain and inward-facing buildings. Use `building-interior` for four accessible interiors: two houses and two simple shops. Draw the neat paved ring path as scene-specific circulation around the fountain; it is not a racing circuit or a progression route.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawn-first-reveal` marker at the fountain plaza, oriented to show the fountain first and the four buildings beyond it. Associate one entrance transition with each of the four `building-interior` spaces, linking each inward-facing exterior doorway to its corresponding interior. No video-feed UI, buttons, or phone icon belong in the spatial layout.

## Layout components
Build a small square or circular garden carpeted in bright, closely maintained grass. Put one clean fountain at the exact center with a compact paved ring around it. Arrange four buildings evenly beyond the ring: two houses and two simple shops, all facing inward, each with a doorway and a modest accessible interior. Use short radial walks between the ring and entrances. Enclose the garden with a low manicured hedge or simple park boundary, keeping every building visible from the central spawn. The exact stated counts are one fountain, two houses, and two shops.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the park’s symmetry, inward-facing architecture, and pleasant first impression matter more than difficult topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small because players can cross the garden in seconds. The theme is a bright, clean, manicured neighborhood park. Use `P3` because play crosses four outside-to-inside boundaries; otherwise the exterior remains a single uncomplicated surface.
