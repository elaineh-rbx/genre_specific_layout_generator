# Scene prompt

Create a 3D survival escape map on a small, uninhabited jungle island dominated by a volcano. A short, tense route under a minute long leads from the volcano across jungle terrain to a coastal rescue boat waiting at the shore.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Survival: players flee a timed volcanic disaster and must reach extraction before the round ends.

## Shape and preset

Use `route-guided`, taken from outside Survival’s usual set, because this scene is a directed escape from the volcano to one coastal endpoint rather than an arena survived in place. The preset is none. Escape contributes `gate-escape`, but its looping warren, hiding cover, and entered buildings contradict the short uninhabited outdoor run; Disaster Survival is also wrong because that preset deliberately has no exit.

## Config requirements

Use `hazard-kill` for the damaging eruption region advancing from the volcano, `terrain-relief` for the volcano’s descending jungle slopes, `water-body` for the sea enclosing the island and receiving the rescue boat, and the visible half of `gate-escape` for the clearly readable boat extraction point. Do not add shelters, ruins, or other buildings.

## Layout requirements

Place the functional half of `gate-escape` at the shoreline boarding point where the route meets the rescue boat. Place one round-start area on safe traversable ground near the volcano-facing end of the route and keep the eruption countdown as gameplay rather than visible geometry. The extraction trigger must remain reachable in under one minute and close when the countdown reaches zero.

## Layout components

The small island has two primary zones: a high volcanic origin inland and a low coastal extraction beach. One short jungle path descends between them, staying readable through dense tropical vegetation and avoiding branches that could waste the limited run time. The volcano dominates the rear of the composition; sloped jungle terrain forms the middle ground; the beach, boarding point, and one rescue boat occupy the foreground shore. Ocean encloses the island as the hard boundary. Frame the route diagonally so its complete volcano-to-boat progression reads at once.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: the route plan is rendered first and is authoritative because a blocked, ambiguous, or overlong escape path would make the timed round unplayable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is small, driven by the stated sub-minute crossing. The theme is humid tropical disaster survival with volcanic ash, dark rock, dense green jungle, and urgent coastal rescue. Use `tiered` for the sloped volcanic relief and `CHECK` for the surrounding water play envelope; no overhang or interior transition is required.
