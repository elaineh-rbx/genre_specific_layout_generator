# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] scale — Q: How long should the run from the volcano to the boat take - under a minute, or a real trek? A: Make it a short, tense run that takes under a minute on a small island map.
- [author] goal — Q: Is there a countdown the eruption runs on, and does the boat leave without stragglers? A: Use a clear eruption countdown, and have the rescue boat leave when it reaches zero; players who miss it lose the round.
- [author] shape — Q: Are there buildings on the island to shelter in, or is it uninhabited jungle? A: Make it an uninhabited jungle island; the prompt does not call for shelter buildings.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a small uninhabited volcanic jungle island built around a short, tense escape route that takes under a minute. Begin the route on the inland volcanic slope, then lead it clearly downhill through dense but traversable jungle to a shoreline evacuation point. Show the eruption as a dangerous volcanic region behind the run, with glowing lava and ash advancing from the cone while the safe direction remains visually obvious. Place one rescue boat at the water's edge as the terminal destination, with a clear boarding approach from the beach. Keep the island compact, exclude all shelter buildings and settlements, and compose the volcano in the background, jungle route in the midground, and boat at the foreground coast.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Survival because players flee a timed volcanic disaster to an escape point.

## Shape and preset
Use `route-guided`, Path (Guided Route), taken from outside Survival's usual shape list because this is one directed continuous run from volcano to boat, not survival in place or a looping chase map. Neither Disaster Survival nor Escape remains recognisable after the explicit boat exit, jungle route, and lack of interiors, so the preset is none.

## Config requirements
Use `hazard-kill` for the erupting lava and dangerous volcanic ground, `path-trail` from Adventure for the short downhill jungle escape trail, `terrain-relief` for the volcanic cone and descending slope, `water-body` for the coastal water, and the drawn form of `gate-escape` for the rescue boat and its boarding point.

## Layout requirements
Place one free-text SpawnZone at the inland start of the trail below the volcanic cone. Place exactly one `gate-escape` Gate trigger at the boat's boarding point on the shoreline, reached only after the downhill jungle route. The eruption countdown and departure timing remain mechanics; they do not add map components.

## Layout components
The map contains one small volcanic island, one inland volcano, one descending jungle trail, hazardous lava and eruption ground behind the route, one beach evacuation zone, one rescue boat, and surrounding coastal water. The trail connects the inland spawn directly to the boat without shelter buildings or settlement detours. The shoreline and water form the outer boundary.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the under-a-minute route must connect the volcano-side start to the single boat boarding point before the jungle and eruption are dressed isometrically.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small because the author requires a compact island and sub-minute run, with jungle obstacles and the boarding point still readable. The theme is a tense tropical volcanic eruption. Modifier `tiered` is required for the sloped volcanic terrain, and modifier `CHECK` comes from the coastal `water-body`; the surface boat approach should not self-occlude.
