# Scene prompt
Create a bright, cartoony neighbourhood for a simple house-building and dress-up game. Lay out 8 separate empty house plots around a shared town area, with shops positioned as accessible destinations for furniture, decorations, and clothing.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim: player-owned house design, decoration shopping, and dress-up all belong to its home-building life play.

## Shape and preset
Use `settlement-buildable` for a strict neighbourhood of separate empty plots where players construct their own houses. The preset is Home Builder, matching its buildable plots, shared town core, road circulation, and enterable commercial venues.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for wide, flat cartoony streets connecting every plot to the town center and shops, with at least 30 studs on two-way sections. Use `social-hub` for a deliberately open shared town area that can hold several avatars comfortably. Use `building-interior` for accessible furniture, decoration, and clothing shops entered from the street. Preserve exactly 8 uniform empty house plots, with clear plot edges and build-ready flat ground.

## Layout requirements
Nothing additional must be placed after segmentation: the brief does not request spawn markers, teleporters, pickups, checkpoints, or invisible interaction volumes. The eight ownership plots are expressed by the `settlement-buildable` shape and must remain individually separable by downstream building systems.

## Layout components
Arrange exactly 8 square house plots around a compact shared town center, keeping equal setbacks and direct road access for each. Place the furniture and decoration shop together on one commercial edge and the clothing shop on another, all facing the social center. Connect the plots and shops with a simple loop or short grid of wide streets and pedestrian margins. Keep every plot flat, empty, and large enough for a starter house without overlap. Use bright grass, rounded curbs, colorful storefronts, simple signs, lamps, trees, and playful street furniture. A low neighbourhood perimeter marks the build boundary, while the composition keeps all eight plots and the commercial center legible in one view.

## Render order
Render in `isometric` order: the bright cartoony neighborhood presentation leads, and the regular eight-plot plan can be derived reliably from it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, driven by exactly 8 house plots plus the shared shops. The theme is bright, simple cartoon suburbia. Use `P3` because play crosses between the outdoor neighborhood and enterable shop interiors.
