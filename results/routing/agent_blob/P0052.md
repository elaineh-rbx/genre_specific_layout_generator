# Scene prompt

Create a small, tight 3D chase arena in a plain abstract blocky Roblox-style build. Arrange compact block structures, platforms, and clear connecting paths to keep all areas close together. Include character representations: Verity as a yellow ball, Falsity as a blue ball, and Creator as a tall humanoid figure.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Survival: the round is about one player chasing up to four others until the timer expires or everyone is caught.

## Shape and preset

Use `warren-looping` from the 1 vs All preset. For this compact arena, it means short interconnected loops with no dead ends, so chased players cannot be unfairly trapped by the map. Keep the preset but omit its escape-gate feature because this round ends by time or capture, not by reaching an exit.

## Config requirements

Use `cover-los` as chunky block structures and low wall cutouts that repeatedly break sightlines while preserving clear circulation. Also draw three named character representations: Verity as one yellow ball, Falsity as one blue ball, and Creator as one tall blocky humanoid. Platforms remain low and readable rather than forming overlapping floors.

## Layout requirements

Place `spawn-protected` role starts: one chaser start on one side of the arena and four chased-player starts distributed around the opposite side, all shielded from immediate line of sight. No exit trigger or hostile-NPC emitter is needed.

## Layout components

Build one small bounded arena around a compact central cluster, with several perimeter block clusters and short paths joining them into multiple loops. Add a few low platforms connected by ramps, never overhanging another playable surface. Keep paths broad and obvious, hide cutouts at least four studs deep, and enclose the whole arena with a simple block wall. Place the three character representations as visible focal props without obstructing chase routes. Frame the complete arena legibly from above.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, then dress the isometric view from it; the zero-dead-end loop topology must be valid for fair chasing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, driven by the requested active one-versus-four chase. The theme is plain abstract, blocky classic Roblox with strong yellow and blue accents. Use `P6` because `warren-looping` must be connected and dead-end-free by construction.
