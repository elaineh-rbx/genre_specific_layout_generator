# Scene prompt
Create a stylized Roblox wizard-school world arranged as seven small, focused chapter set pieces following a young wizard’s seven school years. Begin with arrival at a grand castle school, then progress through distinct magical school-year locations, culminating in a final battle set piece against a dark wizard; each chapter is a clear, compact space for its major life moment rather than a large explorable region.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Adventure is the sole genre. The defining structure is a story-driven journey through seven school-year chapters; the final battle is one episode, not a separate dominant combat loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-chaptered`, with exactly seven genuinely separate compact maps representing the seven school years. The matching preset is Story, retaining its chapter thresholds, staged reveals, and one-way progression.

## Config requirements
Use `gate-chapter` to give every set piece a clear architectural arrival and departure threshold. Use `reveal-exit` so each compact chapter opens toward its major magical-school landmark or dramatic event space, and use `gate-oneway` to keep completed story beats behind the player. The first chapter must visibly establish arrival at a grand castle school; the seventh must provide a distinct ruined or battle-ready castle setting for the confrontation with the dark wizard. Keep all imagery an original, stylized Roblox wizard-school interpretation rather than a direct film replica.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly seven uncatalogued chapter-entry spawn markers, one at the beginning of each set piece, oriented toward that chapter’s first landmark. Place exactly seven chapter-completion trigger volumes at the far end of the corresponding spaces, each against its `gate-chapter` threshold. Transitions, story scripting, and combat behavior remain outside layout.

## Layout components
Build seven small, separate set pieces. Chapter one is a ceremonial castle-school arrival court and entry hall. Chapters two through six are distinct compact magical school-year spaces, each with one strong landmark, a short readable route, and a chapter exit; vary them across castle chambers, courtyards, grounds, and magical study or mystery spaces without expanding into full regions. Chapter seven is the final battle arena within a damaged castle precinct. Give every map a firm boundary and compose each around one major life moment, with no unnecessary open-world connective terrain.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first for each chapter and make it authoritative, then derive each top-down plan; these are short bounded set pieces whose visual identity leads and whose topology is not procedurally constrained.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use large aggregate scale because there are exactly seven maps, although each individual map is small. The theme is colorful stylized wizard-school fantasy that darkens toward the finale. Route with `P4` because the chapters are separate maps.
