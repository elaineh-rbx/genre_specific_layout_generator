# Scene prompt

Create a small, playable free-driving steam railway map set on a period New Zealand branch line, spanning one or two valleys. Rail lines run through mountainous hills and alongside or across rivers, with a few simple stations, small rail yards, and depots placed along the route as places to assemble trains. Include open track areas suitable for placing passenger and freight cars anywhere in the world, with steam engines as the rolling-stock focus. The landscape supports changing weather conditions.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Simulation: this is free-form steam-train operation and consist assembly, with no race, lap, or finish condition.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open`, one Shared Persistent World spanning one or two connected valleys and allowing trains to travel freely among stations and yards. Match the Vehicle Sim preset, retaining its open common world but replacing road hauling with a branch-line rail network and omitting the unrelated shared resource field.

## Config requirements

Draw a continuous branch-line railway with passing and yard tracks rather than use the road-specific `path-road-vehicle`. Use `terrain-relief` for mountainous hills enclosing the valleys, and `water-body` for rivers running beside and beneath the railway. Draw a few simple stations, small rail yards, depots, steam locomotives, passenger cars, and freight cars. Keep station and depot buildings as exterior structures because enterable interiors were not requested.

## Layout requirements

Place non-drawn rolling-stock spawn markers throughout usable open track areas, with concentrated placement in every yard and depot so steam engines, passenger cars, and freight cars can be assembled without fouling the main line. Place coupling alignment points along yard tracks. No finish, checkpoint, scoring, or enemy volumes are needed.

## Layout components

Build one or two compact valleys connected by a winding rail corridor. The main line should cross rivers on bridges, run beside riverbanks, and curve around hills rather than climb impossible grades. Distribute a few simple stations along the route, each paired where practical with a small yard or depot. Provide long clear sidings for spawning and coupling cars. Mountains and wooded hills form the outer boundary; river channels and grassy valley floors organize the interior. Frame the scene so the branch line remains traceable across the whole map. Changing weather is a runtime visual state, not additional geometry.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, then dress the isometric view from it, because connected, non-conflicting rail lines and usable yards are essential.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is small, constrained to one or two playable valleys. The theme is a period New Zealand steam branch line under changeable rural weather. Use `tiered` for mountainous relief and `CHECK` for river play-space representation; no overhang, interior transition, or separate map is required.
