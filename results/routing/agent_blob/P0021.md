# Scene prompt
Create a 3D ocean exploration map with three small, distinct islands inspired by recognizable Japanese areas, all close enough to see one another across open water. Each island has a compact shore settlement or landmark area, paths leading inland to quest and combat spaces, and locations for cursed-spirit-inspired NPCs and major disaster-themed boss encounters. Arrange navigable sea lanes between the islands for boat travel, with each island forming a separate explorable zone.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG, with Adventure secondary. The source explicitly combines fighting, technique leveling, quests, and bosses with Blox Fruits-style island exploration.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`: all three islands and the traversable sea coexist on one contiguous ocean map. No preset remains recognisable because the requested explore-fight-quest loop lacks the fixed sanctuary/economy bundle of Action RPG and explicitly excludes building from the survival bundle.

## Config requirements
Use `island-cluster` for exactly three small Japanese-inspired islands close enough for mutual visibility; `water-body` for open ocean deep and broad enough for boat travel; `settlement-density` for a compact shore settlement on each island; `path-trail` for routes from each shore inland; `landmark-focal` for recognizable Japanese-area silhouettes and boss destinations; and `hazard-aggro` for cursed-spirit combat grounds set back from ordinary travel paths.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` emitters in each island’s inland combat spaces, with separate boss emitters at the major disaster-themed encounter locations. Place exactly three boat embarkation and arrival markers, one at each shore settlement, aligned to unobstructed sea lanes. Keep enemy emitters away from docks and settlement circulation so arrival remains readable and usable.

## Layout components
Build exactly three distinct small islands on one ocean surface. Give every island a compact shore settlement or landmark area, a dock, inland paths, quest spaces, ordinary cursed-spirit encounter grounds, and a major boss destination. Differentiate the islands through recognizable Japanese coastal, urban, shrine, mountain, or garden motifs without copying one arrangement. Form a clear triangular boat-travel network with broad navigable water and uninterrupted sightlines between islands. Beaches and rocky shorelines bound each land zone; the outer ocean boundary encloses the full map. Compose all three islands together in frame.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the top-down plan is rendered first and authoritative because island spacing, clear sea lanes, and dock alignment determine whether boat travel works.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, driven by three small islands plus travel water. The theme is colorful supernatural Japanese fantasy. Use `CHECK` because water travel is volumetric and the three-island cluster must be checked for self-occluding play space; no `P4` applies because all islands share one ocean map.
