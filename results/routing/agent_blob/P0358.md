# Scene prompt
Crea un mapa 3D para un entrenador de puntería moderno, con una estética oscura e iluminación de neón azul y rojo. Un lobby central limpio contiene accesos físicos mediante botones a seis salas de entrenamiento separadas: una sala de blancos estáticos, una de blancos móviles, una para practicar contra bots humanoides, una de prueba de reacción, una de disparos rápidos a posiciones variables y una sala de seguimiento continuo. Cada sala es un espacio enfocado de práctica con líneas de tiro y zonas despejadas para objetivos; la pista de tiro más larga mide aproximadamente 60 studs. Mantén una composición interior compacta de instalación tecnológica, con el lobby como núcleo y las salas conectadas alrededor de él.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Shooter: this is explicitly an aim trainer with six shooting-practice modes.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single` from outside Shooter’s usual shape set: one compact enclosed facility is the entire map, with a central lobby and six surrounding rooms. The preset is Aim Trainer, substituting this hub interior for its directed range and dropping the unrequested weapon station.

## Config requirements
Use `target-practice` for visible static, moving, reaction, flick, and tracking targets within dedicated clear firing rooms. Draw six distinct training rooms around the clean central lobby, with the longest firing lane approximately 60 studs.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `target-practice` in five target rooms, with target points distributed according to each room’s mode. Use `spawner-npc` only in the humanoid-bot room. Place six button trigger zones, one at each lobby access, each paired to its corresponding room. Keep target motion, timing, statistics, and sensitivity controls out of geometry.

## Layout components
Build one central lobby plus six separate connected rooms: static targets, moving targets, humanoid bots, reaction test, flick shots, and continuous tracking. Short corridors or doorways radiate from the lobby; every room has a firing position, clear target field, and dark technological walls. The boundary is the sealed facility shell. Compose the plan with the lobby centered and all six rooms legible around it.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the hub-to-six-room connectivity must be valid before the neon interior is dressed.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by six rooms and a maximum 60-stud lane. The theme is dark professional technology with blue and red neon. Use `P0`; this is one interior-only map with no outside transition or overhang.
