## Enriched image prompt

Centre a safe Spawn Village containing a clustered shop, Quest NPC, and Monster Research Lab, with simple enterable interiors for the shop and lab. Dirt paths branch from the village to a Slime Meadow, Dense Forest, Flower Field, and a short accessible cave entered through a visible cave mouth. A river crosses the map into a pond and waterfall, with a wooden bridge carrying a main path over it. Add an ancient tree as the major landmark, small rounded hills, animated grass, and cliffs around the map edges. Provide habitat ground for Green Slime, Blue Slime, Bee, Bunny, and Fox populations. Keep the style premium but efficient: rounded low-poly forms, bright colours, soft lighting, wind, and restrained atmospheric particles.

## Shape and preset

Use `building-interior` for the shop, lab, and short cave; `path-trail` for dirt paths; `water-body` for river, pond, and waterfall; `terrain-relief` for hills, cliffs, waterfall drop, and cave overhang; `landmark-focal` for the ancient tree; and `gate-progression` for visible future-area boundaries. Use `npc-population` in both streams for the Quest NPC and the five named wild-monster species with their habitat ground.

## Layout requirements
Place `npc-population` zones for Green Slime and Blue Slime around meadow and water ground, Bees in the Flower Field, Bunnies near meadow or forest edge, Foxes in Dense Forest, and the Quest NPC in Spawn Village. Add a free-text SpawnZone in the village. No hostile `spawner-npc` is used because these wild monsters flee rather than attack.

## Layout components

Subordinate interior zones are the Shop, Monster Research Lab, and Cave, all linked physically to the exterior rather than split into separate maps.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the compact zone relationships, path connectivity, bridge crossing, habitats, and linked interiors should be fixed before decorative dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale, fixed by the 250-by-250-stud world and the need to read individual buildings and habitats. The theme is bright rounded low-poly fantasy forest. `P3` covers exterior-to-interior transitions, `P2` covers the cave overhang, `tiered` captures hills and cliffs, and `CHECK` applies to the river, pond, and waterfall.
