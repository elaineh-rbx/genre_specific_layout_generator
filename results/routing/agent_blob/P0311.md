# Scene prompt
Create a bright, colorful low-poly open-world monster-collecting adventure map, World 1, approximately 250x250 studs. Build a Spawn Village as the central hub, with a simple enterable Shop and Monster Research Lab, plus a Quest NPC area. Dirt paths lead outward through a Slime Meadow, dense forest, and flower field, connecting small hills, an ancient tree landmark, a river with a wooden bridge, a pond, and a waterfall. Place a cave entrance leading into a short accessible cave area. Enclose the outer edges with cliffs, with the major biomes and landmarks distributed to encourage exploration. Use rounded stylized terrain, bright foliage, soft atmospheric lighting, and subtle animated grass, wind, and environmental particle effects.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure, with RPG secondary. Exploration, discovery, and collecting lead the brief; XP, levels, equipment upgrades, and unlockable areas provide RPG progression.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open` as one contiguous 250-by-250-stud World 1 with an attached short cave interior. The preset is Exploration, retaining its trails, focal landmark, and discovery spaces while adding the stated village services and monster habitats.

## Config requirements
Use `path-trail` for dirt paths radiating from Spawn Village through the meadow, forest, and flower field. Use `landmark-focal` for the ancient tree and waterfall as orientation anchors. Treat the short cave as `alcove-secret`, an optional discovery pocket off the main exploration network. Add `building-interior` for the enterable Shop, Monster Research Lab, and cave transition. Use `npc-population` for the Quest NPC and five starter monster species—Green Slime, Blue Slime, Bee, Bunny, and Fox. Include `water-body` for river, pond, and waterfall and `terrain-relief` for small hills and enclosing cliffs.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `npc-population` at the Quest NPC area and as habitat markers for all five starter species. Put slime markers in Slime Meadow, bees in the flower field, bunnies at meadow-forest edges, foxes in denser woods, and reserve multiple separated points for both slime colors. Add catch-and-respawn spawn points inside each habitat radius. Place one player spawn in the village, and link doors for the Shop and Lab plus the cave entrance to their interior arrivals.

## Layout components
Build a roughly 250-by-250-stud cliff-bounded world. Spawn Village occupies the center with Shop, Research Lab, and Quest NPC area. Dirt paths branch to Slime Meadow, dense forest, flower field, small hills, ancient tree, river crossing, pond, waterfall, and cave entrance. One wooden bridge crosses the river. The cave is short and accessible rather than a deep dungeon. Use rounded low-poly ground, bright foliage, animated grass, and subtle wind and particles. Keep every biome reachable from the hub and frame the ancient tree and waterfall as distant navigational pulls.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the premium colorful world look leads, and its top-down is projected afterward because the exploration network needs clarity but not generated maze or circuit validity.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, fixed by 250 by 250 studs. The theme is bright rounded low-poly fantasy forest with soft atmosphere. Use `P3` for entered buildings and cave, `P2` because the cave lies beneath enclosing terrain, `tiered` for hills and cliffs, and `CHECK` for the river, pond, and waterfall.
