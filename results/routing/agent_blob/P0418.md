# Scene prompt
Create a realistic 3D deep-sea fishing map centered on a seaworthy fishing boat in a small navigable open-ocean area. The boat has an enclosed small navigation cabin with a wheel, attached fishing rods along the sides, and crab nets on deck; it sits amid strong rolling waves and visible seawater spray. Scatter fishing spots across the surrounding ocean, with at most one small island kept distant in the background as minor scenery rather than a destination.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation: players operate a fishing vessel and repeat fishing tasks in a persistent navigable space.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, a small shared navigable ocean explored in any order. Vessel Operation is the nearest preset, but the preset is none because its `vehicle-deck` assumption treats the passing world as backdrop, while this prompt explicitly makes the ocean navigable and drops the preset’s shop bundle.

## Config requirements
Use `water-body` for the strong rolling open sea, `building-interior` for the small enclosed navigation cabin entered from the exposed deck, `path-circulation` for clear walkable movement between the wheel, rods, and crab nets, and `trigger-task` for visibly differentiated fishing spots. Draw one realistic seaworthy boat, attached rods along both sides, crab nets on deck, seawater spray, and at most one distant minor island.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `trigger-task` at several dispersed fishing spots across the navigable water, away from the distant scenery island. Fishing interactions should align with the rods and nets attached to the boat. The cabin wheel needs one operating point inside the cabin. No count is implied for the fishing spots.

## Layout components
The main zone is a compact open-ocean area containing the movable boat and scattered fishing grounds. The boat has an open working deck, one enclosed wheel cabin, side-mounted rods, and crab nets. Rolling water encloses the map; one small island may sit near the far boundary only as scenery. Compose the frame around the boat, with enough surrounding sea to communicate travel.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative, then project the top-down view; vessel detail, wave scale, and realism lead this scene.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium because the sea must support short vessel travel while remaining compact. The theme is photoreal deep-sea fishing in rough weather. Use `P3` for the deck-to-cabin transition and `CHECK` for traversable open water; the modest wave volume should be checked for self-occlusion.
