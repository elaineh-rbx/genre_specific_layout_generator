# Scene prompt
Create a compact classic Roblox 3D vertical-slice map with a small green studded baseplate, sized only for a player to spawn and physically walk to a shop stall. Place the spawn point on the baseplate with clear open ground leading to a physical shop stall model nearby; leave enough surrounding space for walking and approaching the stall.

# Agent decision

## Genre
This is `No Genre`: the brief is a focused interaction and UI vertical slice, not a tycoon or other simulation loop. The build still has a real walkable 3D space.

## Shape and preset
Use the Explorable Place preset, keeping all five no-genre axes at their defaults: exterior, single-surface, single-zone, dressed topology, and grounded play-space. The compact baseplate is one contiguous walkable setting centered on the shop interaction.

## Config requirements
Use `landmark-focal` for the physical classic-Roblox shop stall, visually distinct and immediately readable from the spawn. Use `path-circulation` for the clear open strip of baseplate between arrival and stall. Use `spawn-area` in its drawn form for an unobstructed arrival pad facing the stall. Also render the stall itself with a counter, blocky canopy, and stud-textured classic parts.

## Layout requirements
Use `spawn-area` in its placed form for exactly one player spawn on the green baseplate, oriented toward the stall with clear walking room ahead. Place one interaction marker at the stall counter for the ProximityPrompt, close enough to require a physical approach. No other checkpoints, pickups, or trigger volumes belong in this layout.

## Layout components
Build one small rectangular green studded baseplate. Put the single spawn near one short edge and the shop stall nearby across a short stretch of open ground, leaving a direct, wide mobile-friendly approach. The stall is a compact block-built structure with a counter and readable front face; keep its rear near the opposite edge so the composition leads from spawn to destination. Preserve open clearance around both the spawn and counter. The baseplate edge is the explicit map boundary. Frame both spawn and stall together, with the stall as the dominant subject.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the classic studded look and clear spawn-to-stall composition first, then derive the simple top-down plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a small scale suited to a focused vertical slice. The theme is classic blocky Roblox with green studs and retro proportions. Route through `P0`; the single flat exterior surface adds no modifier.
