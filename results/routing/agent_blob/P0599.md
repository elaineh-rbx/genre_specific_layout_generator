## Enriched image prompt
Create one small bright stylized mountain peak designed for a focused walk of about one minute from one side to the other. Shape a clear walkable route across the mountain relief and compose attractive scenic views along the ascent and from the summit. Keep the map tidy and easy to read, with the single peak as the dominant focal form rather than extending into a mountain range. Use natural mountain edges to contain the walk and frame the route, summit, and surrounding view together in one appealing isometric scene. Do not add mechanics, objectives, hazards, buildings, or activities.

## Genre
The result is No Genre. The author explicitly requests only a mountain place to walk through and admire, with no game activity owned by a genre.

## Shape and preset

## Config requirements
Use `terrain-relief` for the single small mountain peak and its climbable slopes. Use `landmark-focal` for the summit as the dominant orientation anchor. Use `path-circulation` for the one-minute crossing route, and `vignette-photo` for deliberately composed scenic views along the route and at the summit. Bend all four concepts to the same compact bright mountain rather than adding unrelated attractions.

## Layout requirements
Nothing additional must be placed after segmentation. The author requests a visible walking environment only, with no collectibles, triggers, spawners, checkpoints, scoring volumes, or other mechanics.

## Layout components
The build is one exterior mountain zone containing a single peak, a clear crossing path, lower approach ground on each side, and a summit scenic point. The path climbs and descends over tiered natural relief without stacked or overhanging surfaces. Natural slopes and map edges form the boundary. Compose the route diagonally through the frame, with the summit above the midground and the attractive outward view remaining visible.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the bright stylized mountain silhouette, crossing route, summit, and scenic composition first, then derive the top-down plan. The isometric image is authoritative because the visual experience leads and the route is simple.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is medium, driven by a single mountain crossed in about one minute while trail and summit details remain legible. The theme is bright stylized mountain scenery with an inviting, polished look. `tiered` is required for the mountain's real elevation without overhangs; no other modifier is forced.
