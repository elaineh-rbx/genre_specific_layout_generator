# Scene prompt

Create a 3D game map showing a small, dense abandoned Japanese village entered from a tunnel lobby. In the foreground lobby, players spawn before the tunnel beside an NPC station; passing through the tunnel leads into the village. The village contains a handful of weathered, genuinely enterable houses arranged along narrow paths with key outdoor areas between them, all compact enough to explore in one session. Distribute five possessed dolls across house interiors and outdoor locations, with memories, exorcising tools, and special items placed throughout searchable rooms and the surrounding village spaces. The tunnel serves as the return connection from the cleared village back to the lobby.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Adventure, with Puzzle secondary because players recover memories that support confrontations with the dolls.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open` from the Scavenger Hunt preset: one compact village searched freely for five targets and supporting items. Retain collectible objectives and discovery spaces, but omit the preset’s physical quest board because progress is tracked in UI.

## Config requirements

Build `path-trail` as narrow village lanes connecting the tunnel, houses, and outdoor search areas. Add `alcove-secret` as tucked-away rooms, overgrown corners, and spaces behind household debris. Use `settlement-density` for the small dense cluster of weathered Japanese houses and `building-interior` for the handful of genuinely searchable homes. Represent `npc-population` as the tutorial-and-shop NPC station in the lobby and the five possessed dolls, with each doll’s visible setting preserved.

## Layout requirements

Place the functional side of `npc-population` for one lobby NPC station and exactly five possessed dolls divided between house interiors and outdoor village locations. Place `collectible-nodes` for memories, exorcising tools, and special items throughout searchable rooms and exterior alcoves. Add a player spawn before the tunnel and a return destination at the same lobby connection after the village is cleared.

## Layout components

Compose a foreground lobby with a spawn apron and NPC station facing a tunnel. Let the tunnel open into a compact abandoned village of a handful of weathered houses along narrow, interconnected paths. Include small courtyards, an overgrown shrine-like pocket, and other key outdoor clearings between houses. Furnish enterable rooms with sparse decayed interiors and searchable corners. Distribute five doll settings across both interiors and exteriors, and spread item hiding places throughout. Enclose the village with dense vegetation, walls, and terrain, keeping the tunnel as its only connection to the lobby.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: establish the abandoned Japanese village atmosphere and readable doll settings first, then derive linked top-down plans from the authoritative view.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is medium: one-session exploration across a lobby, a handful of houses, and five doll sites. The theme is eerie, weathered Japanese folk horror without a sprawling footprint. Use `P3` because play crosses tunnel and house exterior-to-interior boundaries.
