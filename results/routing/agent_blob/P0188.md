# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] scale — Q: How big is the village - a handful of houses, or a sprawl you need several sessions to search? A: Make it a small, dense abandoned village with a handful of houses and key outdoor areas, intended to be explored in one session.
- [author] goal — Q: How many dolls are there, and does clearing them all end the run? A: Use a small set of 5 possessed dolls. Clearing all of them completes the village run and returns players to the lobby.
- [author] shape — Q: Should the buildings be genuinely enterable interiors, or searchable from outside? Interiors are a slower build. A: Buildings should have genuinely enterable interiors, limited to the handful of key houses so players can search for memories, tools, and special items.

## Enriched image prompt
Create two linked spaces for a supernatural exploration game set around an abandoned Japanese village. The lobby is a compact forecourt where players spawn facing a tunnel, with one tutorial-and-shop NPC beside the entrance. The tunnel transports players into a small, dense abandoned village containing a handful of key houses with genuinely enterable interiors and several searchable outdoor areas. Arrange narrow village circulation between the houses and distribute places for memories, exorcising tools, and a special item across interiors and exterior landmarks. Provide five distinct doll encounter locations throughout the village, mixing indoor and outdoor sites, while keeping the whole village explorable in one session.

## Genre
The genre is Adventure.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-staged`, a Zone (Lobby and Isolated Stage) taken from outside Adventure's usual set because the tunnel lobby and village are separate maps. The preset is none; no Adventure bundle combines this lobby split, enterable village search, and fixed doll encounters.

## Config requirements
Use `social-hub` for the tunnel lobby, `spawn-first-reveal` from Entertainment for the arrival view toward the tunnel, `npc-population` for the tutorial-and-shop NPC, `teleporter-link` for the tunnel connection routed as separate places, `settlement-density` for the small dense village, and `building-interior` for the handful of searchable key houses.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawn-first-reveal` SpawnZone in the lobby facing the tunnel and one `npc-population` Zone beside it for the tutorial-and-shop NPC. Place paired `teleporter-link` Teleporters at the lobby tunnel and village arrival point, also serving the return. Distribute `collectible-nodes` for memories, tools, and the special item across key-house interiors and outdoor areas. Place exactly five `spawner-npc` Spawners at distinct indoor and outdoor doll encounter sites.

## Layout components
Create two map segments: Lobby, a bounded `space-bounded` tunnel forecourt; and Village, a fixed `settlement-static` cluster with a handful of houses, narrow paths, key outdoor search areas, and enterable interior subspaces. The tunnel is the only connection between maps. Keep all village routes connected and compose each map as its own readable frame.

## Render order
Use `topdown` because village circulation, interior links, collectible coverage, and five distinct encounter sites must be established before atmospheric dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale so house interiors and searchable objects remain legible across the small dense village. The theme is abandoned rural Japanese supernatural horror. `P4` is required for the separate lobby and village maps, and `P3` is required for travel between village streets and house interiors.
