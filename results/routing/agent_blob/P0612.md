- [author] goal — Q: Does the player drive the bus or ride it as a passenger, and does the route loop or end? A: The player rides the bus as a passenger, sitting down while it moves. The route loops so players can stay on and keep riding.
- [agent_inferred] theme — Q: What should the unspecified road environment look like? A: Use a plain modern urban street with simple Roblox-style buildings and sidewalks.
- [agent_inferred] bus_access — Q: Do players board from outside during the ride? A: No; begin inside one bus and keep the playable passenger space on the vehicle.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a small 3D bus-riding map centered on one passenger bus traveling around a short looping modern urban street. Build the bus with a walkable interior, rows of seats, a clear central aisle, windows, and enough headroom for normal Roblox movement and jumping while aboard. Keep the player inside the bus rather than building an exterior boarding sequence. Lay out one simple closed road loop with gentle corners, minimal sidewalks, and plain modern building facades as passing scenery. Make the road centerline continuous so the bus can circulate indefinitely without reversing or reaching a terminal finish. Frame the bus interior as the primary playable space and the compact street loop as its surrounding set.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation because the experience is riding a vehicle continuously rather than racing to a finish.

## Shape and preset
Use `vehicle-deck`, a Zone (Vehicle Deck), because the walkable player space is the moving bus while the street scenery passes outside. No Simulation preset fits a passenger-only bus ride without adding operation tasks or economy, so the preset is none.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for the short closed bus roadway, selecting its `P6` route because this road is the vehicle’s required movement course rather than a background street. Add one scene-specific bus cabin with seats, aisle, and windows because no canonical option represents passenger seating.

## Layout requirements
Place one free-text Path with no canonical option ID along the exact center of the closed road loop to drive the bus’s repeated motion. No finish, lap-count, task, or vehicle-control trigger is required.

## Layout components
The map contains one passenger bus, one walkable cabin, seat rows, one central aisle, windows, one short closed road loop, minimal sidewalks, simple exterior facades, and a compact outer boundary. The road joins back to itself cleanly and has no branch or terminal.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: make the closed road centerline authoritative so the bus can loop indefinitely, then dress the bus and urban isometric scene from that plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is small because the bus interior and one short street loop must remain legible. The theme is plain modern urban Roblox styling. The modifier is `P6` because the bus route must be a valid closed loop by construction.
