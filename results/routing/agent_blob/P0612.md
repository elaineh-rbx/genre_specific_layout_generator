# Scene prompt
Create a small 3D bus-riding map centered on one short street route that loops continuously. A passenger bus travels along the road with seating inside for riders, while simple roadside surroundings frame the compact route.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation: the activity is an open-ended passenger-bus ride rather than a race or goal-driven journey.

## Shape and preset
Use `route-circuit`, taken from outside Simulation’s usual shapes because the short street must close into a continuous loop. The preset is none; Vessel Operation focuses on the vehicle as the whole walkable map, while this brief requires the surrounding road loop as part of the build.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for one bus-width street loop with safe turns and an uninterrupted drivable surface. The image must include one passenger bus with a visible, simple cabin and rider seating, plus uncluttered roadside scenery that frames the route. No settlement-density option is needed because the prompt asks only for simple surroundings, not a town or city.

## Layout requirements
Place one bus route spline around the full loop, with ordered movement markers through every bend and no branching path. Place one bus spawn on a straight section, oriented with the route. Add seat interaction positions inside the bus for passengers and a player arrival or boarding marker beside the bus, clear of its travel lane. No winner zone or endpoint is required because the ride is intentionally continuous.

## Layout components
Build one small closed street loop with a short straight, broad corners, and enough clearance for the bus to circulate without clipping. Put the bus and boarding area on the straight section. Inside the bus, provide a simple aisle and several fixed passenger seats; no exact seat count was stated. Surround the road with lightweight sidewalks, grass verges, a few low-detail buildings or trees, and a simple outer boundary that does not crowd the vehicle. Compose the loop so its closure and the bus are both immediately readable from above.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the loop must close and remain continuously drivable, but this is not a racing circuit eligible for `authored_plan`.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by the single short street. The theme is simple everyday Roblox transit. Use `P6` because the circuit must be valid by construction; no additional modifier is forced.
