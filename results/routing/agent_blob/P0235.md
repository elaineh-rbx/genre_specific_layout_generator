# Scene prompt

Create a compact open-world suburban city map arranged in four quadrants, each spanning a few city blocks and connected by asphalt roads with sidewalks and streetlights. Place a central town-square park at the intersection of the quadrants. Build a downtown district around the park containing a hospital, police station with visible jail-cell area, fire station, bank, grocery store, daycare, and a two-story school. Dedicate one quadrant or neighborhood section to six separate pre-built residential houses on claimable lots, each with a driveway and simple customizable yard. Include vehicle garage areas at the police, hospital, fire, and civilian-facing locations, with parking space for cruisers, ambulances, fire trucks, and civilian cars; place relevant job equipment racks inside the police station, hospital, fire station, and bank.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, centered on open-ended suburban town life.

## Shape and preset

Use `settlement-claimable`: a fixed city with six finished houses that players claim and customize rather than build. The preset is Life.

## Config requirements

Draw `social-hub` as the central town-square park, `path-road-vehicle` as asphalt two-way roads at least 30 studs wide, and `district-zoned` as four clear quadrants separating downtown, housing, and service uses. Use `building-interior` for the hospital, police station and jail, fire station, bank, grocery, daycare, two-story school, and homes. Apply `settlement-density` as a compact city of a few blocks per quadrant. Include sidewalks, streetlights, driveways, parking and garage aprons, visible jail cells, and the named job-equipment racks.

## Layout requirements

Place six house-claim markers, one per residential lot at its driveway approach. Place vehicle spawn markers at the police, hospital, fire, and civilian garage areas, each within its matching parking apron. Place interactable pickup markers at the handcuff rack in the police station, medical-kit rack in the hospital, fire-hose rack in the fire station, and duffel-bag rack in the bank.

## Layout components

Center the four-quadrant road plan on one park. Cluster seven downtown buildings around it: hospital, police station, fire station, bank, grocery store, daycare, and two-story school. Reserve one neighborhood section for exactly six detached houses, each on a separate claimable lot with driveway and customizable yard. Connect every quadrant by asphalt streets and sidewalks; place streetlights along them. Keep terrain mostly level and enclose the compact city with a simple suburban perimeter.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, then dress the isometric view; the four-quadrant street plan and exact building-to-road relationships must remain legible and connected.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a large scale, driven by four multi-block quadrants and fourteen named primary buildings. The theme is bright contemporary suburban roleplay. Apply `P3` for linked exterior streets and enterable interiors, plus `P2` because the two-story school contains vertically overlapping floors.
