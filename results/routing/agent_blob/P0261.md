# Scene prompt

Create a huge outdoor music festival map on one large festival field, centered on a massive main stage with neon lighting, moving LED screens, and laser fixtures. A broad open dance floor fills the area directly in front of the stage. Arrange food trucks, VVIP lounge areas, and neon decorations around the field perimeter, framing the central stage-and-dance-floor space without additional stages.

# Agent decision

The dominant genre is Entertainment: the map is an outdoor music hangout and dance venue with no competitive or timed objective.

Use `venue-stage` from the Performance Venue preset. The entire field follows one-way venue orientation toward a single massive main stage, with the dance floor and perimeter amenities supporting that focal composition.

Draw the visible half of `spawn-first-reveal` as a rear-field arrival view aimed at the stage. Arrange food trucks and distinct VVIP lounge areas around the perimeter, leaving the center open.

## Layout requirements

Place the layout half of `spawn-first-reveal` near the rear center of the festival field, facing the main stage. Add uncatalogued VVIP-access triggers at each lounge entrance, aligned to the lounge boundaries while keeping the general perimeter route public. No performer queue, scoring volume, checkpoint, or stage transport is needed.

## Layout components

Build one huge flat outdoor field. Place the massive stage on one edge, a broad rectangular dance floor immediately before it, and a generous circulation ring beyond the dance floor. Distribute food trucks and VVIP lounges around the outer ring, alternating them with neon sculptures and wayfinding decorations so they frame rather than block the stage. Keep all major sightlines radial toward the stage. Use crowd barriers, speaker towers, lighting truss, LED panels, and laser fixtures as named props. Enclose the field with a festival fence and service boundary, with no additional stages.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: establish the authoritative festival spectacle first, then derive the top-down plan because the simple radial topology is not validity-critical.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is huge, driven by one mass-audience field and its perimeter amenities. The theme is neon outdoor mega-festival. Use `P0`; everything occupies one continuous, single-level field.
