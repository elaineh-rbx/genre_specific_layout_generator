# Scene prompt

Create a cartoon-bright 3D pet-stealing game map with 6 open, visible base plots arranged close around a central pet market. The market centers on a red carpet display area with one pet for each rarity, including a first pet priced at $50. Each base is clearly readable from outside, with space for displayed pets and a prominent owner-name sign; unoccupied plots show an "empty base" sign. Keep short, direct paths between the central market and every base for quick sprints.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Simulation: each player owns a persistent base, acquires and displays pets, and raids nearby bases in a compact collection loop.

## Shape and preset

Use `plot-isolated` for 6 independent player-owned plots that cannot overlap. Start from the Sandbox preset because it supplies isolated personal plots, collectible subjects, and a shared social center, adapted here from resources to displayed pets.

## Config requirements

Apply `social-hub` as the central pet market and red-carpet display; `path-circulation` as six short direct sprint paths from the market to the bases; and `collectible-nodes` as visible pet display positions in the market and each owned plot. Keep every base open-sided and readable from the exterior, with a prominent owner-name sign and an “empty base” state for vacant plots.

## Layout requirements

Place the functional half of `collectible-nodes` as one market pet point for each rarity and as pet display slots inside every base. Mark the first market pet position with a visible $50 price sign. Place one ownership/arrival marker per base, paired with its owner-name sign and empty-base state. Keep all collectible positions clear of the sprint paths.

## Layout components

Build one central red-carpet market surrounded closely by exactly 6 comparable open base plots. Give each base a visible pet-display floor, an owner-sign frontage, and no enclosing walls that obscure the collection. Six short radial paths connect the market directly to the six plot entrances. The market has a rarity display lineup with one pet per rarity and the first pet labeled $50. Use flat bright paving and a clear outer map boundary. Frame all six bases and the market together.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative; visibility, signage, and the compact radial composition lead, while topology is simple.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is small, driven by 6 tightly grouped bases. The theme is simple cartoon-bright Roblox pet-market play. Use `P0`; all requested geometry fits one open surface with no interiors, overhangs, or separate maps.
