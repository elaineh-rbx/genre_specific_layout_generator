# Scene prompt
Create a whimsical storybook-inspired world centered on a small, tall tower with about five interior floors to explore and climb through. The tower stands within a compact whimsical courtyard world surrounding its base.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is `adventure`: exploration and the climb through a storybook tower are the activity, with no combat, stats, or timed obstacle challenge.

## Shape and preset
Use a described shape: one compact exterior courtyard feeding an enterable tower with about five stacked interior floors. Its axes are transition enclosure, stacked verticality, single-zone count, dressed structure, and grounded-surface play. The catalogue’s `interior-single` rejects the surrounding courtyard, `rooms-sequence` assumes a corridor-led room sequence rather than stacked floors, and `course-tower` would incorrectly impose a physics-valid obby route. Use the Exploration preset with this substituted shape; keep its landmark and trail, but drop the unrequested secret alcoves.

## Config requirements
Use `landmark-focal` for the tall whimsical tower dominating the courtyard. Use `path-trail` for a short storybook path from the courtyard edge to its entrance. Use `building-interior` for the tower’s about five explorable floors, visibly connected by interior stairs or ramps. Add freeform whimsical courtyard planting and storybook architectural details.

## Layout requirements
Nothing needs post-segmentation placement. There are no stated quest items, save checkpoints, triggers, gates, teleporters, or spawners.

## Layout components
Build one compact courtyard around a small-footprint tower. Lead a curved path from the courtyard boundary to the single tower entrance. Inside, stack about five distinct floors connected upward in a continuous climb, with landings and openings that make each level readable. Keep the courtyard flat and bounded by low storybook walls, hedges, and whimsical planting. Center the tower in composition and show enough cutaway or separated floor information for every interior level to remain buildable.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the authoritative stacked tower and courtyard relationship first, then derive the top-down views needed from it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium because a compact footprint contains about five floors. The theme is whimsical, literary storybook fantasy. Use `P3` for movement between courtyard and interior, and `P2` because the tower floors overhang one another.
