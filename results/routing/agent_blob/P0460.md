# Scene prompt
Create a 3D small-sided association football pitch with standard soccer field markings and a goal at each end, set within a simple park setting around the field.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant and only genre is Sports, specifically a small-sided association football game.

## Shape and preset
Use the single `space-bounded` shape for the pitch’s rigid touchline and goal-line perimeter. The preset is Court Sport, adapted to a small-sided outdoor soccer field with the simple park surround requested.

## Config requirements
Draw `trigger-bounds` as standard visible touchlines and goal lines around the rectangular pitch. Draw `trigger-scoring` as two goal frames with nets, one centered on each short end. Draw `startpoint-play` as a center spot and balanced restart positions. Beyond the playing surface, draw a restrained park border of level grass, a few trees, and simple pedestrian paths without stadium architecture or dense clutter.

## Layout requirements
Place one `trigger-bounds` detection perimeter exactly along the soccer markings. Place two `trigger-scoring` goal volumes within the two net mouths. Place `startpoint-play` markers at midfield and in mirrored team positions on opposite halves, preserving balanced approaches to both goals.

## Layout components
Build one flat, compact rectangular small-sided pitch at the center of the map. Mark its halfway line, center circle and spot, touchlines, goal lines, and compact penalty areas in clear white paint. Center one goal at each end. Wrap the pitch in a narrow safety apron before transitioning to open park grass. Use sparse trees and simple paths around the outer edge to establish the park setting while keeping sightlines and ball retrieval clear. The boundary is an open park perimeter rather than stadium walls. Compose the pitch as the clear focal point, with the park acting only as a quiet frame.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: render the plan first and make it authoritative because field proportions, mirrored goals, markings, and functional trigger alignment must remain exact; dress the park view from that plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, determined by a compact small-sided pitch and narrow park surround. The theme is casual neighborhood soccer in a clean green park. Route through `P0`; the entire map is a single flat, unobstructed surface with no costly transition or overhang.
