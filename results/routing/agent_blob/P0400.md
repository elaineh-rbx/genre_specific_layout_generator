# Scene prompt

Create a bright, cartoony 3D factory map divided into multiple small individual engineer plots, where each player builds a separate room-sized disaster-course gauntlet from scratch. Each plot has a clear entrance at one end and exit at the other, with a compact route between them for NPCs to traverse quickly, leaving space for modular hazards such as lava channels, spinning blades, collapsing bridges, giant boulders, tornado devices, lightning emitters, lasers, explosive props, rolling obstacles, and swinging wrecking balls. Arrange the plots as a shared factory floor with visible neighboring courses, open viewing space around them, and an industrial factory backdrop with exaggerated machinery and room for chain-reaction setups.

# Agent decision

## Genre

Simulation is dominant, with Strategy secondary. Players design and operate their own physics contraptions, while scripted NPC waves following each course contribute the lane-management structure.

## Shape and preset

Use `plot-isolated`: multiple equally spaced, independent engineer plots on one shared factory floor. Use the Physics Sim preset with this shape substituted for its open-world default, retaining its physics rigs and hazards while omitting unrequested circulation between production areas.

## Config requirements

Draw `physics-rig` as modular spinning blades, collapsing bridges, giant boulders, tornado and lightning devices, lasers, explosive props, rolling objects, and swinging wrecking balls arranged for exaggerated chain reactions. Draw `hazard-kill` as bright lava channels and other clearly marked danger surfaces. Draw `buildzone-grid` as unobstructed structural mounting areas around each compact NPC route. The shared industrial backdrop has oversized cartoon machinery and clear open viewing aisles.

## Layout requirements

Place one `spawner-npc` at the entrance of every engineer plot, aligned to its route. Place an exit tracker at the opposite end of every plot using `tracker-core` as the terminal destination rather than a health base. The number of plots is unspecified, so match all per-plot placements to the generated plot count.

## Layout components

Build a repeated row-and-aisle arrangement of small room-sized plots. Every plot has one entrance, one short continuous central route, one opposite exit, and hazard mounting space on both sides and across permitted route segments. Wide shared viewing aisles surround the plots without entering their courses. Factory walls, pipes, tanks, gears, and exaggerated machinery frame the overall floor. Keep neighboring courses visibly comparable and compose the scene from an elevated angle showing several complete entrance-to-exit plots at once.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: the plan is authoritative so every short NPC route remains continuous through its modular build area, then the isometric image dresses it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is large overall but each plot is small. Theme is bright cartoon industrial chaos. Use `P6` because every NPC lane must remain valid by construction despite modular hazard placement.
