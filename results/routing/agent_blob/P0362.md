## Layout components
Build one regulation-sized central pitch with two goals on the short ends, a center mark, balanced kickoff positions, and bright polished surfacing. Tall curved sidewalls wrap the field into a contained bowl. Outside them, add a simple stadium shell with tiered seating and overhead arena lights. The boundary is fully enclosed; compose symmetrically along the goal-to-goal axis so field geometry and both goals read clearly.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the regulation field, mirrored goals, and reset positions should be established before the futuristic stadium dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large, driven by a regulation pitch and surrounding shell. The theme is bright, polished futuristic sport. Use `tiered` because the seating rises in non-overhanging steps; no other pipeline modifier is forced.
