# Scene prompt
Create a compact, camera-ready city filming set in a clean, accessible Roblox creative-tool style. Build it as a small, dense collection of recognizable urban filming areas with streets, building facades, and flexible open spaces for placing characters, props, and cameras. Keep this city set separate from other selectable built-in filming locations, which include distinct school, castle, beach, and forest sets in their own matching visual registers.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Entertainment because this is a creative filming showcase where players compose and record scenes rather than pursue a spatial objective.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-chaptered`, taken outside Entertainment’s usual set, because the city, school, castle, beach, and forest are explicitly separate selectable filming maps rather than districts on one surface. Keep the Showcase preset with the shape substituted and omit `landmark-focal`, since the city is an ensemble of flexible sets rather than one hero structure.

## Config requirements
Use `spawn-first-reveal` for a clean opening composition that immediately exposes the city set’s filming possibilities. Use `vignette-photo` for several recognizable urban compositions suited to different camera angles. Draw dense streets, exterior building facades, and flexible open staging areas as scene-specific image geometry. Do not add enterable interiors, characters, cameras, or props that the prompt reserves for creators to place.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawn-first-reveal` marker at the city set entrance, oriented down the principal street with secondary filming areas visible beyond it. Reserve clear placement surfaces and unobstructed camera positions in each open staging area, but place no fixed characters, filming props, collectibles, or gameplay triggers.

## Layout components
Build the city first as one compact map: a short principal street, one or two branching street fragments, a small plaza or open lot, and a dense perimeter of varied urban facades. Give each area a recognizable filming identity while keeping routes short and camera sightlines flexible. Bound the set cleanly behind the facades so backstage voids never enter frame. The other four named locations—school, castle, beach, and forest—remain separate selectable maps with distinct visual registers, not adjacent city zones.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: camera-ready compositions and visual flexibility lead, while the compact city topology is simple.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small for each dense filming set. The city theme is clean, accessible Roblox urban production design; later maps retain that tool-like clarity in their own registers. Use `P4` because the five selectable filming locations are separate maps.
