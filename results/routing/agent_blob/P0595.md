# Scene prompt
Create a large indoor trampoline park in a bright commercial style, centered on a large main trampoline floor with a foam pit beside it. Do not include extra game attractions.

# Agent decision

The answer is No Genre: this is a place for freeform bouncing with no named game mode, scoring, or objective.

## Shape and preset
No genre shape applies. Set `axis-enclosure` to interior-only; leave verticality single-surface, zone count single, structure dressed, and play-space grounded at their defaults. Use the Explorable Place preset only for clear circulation and composed views, dropping its landmark because the trampoline floor itself is the subject.

## Config requirements
Draw `path-circulation` as padded walkways around the main trampoline floor and to the foam pit. Use `vignette-photo` to compose a readable bright commercial hall showing both attractions together. Render one large central field of adjoining trampoline beds and one foam pit directly beside it. Do not add dodgeball courts, basketball hoops, climbing walls, or other attractions.

## Layout requirements
Place one uncatalogued arrival marker at the hall entrance on a padded perimeter walkway, facing the main floor. Place one uncatalogued foam-pit recovery marker at the accessible edge of the pit so players can return to the walkway. No score, team, checkpoint, or objective volumes are needed.

## Layout components
Build one enclosed commercial hall with a high clear ceiling, full walls, and a rectangular footprint. Center the layout on a broad main trampoline field made from a grid of beds with padded dividers. Put one foam pit beside the field along a long edge, separated by a padded lip and served by the perimeter walkway. Keep circulation around both features open and obvious, with the entrance at one end. Use bright safety padding, clean commercial finishes, overhead lights, and simple wall graphics. The hall walls form the boundary. Frame from a high interior corner so the main floor, foam pit, entrance, and circulation all remain visible.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the bright interior look and attraction proportions lead, then the simple plan is derived.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large because both the park and main trampoline floor are explicitly large. The theme is bright modern commercial recreation. Use `P0`: play remains entirely inside one hall, so there is no outside-to-inside transition or stacked geometry.
