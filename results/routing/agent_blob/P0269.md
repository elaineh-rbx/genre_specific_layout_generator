# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map inside a large cosy realistic house, viewed at doll-sized scale so normal furniture and rooms feel oversized. Arrange connected household rooms with towering tables, chairs, sofas, cabinets, and doorways forming the escape route through the house; include a large person moving through the interior as a looming presence. Provide a clear path leading from the starting rooms toward an exit from the house.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Survival: doll-sized players are pursued by one oversized hostile person and win by escaping the house.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single`, taken from the Survival set, because the entire playable map is the inside of one house. The preset is Escape, with its usual looping-warren shape replaced by this room-scale house and its outside-to-inside shelter option omitted because play starts and remains indoors until the terminal exit.

## Config requirements
Draw `cover-los` as doll-scale hiding gaps beneath sofas, tables, chairs, beds, and cabinets, with openings at least four studs wide on the entry axis. Draw `gate-escape` as a large, unmistakable final house exit at the far end of the route. Oversized realistic furniture should create the traversal landmarks without blocking the main path.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawner-npc` for the single large hostile person on navigable floor with access to the connected rooms. Place `spawn-protected` player starts together in the first room, screened from the pursuer’s initial sightline. Place the functional half of `gate-escape` once, directly across the final exterior door threshold.

## Layout components
The map is a sequence of connected cosy household rooms at doll scale, beginning in a sheltered starting room and progressing through living, dining, kitchen, hall, and final entry spaces toward the exit. Towering tables, chairs, sofas, cabinets, beds, and doorways form landmarks, cover, and route gates. Floor-level gaps and short furniture-underpasses provide alternate evasion paths. The house walls form the full boundary. Compose each room around one oversized furniture silhouette while keeping the forward doorway and final exit visually readable.

## Render order
Use `topdown` because the room connections, pursuer access, alternate evasion paths, and reachable exit must be validated before furnishing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium in traversal terms despite one house, driven by the doll-sized viewpoint. The theme is cosy realistic domestic space made threatening by extreme scale. Use `P0`; this is one interior-only map with no exterior transition or overlapping floor system.
