Add `checkpoint-respawn` recovery points after the start and at major track sectors, each oriented with the clean racing direction.

Include a four-kart grid, start/finish straight, broad readable bends, continuous track-edge markings, and guardrails at exposed outer edges. Voxel mountain forms rise around the track without creating overhanging road segments. Keep the racing line clean and uninterrupted, with no alternate branches. The mountain terrain forms the outer boundary and frames the entire loop in a calm scenic composition.
