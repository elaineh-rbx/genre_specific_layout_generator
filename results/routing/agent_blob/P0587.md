# Scene prompt
Create a 3D free-roam driving map set in a small Delhi-inspired neighbourhood, with a few connected city blocks of street-level exterior roads. Arrange dense urban buildings along the roads with intersections and continuous vehicle routes for exploring the neighbourhood; buildings remain exterior-only, with no enterable interiors.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is `simulation`, specifically free-roam vehicle operation: driving and exploring are the activity, with no race finish, missions, crime loop, or roleplay.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, one shared persistent neighbourhood that can be driven through in any order. Use the Vehicle Sim preset, but drop its resource-field option because the prompt asks only for urban free roaming; retain its world shape and vehicle roads.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for vehicle-width Delhi streets forming continuous loops through the neighbourhood. Add `settlement-density` for a few dense city blocks lined with close exterior-only buildings. The image should include Delhi-inspired street geometry such as varied shopfront facades, balconies, signs, utility poles, medians, sidewalks, and roadside clutter, while providing no enterable interiors.

## Layout requirements
Place one freeform player-and-vehicle arrival area on a wider road near the neighbourhood edge, aligned with traffic and clear of the first intersection. No mission triggers, crime objectives, race checkpoints, scoring volumes, task stations, pickups, hostile spawners, or interior links are required.

## Layout components
Build a few compact city blocks around a connected road network with several intersections and at least one loop so vehicles can keep exploring without reaching a dead end. Use a mix of wider through streets and narrower side streets, all broad enough for vehicle turns. Line both sides with dense, non-enterable urban facades and sidewalks. Keep terrain flat and the whole map exterior-only. Bound the footprint with continuous facade rows and road continuations that imply more Delhi beyond the playable area. Compose the blocks tightly while keeping every intersection and route readable.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: draw the authoritative street plan first because disconnected roads or impossible turns would break free-roam driving, then dress it as the isometric Delhi scene.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by a few connected dense blocks. The theme is lively, grounded Delhi street realism. The route is `P0`: one contiguous, flat exterior map requires no extra capture or structural pipeline.
