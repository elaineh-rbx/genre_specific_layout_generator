# Scene prompt

Create a bright, colorful 3D floating-island arena with 8 separate player islands suspended in open sky, spaced so each remains visible and readable across the server. Each island begins as a tiny 16x16-stud landmass and has room to expand into a 48x48-stud upgraded island with resource areas, pets, tools, and buildings. Place a clearly marked portal at each island hub leading instantly to a prestigious Golden Island that players can visit; keep the central sky open for server-wide events such as meteor showers, treasure rain, black holes, zombie invasions, and giant bosses.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Simulation because players gather resources, expand personal plots, unlock buildings and tools, and rebirth through a persistent progression loop.

## Shape and preset

Use `plot-isolated`: eight independent, equally spaced player plots realized as floating islands, each reserved for one player and unable to overlap its neighbors at maximum size. The preset is Sandbox, retaining isolated plots and resource collection while replacing the generic shared shop with each island’s own portal hub.

## Config requirements

Apply `island-cluster` to eight player landmasses plus one prestigious Golden Island separated by open sky. Apply `hazard-kill` as visible event-ready sky and island surfaces capable of supporting meteor, black-hole, zombie, and boss disruptions. The portal hub, growth-ready resource ground, and bright building footprint on each island must be visible. Do not prefill the player-controlled expansion area with permanent buildings, pets, or tools.

## Layout requirements

Place `collectible-nodes` within the resource area of each of the eight player islands. Place eight functional portal markers, exactly one at each island hub, all linked to the Golden Island. Keep these portals separate from the central event volume. No fixed building or pet placements are required because those are progression-driven player content.

## Layout components

Build eight player islands in a readable ring around open central sky, plus one distinct Golden Island outside or above that ring without vertically occluding it. Each player island starts with a 16x16-stud usable core and reserves a clearly readable expansion envelope up to 48x48 studs. Divide each into a portal hub, resource area, and open buildable ground. Keep generous sky gaps between full-size envelopes. Use soft grass, colorful rock undersides, bright trim, and polished fantasy clouds. The Golden Island should be visually richer and unmistakably prestigious. Frame all eight player islands and the open event center in one server-wide composition.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first as authoritative, then derive top-down; the colorful floating silhouette, expansion readability, and visual hierarchy lead.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a large scale, driven by eight 48x48-stud maximum plots plus the Golden Island. The theme is bright, polished, high-reward floating fantasy. Apply `CHECK` to verify that the open-air island arrangement does not self-occlude; no stacked overlap or separate-map pass is required.
