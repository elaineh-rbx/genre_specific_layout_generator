# Scene prompt
Create a compact 3D stormy island map for a short narrative exploration game. A landing point on the shoreline sits a short walk from an old lighthouse, with dangerous rocks in the surrounding water and a distant approaching ship visible through rain and fog. The lighthouse is the central structure, with three interior floors connected vertically; warm interior light contrasts with the dark, wind-swept exterior. Arrange exploration spaces and interactable repair points throughout the lighthouse, with notes and signs of the previous keeper placed along the route.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The genre is Adventure. This is a short story-driven exploration journey with physical objectives and environmental narrative, not combat or survival pressure.

## Shape and preset
Use `route-guided`: one continuous directed route runs from shoreline landing to lighthouse and upward through its three floors. The preset is Guided Trail, dropping its cinematic reveal exit and adding the requested enterable landmark, repair objectives, and storm hazards.

## Config requirements
Draw `path-trail` as the short weathered route from landing to lighthouse; `landmark-focal` as the old lighthouse visible from shore; and `building-interior` as its three linked enterable floors. Draw `water-body` as the storm sea, `hazard-kill` as dangerous rock-strewn water around the island, and `gate-progression` as repair-gated doors or mechanisms between lighthouse work areas. Keep the lighthouse warm inside against a dark, rain-lashed exterior.

## Layout requirements
Place one arrival marker at the shoreline landing. Place `collectible-nodes` as interactable repair targets across all three floors, with at least one target per floor and each attached to relevant machinery. Place narrative-note interactions sequentially beside the upward route. Place the final objective at the lantern mechanism on the third floor.

## Layout components
Build exactly one small island, one shoreline landing, one short trail, and one old lighthouse with exactly three stacked interior floors joined by stairs. Surround it with rough water and dangerous rocks, and place one distant approaching ship in the seaward composition. The ground floor is an entry and utility space, the middle floor holds keeper traces and repair machinery, and the top floor contains the lantern. The island edge and storm sea form the boundary.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish linked exterior and per-floor plans before dressing them, because a broken stair or objective sequence would make the story route unplayable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by a compact island and 15–20 minute route. The theme is moody stylized maritime storm with warm amber interiors. Use `P2` for three stacked floors, `P3` for outside-to-inside traversal, and `CHECK` for the surrounding water volume.
