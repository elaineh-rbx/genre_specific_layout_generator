# Scene prompt
Create a compact free-roam 3D Movie Tycoon district with exactly ten separate, walkable movie-inspired tycoon bases arranged around shared central paths, all within easy walking distance. Build each base as a distinct original homage using generic parts, with a clear themed entrance marker and a home/base emblem so players can identify its territory; include visual themes such as a jungle dinosaur enclosure with heavy gates, a superhero city block, and other varied cinematic sets without direct licensed characters or exact replicas. Place a reachable obby course somewhere in the shared map, and a giant statue of the creator as a landmark. Keep the map polished and visually striking, with bases, paths, and the obby clearly visible in the free-roam composition.

# Agent decision

The dominant genre is Simulation, specifically a tycoon, with Obby & Platformer secondary because the shared district contains a playable obstacle course. Simulation owns the overall shape and route.

Also draw ten distinct entrance markers and home emblems, the dinosaur-enclosure and superhero-city homages, other original cinematic sets, and one giant creator statue landmark.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional side of `checkpoint-respawn` at the obby start and after each substantial obstacle section, with respawn orientation facing the next jump. No other invisible placement is required.

## Layout components

Give every plot a visible themed gateway and emblem; include one gated jungle enclosure and one stylized superhero city block without licensed characters or copied assets. Use level paved ground, clear plot borders, and a contained district boundary. Frame every base, the obby, and statue together.
