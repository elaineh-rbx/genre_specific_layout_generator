# Scene prompt

Create a compact, readable block-scale city arena for up to 8 fighters, roughly 100–500 studs across. The arena has a broad flat concrete fighting ground, with destructible walls arranged as cover and boundaries, a few tall non-enterable, non-climbable buildings around the edges, and small trees lining the perimeter.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Action: this is a multiplayer free-for-all fighting game organized around one shared clash space.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-bounded` from the Battlegrounds preset. It becomes a compact, single-level concrete city block with clean collision and no playable building floors. Keep the preset but omit its ring-out hazard because the brief calls for destructible walls and buildings around the boundary rather than open kill edges.

## Config requirements

Use `cover-los` as large wall sections distributed across the concrete floor so fighters can disengage without cluttering movement. Use `destructible-cluster` for the breakable multi-part walls, built to fracture under combat. Draw the visible screening geometry for `spawn-protected` around perimeter respawn alcoves. Add a few tall facade-only buildings and small trees as edge dressing; none may be entered or climbed.

## Layout requirements

For `spawn-protected`, place eight respawn markers in separate screened alcoves around the perimeter, each turned away from direct sightlines across the arena. These are recurring free-for-all respawns, not team bases. No lock-in gates, NPC emitters, or round-only staging points are needed.

## Layout components

Build one broad flat concrete fighting zone, approximately 100–500 studs across, sized readably for up to eight simultaneous fighters. Arrange several destructible wall clusters across the interior as cover while retaining generous routes around every cluster. Ring the floor with a few tall, sealed city facades and a line of small trees, using walls and facades as the hard boundary. Keep all playable geometry on one surface with no stairs, roofs, balconies, interiors, or climbable ledges. Compose the arena so its open center, cover rhythm, and all perimeter spawn alcoves are visible.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative, then derive the top-down image; the visual character of the compact city arena leads, and its topology is uncomplicated.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is medium, driven by the stated 100–500-stud range and capacity of eight fighters. The theme is a flat, concrete, destructible block-city arena with sparse perimeter greenery. Use `P0` because one non-overlapping exterior surface captures the full layout.
