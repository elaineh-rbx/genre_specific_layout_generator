# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map showing a cozy magical forest glade centered around a lake, designed for animal roleplay and wandering. Arrange trees and soft forest ground around the lake, with open shore areas and gentle paths through the glade for horses, piglets, cats, dogs, and quokkas to explore.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim because players inhabit cute animal forms and wander together for open-ended roleplay.

## Shape and preset
Use the single `wilderness-open` shape: one natural glade with no settlement, centered on a lake and freely explored in any direction. It comes from the Animal Sim preset, adjusted to omit the unrequested den, formal social hub, and navigation landmark.

## Config requirements
Apply `water-body` to draw the central magical lake with open, approachable shores. Draw soft forest ground, cozy tree clusters, flowers and gentle paths with clear widths suitable for the largest playable animal, the horse. The named horses, piglets, cats, dogs, and quokkas are player forms rather than an ambient `npc-population`, so the image should not add unrequested NPC animals.

## Layout requirements
After segmentation, place several player spawn markers on open soft ground around the glade, distributed near but not directly at the water’s edge. Each start needs horse-sized clearance and immediate access to the shore and path loop. No pickups, hostile spawners, checkpoints, scoring volumes, or destination triggers are needed because wandering and roleplay have no fixed endpoint.

## Layout components
Build one cozy forest-glade zone wrapped around a central lake. Leave several open shore pockets for animal groups, and connect them with broad, gently curving woodland paths that form an informal loop without turning the map into a course. Use clusters of magical trees, soft mossy ground, flowers, mushrooms, small rocks, and low foliage, keeping dense vegetation away from the main routes. Enclose the outer boundary with thicker forest growth. Compose the lake as the visual center, with open shore in the foreground and layered trees framing the glade.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down image, because the cozy magical appearance and animal-scale clearances lead while the roaming topology is simple.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by the request for one cozy glade. The theme is cute enchanted woodland with soft natural materials. Use `CHECK` for the central `water-body`; the shallow, single-surface lake should not self-occlude. No other modifier is forced.
