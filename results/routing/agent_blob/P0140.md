# Scene prompt
Create a 3D roleplay map set in a modern city: a walkable town-sized shared urban space that feels large while remaining easy to explore on foot. Include a custom wardrobe location with many outfit choices for changing appearance in-game; do not include player housing.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim. The map supports shared city roleplay and avatar customization without jobs, property ownership, or a competitive loop. There is no secondary genre.

## Shape and preset
Use `settlement-static` for a fixed, fully shared modern city with no claimable or buildable housing. The preset is none: Morph Roleplay is the closest family, but the brief specifies a large walkable city and custom wardrobe rather than its landmark-and-photo bundle.

## Config requirements
Use `path-street` for a connected pedestrian network of broad sidewalks, plazas, and short streets sized for comfortable exploration on foot. Use `social-hub` for a deliberately roomy central city plaza where players can gather. Use `district-zoned` to distinguish the active wardrobe and commercial core from quieter scenic blocks without adding residential ownership. Use `building-interior` for one enterable custom wardrobe boutique or studio. Use `settlement-density` for town-sized continuous urban frontage. Add visible free-text wardrobe geometry with many varied racks, mannequins, mirrors, and changing stations; do not add player houses or private plots.

## Layout requirements
Place one free-text shared avatar spawn area in the central plaza, oriented toward the wardrobe location and main pedestrian routes. Site numerous outfit-change interaction positions beside the custom racks and mannequins inside the wardrobe venue, distributed to avoid crowding. No property claims, vehicle spawns, job stations, checkpoints, scoring zones, or hostile emitters are needed.

## Layout components
Build a walkable modern city of several connected blocks around one oversized central plaza. Use a legible network of sidewalks, pedestrian streets, crosswalks, and pocket plazas, with the wardrobe venue on a prominent frontage near the center. Give that venue a linked interior full of diverse outfit displays and changing areas. Surround it with shared urban facades, street furniture, lighting, planters, signs, and small gathering pockets, but no player housing. Keep terrain level and frame the map to communicate scale while preserving clear walking routes.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the modern city atmosphere first, then project its straightforward pedestrian plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is large, driven by a town-sized network of several walkable blocks. The theme is polished contemporary urban roleplay. Use `P3` because players cross from city streets into the wardrobe interior.
