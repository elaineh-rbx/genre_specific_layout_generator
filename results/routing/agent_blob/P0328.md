# Scene prompt
Create a bright, colorful cartoon 3D pet-simulator map with 3 small connected zones. Each compact play area has open space for roaming pets, several egg pedestals formed by stone spots with eggs set into and slightly floating above them, and a zone gate leading to the next area with a displayed price. Arrange the zones as a clear forward route, with the starting zone in front, the second beyond its gate, and the final zone at the back; do not include a separate underground level.
