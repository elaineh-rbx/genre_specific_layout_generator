# Scene prompt
Create a 3D single-player JRPG pilgrimage vertical slice set along a strictly linear, lush route toward a distant sacred goal. Build three traversable zones, each roughly 3–5 minutes to cross end to end: an opening first town with story NPCs, save points, treasure chests, and a clear forward exit; a first field region with a distinct atmospheric biome, a defined main path, encounter spaces, and scenic depth; and a slightly more compact, encounter-focused first dungeon culminating in a dramatic boss arena. Connect completed zones through a stylized world-map route that reveals the next destination, with no backtracking. Use painterly cinematic composition, including sunlit coastal, solemn temple, or storm-lit ruin moods, water and reflections where appropriate, haze, volumetric light, hero-lit save points and chests, and a battle presentation space with clear readable silhouettes.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is RPG: party progression and turn-based combat drive a linear pilgrimage through towns, field encounters, and a dungeon.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-chaptered`, outside RPG’s usual shape list, because the three destinations are separate maps unlocked in strict order with no backtracking. Start from the Turn-based RPG preset, substituting this chaptered shape while retaining its safe town, hostile placements, and progression gate.

## Config requirements
`safezone-town` makes the opening town fully separated from hostile encounters. `hazard-aggro` creates readable encounter clearings beside the field path and inside the dungeon. `gate-progression` is the prominent forward exit and world-map destination lock. `teleporter-link` draws standardized world-route departure and arrival plinths. `npc-population` draws story NPCs in the town. `water-body` adds reflective coastal water as scenic depth. Draw hero-lit save points and treasure chests in all three zones.

## Layout requirements
Place `npc-population` markers at the town square, route exit, and story focal points. Place `spawner-npc` markers only inside field and dungeon encounter spaces, plus one boss marker at the dungeon’s terminal arena. Place paired `teleporter-link` endpoints between town, field, and dungeon, enabled only forward. Site save-point markers near each zone entrance and before the boss; distribute treasure pickups in optional path-side alcoves.

## Layout components
Build three separate 3–5-minute maps: a sunlit coastal town with square, NPC ground, save point, chests, and one forward exit; a lush atmospheric field with one directed main path, reflective water, scenic overlooks, encounter clearings, and side alcoves; and a slightly shorter solemn or storm-lit dungeon ending in a broad boss arena. A stylized world-map route presents the three destinations sequentially. Boundaries use coast, cliffs, architecture, and dungeon walls. Compose each zone cinematically around a distant forward landmark.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish the strictly linear route, encounter clearances, forward-only links, and terminal boss arena before dressing each isometric image.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large: three separate traversable maps of 3–5 minutes each. The theme is painterly cinematic pilgrimage with coastal light, temple solemnity, storm-lit ruins, haze, and volumetric illumination. `P4` is required for separate zone maps; `CHECK` applies to the water play-height envelope.
