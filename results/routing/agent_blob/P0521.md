# Scene prompt
Create a grimy post-apocalyptic 3D tower map: a dark, abandoned five-floor tower with zombie-filled interior floors stacked vertically. Each floor is a distinct combat area with one boss encounter, connected by a clear upward route toward the top floor. Place the hidden key at the tower’s summit as the final destination.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Shooter, canonically `shooter`, because gun combat against zombies and bosses organizes movement through the tower.

## Shape and preset
Use `rooms-sequence`, worded as a five-floor breach sequence cleared upward in strict order. PvE Shooter is the matching preset, substituting this sequential-room shape for its lane network and omitting an outside-to-inside transition because the entire map is internal.

## Config requirements
Use `choke-bottleneck` for exposed stairs and doorways between floors, forcing each cleared combat area into the next. Add `cover-los` as overturned furniture, concrete barriers, and ruined machinery distributed across each boss floor without blocking the upward route. The hidden summit key is a visible one-off prop with no matching loaded option.

## Layout requirements
Place ten `spawner-npc` origins: one readable zombie-wave origin and one boss origin on each of the five floors. Keep each floor’s two origins inside its combat area, with the boss deeper than the regular zombie entry. Place one hidden-key collectible marker at the summit after the fifth boss arena, positioned against a concealed but reachable alcove.

## Layout components
Build exactly five stacked interior floors inside one abandoned tower. Each floor is a distinct combat room with cover, a zombie entry side, a central engagement area, one boss position, and a locked-looking upward stair or breach doorway. Connect floors in a single readable bottom-to-top sequence with no bypasses. Use cracked concrete, exposed rebar, rusted industrial fixtures, boarded openings, grime, emergency lights, and scattered debris. The fifth floor opens into a summit chamber containing the hidden key. Exterior walls enclose the map; cutaway composition should reveal all five floors and their upward connection without implying outdoor traversal.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the ordered five-floor clearance route and isolated encounter spaces must be authoritative before the isometric dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by five full combat floors and five bosses. The theme is dark, grimy post-apocalyptic abandonment. `P2` is required because stacked floors occlude one another; no `P3` applies because play never crosses an exterior boundary.
