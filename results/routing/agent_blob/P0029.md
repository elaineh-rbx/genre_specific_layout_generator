# Scene prompt
Create a 3D social game map showing a cozy Alaskan forest valley, compact enough to cross in about a minute. The shared wilderness has natural bear shelters open to everyone, with forest terrain arranged for easy exploration and gathering together.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim because customized bear avatars romp, explore, and socialize together with no hunting or progression loop.

## Shape and preset
Use the single `wilderness-open` shape: one shared natural valley with no settlement or private territory. It comes from the Animal Sim preset, retaining its natural shelters and gathering focus while omitting the unrequested navigation landmark.

## Config requirements
Apply `den-shelter` to draw several open natural bear shelters—shallow caves, log hollows, or protected rock recesses—that any bear can use. Use `social-hub` as a broad central meadow sized for groups of bear avatars to gather without congestion. Apply `terrain-relief` to shape the requested Alaskan valley with gently raised wooded sides and a traversable floor, with no cliffs or overhangs.

## Layout requirements
After segmentation, place several player spawn markers around the edge of the central meadow, separated enough for bear-sized avatars and oriented toward the shared gathering ground. Every spawn must have a clear route to at least one shelter and around the valley loop. No pickups, hostile spawners, checkpoints, private den claims, or winner zone are needed because hanging out is the entire activity.

## Layout components
Build one compact Alaskan forest valley crossable in about a minute. Put a broad social meadow near the center, surrounded by spruce and birch clusters, mossy rocks, low shrubs, fallen logs, and gently sloping valley sides. Distribute several communal shelters around the woodland edge rather than assigning one per player. Connect meadow, shelters, and forest pockets with easy natural trails that branch and rejoin. Use thicker forest and rising terrain as the outer boundary. Compose the meadow as the main visual opening, with shelters visible as inviting recesses beneath the layered forest backdrop.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down image, because the cozy Alaskan mood and readable bear-scale shelters lead while the circulation is simple.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, fixed by the roughly one-minute crossing time. The theme is cute, cozy Alaskan woodland. Use `tiered` for the valley relief on one non-overhanging surface; no additional modifier is forced.
