# Scene prompt
Create a compact, roughly 500-stud 3D mech combat battlefield in a grimy, war-torn industrial sci-fi setting, scaled for mechs about three times avatar height. Arrange uneven terrain with open combat lanes, industrial ruins and damaged structures for cover, plus scattered trees and terrain doodads across the battlefield.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Shooter because this free-for-all mech game is organized around Gatling-gun and rocket sightlines, firing routes, and ranged cover.

## Shape and preset
Use `open-battlefield` for one contiguous combat map with dispersed ruins and terrain positions rather than fixed parallel lanes. The preset is none: Arena Deathmatch is the closest mode, but its lane-network shape, fixed powerups, elevated nests, and looping rooms are not requested.

## Config requirements
Apply `cover-los` as mech-scaled industrial ruins and damaged structures distributed across the battlefield, mixing partial-height and full-body cover for machines three times avatar height while preserving long open firing lanes. Apply `terrain-relief` as uneven, war-scarred ground with gentle rises and depressions but no overhangs. Also draw scattered generated-mesh trees and industrial terrain doodads as sparse environmental dressing, keeping them out of the primary combat lanes.

## Layout requirements
Place a set of free-for-all player spawn markers around separated perimeter sectors and secondary cover pockets, oriented toward the battlefield but outside immediate direct fire from one another. No team bases, objective volumes, weapon pickups, NPC emitters, or shrinking boundary are requested.

## Layout components
Build one roughly 500-stud-wide battlefield scaled for mechs about three times avatar height. A broad irregular central combat area branches into several open firing lanes separated by low rises, damaged industrial shells, broken walls, and large debris formations. Keep ruins exterior-only and penetrable only where openings are plainly visible; do not invent full interiors or tunnels. Scatter trees and smaller terrain doodads between combat routes without turning them into collision clutter. Use churned earth, stained concrete, scorched metal, rubble, and sparse dead vegetation. Bound the map with ruined industrial massing and impassable war-torn terrain. Frame the full battlefield so open lanes, major cover islands, and uneven relief are legible.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive top-down, because the war-torn visual composition leads and invalid topology would not make this open battlefield unplayable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is large in avatar terms but compact for mechs, fixed at roughly 500 studs across. The theme is grimy industrial war-torn science fiction. Use `tiered` because uneven ground changes elevation without overhanging surfaces.
