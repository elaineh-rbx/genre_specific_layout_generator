# Scene prompt

Create a 3D glossy modern fashion-show space with a central runway for outfit displays, surrounded by a dressing and outfit-creation area, plus a separate display area for posted outfit looks.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, combining dress-up creation with a social, unscored fashion showcase.

## Shape and preset

Use `stage-runway`: a central fashion catwalk is fed by preparation spaces and presented to surrounding viewers. The preset is Dress Up, retaining its social-showcase and scenic presentation logic while omitting any judging mechanic.

## Config requirements

Use `social-hub` for a generous audience and gathering floor around the runway, sized so players can socialize without blocking the catwalk. Use `vignette-photo` for polished fashion-photo backdrops and the separate posted-look gallery. Draw a central glossy runway, nearby dressing and outfit-creation stations, audience seating, lighting trusses, display screens, and a distinct gallery of outfit-post panels.

## Layout requirements

Place uncatalogued outfit-editing interaction points at each dressing or creation station, positioned in front of visible mirrors and wardrobes. Place one posting trigger beside each submission terminal, and bind the resulting display locations to the separate posted-look gallery panels. Mark the runway entrance and exit as navigation points, but place no judging, voting, or scoring volumes because the walk is purely for show.

## Layout components

Build one glossy modern fashion-show venue centered on a long raised runway with a clear entrance from the dressing zone and a turnaround or presentation end facing the audience. Arrange dressing booths, mirrors, wardrobes, and outfit-creation counters behind or beside the runway entrance. Spread audience seating and open social floor along both sides while preserving unobstructed views. Create a separate posted-outfit gallery with evenly spaced digital frames, mannequin bays, or display panels connected by a short corridor or open aisle. Use polished dark floors, bright white surfaces, neon accents, spotlights, and reflective fashion-show materials. Enclose the venue and frame the runway as the dominant central axis.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render the glossy stage atmosphere, audience arrangement, and posted-look gallery first, then derive the top-down plan; presentation leads.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is medium, accommodating runway, preparation, audience, and gallery zones. The theme is glossy contemporary fashion show with restrained neon accents. Use `P0`; all zones share one representable venue surface without outside transitions or overlapping playable floors.
