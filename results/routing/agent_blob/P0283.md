## Enriched image prompt
Create an original compact but drivable 3D Tokyo city slice on one connected surface. Link a busy downtown commercial area and a quieter residential area with a few vehicle-width streets, intersections, alleys, and looping escape routes. Place readable curbside destinations, vehicle-or-item pickup areas, and matching delivery areas across both districts, with street-level cover for short third-person fights and several unobtrusive police entry routes. Keep buildings exterior-only, vary the streets and landmarks, and frame both urban areas and their connection in one map rather than attempting an enormous full city.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Action because combat and police chases define the open-city pressure; Simulation is secondary because driving and pickup-delivery tasks materially shape the map.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, taken from outside Action's usual shape set, because both city districts share one freely navigable road network. The matched secondary-genre preset is Role Sim, whose shared world, task stations, circulation, and vehicle roads fit the stated missions.

## Config requirements
Secondary Simulation contributes `path-road-vehicle`, drawing vehicle-width streets between the districts and taking its ordinary-street `P0` route rather than a course route. Its `path-circulation` gives destinations and escape routes multiple connections, while `trigger-task` draws clear curbside pickup, delivery, and marked-destination areas. Action contributes `cover-los` for street-level combat cover, and universal `settlement-density` establishes the compact downtown and residential urban fabric. Buildings remain facades.

## Layout requirements
Place `trigger-task` markers as a few drive-to destinations and paired pickup-delivery points distributed across both districts; preserve “a few” because no exact count was supplied. Place `spawner-npc` police entry points on side streets outside immediate player view, feeding the connected chase network. No indoor mission markers are required.

## Layout components
The single city map contains one downtown commercial district, one quieter residential district, a few connected streets and intersections, alleys and looping evade routes, original urban landmarks, curbside task areas, street cover, and police entries. Roads connect every mission area without a separate map load. Building rows and closed street ends define the compact outer boundary; compose both districts together.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the drivable road network, mission pairs, and police chase loops must connect before the Tokyo urban dressing is applied.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is medium, balancing two recognizable districts with the author's compact city-slice limit. The theme is a contemporary Tokyo-inspired urban streetscape. `P0` is sufficient because the build is one grounded connected exterior map with no enterable buildings or stacked road surfaces.
