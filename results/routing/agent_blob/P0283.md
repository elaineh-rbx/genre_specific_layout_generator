# Scene prompt
Create a 3D open-city map based on a compact but drivable Tokyo slice, built district by district. Connect several city blocks with drivable streets and intersections, featuring a dense downtown commercial area of tall buildings and storefront-lined roads alongside a quieter residential area of lower-rise homes and narrow streets. Include distinct destination points across both districts for vehicle pickup and delivery locations, with open road routes between them.

### Layout components

A quieter residential zone uses lower homes, narrower streets, and separate destination points. Multiple road connections should cross the district seam so no job relies on one chokepoint. Streets form the principal paths; sidewalks and block edges define the pedestrian ground. Bound the slice with believable continuation streets and dense skyline framing rather than abrupt walls.

Use `P0`; one top-down surface captures the connected street network without stated interiors, stacked roads, or separate maps.
