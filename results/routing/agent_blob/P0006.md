# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map with a safe futuristic floating citadel lobby in space. The lobby centers on a large neon dungeon portal, with a training-dummy area nearby and a platform of freestanding 3D leaderboard displays. Beyond the portal, build one continuous descending Crystal Citadel dungeon, divided by clear transition gates into three compact, easily visible arena-sized zones with a checkpoint at each entrance. First, a closed black-stone courtyard holds four colored crystal pillars and a large exit gate. Next, a laser chamber contains a floor of shifting-height cubes, crossing red horizontal and vertical laser beams, three physical energy keys hidden behind the moving blocks, and a central control panel. At the bottom, a vast crystal throne hall has a glass floor over neon volcanic lava, four glowing pressure circles across the arena floor, and a raised throne area for the crystal boss.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Action (`action`) because arena combat, minion waves, and a boss encounter drive the run. Puzzle (`puzzle`) is secondary because ordered pillars, energy keys, and pressure circles gate progress.

## Shape and preset
Use `rooms-sequence`: the portal leads to three compact dungeon arenas in one continuous descent. The Hack & Slash preset fits the combat-room chain, augmented with explicit puzzle inputs and a separate safe lobby.

## Config requirements
Draw `arena-lockin` as transition gates between the three zones; `button-solve` as the four colored pillars and four glowing boss pressure circles; `trigger-solve` as the central energy-key panel; `gate-progression` as the first arena’s exit gate; `collectible-nodes` as three visible energy keys; and `hazard-kill` as crossing lasers and lava below the throne floor. Draw the neon portal, training area, leaderboard platform, shifting cubes, glass floor, and throne as scene-specific geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` points for exactly 20 guard monsters in Zone 1, flying enemies in Zone 2, and one boss in Zone 3. Place three `checkpoint-respawn` markers, one at each zone entrance. Place the functional halves of `button-solve` on four pillars and four boss plates, `trigger-solve` at the central panel, and `collectible-nodes` at three hiding positions behind moving cubes. Link one portal endpoint in the lobby to the dungeon start.

## Layout components
Build a floating space lobby with one neon portal, one training-dummy area, and one leaderboard platform. The dungeon descends through a black-stone courtyard, laser-and-cube chamber, and larger crystal throne hall. Gates and short descending corridors connect the zones. The final hall holds a raised throne and glass floor over neon lava. Enclose each arena, leave the lobby floating against open space, and compose the descent diagonally beneath the portal.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: render the plan first and make it authoritative because the gated sequence, trigger placements, and continuous descent must remain playable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium, driven by a lobby plus three arenas. The theme is black-stone neon crystal science-fantasy. Use `P4` for the portal-separated lobby and dungeon, `P2` for shifting-height blocks and the glass-over-lava throne floor, and `P6` because the ordered puzzle route must be valid.
