# Scene prompt
Create a compact, chunky low-poly multiplayer map set in a 140-stud-wide suburban cul-de-sac at golden-hour dusk, with warm orange light, long purple shadows, fireflies, and hissing lawn sprinklers. Center the scene on an impossibly tall, slightly leaning suburban house: five vertically stacked mismatched stories of vinyl siding, brick, log cabin, stucco, and a formal Victorian top with a widow’s walk. Its entire front facade is a dense collage of 50 shallow, non-walkable reveal doors, crammed at ground level and high on walls, including upside-down and overlapping doors. Mix screen doors, a castle drawbridge, refrigerator, cat flap, vault, airlock, submarine hatch, saloon doors, revolving and elevator doors, beaded curtain, cardboard door, painted fake door, tiny doggy door, heavily locked door, and a DO NOT door. Reach upper facade doors via exterior wooden stairs, a fire escape, scaffolding, trellis, and one long ladder. Place a comically oversized mailbox roughly 80 studs from the main door cluster at the street edge; arrange the ice cream truck and a large leaderboard billboard in the cul-de-sac, with lawn space where a three-place podium can rise. Use mint green, salmon, mustard yellow, and dusty teal, with oversized rounded doorbells and glowing coin drops.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation, specifically a repeatable earning-and-upgrade task loop; Party & Casual is secondary because play is round-based and supports twelve competing players.

## Shape and preset
Use `arena-stacked`, the Multi-Level Arena, taken from outside Simulation’s usual set because five externally traversed facade levels overhang one another. The closest preset is Role Sim, with its repeatable task stations and circulation, adapted from a job setting to doorbell runs.

## Config requirements
Use `trigger-task` for exactly 50 oversized door-and-doorbell stations spread across the five-story facade. Use `path-circulation` for the compact network linking the door cluster, mailbox, truck, billboard, and lawn. Use `social-hub` for the ice cream truck upgrade area. Use `tracker-leaderboard` for the large physical billboard. No `building-interior` applies because every door is explicitly a shallow facade reveal.

## Layout requirements
Site the functional half of all 50 `trigger-task` interactions at their matching doorbells. Place the `tracker-leaderboard` surface on the billboard. Add one mailbox deposit trigger about 80 studs from the main door cluster, coin-drop pickup markers at every door, resident emergence markers behind usable doors, twelve starts in the cul-de-sac, and three podium positions on the reserved lawn.

## Layout components
Build one 140-stud-wide cul-de-sac around a leaning five-story house, with all 50 varied doors visible on its front. Connect upper doors by exterior stairs, fire escape, scaffolding, trellis, and one long ladder. Keep a direct committed sprint between house and giant mailbox; place the truck and billboard off that route and preserve open podium lawn. Houses remain facade-only. Compose the impossible house as the dominant vertical focal point.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: render the top-down first and treat it as authoritative so every door, access structure, banking route, and gathering prop remains reachable without circulation conflicts.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale, fixed by the 140-stud cul-de-sac and five vertical levels. The theme is saturated low-poly suburban slapstick at golden hour. `P2` is required because stacked facade platforms and access structures overhang lower play surfaces.
