# Scene prompt
Create a 3D horror survival map set inside a single compact, very dark building complex. Arrange a dense network of rooms, corridors, closeable doors, and intersecting hiding routes so players can break line of sight and make short escapes without remaining lost for minutes. Use sparse flickering lights and deep shadowed corners, with the interior framed as an oppressive enclosed space rather than an outdoor map.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Survival: asymmetric Runner and Hunter teams play timed pursuit rounds inside a hostile enclosed environment.

## Shape and preset
Use `warren-looping`, because the dense room-and-corridor network must provide intersecting short escape loops without long disorienting branches or terminal traps. The preset is none: the requested mode supports multiple Hunters and rotating team roles rather than the preset’s one-versus-all structure.

## Config requirements
Draw `cover-los` as deep wall recesses, room corners, bulky interior fixtures, and at least four-stud-wide hiding cutouts that let Runners briefly break sightlines. Draw numerous closeable doors at corridor and room thresholds, along with sparse flickering light fixtures. Keep the architecture oppressive and dark while making junctions and doorways readable enough for fast pursuit.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` as two separated banks of role starts, one for Runners and one for Hunters, with geometry screening each bank from the other; no player count was given, so do not invent one. Place an interaction trigger on every rendered closeable door. Place ambient sound emitters at corridor intersections, door clusters, and distant rooms to support footsteps, breathing, flicker, and misleading noises. No escape gate is required because Runners win on the timer.

## Layout components
The entire map is one compact dark building complex. Several medium rooms connect through short corridors, cross-passages, and doors into a dense loop network. Intersections offer quick route choices, while shallow side pockets provide risky hiding spots without becoming deep dead ends. Large fixtures, wall returns, and shadowed corners break sightlines. Solid exterior walls enclose the whole interior. Compose the plan around a few recognizable junction landmarks under sparse flickering light, with deep shadows between them.

## Render order
Use `topdown` because every chase route must reconnect and remain compact enough for short escapes without trapping or losing players.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, defined as one compact complex. The theme is psychological pursuit horror with sparse flicker, deep shadow, and oppressive silence. Use `P6` because the looping chase topology must be valid by construction.
