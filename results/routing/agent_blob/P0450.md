# Scene prompt
Create a 3D concert venue layout for a 1980s stadium show. A large performance stage faces a broad crowd area, with the audience kept in front of the stage for watching and dancing; stadium seating and venue structure surround the concert floor.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Entertainment because this is a live concert environment where players gather, dance, and watch rather than pursue a win condition.

## Shape and preset
Use `venue-stage`, with a raised performance stage commanding a one-directional audience floor and surrounding stadium seating. Match the Performance Venue preset without substitution.

## Config requirements
Use `spectator-bleachers` for stepped stadium seating that preserves sightlines from the rear. Use `social-hub` for the broad danceable crowd floor in front of the stage. Use `spawn-first-reveal` so arriving players immediately see the performer and full 1980s stadium composition rather than backstage structure. Use `npc-population` for Michael Jackson as the central singer and for a supporting concert audience occupying the floor and seats.

## Layout requirements
Place one `spawn-first-reveal` marker at the rear or side entrance to the crowd floor, oriented directly toward the stage. Place the functional half of `npc-population` with one central performer anchor onstage and distributed audience anchors across the concert floor and stadium seating. Keep player movement and dancing on the audience side of the stage, with no access marker leading onto the performance platform.

## Layout components
Build one large raised stage at one end of the venue, with a broad open concert floor extending in front of it. Wrap raked stadium seating and venue structure around the sides and rear while leaving every audience orientation converging onstage. Provide clear entry aisles into the floor and seating without creating a route backstage. The stage should read as a classic 1980s stadium production, with strong performance lighting, speaker stacks, rigging, and a commanding central microphone position. Bound the venue with the stadium bowl and frame the stage as the dominant focal point.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the look, crowd-facing composition, and stage spectacle lead, while the venue topology is straightforward.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large because this is a stadium show with a broad floor and surrounding seating. The theme is polished classic 1980s pop-concert spectacle. Use `tiered` because the stepped seating has strong elevation without requiring overlapping playable floors; no backstage or outside-to-inside traversal is requested.
