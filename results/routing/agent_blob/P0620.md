# Scene prompt

Create a 3D runaway-train route along a continuous railroad line passing through three small industrial railroad towns before reaching a sharp curve above a river. The train moves through warehouses, loading yards, modest trackside buildings, and obstacles positioned on or beside the rails. Leave a clear stretch of track for a second train to approach, couple behind it, and reverse direction; the far end of the route culminates in the tight riverside curve and derailment area.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Simulation because operating, catching, and coupling trains defines the activity. Its destructive runaway-train spectacle is handled within that genre rather than introducing a separate combat or racing genre.

## Shape and preset

Use `route-guided`, taken from outside Simulation’s usual shape set: one continuous railroad sequences the chase through three towns toward a fixed riverside danger point. Match the Physics Sim preset with its shape substituted, because impacts, trackside obstacles, and the derailment spectacle make physical reactions central.

## Config requirements

Use `path-circulation` as the continuous rail corridor carrying both trains through the complete scenario. Use `physics-rig` for breakable trackside obstacles and the sharp-curve derailment setup. Use `hazard-kill` for the terminal riverside crash area, `settlement-density` with a count of three for the three small industrial railroad towns, and `water-body` for the river below the final curve. Draw warehouses, loading yards, modest buildings, railside clutter, and a long unobstructed coupling stretch.

## Layout requirements

Place one runaway-train start marker before the first town and one pursuing-train start marker on the clear rear approach. Place coupling alignment points on the straight chase section. Place a derailment trigger at the sharp final curve and a failure volume in the river below it. The ten-minute countdown is gameplay logic, not map geometry, so it does not enter the image config.

## Layout components

Build a single point-to-point rail corridor passing sequentially through exactly three compact industrial towns. Each town contains warehouses, a loading yard, modest trackside buildings, and obstacles on or beside the rails. Reserve a long straight section behind the runaway train for approach and coupling. Beyond the third town, bend the railway sharply along an elevated riverbank into a visibly staged derailment area. Industrial fencing and terrain edges bound the route; compose the frame to keep the full chase direction readable.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, then dress the isometric image, because the continuous rail connection, coupling straight, three-town sequence, and terminal curve must remain playable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is large enough for a ten-minute route through three towns. The theme is gritty small-town industrial rail country. Use `CHECK` for the river; otherwise the continuous surface remains `P0`, with no interiors, overhangs, or separate maps.
