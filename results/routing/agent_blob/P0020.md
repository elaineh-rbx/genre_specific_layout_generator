# Scene prompt
Create a 3D playable dungeon for a Roblox-style character: a classic stone-and-torches dungeon with a linear sequence of three enemy chambers, each separated by a heavy gate. The route leads from the entrance through the three chambers to a final boss chamber at the end, with stone walls, torch-lit corridors, and spaces for NPC enemies in every chamber.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG, with Action secondary. The boss reward of swords and armor gives the dungeon a role-playing progression purpose, while chamber-by-chamber combat supplies the action structure.

## Shape and preset
Use `rooms-sequence`: a single linear dungeon of enclosed combat rooms joined by corridors and cleared in order. The matched preset is Hack & Slash from the secondary genre, retaining its room sequence, combat lock-in gates, and hostile emitters while omitting unrequested destructible cover.

## Config requirements
Use `arena-lockin` for heavy stone gates that seal each enemy chamber and open only after that chamber is cleared. The visible dungeon is one interior-only build: classic masonry, torch brackets, compact connecting corridors, three ordinary combat rooms, and a larger final boss chamber. Do not add an exterior transition, town, or social hub.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` emitters inside each of exactly three enemy chambers, positioned beyond the entry threshold so enemies do not overlap gates or corridors. Place one separate boss emitter at the center or far end of the final chamber. Place one post-boss reward point for dropped swords and armor in a clearly visible safe position beyond the boss fight.

## Layout components
Build an entrance room followed by exactly three enemy chambers and one final boss chamber. Connect them with short, narrow torch-lit corridors. Associate one heavy exit gate with each of the three enemy chambers, producing three sequential locks before the boss. Use thick stone walls, flagstone floors, iron gatework, wall torches, columns, and restrained dungeon clutter that leaves combat movement clear. Make the boss chamber larger and more ceremonial, with the reward location framed on its far axis. The enclosing masonry is the boundary, and the whole dungeon should compose as a readable linear chain.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the top-down plan is rendered first and authoritative because the ordered room chain, gate sequence, and reachable boss endpoint must remain unambiguous.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, fixed by three enemy chambers plus one boss room. The theme is a classic stone-and-torches dungeon. Use `P0`; the entire map is one non-overlapping interior surface with no outside transition or separate map.
