# Scene prompt
Create a bright cartoon tropical 3D island with colorful beaches around the shoreline, a friendly volcano rising in the middle, and stylized jungle trees surrounding it. Place a few cabanas along the beach areas, with five coconuts distributed across the island.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure: the round is a finite island-wide scavenger hunt for five coconuts, with no secondary combat, racing, or simulation loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, one contiguous island that players explore in any order. The preset is Scavenger Hunt, retaining its open search space and collectible objectives while omitting the unrequested quest board and hidden discovery alcoves.

## Config requirements
Use `terrain-relief` for the friendly central volcano, rising in readable terraces without overhangs. Use `water-body` for the surrounding tropical sea and colorful beach edge. The image must also include dense stylized jungle trees around the volcano and a few beach cabanas; these visible scene-specific props have no more specific loaded option.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly five `collectible-nodes` as coconuts across distinct reachable island locations. Site at least one along a beach, one near the cabanas, one within the jungle, and one on a safe volcano approach, with the remaining node separated from those so the full island is used. Keep every coconut on walkable ground and outside dense prop collisions.

## Layout components
Build one roughly circular tropical island zone with colorful beaches ringing its shoreline, a broad jungle belt inside the beaches, and a friendly volcano centered as the dominant landmark. Provide continuous walkable ground connecting the beach, cabana, jungle, and volcano areas without portals or interiors. Scatter a few cabanas along the beach areas rather than consolidating them into a town; preserve “a few” without inventing an exact count. Shape the volcano as stepped slopes and keep its summit readable in the overview. Bound the island naturally with the surrounding sea. Compose the frame radially, with the volcano centered and beaches, jungle, and cabanas visible around it.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: establish the bright tropical character and central-volcano composition first, then derive the top-down plan, because the search has no topology that requires a computed blueprint.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, driven by a full island with five separated search destinations. The theme is bright cartoon tropical with a friendly volcano. Use `tiered` for elevated volcano terrain with no overhangs and `CHECK` for the surrounding water surface, which should remain non-self-occluding.
