# Scene prompt

Create a 3D zombie apocalypse survival map set in a ghost town, using one main street as a small arena to hold. The street runs through deserted town buildings and open approaches where normal, gold, and larger diamond zombies can enter and gather.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Survival: the game is an endless zombie horde defense where the player holds one arena until defeated.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-bounded` from the Horde Defense preset. The contained arena is one ghost-town main street, bounded tightly enough to keep players and each escalating wave in contact. There is deliberately no escape or winner zone because the run ends only when the player dies.

## Config requirements

Draw `cover-los` as ghost-town street cover: abandoned wagons, wreckage, porch corners, troughs, and barricade fragments that break zombie sightlines without sealing the road. Keep the deserted buildings as exterior arena walls and silhouettes rather than enterable interiors. Omit destructible structures because the brief does not ask for collapsing buildings.

## Layout requirements

Place `spawner-npc` origins around the open approaches into the street, outside immediate player view and distributed between both street ends and side passages. They must support exactly 3 zombie species: normal ground zombies, levitating gold zombies, and larger flying diamond zombies. Their navigable gathering ground is the central street, with airborne entries kept clear of roofs and signs.

## Layout components

Build one short main-street arena flanked by deserted false-front buildings. Keep a broad combat corridor through the center, with several open approach mouths feeding into it from the street ends and narrow side alleys. Scatter cover clusters along the edges while preserving a readable central firing lane and enough overhead clearance for levitating and flying enemies. Use dusty road material, boarded storefronts, dead lamps, broken wagons, signs, and sparse apocalypse debris. Close the outer boundary behind the facades and approach mouths so the horde remains contained. Frame the street lengthwise to show its defensive center and incoming routes.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render the authoritative ghost-town atmosphere first, then derive the top-down plan because the bounded street does not require computed topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, explicitly one main street. The theme is abandoned frontier ghost town during a zombie apocalypse. Use `P0`; the arena is a single-level exterior with no enterable buildings or separate maps.
