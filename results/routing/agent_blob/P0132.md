# Scene prompt

Create a glossy pink fashion-show dress-up map with a shared open styling area for all players and a fenced-off VIP section within the same space. Place a prominent runway/catwalk as the central presentation area, with surrounding open floor space for dressing up.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, specifically a timed dress-up experience followed by a fashion presentation.

## Shape and preset

Use `stage-runway`: the central catwalk is the presentation focus, fed by preparation and styling areas. The preset is Dress Up, with its social gathering and scenic fashion-display features retained.

## Config requirements

Use `social-hub` for the generous shared dressing and gathering floor around the runway. Use `vignette-photo` for glossy pink fashion backdrops and polished presentation spots at the catwalk end. Draw a clearly accessible free styling area, a fenced VIP styling section within the same open room, and a prominent runway with audience sightlines. The VIP fence must be visually permeable enough to keep the venue feeling unified while making its boundary unmistakable.

## Layout requirements

Place uncatalogued outfit-selection interaction points throughout the free styling area and additional ones inside the VIP section. Place a purchase-gate trigger at the single controlled VIP entrance, aligned to the visible fence opening; the Robux price and access check are gameplay configuration, not geometry. Mark a runway entrance, presentation endpoint, and exit so the timed show can move players through a clear sequence. Rankings and the ten-minute dressing timer are gameplay systems and add no further layout placement.

## Layout components

Build one glossy pink fashion venue around a long raised central runway. Spread the free styling area across the larger surrounding floor with mirrors, wardrobes, makeup counters, and open circulation. Reserve a smaller premium cluster behind decorative fencing, using a single gated entrance and more elaborate stations. Arrange audience space along both sides of the catwalk and put a polished photo backdrop at its presentation end. Keep every zone on one level, with broad paths from styling stations to the runway entrance. Enclose the venue with bright fashion-show walls, lighting rigs, and signage while preserving a clear central composition.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: establish the glossy venue hierarchy, VIP distinction, and runway presentation first, then derive the top-down plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is medium, supporting free styling, VIP styling, runway, and audience areas. The theme is glossy pink modern fashion show. Use `P0`; the fenced VIP section remains part of the same flat open space.
