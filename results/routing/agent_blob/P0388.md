# Scene prompt
Create a stylized cartoon New York City rooftop-running map: a forward route across connected skyscraper rooftops, with three fixed lanes spanning the rooftop path for clear dodging. Arrange rooftop gaps, elevated obstacles, and connecting structures along the route, with a dense city skyline extending around and below the course.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Infinite Runner: this is an automatically advancing reaction run rather than a player-paced rooftop obby.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-snap`, giving every traversed rooftop and connector exactly three fixed dodge lanes. The preset is Free Runner, but its usual free-steering shape is replaced with `lane-snap` because the author explicitly chose fixed lanes; its modular path, jumping, and run-ending hazards remain recognizable.

## Config requirements
Use `chunk-modular` for connected rooftop segments whose exits align cleanly with the next skyscraper approach. Use `obstacle-jump` for rooftop gaps and raised obstacles, `hazard-kill` for falls into the city void and collision-ending blockers, and `settlement-density` for the dense stylized New York skyline surrounding and extending below the playable rooftops.

## Layout requirements
No pickups or powerups were requested, so none are added. Place one `trigger-despawn` cleanup volume a fixed safe distance behind the camera to recycle cleared rooftop segments. Segment assembly must preserve three readable lanes and must always leave a clearable jump or dodge action; the exact spacing should expand with runner speed.

## Layout components
Build a forward chain of broad skyscraper rooftops, each carrying three visually distinct lanes, connected by short bridges, rooftop links, or clear jump gaps. Raised vents, signs, ducts, and simple blockers create the dodge rhythm. The dense city grid is non-playable scenery far below and around the course. Keep rooftop edges unmistakable, avoid ambiguous landings, and compose the scene toward a strong skyline vanishing point with the active route centered.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, because every rooftop transition, lane continuation, and jump landing must form a valid endless sequence.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is huge because the rooftop route repeats without a fixed endpoint. The theme is a bright stylized cartoon New York skyline. Use `P6` for valid-by-construction modular routing and jump spacing; the elevated setting itself adds no extra route because playable surfaces do not overlap.
