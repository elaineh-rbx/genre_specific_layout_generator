# Scene prompt
Create a 3D zombie horror map set on a single enclosed mall floor, with dim lighting and fog. Arrange a central concourse with storefronts and interior corridors branching off it, leaving clear sightlines and walkable routes through the tense indoor space.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Use Shooter. The stated activity is a gun-wielding player shooting zombie enemies; the horror treatment changes the mood, not the dominant combat genre.

## Shape and preset
Choose `interior-single` from outside Shooter’s usual set because the entire map is one enclosed mall floor and the player never crosses an exterior boundary. Use the PvE Shooter preset, substituting this single-interior shape while retaining hostile origins and corridor chokepoints; drop its outside-to-inside structure.

## Config requirements
Use `choke-bottleneck` where branching mall corridors narrow at storefront corners and entrances, focusing zombie encounters without blocking circulation. Draw one central concourse, attached storefront facades and shop spaces, branching interior corridors, dim practical lighting, and low fog. Preserve clear firing sightlines down the concourse and major corridors. Do not add upper floors, outdoor streets, or unrelated tactical fortifications.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` origins for zombies at the concealed far ends of several branch corridors and in selected back-of-store recesses, oriented toward the central concourse and kept outside the player’s initial view. Place one player spawn at one end of the concourse with a protected clear radius and a readable first route forward. Ensure every zombie origin has a connected walkable route to the concourse; no exterior transition or separate lobby is placed.

## Layout components
Build one small enclosed mall floor centered on a broad straight concourse. Branch several shorter corridors from it and line both with storefronts, occasional open shop interiors, support columns, benches, planters, and shuttered units used sparingly so sightlines remain clear. Use level tiled flooring throughout with no stairs, stacked surfaces, or overhang-dependent play. Seal the boundary with mall walls and closed service doors. Compose the concourse as the strong central axis, with darker branches receding to either side through fog.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: draw the authoritative corridor plan first, then dress the isometric, because every concealed zombie origin needs a connected route into the concourse and accidental sealed branches would break encounters.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by one mall floor. The theme is tense enclosed zombie horror with dim light and fog. Route `P0`; the map is interior-only, single-surface, continuous, and free of outside-to-inside transitions or stacked floors.
