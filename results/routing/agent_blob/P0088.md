# Scene prompt
Create a real 3D obby map in a simple bright sky-world, with a small floating course suspended above clouds. Arrange clear, readable 3D platforms, moving spinner obstacles, and gaps in a sequential route toward a finish area, sized for a short 3–5 minute run. Include a few physical checkpoint markers along the course.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant and only genre is Obby & Platformer because the avatar deliberately walks and jumps through a finite obstacle course.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-flat` for a mostly horizontal point-to-point chain of floating platforms. Match the Classic Obby preset exactly, with its sequential track, checkpoints, jump gaps, and moving obstacles.

## Config requirements
Apply `path-track` as one ordered platform chain suspended above clouds and spaced to valid jump limits. Apply `obstacle-jump` to the readable gaps between platforms. Apply `obstacle-moving` to rotating spinner obstacles placed on stable platform sections. Draw the physical pads for a few `checkpoint-respawn` markers and draw `winner-zone` as a distinct finish platform at the route’s end.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional side of `checkpoint-respawn` on the physical pads at safe intervals through the 3–5 minute route, always after a completed obstacle cluster rather than inside a spinner sweep or near an edge. “A few” is not an exact count, so preserve that wording rather than inventing a number. Place the functional `winner-zone` once on the final platform, beyond the last obstacle and reachable from the critical path.

## Layout components
Build one small floating course above a bright cloud layer. Start on a broad safe platform, then sequence clear jump platforms, several stable spinner platforms, and additional gap crossings toward one visually prominent finish platform. Group obstacles into short readable sections separated by checkpoint pads. Keep the critical path unambiguous, every gap within supported avatar physics, and recovery starts clear of moving hazards. The open boundary is a sky void with clouds far below; no surrounding terrain or unrelated hub is needed. Compose the route diagonally through frame so its start, rising rhythm, and finish remain legible.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: platform connectivity, gap legality, and spinner clearance must be valid before the sky-world styling is applied.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, set by a short 3–5 minute first clear. The theme is simple, bright, high-readability sky world above clouds. Use `P6` because the course must be valid by construction; no additional modifier is forced.
