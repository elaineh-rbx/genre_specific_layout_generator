# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map showing a single polished boutique fashion studio room with a cozy luxury, modern feel, using warm lighting and pink, cream, gold, and white decor. Arrange distinct connected zones within the room: a dressing area with feminine avatar customization stations, clothing displays, and large dressing-room mirrors; a mirror-walk runway with elegant lighting for presenting completed outfits; and a photo booth with changeable backdrop space and posing area. Keep the studio compact and cohesive, with the dressing area serving as the main outfit-assembly zone and a clear path leading to the runway for presentation.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim. Avatar styling is the core activity; the single-player score and coin progression do not require a second spatial genre.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single` for one compact boutique studio containing all three activity zones. The preset is none: Dress Up contributes the photo-ready idea, but its audience-facing `stage-runway` and social gathering bundle contradict this private mirror-walk studio.

## Config requirements
Use `vignette-photo` for the dedicated photo booth with changeable backgrounds, flattering lights, and a posing area. Add free-text visible geometry for eight customization categories—Tops, Bottoms, Dresses, Shoes, Hair, Accessories, Makeup, and Props—with around 50 placeholder fashion items across the initial collection. Include clothing displays, vanity surfaces, full-height mirrors, elegant runway lights, backdrop supports, and a clear visual path from dressing to presentation.

## Layout requirements
Place one free-text player spawn at the dressing-area entrance, facing the customization stations. Site interaction positions at all eight category stations and distribute the roughly 50 selectable items without overlapping access. Place one presentation-and-scoring trigger along the mirror-walk runway and one screenshot pose marker in the photo booth. Currency, login rewards, outfit saving, thumbnails, and score calculations are systems rather than layout geometry.

## Layout components
Build one enclosed studio room divided into three connected zones: a large dressing area as the primary zone, a narrow mirror-walk runway reached by a clear aisle, and a compact photo booth beside the runway. Arrange the eight category stations around open central dressing space, with large mirrors and warm-lit displays. Give the runway no audience seating or judging desk. Use pink, cream, gold, and white finishes, soft upholstery, polished floors, and warm modern lighting. Frame all three zones together so the room reads as cohesive.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the luxury visual identity first, then derive the simple single-room plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by one compact room with three zones and around 50 display items. The theme is cozy modern boutique luxury. Use `P0`; all play occupies one level in one interior without transitions or overhangs.
