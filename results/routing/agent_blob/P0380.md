# Scene prompt

Create a 3D space-flight map with an infinite small three-lane road stretching forward through open space. A small flying vehicle travels just above the road, while large asteroids appear ahead as lane-blocking obstacles, each filling one lane at a time.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant and sole genre is Infinite Runner. The vehicle advances endlessly along a scrolling route while the player reacts to lane obstacles.

## Shape and preset

Use `lane-snap` as the single shape: three rigid parallel lanes support discrete lateral dodges by the flying vehicle. Match the Lane Runner preset, retaining its modular chunk structure but replacing coin trails and moving obstacles with the explicitly requested jumpable, lane-blocking asteroids.

## Config requirements

Use `chunk-modular` for small straight three-lane road segments whose exit and origin pivots align on one forward axis. Use `obstacle-jump` for large asteroids that each obstruct exactly one lane and leave the other lanes readable and clear. The road should float through open space with a dark starfield backdrop, while the vehicle’s low flight height remains visually anchored to the road.

## Layout requirements

Place one `trigger-despawn` cleanup volume a fixed distance behind the camera and behind the active chunk queue. Asteroid patterns must preserve at least one clear lane in every obstacle row, and their forward spacing must scale with current speed to preserve reaction time. No collectibles, power-ups, lap triggers, checkpoints, or terminal finish region are required.

## Layout components

Build a repeating queue of small, straight road chunks, each exactly three lanes wide. Mark each lane clearly and keep the road edges crisp against empty space. Arrange oversized asteroids ahead in readable one-lane blocks, never sealing all three lanes at one depth. The open starfield has no ground plane, side scenery, intersections, finish line, or endpoint. Compose the active road queue toward a strong forward vanishing point.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: draw the lane and chunk plan first, then dress it as an isometric space road. The top-down plan is authoritative because every generated sequence must retain valid alignment and a clearable lane.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale band is small per generated chunk, although the assembled run is endless. The theme is minimalist space flight over a three-lane cosmic road. Use `P6` because modular alignment and speed-dependent obstacle clearance must be valid by construction. No `CHECK` is needed because play stays in one shallow flight envelope above a visible surface.
