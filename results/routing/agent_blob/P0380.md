# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] movement — Q: Is the player running on the road, or piloting something that flies just above it? A: The player is piloting a small flying vehicle that travels just above the road through space.
- [author] shape — Q: Should lateral movement snap between fixed lanes, or steer freely across the road? A: Use fixed lanes for simple, readable obstacle dodging.
- [author] scale — Q: How wide is the road, and how big should the asteroids be relative to it? A: Make it a small three-lane road; asteroids should be large enough to block one lane at a time.

## Enriched image prompt
Create an endless small three-lane road suspended through outer space for a compact flying vehicle travelling just above its surface. Keep the three fixed lanes clearly separated and assemble the road from seamless straight modular segments. Place large asteroids as jump obstacles, each sized to block exactly one lane at a time while leaving readable alternatives. Frame the active road segment against open space and conceal the distant spawning edge without adding unrelated structures.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Infinite Runner because forward travel is endless and obstacle avoidance happens reactively along a procedurally extended path.

## Shape and preset
Use `lane-snap`: exactly three rigid parallel lane slots support simple lateral dodging. Match the Lane Runner preset for its fixed-lane shape and deterministic chunks, but replace its coin trails and moving traffic with the author’s jumpable asteroid hazards.

## Config requirements
Use `chunk-modular` for road segments whose exit and origin pivots align on one axis without drift. Use `obstacle-jump` for asteroids that the vehicle must clear vertically and `hazard-kill` for asteroid collision. Use `barrier-horizon` to conceal the far end of the active space road while new segments appear.

## Layout requirements
Place one `trigger-despawn` cleanup volume behind the camera, one completed modular segment beyond the trailing edge of the active road. Obstacle placement must keep every generated sequence clearable: each asteroid blocks one lane, never all three at once, and spacing scales with current forward speed to preserve reaction time. No collectible or power-up placement is requested.

## Layout components
Build a repeating queue of straight three-lane road chunks floating in space. Every chunk shares identical entry and exit pivots and lane centres. Distribute one-lane-wide asteroids across the road with valid open-lane or jump responses, keep the flying vehicle close to the road surface, and use the horizon treatment to hide chunk generation. Open space forms the outer visual boundary.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the image model should establish readable lane occupancy and valid obstacle spacing before isometric dressing; `authored_plan` is reserved for supported maze and racing-route generators, not infinite-runner chunks.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use small scale for each active chunk because the three lanes, one-lane asteroids, and compact vehicle are the legible units; the world is endless through repetition rather than one huge frame. The theme is a clean outer-space road. Use `P6` because chunk alignment and obstacle sequences must be valid by construction.
