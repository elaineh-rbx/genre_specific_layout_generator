# Scene prompt
Create a compact, plainly playable 3D idle farming simulator map in a retro studded blocky Roblox style with vibrant pastel colors and soft ambient lighting, viewed from a fixed third-person isometric top-down camera. A bustling central courtyard forms the hub, with courtyard-facing NPC market stalls for seeds, crop selling, and trading, plus a prominent leaderboard board. Six individual fenced garden plots surround the hub in a clear ring; each plot has a flat grass grid, dark-brown plantable dirt tiles, a simple white wooden fence, and a customizable front gate sign reading "[Player Name]'s Garden." Design plots to visibly support expansion from a 3x3 to a 10x10 dirt-tile farm grid, with open walking paths connecting every plot to the central courtyard.
