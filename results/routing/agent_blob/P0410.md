# Scene prompt

Create a 3D vertical escape-course map beginning at the bottom of a giant active volcano and rising to its summit as a compact, roughly two-minute climb. The Volcano course ascends through lava rocks, broken bridges, volcanic platforms, giant glowing crystals, and floating magma islands, with checkpoints spaced along the route and lava-filled drops below. Place teleport portals connecting this starting world to four separate themed courses: Ice Mountain, Sky Islands, Crystal Caverns, and Space Volcano, each with its own distinct obstacle layout. Use vivid lava, glowing crystals, particles, and polished volcanic lighting.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Simulation because this is explicitly a “+1” stat grinder whose rising speed, upgrades, and rebirth loop drive progression. Obby & Platformer is secondary because progress is earned on player-paced jumping courses.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-chaptered`, outside Simulation’s usual set, because the Volcano and four destination worlds are five genuinely separate maps reached by portals. The closest preset is Classic Obby, with its flat-course shape replaced by the chaptered world structure while retaining its sequential track, jumps, and checkpoints.

## Config requirements

Apply `path-track` as a physics-valid ascending chain of lava rocks, broken bridges, volcanic platforms, and magma islands. Apply `obstacle-jump` to the discrete gaps, `checkpoint-respawn` to visible safe pads, `hazard-kill` to the lava filling failed drops, `winner-zone` to the summit payoff, and `teleporter-link` to visible world portals. Apply `island-cluster` to the separated floating magma landings. Giant glowing crystals are both landmark props and pickup cues.

## Layout requirements

Place `checkpoint-respawn` pads at regular progress breaks along the roughly two-minute Volcano climb, each on a broad safe landing and respawning at the latest reached pad. Place `collectible-nodes` as glowing lava crystals along the main route and slightly riskier side lines. Place one `winner-zone` at the summit. Place four `teleporter-link` destinations, each clearly labeled and linked to exactly one separate course: Ice Mountain, Sky Islands, Crystal Caverns, or Space Volcano.

## Layout components

The starting world runs from a lava-filled volcanic basin to a summit, using a single readable upward route of rock steps, broken bridge spans, glowing crystal ledges, platforms, and floating magma islands. Below the critical path, lava visually fills the fall volume. Safe checkpoint shelves divide the climb. A summit platform holds the payoff and four destination portals. Build five separate courses total, each with a distinct obstacle layout and theme. Keep the Volcano compact enough for about two minutes of successful traversal. Frame the Volcano vertically so the summit remains an obvious destination.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first and treat it as authoritative, then dress the isometric image from it, because every jump chain and portal connection must be valid; this is not a maze or racing circuit, so `authored_plan` is unavailable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a huge scale because there are five separate course maps, although the starting Volcano itself is compact. The theme is vivid polished volcanic fantasy with emissive crystals, magma particles, and dramatic hot lighting. Use `P4` for separate worlds, `P6` for valid obstacle structure, `P2` for vertically overhanging platforms, and `CHECK` for the floating-island play envelope.
