Make the starting Volcano a roughly two-minute climb from the base to the summit, using lava rocks, broken bridges, volcanic platforms, giant glowing crystals, and floating magma islands above lethal lava. Place clear checkpoint pads throughout and a distinct summit reward area. At the Volcano base, include one compact progression hub with egg and upgrade displays, a rebirth pad, a global leaderboard wall, and the first portal.

Give each later world its own simple themed obstacle layout rather than reskinning the same climb: icy mountain surfaces, floating sky landmasses, crystal-cavern passages, and a cosmic volcano course. Keep geometry and effects sparse enough for low lag while retaining polished lighting, particles, lava glow, and crystal readability. Do not depict UI, gamepasses, pets as menus, save systems, scripts, or controls.

Place four paired `teleporter-link` connections linking the five worlds in order. Place one `trigger-rebirth` TriggerZone and one `tracker-leaderboard` Tracker in the Volcano-base hub. Sky Islands and Volcano include discrete floating landings. Four portal pairs connect adjacent maps in the listed order. Each map has its own boundary and endpoint.
