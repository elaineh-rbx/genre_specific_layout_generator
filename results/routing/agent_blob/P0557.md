# Scene prompt

Create a small modern military-themed FPS lobby for up to 8 players, arranged as a compact staging area with clear open space for players to gather. Include a physical exit or deployment point that leads toward a future conventional team-versus-team lane-based match map.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Shooter. The lobby belongs to an FPS even though the match space itself is deferred.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-bounded`, taken from outside Shooter’s usual shape list: one compact, single-level staging space contains the whole current build. No Shooter preset fits a lobby-only deliverable, so the preset is none; the future team map may later use Team Deathmatch, but its lanes must not be built now.

## Config requirements

Draw an uncluttered modern military staging room with a central gathering floor and one visibly framed deployment exit. No canonical Shooter image option is required: combat cover, opposing bases, and chokepoints would incorrectly turn the lobby into the future match map.

## Layout requirements

Place eight player spawn markers around the perimeter of the gathering floor, facing inward and clear of the deployment path. Place one deployment trigger at the physical exit, sited across its threshold. These are scene-specific placements without canonical option IDs.

## Layout components

Build one small rectangular lobby for up to eight players. Keep a broad open gathering zone at center, with restrained military wall panels, equipment cases, and briefing details pushed to the edges. A short, broad circulation path runs from the gathering floor to the single deployment doorway on one side. Use a flat hard-surface floor, solid enclosing walls, and no exterior terrain. Frame the room from above so the gathering area, all spawn positions, and the deployment threshold remain legible.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down view; visual staging leads because the room has no topology that could make play invalid.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is small, driven by the explicit eight-player capacity. The theme is simple modern military FPS staging. Route `P0`: one flat interior-only surface needs no linked exterior, overhang, separate map, or structural-validity pass.
