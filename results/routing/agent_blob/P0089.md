# Scene prompt
Create a 3D toy-like plastic Roblox obby built as a compact tower stacking upward, with successive platforming stages in bright solid colours.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant and only genre is Obby & Platformer, explicitly named by the author and expressed as avatar platforming.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-tower` for a compact upward route whose successive stages stack directly above lower ones. Match the Tower Obby preset.

## Config requirements
Apply `path-track` as one continuous upward chain of physics-legal platforms. Apply `obstacle-climb` to compact vertical trusses, short ladders, ledge wraps, and stepped climbs that preserve the tower form. Apply `obstacle-timing` sparingly as bright platforms that disappear or cycle within clearly bounded stages. Draw `winner-zone` as a distinct top platform and reward area. Keep every stage in a bright solid colour so adjacent sections are immediately distinguishable.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional side of `winner-zone` once at the tower summit, on a flat enclosed landing beyond the final obstacle. No checkpoint was requested, and the Tower Obby preset does not imply one, so do not add checkpoint placement.

## Layout components
Build one compact vertical tower with successive platforming stages stacked upward around a central open shaft or compact structural core. Start with a safe ground-level pad, then alternate stepped platforms, ledge wraps, climbable pieces, and a few timed platform sections. Give each successive stage a distinct bright solid colour in a toy-like plastic palette, using clean silhouettes and high contrast between active platforms and background structure. Keep all critical jumps and climbs within supported avatar movement limits and prevent upper platforms from creating unreadable collisions on the route below. Bound the playable tower with selective rails or outer framing where needed while keeping falls visually clear. Compose the isometric view to show the base-to-summit colour progression.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the ordered jump-and-climb chain must be valid before the toy-plastic tower is dressed, even though the stacked captures require additional vertical handling.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small because the tower is explicitly compact. The theme is bright solid-colour toy plastic in a classic Roblox register. Use genre-wide `P6` for valid platforming and `P2` because upper tower stages overhang and occlude lower ones.
