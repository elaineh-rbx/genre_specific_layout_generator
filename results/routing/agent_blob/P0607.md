# Scene prompt
Create a 3D free-driving map set in a small realistic city-street environment, with interconnected paved roads for driving a Pagani-style supercar. Include an open street network without a race start, finish line, or track layout.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The genre is Simulation, specifically free vehicle operation. Racing is not secondary because the author explicitly excludes a start, finish, and track layout.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open` with the Vehicle Sim preset. Keep its one shared persistent map and vehicle-width road circulation, but drop `resource-shared` because there is no extraction or production loop. The small city is freely traversed in any order.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for interconnected realistic paved streets with lane markings, junctions, turning room, and several ways to loop through the map. Use `settlement-density` for a compact realistic city-street setting of exterior building façades around the roads. Render one Pagani-style supercar as the focal drivable vehicle on a clear street; do not add race gates, start gantries, finish lines, or checkpoint props.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly one vehicle spawn and interaction position for the Pagani-style supercar on a broad, level street segment, aligned with the road and clear of intersections. No race start, finish trigger, lap checkpoint, resource node, delivery station, or progression gate belongs in the layout.

## Layout components
Build one small city zone with a connected paved street network, sidewalks, realistic intersections, and compact blocks of non-enterable urban buildings. Include at least one wider avenue and several shorter side streets that reconnect, allowing continuous free-driving loops without forming a formal circuit. Position the single supercar in a safe curbside bay or open lane with immediate access to the network. Add restrained realistic street props such as lamps, signs, barriers, and planters on sidewalks while preserving supercar turning clearance and long sightlines. Outer building blocks and naturally closed street ends form the map boundary. Compose the car in the foreground with multiple connected road choices visible ahead.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: draw the interconnected street plan first and treat it as authoritative so every route remains driveable, then dress it as the realistic city isometric.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a small scale, matching the requested compact city while leaving enough road for free driving. The theme is realistic contemporary city streets with a premium Pagani-style supercar. Route through `P0`; one exterior ground surface and façade-only buildings require no extra modifier.
