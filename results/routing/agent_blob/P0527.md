# Scene prompt
Create a 3D ASMR-themed obby tower: a small, clearly climbable vertical tower with 10 stacked stages made from satisfying-looking objects. Each stage contains one this-or-that choice point splitting into two short routes themed around different ASMR objects, then rejoining before the next stage. Place large screens playing ASMR videos at the bottom and at the top of the tower. The summit opens onto a playground area with cool items and a restart portal.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Obby & Platformer, with the player climbing discrete movement challenges at their own pace.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-tower` from the Tower Obby preset: a compact vertical course of 10 stacked stages. Keep the preset’s ordered track and summit reward area, while replacing its assumed timed and climbing obstacle mix with the prompt’s paired ASMR-object routes.

## Config requirements
`path-track` is the physics-valid ascending platform chain, split once on every stage into two short satisfying-object branches that rejoin before climbing onward. `checkpoint-respawn` draws a flat safe pad at each stage transition. `winner-zone` is the summit playground with cool interactive-looking items. `teleporter-link` is the visible restart portal at the summit. Also draw two large ASMR video screens, one facing the base area and one at the summit.

## Layout requirements
Place 10 `checkpoint-respawn` markers, one on the safe entry pad of each stage, so a fall returns the player to that stage. Place one `winner-zone` trigger over the summit playground. Place one `teleporter-link` destination link from the summit portal back to the tower’s ground-level start.

## Layout components
The ground-level base holds the first large screen and the tower entrance. Above it, 10 compact stages stack vertically; each has one fork into two short object-themed platform routes, then a safe rejoining pad before the next ascent. The top opens into a bounded playground deck containing cool items, the second large screen, and the restart portal. Platforms resemble tactile ASMR objects and remain within legal jump spacing. Open air surrounds the tower, with rails or clear edge treatment on safe pads. Frame the full tower from a slightly elevated isometric angle while keeping both endpoint decks legible.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: render the plan first because every fork, rejoin, checkpoint landing, and vertical jump chain must remain playable, then dress the isometric view from it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by 10 short stacked stages. The theme is calming, colorful ASMR tactility with satisfying toy-like materials. `P6` is required because the obby route and jump spacing must be valid by construction; `P2` is also required because stacked stages overhang and occlude one another.
