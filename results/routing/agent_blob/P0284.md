# Scene prompt
Create a bright, cartoonish 3D UFO flight map across a compact countryside of a few connected fields. Place scattered cows on the ground below, with open airspace above the rolling landscape for the UFO and passing airplanes.

# Agent decision
### Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation because the player freely operates a UFO in a persistent score loop, collecting targets while navigating vehicle-scale airspace.

### Shape and preset
Use `volume-open-air`, outside Simulation’s usual short shape list but already used by its Aircraft Operation preset: open air is the playable volume, with the countryside as the surface below. The preset is Aircraft Operation, retaining its airspace shape and repeatable task interaction while dropping its hub and progression gates because neither was requested.

### Config requirements
Use `trigger-task` for the visible UFO beaming interaction over cow targets. Use `npc-population` for cartoon cows scattered across the ground and the field areas they occupy. Use `terrain-relief` for gentle rolling countryside without overhangs. Draw a few connected agricultural fields with clear boundaries, plus bright cartoon airplanes passing through the open air above them.

### Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `trigger-task` at each cow’s ground location so the UFO beam can acquire it from above. The layout half of `npc-population` distributes cows across all fields rather than clustering them at one edge; the prompt gives no exact count. Place airplane entry points and broad aerial travel lanes around the perimeter at varied headings and heights, keeping a safe initial flight pocket while sustaining the avoidance challenge.

### Layout components
Build a compact countryside of a few adjoining fields, separated by low fences, hedges, or contrasting crop edges while remaining visually connected. Gentle hills shape the ground and scattered cows provide targets across the full footprint. The principal play space is the unobstructed air volume above, crossed by several readable airplane paths. Keep tall obstacles sparse so the UFO can descend toward cows. Bound the ground with distant stylized countryside and bound the flight volume invisibly above and around the fields. Frame the isometric view to show both targets below and aerial traffic overhead.

### Render order
Use `isometric`: the vertical relationship between UFO airspace, passing airplanes, and cow targets is the defining composition, while no maze or circuit requires an authored plan.

### Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by a compact flight area over only a few fields. The theme is bright, cartoonish pastoral science fiction. Use `CHECK` because the playable volume is open air and must be checked for self-occlusion, plus `tiered` for rolling ground with no overhang.
