# Agent decision

## Clarifications resolved
- [author] theme — Q: Should this look bright and cartoonish, or grounded and realistic? A: Make it bright and cartoonish, matching the playful UFO-and-cows premise.
- [author] scale — Q: How much ground should one flight cover — a few fields, or a whole countryside you can cross for a minute? A: Use a few fields; keep the map compact and focused for a small playable flight area.
- [author] goal — Q: Is the goal a quota of cows in a time limit, an endless score, or something else? A: Use an endless score: beam up cows for points while avoiding airplanes.

## Enriched image prompt
Create a compact bright-cartoon countryside flight map with a few clearly separated fields beneath an open playable airspace. Distribute cows across the fields in readable groups with unobstructed UFO approach space above them. Keep the air volume broad enough for the UFO to maneuver and for airplanes to cross through on distinct paths, while the ground remains close and legible for beaming targets. Use a bounded looping play area with no finish destination or terminal reward zone.

## Genre
The genre is Simulation.

## Shape and preset
Use `volume-open-air`, a Zone (Open Airspace) where the UFO flies over discrete target fields rather than walking on one continuous route. The preset is Aircraft Operation, keeping its flight-space shape and adapting its task stations into cow-abduction target areas while dropping unsupported hub and progression gates.

## Config requirements
Use `npc-population` for the non-hostile cows and their field ground, and `trigger-task` for visible clearings around those cow groups where the UFO performs repeated beam-up actions. The separated fields and crossing airplane lanes are direct scene-specific visual requirements.

## Layout requirements
Place `npc-population` Zones across each field for cow groups, with a corresponding `trigger-task` TriggerZone around every active beam-up target area. Place `spawner-npc` Spawners around the airspace perimeter so airplanes enter on distinct crossing paths and exit without originating over a cow target. Scoring, beam behavior, collision penalties, and endless progression are mechanics.

## Layout components
The build contains a few compact fields arranged beneath one bounded flight envelope, multiple cow target clearings, open maneuvering space above every field, and several non-overlapping airplane crossing corridors through the air. The countryside forms the visible floor of the scene; the playable boundary encloses the air volume without adding a finish line.

## Render order
Use `isometric` because the relationship between the UFO-height airspace, fields, cows, and crossing airplanes must read volumetrically and no fixed route needs procedural construction.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale so several fields fit in frame while individual cows remain legible. The theme is playful bright-cartoon UFO countryside. `CHECK` is required because players traverse a three-dimensional flight volume that may self-occlude; no separate-map or overhang pass is required.
