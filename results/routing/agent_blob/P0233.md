# Scene prompt
Create a 3D game map set entirely inside one small barbershop, with a compact customer area and a barber chair/workstation as the central cutting space. Include a simple queue area for NPC customers waiting their turn, arranged clearly around the focused interior.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Simulation: cutting each queued NPC customer’s hair is a defined repeatable job task that receives a score, rather than open-ended salon roleplay.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single`, taken from outside Simulation’s usual shape set, because the entire map is one small barbershop and players never go outside. The preset is none; Role Sim assumes a shared world with circulation and vehicle routes, while this brief is one focused task room.

## Config requirements
Use the visible form of `trigger-task` for the central barber chair and complete cutting workstation, including mirror, counter, tools, products, and floor space around the seated customer. Use `npc-population` for the non-hostile customers and the compact waiting area they occupy. Add humorous haircut references, exaggerated style posters, playful product bottles, and a visible results backdrop as scene-specific geometry, without turning scoring or failure outcomes into static map objects.

## Layout requirements
Place one `trigger-task` interaction volume directly at the barber chair, aligned so the active NPC sits before the mirror and the player can stand behind and beside the chair. Place the `npc-population` queue as an ordered sequence of waiting positions beginning near the customer seating and ending at the workstation, with enough clearance that waiting NPCs do not block the active station or entrance. No collectibles, checkpoints, or progression gates are needed.

## Layout components
Build one compact rectangular salon centered on a single prominent barber chair and mirrored workstation. Put the waiting and queue area along one side wall, leaving a clear path from the entrance edge to the chair. Add a small reception counter near the front, storage shelves and wash or tool surfaces toward the back, and readable open floor around the task station. Use colorful flooring, oversized mirrors, bright lights, funny haircut posters, quirky décor, product displays, and tidy salon props. The enclosing shop walls are the hard boundary. Compose the view so the chair is the focal point while the complete customer queue remains visible.

## Render order
Render in `isometric` order: the funny salon character and workstation composition are authoritative, and the simple one-room plan can be projected afterward.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by one chair, one queue, and one customer area. The theme is bright comedic barbershop. Use `P0`: this is a single interior surface with no exterior transition or structural modifier.
