## Enriched image prompt
Build one small playful barbershop interior focused on a funny NPC haircut loop. Place one barber chair and haircut station facing a large mirror, with a clear working space around it. Near the shop entrance, arrange two simple waiting seats or queue spots leading toward the chair, supporting three NPC customers total: one active customer and two waiting. Add one compact counter for basic barber tools while keeping the room uncluttered and easy to read. Use a bright, humorous cartoon barbershop look; wrong haircut results affect the NPC appearance rather than adding map geometry.

## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation because the player performs a defined repeatable barber task for queued customers.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single`, taken from outside Simulation's usual set, because the entire build is one small barbershop room and no exterior is generated. The matched preset is Role Sim, substituting the single-interior shape and removing its unrequested vehicle route.

## Config requirements
Draw the visible half of `trigger-task` as one barber chair, mirror, and haircut work station. Use `path-circulation` for the short customer route from the entrance through two waiting positions to the chair and then out. Draw the visible half of universal `npc-population` as exactly three NPC customers occupying that queue.

## Layout requirements
Place one `trigger-task` TriggerZone at the barber chair and work station. Place `npc-population` occupancy for three customers: one chair position and two ordered waiting positions. Place one free-text Spawner at the shop entrance for the NPC queue. Haircut scoring and funny failed-cut appearance changes are mechanics and do not create scoring volumes.

## Layout components
The single enclosed room contains one entrance, two waiting positions, one barber chair, one large mirror, one haircut work surface, and one compact tool counter. The circulation line runs directly from the entrance to the waiting area and active chair without crossing the work zone. Three NPC positions make the queue visible. Frame the whole room so the chair, mirror, waiting line, and entrance are readable together.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the playful interior presentation leads, and the one-room queue path is spatially simple.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale band is small because individual furniture, tools, and three customer positions must remain legible. The theme is a bright, funny, cartoon barbershop. The pipeline uses `P0`: this is one interior-only room on a single surface, with no outside-to-inside transition or stacked play.
