# Scene prompt

Create a 3D third-person collectathon map in a stylized, colourful Roblox-style open world, kept small and readable as a vertical slice. Ground-level paths and raised platforms form a simple exploration route, with coins or gems scattered across the level; include 10 collectibles and a distinct goal area. Leave open space around the playable terrain with a fall-off edge.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Adventure, with Obby & Platformer secondary because optional exploration includes required jumps across raised platforms.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open` as one compact, freely explored map with ground routes and elevated collectible platforms. The preset is none: no published bundle preserves both the open collectathon structure and this sparse vertical-slice brief.

## Config requirements

Build `path-trail` as short ground-level routes circulating through the playable terrain. Add `obstacle-jump` as raised platforms reached by simple, physics-legal jumps, with no required horizontal gap over 11 studs or vertical rise over 7.2 studs. Draw one visible `checkpoint-respawn` start pad on safe ground and one distinct `winner-zone` goal area. Leave open void around the map so the fall-off edge reads clearly.

## Layout requirements

Place exactly 10 `collectible-nodes` coins or gems across ground routes and raised platforms, distributing them so both levels are explored. Place the functional side of one `checkpoint-respawn` on the safe starting pad to receive players after a fall. Place one `winner-zone` completion volume in the distinct goal area, configured to open after all 10 collectibles are gathered.

## Layout components

Build one small contiguous ground island with a few colourful path loops, low grassy forms, and several raised platforms positioned above or just beyond its edges. Connect ground landmarks through readable paths and make elevated pickups visible enough to suggest routes without forcing a single order. Keep every jump reachable with default Roblox movement. Put the start pad on broad protected ground, distribute the 10 collectible locations across low and high routes, and place the goal at a visually distinct far-side platform or clearing. Enclose the terrain only through its clearly visible fall-off boundary and frame all playable surfaces against open space.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: establish reachable ground and platform routes first because impossible jump spacing would make collectibles inaccessible, then dress that authoritative plan isometrically.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, driven by a 10-collectible vertical slice. The theme is simple, colourful Roblox platforming. Use `P6` for valid jump construction and `P2` because raised platforms overhang lower playable ground.
