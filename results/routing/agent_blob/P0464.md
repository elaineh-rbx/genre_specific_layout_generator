# Scene prompt
Create a compact indoor professional basketball arena centered on a new standard full-size court, with marked hardwood flooring and simple bleachers surrounding the court.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant and only genre is Sports, focused on professional full-court basketball.

## Shape and preset
Use the single `space-bounded` shape for the rigid full-court perimeter within the compact arena. The preset is Court Sport, with `spectator-bleachers` added because the prompt explicitly surrounds the court with seating.

## Config requirements
Draw `trigger-bounds` as the standard full-court boundary and all painted hardwood markings. Draw `trigger-scoring` as two professional hoop-and-backboard assemblies on opposite ends. Draw `startpoint-play` as the center-circle tip-off and balanced player reset marks. Draw `spectator-bleachers` as simple tiered seating closely wrapping the sidelines and baselines. Keep the arena shell compact and entirely indoor.

## Layout requirements
Place one `trigger-bounds` detection perimeter exactly along the court edge. Place two `trigger-scoring` cylinders through the two rims, one at each end. Place `startpoint-play` markers at center court and in mirrored positions for opposing teams. Leave a clear safety and circulation strip between every court edge and the first bleacher row.

## Layout components
Build one standard-proportioned full basketball court at the center of a single enclosed arena. Use polished hardwood with sidelines, baselines, center line and circle, free-throw lanes, arcs, and three-point lines. Install exactly two hoops with transparent backboards on opposite baselines. Wrap the floor in a narrow unobstructed apron, then simple connected banks of bleachers along all four sides. Use plain arena walls and a high clear ceiling beyond the seats, without luxury boxes, concourses, or unrelated rooms. Compose the view symmetrically so the entire marked floor and both baskets remain readable.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: render the authoritative court plan first because regulation markings, mirrored baskets, safety clearances, and trigger alignment must be exact; derive the indoor arena view from it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, driven by a full-size court inside a compact seating bowl. The theme is polished professional hardwood basketball with restrained indoor arena dressing. Use `tiered` because the bleachers rise in steps without overhang; no `P3` applies because the whole map is one interior.
