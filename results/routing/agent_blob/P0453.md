# Scene prompt

The main play space is a compact quarter-field turf arena sized so players can cross it in a few seconds, with a goal and marked goalie crease at each short end, field lines, a central faceoff spot, and open 3v3 play space between them. Include a separate small practice area for Skills Challenge drills, arranged with shooting targets and dodging cones.

Place one central faceoff `startpoint-play` and six player starts, three per team, mirrored across midfield. Keep each marked crease clear around its goal and site the practice triggers away from match play.

Put one team bench along each sideline. Frame the field with crowd seating, lights, team-color accents, and a visible scoreboard. Add a small adjacent practice enclosure containing a shooting lane with targets and a clear cone-dodging route. Separate practice from the live field with a low barrier or safe gap while keeping both in one arena map.
