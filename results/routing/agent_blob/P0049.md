# Scene prompt

Create a 3D medieval sword-fighting arena as a small, simple flat duelling ring for a few fighters at once, with an open focused combat floor and minimal surrounding arena boundary elements.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Action because the space is a dedicated close-range sword-fighting arena.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-bounded` as a single flat, collision-clean duelling ring. The preset is none: the Sword Fighting preset assumes terraces, powerups, hazards, and cover that contradict the explicitly simple flat floor.

## Config requirements

Use `spawn-protected` only at the arena entrances, expressed as shallow screened medieval entry alcoves that keep respawning fighters out of an immediate attack line without intruding on the ring. Keep the central combat floor open. Add no pillars, high ground, pickups, chokepoints, or ring-out pits.

## Layout requirements

Place several evenly spaced respawn markers in the entry alcoves around the ring, with each marker facing the open combat floor and separated from active fighting by its `spawn-protected` screen. The brief says a few fighters rather than an exact number, so no numeric spawn count is imposed. Health bars and bloodless combat presentation are gameplay and interface behavior, not layout placements.

## Layout components

Build one small circular or compact polygonal duelling ring on a completely flat floor. Surround it with a low medieval stone, timber, or rope boundary that clearly marks the arena without blocking the view. Set shallow entrance alcoves just beyond that perimeter and preserve an uninterrupted open center for sword exchanges. Use restrained heraldic banners, worn stone paving, and simple timber details as minimal decoration. Compose the ring centrally and keep every fighting position visible in one frame.

## Render order

Render in `isometric` order because visual character leads and the single flat ring has no topology that requires a plan-first build.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, sized for only a few simultaneous fighters. The theme is stylized, bloodless medieval tournament combat. The route is `P0`: one flat bounded surface has no overhangs, interior transition, separate maps, or structural-validity requirement.
