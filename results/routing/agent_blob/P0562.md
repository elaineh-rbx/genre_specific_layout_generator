# Scene prompt

Create a small modern urban PvP survival map with simple buildings, streets, and cover. Arrange six gun boxes and six knife boxes around the map, distributed among building edges, street corners, and cover areas. Include a distinct trade area set apart from the combat streets.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Survival because the stated round is about outlasting other players, with Shooter secondary for armed PvP and weapon pickups.

## Shape and preset

Use `space-bounded` as one compact contained urban arena. No preset fits: the Survival presets concern disasters, hostile AI, or escape, while Shooter’s Arena Deathmatch would add a lane network, elevated positions, and looping rooms that this brief does not request.

## Config requirements

Draw `cover-los` as simple waist-high and full-body cover distributed across streets, corners, and building edges. Include a distinct trade zone physically set back from combat circulation, with a clear urban storefront or sheltered plaza identity. Show twelve box props in total: six visually identified as gun boxes and six as knife boxes.

## Layout requirements

Use `collectible-loot` for the weapon-box functionality. Place exactly six gun-box pickups and six knife-box pickups, spreading each type among building edges, street corners, and cover pockets without clustering all weapons in one route. Each pickup marker must bind to its matching visible box and preserve room for respawn after collection. Place the player starts around the combat perimeter, outside direct initial sightlines. The five-minute round duration and trading logic are scripting, not additional geometry.

## Layout components

Build a small urban block with a central combat street, two short side streets, simple building facades, corner recesses, and scattered cover. Connect every combat pocket by flat pavement while avoiding dead-end pickup traps. Put the trade area on one edge behind a clear spatial buffer from the streets, but keep it reachable by one obvious walkway. Enclose the map with continuous building walls and urban barriers. Frame the full combat area and separated trade zone clearly from above.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first as authoritative, then dress the isometric image, because fair pickup distribution, protected starts, and trade-zone separation depend on the plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is small, matching one five-minute round. The theme is clean modern urban survival. Route `P0`: the map is one flat bounded surface with no enterable interiors, overhangs, or separate maps.
