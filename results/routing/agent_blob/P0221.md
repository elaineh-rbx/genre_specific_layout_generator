# Scene prompt
Create a bright, cartoon-style 3D suburban map focused on one small residential street. Several prebuilt houses sit on individual lots along the street, with simple open space between homes and a casual neighborhood composition.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, canonical genre `roleplay-avatar-sim`, because players move into prebuilt homes and use the neighborhood for casual social roleplay.

## Shape and preset
Use `settlement-claimable`, with already-standing houses on individual lots that players claim rather than construct. The preset is Life, keeping its claimable housing, vehicle-capable road, and enterable interiors while dropping district separation because the brief asks for only one residential street.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for the single neighborhood street, with a simple two-way carriageway and pedestrian access to every lot. Use `building-interior` for houses players can enter after claiming them, and `settlement-density` for the coherent small suburban street rather than disconnected buildings. Keep the visible language bright, cartoon-like, and casual, with simple yards and generous spacing rather than realistic urban density.

## Layout requirements
Nothing canonical needs placement after segmentation. The prompt does not request pickups, NPCs, teleporters, task stations, or explicit spawn markers; claiming a home is a system attached to the rendered house lots and interiors.

## Layout components
Create exactly one short residential street with several prebuilt detached houses, each centered on its own individual lot. A sidewalk or simple pedestrian verge should connect each front path to the street, while open lawn and modest setbacks separate neighboring homes. Give the houses varied cartoon façades, roof colors, and silhouettes while maintaining one coherent suburban style. The street and sidewalks are the only main circulation; do not add a town center, shops, or extra districts. Bound the scene at both street ends and behind the lots with soft neighborhood edges such as trees or low fencing. Compose the map lengthwise so the full street and the rhythm of houses on both sides read clearly. No exact house count was stated.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: render the bright neighborhood look first as authoritative, then derive the top-down plan, since the single street has no topology-sensitive structure.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, driven by one street and several homes. The theme is bright casual cartoon suburbia. Use `P3` because players cross from exterior lots into enterable house interiors.
