# Scene prompt
Create a compact 3D survival arena for 12 players, with a living floor suspended above surrounding lava. The central ground mass is visibly organic and uneven, with sections that bulge, shrink, crack apart, ripple, and sprout physical obstacles, while lava fills the exposed gaps and encircles the arena.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Survival, specifically a timed disaster-survival round where everyone remaining after two minutes wins.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded`, one compact contained arena that keeps all 12 players exposed to the living-floor threat. The preset is Disaster Survival.

## Config requirements
Draw `hazard-kill` as lava surrounding the arena and visible through cracks and exposed gaps in its surface. Draw `terrain-tiered` as uneven organic bulges and raised safe spots that remain on one non-overhanging surface. Draw `destructible-cluster` as clearly segmented floor plates and obstacle sprouts that can crack, shrink, or break apart under scripts. The central mass should read as a living creature rather than static rock.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawn-protected` as exactly 12 evenly spaced round-start positions on initially stable portions of the central floor, each outside immediate lava gaps and clear of the first obstacle motion. Mark the exposed lava and opening cracks as damaging regions aligned to their visible geometry. No escape gate is needed because survival is determined by the timer.

## Layout components
The build is one compact central organic ground mass suspended over a continuous lava field. Its uneven surface is divided into readable sections that can bulge, contract, ripple, wiggle, crack apart, and sprout physical obstacles. Narrow fissures expose lava within the arena while a broad lava moat encircles the outer edge. Raised bulges provide temporary high ground without creating overhanging floors. Lava forms the hard boundary. Compose the living floor as the dominant centered silhouette, with glowing fissures tracing its segmented anatomy and enough clear initial footing for all 12 starts.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the expressive living-floor silhouette and readable lava exposure lead, while no maze or circuit requires a computed blueprint.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, explicitly compact for 12 players and fast two-minute rounds. The theme is grotesque organic terrain over bright molten lava. Use `tiered` for the non-overhanging uneven relief; no separate map, interior transition, or stacked surface is required.
