# Scene prompt

## Shape and preset

Preset is none because no Simulation preset describes this compact fishing route without adding unrelated jobs, upgrades, or extraction infrastructure.

## Config requirements
Use `water-body` for a shallow, visibly clear forest creek with several deeper fishable pools. Use `path-circulation` for walkable grassy banks following the water. The image must include trees, scattered rocks, grass, and natural pool edges while preserving open casting room. No buildings, shops, roads, or progression gates are requested.

## Layout requirements
Use `collectible-nodes` as fish encounter nodes placed only inside the several distinct deeper pools, with a few nodes per pool and none on shallow riffles or dry banks. Place one free-text player arrival at the downstream end on a broad bank, facing upstream along the creek. Keep fish-fighting space clear of rocks immediately around each pool while retaining natural cover along its margins.

## Layout components
Build one compact creek corridor with a short continuous clear-water channel, several distinct deeper pools, shallow connecting riffles, and grassy banks walkable along both sides where practical. Scatter rocks within shallows and along edges, cluster forest trees beyond casting clearance, and use level-to-gently rolling natural ground. The creek is the central path; bank trails connect every pool without crossings being required. Enclose the map with dense forest and rising vegetation. Compose the creek diagonally through frame so the sequence of pools remains visible.

Use `isometric`: render the natural creek materials, water clarity, and forest composition first, then derive the authoritative top-down because the route is simple and not topology-critical.
