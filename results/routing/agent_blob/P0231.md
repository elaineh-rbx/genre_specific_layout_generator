# Scene prompt
Create a 3D roleplay map as a pastel, cosy suburban town with prebuilt houses available to claim. Lay out residential streets around a central town area, connected by drivable roads about 20 studs wide.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim: this is a simple, immediately playable suburban roleplay town centered on claiming pre-built homes and driving between them.

## Shape and preset
Use `settlement-claimable` for a fixed town of pre-built houses that players select rather than construct. The preset is Life, matching its claimable housing, enterable homes, vehicle roads, and separation between the residential streets and central town area.

## Config requirements
Use `path-road-vehicle` for drivable roads about 20 studs wide as explicitly requested. Keep them primarily one-way or low-speed residential routes so that width remains compatible with vehicle clearance rather than treating them as full 30-stud two-way arterials. Use `district-zoned` to organize the housing around a distinct central town area. Use `building-interior` for the claimable homes players enter. Use `social-hub` for an open, welcoming central gathering space without inventing additional shops or activities.

## Layout requirements
Place one scene-specific claim marker outside the front entrance or at the driveway edge of every pre-built house, with each marker clearly associated with only one property. Place a player arrival marker at the edge of the central town area facing toward the surrounding residential streets. No purchase, job, collectible, or race triggers are required.

## Layout components
Create a compact central green or paved gathering area, ringed by curved or short-grid residential streets. Arrange varied pre-built houses on individual landscaped lots along those roads, each with a driveway, front walk, and readable claim point. Connect the center and every home with roads measuring about 20 studs wide, using simple intersections and restrained traffic furniture. Furnish the town with pastel house facades, soft roofs, white fences, flowering trees, tidy lawns, lamps, benches, and gentle signage. Low hedges and the outermost back gardens form a soft boundary. Compose the frame around the central area with the residential streets visibly wrapping outward.

## Render order
Render in `isometric` order: the pastel architectural character and cosy composition lead, and the simple suburban road plan can be projected afterward.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, driven by a complete but compact residential town. The theme is pastel, cosy suburbia. Use `P3` because players move from the outdoor streets into claimable house interiors.
