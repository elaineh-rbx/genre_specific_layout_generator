# Scene prompt

Create a 3D motocross freestyle map as one large desert riding compound with open dirt terrain. Arrange dirt tracks and large freestyle ramps across the main riding area, leaving broad open space between features for bike runs and trick lines. Place a dedicated build plot to one side of the compound for custom player-made ramps, kept separate from the main tracks. Include an in-world garage where players select and customize their bikes.

# Agent decision

## Genre

Simulation is the sole genre. Players freely operate and customize motocross bikes, earn from riding and tricks, and build ramps; there is no lap or finish condition that would make this Racing.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open`, one contiguous shared freestyle compound traversed in any order. Use the Physics Sim preset, retaining its physics contraptions and circulation while omitting the unrequested environmental hazard.

## Config requirements

Draw `physics-rig` as large dirt takeoffs, landing ramps, tabletops, quarter pipes, and other freestyle structures arranged for airborne bike tricks. Draw `path-circulation` as broad connected dirt tracks and straight run-ups linking ramps without forcing a race route. Draw `building-interior` as an accessible in-world bike garage entered from the compound, with recognizable selection and customization bays. The dedicated custom-build plot is additional flat image geometry on one side, physically separated from main trick lines but connected by a short access track.

## Layout requirements

Place bike-selection and bike-customization interaction markers inside the garage, aligned to distinct display or service bays; no loaded canonical option covers these controls. Place a build-boundary volume exactly around the dedicated custom-ramp plot so construction cannot clutter the main compound. Keep every fixed ramp’s approach and landing clear of other interaction zones, and leave enough straight dirt before each major takeoff for bike acceleration.

## Layout components

The main zone is a large open desert riding compound containing several loose dirt-track loops, broad run-up lanes, and widely spaced freestyle ramp groups. A flat rectangular build plot sits to one side behind a low boundary, reached by one access spur. The garage occupies another edge of the compound, with a wide vehicle doorway connecting its interior bays directly to the riding ground. Sandy terrain and low desert berms enclose the site without overhangs. Props include multiple large ramps and bike-brand display areas, with no invented counts. Compose from a high three-quarter view showing the riding field, separated build plot, and garage relationship.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: trick-line appearance, ramp scale, and open-space composition lead, then derive the top-down plan from the authoritative image.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is large, driven by one expansive compound with safe bike run-ups. Theme is realistic desert motocross freestyle. Use `P3` because players cross from the outdoor compound into the garage interior.
