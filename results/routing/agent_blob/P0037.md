# Scene prompt
Create a bright, cartoony pig roleplay map set on a small farm holding beside a compact forest edge. Arrange farm areas with collectible items and berry patches across open ground, crop areas, and around simple farm structures, with more berries and forage spread along paths into the forest. Include a fixed home area containing a small shed as the player house, positioned separately from the main farm and forest wandering zones.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim, with Simulation secondary because gathering berries and other resources is a repeatable earning loop.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`, taken from outside Roleplay & Avatar Sim’s usual shapes, for one compact shared holding where farm, forest edge, and home area coexist. No preset fits without adding a town, base construction, or upgrade bazaar, so the preset is none.

## Config requirements
Use `resource-shared` for communal farm crop ground and forest-edge berry patches; `path-circulation` for short walkable paths linking the productive areas; and `building-interior` for the small shed used as the pig’s house. The bright farm structures, open ground, crop rows, forest edge, berry shrubs, and forage-bearing undergrowth are required visible geometry.

## Layout requirements
Place `collectible-nodes` across all described gathering zones: berries among farm patches and along forest paths, plus other collectible items around crop areas, open ground, and simple farm structures. Keep nodes dispersed but easy to reach in the compact map, with the densest berry groups at the forest edge. No procedural house-placement marker is needed because the shed has one fixed home location.

## Layout components
Build one small farm holding beside a compact forest edge. Separate the main farm, forest wandering zone, and fixed home area clearly while joining all three with short paths. The farm includes open ground, crop rows, berry patches, and simple exterior structures; the forest uses denser trees and forage-lined paths. Place one small enterable shed in its own home area apart from the collection zones. Enclose the map with fields, hedges, and dense woodland, and frame the composition so all three zones read at once.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the playful farm-and-forest look leads, and the compact circulation does not require structure-first planning.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small. The theme is bright, cartoony, playful pig countryside. Use `P3` because play crosses between the outdoor holding and the shed interior.
