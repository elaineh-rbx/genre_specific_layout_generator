# Scene prompt
Crea un mapa 3D de obby colorido sobre un enorme escritorio, visto a escala de personas miniatura. Una ruta continua atraviesa nueve zonas sin teletransportes: teclas normales gigantes, teclas que desaparecen, rebotan o se hunden, seguida por las zonas del mouse, monitor, cables, escritorio y CPU. Cada tecla es una plataforma individual lo bastante grande para caminar sobre ella y saltar hacia las teclas vecinas; integra láseres, ventiladores, teclas móviles, plataformas giratorias, cables eléctricos, barras de empuje, objetos que caen y zonas peligrosas a lo largo del recorrido. El trayecto termina en la zona de la CPU, presentada como la meta final de escape. Coloca monedas coleccionables sobre rutas, saltos y zonas de riesgo.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant and only genre is Obby & Platformer because the player traverses a self-paced 3D obstacle course with discrete jumps.

## Shape and preset
Use `course-flat` for one continuous horizontal route across the same giant desk. Match the Classic Obby preset, extending it with the explicitly requested timed hazards, dangerous surfaces, collectibles, and finish.

## Config requirements
Apply `path-track` across exactly nine ordered zones. Apply `obstacle-jump` to giant key-to-key gaps and bouncing keys, `obstacle-timing` to disappearing and sinking keys, and `obstacle-moving` to moving keys, rotating platforms, fans, push bars, and falling desk objects. Apply `hazard-kill` to lasers, electrical cables, and elimination zones. Draw the pads for `checkpoint-respawn` and draw `winner-zone` as the final CPU escape area.

## Layout requirements
Place `checkpoint-respawn` at safe transitions between major zone groups, never on a moving or sinking surface. Place `collectible-nodes` as coins along the main route, jump arcs, and optional risk lines throughout all nine zones. Place the functional `winner-zone` once in the CPU area after the final hazard.

## Layout components
Build one enormous desk at miniature-player scale and one uninterrupted route through nine zones in this order: normal keys; disappearing, bouncing, and sinking keys; mouse; monitor; cables; open desk; CPU. Each keyboard key is an individually walkable giant platform. Connect later desk objects with readable jumps, ramps, or narrow traversable surfaces, never teleporters. Distribute lasers, fans, moving keys, rotating platforms, electrical cables, push bars, falling objects, and lethal floor patches without obscuring the critical path. Make the CPU the prominent terminal escape landmark. Bound the course at desk edges and compose it so the complete progression reads left to right.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the nine-zone order, legal gaps, hazard clearances, and checkpoint reachability must be valid before visual dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large, driven by nine zones across a giant desk. The theme is colourful modern Roblox obby with oversized electronics. Use `P6` because the continuous course must be valid by construction.
