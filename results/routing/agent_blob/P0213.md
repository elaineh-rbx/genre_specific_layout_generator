# Scene prompt
Create a 3D city map with a bold, high-contrast stylized Tokyo-inspired visual register, built from a few compact urban streets centered on six clearly visible, individually zoomable investment businesses. Arrange a cafe, doctor’s office, jazz club, bar, airsoft store, and tarot reader storefront along connected street blocks, each distinct through its facade, signage, and street-facing entrance. Frame the businesses with dense city buildings, narrow sidewalks, road intersections, and a limited surrounding skyline so the six locations remain the visual focus.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Simulation, specifically a passive incremental investment manager. The city is a real 3D presentation space, but no avatar traversal is requested.

## Shape and preset
Use `set-display`, taken from outside Simulation’s usual shape set, because the deliverable is a Tokyo city model viewed as a whole and zoomed into around individual investments rather than walked through. Preset is none: Incremental Simulator would imply traversable circulation and physical progression gates that this camera-operated investment view does not use.

## Config requirements
Use `settlement-density` for a compact, dense cluster of Tokyo-inspired street blocks. Use `social-hub` in its visible storefront sense for exactly six investment businesses: one cafe, one doctor’s office, one jazz club, one bar, one airsoft store, and one tarot reader. Give each a distinct facade, signage, and street-facing entrance while keeping all six visually dominant over the surrounding city dressing.

## Layout requirements
Place exactly six uncatalogued camera-focus and investment-interaction anchors, one aligned to the main facade of each named business, with an unobstructed zoom view from the city overview. These anchors must let the camera select each storefront independently without confusing it with background buildings. No player spawn, walking checkpoint, collectible, NPC emitter, or physical purchase gate is needed.

## Layout components
Build a few compact connected streets with narrow sidewalks and several road intersections. Distribute the six named storefronts across adjacent blocks so none overlaps another’s zoom framing. Use dense secondary city buildings and a limited skyline as backdrop rather than competing focal points. Keep entrances facing the street, signage readable from the intended zoom angle, and the overall boundary cropped as a composed city diorama. Frame the authoritative overview to show all six investments at once.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative, then derive the top-down plan, because this is a display set whose bold visual hierarchy and zoomable facade composition lead.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use medium scale for a few compact streets and six focal businesses. The theme is bold, high-contrast, graphic Tokyo nightlife with stylized urban density. Use `P0` with `SET`: the geometry is built and operated through camera views, but nobody walks through it, so traversal and jump-gap checks are skipped.
