# Scene prompt
Create a 3D FPS map set in an original compact urban neighbourhood covering about four city blocks. Arrange a distinct network of streets between varied buildings and neighbourhood landmarks, with clear routes and open areas for players to move through and encounter each other; keep the setting grounded in a hood-like city atmosphere without copying any specific map.

## Layout requirements

No team bases, capture zones, loot, powerups, or interior transition markers are required. Maintain multiple ways into and out of each central encounter area.

## Layout components
Build about 4 city blocks around an original asymmetrical street network. Mix narrow side streets, short alleys, a central intersection, small parking areas, back-lot courtyards, and pedestrian cut-throughs. Give each block a distinct landmark, such as a corner store facade, basketball court, mural wall, community stoop, or small service garage, without copying a known map. Use varied low-rise buildings as the outer massing and street furniture as calibrated cover. Keep terrain flat and paved, with solid perimeter blocks and fencing enclosing play. Frame the district from a raised diagonal view that shows all four blocks and their reconnecting routes.

Scale is small, set by the stated approximately 4-city-block footprint. The theme is a grounded, original hood-like urban neighbourhood. Use `P0` because the map is one flat outdoor surface with facade buildings and no enterable interiors.
