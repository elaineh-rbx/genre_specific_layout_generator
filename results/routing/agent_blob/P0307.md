# Scene prompt
Create a 3D survival map showing a black, white, and brown Laika in a realistic forest a few minutes of walking across. Fill the forest with natural woodland terrain and place lakes, a river, and ponds throughout it as water locations; include hares and squirrels among the forest areas.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Survival. Hunger and hydration maintained through hunting and drinking are the core loop; nothing hunts the player, so this is resource survival rather than chase survival.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded` as a finite natural forest a few minutes across, with an unobtrusive woodland boundary keeping the activity concentrated. The preset is none: Resource Survival assumes a larger biome world with gathering and base-building ground, while this brief asks for a compact forest focused only on prey and water.

## Config requirements
Use `water-body` for multiple drinkable lakes and ponds linked by a river through the forest. Use `npc-population` for visible hares and squirrels and the woodland patches they inhabit; they are prey animals, not hostile emitters. The rest is dense but navigable realistic woodland geometry with natural clearings, tree clusters, undergrowth, stones, and soil paths. Do not add shelters, disasters, hostile origins, or player build plots.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `npc-population` as hare and squirrel habitat markers spread among several separated forest areas, avoiding clustering every prey source beside the start. Add huntable-animal spawn points at those habitats, distinguished by species. Place invisible drink interaction volumes along safely reachable banks of every lake and pond and at several spaced points on the river. Set one player spawn on dry central woodland ground with more than one route toward water and prey.

## Layout components
Build one enclosed realistic forest that takes a few minutes to cross. Interweave dense tree stands with open hunting clearings and readable natural footpaths. Distribute lakes, a winding river, and smaller ponds throughout rather than collecting all water in one corner. Reserve forest patches around clearings and undergrowth for hares and squirrels. Use uneven woodland floor, roots, rocks, grasses, and muddy water edges, but avoid overhangs or caves. Enclose the map with dense distant forest and natural impassable terrain. Compose the view so the river provides a visual spine while several water bodies and habitat clearings remain legible.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the realistic forest atmosphere leads, and the top-down is derived from it because the habitat and water network does not require procedurally guaranteed topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is medium, set by the few-minute walking distance. The theme is grounded realistic temperate woodland centered on a black-white-brown Laika. Use `CHECK` for swimmable or drinkable water volumes; otherwise the single-surface forest remains on the default route.
