## Enriched image prompt

Arrange dense but navigable woodland around one lake, one winding river, and two small ponds, giving every water feature at least one accessible shore where the dog can drink. Distribute open patches and undergrowth throughout the forest as hunting ground for hares and squirrels, without adding a predator threat. Use natural tree density, earthy ground, reeds and stones near water, and a natural treeline boundary. Hunger and hydration bars are UI; their physical map footprint is the prey habitat and accessible drinking shores.

## Layout requirements

## Layout components

Open ground and undergrowth alternate so the dog can track prey while maintaining realistic forest cover. Every body of water has a reachable bank. Compose the scene so the river threads through the map and the four water features and major hunting clearings remain distinguishable.
