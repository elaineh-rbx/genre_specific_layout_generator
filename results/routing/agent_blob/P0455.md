# Scene prompt
Create a 3D arcade soccer map in a fully enclosed stadium bowl dressed in a cartoon jungle theme. At the center, place a compressed fast-play soccer pitch with goals at opposite ends, clear sidelines, penalty areas, and open space for quick team movement. Tiered spectator stands tightly surround the field, packed with a loud reactive crowd; jungle foliage, oversized tropical plants, vines, and banana-themed details decorate the stadium perimeter. Include muddy patches and occasional banana peels on the pitch, with a robot referee positioned to oversee play from the field.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Sports because two teams play scored soccer matches on a fixed marked pitch.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-bounded` for the compressed field inside a rigid stadium perimeter. Match the Field Sport preset, then add the explicitly requested full-stadium dressing.

## Config requirements
Use `trigger-bounds` for the visible sidelines, `startpoint-play` for kickoff and mirrored team positions, `trigger-scoring` for the two goal mouths, and `marker-distance` for the centerline, penalty boxes, and other field markings. Use `spectator-bleachers` for tightly surrounding tiered stands and `barrier-perimeter` for the enclosed stadium bowl. Use `npc-population` for the packed reactive crowd and the robot referee. Draw muddy patches and occasional banana peels as visible pitch obstacles with jungle foliage, vines, oversized tropical plants, and banana motifs around the perimeter.

## Layout requirements
Place one `trigger-bounds` perimeter on the sidelines, two `trigger-scoring` planes inside the opposing goals, and `startpoint-play` markers at center kickoff and mirrored team formations. Place one robot-referee `npc-population` anchor on the field with clear sightlines, plus crowd anchors throughout the stands. Associate slippery interaction areas with every drawn mud patch and banana peel.

## Layout components
Build one compressed rectangular soccer field with two opposite goals, clear sidelines, penalty areas, a center mark, and generous open movement lanes. Wrap the pitch tightly in tiered spectator stands and a sealed stadium bowl. Fill the outer bowl with cartoon jungle foliage, giant tropical leaves, hanging vines, and banana-themed details while keeping the playing surface readable. Scatter occasional mud patches and banana peels without blocking the principal routes. Compose the robot referee as a visible field presence and preserve crowd sightlines toward the whole pitch.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: opposing goals, field markings, bounds, starts, and referee sightlines must be spatially correct before jungle dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium because the arcade pitch is compressed but surrounded by a complete stadium bowl. The theme is colorful slapstick jungle soccer. Use `tiered` for the raised stands; there are no overlapping playable floors or outside-to-inside transitions.
