# Scene prompt

Create a small, cosy modern shared bedroom for two players, designed as a warm, comfortable private social space. Keep it to one focused room with furnishings arranged for two occupants and no en-suite or balcony.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim because the room supports private social interaction rather than a scored or task-based loop.

## Shape and preset

Use `interior-single`: the complete map is one enclosed bedroom and players never cross to an exterior. No published preset remains recognisable at this room-only scale, so the preset is none.

## Config requirements

Include the visible part of `teleporter-link` as a subtle shared arrival point integrated into the room, not a distant-district transport structure. Arrange two coordinated sleeping and seating positions so neither occupant is treated as a guest. Do not add `building-interior`, because this is the entire interior map rather than a building entered from outside.

## Layout requirements

Place the functional half of `teleporter-link` as one paired destination centered just inside the arrival area, with two non-overlapping arrival markers so both players materialize safely together. The private-instance-per-pair behavior is scripting rather than geometry and is only a siting constraint here.

## Layout components

Build one small, focused bedroom for two occupants. Put the paired arrival area near the room entrance and keep a clear path from it into the room. Arrange two beds or one clearly shared two-person sleeping composition along a wall, two bedside surfaces, and a compact two-seat conversation nook facing inward. Use warm wood, soft textiles, modern lamps, and a rug to establish a cosy contemporary tone. Enclose the room with four walls and a ceiling; include no en-suite, balcony, exterior, or secondary room. Compose the view to show the arrival point, sleeping area, and conversation nook together.

## Render order

Render `isometric` first as authoritative, then derive the top-down image, because atmosphere and furniture composition lead and the single room has trivial topology.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is small, driven by exactly two occupants and one room. The theme is cosy modern warmth for a social game. Route `P0`: this interior-only map has one flat surface and no outside-to-inside transition, overhang, or separate map.
