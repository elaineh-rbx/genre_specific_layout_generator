# Scene prompt
Create a small playable vertical slice of the contiguous Island of Brainrot, covering two connected regions: Skibidi Sewers and Sigma City. Skibidi Sewers occupies the lower area, a grimy underground network of sewer tunnels, drainage channels, pipes, maintenance walkways, side chambers, hidden collectible alcoves, and an arena-like boss chamber. Above and beyond it, Sigma City rises as a dense, exaggerated urban district of streets, alleys, rooftops, plazas, and tall buildings, with scattered Brainrot creatures, collectibles, secrets, and a larger boss encounter space. A clear transition route links the sewer exits to the city streets; temporary Reality Rift portals appear only as special portal structures, not as zone connections.

# Agent decision
## Genre
RPG is dominant because collection, leveling, squad growth, quests, enemies, and bosses define the authored game. Adventure is secondary because exploration, secrets, and hidden collectibles materially shape this vertical slice.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-underground`, taken from outside RPG’s usual set, for one contiguous city surface linked to a playable sewer layer below. Preset is none: the slice lacks the sanctuary-and-economy hub of Action RPG and the separate instanced structure of Dungeon Crawler.

## Config requirements
Use `hazard-aggro` for readable creature-combat pockets and the two boss spaces, set back from the main transition route. Use `alcove-secret` for hidden collectible recesses in sewer side chambers and concealed city corners. Use `settlement-density` for dense, exaggerated Sigma City blocks. Use `teleporter-link` in visible form for temporary Reality Rift portal structures, clearly distinguished from ordinary sewer-to-city circulation.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` points in sewer combat pockets, the sewer boss chamber, city plazas and alleys, and the larger city boss space. Place `collectible-nodes` across both regions, with some inside hidden alcoves and others along visible exploration routes. Place the functional half of `teleporter-link` at special Rift portal structures only; these are destinations to temporary worlds, never links between the two current regions. Provide one player arrival in a safe city-edge plaza and a clear return path from below.

## Layout components
Build exactly two connected regions. The lower Skibidi Sewers contain looping tunnels, drainage channels, pipes, maintenance walkways, side chambers, secret alcoves, and one arena-like boss chamber. Sigma City above contains dense streets, alleys, plazas, rooftops, tall exaggerated buildings, and one larger boss encounter space. At least one broad, unmistakable ramp, stair, or service exit links the sewer layer to city streets. Keep the layered zones contiguous while preventing accidental drops between them. Compose separate readable views for the lower tunnel network and upper skyline, tied together by the transition landmark.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: both layer plans, their transition, combat pockets, secrets, and return circulation must be valid before chaotic thematic dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium: it is a small vertical slice, but it contains two substantial layered regions. The theme is colorful absurd internet-meme chaos, split between grimy industrial sewers and an exaggerated energetic city. Use `P2` for overlapping surface and underground geometry and `P3` for the linked exterior-to-underground transition.
