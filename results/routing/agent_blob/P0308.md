# Scene prompt
Create a soft-storybook countryside farming RPG valley with individual player-owned farms, each selectable as a classic agricultural plot, a riverland fishing plot, or a wilderness plot with monster-filled surroundings. Farms contain seasonal crop fields, animal barns and coops for chickens, ducks, cows, goats, and pigs, plus space for a farmhouse, fences, and artisan-production buildings. Connect the farms to a central rural town with over 30 villagers’ homes, shops, festival space, and residential houses. Surround the valley with forageable woods, berry and mushroom patches, and distinct ocean, river, and mountain-lake fishing waters. Include a 120-floor underground mine descending through copper, iron, gold, and iridium tiers, with gemstone chambers, hostile-monster areas, and a deeper cavern region.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Simulation, with RPG secondary. Farming, husbandry, production, and fishing are the main repeated work loops; mine combat and progression add the RPG layer.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-underground`: a shared surface valley connects to a true 120-floor mine descending beneath it. The preset is none. Mining & Extraction does not represent selectable personal farms, the populated town, or the wider farming loop, while Sandbox omits the defining underground stack.

## Config requirements
Use `path-circulation` for roads joining farms, town, woods, waters, and mine entrance. Draw `social-hub` as the central rural town. Use `trigger-task` for visible crop plots, animal stations, artisan buildings, and fishing spots. Add `hazard-aggro` for monster-filled wilderness and mine areas. Use `npc-population` for over 30 villagers and five livestock species: chickens, ducks, cows, goats, and pigs. Include `building-interior` for farmhouses, barns, shops, homes, and mine access; `settlement-density` for the town; and `water-body` for ocean, river, and mountain lake.

## Layout requirements
Place `trigger-task` stations at each crop field, livestock building, artisan structure, and fishing bank. Place `collectible-nodes` across seasonal fields, forage woods, berry and mushroom patches, and all 120 mine floors, with copper, iron, gold, then iridium progressing by depth and gemstone nodes in chambers. Place `npc-population` markers across over 30 homes and shops and livestock markers on farms. Place `spawner-npc` emitters only in wilderness farms and hostile mine floors. Give each player one isolated farm allocation supporting three selectable layouts.

## Layout components
The surface contains individual farms, a central town, forageable woods, ocean coast, river, mountain lake, festival square, shops, homes, and a mine entrance, all linked by rural roads. Each farm supports classic, riverland, or wilderness arrangement and includes fields, fences, farmhouse, coops, barns, and artisan buildings. Below, stack exactly 120 distinct mine floors through four ore tiers, gemstone chambers, monster zones, and a deeper cavern region. Compose the valley as a readable storybook basin, with the mine represented separately floor by floor.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the surface circulation and vertical mine sequence must be planned first, with the top-down plans authoritative before isometric dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is huge, driven by personal farms, a town of over 30 villagers, and 120 mine floors. The theme is cozy soft-storybook countryside over increasingly dangerous mineral depths. Use `P2` for stacked underground floors, `P3` for surface-to-mine and enterable-building transitions, and `CHECK` for fishing waters.
