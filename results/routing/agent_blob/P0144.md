# Scene prompt

Create a 3D fashion game layout centered on a huge, continuous luxury fashion mall lobby. A spinning central runway anchors the main hall, surrounded by glowing waterfalls, glass elevators, giant digital billboards, portals to separate runway-show maps, a daily reward fountain, trading plaza, photo studio, and event stage. Connect a VIP wing to the shared mall through sparkling golden doors, containing a luxury boutique, VIP-only dressing booths, a private runway practice room, and chat lounge. Use private per-player dressing-booth instances with smart mirrors, makeup and hair stations, accessory organizers, and clothing display sections. Include a fashion magazine display area for winning outfits.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Roleplay & Avatar Sim: avatar styling and a judged dress-up round define the game. Entertainment is secondary because the mall includes an event stage and separate audience-facing runway shows.

## Shape and preset

Use `hub-portals`, taken from outside the dominant genre’s usual set, because the shared fashion mall is explicitly a portal hub for isolated runway-show maps and private booth instances. Start from the Dress Up preset, retain its social and photo-scene intent, and substitute this portal-hub shape for `stage-runway` because the runway is not the whole map.

## Config requirements

Use `social-hub` for the oversized central mall hall and spinning runway; `vignette-photo` for the dedicated photo studio; `building-interior` for the connected VIP boutique, practice room, lounge, and mall interior; and the visible half of `teleporter-link` for clearly framed portals to runway maps and private booths. Draw the glowing waterfalls, glass elevators, billboards, reward fountain, trading plaza, event stage, golden VIP doors, smart-mirror booths, clothing displays, and fashion-magazine display.

## Layout requirements

Place `teleporter-link` destinations at every runway portal and each private per-player booth entrance, linking them to their separate maps. No count is stated, so preserve a flexible portal bank and one booth destination per active player.

## Layout components

The huge central mall hall is the primary zone, with the spinning runway centered in frame and pedestrian concourses linking the fountain, trading plaza, photo studio, event stage, and portal bank. Glowing waterfalls and glass elevators flank the hall. Sparkling golden doors lead laterally into the VIP wing, whose boutique, booths, practice runway, and chat lounge form distinct rooms. Private booth instances contain smart mirrors, makeup and hair stations, accessory organizers, and clothing sections. The magazine display sits on a prominent lobby edge. Exterior boundaries are absent; this is an enclosed mall composition.

## Render order

Use `topdown`: the plan is rendered first and remains authoritative so portal circulation, room adjacency, and the VIP connection are unambiguous.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is huge, driven by the mall plus multiple isolated destinations. The theme is luminous luxury fashion retail with glossy glass, gold, and digital spectacle. Use `P4` for separate runway and booth maps and `P3` for the connected interior-room transitions.
