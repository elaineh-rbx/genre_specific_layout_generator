# Scene prompt
Create a 3D penalty-shootout stadium map focused on one polished penalty end, dressed to suggest a full international stadium bowl. A marked grass pitch runs from the foreground penalty spot to a full-size goal at the far end, with animated netting, posts, and crossbar. Surround the goal end with packed crowd stands, fictional team banners and flags, bright floodlights beneath a clear night sky, a large stadium scoreboard, benches, and a player tunnel. Compose clear behind-the-striker and goalkeeper-facing sightlines across the penalty area, with stadium-scale establishing views framing the goal and crowd.

# Agent decision

## Genre
The dominant genre is Sports because the entire experience is a competitive soccer penalty shootout scored against one goal.

## Shape and preset
Use `range-directed` for the single directed run from penalty spot to goal, replacing a full bounded soccer field. Match the Target Sport preset, with the goal as the discrete scoring target.

## Config requirements
Use `trigger-scoring` for the one full-size goal mouth and its visible posts, crossbar, and animated net. Use `marker-distance` for the penalty spot, goal line, penalty-area markings, and short marked pitch. Add `startpoint-play` for the fixed striker and goalkeeper positions. Use `spectator-bleachers` for packed stands framing the goal and `spectator-zone` for the benches. Use `npc-population` for the crowd. Draw fictional banners and flags, bright floodlights, the large scoreboard, and a player-tunnel façade.

## Layout requirements
Place one `trigger-scoring` plane across the goal line, bounded precisely between the two posts and below the crossbar. Place two `startpoint-play` markers: the striker at the single penalty spot and the goalkeeper centrally on the goal line. Place crowd `npc-population` anchors throughout the stands while keeping both behind-the-striker and goalkeeper-facing camera corridors unobstructed.

## Layout components
Build one grass penalty end extending from a foreground penalty spot to one full-size goal. Mark the goal line and penalty area clearly, with sufficient run-up and camera space behind the striker. Wrap the far end and sides in tightly framed crowd stands, benches, fictional flags and banners, floodlight towers, one prominent scoreboard, and a tunnel façade that suggests the rest of a stadium without building a full pitch. Use a clear night sky and compose the goal, crowd, and lights for both low gameplay views and broad establishing shots.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the exact spot-to-goal alignment, scoring plane, keeper line, and two opposing camera corridors must be established before stadium dressing.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium because only one penalty end is playable, though its dressing implies an international stadium. The theme is polished fictional international soccer under a clear floodlit night. Use `tiered` for raised crowd stands; no interior transition or overlapping playable floor is required.
