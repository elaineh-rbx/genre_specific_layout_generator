# Scene prompt
Create two separate compact 3D maps connected by a short driving transition: a family house map and a school map. The house contains the player's bedroom with a bed and packable books, pencil case, lunch, and notebook; a nearby kitchen with a breakfast table for both parents; and an exterior exit leading to the car pickup.

Build the school as a compact interior-focused map entered through a front entrance, with a welcoming teacher area, a classroom containing assigned student desks and a classroom phone, a lunch space with tables, and a principal's office along the route to the exterior exit. Outside the school, place the exit path with a distant parent-like figure visible ahead. Reuse the same classroom for the ending: re-light it into a dark, silent version with the player waking near the desks and the teacher seated across the room.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Adventure. The author explicitly frames a story-horror adventure, but the spatial play is a directed narrative journey rather than sustained threat evasion.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-chaptered` for the separate house and school maps connected by a driving transition. The preset is Story, retaining its chapter structure and `gate-chapter` transition while omitting unrelated one-way drops.

## Config requirements
Use `gate-chapter` for the car pickup and drive between maps, `building-interior` for both compact buildings and their exterior thresholds, and `npc-population` for both parents, the teacher, students, the new friend, principal, and distant parent-like figure. The same classroom must support normal bright lighting and a dark silent ending treatment rather than becoming a third build.

## Layout requirements
Place one initial spawn beside the bedroom bed, one school-arrival marker at the front entrance, and one ending respawn among the same classroom desks. Use `collectible-nodes` for exactly four packable objects—books, pencil case, lunch, and notebook—sited in the bedroom. Place character markers for two parents at the breakfast table, the teacher at reception and later across the classroom, students at assigned desks, one friend at lunch, the principal in the office, and one distant figure beyond the school exit path.

## Layout components
The house map contains a bedroom, nearby kitchen, breakfast table, exterior door, and car pickup, joined by a short domestic hallway. The school map follows a compact readable route from entrance to classroom, lunch area, classroom return, principal's office, and exterior exit. Include assigned desks, classroom phone, lunch tables, and the outside sightline toward the distant figure.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and make it authoritative, then derive top-down plans, because mood, props, and the classroom's before-and-after visual reuse lead the scene.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is medium across two compact maps. The theme moves from warm ordinary family life to uncanny school horror. Use `P4` for separate maps and `P3` for interior-to-exterior transitions.
