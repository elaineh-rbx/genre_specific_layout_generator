# Scene prompt
Create a compact 3D lobby hall for one four-player party, in a classic Roblox retro blocky/pixel style with clean geometric forms, vibrant colors, retro neon glow, and dramatic atmospheric shadows. Place a clear player spawn zone near the entrance/foreground, leading into an open central hall dominated by a visible Create Room / Enter Game portal area. Arrange subtle blocky zombie-apocalypse decorations around the hall edges—wooden barricades, ammo crates, warning signs, and spotlight lanterns—while keeping clear open space around the central portal.

# Agent decision
## Genre
Entertainment is dominant because this scene is specifically a walkable matchmaking lobby rather than the later zombie combat map.

## Shape and preset
Use `interior-single`: the entire build is one compact enclosed hall, with no outside transition. The preset is Hub, but its usual portal-hub shape is replaced by this single interior and `teleporter-link` is dropped because the portal is only a placeholder trigger and does not lead to another zone yet.

## Config requirements
Use `landmark-focal` for the large, unmistakable Create Room / Enter Game portal structure at the hall center. Use `social-hub` for open gathering ground sized for one four-player party. Use the visible half of `spawn-first-reveal` to make the entrance foreground frame the portal immediately. Add retro-blocky wooden barricades, ammo crates, warning signs, and spotlight lanterns around the edges without obstructing circulation.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional half of `spawn-first-reveal` as one clear spawn zone near the entrance, oriented toward the central portal. Place one unkeyed Create Room trigger volume inside the portal footprint; it opens the lobby setup interface but performs no teleport.

## Layout components
The build has an entrance/spawn bay in the foreground and one open central hall beyond it. The portal occupies the middle as the visual focus, surrounded by a generous clear interaction apron. Short direct walking space connects spawn to portal. Zombie-apocalypse props hug the perimeter walls in small clusters, leaving the center open. The enclosed hall boundary is clean blocky architecture, composed symmetrically toward the portal.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the retro neon atmosphere and portal reveal are authoritative, followed by a derived top-down plan.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, sized for one party of four. The theme is classic vibrant Roblox block geometry with retro neon, dramatic shadows, and restrained zombie-apocalypse dressing. The route is `P0`; this is one interior surface, and the inactive placeholder portal adds no `P4` transition.
