# Scene prompt
Create a 3D clean sci-fi glass-and-neon map with a high floating lobby platform above a giant straight vertical glass tunnel. The lobby has floor-level upgrade pads grouped into an upgrade zone. Below it, the transparent shaft drops in one continuous view to a visible safe floor at the bottom, centered on a massive golden trophy. Fill the roughly 15–20-second descent through the shaft with floating obstacles, giant blocks, and rotating hazards arranged at varied heights.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is Party & Casual because this is a timed multiplayer round with a shared lobby and first-place scoring. Obby & Platformer is secondary because the round is a movement challenge through hazards.

## Shape and preset
Use `space-staged`, with the lobby staging area directly above the isolated descent arena. The preset is Minigame, retaining lobby-to-stage transport and round scoring while omitting unrequested leaderboard and spectator structures. Although this shape normally implies separate maps, the route is overridden because the prompt explicitly requires one continuous visible shaft.

## Config requirements
Use `teleporter-link` for a visible lobby departure pad feeding the shaft. Use `obstacle-moving` for rotating hazards and giant moving blocks, `hazard-kill` for contact-reset obstacles, and `winner-zone` for the massive golden trophy on the safe bottom floor. Add the grouped neon upgrade pads as scene-specific lobby geometry without a canonical option ID.

## Layout requirements
The layout half of `teleporter-link` places one group transport marker in the lobby and its arrival across the shaft’s top entry. Place `trigger-scoring` at the safe bottom floor around the trophy. The layout half of `winner-zone` centers one reward detector on the trophy. Hazard reset volumes return failed players to evenly distributed lobby spawn points.

## Layout components
Build one broad floating lobby platform, one grouped floor-level upgrade area, and one giant straight transparent shaft beneath it. Arrange floating obstacles, giant blocks, and rotating hazards at varied heights across a 15–20-second descent, always leaving a navigable falling line. A safe floor closes the bottom around one oversized golden trophy. Frame the composition vertically so the trophy remains visible from the lobby through the glass.

## Render order
Render `topdown` first and make the staged obstacle plan authoritative, because blocked descent bands would make the round unplayable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is large, driven by the 15–20-second vertical fall. The theme is clean sci-fi glass and neon. Use `P2` because the lobby and many hazard tiers vertically overlap and cannot be represented by one top-down; no `P4` is used because this is explicitly one connected build.
