# Scene prompt

Create a 3D boxing PvP map as a full fight-night arena inside an enclosed hall. A raised boxing ring sits at the center, built with a platform, ropes, and four corner posts; its floor uses cloth or wood material. Surround the ring with spectator stands and audience seating, with bright arena lighting overhead. Size the space for a small, clear one-on-one match with a maximum of two players.

## Layout requirements

Compose the ring centrally and symmetrically so all four corners and the surrounding audience are legible.
