## Enriched image prompt

Arrange exactly four large, independent garage plots with equal spacing so each player can build and expand without overlapping another business. Connect all plot entrances with a simple vehicle-width road network and separate clear pedestrian circulation for friends and hired workers. On every plot, include an enterable garage work building, a visible job or work station, room for worker activity, and physically marked expansion sections that can unlock as the business grows. Keep the four plots balanced in size and access while making each read as its own self-contained garage operation.
