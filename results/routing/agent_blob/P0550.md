# Scene prompt
Build a 3D garage-business map with up to four separate garage plots, each providing space for its own garage building and business expansion. Connect the plots with roads and sidewalks where pedestrian NPCs walk around.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Simulation, specifically a persistent garage-business tycoon. Players earn money, expand independently, and return to the same business world rather than completing a race or round.

## Shape and preset
Use `plot-isolated`: provide up to four independent player plots, each large enough for one garage business and later expansion without colliding with neighboring builds. Match the Tycoon preset, but substitute isolated per-player plots for its shared team plot because each player owns a separate garage.

## Config requirements
Apply `gate-progression` as visible fenced or barricaded expansion pads attached to every garage plot, opening as that business grows. Apply `path-circulation` as the connected road-and-sidewalk network tying all plots together. Apply the drawn side of `npc-population` as pedestrian-friendly sidewalks and small public verge areas visibly suited to friends and ambient workers walking around.

## Layout requirements
Place the functional side of `npc-population` along the sidewalks between plots and near garage forecourts, keeping pedestrian routes out of vehicle entrances and road lanes. No fixed NPC count was given, so use distributed population zones rather than inventing a quantity. Hiring, job offers, money production, and business unlocks are gameplay systems and require no additional layout marker here.

## Layout components
Build up to four similarly sized garage plots around a compact shared road loop or short connected street. Each plot needs a street-facing garage shell, a broad paved forecourt, a driveway, and adjacent clear expansion pads behind low fencing. Add sidewalks continuously along the roads, with safe curb crossings near each garage rather than mid-block. Keep the terrain level, mixing asphalt roads, concrete work yards, and narrow grass verges. Separate the whole business park from the horizon with a simple perimeter wall or tree line. Compose the isometric frame to show all four plots, their expansion capacity, and the complete pedestrian and road circulation.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: lead with the readable garage-business appearance, then derive the top-down plan, since the compact road network has no topology that must be generated procedurally.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a medium scale band, driven by no more than four expandable plots. The theme is a clean contemporary roadside garage park with practical industrial details. Route through `P0`; the entire map is a single unobstructed outdoor surface.
