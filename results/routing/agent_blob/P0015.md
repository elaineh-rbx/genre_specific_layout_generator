# Scene prompt
Create a 3D dark storybook fantasy overworld with colorful cartoon characters and readable, playful environments. Build a small connected first-playable-slice map: a simple town hub leading along paths into a distinct dungeon area, with clear transitions between zones and space for the player to walk with two companion characters following behind. Keep the town, paths, and dungeon as the primary explorable spaces; story-scene areas are minimal.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG, with Adventure secondary. The source establishes turn-based fights and a party, while walking through the connected overworld is the priority.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open`: the town, paths, and dungeon entrance belong to one small connected map rather than separate instances. The matched preset is Turn-based RPG, retaining the contiguous world, sanctuary town, and hostile emitters while dropping the unrequested level-gated blockade.

## Config requirements
Use `safezone-town` for the simple hub where the playable slice begins. Use `path-trail` for clear, playful walking routes from town to dungeon. Use `building-interior` for the dungeon entered from the overworld. Use `npc-population` for exactly two colorful companion characters, Susie and Ralsei, and the traversable ground wide enough for both to follow behind the player.

## Layout requirements
Place the layout half of `npc-population` as exactly two companion start positions beside the player in the town hub, then preserve a three-character-wide following corridor along every primary path and dungeon passage. Place `spawner-npc` emitters only in designated dungeon encounter spaces, set beyond the entrance transition and outside narrow connective corridors.

## Layout components
Build three primary spaces: a compact town hub, an overworld path zone, and one dungeon. The town sits at one end with a readable central gathering area and a clear outbound gate. Broad paths wind through a small storybook landscape toward a strongly framed dungeon entrance. Inside, use a concise sequence of playful rooms and short corridors with enough width for the player plus two followers. Keep story-scene pockets minimal and off the main circulation line. Bound the slice with dark fantasy foliage, cliffs, and stylized walls; compose the town and dungeon entrance as opposing anchors.

## Render order
Use `isometric`: the isometric image is rendered first and is authoritative because the dark storybook look and character-readable proportions lead, while the simple route can be derived from it.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is small, limited to one first-playable slice. The theme is dark storybook fantasy with colorful cartoon accents. Use `P3` because play crosses from the exterior overworld into the dungeon interior.
