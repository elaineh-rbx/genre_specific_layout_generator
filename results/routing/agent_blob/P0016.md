# Scene prompt
Create a large open-world 3D fantasy adventure map with a safe central town serving as the hub, surrounded by a few large connected regions with distinct landscapes and level-gated routes. The town contains physical shop and preparation spaces, while hostile wilderness expands outward through rolling hills, forests, trees, and varied natural terrain toward enemy and boss areas. Arrange lower-level regions nearer the town and increasingly remote or blocked high-level areas farther away, with strong landmark vistas and stylized high-fidelity lighting, detailed nature, armor, and sword-themed visual effects.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The dominant genre is RPG. Enemy grinding, levels, stats, equipment tiers, a safe economy hub, and progressively unlocked regions all belong to one open-world RPG loop; Action is already covered by the Action RPG preset rather than requiring a second genre.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-open-biomes` for regions whose danger rises with distance from town. The matched preset is Action RPG, but its hub-and-dungeon shape is replaced because the prompt explicitly requires a few large connected regions rather than instanced dungeons. Retain the preset’s sanctuary, economy cluster, hostile grounds, and emitters.

## Config requirements
Use `safezone-town` for the central protected town; `social-hub` for clustered shops and preparation spaces; `hazard-aggro` for enemy and boss grounds set away from travel roads; `gate-progression` for visible blocks into higher-level regions; `landmark-focal` for distant regional vistas; `building-interior` for shops players enter; `settlement-density` for the compact central town; and `terrain-relief` for rolling hills and varied natural landforms. Armor, swords, and their stylized effects appear as shop and landmark dressing, without attempting to lay out hundreds of inventory items.

## Layout requirements
Place `spawner-npc` emitters throughout each hostile region, with lower-tier emitters nearest town, stronger clusters farther out, and boss emitters in distinct destination arenas. Keep emitters outside the safe town and off primary roads.

## Layout components
Build one central town and a few large connected wilderness regions radiating outward. Roads branch from a compact shop square through forests, hills, and distinct natural palettes. Guarded bridges, passes, or monumental gates mark level thresholds without breaking world continuity. Place ordinary enemy clearings between routes and boss arenas at remote landmarks. The outer terrain and natural barriers enclose the world while leaving long views back to town.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: the top-down plan is rendered first and authoritative so region adjacency, safe travel, threat gradients, and progression gates remain coherent.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
The scale is large, driven by several expansive regions. The theme is stylized high-fidelity new-generation fantasy. Use `P3` for enterable shops and `tiered` for hills without overhangs. Override the shape’s usual `P4` to `P0` for world scale because the prompt explicitly requires one connected world.
