# Scene prompt

Create a 3D gaming-themed castle map with three minigame areas arranged as distinct rooms or grounds inside and around the castle: an obby course, a sword-fighting arena, and a short racing track. Include a casino-style decorative area with a roulette table, non-playable arcade cabinets, slot machines, and card tables.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The genre is Party & Casual. The obby, sword fight, and race are three contained minigames within one party-game collection rather than three equal whole-experience genres.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open`, taken from outside the genre’s usual shapes: one contiguous castle and its grounds can be explored in any order, with three distinct activity areas connected physically. The preset is none because the Minigame preset assumes an isolated stage and lobby transport, while this brief explicitly places all games together inside and around one castle.

## Config requirements

Draw exactly three minigame areas: a compact obstacle course, a bounded sword-fighting arena, and a short closed racing track. Create a separate casino-style social room containing one roulette table plus decorative, non-playable arcade cabinets, slot machines, and card tables. Keep each activity visually separated by castle rooms, walls, courtyards, or grounds so their play spaces do not overlap.

## Layout requirements

Place start markers at all three minigames. Add checkpoint and respawn placements along the obby at safe completed sections, balanced opposing start positions in the sword arena, and racing start positions on a shared start line. Use `trigger-scoring` for the obby completion zone and the racing finish line; the exact number of obby checkpoints and racers was not stated.

## Layout components

Build one gaming-themed castle with an entry hall linking to the casino room and the indoor minigame spaces, plus doors opening to the exterior grounds. The obby forms a compact, ordered chain of reachable platforms. The sword arena is one clear, enclosed combat floor. The racing area is one short closed-loop track with a start/finish line and safe outer barriers. Corridors, doors, and courtyard paths connect all four destinations. Castle walls and the outer track barrier define the boundary. Compose the map so the castle is the central landmark and outdoor activities remain visible around it.

## Render order

Render `topdown` first and make it authoritative, then dress the isometric view; the obby chain and racing loop must remain connected and playable.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Use a medium scale, driven by exactly three compact games plus one decorative casino room. The theme is playful fantasy castle meets colorful arcade casino. Apply `P3` for movement between castle interiors and exterior grounds and `P6` because the obby route and racing circuit must be valid by construction.
