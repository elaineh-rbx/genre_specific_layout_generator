# Scene prompt

Create a 3D indoor club concert venue with a raised performance stage for players, built-in music equipment on the stage, and house lights illuminating the room.

# Agent decision

The dominant genre is Entertainment: this is a player-operated music venue built around performing rather than winning.

Use `venue-stage` from the Performance Venue preset. The raised player stage is the visual and spatial focus, with the club floor oriented toward it. Omit raked spectator seating because the brief calls only for an indoor club room.

## Config requirements

Draw `social-hub` as an open club floor in front of the stage where visitors can gather while keeping a clear view of performers. Draw the visible half of `spawn-first-reveal` as an entrance composition that opens toward the raised stage rather than backstage equipment. Place built-in music equipment directly on the stage, including a performance console, speakers, microphones, monitors, and compact lighting controls. Arrange house lights across the room and performance fixtures around the stage.

## Layout requirements

Place the layout half of `spawn-first-reveal` at the club entrance, oriented directly toward the stage. Add uncatalogued performer anchors on the raised deck beside the built-in music equipment so players can occupy clear performance positions without clipping props. No scoring volumes, checkpoints, teleports, or audience NPC markers are required.

## Layout components

Build one enclosed club room with a raised stage along one end and a broad, open audience floor in front. Connect the entrance to the floor with a direct, unobstructed approach. Keep the music console and speaker stacks toward the rear and sides of the stage, leaving its center usable by players. Mount house lights around the ceiling perimeter and aim colored performance lights toward the deck. Use dark club walls, acoustic panels, truss, cables, and modest lounge details as supporting props. The enclosing room boundary is solid, and the composition converges on the stage.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render the authoritative club atmosphere and stage composition first, then derive the top-down plan because no computed topology is required.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

The scale is small, driven by one stage and one club floor. The theme is intimate indoor concert club with practical house lighting. Use `P0`; the venue is one single-level interior without overhangs or map transitions.
