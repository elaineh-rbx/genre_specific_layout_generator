# Scene prompt
Create a compact 3D tsunami-escape map focused on the layout of a short run from the waterline to safe high ground, taking roughly 20–30 seconds to cross. The low coastal starting area leads inland along a clear ascending route toward elevated terrain where players can shelter from an incoming wave; no meme characters or themed props.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Survival: the activity is surviving a tsunami by reaching terrain above the hazard and outlasting the wave, not escaping through an exit.

## Shape and preset
Use `route-guided`, taken from outside Survival’s usual shape shortlist, because this scene is a compact directed climb from coast to refuge rather than an arena explored freely. The matching preset is Disaster Survival, with its usual destructible structures omitted and its shape replaced by the prompt’s explicit route.

## Config requirements
Use `hazard-kill` for the visible incoming tsunami and inundated low ground, `terrain-tiered` for raised safe ground above the flood level, and `water-body` for the coastal waterline feeding the wave. No Brainrots characters or themed props appear.

## Layout requirements
No separate escape gate is placed: survival concludes on the high ground. Place one unnumbered player start marker in the low coastal starting area and one survival volume across the elevated refuge, set fully above the wave’s reach.

## Layout components
Build a small low coastal start at the foreground edge, a single clear inland route sized for a 20–30 second run, and an elevated refuge at the rear. The path climbs continuously by broad slopes or stepped terrain without overhangs. Coastal water borders the low side; bare floodable ground transitions into dry high ground. Keep the boundary compact and readable, with the ascent and refuge visible together in frame.

## Render order
Render `isometric` first and treat it as authoritative, then derive the top-down view, because the elevation and visual read lead while the route remains a simple continuous path.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Scale is small, driven by the 20–30 second crossing. The theme is a clean natural coastal disaster map. Use `tiered` for the strong non-overhanging climb and `CHECK` for the coastal water volume; no exit, interior, separate-map, or overhang pass is required.
