# Scene prompt
Créez une carte 3D de tycoon de concessionnaire automobile dans un quartier moderne en verre et acier au style Roblox propre et accessible. Le centre de la petite carte ouverte est un immense concessionnaire, avec une salle d’exposition vitrée, une boutique intégrée et un grand garage souterrain relié au bâtiment. Quelques rues praticables entourent le concessionnaire pour la conduite libre, sans autres destinations ni lieux majeurs.

# Agent decision
## Genre
The genre is Simulation, specifically an automotive dealership tycoon with a compact vehicle free-roam area. The tycoon business remains dominant over the driving activity because there is no race or destination-based vehicle objective.

## Shape and preset
Use `world-underground`: one shared dealership district has a surface showroom and connected streets above a large underground garage. Match the Tycoon preset, substituting this layered shared-world shape for its team plot while retaining the integrated shop, progression spaces, and circulation.

## Config requirements
Apply `social-hub` as the dealership’s integrated upgrade shop and customer-facing glass showroom. Apply `gate-progression` as visible closed-off showroom or dealership expansion bays that unlock over time. Apply `path-circulation` and `path-road-vehicle` as the compact drivable street loop and the internal routes between showroom, garage ramp, and exterior roads. Apply `building-interior` to the enterable showroom and shop, with the underground garage linked through a clear vehicle ramp.

## Layout requirements
Nothing additional needs placement after segmentation. Starting capital, income pacing, vehicle ownership, and purchases are gameplay systems, and the prompt does not request task stations, pickups, NPCs, or spawn emitters.

## Layout components
Place one immense glass-and-steel dealership at the center of a small open map. Give it a broad glazed showroom, an attached integrated shop zone, a forecourt, and one obvious ramp descending to a large garage footprint beneath the building. Wrap the dealership with a few connected, driveable modern streets forming a compact free-roam loop, with curb cuts returning naturally to the forecourt and underground ramp. Add sidewalks, parking/display bays, road markings, lamps, and restrained city-edge facades, but no additional destinations. Keep surface terrain level and enclose the map with a clean urban skyline boundary. Compose the view around the dealership while preserving the complete road loop.

## Render order
Use `topdown`: establish the surface road loop and linked underground garage plan first, then dress both views isometrically, because the garage ramp must connect the two traversable levels.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost
Use a medium scale band: the dealership is immense, but the surrounding open map contains only a few streets. The theme is clean accessible Roblox modernism in glass, steel, concrete, and bright showroom lighting. Route through `P2` for overlapping surface and underground levels and `P3` for exterior-to-showroom and exterior-to-garage transitions.
