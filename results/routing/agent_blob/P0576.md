# Scene prompt

Create a small realistic modern town map with streets, buildings, and outdoor areas arranged to showcase realistic lighting, shadows, and glare.

# Agent decision

## Genre

The dominant genre is Adventure because the author explicitly describes a simple exploration game. Entertainment is secondary because experiencing the realistic lighting showcase is the activity, but it contributes no separate shape.

## Shape and preset

Use `world-open`, one contiguous small town that players can explore in any order. The preset is none: the available Adventure and Entertainment presets would add landmarks, hidden discoveries, badges, or portals absent from this visual exploration brief.

## Config requirements

Use `path-trail` as a network of walkable modern streets and outdoor pedestrian routes. Use `settlement-density` for a compact, coherent town rather than scattered unrelated buildings. Arrange building facades, street edges, and open outdoor areas to catch convincing directional light, cast readable shadows, and produce controlled glare across varied sightlines. Buildings remain exterior scenery unless otherwise stated.

## Layout requirements

Nothing needs post-segmentation placement. The brief does not call for collectibles, triggers, quest boards, teleports, checkpoints, NPCs, or enterable-building links.

## Layout components

Create one compact town zone with several streets linking a small collection of modern building blocks and outdoor plazas or pocket spaces. Keep pedestrian circulation continuous around the exteriors. Use mostly level urban ground and avoid tunnels, stacked roads, or inaccessible courtyards. The outermost building backs and street ends form the boundary. Compose long and short sightlines so building faces cast shadows across streets while select open angles catch glare, with no single landmark dominating the map.

## Render order

Use `isometric`: render the realistic material, light, shadow, and glare composition first, then derive the top-down plan because the street network need only remain generally connected, not structurally exact.

## Scale, theme, and pipeline cost

Scale is small, driven by a compact walkable town rather than a large city. The theme is realistic contemporary urban lighting showcase. Use `P0`; the town is one continuous exterior surface with facade-only buildings, no overhang-dependent play, separate maps, or structure-critical topology.
